# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Dependency Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed identification, modelling, monitoring and management of dependencies between Enterprise information assets, systems, applications, integrations, processes and business capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Dependency Management Services shall

- provide centralized dependency management capabilities
- identify information relationships
- model dependency structures
- support impact analysis
- improve change management
- strengthen governance visibility
- support risk assessment
- support compliance evaluation
- improve operational resilience
- support lineage-based analysis
- support lifecycle governance
- support information quality management
- support privacy and security requirements
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure dependency traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Dependency Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable dependency management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Dependency Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Dependency Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Dependency Transparency by Design

Enterprise dependencies shall be identified, documented and governed as an integrated part of Enterprise Architecture.

---

## Relationship-Driven Management

Enterprise information, technology and business relationships shall be managed through controlled dependency models.

---

## Complete Dependency Visibility

Critical dependencies shall remain visible across information, applications, systems, integrations and business capabilities.

---

## Evidence-Based Dependency Management

Dependency decisions shall be based on trusted lineage, metadata, operational information and governance evidence.

---

## Proactive Dependency Control

Dependencies shall be identified and managed before changes create unintended consequences.

By integrating information relationship management, data dependency management, system dependency management, application dependency management, integration dependency management, process dependency management, business capability dependency management, technical dependency management, dependency mapping services, dependency repository management, dependency visualization services, dependency monitoring services, dependency risk analysis, dependency impact assessment, lineage integration, metadata integration, master information integration, reference data integration, information quality integration, governance integration, compliance integration, audit integration, event integration, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables transparent, controlled and policy-driven dependency management while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Information Dependency Management Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise dependency management capabilities across the Enterprise.
