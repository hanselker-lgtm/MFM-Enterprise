# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Knowledge Management Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise knowledge capabilities.

The Knowledge Management Architecture Framework shall provide:

- knowledge management principles
- knowledge governance
- knowledge strategy
- knowledge capability architecture
- knowledge repository architecture
- knowledge graph architecture
- semantic knowledge architecture
- expert knowledge architecture
- organizational learning architecture
- knowledge sharing architecture
- knowledge discovery architecture
- knowledge integration architecture
- knowledge security architecture
- knowledge operations
- knowledge performance management
- knowledge evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise knowledge is captured, structured, governed and reused as a strategic capability.

---

---

# 3. Enterprise Knowledge Management Architecture Principles

The Knowledge Management Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Knowledge as an Enterprise Asset

Knowledge shall be managed as a strategic Enterprise asset.

---

## Principle 2 – Knowledge Accessibility

Relevant knowledge shall be available to authorized users when required.

---

## Principle 3 – Knowledge Quality

Knowledge shall be accurate, relevant, maintained and trustworthy.

---

## Principle 4 – Knowledge Sharing

The Enterprise shall promote structured knowledge exchange and collaboration.

---

## Principle 5 – Knowledge Preservation

Critical knowledge shall be captured and preserved throughout organizational change.

---

## Principle 6 – Knowledge Security

Knowledge shall be protected according to confidentiality, integrity and access requirements.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Knowledge Evolution

Knowledge capabilities shall continuously improve through learning and experience.

---

# 9. Knowledge Capability Architecture

The Knowledge Capability Architecture defines how Enterprise knowledge capabilities are identified, structured and managed to create effective knowledge services.

Knowledge capabilities provide the foundation for capturing, organizing, sharing and applying organizational knowledge.

---

# 9.1 Knowledge Capability Principles

Knowledge capabilities shall ensure:

- accessible knowledge
- reliable information
- knowledge reuse
- organizational learning
- measurable knowledge value

# 21. Knowledge Governance Operating Model

The Knowledge Governance Operating Model defines how Enterprise Knowledge Management capabilities are governed, managed and continuously improved across the organization.

Knowledge Governance ensures that knowledge assets, repositories, expert knowledge, semantic models, learning capabilities and operational responsibilities remain aligned with Enterprise strategy and architecture principles.

---

# 21.1 Knowledge Governance Principles

Knowledge Governance shall ensure:

- clear ownership
- defined accountability
- knowledge quality
- controlled knowledge lifecycle
- secure knowledge usage
- effective knowledge sharing
- measurable knowledge value
- continuous improvement
