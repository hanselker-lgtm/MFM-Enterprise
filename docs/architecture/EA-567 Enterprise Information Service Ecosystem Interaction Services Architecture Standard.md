# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Interaction Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Interaction Services shall follow these principles.

## Interaction by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include interaction considerations from initial design through operational lifecycle.

---

## Controlled Interaction

Interactions shall only occur through approved governance, security and communication mechanisms.

---

## Trusted Communication

Interactions shall maintain appropriate trust relationships between participating entities.

---

## Interaction Ownership

Every interaction capability shall have defined ownership, responsibility and accountability.

---

## Interaction Lifecycle Management

Interactions shall be established, maintained, monitored, evolved and retired through controlled lifecycle processes.

---

## Governed Interaction Management

Interaction activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.

---

## Continuous Interaction Improvement

Interaction capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
