# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Compliance Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed compliance capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Compliance Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem compliance capabilities
- support regulatory compliance
- support contractual compliance
- support policy compliance
- support partner compliance
- support supplier compliance
- support information compliance
- support data compliance
- support security compliance
- support intelligence compliance
- support analytics compliance
- support automation compliance
- support orchestration compliance
- support governance compliance
- support compliance monitoring
- support compliance reporting
- support compliance lifecycle management
- improve ecosystem trust
- improve ecosystem transparency
- improve ecosystem accountability

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Compliance Services shall provide trusted and reusable compliance capabilities enabling controlled ecosystem operations.

# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Compliance Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Compliance Services shall follow these principles.

## Compliance by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include compliance requirements from the beginning.

---

## Continuous Compliance

Compliance shall be continuously monitored and maintained throughout the ecosystem lifecycle.

---

## Evidence-Based Compliance

Compliance decisions shall be supported by documented and traceable evidence.

---

## Transparent Compliance

Compliance status shall be visible, measurable and understandable.

---

## Risk-Based Compliance

Compliance activities shall consider business, operational, security and regulatory risks.
