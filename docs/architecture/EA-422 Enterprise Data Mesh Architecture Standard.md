# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Mesh Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Infrastructure Architecture principles are inherited from EA-320.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Fabric Architecture principles are inherited from EA-421.

All Enterprise Data Mesh implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Mesh Architecture governing decentralized, domain-oriented ownership of Enterprise data products while ensuring interoperability, governance and trusted information exchange across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Data Mesh Architecture shall

- decentralize data ownership
- establish domain-oriented data products
- enable self-service data platforms
- improve scalability
- strengthen business ownership
- increase interoperability
- improve data quality
- accelerate analytics
- support artificial intelligence
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Mesh shall function as a strategic Enterprise capability enabling autonomous domain teams while maintaining Enterprise-wide governance.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- business domains
- data domains
- data products
- domain ownership
- self-service data platforms
- federated governance
- metadata integration
- data sharing
- analytical workloads
- operational workloads

The standard applies regardless of infrastructure platform, cloud provider or deployment model.

---

# 3. Enterprise Data Mesh Principles

Enterprise Data Mesh shall be governed by the following principles.

## Domain-Oriented Ownership

Business domains shall own and manage their data products throughout the complete lifecycle.

---

## Data as a Product

Every significant business dataset shall be managed as a reusable Enterprise Data Product.

---

## Self-Service Platform

The Enterprise shall provide standardized self-service capabilities enabling domain teams to develop, publish and manage Data Products independently.

---

## Federated Computational Governance

Enterprise governance shall define common policies while allowing domains operational autonomy within those policies.

---

## Interoperability by Design

Data Products shall be designed for reuse and interoperability across Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Mesh Architecture shall remain independent of specific implementation technologies, cloud providers and vendors.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Mesh Architecture establishes the standardized foundation for decentralized, domain-oriented Enterprise data management across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

By combining Domain Ownership, Data Products, Self-Service Platforms, Federated Computational Governance, Metadata Management, Artificial Intelligence, Event-Driven Architecture, Observability and Automation, the Enterprise enables scalable, reusable and trusted data capabilities while maintaining Enterprise-wide governance and interoperability.
