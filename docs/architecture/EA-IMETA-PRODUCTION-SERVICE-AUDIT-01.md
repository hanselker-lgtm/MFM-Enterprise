# 1. PURPOSE

EA-IMETA-PRODUCTION-SERVICE-AUDIT-01 establishes the formal audit framework above production assurance.

It defines how EA-IMETA can demonstrate, through controlled evidence, that:

```text
GOVERNANCE
CONTROLS
SERVICE MANAGEMENT
SECURITY
DATA
ARCHITECTURE
OPERATIONS
ASSURANCE
```

operate within approved boundaries.

---

# 2. AUDIT PRINCIPLE

> AUDIT SHALL PROVIDE INDEPENDENT, TRACEABLE AND EVIDENCE-BASED EVALUATION OF WHETHER EA-IMETA'S GOVERNANCE, CONTROLS AND SERVICE OPERATIONS CONFORM TO APPROVED REQUIREMENTS.

---

# 3. AUDIT OBJECTIVES

Audit shall determine:

```text
IS THE GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK FOLLOWED?
ARE MATERIAL CONTROLS OPERATING?
IS EVIDENCE COMPLETE?
ARE RISKS PROPERLY MANAGED?
ARE FINDINGS CLOSED?
IS SERVICE PERFORMANCE ACCEPTABLE?
IS SECURITY EFFECTIVE?
IS DATA CONTROLLED?
IS ARCHITECTURE GOVERNED?
IS CHANGE CONTROLLED?
IS RECOVERY TESTED?
```
