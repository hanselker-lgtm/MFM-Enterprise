# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Messaging Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Messaging Architecture principles are inherited from EA-342.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Messaging Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Messaging Services Architecture supporting secure, reliable and scalable message-based communication across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Messaging Services shall

- provide standardized messaging services
- support asynchronous communication
- support synchronous communication
- improve interoperability
- strengthen scalability
- ensure reliable message delivery
- support reusable messaging capabilities
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- enable continuous optimization

Enterprise Messaging Services shall provide trusted and reusable messaging capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- message publishers
- message consumers
- message brokers
- queue managers
- messaging platforms
- enterprise applications
- integration platforms

- hybrid environments
- external messaging integrations

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, messaging technology or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Messaging Services Principles

Enterprise Messaging Services shall follow these principles.

## Reliable by Default

Enterprise Messaging Services shall provide reliable, durable and guaranteed message delivery across all supported messaging channels.

---

## Secure by Default

Every message shall be authenticated, authorized, encrypted and continuously monitored.

---

## Standardized Messaging Services

Enterprise Messaging Services shall expose standardized messaging interfaces supporting interoperability throughout the Enterprise.

---

## Metadata Driven Messaging

Messaging services shall leverage Enterprise metadata for routing, discovery, lifecycle management and governance.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Messaging Services shall remain independent of vendors, messaging platforms, brokers, cloud providers and infrastructure technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Messaging Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, reliable and governed message-based communication across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating standardized messaging services, reliable delivery mechanisms, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled message exchange while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Messaging Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise messaging capabilities across the Enterprise.

The Enterprise Messaging Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted message routing, autonomous queue optimization, semantic-aware messaging, adaptive broker management, intelligent workload distribution, self-optimizing messaging infrastructures, edge-native messaging services, decentralized messaging ecosystems and quantum-safe Enterprise messaging platforms.
