# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Quantum Computing Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise Quantum Computing capabilities.

The Quantum Computing Architecture Framework shall provide:

- quantum computing principles
- quantum governance
- quantum strategy
- quantum capability architecture
- quantum hardware architecture
- quantum software architecture
- quantum algorithm architecture
- quantum data architecture
- hybrid quantum-classical architecture
- quantum integration architecture
- quantum security architecture
- post-quantum security architecture
- quantum operations
- quantum performance management
- quantum evolution

The framework ensures that quantum computing capabilities are evaluated, governed and integrated responsibly as part of future Enterprise computational capabilities.

# 3. Enterprise Quantum Computing Architecture Principles

The Quantum Computing Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategic Quantum Adoption

Quantum computing capabilities shall be introduced based on measurable Enterprise value and strategic relevance.

---

## Principle 2 – Hybrid Computing Integration

Quantum capabilities shall operate together with classical computing environments where appropriate.

---

## Principle 3 – Responsible Quantum Usage

Quantum computing shall be applied according to governance, security and ethical principles.

---

## Principle 4 – Algorithm Suitability

Quantum algorithms shall be selected according to computational requirements and expected benefits.

---

## Principle 5 – Future Readiness

Enterprise architecture shall prepare for future quantum technology development.

---

## Principle 6 – Secure Quantum Environment

Quantum environments shall include appropriate security controls throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Quantum Evolution

Quantum capabilities shall continuously evolve according to technology maturity and Enterprise requirements.
