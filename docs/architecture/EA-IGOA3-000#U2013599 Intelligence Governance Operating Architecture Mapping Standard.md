# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational governance architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities are strategically directed, responsibly managed and continuously improved.

The Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- governance operating principles
- strategy governance frameworks
- decision governance structures
- policy management models
- standard management structures
- responsibility models
- risk governance structures
- compliance governance models
- investment governance structures
- performance governance models
- governance monitoring structures
- governance automation structures
- governance evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for controlled, aligned and continuously improving Intelligence operations.

# 3. Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Governance by Design

Governance shall be integrated into Intelligence capabilities from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Strategic Alignment

Intelligence activities shall support enterprise objectives.

---

## Principle 3 – Clear Accountability

Ownership and responsibility shall be explicitly defined.

---

## Principle 4 – Controlled Decision Making

Important decisions shall follow defined governance processes.

---

## Principle 5 – Policy and Standard Alignment

Intelligence operation shall follow approved policies and standards.

---

## Principle 6 – Risk-Aware Governance

Governance decisions shall consider risk and impact.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Governance Evolution

Governance capability shall continuously improve.
