# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, and intelligence capabilities required for an **Intelligence Sentient Enterprise Architecture (ISENEA)**.

While EA-ISEA60 enables Enterprise Architecture to autonomously generate new architectural knowledge, EA-ISENEA61 enables Enterprise Intelligence to continuously maintain an internal understanding of its own operational state, architectural identity, strategic intent, organizational context, and long-term objectives.

The purpose is not to emulate human consciousness, but to establish a governed architectural capability for persistent enterprise self-awareness, contextual understanding, reflective reasoning, intentional adaptation, and continuous strategic alignment.

Enterprise Architecture shall therefore evolve from autonomous knowledge generation to continuously self-aware Enterprise Intelligence.
