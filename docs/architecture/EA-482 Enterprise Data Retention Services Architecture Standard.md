# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Retention Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Data Privacy Services principles are inherited from EA-481.

Enterprise Data Protection Services principles are inherited from EA-480.

Enterprise Data Lifecycle principles are inherited from EA-355.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Data Retention Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Retention Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of Enterprise data retention requirements across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Retention Services shall

- provide centralized retention management
- standardize retention policies
- ensure controlled data preservation
- support regulatory requirements
- support legal and business obligations
- reduce unnecessary data storage
- strengthen data lifecycle governance
- support privacy requirements
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure retention traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Retention Services shall provide trusted and reusable retention management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Data Retention Services Principles

Enterprise Data Retention Services shall follow these principles.

## Retention by Design

Retention capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Retention by Default

Enterprise data shall be managed according to approved retention policies, regulatory requirements and business obligations.

---

## Lifecycle-Driven Retention

Data retention shall be managed as an integrated part of the complete Enterprise Data Lifecycle.

---

## Minimum Necessary Retention

Enterprise data shall not be retained longer than required by approved business, legal or regulatory requirements.

---

## Governed Data Disposal

Data deletion and disposal shall be controlled, traceable and executed according to approved Enterprise policies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Retention Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed management of Enterprise data retention across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating retention policy management, retention classification, retention schedule management, data archiving, data preservation, legal hold services, data deletion, data disposal, retention compliance management, data lifecycle integration, data governance integration, privacy integration, data protection integration, encryption integration, identity integration, authentication integration, authorization integration, risk, audit, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables controlled, compliant and policy-driven data retention while maintaining resilience, transparency and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Retention Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise retention capabilities across the Enterprise.
