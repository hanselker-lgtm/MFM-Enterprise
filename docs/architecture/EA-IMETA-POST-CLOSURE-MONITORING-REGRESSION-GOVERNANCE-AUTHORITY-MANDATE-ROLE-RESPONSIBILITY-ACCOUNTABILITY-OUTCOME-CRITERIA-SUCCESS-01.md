## Purpose
Establish the authoritative success layer beneath outcome criteria, defining what success means, which required states must exist, what evidence demonstrates achievement, how success is measured, how partial success and failure are classified, and how success is accepted and reviewed.

## Core Principle
Success is a governed determination that the defined outcome has achieved its required conditions within approved tolerances and with sufficient evidence. Activity completion alone is not success.

```text
CRITERIA
   ↓
SUCCESS
   ↓
SUCCESS CONDITIONS
   ↓
REQUIRED STATE
   ↓
OBSERVABLE EVIDENCE
   ↓
MEASUREMENT
   ↓
PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL
   ↓
ACCEPTANCE
   ↓
REVIEW
```

## Success Quality Test
```text
DEFINED OUTCOME
+
APPROVED CRITERIA
+
CLEAR SUCCESS CONDITIONS
+
REQUIRED STATE
+
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
+
VALID MEASUREMENT
+
PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL RULES
+
ACCEPTANCE AUTHORITY
=
VALID GOVERNED SUCCESS
```

## Success Structure
| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Outcome | Result being evaluated |
| Criteria | Approved rules for evaluation |
| Success Condition | Condition that must be satisfied |
| Required State | State that must exist |
| Evidence | Proof supporting achievement |
| Measurement | Method for determining achievement |
| Pass Rule | Conditions for full success |
| Partial Rule | Conditions for partial success |
| Failure Rule | Conditions for failure |
| Tolerance | Permitted deviation |
| Acceptance | Governed determination |
| Escalation | Conditions requiring intervention |
| Review | Reassessment requirements |

## Success Definition
Success shall be defined as achievement of all mandatory success conditions and sufficient achievement of applicable conditional conditions within approved tolerance.

```text
SUCCESS
=
MANDATORY CONDITIONS SATISFIED
+
ACCEPTABLE CONDITIONAL CONDITIONS
+
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
+
WITHIN TOLERANCE```

## Success Conditions
Success conditions shall be observable, verifiable or objectively assessable. They shall describe the required state rather than merely the action taken to reach it.

```text
ACTIVITY
  ↓
REQUIRED STATE
  ↓
OBSERVABLE CONDITION
  ↓
EVIDENCE
  ↓
SUCCESS DETERMINATION```

## Required State
The required state is the condition the service, system, process, control or governance object must be in for success to exist.

Examples of required-state dimensions may include availability, integrity, security posture, resilience capability, compliance state, data quality, decision validity or operational readiness.

## Observable Evidence
Evidence shall demonstrate that the required state actually exists. Statements of intent, plans or activity logs alone shall not be sufficient where objective evidence is required.

```text
REQUIRED STATE
      ↓
OBSERVABLE EVIDENCE
      ↓
VERIFY
      ↓
ACCEPT / REJECT```

## Success Measurement
Measurement shall define source, method, frequency, quality, interpretation and treatment of uncertainty.

```text
DATA
 ↓
VALIDATE
 ↓
MEASURE
 ↓
COMPARE WITH CRITERIA
 ↓
CLASSIFY```

## Pass Criteria
Pass requires satisfaction of all mandatory conditions and achievement of the applicable success thresholds.

```text
ALL MANDATORY CONDITIONS?
 ├── NO  → NOT PASS
 └── YES
       ↓
THRESHOLDS SATISFIED?
 ├── NO  → PARTIAL / FAIL
 └── YES → PASS```

## Partial Success
Partial success shall be explicitly defined and shall not be silently treated as full success.

```text
RESULT
 ↓
FULL SUCCESS?
 ├── YES → PASS
 └── NO
      ↓
PARTIAL CONDITIONS SATISFIED?
       ├── YES → PARTIAL PASS
       └── NO  → FAIL```

Partial success shall include impact assessment and, where material, remediation or escalation.

## Failure Conditions
Failure shall include material unmet mandatory conditions, unacceptable risk, unsafe state, mandatory non-conformity, insufficient evidence where evidence is mandatory, or performance outside approved limits.

## Tolerance
Tolerance defines the permitted deviation within which success may still be recognized.

```text
EXPECTED SUCCESS
      ↓
ACCEPTABLE TOLERANCE
      ↓
WARNING / AT-RISK
      ↓
FAILURE THRESHOLD```

Tolerance shall not override mandatory security, safety, resilience or compliance obligations.

## Success Thresholds
Success thresholds shall be explicit, versioned and associated with consequences. Thresholds shall not be chosen solely for convenience of reporting.

## Security Success
Security success shall include applicable protection, detection, response, containment and control conditions. A result that meets operational targets but violates a mandatory security condition shall not be treated as full success.

## Resilience Success
Resilience success shall consider continuity, recovery, restoration, dependency behavior, capacity and service recovery objectives where applicable.

## Data Success
Data success shall consider required quality dimensions such as accuracy, completeness, integrity, timeliness, lineage, availability and authorized use.

## AI and Agent Success
AI and agent success shall require more than task completion.

```text
AI / AGENT TASK
      ↓
TASK COMPLETED?
      ↓
RESULT CORRECT?
      ↓
POLICY COMPLIANT?
      ↓
WITHIN AUTHORITY / AUTONOMY LIMITS?
      ↓
NO UNACCEPTABLE SIDE EFFECT?
      ↓
SUCCESS```

## Compliance Success
Compliance success requires satisfaction of applicable mandatory obligations. A business outcome shall not be declared successful if achieving it required an impermissible violation.

## Success Escalation
```text
SUCCESS DETERMINATION
      ↓
MANDATORY CONDITION FAILED?
 ├── NO  → CONTINUE
 └── YES → ESCALATE / REMEDIATE / REJECT```

Material partial success, repeated failure or systemic degradation shall also trigger escalation according to approved thresholds.

## Success Acceptance
Acceptance is the governed decision that the success determination is sufficient for the lifecycle state being entered.

```text
SUCCESS RESULT
      ↓
EVIDENCE COMPLETE?
      ↓
CRITERIA SATISFIED?
      ↓
AUTHORIZED ACCEPTANCE
      ↓
ACCEPTED / CONDITIONALLY ACCEPTED / REJECTED```

## Success Evidence
Evidence shall be attributable, relevant, current, sufficient and protected. The evidence set shall support both the success determination and any material exceptions.

## Success Reopening
If later evidence demonstrates that a previously accepted success state was invalid, the outcome may be reopened under governed reopening criteria.

```text
ACCEPTED SUCCESS
      ↓
NEW / CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE
      ↓
REASSESS
      ↓
VALID SUCCESS?
 ├── YES → RETAIN
 └── NO  → REOPEN / REMEDIATE / RE-DETERMINE```

## Success Review
Success definitions and conditions shall be reviewed after material regression, repeated partial success, changed criteria, baseline change, architecture change, risk change or evidence that the success definition no longer reflects the intended outcome.

```text
SUCCESS MODEL ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
STILL VALID?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → MODIFY / SUPERSEDE / RETIRE```

## Governance Learning
Repeated partial success or failure shall trigger review of outcome design, criteria quality, control effectiveness, measurement quality, architecture, responsibilities and governance assumptions.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes the success layer beneath outcome criteria for post-closure regression governance. It does not replace governance, authority, mandate, role, responsibility, accountability, outcome or criteria layers.
