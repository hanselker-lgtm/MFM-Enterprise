# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Platform Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational platform architecture that ensures Intelligence platforms are designed, deployed, operated, monitored and continuously improved.

The Intelligence Platform Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- platform operating principles
- platform design frameworks
- infrastructure structures
- application platform models
- data platform structures
- integration models
- platform operation models
- performance models
- monitoring structures
- automation models
- evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for reliable, scalable and continuously improving Intelligence platform capabilities.

---

The Intelligence Platform Operating Architecture Framework covers:

# 3. Intelligence Platform Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Platform Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Platform by Design

Platforms shall be designed according to defined enterprise and Intelligence requirements.

---

## Principle 2 – Scalability

Platforms shall support future growth and changing requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Reliability

Platforms shall provide stable and dependable operation.

---

## Principle 4 – Security by Design

Platform security shall be integrated from the beginning.

---

## Principle 5 – Operational Transparency

Platform condition and performance shall be measurable.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Optimisation

Platforms shall continuously improve through measurement and learning.

---

## Principle 7 – Platform Evolution

Platforms shall adapt to future technology and business needs.
