# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational resilience architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities can continue, recover and evolve under changing conditions.

The Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- resilience operating principles
- continuity frameworks
- recovery structures
- fault tolerance models
- availability models
- adaptation resilience models
- crisis management structures
- operational recovery models
- resilience monitoring structures
- resilience automation structures
- resilience evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for robust, adaptive and continuously available Intelligence operations.

---

# 3. Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

Resilience shall be integrated into Intelligence capabilities from the beginning.

## Principle 2 – Continuous Availability

Critical Intelligence capabilities shall maintain operational availability.

## Principle 3 – Adaptive Recovery

Recovery processes shall adapt to changing conditions.

---

## Principle 4 – Fault Tolerance

Intelligence capabilities shall withstand failures.

---

## Principle 5 – Operational Continuity

Essential Intelligence services shall continue during disruption.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Resilience Improvement

Resilience capability shall continuously improve.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolutionary Resilience

Resilience shall evolve with Intelligence capability.
