# 1. Purpose

POST-N9-005 activates the governance baseline established through POST-N9-003 and validated through POST-N9-004.

The purpose is to move from:

```text
DEFINED
↓
STRUCTURALLY VALIDATED
↓
ACTIVATED
```

without claiming that the governance model is already proven effective.

Activation establishes:

```text
Governance Ownership
Review Cadence
Evidence Capture
Condition Tracking
Escalation Routing
Initial Operating Records
```

---

# 8. Decision Authority

Decision authority shall be explicitly identified for material decisions.

The following distinction remains mandatory:

```text
Participant
≠
Owner

Owner
≠
Decision Authority

Decision Authority
≠
Funding Authority
```

No authority shall be inferred from participation.
