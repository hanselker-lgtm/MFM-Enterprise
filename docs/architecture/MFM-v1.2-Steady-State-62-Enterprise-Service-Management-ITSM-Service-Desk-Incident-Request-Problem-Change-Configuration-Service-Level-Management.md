# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the sixty-second document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-61 – Enterprise Business Continuity, Operational Resilience, Disaster Recovery, Crisis Management & Emergency Response.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for service management, IT service management, service desk operations, incident management, service request management, problem management, change management, configuration management, service-level management, service catalogue management, knowledge management, service monitoring, event management, availability management, capacity management, release management, deployment coordination, service continuity integration, service reporting, service assurance, service findings, exceptions, remediation, service metrics, service dashboards, service maturity and continual service improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must deliver stable, measurable, supportable and continuously improving services through controlled service-management processes, clear ownership, effective support, disciplined change and reliable service-level governance.**

# 3. Service Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Deliver reliable services that meet agreed business and user requirements.**

# 4. Service Desk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide an effective and accessible point of contact for users requiring support, incident resolution and service fulfilment.**

# 5. Incident Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore normal service operation as quickly and safely as possible while minimizing business impact.**

# 6. Request Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Fulfil authorized standard service requests consistently, efficiently and transparently.**

# 7. Problem Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and address underlying causes of recurring or significant incidents and reduce future service disruption.**

# 8. Change Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable necessary change while controlling risk to service stability, security, compliance and continuity.**

# 9. Configuration Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain reliable information about service components and their relationships to support operational control and decision-making.**

# 10. Service Level Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Define, measure and continuously improve service performance against agreed requirements.**

# 112. Definition of Ready

A service, incident, request, problem, change, release, configuration item, service-level agreement, finding, exception or improvement item is Ready when scope, owner, service, priority, dependencies, risk, acceptance criteria, target and evidence requirements are defined.

# 113. Definition of Done

A service-management work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement / Event Identified
        ↓
Owner Assigned
        ↓
Service Action Completed
        ↓
Validation Completed
        ↓
Records / Configuration / Knowledge Updated where Required
        ↓
Evidence Captured
        ↓
Findings / Exceptions Addressed
        ↓
Outcome Accepted
```

# 114. Final Service Principle

> **MFM must deliver stable, measurable, supportable and continuously improving services through controlled service-management processes.**

# 115. Final Incident Principle

> **Incidents must be prioritized by business impact and resolved as quickly and safely as possible.**

# 116. Final Problem Principle

> **Recurring and significant incidents must drive investigation of underlying causes and permanent improvement.**

# 117. Final Change Principle

> **Necessary change must be enabled without creating unacceptable risk to service stability, security, compliance or continuity.**

# 118. Final Configuration Principle

> **Configuration information must remain sufficiently accurate and current to support operational decision-making and service control.**

# 119. Final Service Level Principle

> **Service performance must be measured against agreed business-relevant targets and continuously improved.**

# 120. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material service-management processes and controls must be supported by reliable evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

# 121. Final Improvement Principle

> **Incidents, problems, changes, service-level results, customer feedback, findings and lessons learned must continuously improve MFM services.**

# 122. Final Integration Principle

> **Service Management must integrate with Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Enterprise Risk, Cybersecurity, Data, Privacy, Applications, Infrastructure, Suppliers, Finance, People, Projects and Enterprise Architecture.**
