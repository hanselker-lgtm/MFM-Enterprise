## Purpose
Establish the authoritative applicability layer for mandatory and non-negotiable requirements. This document defines how the architecture determines whether a requirement applies, records the decision, preserves evidence, prevents convenience-based exclusions, and governs reassessment when circumstances change.

## Core Principle
Applicability is a governed determination, not an assumption. A requirement shall be treated as applicable whenever its approved applicability conditions are satisfied. A requirement shall not be declared not applicable merely because compliance is inconvenient, costly, delayed or operationally difficult.

```text
NON-NEGOTIABLE REQUIREMENT
        ↓
APPLICABILITY RULE
        ↓
CONTEXT / CONDITIONS
        ↓
APPLICABILITY TEST
        ↓
APPLICABLE / NOT APPLICABLE / UNDETERMINED
        ↓
EVIDENCE
        ↓
ENFORCEMENT / ESCALATION
```

## Applicability Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Requirement | Requirement being assessed |
| Source | Authority establishing the requirement |
| Rule | Approved applicability logic |
| Context | Relevant system, service, process, event or condition |
| Test | Method used to determine applicability |
| Result | Applicable, N/A, conditional or undetermined |
| Authority | Role authorized to make the determination |
| Evidence | Proof supporting the decision |
| Effective Date | When the decision applies |
| Reassessment | Trigger for reassessment |
| Escalation | Response to ambiguity or dispute |

## Applicability Governance

Applicability shall be governed at the same level of rigor as the requirement itself. A mandatory requirement shall not become optional because its applicability has been left undocumented.

```text
REQUIREMENT
   ↓
APPLICABILITY GOVERNANCE
   ↓
RULE
   ↓
DECISION
   ↓
EVIDENCE```

## Applicability Source

Every applicability rule shall identify its source. Sources may include mandate, policy, architecture principle, legal obligation, contract, risk decision, security requirement, resilience requirement or approved outcome criterion.

## Applicability Scope

Scope shall define which services, systems, environments, processes, roles, data classes, transactions, events, locations or lifecycle states are covered.

```text
SCOPE
 ├── SERVICE
 ├── SYSTEM
 ├── PROCESS
 ├── DATA
 ├── ROLE
 ├── EVENT
 └── LIFECYCLE STATE```

## Applicability Criteria

Criteria shall be objective enough to produce repeatable determinations. Where judgment is unavoidable, the decision framework and authority shall be explicit.

## Applicability Decision

```text
CONTEXT
   ↓
APPLICABILITY TEST
   ↓
RESULT
 ├── APPLICABLE
 ├── NOT APPLICABLE
 ├── CONDITIONALLY APPLICABLE
 └── UNDETERMINED```

An undetermined result shall not be silently converted to not applicable.

## Applicability Evidence

Evidence shall show the context assessed, rule applied, date/time, decision authority, result and supporting facts. Material decisions shall be reproducible from the evidence.

## Applicability Timing

Applicability may change over time. Effective dates, triggering events and lifecycle transitions shall be defined where timing affects the requirement.

## Applicability Dependencies

Where applicability depends on another service, system, data source, role, event or external condition, the dependency shall be explicit and its failure behavior governed.

## Security Applicability

Security requirements shall be assessed against relevant assets, threats, interfaces, identities, data, environments and exposure conditions.

```text
SECURITY CONTEXT
      ↓
APPLICABILITY TEST
      ↓
APPLICABLE?
 ├── YES → ENFORCE
 └── NO  → RECORD BASIS```

## Resilience Applicability

Resilience applicability shall consider service criticality, recovery requirements, dependencies, continuity needs, degraded modes and disruption scenarios.

## Compliance Applicability

Compliance applicability shall consider the obligation source, jurisdiction or governing context, service scope, data type, activity and applicable lifecycle state. Where applicability is legally ambiguous, the issue shall be escalated rather than assumed away.

## Data Applicability

Data requirements shall be assessed according to data classification, sensitivity, integrity requirements, lifecycle state, location, access pattern and authorized use.

## AI and Agent Applicability

AI and agent requirements shall consider the model or agent role, action authority, tool access, data access, autonomy level, environment and consequence of action.

```text
AI / AGENT CONTEXT
       ↓
ACTION / AUTHORITY TEST
       ↓
REQUIREMENT APPLICABLE?
 ├── YES → ENFORCE
 └── NO  → RECORD BASIS```

Technical capability shall not be treated as evidence of non-applicability.

## Conditional Applicability

Conditionally applicable requirements shall have explicit activation criteria.

```text
REQUIREMENT
   ↓
TRIGGER CONDITION?
 ├── NO  → NOT ACTIVE
 └── YES → APPLICABLE```

Activation conditions shall be observable or otherwise governed.

## Exclusion and N/A

Not-applicable and exclusion decisions shall identify the exact rule that makes the requirement inapplicable. Broad statements such as 'not relevant' are insufficient for material requirements.

```text
NOT APPLICABLE
      ↓
WHY?
      ↓
RULE / FACT / EVIDENCE
      ↓
AUTHORIZED DECISION```

## Applicability Override

An applicability decision shall not be overridden locally without explicit authority. An override shall be logged, justified, time-bounded where appropriate and subject to review.

```text
N/A DECISION
    ↓
OVERRIDE REQUEST
    ↓
AUTHORIZED?
 ├── NO  → RETAIN N/A DECISION / ESCALATE
 └── YES → GOVERNED OVERRIDE```

## Applicability Escalation

```text
APPLICABILITY UNCERTAIN / DISPUTED
        ↓
MATERIAL IMPACT?
 ├── NO  → RESOLVE / DOCUMENT
 └── YES → ESCALATE TO AUTHORIZED GOVERNANCE```

Where uncertainty could result in an unprotected material risk, the safer governed interpretation shall be used pending authoritative determination.

## Applicability Continuity

Applicability determinations for critical requirements shall remain available during organizational or system disruption. Backup roles and preserved decision records shall support continuity.

## Applicability Reassessment

Reassessment shall occur after material changes to architecture, service scope, data, threat, regulation, mandate, ownership, environment, AI autonomy or lifecycle state.

```text
CURRENT APPLICABILITY
        ↓
CONTEXT CHANGE
        ↓
REASSESS
        ↓
NEW DETERMINATION
        ↓
UPDATE / RETAIN```

## Applicability Review and Learning

Repeated disputes, incorrect N/A decisions, unexpected control gaps or recurring late applicability discovery shall trigger review of the applicability rules themselves.

```text
APPLICABILITY ACTIVE
        ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
        ↓
RULE STILL VALID?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → MODIFY / SUPERSEDE / RETIRE```
