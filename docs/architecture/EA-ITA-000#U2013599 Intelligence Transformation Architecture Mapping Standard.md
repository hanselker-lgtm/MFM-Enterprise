# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Transformation Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive transformation architecture for planning, governing, executing and evolving Enterprise Intelligence transformation initiatives.

The Intelligence Transformation Architecture Framework shall provide:

- transformation principles
- transformation governance
- transformation architecture
- transformation portfolios
- transformation roadmaps
- change management
- initiative management
- transition architecture
- migration planning
- transformation measurement
- transformation maturity
- transformation evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence transformation is executed in a structured, controlled and value-driven manner.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Transformation Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture transformation domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Transformation Model
- Transformation Governance
- Transformation Architecture
- Transformation Portfolio Management
- Transformation Roadmaps
- Change Management
- Initiative Management
- Transition Architecture
- Migration Planning
- Transformation Measurement
- Transformation Maturity
- Transformation Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- digital transformation initiatives
- intelligence-driven transformation
- future adaptive enterprises

---

# 3. Intelligence Transformation Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Transformation Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategic Alignment

Transformation shall support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Controlled Transformation

Transformation activities shall be governed and managed.

---

## Principle 3 – Value-Driven Transformation

Transformation shall create measurable Enterprise value.

---

## Principle 4 – Architecture-Based Transformation

Transformation shall follow Enterprise Architecture principles.

---

## Principle 5 – Incremental Evolution

Transformation shall enable controlled progression.

---

## Principle 6 – Change Enablement

Transformation shall include organisational adoption.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Transformation

Transformation shall continuously evolve with changing requirements.
