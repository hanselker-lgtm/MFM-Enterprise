# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the twenty-ninth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-28 – Enterprise Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Resilience, Crisis Management & Recovery Governance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for service management, IT service operations, service desk, incident management, problem management, change management, configuration management, service requests, event management, service levels, knowledge management and operational improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM services must be delivered, supported, changed, monitored and restored through controlled, measurable and accountable service-management and operational processes.**

# 124. Definition of Ready

A service-management or operational work item is Ready when:

- The service, incident, problem, change, release, configuration, request, event, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, impact, priority, dependencies, risk, service level and applicable requirements are known.
- Implementation, support, testing, rollback, evidence, communication and acceptance requirements are defined.

---

# 125. Definition of Done

A service-management or operational work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement Identified
        ↓
Owner / Resolver Assigned
        ↓
Service / Operational Action Completed
        ↓
Technical / Business / Security / Privacy Validation Completed
        ↓
Service / Configuration / Knowledge / Records Updated
        ↓
Evidence Captured
        ↓
Exceptions / Findings Addressed
        ↓
Outcome Accepted
```
