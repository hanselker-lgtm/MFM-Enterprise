# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Data Retention throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Retention ensures that enterprise data is retained, archived and disposed of according to business requirements, legal obligations and governance policies while preserving traceability, operational integrity and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Data Classification
- Data Retention Policies
- Data Archiving
- Legal Hold
- Secure Data Disposal
- Data Retention Reporting
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## RET-001

Provide standardized enterprise data retention.

---

## RET-002

Protect enterprise information assets.

## RET-003

Ensure regulatory and legal compliance.

## RET-004

Support secure information lifecycle management.

---

## RET-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Data Retention Principles

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall follow these principles.

- Information Lifecycle Management
- Risk-Based Retention
- Secure Archiving
- Secure Disposal
- Complete Traceability
- Regulatory Compliance
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Data Retention implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

Enterprise Data Retention shall provide

- secure disposal management
- reporting

- compliance verification

# 8. Data Classification

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall implement standardized data classification.

Data classification shall

- identify business data
- identify confidential data
- identify regulated data
- identify archival data
- preserve classification traceability
- maintain classification consistency

Data classification shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 9. Retention Policies

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall implement standardized retention policies.

Retention policies shall

- define retention periods
- define retention responsibilities
- define regulatory requirements
- define business retention requirements
- preserve policy traceability
- maintain policy consistency

Retention policies shall align with enterprise governance requirements.

---

# 10. Data Archiving

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall implement standardized data archiving.

Data archiving shall

- define archive procedures
- define archive storage
- define archive accessibility
- define archive integrity requirements
- preserve archive traceability
- maintain archive consistency

Archived information shall remain protected throughout its retention period.

---

# 11. Legal Hold

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall implement standardized legal hold management.

Legal hold management shall

- identify legal hold requirements
- suspend scheduled disposal
- preserve affected information
- document legal hold decisions
- preserve legal hold traceability
- support regulatory compliance

Legal hold procedures shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Secure Data Disposal

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall implement standardized secure disposal.

Secure data disposal shall

- define disposal authorization
- define disposal procedures
- verify secure destruction
- preserve disposal traceability
- maintain disposal consistency
- support regulatory compliance

Secure disposal shall be performed only after retention obligations have been fulfilled.

---

# 13. Retention Verification

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall implement standardized retention verification.

Retention verification shall

- verify retention compliance
- verify archive integrity
- verify legal hold compliance
- preserve verification traceability
- maintain verification consistency
- support operational governance

Retention verification shall be performed regularly.

---

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall handle data retention-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify data classification failures
- classify retention policy failures
- classify archive management failures
- classify legal hold failures
- classify secure disposal failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Data Retention exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, information integrity, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Data Retention implementations may depend upon

- approved backup management services
- approved data governance services
- approved storage infrastructure
- approved security services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external data retention providers

Data Retention capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Data Retention implementation is compliant when

- Data classification is documented.
- Retention policies are maintained.
- Archive procedures are implemented.
- Legal hold procedures are documented.
- Secure disposal procedures are implemented.
- Retention verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Data Classification

Enterprise information shall never exist without an approved data classification.

---

## Undefined Retention Policies

Protected information shall never remain without documented retention requirements.

---

## Uncontrolled Archiving

Information shall never be archived outside approved archive management procedures.

---

## Unauthorized Data Disposal

Enterprise information shall never be destroyed without documented authorization and verification.

---

## Ignored Legal Hold

Information subject to legal hold shall never be modified, archived or disposed of contrary to approved legal hold procedures.

---

## Data Retention Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent data retention mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Data Retention services.

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Data Retention Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Data Retention throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise information is retained, archived and securely disposed of according to business requirements, legal obligations and governance policies while preserving traceability, operational integrity and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Data Retention implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
