# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Recovery Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Retention Architecture principles are inherited from EA-433.

All Enterprise Data Recovery implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Recovery Architecture governing secure, resilient and controlled recovery of Enterprise information and supporting services following operational failures, cyber incidents, human errors, infrastructure failures and disaster events.

Enterprise Data Recovery shall

- restore Enterprise information
- minimize business disruption
- support operational resilience
- achieve defined recovery objectives
- preserve information integrity
- strengthen cyber resilience
- improve recovery readiness
- support business continuity
- enable automation
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Recovery shall provide a standardized architectural framework for trusted restoration of Enterprise information and services.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- database recovery
- application recovery
- file recovery
- cloud recovery
- storage recovery
- infrastructure recovery
- virtual environment recovery
- disaster recovery environments
- enterprise recovery services
- cross-platform recovery

The standard applies regardless of infrastructure platform, cloud provider, storage technology or deployment model.

# 3. Enterprise Data Recovery Principles

Enterprise Data Recovery shall be governed by the following principles.

## Recovery by Design

Enterprise information and services shall be recoverable within approved business recovery objectives.

---

## Resilience by Design

Recovery capabilities shall maximize Enterprise operational resilience and service continuity.

---

## Secure Recovery

Recovery operations shall protect Enterprise information against unauthorized access, corruption and compromise.

---

## Metadata-Driven Recovery

Recovery planning and execution shall be governed through Enterprise metadata and standardized recovery policies.

---

## Reusable Recovery Services

Recovery capabilities shall be reusable across Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Recovery shall remain independent of recovery technologies, infrastructure vendors and cloud providers.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Recovery Architecture establishes the standardized framework for secure, resilient and controlled restoration of Enterprise information and supporting services following operational failures, cyber incidents, human errors and disaster events.

By integrating Enterprise Data Governance, Backup & Recovery, Information Lifecycle Management, Security, Observability, Automation, Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity capabilities, the Enterprise ensures trusted recovery services that minimize downtime, preserve information integrity and restore critical business operations across all Business and Technology Domains.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Recovery initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing Enterprise recovery capabilities.
