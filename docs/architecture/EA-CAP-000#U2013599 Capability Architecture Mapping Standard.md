# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Capability Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise capabilities as strategic assets.

The Capability Architecture Framework shall provide:

- capability principles
- capability governance
- capability strategy
- capability structures
- capability domains
- capability mapping
- capability assessment
- capability maturity management
- capability development
- capability improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise capabilities are identified, understood and developed in alignment with strategic objectives, architecture direction and business value.

---

# 2. Scope

The Capability Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture capability domains:

- Enterprise Capability Model
- Capability Governance
- Capability Strategy
- Capability Domains
- Capability Mapping
- Capability Assessment
- Capability Maturity
- Capability Development
- Capability Roadmaps
- Capability Performance
- Capability Evolution

The framework applies to:

- business capabilities
- operational capabilities
- digital capabilities
- technology capabilities
- organizational capabilities
- service capabilities
- transformation capabilities

---

# 3. Enterprise Capability Architecture Principles

The Capability Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Capability-Based Thinking

Enterprise planning shall focus on the capabilities required to achieve strategic outcomes.

---

## Principle 2 – Strategic Capability Alignment

Capabilities shall directly support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 3 – Capability Transparency

Enterprise capabilities shall be identified, documented and understood.

---

## Principle 4 – Capability Ownership

Every capability shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Principle 5 – Capability Evolution

Capabilities shall continuously develop according to changing Enterprise requirements.

---

## Principle 6 – Capability Integration

Capabilities shall be connected with processes, services, applications, technology and resources.

---

## Principle 7 – Value-Oriented Capability Development

Capability improvements shall focus on measurable business value.

---

# 9. Capability Domain Architecture

The Capability Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise capabilities are structured into logical domains to support strategic planning, governance, investment and development.

Capability domains provide a common structure for organizing Enterprise abilities and ensuring alignment between business needs and architecture development.

---

# 9.1 Capability Domain Principles

Capability domains shall ensure:

- clear capability organization
- strategic alignment
- ownership transparency
- capability visibility
- continuous capability improvement
