# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Policy Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Decision Services principles are inherited from EA-463.

Enterprise Rules Services principles are inherited from EA-464.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Policy Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Policy Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and governed policy management across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Policy Services shall

- provide centralized policy management
- standardize policy enforcement
- support reusable enterprise policies
- improve governance
- strengthen regulatory compliance
- enable enterprise automation
- support interoperability
- improve operational consistency
- reduce implementation complexity
- support continuous optimization

Enterprise Policy Services shall provide trusted and reusable policy capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise policy services
- policy repositories
- policy authoring services
- policy validation services
- policy distribution services
- policy enforcement services
- policy lifecycle management
- cloud services
- hybrid environments
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Policy Services Principles

Enterprise Policy Services shall follow these principles.

## Centralized Policy Governance

Enterprise policies shall be centrally governed, approved, version controlled and managed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Secure by Default

Every policy definition, modification and enforcement activity shall be authenticated, authorized, audited and continuously monitored.

---

## Reusable Policy Services

Enterprise policies shall be reusable across business processes, workflows, decision services, rules services, APIs and Enterprise applications.

---

## Metadata Driven Policies

Enterprise policies shall be described, classified and governed using Enterprise metadata to support traceability, discoverability and lifecycle management.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Policy Services shall remain independent of vendors, policy engines, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Policy Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed management of Enterprise policies across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating policy authoring, validation, repositories, distribution, enforcement, lifecycle management, metadata, event integration, decision services, rules services, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables consistent, transparent and reusable policy governance while maintaining compliance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Policy Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise policy capabilities across the Enterprise.

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Policy Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted policy authoring, autonomous policy optimization, semantic-aware policy discovery, adaptive compliance enforcement, self-validating policy repositories, decentralized policy ecosystems, intelligent governance analytics and quantum-safe Enterprise policy infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Policy Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven policy capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
