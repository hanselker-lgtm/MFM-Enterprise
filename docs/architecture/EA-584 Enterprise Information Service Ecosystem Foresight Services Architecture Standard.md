Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Foresight Services shall follow these principles.

Enterprise foresight shall be deliberately developed to understand future conditions.

---

## Strategic Anticipation

Enterprise shall identify possible future developments before they impact current capabilities.

---

Future assessments shall be supported by evidence, analysis and relevant insight.

---

Enterprise foresight shall consider multiple possible future scenarios.

---

## Foresight Ownership

Foresight domains shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Continuous Scanning

Enterprise shall continuously monitor emerging developments.

---

## Adaptive Planning

Enterprise planning shall adapt based on foresight outcomes.

---

## Governed Foresight Management
