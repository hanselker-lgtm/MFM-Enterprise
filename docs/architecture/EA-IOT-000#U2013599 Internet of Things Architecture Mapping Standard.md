# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Internet of Things Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise Internet of Things capabilities.

The IoT Architecture Framework shall provide:

- IoT principles
- IoT governance
- IoT strategy
- IoT capability architecture
- device architecture
- sensor architecture
- edge computing architecture
- connectivity architecture
- IoT data architecture
- IoT platform architecture
- IoT security architecture
- IoT operations
- IoT analytics
- IoT performance management
- IoT evolution

The framework ensures that connected devices, physical environments and digital services are integrated through secure, scalable and intelligent IoT capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

The IoT Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture IoT domains:

- Enterprise IoT Model
- IoT Governance
- IoT Strategy
- IoT Capability Architecture
- Device Architecture
- Sensor Architecture
- Edge Computing Architecture
- Connectivity Architecture
- IoT Data Architecture
- IoT Platform Architecture
- IoT Security Architecture
- IoT Operations
- IoT Analytics
- IoT Performance
- IoT Maturity
- IoT Roadmaps
- IoT Evolution

The framework applies to:

- connected devices
- industrial IoT environments
- sensor networks
- smart assets
- edge computing environments
- connected operational systems
- intelligent monitoring solutions
- digital twin integrations

---

# 3. Enterprise IoT Architecture Principles

The IoT Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Connected Intelligence

IoT capabilities shall enable intelligent connection between physical and digital environments.

---

## Principle 2 – Secure Connectivity

All connected devices and communication channels shall be protected through security-by-design.

---

## Principle 3 – Data Value Creation

IoT data shall be transformed into useful operational and business insights.

---

## Principle 4 – Device Lifecycle Management

IoT devices shall be managed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 – Interoperability

IoT solutions shall integrate with Enterprise platforms, services and systems.

---

## Principle 6 – Scalability

IoT architectures shall support growth in devices, data volumes and services.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

IoT capabilities shall continuously evolve according to business and technology requirements.

---

# 21. IoT Governance Operating Model
