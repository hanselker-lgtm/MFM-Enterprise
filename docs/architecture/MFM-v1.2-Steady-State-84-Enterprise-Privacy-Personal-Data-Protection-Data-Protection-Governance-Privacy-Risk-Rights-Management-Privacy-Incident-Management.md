# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eighty-fourth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-83 – Enterprise Data Governance, Data Management, Data Quality, Metadata, Master Data, Data Lifecycle & Data Protection Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for privacy governance, personal-data protection, privacy strategy, privacy principles, privacy roles and accountability, processing governance, records of processing, data protection principles, lawful processing coordination, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, privacy by design, privacy by default, transparency, privacy notices, consent where applicable, data subject rights, access requests, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, automated decision-making coordination, privacy risk management, data protection impact assessments, processor governance, subprocessor governance, third-country and international transfer considerations, privacy incidents, personal-data breach coordination, privacy assurance, privacy findings, privacy exceptions, privacy remediation, privacy metrics, privacy dashboards, privacy maturity and continual privacy improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must protect personal data and privacy rights through accountable, transparent, risk-based and lifecycle-controlled privacy governance.**

# 3. Privacy Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish clear accountability and controls for the lawful, fair, transparent and responsible processing of personal data.**

# 4. Personal Data Protection Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, disclosure, alteration and unnecessary retention.**

# 5. Privacy Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and manage privacy risks before they create unacceptable impact to individuals or the organization.**

# 6. Rights Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide controlled and timely mechanisms for responding to applicable data subject rights.**

# 7. Privacy Incident Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Detect, assess, contain and resolve privacy incidents and personal-data breaches through coordinated response and appropriate notification processes.**

# 8. Privacy Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide evidence that material privacy requirements, controls and obligations are operating effectively.**

# 12. Transparency Principle

Individuals should receive appropriate information about relevant processing in clear and understandable form.

# 13. Data Minimization Principle

Only personal data reasonably required for an identified purpose should be collected and processed.

# 14. Purpose Limitation Principle

Personal data should be processed for defined and legitimate purposes and not used incompatibly without an appropriate basis.

# 15. Accuracy Principle

Personal data should be maintained with appropriate accuracy for the intended processing.

# 16. Storage Limitation Principle

Personal data should not be retained longer than justified by applicable requirements and legitimate business purposes.

# 17. Security Principle

Personal data should receive proportionate technical and organizational protection.

# 111. Final Privacy Governance Principle

> **MFM must protect personal data and privacy rights through accountable, transparent, risk-based and lifecycle-controlled privacy governance.**

# 112. Final Lawfulness Principle

> **Personal-data processing must have an appropriate lawful basis or other applicable legal justification and must remain within its defined purpose.**

# 113. Final Minimization Principle

> **Only personal data reasonably required for an identified purpose should be collected and processed.**

# 114. Final Transparency Principle

> **Individuals must receive appropriate, clear and understandable information about relevant processing and applicable rights.**

# 115. Final Rights Principle

> **Applicable data subject rights must be supported through controlled, accountable and evidence-based processes.**

# 116. Final Privacy by Design Principle

> **Privacy requirements must be integrated into the design, implementation, operation and change of systems, services and processes involving personal data.**

# 117. Final Processor Principle

> **Third parties processing personal data must be appropriately assessed, contractually governed and monitored according to privacy risk.**

# 118. Final Incident Principle

> **Potential personal-data breaches must be assessed, contained, investigated and escalated through coordinated privacy, security, legal and business processes.**

# 119. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material privacy controls and decisions must be supported by reliable evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

# 120. Final Improvement Principle

> **Privacy incidents, rights requests, DPIAs, assessments, findings and changes in processing must continuously improve MFM's privacy capability.**

# 121. Final Integration Principle

> **Privacy Management must integrate with Data Management, Cybersecurity, Identity, Enterprise Architecture, Applications, Infrastructure, Workplace, Service Management, Suppliers, Legal, Compliance, Risk, Finance and Business Continuity.**

# 122. Final Steady-State Privacy Principle

> **MFM must protect personal data and privacy rights through accountable, transparent, risk-based and lifecycle-controlled privacy governance.**
