# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Master Data Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Master Data Management ensures that authoritative master data is created, governed, maintained, synchronized and protected consistently across the enterprise while preserving accuracy, integrity, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Master Data Domains
- Master Data Ownership
- Master Data Governance
- Master Data Lifecycle
- Master Data Quality
- Master Data Synchronization
- Master Data Distribution
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Master Data Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## MDM-001

Provide standardized enterprise master data management.

---

## MDM-002

Ensure a single authoritative source for enterprise master data.

---

## MDM-003

Maintain master data quality, consistency and integrity.

---

## MDM-004

Support regulatory and architectural compliance.

---

## MDM-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Master Data Management Principles

Enterprise Master Data Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Single Source of Truth
- Master Data by Design
- Standardized Data Domains
- Controlled Data Ownership
- Complete Traceability
- Data Quality First
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Master Data Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

Enterprise Master Data Management shall provide

- reporting

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Master Data Management implementations shall handle master data management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify master data domain failures
- classify master data lifecycle failures
- classify master data quality failures
- classify synchronization failures
- classify distribution failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Master Data Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, master data integrity, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Master Data Management implementations may depend upon

- approved metadata management services
- approved knowledge management services
- approved data governance services
- approved integration services
- approved security services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Master Data Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external master data providers

Master Data Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Master Data Management implementation is compliant when

- Master data domains are documented.
- Master data lifecycle management is implemented.
- Master data quality is continuously monitored.
- Synchronization procedures are implemented.
- Distribution procedures are controlled.
- Master data verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Master Data Ownership

Authoritative master data shall never exist without documented ownership and stewardship.

---

## Duplicate Master Data Sources

Multiple competing authoritative sources for the same master data domain shall never exist.

---

## Poor Master Data Quality

Master data shall never be distributed without validation for completeness, consistency and accuracy.

---

## Uncontrolled Synchronization

Master data synchronization shall never occur outside approved enterprise integration mechanisms.

---

## Unauthorized Master Data Distribution

Master data shall never be distributed without documented authorization and governance approval.

---

## Master Data Management Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent master data management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Master Data Management services.

---

- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Master Data Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Master Data Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that authoritative master data is created, governed, maintained, synchronized and protected consistently across the enterprise while preserving accuracy, integrity, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Master Data Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
