# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Master Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of authoritative Enterprise information across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Master Management Services shall

- provide centralized master information management
- establish authoritative information sources
- manage master information ownership
- support golden record management
- standardize reference information
- improve information consistency
- improve information quality
- support business processes
- strengthen information governance
- reduce duplicate information
- support regulatory requirements
- support lifecycle governance
- support privacy and security requirements
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure information traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Master Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable master information capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Information Master Management Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed management of authoritative Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating master information governance, master data management integration, golden record management, authoritative source management, reference information management, master information quality management, master information synchronization, master information distribution services, master information lifecycle management, ownership integration, stewardship integration, metadata integration, information quality integration, data governance integration, privacy integration, security integration, compliance integration, risk integration, audit integration, identity integration, authorization integration, event integration, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables consistent, trusted and policy-driven master information management while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Information Master Management Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise master information capabilities across the Enterprise.
