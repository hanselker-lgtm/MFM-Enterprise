# 2. Purpose

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-473 establishes the portfolio-level coordination layer above the execution-control architecture defined by RG-472.

RG-472 governs the controlled execution of an individual strategic adaptation and its cross-domain change cascade.

RG-473 governs the enterprise when multiple strategic transformations are active simultaneously and compete for the same people, capital, technology, suppliers, decision capacity, operational windows and organisational attention.

The architecture SHALL answer:

> **How does the enterprise synchronise multiple concurrent transformations, resolve cross-change dependencies and collisions, prioritise limited capacity, protect strategic value and resilience, coordinate benefit interlocks, and continuously rebalance execution as conditions change?**

# 3. Core Principle

> **The enterprise SHALL optimise the transformation portfolio as an interconnected system rather than as a collection of independent projects; priority, sequence and capacity SHALL therefore be continuously evaluated against strategic value, dependencies, resilience, benefits, risk, reversibility and execution constraints.**

# 157. Acceptance Criteria

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-473 is accepted when:

- the transformation portfolio is represented as an interconnected system;
- active, queued, paused and completed transformations are visible;
- strategic alignment and priority criteria are explicit;
- multi-dimensional priority and trade-offs remain visible;
- shared dependencies and critical paths are identifiable;
- dependency collisions and cascades can be detected;
- capacity pools, contention and saturation are governed;
- WIP limits can be established and changed through authority;
- sequencing can account for dependency, capacity, value, risk and operating windows;
- benefit interlocks, synergy and cannibalisation are visible;
- portfolio risk correlation and concentration can be assessed;
- portfolio shocks trigger adaptive rebalancing;
- transformations can be reprioritised, resequenced, accelerated, paused, deferred or stopped under governance;
- portfolio decision and action latency are measurable;
- resilience floors remain protected;
- historical portfolio states and decisions remain reconstructable;
- AI assistance remains bounded and explainable;
- manual fallback exists;
- negative and scenario tests prevent uncontrolled priority changes, hidden dependencies and capacity overload.
