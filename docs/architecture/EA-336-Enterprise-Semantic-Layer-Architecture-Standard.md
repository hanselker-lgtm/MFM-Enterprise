# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to define the Enterprise principles governing semantic abstraction throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Semantic Layer shall

- provide a unified semantic abstraction
- standardize business terminology
- improve interoperability
- simplify data consumption
- support Artificial Intelligence
- enable semantic consistency
- remain technology independent

The Enterprise Semantic Layer shall provide a common understanding of Enterprise information regardless of physical storage technologies.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every Enterprise Semantic Layer implementation throughout the Enterprise Platform.

It governs

- semantic abstraction
- business vocabulary
- semantic APIs
- metadata integration
- ontology integration
- semantic governance
- interoperability
- lifecycle management

The standard applies independently of storage technologies, semantic engines and application platforms.

# 21. Enterprise Semantic Layer Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Duplicate Business Terminology

Multiple conflicting definitions for the same business concept shall never exist.

The Canonical Business Vocabulary shall remain authoritative.

---

## Application-Specific Semantics

Applications shall never define their own independent business terminology where Enterprise semantic definitions already exist.

Semantic consistency shall be maintained across the Enterprise.

---

## Direct Technology Exposure

Applications shall never depend directly upon ontology engines, graph databases or semantic middleware.

All semantic access shall occur through approved Enterprise Semantic APIs.

---

## Unmanaged Metadata

Semantic metadata shall never exist without ownership, lifecycle management and governance.

All metadata shall remain centrally governed.

---

## Uncontrolled Semantic Evolution

Business terminology, ontologies and semantic mappings shall never change without formal governance, review and version control.

Semantic stability shall be preserved.

---

## Missing Traceability

Semantic definitions, mappings and relationships shall always remain traceable to approved Enterprise business definitions and governance decisions.

Semantic provenance shall remain permanently available.
