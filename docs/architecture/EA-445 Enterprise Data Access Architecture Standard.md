# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Access Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Virtualization principles are inherited from EA-443.

Enterprise Data Federation principles are inherited from EA-444.

All Enterprise Data Access implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Access Architecture enabling secure, governed and standardized access to Enterprise information for users, applications, APIs, services, analytics platforms and artificial intelligence solutions across all Business and Technology Domains.

Enterprise Data Access shall

- provide secure information access
- standardize access mechanisms
- improve interoperability
- strengthen governance
- reduce implementation complexity
- support regulatory compliance
- improve operational efficiency
- optimize information utilization
- support business agility
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Access shall establish a unified architectural framework for Enterprise information consumption throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

This standard applies to

- enterprise applications
- business services
- APIs

- analytical platforms
- reporting solutions
- business intelligence platforms
- artificial intelligence platforms
- mobile applications
- external consumers

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform, cloud provider or information technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Access Principles

Enterprise Data Access shall be governed by the following principles.

## Access by Design

Enterprise information shall be exposed through standardized access mechanisms designed as reusable Enterprise capabilities.

---

## Least Privilege Access

Consumers shall receive only the minimum information and permissions required to perform approved business functions.

---

## Standardized Access Interfaces

Enterprise information shall be accessed through standardized Enterprise interfaces.

---

## Secure by Default

All information access shall be authenticated, authorized, encrypted and continuously monitored.

---

## Metadata Driven Access

Enterprise Data Access shall utilize metadata, business definitions and governance policies to control and simplify information access.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Access Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, cloud providers, middleware technologies and implementation platforms.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Access Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed access to Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating logical access layers, standardized access services, identity management, authorization, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables consistent, trusted and business-driven information consumption for users, applications, services, APIs, analytical platforms and artificial intelligence solutions.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Access initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise information access capabilities across the Enterprise.
