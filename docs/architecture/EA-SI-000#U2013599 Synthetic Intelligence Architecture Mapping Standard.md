# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Synthetic Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise synthetic intelligence capabilities.

The Synthetic Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- synthetic intelligence principles
- synthetic intelligence governance
- synthetic intelligence strategy
- synthetic intelligence capability architecture
- synthetic intelligence entity architecture
- multi-agent intelligence architecture
- generative intelligence architecture
- synthetic knowledge architecture
- synthetic reasoning architecture
- synthetic collaboration architecture
- synthetic data architecture
- synthetic security architecture
- synthetic operations
- synthetic performance management
- synthetic evolution

The framework ensures that synthetic intelligence capabilities are designed, governed and operated as advanced Enterprise intelligence services.

---

# 3. Enterprise Synthetic Intelligence Architecture Principles

The Synthetic Intelligence Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Synthetic Intelligence Value Creation

Synthetic intelligence capabilities shall create measurable Enterprise value through advanced intelligent services.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Synthetic Intelligence

Synthetic intelligence systems shall operate according to ethical, secure and governed principles.

---

## Principle 3 – Intelligent Entity Governance

Synthetic intelligent entities shall have defined identity, purpose, responsibility and operational boundaries.

---

## Principle 4 – Collaborative Intelligence

Synthetic intelligence shall support cooperation between intelligent entities, systems and humans.

---

## Principle 5 – Knowledge-Driven Intelligence

Synthetic intelligence shall use structured knowledge, context and learning capabilities.

---

## Principle 6 – Secure Synthetic Operation

Synthetic intelligence environments shall include protection mechanisms throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Synthetic Evolution

Synthetic intelligence capabilities shall continuously improve through learning, feedback and innovation.
