# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Governance Evolution Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced governance architecture for managing the continuous evolution, operation and control of Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Governance Evolution Architecture Framework shall provide:

- governance evolution principles
- adaptive governance
- governance operating model
- governance automation
- decision governance
- policy governance
- regulatory governance
- human oversight governance
- autonomous Intelligence governance
- governance measurement
- governance maturity
- governance evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence remains aligned, controlled, responsible and adaptable throughout its evolution.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Governance Evolution Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture governance evolution domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Governance Evolution Model
- Adaptive Governance Architecture
- Governance Operating Model
- Governance Automation
- Decision Governance
- Policy Governance
- Regulatory Governance
- Human Oversight Governance
- Autonomous Intelligence Governance
- Governance Metrics
- Governance Maturity
- Governance Roadmaps
- Governance Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- autonomous systems
- adaptive decision environments
- future Intelligence ecosystems

---

# 3. Intelligence Governance Evolution Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Governance Evolution Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Adaptive Governance

Governance shall adapt continuously to changing Intelligence capabilities and environments.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Control

Enterprise Intelligence shall operate within defined governance boundaries.

---

## Principle 3 – Human Oversight

Human responsibility shall remain integrated into governance decisions.

---

## Principle 4 – Transparent Governance

Governance decisions and policies shall be understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 5 – Integrated Governance

Governance shall integrate ethics, trust, assurance, security and resilience.

---

## Principle 6 – Automated Governance

Appropriate governance activities shall be supported by automation.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Governance Evolution

Governance capability shall continuously improve through learning and adaptation.
