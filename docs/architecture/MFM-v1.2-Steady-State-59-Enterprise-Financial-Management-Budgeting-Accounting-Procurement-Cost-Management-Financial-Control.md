# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the fifty-ninth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-58 – Enterprise Risk Management, Governance, Compliance, Regulatory Management, Internal Control & Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for financial management, financial governance, budgeting, forecasting, accounting, financial reporting, accounts payable, accounts receivable, cash management, treasury coordination, procurement, purchasing, supplier financial management, contract financial controls, cost management, project financial management, asset accounting, financial planning, financial controls, financial approvals, financial assurance, financial metrics, financial reporting, financial maturity and continual financial improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain accurate, transparent, controlled and sustainable financial management that supports planning, decision-making, accountability, procurement, service delivery, compliance and long-term organizational resilience.**

# 3. Financial Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain accurate, timely and decision-useful financial information and controls across MFM.**

# 4. Budget Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Translate strategic and operational priorities into approved financial plans and maintain control of expenditure against those plans.**

# 5. Accounting Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain complete, accurate, timely and traceable accounting records.**

# 6. Procurement Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Acquire goods and services transparently, economically, appropriately and in accordance with approved requirements and authority.**

# 7. Cost Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Understand and manage the cost of services, activities, projects, suppliers and resources.**

# 8. Financial Control Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Protect financial resources and reporting integrity through appropriate preventive, detective and corrective controls.**

# 102. Definition of Ready

A budget, procurement, purchase, invoice, payment, financial close, project financial item, asset, control, finding, exception or improvement item is Ready when scope, owner, amount or exposure, funding, authority, dependencies, evidence and acceptance requirements are defined.

# 104. Final Financial Principle

> **MFM must maintain accurate, transparent, controlled and sustainable financial management throughout its operating lifecycle.**

> **Financial plans and budgets must translate approved priorities into controlled and measurable financial commitments.**

# 106. Final Accounting Principle

> **Accounting records must be complete, accurate, timely, reconciled and traceable to reliable evidence.**

# 107. Final Procurement Principle

> **Procurement must obtain required goods and services through authorized, transparent and value-oriented processes.**

# 108. Final Cost Principle

> **MFM must understand the cost of services, activities, projects, suppliers and resources and use that information to support sound decisions.**

# 109. Final Control Principle

> **Financial resources and reporting integrity must be protected through appropriate authorization, segregation, reconciliation, monitoring and assurance.**

# 110. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material financial controls must be supported by reliable evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

# 111. Final Improvement Principle

> **Financial performance, variances, findings, audit results, supplier performance and lessons learned must continuously improve MFM financial capability.**

# 112. Final Integration Principle

> **Financial Management must integrate with Enterprise Risk, Governance, Compliance, Procurement, Suppliers, Projects, Applications, Data, Cybersecurity, Infrastructure, Service Management, People and Business Continuity.**

# 113. Final Steady-State Financial Principle

> **MFM must maintain accurate, transparent, controlled and sustainable financial management that supports planning, decision-making, accountability, procurement, service delivery, compliance and long-term organizational resilience.**
