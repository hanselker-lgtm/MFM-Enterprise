# 1. Purpose

N8-D establishes controlled governance for the relationship between:

```text
Investment
    ↓
Initiative
    ↓
Capability Change
    ↓
Operational Change
    ↓
Outcome
    ↓
Benefit
    ↓
Value
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Realization Assessment
```

The purpose is to ensure that claims of benefit, outcome and value remain traceable, measurable and evidence-dependent.

N8-D does not assume that implementation, operation or value realization has occurred.

# 47. Final N8-D Statement

> **N8-D establishes the Value, Outcome & Realization Governance layer of the authorized N8 workstream. It creates controlled governance from investment and initiative through capability change, operational change, outcome, benefit, value, evidence and realization assessment. It preserves the distinction between implementation, adoption, outcome achievement, benefit realization and value realization. All material realization claims remain evidence-dependent, measurable and attributable only to the extent supported by evidence. The outputs of N8-D provide the controlled input to N8-E Evidence, Validation & Completion Assessment.**
