# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Communication Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Messaging Architecture principles are inherited from EA-342.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Communication Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Communication Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and scalable communication capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Communication Services shall

- provide standardized communication services
- support unified communications
- enable multi-channel communication
- improve interoperability
- strengthen scalability
- support internal and external communication
- support reusable communication capabilities
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- enable continuous optimization

Enterprise Communication Services shall provide trusted and reusable communication capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- communication services
- enterprise applications
- messaging platforms
- notification platforms
- collaboration platforms
- communication gateways
- cloud services
- hybrid environments
- external communication providers
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, communication technology or infrastructure platform.

Enterprise Communication Services shall follow these principles.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Communication Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed communication capabilities across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating unified communication services, messaging, notifications, collaboration capabilities, metadata, security, observability and automation into a common Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled communication while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Communication Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise communication capabilities across the Enterprise.
