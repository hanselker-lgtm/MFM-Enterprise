# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Portfolio Management Framework is to establish a structured approach for identifying, managing, prioritizing and optimizing Enterprise portfolios across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture environment.

The Portfolio Management Framework shall provide:

- portfolio principles
- portfolio governance
- portfolio ownership
- portfolio prioritization
- investment alignment
- portfolio performance management
- portfolio optimization

The framework ensures that Enterprise assets and investments continuously support strategy, value creation and evolution.

---

# 2. Scope

The Portfolio Management Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture portfolio domains:

- Enterprise Portfolio
- Architecture Portfolio
- Capability Portfolio
- Service Portfolio
- Process Portfolio
- Application Portfolio
- Technology Portfolio
- Infrastructure Portfolio
- Information Portfolio
- Data Portfolio
- Knowledge Portfolio
- Decision Portfolio
- Outcome Portfolio
- Value Portfolio
- Evolution Portfolio
- Transformation Portfolio
- Investment Portfolio

The framework applies to:

- strategic portfolio management
- operational portfolio management
- technology portfolio management
- transformation portfolio management
- investment management
- continuous optimization

---

# 3. Enterprise Portfolio Management Principles

The Portfolio Management Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategy Alignment

All portfolios shall support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Value-Based Prioritization

Portfolio decisions shall be based on measurable value contribution.

---

## Principle 3 – Complete Portfolio Visibility

Enterprise assets and investments shall be visible and traceable.

---

## Principle 4 – Lifecycle Management

All portfolio elements shall be managed throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 – Risk-Based Management

Portfolio decisions shall consider Enterprise risks and dependencies.

---

## Principle 6 – Investment Optimization

Resources shall be allocated according to strategic importance and value.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Optimization

Portfolios shall continuously evolve through measurement and improvement.

---

# 9. Capability Portfolio Management

The Capability Portfolio Management Model defines how Enterprise capabilities are identified, managed, assessed, prioritized and evolved.

The Capability Portfolio provides visibility of the Enterprise ability to execute strategy and create value.

---

# 9.1 Capability Portfolio Principles

The Capability Portfolio shall ensure:

- capability visibility
- capability ownership
- capability maturity management
- capability investment alignment
- capability evolution planning

---
