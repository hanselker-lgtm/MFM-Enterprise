# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Technology Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise Technology capabilities across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Technology Reference Model shall provide:

- technology identification
- technology classification
- technology ownership
- technology relationships
- technology application mapping
- technology service mapping
- technology lifecycle management
- architecture traceability

The model shall establish a common Enterprise language for defining, managing and evolving technology capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

The Technology Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture technology domains:

- Architecture Technologies
- Business Technologies
- Information Technologies
- Data Technologies
- Application Technologies
- Integration Technologies
- Infrastructure Technologies
- Security Technologies
- Governance Technologies
- Ecosystem Technologies
- Evolution Technologies

The model applies to:

- strategic technologies
- operational technologies
- enabling technologies
- supporting technologies
- transformation technologies
- evolution technologies

---

# 3. Enterprise Technology Architecture Principles

The Technology Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Business Alignment

Technology shall support Enterprise capabilities and strategic objectives.

---

## Service Enablement

Technology shall enable delivery of Enterprise Services.

---

## Application Support

Technology shall provide stable foundations for Enterprise Applications.

---

## Standardization

Technology shall follow Enterprise standards and approved architectures.

---

## Security by Design

Technology shall incorporate security requirements throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Lifecycle Management

Technology shall be managed from introduction to retirement.

---

## Continuous Evolution

Technology shall continuously evolve according to Enterprise requirements.
