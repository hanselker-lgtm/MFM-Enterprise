# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Session Management Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Authentication Services principles are inherited from EA-473.

Enterprise Authorization Services principles are inherited from EA-472.

Enterprise Identity Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-471.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Session Management Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Session Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of user, application and workload sessions across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Session Management Services shall

- provide centralized session management
- standardize session lifecycle handling
- strengthen security controls
- reduce session-related risks
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure session traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Session Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable session management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise session management services
- session creation
- session validation
- session lifecycle management
- token management
- session security
- session timeout management
- session revocation
- distributed session management
- application sessions
- user sessions
- service and workload sessions

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Session Management Services Principles

Enterprise Session Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Session Security by Design

Session management capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every session shall be created, validated, monitored and terminated according to approved Enterprise security requirements.

---

## Continuous Session Validation

Session trust shall be continuously evaluated throughout the complete session lifecycle.

---

## Least Session Privilege

Sessions shall provide only the minimum required privileges and access duration necessary to perform authorized activities.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Session Management Services shall remain independent of vendors, session platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Session Management Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed session management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating session creation, session validation, lifecycle management, token management, session security, timeout management, session revocation, distributed session management, application integration, identity integration, authentication integration, authorization integration, governance, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables secure, scalable and policy-driven session handling while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Session Management Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise session management capabilities across the Enterprise.
