# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, intelligence capabilities, and enterprise coordination framework required for an **Intelligence Singular Enterprise Architecture (ISEA)**.

Where **EA-IOEA67** establishes complete synchronization across every enterprise capability, **EA-ISEA68** establishes a **Singular Enterprise Intelligence**, where every architectural domain, governance capability, knowledge asset, Digital Twin ecosystem, Human Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, organizational memory, Enterprise Wisdom, sustainability objective, and strategic capability operate as a single indivisible enterprise cognition.

The objective is no longer merely synchronization.

The objective is cognitive unification.

Enterprise Architecture shall become one coherent intelligence organism.

Every enterprise capability shall continuously contribute to one unified Enterprise Mind.
