# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Cognition Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities process information, develop understanding, create models, learn from experience and support advanced decision-making.

The Intelligence Cognition Architecture Framework shall provide:

- cognition architecture principles
- information processing models
- reasoning capability models
- knowledge representation models
- learning mechanisms
- cognition governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through cognition, reasoning and knowledge-based understanding.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Cognition Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Cognition Model
- Cognition Intelligence Architecture
- Cognition Knowledge Architecture
- Cognition Capability Model
- Cognition Architecture Integration
- Cognition Governance Model
- Cognition Evolution Framework
- Cognition Metrics
- Cognition Maturity Model
- Cognition Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

# 3. Intelligence Cognition Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Cognition Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

Intelligence capability shall transform information into structured understanding.

---

## Principle 2 – Knowledge Representation

Knowledge shall be organised, connected and available for reasoning.

---

## Principle 3 – Advanced Reasoning

Cognition shall support analysis, inference and decision support.

---

## Principle 4 – Continuous Learning

Cognitive capability shall improve through experience and feedback.

---

## Principle 5 – Contextual Intelligence

Understanding shall consider context, relationships and dependencies.

---

## Principle 6 – Adaptive Cognition

Cognition capability shall evolve according to changing conditions.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Development

Cognitive evolution shall maintain governance, quality and responsibility.
