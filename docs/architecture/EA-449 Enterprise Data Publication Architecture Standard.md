# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Publication Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Distribution principles are inherited from EA-448.

All Enterprise Data Publication implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Publication Architecture supporting secure, governed and standardized publication of authoritative Enterprise information to internal consumers, external partners, APIs, event platforms, analytics environments and regulatory authorities.

Enterprise Data Publication shall

- publish authoritative information
- improve information availability
- standardize publication mechanisms
- strengthen governance
- improve interoperability
- optimize information reuse
- support regulatory reporting
- improve operational efficiency
- strengthen security
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Publication shall ensure that published Enterprise information remains trusted, governed and consistently accessible.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise applications
- APIs
- event platforms
- business domains
- analytics platforms
- data catalogs
- cloud platforms
- external organizations
- strategic partners
- regulatory authorities

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or implementation technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Publication Principles

Enterprise Data Publication shall follow these principles.

## Publication by Design

Enterprise information shall be designed for secure and reusable publication wherever business value exists.

---

## Authoritative Publication

Only authoritative Enterprise information shall be published.

---

## Secure Publication

Published information shall be protected through authentication, authorization, encryption and auditing.

---

## Metadata Driven Publication

Publication services shall utilize Enterprise metadata to govern publication, discovery and lifecycle management.

---

## Standardized Publication

Enterprise information shall be published through approved Enterprise publication services and interfaces.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Publication Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, middleware, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Publication Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed publication of Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating standardized publication services, publication workflows, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, reusable and compliant publication of authoritative Enterprise information while maintaining governance, operational excellence and long-term architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Publication initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise publication capabilities across the Enterprise.
