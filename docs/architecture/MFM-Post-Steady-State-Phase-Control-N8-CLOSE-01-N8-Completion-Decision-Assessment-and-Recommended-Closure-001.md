# 1. Purpose

N8-CLOSE-01 provides the final assessment immediately preceding the formal N8 completion authority decision.

It does not itself close N8.

Its purpose is to consolidate the completion position and provide a controlled recommendation to the designated authority.

# 3. Recommended Decision

The recommended decision is:

```text
CLOSE N8 WITH CONDITIONS
```

This recommendation preserves the established governance pattern from N5, N6 and N7.

It also prevents the closure decision from being interpreted as elimination of all residual conditions, risks, dependencies or future governance obligations.

# 5. Closure Conditions

The following conditions are recommended to remain active after N8 closure:

```text
CLOSE-N8-01
N5, N6 and N7 carry-forward conditions remain active where not formally closed.

CLOSE-N8-02
N8 authorization conditions remain applicable to any residual N8-derived activity.

CLOSE-N8-03
Material strategy, architecture, portfolio or investment changes shall use the applicable future governance mechanism.

CLOSE-N8-04
Value and outcome claims shall remain evidence-dependent.

CLOSE-N8-05
Investment, approval, funding and execution shall remain distinct decision states.

CLOSE-N8-06
Residual findings shall retain owners, status and evidence requirements.

CLOSE-N8-07
Unverified claims shall remain explicitly marked UNVERIFIED.

CLOSE-N8-08
N8 evidence and decisions shall remain part of the controlled architecture record.

CLOSE-N8-09
N8 closure shall not automatically authorize a future phase.

CLOSE-N8-10
Any future phase requires separate scope, authorization and completion criteria.
```

---

# 6. Closure Boundary

If the authority approves:

```text
CLOSE N8 WITH CONDITIONS
```

then:

```text
N8
=
CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS
```

This means:

```text
N8 execution authority ends.
```

It does not mean:

```text
All conditions disappear.
All risks disappear.
All dependencies disappear.
All future work is authorized.
All outcomes are certified.
All value is certified.
All implementation is certified.
```

---

# 7. Residual Governance

Residual matters should transition to the appropriate governance layer:

```text
Architecture Governance
Portfolio Governance
Investment Governance
Value Governance
Risk Governance
Operational Governance
Future Phase Governance
```

The receiving governance mechanism shall be explicitly identified where applicable.

---
