# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Problem Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Problem Management ensures that recurring operational, security and business problems are systematically identified, analyzed, controlled and permanently eliminated while preserving operational resilience, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Problem Identification
- Problem Classification
- Problem Prioritization
- Known Error Management
- Root Cause Elimination
- Preventive Actions
- Problem Lifecycle
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Problem Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Problem Management implementations shall handle problem management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify problem registration failures
- classify investigation failures
- classify corrective action failures
- classify verification failures
- classify reporting failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Problem Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational resilience, traceability, governance, compliance or continuous improvement.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Problem Management implementations may depend upon

- approved monitoring platforms
- approved alert management services
- approved incident management services
- approved knowledge repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Problem Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external problem management providers

Problem Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Problem Management implementation is compliant when

- Problem identification is implemented.
- Problem classification follows enterprise policy.
- Problem prioritization is operational.
- Known Error Management is implemented.
- Root cause elimination is documented.
- Preventive actions are tracked.
- Operational verification is completed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Repeated Incidents Without Problem Records

Recurring incidents shall never occur without creating or evaluating an associated problem record.

---

## Missing Root Cause Elimination

Problems shall never be closed without addressing or documenting verified root causes where feasible.

---

## Untracked Corrective Actions

Corrective actions shall never be implemented without documented ownership, progress tracking and verification.

---

## Undocumented Workarounds

Known workarounds shall never be communicated solely through informal channels without inclusion in the approved Knowledge Management repository.

---

## Premature Problem Closure

Problems shall never be closed before corrective actions and verification activities are completed.

---

## Problem Management Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent problem management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Problem Management services.

---
