# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Digital Twin Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise Digital Twin capabilities.

The Digital Twin Architecture Framework shall provide:

- digital twin principles
- digital twin governance
- digital twin strategy
- digital twin capability architecture
- asset twin architecture
- process twin architecture
- system twin architecture
- data synchronization architecture
- sensor and IoT integration
- simulation architecture
- digital twin security
- digital twin operations
- digital twin performance management
- digital twin evolution

The framework ensures that physical and digital entities can be represented, monitored, analyzed and optimized through controlled digital twin capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

The Digital Twin Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture digital twin domains:

- Enterprise Digital Twin Model
- Digital Twin Governance
- Digital Twin Strategy
- Digital Twin Capability Architecture
- Asset Twin Architecture
- Process Twin Architecture
- System Twin Architecture
- Data Synchronization Architecture
- Sensor and IoT Integration
- Simulation Architecture
- Digital Twin Security
- Digital Twin Operations
- Digital Twin Performance
- Digital Twin Maturity
- Digital Twin Roadmaps
- Digital Twin Evolution

The framework applies to:

- physical assets
- operational environments
- industrial systems
- infrastructure
- business processes
- connected devices
- IoT environments
- simulation platforms
- intelligent operational systems

---

# 3. Enterprise Digital Twin Architecture Principles

The Digital Twin Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Physical and Digital Alignment

Digital twins shall maintain a meaningful relationship between physical entities and their digital representations.

---

## Principle 2 – Data Synchronization

Digital twins shall rely on accurate and timely data exchange.

---

## Principle 3 – Operational Value Creation

Digital twins shall support monitoring, analysis, prediction and optimization.

---

## Principle 4 – Model Integrity

Digital twin models shall maintain accuracy, consistency and traceability.

---

## Principle 5 – Security by Design

Digital twin environments shall include security controls throughout the lifecycle.

---

## Principle 6 – Interoperability

Digital twins shall integrate with Enterprise systems, platforms and services.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

Digital twin capabilities shall continuously evolve according to business and technology requirements.

---

# 9. Digital Twin Capability Architecture

The Digital Twin Capability Architecture defines how Enterprise digital twin capabilities are identified, structured and managed to create operational value through digital representation, monitoring, analysis and optimization.

Digital twin capabilities provide the foundation for connecting physical assets, processes and systems with digital models.

---

# 9.1 Digital Twin Capability Principles

Digital twin capabilities shall ensure:

- physical-digital alignment
- operational value creation
- capability reuse
- data-driven insights
- continuous improvement

# 15. Simulation Architecture

The Simulation Architecture defines how Enterprise Digital Twin environments use simulation capabilities to analyze behaviour, evaluate scenarios and optimize future operational outcomes.

Simulation enables organizations to test changes, predict consequences and improve decision-making before physical implementation.

---

# 15.1 Simulation Architecture Principles

Simulation architecture shall ensure:

- accurate digital representation
- scenario-based analysis
- predictive capability
- operational optimization
- informed decision support

# 21. Digital Twin Governance Operating Model

The Digital Twin Governance Operating Model defines how Enterprise Digital Twin capabilities are governed, managed and continuously improved across the organization.

Digital Twin Governance ensures that digital representations, physical assets, data flows, security requirements, operational responsibilities and improvement activities remain aligned with Enterprise strategy and architecture principles.

---

# 21.1 Digital Twin Governance Principles

Digital Twin Governance shall ensure:

- clear ownership
- defined accountability
- trusted digital representations
- controlled data usage
- secure operation
- measurable operational value
- continuous improvement
