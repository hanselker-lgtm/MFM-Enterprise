# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Lineage Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Consistency principles are inherited from EA-438.

All Enterprise Data Lineage implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Lineage Architecture ensuring complete visibility, traceability and transparency of Enterprise information from its origin through every transformation, movement, consumption, archival and disposal activity.

Enterprise Data Lineage shall

- establish complete information traceability
- improve Enterprise transparency
- support regulatory compliance
- strengthen governance
- enable impact analysis
- support root cause analysis
- improve operational reliability
- increase trust in Enterprise information
- enable audit readiness
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Lineage shall provide a standardized architectural framework for documenting and governing the complete lifecycle and movement of Enterprise information.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise databases
- master data
- transactional systems
- analytical platforms
- data warehouses
- data lakehouses
- APIs
- integration platforms
- event streams
- reporting environments

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure technology, cloud provider or storage platform.

# 3. Enterprise Data Lineage Principles

Enterprise Data Lineage shall be governed by the following principles.

## Lineage by Design

Enterprise information shall be traceable from creation through archival and disposal.

---

## End-to-End Visibility

Enterprise Data Lineage shall provide complete visibility across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Metadata Driven

Enterprise Data Lineage shall be governed through standardized Enterprise metadata.

---

## Automated Discovery

Lineage information shall be automatically discovered, maintained and validated wherever technically feasible.

---

## Continuous Traceability

Every movement, transformation and consumption of Enterprise information shall remain continuously traceable.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Lineage Architecture shall remain independent of implementation technologies, cloud providers, storage platforms and software vendors.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Lineage Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for complete visibility, transparency and traceability of Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating lineage discovery, metadata management, transformation tracking, dependency analysis, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted information governance, improved operational resilience, regulatory compliance and evidence-based decision-making.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Lineage initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing lineage capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Data Lineage Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted lineage discovery, autonomous metadata enrichment, semantic lineage intelligence, predictive impact analysis, self-maintaining lineage repositories, graph-based dependency analytics, digital twin representations of Enterprise information flows and post-quantum protected audit chains.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Lineage continues to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven traceability across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
