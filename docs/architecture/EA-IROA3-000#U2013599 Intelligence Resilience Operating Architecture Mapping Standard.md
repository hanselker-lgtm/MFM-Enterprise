# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational resilience architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities can withstand disruption, recover from failure and continuously adapt to changing conditions.

The Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- resilience operating principles
- business continuity frameworks
- operational resilience structures
- technical resilience models
- recovery models
- crisis management structures
- availability models
- reliability models
- dependency resilience structures
- resilience monitoring structures
- resilience automation structures
- resilience evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for resilient, adaptive and continuously improving Intelligence operations.

# 3. Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Resilience by Design

Resilience shall be integrated into Intelligence capabilities from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Continuous Availability

Critical Intelligence capabilities shall maintain required availability.

---

## Principle 3 – Adaptive Recovery

Intelligence capabilities shall be able to recover and adapt after disruption.

---

## Principle 4 – Dependency Awareness

Critical dependencies shall be identified and managed.

---

## Principle 5 – Preparedness

Resilience capabilities shall be planned, tested and maintained.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Improvement

Resilience capability shall continuously improve.

---

## Principle 7 – Operational Readiness

Intelligence operations shall maintain readiness for unexpected events.
