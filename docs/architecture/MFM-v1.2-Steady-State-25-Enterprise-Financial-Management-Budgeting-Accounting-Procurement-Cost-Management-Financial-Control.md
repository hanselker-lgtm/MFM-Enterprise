# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the twenty-fifth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-24 – Enterprise Risk Management, Governance, Compliance, Internal Controls, Audit & Assurance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for financial management, budgeting, accounting, procurement, cost management, financial planning, financial reporting and financial control.

The central objective is:

> **MFM financial resources must be planned, authorized, recorded, controlled, reported and reviewed accurately, transparently and accountably throughout their lifecycle.**

# 126. Definition of Ready

A financial-management work item is Ready when:

- The transaction, budget, purchase, invoice, payment, receivable, reconciliation, project cost, funding item, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, authority, budget, accounting treatment, risk, materiality and applicable requirements are known.
- Approval, evidence, reconciliation, reporting and acceptance requirements are defined.

---
