# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the fifty-seventh document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-56 – Enterprise Data Management, Data Governance, Information Lifecycle, Master Data, Data Quality, Analytics & Data Security.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for privacy governance, personal data protection, records management, information governance, data-subject rights, privacy accountability, processing inventories, lawful processing, purpose limitation, data minimization, transparency, retention, deletion, legal hold, records preservation, information classification, privacy risk assessment, privacy impact assessment, data protection by design and default, breach management, privacy incidents, processor management, international data transfers, consent management where applicable, data sharing, disclosure, regulatory cooperation, privacy assurance, records assurance, evidence, metrics, reporting, maturity and continual improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage personal data, records and information lawfully, transparently, securely and throughout their lifecycle, protecting individual rights while preserving information required for business, legal, regulatory, evidentiary and operational purposes.**

# 3. Privacy Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Protect personal data and individual rights through lawful, fair, transparent and risk-based privacy governance.**

# 4. Records Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure that authoritative records are created, maintained, protected, retained and disposed of in accordance with approved business, legal and regulatory requirements.**

# 5. Information Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure that information is appropriately owned, classified, accessed, retained, disclosed, preserved and disposed of throughout its lifecycle.**

# 6. Data Rights Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable individuals to exercise applicable data-protection rights through controlled, timely, secure and traceable processes.**

# 7. Legal Hold Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Prevent inappropriate alteration or destruction of relevant information when legal, regulatory, investigative or evidentiary preservation obligations arise.**

# 8. Privacy Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide evidence that privacy and information-governance controls operate effectively and remain aligned with applicable requirements.**

# 126. Final Privacy Principle

> **MFM must process personal data lawfully, fairly, transparently, proportionately and securely throughout its lifecycle.**

# 127. Final Accountability Principle

> **Every material personal-data processing activity must have clear ownership, purpose, applicable requirements and accountable governance.**

# 128. Final Rights Principle

> **Individuals must have practical, secure and traceable means to exercise applicable data-protection rights.**

# 129. Final Records Principle

> **Authoritative records must remain authentic, reliable, integral, usable and appropriately protected throughout their required lifecycle.**

# 130. Final Retention Principle

> **Information and personal data must not be retained longer than justified by applicable business, legal, regulatory, privacy or evidentiary requirements.**

# 131. Final Legal Hold Principle

> **Relevant information must be preserved when legal, regulatory, investigative or evidentiary obligations require it, overriding ordinary disposal where applicable.**

# 132. Final Processor Principle

> **Third parties processing personal data must be appropriately assessed, governed, contracted, monitored and controlled throughout their relationship with MFM.**

# 133. Final Breach Principle

> **Privacy incidents must be assessed, contained, investigated and escalated promptly, with notification performed where required.**

# 134. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material privacy and information-governance controls must be supported by reliable evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

# 135. Final Improvement Principle

> **Rights requests, incidents, findings, regulatory developments, audits and lessons learned must continuously improve MFM privacy and information-governance capability.**

# 136. Final Integration Principle

> **Privacy and Information Governance must integrate with Enterprise Data Management, Cybersecurity, Applications, Infrastructure, Service Management, Legal, Risk, Compliance, Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Suppliers, Finance, People and Project Management.**

# 137. Final Steady-State Privacy Principle

> **MFM must manage personal data, records and information lawfully, transparently, securely and throughout their lifecycle, protecting individual rights while preserving information required for business, legal, regulatory, evidentiary and operational purposes.**
