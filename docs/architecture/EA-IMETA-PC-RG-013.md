## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-reassessment layer defining when and how an established mandatory state, verification, evidence, measurement, threshold, classification, consequence, response or effectiveness determination must be reassessed.

## Core Principle
A mandatory state is not permanently valid merely because it was once verified or an earlier response was effective. Reassessment confirms that the required state remains valid under current conditions and detects regression, changed assumptions and emerging risk.

```text
CURRENT GOVERNED STATE
      ↓
REASSESSMENT TRIGGER
      ↓
REASSESSMENT SCOPE
      ↓
REASSESS / REVERIFY
      ↓
COMPARE WITH REQUIRED STATE
      ↓
CURRENT / REGRESSED / CHANGED / UNDETERMINED
      ↓
RESPONSE / ESCALATION / CONTINUE MONITORING
```

## Reassessment Quality Test
```text
DEFINED TRIGGER
+
DEFINED SCOPE
+
CURRENT EVIDENCE
+
VALID MEASUREMENT
+
CURRENT THRESHOLDS / CRITERIA
+
AUTHORIZED METHOD
+
TRACEABLE RESULT
+
DEFINED CONSEQUENCE
=
VALID GOVERNED REASSESSMENT
```

## Reassessment Invariants

```text
EVERY MATERIAL MANDATORY STATE SHALL HAVE A DEFINED REASSESSMENT STRATEGY WHERE CONTINUED VALIDITY MATTERS
```

```text
REASSESSMENT SHALL BE TRIGGERED BY MATERIAL CHANGE, REGRESSION, INCIDENT, TIME, RISK OR OTHER GOVERNED CONDITIONS
```

```text
REASSESSMENT SHALL USE CURRENT EVIDENCE
```

```text
PRIOR VERIFICATION SHALL NOT SUBSTITUTE FOR CURRENT REASSESSMENT WHEN CONDITIONS HAVE CHANGED
```

```text
REASSESSMENT SCOPE SHALL MATCH THE TRIGGER AND MATERIALITY
```

```text
REASSESSMENT CRITERIA SHALL BE VERSIONED
```

```text
REASSESSMENT AUTHORITY SHALL BE IDENTIFIABLE
```

```text
REASSESSMENT SHALL PRESERVE HISTORICAL RESULTS
```

```text
REGRESSION SHALL NOT BE HIDDEN BY REBASELINING
```

```text
UNDETERMINED REASSESSMENT SHALL NOT BE TREATED AS CURRENT
```

```text
SECURITY, RESILIENCE, SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE STATES SHALL BE REASSESSED WITH APPROPRIATE FREQUENCY
```

```text
AI AND AGENT GOVERNANCE BOUNDARIES SHALL BE REASSESSED AFTER MATERIAL MODEL, POLICY, TOOL, DATA OR AUTONOMY CHANGES
```

```text
FAILED REASSESSMENT SHALL TRIGGER THE DEFINED RESPONSE
```

```text
REASSESSMENT DEFICIENCIES SHALL BE ESCALATED WHEN MATERIAL
```

```text
REPEATED REASSESSMENT FINDINGS SHALL DRIVE GOVERNANCE LEARNING
```

## Reassessment Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Condition requiring reassessment |
| Scope | What is reassessed |
| Authority | Authorized assessment authority |
| Criteria | Current decision criteria |
| Method | Assessment method |
| Evidence | Current supporting evidence |
| Timing | Required assessment window |
| Baseline | Current approved reference |
| Result | Current / regressed / changed / undetermined |
| Consequence | Required response |
| Closure | Conditions for closing reassessment |

## Reassessment Objective

The objective is to determine whether the previously established mandatory state remains valid under current conditions and assumptions.

## Reassessment Trigger

Triggers may include scheduled intervals, incidents, material changes, threshold breaches, effectiveness failure, architecture changes, policy changes, new dependencies, environmental changes, data changes, AI/model changes or emerging risk.

```text
TRIGGER
├── TIME
├── CHANGE
├── INCIDENT
├── REGRESSION
├── RISK
└── GOVERNED EVENT
```

## Reassessment Scope

Scope shall be proportionate to the trigger and shall explicitly include affected dependencies and assumptions.

## Reassessment Authority

Authority shall define who may initiate, perform, approve and close a reassessment. Independence shall be used where required by risk or governance.

## Reassessment Criteria

Current criteria shall be explicit and versioned. Historical criteria shall remain attached to historical determinations.

## Reassessment Method

Methods may include re-verification, testing, inspection, measurement, evidence review, simulation, observation, audit or other appropriate techniques.

## Reassessment Evidence

Current evidence shall support the determination. Old evidence may provide history but shall not substitute for current evidence when current state is being determined.

## Reassessment Timing

Reassessment timing shall balance risk, rate of change, materiality and detection requirements. Critical states require shorter reassessment cycles where appropriate.

## Reassessment Baseline

Baselines shall be current, governed and versioned. Rebaselining shall not erase evidence of regression or conceal historical non-conformance.

## Reassessment Thresholds

Thresholds shall be current and applicable. Changes shall be governed and shall not be applied retrospectively to avoid adverse results.

## Reassessment Independence

Where independence is required, the assessor shall have sufficient separation from the activity being assessed to provide credible determination.

## Security Reassessment

Security posture shall be reassessed after material incidents, vulnerabilities, architecture or access changes and at governed intervals.

## Resilience Reassessment

Resilience shall be reassessed after recovery events, dependency changes, capacity changes, architecture changes or continuity exercises where relevant.

## Compliance Reassessment

Compliance shall be reassessed after regulatory, contractual, policy or operational changes and when evidence indicates potential regression.

## Data Reassessment

Data controls and quality states shall be reassessed after source, transformation, lineage, access, retention or material quality changes.

## AI and Agent Reassessment

AI and agent governance shall be reassessed after material model, prompt/policy, tool, data, autonomy, capability or deployment changes.

```text
AI / AGENT CHANGE
↓
REASSESS AUTHORITY / POLICY / DATA / TOOLS / BEHAVIOUR
↓
CURRENT GOVERNED STATE?
├── YES → CONTINUE
└── NO  → LIMIT / REMEDIATE / ESCALATE
```

## Reassessment Failure

Failure to complete a required reassessment, inability to obtain sufficient evidence or discovery of regression shall trigger the defined governance response.

## Reassessment Escalation

Escalation shall occur when reassessment is overdue, blocked, disputed, materially regressed or unable to establish a safe current state.

## Reassessment Review and Learning

Reassessment results shall be reviewed for recurring regression patterns, weak controls, inappropriate intervals, inadequate thresholds and systemic governance gaps.

## Reassessment Determination Model
```text
REASSESSMENT TRIGGERED
↓
CURRENT EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT?
├── NO → UNDETERMINED / ESCALATE
└── YES
     ↓
CURRENT CRITERIA / THRESHOLDS VALID?
├── NO → GOVERNANCE GAP / ESCALATE
└── YES
     ↓
REQUIRED STATE STILL SATISFIED?
├── YES → CURRENT / CONTINUE
├── NO  → REGRESSION / RESPONSE
└── UNCLEAR → UNDETERMINED / ESCALATE
```

## Reassessment Anti-Gaming Control
Reassessment shall not be delayed, narrowed, rebaselined or reclassified solely to avoid detecting or recording regression. Material changes shall be assessed against the criteria applicable to the current state.

## Reassessment Change Control
Changes to reassessment triggers, frequency, scope, criteria, methods, authority or closure conditions shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated.

```text
CURRENT REASSESSMENT MODEL
↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
↓
NEW VERSION
↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

Historical reassessments shall remain preserved and shall not be overwritten by later results.

