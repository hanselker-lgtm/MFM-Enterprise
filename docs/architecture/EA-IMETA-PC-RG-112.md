## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory consequence-determination layer that translates a classified regression into its actual or potential operational, safety, security, resilience, compliance, data, financial, reputational, governance and reliance consequences, so that proportionate controls, restrictions, escalation and response can be determined.

## Core Principle
Classification states what class a regression belongs to. Consequence determination states what that regression means in terms of impact, exposure, affected obligations, reliance and required governance treatment. Classification and consequence shall remain distinct but linked.

```text
REGRESSION CLASSIFIED
      ↓
IDENTIFY AFFECTED OBJECTS
      ↓
ASSESS ACTUAL + POTENTIAL CONSEQUENCE
      ↓
ASSESS DIRECT + INDIRECT + SECOND-ORDER IMPACT
      ↓
ASSESS CONTROL / RELIANCE EFFECT
      ↓
DETERMINE CONSEQUENCE LEVEL
      ↓
SELECT REQUIRED GOVERNANCE TREATMENT
      ↓
ALERT / RESTRICT / RESPOND / ESCALATE
```

```text
+

VALID GOVERNED REGRESSION CONSEQUENCE DETERMINATION
```

## Classification vs Consequence
```text
CLASSIFICATION
→ WHAT CLASS IS THE REGRESSION?

CONSEQUENCE
→ WHAT DOES THE REGRESSION CAUSE, ENABLE OR THREATEN?
```

## Consequence Model
```text
C0 — NO MATERIAL CONSEQUENCE
C1 — LIMITED CONSEQUENCE
C2 — MATERIAL CONSEQUENCE
C3 — SEVERE CONSEQUENCE
C4 — CRITICAL / CATASTROPHIC CONSEQUENCE
CX — CONSEQUENCE UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
```

## Consequence Dimensions
| Dimension | Assessment focus |
|---|---|
| Safety | Injury, life, safe operation |
| Security | Compromise, exposure, unauthorized access |
| Resilience | Availability, continuity, recovery |
| Compliance | Obligations, approvals, regulatory exposure |
| Data | Integrity, confidentiality, availability, quality |
| Financial | Direct and indirect financial impact |
| Operational | Service, process, capacity, delivery |
| Reputational | Trust, stakeholder confidence, credibility |
| Governance | Authority, accountability, control integrity |
| Reliance | Restriction, suspension, revocation or unsafe continued reliance |

| Element | Required definition |

| Consequence Level | Governed consequence category |

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Objective

Determine the actual and potential consequences of a classified regression so that response, restriction, escalation and reliance decisions are proportionate to the credible impact.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Definition

Consequence determination is the governed assessment of what a regression has caused, may cause, enables or threatens across relevant affected objects, obligations, dependencies and reliance relationships.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Scope

Scope shall include direct effects, indirect effects, dependencies, downstream decisions, affected users, obligations, assets, services, data and reliance relationships.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Authority

Authority shall define who may determine, approve, challenge, escalate or revise consequence assessments and who may impose corresponding restrictions.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Criteria

Criteria shall define consequence levels, affected dimensions, credible scenarios, materiality, time horizon, propagation and required treatment.
```text
REGRESSION
↓
WHAT IS AFFECTED?
↓
WHAT HAS HAPPENED?
+
WHAT COULD HAPPEN?
↓
HOW FAR CAN IT PROPAGATE?
↓
WHAT CONTROL / RELIANCE IS AFFECTED?
↓
C0 / C1 / C2 / C3 / C4 / CX
↓
TREATMENT
```

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Preconditions

Preconditions include valid regression classification, current evidence, affected-object mapping, consequence criteria, dependency knowledge and authorized assessment roles.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Evidence

Evidence shall preserve observed impact, scenarios, assumptions, affected objects, dependencies, control state, reliance state, time horizon and consequence rationale.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Method

Methods may include impact assessment, scenario analysis, risk analysis, dependency analysis, business impact assessment, safety assessment and independent assurance.
```text
CLASSIFY
↓
MAP IMPACT
↓
ASSESS ACTUAL
↓
ASSESS POTENTIAL
↓
ASSESS PROPAGATION
↓
DETERMINE CONSEQUENCE
```

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Decision

Decision shall determine C0, C1, C2, C3, C4 or CX and the corresponding governance treatment.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Accountability

Accountability shall remain explicit for assumptions, consequence interpretation, escalation and treatment decisions.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Timing

Consequence determination shall occur within the decision latency appropriate to the potential severity and propagation speed of the regression.

## Security Post-Closure Regression Consequence

Security consequence shall assess compromise, privilege escalation, exposure, unauthorized access, persistence, lateral movement and affected trust boundaries.

## Resilience Post-Closure Regression Consequence

Resilience consequence shall assess service loss, capacity degradation, recovery failure, dependency concentration, continuity impact and recovery time.

## Compliance Post-Closure Regression Consequence

Compliance consequence shall assess breach of mandatory obligations, approvals, reporting, segregation, licensing and regulatory exposure.

## Data Post-Closure Regression Consequence

Data consequence shall assess integrity, confidentiality, availability, quality, lineage, affected populations, downstream decisions and recoverability.

## AI and Agent Post-Closure Regression Consequence

AI/agent consequence shall assess effects from behavior drift, unauthorized authority, tool misuse, data-boundary breach, excessive autonomy and human-oversight failure.
```text
AI / AGENT REGRESSION
↓
BEHAVIOR
+
AUTHORITY
+
AUTONOMY
+
TOOLS
+
DATA
+
OVERSIGHT
↓
CONSEQUENCE
```

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Failure

Failure includes omitted consequence dimensions, optimistic assumptions, ignored dependencies, unsupported low-impact assessment, stale scenarios or delayed consequence determination.
```text
CONSEQUENCE FAILURE
↓
MATERIAL IMPACT POSSIBLE?
├── YES → CONSERVATIVE ASSESSMENT / ESCALATE
└── NO → CORRECT / REASSESS
```

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Independence

Independent consequence assessment may be required where impact is high, disputed, cross-domain, regulated or where the affected party has an interest in minimizing consequence.

## Post-Closure Regression Consequence Review and Learning

Reviews shall examine underestimated consequences, missed propagation, incorrect assumptions, delayed escalation and recurring consequence patterns.

## Actual vs Potential Consequence
Actual consequence describes what has already occurred. Potential consequence describes a credible future outcome if the regression continues, propagates or is exploited.
```text
ACTUAL IMPACT
+
CREDIBLE POTENTIAL IMPACT
=
CONSEQUENCE BASIS
```

## Direct, Indirect and Second-Order Consequence
Consequence assessment shall not stop at the directly affected component where downstream or dependency effects can materially alter the outcome.
```text
DIRECT
↓
INDIRECT
↓
SECOND-ORDER
↓
SYSTEMIC
```

## Time Horizon
Consequence shall consider appropriate time horizons: immediate, near-term, sustained and long-term effects where relevant.

## Propagation
Where a regression can spread through dependencies, shared controls, common infrastructure or decision chains, the consequence assessment shall reflect credible propagation.

## Reliance Consequence
The assessment shall explicitly determine whether continued reliance remains justified, should be limited, suspended or revoked.
```text
CONSEQUENCE
↓
RELIANCE IMPACT
├── NONE → CONTINUE
├── LIMITED → CONDITION
├── MATERIAL → RESTRICT
├── SEVERE → SUSPEND
└── CRITICAL → REVOKE / EMERGENCY GOVERNANCE
```

## Safety Consequence
Safety consequence shall receive priority treatment where regression could affect life, injury, safe operation or safety-critical controls.

## Security Consequence
Security consequence shall include not only realized compromise but credible exposure and loss of trust boundaries where supported by evidence.

## Resilience Consequence
Resilience consequence shall consider whether restored reliance remains justified if recovery, redundancy or fallback capabilities have deteriorated.

## Compliance Consequence
Compliance consequence shall consider whether continued operation creates mandatory reporting, notification, approval or restriction obligations.

## Data Consequence
Data consequence shall include downstream decisions that may become unreliable because of data regression.

## AI and Agent Consequence
AI/agent consequence shall explicitly consider unauthorized decision authority, excessive autonomy and tool/data boundary changes even where output metrics remain apparently acceptable.

## Unknown Consequence
CX shall remain an active governed state, not a permanent escape category. Evidence acquisition and conservative treatment shall continue until the consequence can be sufficiently characterized.

## Anti-Gaming
Consequence shall not be downgraded to improve metrics, reduce reported severity, avoid restrictions or accelerate closure.

```text
CONSEQUENCE

↓
ACKNOWLEDGE
↓
RESPOND
```

```text
REASSESS
```

EA-IMETA SHALL REQUIRE EVERY MATERIAL POST-CLOSURE REGRESSION TO HAVE ITS ACTUAL AND POTENTIAL CONSEQUENCES EXPLICITLY DETERMINED ACROSS RELEVANT SAFETY, SECURITY, RESILIENCE, COMPLIANCE, DATA, OPERATIONAL, FINANCIAL, REPUTATIONAL, GOVERNANCE AND RELIANCE DIMENSIONS, INCLUDING DIRECT, INDIRECT AND SECOND-ORDER EFFECTS, SO THAT RESPONSE, RESTRICTION AND ESCALATION ARE PROPORTIONATE TO THE CREDIBLE IMPACT.
