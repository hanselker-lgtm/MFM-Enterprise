# 1. Purpose

This document defines the fifty-fourth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-53 – Membership, Customer / Member Experience, Communications, Engagement & Relationship Management Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled project and portfolio management capability covering project initiation, governance, planning, work breakdown, milestones, dependencies, resource management, project budgets, risks, issues, changes, reporting, documentation, assurance and closure.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage projects and portfolios through clear objectives, accountable ownership, controlled planning, measurable delivery, managed resources, visible risks and issues, disciplined change, reliable reporting and evidence-based assurance.**

# 117. Definition of Ready

A project work item is Ready when:

- Purpose and outcome are defined.
- Sponsor and owner are identified.
- Scope is understood.
- Initial delivery approach is known.
- Dependencies are identified.
- Resources and budget are considered.
- Risks are understood sufficiently for the next decision.

# 119. Final Project Principle

> **MFM must manage projects as controlled temporary investments designed to produce defined outcomes and measurable value.**

---

# 120. Final Portfolio Principle

> **Portfolio management must optimize the combined value, risk, capacity and strategic alignment of projects rather than treating projects as isolated activities.**

---

# 121. Final Planning Principle

> **Project plans must connect scope, deliverables, schedule, resources, budget, dependencies and risks into one coherent delivery baseline.**

---

# 122. Final Resource Principle

> **Project resources must be allocated according to approved priorities, capacity and organizational value.**

---

# 123. Final Risk Principle

> **Project risks must be identified early, owned explicitly, actively managed and escalated when thresholds are exceeded.**

---

# 124. Final Issue Principle

> **Project issues require accountable ownership, timely action and escalation when their impact exceeds project authority or tolerance.**

---

# 125. Final Change Principle

> **Material project changes must be assessed for scope, schedule, budget, resources, dependencies and risk before approval.**

---

# 126. Final Assurance Principle

> **Project assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that projects remain viable, governed, controlled and capable of delivering their intended outcomes.**

---

# 127. Final Closure Principle

> **Project closure is complete only when deliverables are accepted, financial and governance obligations are resolved, ownership is transferred and lessons are retained.**

---

# 128. Final Benefits Principle

> **Project success should ultimately be assessed by realized business or organizational benefits, not merely by completing scheduled activities.**

---

# 129. Final Integration Principle

> **Project management must integrate with financial management, membership, grants, service management, process governance, architecture, security, procurement, documents and reporting.**

---

# 130. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should operate projects and portfolios through governed initiation, disciplined planning, controlled delivery, visible resources and finances, managed risks and issues, controlled changes, measurable benefits and evidence-based assurance.**
