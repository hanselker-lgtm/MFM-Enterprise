# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eighty-seventh document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-86 – Enterprise Risk Management, Technology Risk, Operational Risk, Risk Appetite, Risk Assessment & Risk Treatment Governance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for compliance governance, regulatory obligations, legal and regulatory change coordination, compliance requirements, policy compliance, standards compliance, control framework management, control ownership, control objectives, control design, control implementation, control operation, compliance assessments, control testing, evidence management, attestations, compliance monitoring, compliance issues, compliance exceptions, compliance remediation, regulatory reporting coordination, compliance assurance, compliance findings, compliance metrics, compliance dashboards, compliance maturity and continual enterprise compliance improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must identify, understand, implement, monitor and evidence applicable compliance obligations so that material legal, regulatory, contractual and internal requirements are consistently met.**

# 95. Final Compliance Principle

> **MFM must identify, understand, implement, monitor and evidence applicable compliance obligations so that material legal, regulatory, contractual and internal requirements are consistently met.**

# 96. Final Regulatory Principle

> **Material regulatory changes must be identified, assessed, assigned, implemented and validated within appropriate deadlines.**

# 97. Final Policy Principle

> **Internal policies and mandatory standards must translate applicable requirements and risk decisions into clear organizational expectations.**

# 98. Final Control Principle

> **Controls must have clear objectives, accountable owners, appropriate design, reliable operation and sufficient evidence.**

# 99. Final Evidence Principle

> **Material compliance claims must be supported by reliable, relevant, complete and traceable evidence.**

# 100. Final Exception Principle

> **Compliance exceptions must be explicitly risk-assessed, authorized, monitored and closed or renewed through controlled governance.**

# 101. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material compliance requirements and controls must be periodically assessed and independently challenged where appropriate.**

# 102. Final Reporting Principle

> **Regulatory reporting must be accurate, complete, timely, approved and traceable to authoritative information.**

# 103. Final Improvement Principle

> **Regulatory changes, findings, control failures, incidents and assurance results must continuously improve MFM's compliance capability.**

# 104. Final Integration Principle

> **Compliance Management must integrate with Enterprise Risk, Legal, Privacy, Cybersecurity, Data, Identity, Enterprise Architecture, Service Management, Suppliers, Finance, Business Continuity and Internal Audit.**

# 105. Final Steady-State Compliance Principle

> **MFM must identify, understand, implement, monitor and evidence applicable compliance obligations so that material legal, regulatory, contractual and internal requirements are consistently met.**
