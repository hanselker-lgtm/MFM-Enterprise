# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Competency Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Competency Services shall follow these principles.

## Competency by Design

Enterprise capabilities shall be supported by deliberately developed and governed competencies.

---

## Competency Ownership

Every competency shall have defined ownership, responsibility and development paths.

---

## Competency Alignment

Competencies shall align with Enterprise strategy, capabilities and operational requirements.

---

## Competency Development

Enterprise competencies shall continuously develop according to future needs.

---

## Competency Measurement

Competencies shall be evaluated through measurable criteria and evidence.

---

## Knowledge and Skill Integration

Competencies shall combine knowledge, skills, experience and practical application.

---

## Continuous Competency Improvement

Competencies shall continuously improve through learning and operational experience.

---

## Governed Competency Management

Competency activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.
