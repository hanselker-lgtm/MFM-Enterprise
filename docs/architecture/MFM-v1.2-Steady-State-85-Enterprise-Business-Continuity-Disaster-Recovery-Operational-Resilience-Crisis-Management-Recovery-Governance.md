# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eighty-fifth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-84 – Enterprise Privacy, Personal Data Protection, Data Protection Governance, Privacy Risk, Rights Management & Privacy Incident Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for business continuity management, continuity strategy, business impact analysis, critical business services, recovery objectives, continuity requirements, disaster recovery, technology recovery, operational resilience, dependency management, recovery planning, crisis management, emergency response, crisis leadership, crisis communications, incident escalation, recovery coordination, alternate operating arrangements, backup and restore coordination, continuity testing, disaster recovery testing, crisis exercises, recovery assurance, resilience findings, resilience exceptions, resilience remediation, continuity metrics, recovery dashboards, resilience maturity and continual enterprise resilience improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain the capability to continue critical business services during disruption and recover them within approved objectives through coordinated continuity, resilience, crisis and recovery governance.**

# 3. Business Continuity Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure critical business services can continue at an acceptable level during disruption.**

# 4. Disaster Recovery Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore technology and supporting capabilities within approved recovery objectives following disruption.**

# 5. Operational Resilience Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable the enterprise to prevent, withstand, respond to, adapt to and recover from material disruption.**

# 6. Crisis Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide coordinated leadership, decision-making, communications and resource mobilization during major disruption.**

# 7. Recovery Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure recovery decisions, actions, priorities, evidence and lessons learned are governed and traceable.**

# 102. Definition of Ready

A critical service, BIA, continuity plan, disaster recovery plan, recovery test, crisis exercise, resilience assessment, exception, remediation or assurance review is Ready when purpose, owner, scope, criticality, dependencies, recovery requirements, scenario, success criteria, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
