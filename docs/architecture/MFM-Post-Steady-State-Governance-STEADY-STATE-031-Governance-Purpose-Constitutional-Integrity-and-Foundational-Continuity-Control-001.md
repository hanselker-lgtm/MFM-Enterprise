# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-031 establishes the controlled mechanism for protecting the deepest foundational layer of governance: purpose, mandate, foundational principles, constitutional constraints, core authority, core accountability and the conditions under which foundational elements may be changed.

The purpose is to ensure that continuous adaptation does not silently alter the fundamental identity, legitimacy or governing intent of the system.

The operating sequence is:

```text
PURPOSE
↓
MANDATE
↓
FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES
↓
CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINTS
↓
AUTHORITY
↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
↓
INTERPRETATION
↓
CONTROLLED CHANGE
↓
CONTINUITY
↓
REASSESSMENT
```
