# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the forty-ninth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-48 – Enterprise Cybersecurity, Information Security, Identity, Access Management, Security Operations & Security Architecture.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for privacy governance, personal data protection, privacy by design, privacy risk management, data-subject rights, lawful processing, records management, information governance, information classification, retention, disposal, legal holds, records appraisal, information access, transparency, consent management where applicable, privacy incidents, records incidents, information assurance, digital trust, third-party privacy, cross-border data handling, privacy compliance, records assurance, evidence management and continual privacy and information-governance improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage personal data, business information and records in a lawful, transparent, accountable and lifecycle-controlled manner while protecting individual rights, preserving authoritative records and maintaining trust in digital services and information.**

# 3. Privacy Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure that personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, transparently and securely throughout its lifecycle while respecting applicable individual rights.**

---

# 4. Records Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure that authoritative business records are captured, classified, protected, retained, retrieved and disposed of according to defined legal, regulatory, business and evidential requirements.**

---

# 5. Information Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

---

# 6. Digital Trust Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain confidence that digital identities, transactions, information and services are authentic, reliable, secure, accountable and appropriately governed.**

# 129. Definition of Ready

A privacy assessment, processing activity, rights request, record class, retention rule, legal hold, disclosure, information asset, digital-trust control, incident, finding, exception or improvement work item is Ready when:

- The activity, data, record, request, risk, finding or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, purpose, classification, sensitivity, legal or business requirement, retention, security, privacy and dependencies are known.
- Assessment, approval, implementation, evidence, monitoring, response and acceptance requirements are defined.

# 131. Final Privacy Principle

> **Personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly, transparently, securely and only for appropriate purposes throughout its lifecycle.**

---

# 132. Final Data Subject Rights Principle

> **Applicable individual rights must be supported through controlled, timely, traceable and accountable processes.**

---

# 133. Final Privacy by Design Principle

> **Privacy must be designed into systems, processes, services and information flows from the beginning.**

---

# 134. Final Records Principle

> **Authoritative records must remain authentic, reliable, integral, usable and appropriately retained for their required period.**

---

# 135. Final Retention Principle

> **Information and records must be retained for as long as justified and disposed of in a controlled manner when retention is no longer required.**

---

# 136. Final Legal Hold Principle

> **Records subject to legal, regulatory or investigative preservation requirements must not be disposed of until the applicable hold has been appropriately released.**

---

# 137. Final Information Governance Principle

> **Enterprise information must have clear ownership, appropriate classification, controlled access and lifecycle accountability.**

---

# 138. Final Digital Trust Principle

> **Digital services must provide sufficient confidence in identity, information, transactions, evidence and accountability for their intended use.**

---

# 139. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material privacy, records and information-governance controls must be supported by evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

---

# 140. Final Improvement Principle

> **Privacy incidents, rights requests, records findings, audit results, disclosure events, user feedback and operational lessons must continuously improve MFM privacy and information-governance capability.**

---

# 141. Final Integration Principle

> **Privacy and Information Governance must integrate with Enterprise Architecture, Data Management, Cybersecurity, Application Management, Integration, Service Management, Risk, Business Continuity, Financial Management, Procurement, Supplier Management, People Management, Compliance, Legal and Project Management.**

---

# 142. Final Steady-State Privacy & Information Principle

> **MFM must manage personal data, business information and records in a lawful, transparent, accountable and lifecycle-controlled manner while protecting individual rights, preserving authoritative records and maintaining trust in digital services and information.**
