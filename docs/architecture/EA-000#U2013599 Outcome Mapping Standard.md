# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Outcome Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise Outcome capabilities across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Outcome Reference Model shall provide:

- outcome identification
- outcome classification
- outcome ownership
- outcome relationships
- outcome measurement
- outcome value mapping
- outcome lifecycle management
- outcome governance
- architecture traceability

The model shall establish a common Enterprise language for defining, managing and evolving Enterprise outcomes.

---

# 2. Scope

The Outcome Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture outcome domains:

- Architecture Outcomes
- Business Outcomes
- Information Outcomes
- Digital Outcomes
- Security Outcomes
- Governance Outcomes
- Ecosystem Outcomes
- Evolution Outcomes

The model applies to:

- strategic outcomes
- operational outcomes
- business outcomes
- technology outcomes
- transformation outcomes
- sustainability outcomes
- evolutionary outcomes

---

# 3. Enterprise Outcome Architecture Principles

The Outcome Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Outcome Value

Every Enterprise initiative shall create measurable outcomes and value.

---

## Outcome Ownership

Every outcome shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Outcome Alignment

Outcomes shall align with Enterprise strategy and capabilities.

---

## Outcome Measurement

Outcomes shall be measurable through defined indicators.

---

## Outcome Transparency

Outcome assumptions, dependencies and results shall be documented.

---

## Outcome Governance

Outcomes shall be governed through defined management structures.

---

## Outcome Lifecycle Management

Outcomes shall be managed from identification to realization.

---

## Continuous Outcome Improvement

Outcome capabilities shall continuously evolve through measurement and learning.
