# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Delivery Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Subscription principles are inherited from EA-450.

All Enterprise Data Delivery implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Delivery Architecture supporting secure, reliable and scalable delivery of authoritative Enterprise information to applications, services, users, analytics platforms and external organizations.

Enterprise Data Delivery shall

- deliver authoritative information
- improve delivery reliability
- support multiple delivery models
- strengthen interoperability
- improve operational efficiency
- optimize delivery performance
- strengthen governance
- improve scalability
- support regulatory compliance
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Delivery shall provide standardized mechanisms ensuring trusted and efficient information delivery throughout the Enterprise.

This standard applies to

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or implementation technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Delivery Principles

Enterprise Data Delivery shall follow these principles.

## Delivery by Design

Enterprise information shall be designed for secure, reliable and reusable delivery across approved Enterprise channels.

---

## Authoritative Delivery

Delivered information shall always originate from authoritative Enterprise information sources.

---

## Secure Delivery

All Enterprise delivery mechanisms shall implement authentication, authorization, encryption and continuous monitoring.

---

## Metadata Driven Delivery

Delivery services shall utilize Enterprise metadata for routing, lifecycle management, policy enforcement and optimization.

---

## Standardized Delivery Services

Enterprise information shall be delivered through approved Enterprise delivery services using standardized interfaces and protocols.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Delivery Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, middleware technologies, cloud providers and infrastructure platforms.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Delivery Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed delivery of Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating standardized delivery services, delivery models, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled delivery of authoritative Enterprise information while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Delivery initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise delivery capabilities across the Enterprise.
