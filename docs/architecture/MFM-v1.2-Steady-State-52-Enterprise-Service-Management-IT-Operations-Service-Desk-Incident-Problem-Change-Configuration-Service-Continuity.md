# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the fifty-second document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-51 – Enterprise Business Continuity, Crisis Management, Disaster Recovery, Resilience & Operational Recovery.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for service management, IT operations, service desk, service requests, incidents, problems, changes, releases, configurations, assets, service levels, availability, capacity, event management, monitoring, knowledge management, service continuity, operational procedures, operational readiness, technical support, supplier coordination, operational security, operational documentation, service reporting, service metrics, operational assurance, service maturity and continual service improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must provide stable, measurable, secure and continuously improved services through disciplined service management and IT operations, with clear ownership, controlled changes, effective incident and problem management, accurate configuration information and defined service continuity.**

# 3. Service Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Deliver reliable, supportable and measurable services aligned with business requirements and agreed service levels.**

# 4. IT Operations Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Operate technology services safely, efficiently, consistently and predictably.**

# 5. Service Desk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide an accessible and accountable point of contact for users, incidents, requests and service communications.**

# 6. Incident Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore normal service as quickly as reasonably possible while minimizing business impact.**

# 7. Problem Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and address underlying causes of incidents and reduce recurrence and operational impact.**

# 8. Change Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable beneficial change while controlling risk, disruption and unintended consequences.**

# 9. Configuration Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain reliable information about services, configuration items, dependencies and relationships needed for effective operation and decision-making.**

# 10. Service Continuity Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain or restore agreed service levels during disruption in alignment with business continuity and disaster recovery requirements.**

# 116. Definition of Ready

A service, request, incident, problem, change, release, configuration item, asset, service level, recovery activity, finding, exception or improvement work item is Ready when the requirement is clear; ownership, impact, priority, dependencies, risk, acceptance criteria and operational requirements are known; and the required documentation, testing, approval, monitoring, recovery and evidence requirements are defined.

# 117. Definition of Done

A service-management work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement Identified
        ↓
Owner Assigned
        ↓
Work Completed
        ↓
Testing / Validation Completed
        ↓
Documentation Updated
        ↓
Monitoring / Support Ready
        ↓
Evidence Captured
        ↓
Findings / Exceptions Addressed
        ↓
Outcome Accepted
```
