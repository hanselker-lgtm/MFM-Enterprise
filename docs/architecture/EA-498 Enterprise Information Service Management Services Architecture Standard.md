# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of Enterprise information services throughout their complete lifecycle across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Management Services shall

- provide centralized information service management capabilities
- establish service governance
- define information service ownership
- manage service lifecycle activities
- support service catalog management
- support service level management
- support availability management
- support performance management
- support capacity management
- support continuity management
- support service improvement
- improve operational resilience
- support compliance requirements
- support audit requirements
- protect information quality
- support lifecycle governance
- support master information services
- support reference information services
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure service traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Service Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable service management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Service Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Service Management by Design

Enterprise information services shall be designed, governed and operated as managed Enterprise capabilities.

---

## Service Ownership and Accountability

Every Enterprise information service shall have defined ownership, accountability and governance responsibility.

---

## Lifecycle-Based Service Management

Enterprise information services shall be managed from creation through operation, improvement and retirement.

---

## Measurable Service Delivery

Enterprise information services shall be managed using defined service levels, performance indicators and operational metrics.

---

## Continuous Service Improvement

Enterprise information services shall continuously improve through measurement, analysis and optimization.
