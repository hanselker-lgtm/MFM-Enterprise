# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise SOP management ensures that operational procedures are created, approved, classified, distributed, maintained, executed and continuously improved through standardized enterprise processes while preserving governance, operational consistency, accountability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Standard Operating Procedures
- Procedure Development
- Procedure Classification
- Procedure Approval
- Procedure Distribution
- Procedure Maintenance
- Procedure Compliance
- Governance

All enterprise SOP implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## SOP-001

Provide standardized enterprise operating procedures.

---

## SOP-002

Ensure operational consistency.

---

## SOP-003

Support procedure lifecycle management.

---

## SOP-004

Ensure complete procedure traceability.

---

## SOP-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. SOP Principles

Enterprise Standard Operating Procedures shall follow these principles.

- Procedure by Design
- Governance by Default
- Operational Consistency
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Accountability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

SOP implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. SOP Domains

Enterprise SOP management shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Operational Procedures
- Administrative Procedures
- Technical Procedures
- Security Procedures
- Compliance Procedures
- Maintenance Procedures
- Emergency Procedures
- Support Procedures

Additional SOP domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. SOP Ownership

Each SOP domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the SOP lifecycle.

---

# 7. SOP Governance

Enterprise SOP governance shall define

- SOP governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- SOP verification
- governance reporting

SOP governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. SOP Responsibilities

Enterprise SOP management shall provide controlled management of enterprise operating procedures.

SOP responsibilities shall

- separate SOP management from business execution
- coordinate procedure ownership
- ensure operational consistency
- validate procedure objectives
- preserve procedure traceability
- support enterprise governance

SOP implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Procedure Development

Enterprise SOP management shall implement standardized procedure development.

Procedure development shall

- define procedure objectives
- identify applicable requirements
- identify affected business areas
- identify implementation responsibilities
- preserve development history
- maintain development traceability

Procedure development shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Procedure Classification

Enterprise SOP management shall classify all enterprise procedures.

Procedure classifications shall

- define procedure category
- define confidentiality level
- define operational applicability
- define ownership
- preserve classification history
- maintain classification traceability

Procedure classifications shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Procedure Approval

Enterprise SOP management shall implement standardized procedure approval.

Procedure approval shall

- validate procedure completeness
- verify governance compliance
- verify stakeholder approval
- preserve approval history
- maintain approval traceability
- support procedure integrity

Procedure approval shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Procedure Distribution

Enterprise SOP management shall implement standardized procedure distribution.

Procedure distribution shall

- distribute approved procedures
- notify affected stakeholders
- support procedure accessibility
- preserve distribution history
- maintain distribution traceability
- support governance communication

Procedure distribution shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. SOP Dependencies

Enterprise SOP management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- compliance capabilities
- audit capabilities
- document management systems
- communication platforms
- enterprise infrastructure

SOP implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. SOP Documentation

Each SOP domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- SOP registers
- ownership information
- procedure classifications
- approval records
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. SOP Lifecycle

Enterprise SOP management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Drafted
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Published
- Implemented
- Verified
- Monitored
- Revised
- Retired
- Archived

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. SOP Quality Attributes

Enterprise SOP implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- usability
- governance compliance
- operational effectiveness

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the SOP lifecycle.

---

# 17. SOP Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized SOP register.

The SOP register shall contain

- SOP identifiers
- ownership assignments
- classifications
- approval status
- lifecycle status
- documentation references
- review schedules
- governance status

The SOP register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise operating procedures.

---

# 18. SOP Reviews

Enterprise SOP implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- procedure completeness
- procedure consistency
- classification accuracy
- approval integrity
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. SOP Metrics

Enterprise SOP management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- approved procedures
- procedures under review
- overdue procedure reviews
- procedure compliance rate
- procedure exceptions
- procedure revisions
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous SOP improvement.

---

# 20. SOP Verification

Enterprise SOP implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm procedure completeness
- verify classifications
- verify approvals
- verify distribution
- verify ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- confirm governance compliance
- approve publication

SOP verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous SOP Improvement

Enterprise SOP management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve procedure quality
- improve governance
- improve procedure accessibility
- improve operational consistency
- improve organizational compliance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise SOP implementations shall handle procedure-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify procedure development failures
- classify procedure approval failures
- classify procedure distribution failures
- classify procedure execution failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

SOP exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational consistency, governance or regulatory obligations.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

SOP implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved compliance capabilities
- approved audit capabilities
- approved document management systems
- approved communication platforms
- approved enterprise infrastructure

SOP implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external SOP management services

SOP capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An SOP implementation is compliant when

- SOP responsibilities are documented.
- Procedure development procedures are implemented.
- Procedure classifications are documented.
- Procedure approval procedures are implemented.
- Procedure distribution is documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- SOP Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- SOP verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Procedure Ownership

Enterprise procedures shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Missing Procedure Approval

Procedures shall never be published without formal approval.

---

## Missing Procedure Distribution

Approved procedures shall never remain unavailable to affected stakeholders.

---

## Outdated Procedures

Enterprise procedures shall never remain active beyond their scheduled review period without documented reapproval or revision.

---

## Undocumented SOP Dependencies

SOP implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## SOP Management Outside Governance

SOP implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise SOP implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- SOP management quality
- procedure completeness
- approval integrity
- distribution effectiveness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational consistency
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise operating procedures throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and business operations implement standardized procedure development, approval, classification, distribution, verification and governance through controlled lifecycle management and continuous improvement while preserving operational consistency, accountability, regulatory alignment and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise SOP implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
