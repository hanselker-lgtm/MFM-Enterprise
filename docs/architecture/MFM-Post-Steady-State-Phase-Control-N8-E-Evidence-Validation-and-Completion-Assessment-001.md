# 1. Purpose

N8-E establishes the integrated evidence, validation and completion assessment layer for the N8 workstream.

It validates the controlled outputs from:

```text
N8-A
Strategy, Outcome & Capability Alignment

N8-B
Architecture Roadmap & Transition Portfolio Governance

N8-C
Investment, Prioritization & Portfolio Traceability

N8-D
Value, Outcome & Realization Governance
```

N8-E prepares the formal completion recommendation.

N8-E does not itself close N8.
