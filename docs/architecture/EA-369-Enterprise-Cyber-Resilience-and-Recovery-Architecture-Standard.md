# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Cyber Resilience & Recovery Architecture governing resilience planning, cyber recovery, disaster recovery, ransomware resilience and operational continuity across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Cyber Resilience capability shall

- maintain business continuity
- minimize operational disruption
- ensure rapid recovery
- strengthen ransomware resilience
- protect critical information assets
- support Enterprise Risk Management
- improve organizational resilience
- support regulatory compliance
- enable continuous recovery improvement
- remain technology independent

Cyber Resilience shall operate as a shared Enterprise capability.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to all Enterprise cyber resilience capabilities including

- cyber resilience
- cyber recovery
- disaster recovery
- immutable backup
- backup governance
- recovery orchestration
- ransomware resilience
- recovery vaults
- recovery testing
- resilience validation
- business continuity integration
- crisis recovery
- cloud recovery
- infrastructure recovery
- application recovery

This standard applies regardless of deployment model or hosting provider.

# 3. Enterprise Cyber Resilience Principles

Enterprise Cyber Resilience shall be governed by the following principles.

## Recovery by Design

Enterprise systems shall be designed to support secure recovery.

---

## Business-Driven Recovery

Recovery priorities shall be determined by business impact and Enterprise risk.

---

## Immutable Protection

Critical recovery data shall be protected against unauthorized modification or deletion.

---

## Continuous Validation

Recovery capabilities shall be regularly validated through structured testing.

---

## Defense in Depth

Recovery capabilities shall complement preventive, detective and responsive security controls.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Cyber Resilience shall remain independent of specific backup platforms, cloud vendors and infrastructure technologies.

# 6. Enterprise Cyber Resilience Architecture

The Enterprise Cyber Resilience Architecture establishes the framework for protecting, recovering and validating Enterprise services following cyber incidents, infrastructure failures or operational disruptions.

The architecture consists of

- Enterprise Backup Services
- Immutable Backup Services
- Recovery Vault Services
- Disaster Recovery Services
- Recovery Orchestration Services
- Business Continuity Integration
- Recovery Validation Services
- Recovery Monitoring Services
- Reporting Services
- Governance Services

The architecture shall provide secure, resilient and measurable recovery capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 7. Recovery Objectives (RTO/RPO)

Enterprise recovery planning shall be based upon clearly defined Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).

Recovery objectives shall consider

- business criticality
- service availability
- regulatory requirements
- financial impact
- operational dependencies
- customer expectations
- data sensitivity
- geographic distribution
- resilience strategy
- Enterprise risk

Recovery objectives shall be documented, approved and periodically reviewed.

---

# 8. Enterprise Backup Architecture

Enterprise Backup Architecture shall provide secure and recoverable protection for Enterprise information assets.

Backup architecture shall support

- full backups
- incremental backups
- differential backups
- application-aware backups
- database backups
- cloud backups
- virtual machine backups
- container backups
- configuration backups
- metadata backups

Backup architecture shall support automated scheduling, verification and reporting.

---

# 9. Immutable Backup

Critical Enterprise backups shall be protected using immutable storage technologies.

Immutable backup capabilities shall support

- write-once protection
- tamper resistance
- ransomware resilience
- retention enforcement
- integrity verification
- secure replication
- cryptographic validation
- controlled deletion
- regulatory retention
- recovery assurance

Immutable backup repositories shall be logically separated from production environments.

---

# 10. Recovery Vaults

Recovery Vaults shall provide isolated environments for secure cyber recovery.

Recovery Vault capabilities shall include

- logical isolation
- administrative isolation
- credential separation
- immutable storage
- secure synchronization
- integrity validation
- malware scanning
- controlled recovery
- audit logging
- resilience reporting

Recovery Vaults shall minimize the risk of compromise during cyber incidents.

---

# 23. Enterprise Cyber Resilience Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Untested Recovery Plans

Recovery plans shall never be considered complete unless validated through regular testing.

---

## Mutable Backup Repositories

Critical recovery data shall never rely solely on backup repositories that can be modified or deleted by attackers.

---

## Shared Administrative Credentials

Recovery environments shall never share administrative credentials with production environments.

---

## Recovery Without Validation

Recovered services shall never return to production without completing recovery validation.

---

## Inconsistent Recovery Objectives

Recovery priorities shall remain aligned with approved Enterprise Recovery Time Objectives and Recovery Point Objectives.

---

## Missing Business Participation

Business stakeholders shall participate in resilience planning, recovery validation and recovery exercises.
