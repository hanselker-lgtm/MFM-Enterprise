## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory effectiveness-determination layer that determines whether an executed response achieved the required outcome, reduced or controlled the material condition, and restored the required state without creating unacceptable secondary regression.

## Core Principle
Response completion is not effectiveness. Effectiveness exists only when sufficient evidence demonstrates that the required response outcome was achieved against explicit criteria, within required boundaries, for the required duration and without unacceptable residual or secondary conditions.

```text
RESPONSE EXECUTED
      ↓
DEFINE REQUIRED EFFECT
      ↓
OBSERVE ACTUAL OUTCOME
      ↓
COMPARE WITH EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA
      ↓
EFFECTIVE?
├── YES → CONFIRM / STABILIZE
├── PARTIAL → EXTEND / ADJUST / REASSESS
├── NO → CORRECT / ESCALATE
└── UNKNOWN → GATHER EVIDENCE
```

## Effectiveness Quality Test
```text
REQUIRED OUTCOME
+
VALID EXECUTION EVIDENCE
+
CURRENT OBSERVATION
+
DEFINED EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA
+
SUFFICIENT DURATION
+
RESIDUAL RISK ASSESSMENT
+
SECONDARY EFFECT CHECK
+
AUTHORIZED DETERMINATION
=
VALID GOVERNED EFFECTIVENESS DETERMINATION
```

## Effectiveness vs Completion Test
```text
ACTION COMPLETED?
        ↓
DID REQUIRED EFFECT OCCUR?
        ↓
DID EFFECT PERSIST?
        ↓
ARE RESIDUAL CONDITIONS ACCEPTABLE?
        ↓
NO MATERIAL SECONDARY REGRESSION?
        ↓
EFFECTIVENESS DETERMINED
```

## Effectiveness Status Model
```text
NOT ASSESSED
UNDER ASSESSMENT
EFFECTIVE
PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE
INEFFECTIVE
TEMPORARILY EFFECTIVE
SUSTAINED EFFECTIVE
UNKNOWN
REJECTED
REASSESSMENT REQUIRED
```

## Response Effectiveness Determination Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Required Outcome | Intended result |
| Response | Executed intervention |
| Criteria | Rules for effectiveness |
| Observation | Actual result |
| Duration | Required persistence |
| Residual Risk | Remaining exposure |
| Secondary Effects | Effects caused by response |
| Determination | Effectiveness result |

## Response Effectiveness Determination Objective

Determine whether the response achieved the required outcome sufficiently and sustainably to support controlled resolution or further action.

## Response Effectiveness Determination Definition

Effectiveness determination is the governed assessment of whether a response produced the required effect against approved criteria, over the required duration and within acceptable residual and secondary conditions.

## Response Effectiveness Determination Scope

Scope shall identify systems, services, users, data, decisions, dependencies, environments, consumers and boundaries affected by the response and its effectiveness.

## Response Effectiveness Determination Authority

Authority shall define who may establish criteria, assess evidence, determine effectiveness, reject insufficient evidence and require further response.

## Response Effectiveness Determination Criteria

Criteria shall distinguish effective, partially effective, ineffective, temporary, sustained, unknown and reassessment-required states.

```text
ACTUAL EFFECT
↓
MEETS REQUIRED CRITERIA?
├── YES
│    ↓
│  PERSISTENT + SAFE?
│    ├── YES → EFFECTIVE / SUSTAINED EFFECTIVE
│    └── NO → TEMPORARY / PARTIAL
└── NO → INEFFECTIVE / REASSESS
```

## Response Effectiveness Determination Preconditions

Preconditions include defined required outcome, valid response record, current observation, effectiveness criteria, duration requirements and residual-risk criteria.

## Response Effectiveness Determination Evidence

Evidence shall preserve baseline, pre-response state, response actions, post-response observations, criteria, duration, residual risk, secondary effects and determination rationale.

## Response Effectiveness Determination Method

Methods may include before/after comparison, threshold comparison, trend analysis, controlled verification, sampling, testing, inspection and sustained observation.

```text
PRE-RESPONSE STATE
↓
RESPONSE
↓
POST-RESPONSE STATE
↓
COMPARE REQUIRED EFFECT
↓
ASSESS DURATION + RESIDUAL RISK
```

## Response Effectiveness Determination Decision

Determination shall explicitly decide effective, partial, temporary, ineffective, unknown or reassessment-required.

```text
EVIDENCE
├── SUFFICIENT + EFFECT ACHIEVED → EFFECTIVE
├── PARTIAL → PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE
├── TEMPORARY → TEMPORARILY EFFECTIVE
├── NOT ACHIEVED → INEFFECTIVE
└── INSUFFICIENT → UNKNOWN / REASSESS
```

## Response Effectiveness Determination Accountability

Accountability shall remain explicit for evidence assessment, determination, residual-risk acceptance and decision to proceed toward resolution.

## Response Effectiveness Determination Timing

Effectiveness assessment shall occur at the appropriate point after execution and, where required, continue for a defined observation period to establish persistence.

## Security Response Effectiveness Determination

Security effectiveness shall establish that exposure, unauthorized access, control failure or threat condition was actually controlled and not merely hidden.

## Resilience Response Effectiveness Determination

Resilience effectiveness shall establish that availability, recovery, continuity, capacity or dependency conditions have returned to required state and remain stable.

## Compliance Response Effectiveness Determination

Compliance effectiveness shall establish that the required control, obligation, evidence and reporting state has been restored or otherwise governed as required.

## Data Response Effectiveness Determination

Data effectiveness shall establish that integrity, quality, access, lineage, authorized use, retention or downstream conditions meet required criteria.

## AI and Agent Response Effectiveness Determination

AI/agent effectiveness shall assess whether authority, policy, tool use, data access, autonomy, behaviour and material outcomes returned to required governed conditions.

```text
AI / AGENT RESPONSE
↓
OBSERVE AUTHORITY + POLICY + DATA + TOOLS
↓
OBSERVE AUTONOMY + BEHAVIOUR + OUTCOMES
↓
REQUIRED STATE RESTORED?
├── YES → EFFECTIVE
└── NO → FURTHER RESPONSE / REASSESS
```

## Response Effectiveness Determination Failure

Failure includes insufficient evidence, ambiguous criteria, temporary improvement, residual risk above tolerance, secondary regression or inability to establish required effect.

```text
EFFECTIVENESS FAILURE
↓
CONDITION STILL MATERIAL?
├── YES → FURTHER RESPONSE / ESCALATE
└── NO BUT UNCERTAIN → EXTEND OBSERVATION / VERIFY
```

## Response Effectiveness Determination Independence

Material effectiveness determinations may require independent validation where the response owner has conflict of interest or the consequence of false assurance is high.

## Response Effectiveness Determination Review and Learning

Reviews shall identify ineffective responses, misleading indicators, weak criteria, insufficient observation periods, residual-risk normalization and recurring response failure.

## Effectiveness Determination Model
```text
RESPONSE EXECUTED
↓
REQUIRED OUTCOME DEFINED?
├── NO → GOVERNANCE GAP
└── YES
     ↓
ACTUAL EFFECT OBSERVED?
├── NO → UNKNOWN / GATHER EVIDENCE
└── YES
     ↓
CRITERIA MET?
├── NO → INEFFECTIVE / PARTIAL
└── YES
     ↓
REQUIRED DURATION MET?
├── NO → TEMPORARY / CONTINUE MONITORING
└── YES
     ↓
RESIDUAL + SECONDARY CONDITIONS ACCEPTABLE?
├── NO → REASSESS / FURTHER RESPONSE
└── YES → EFFECTIVE
```

## Effectiveness Outcome Matrix
| Outcome | Meaning | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Effective | Required effect achieved | Stabilize / proceed to resolution assessment |
| Partially Effective | Some required effect achieved | Further response / reassess |
| Temporarily Effective | Effect achieved but persistence unproven | Continue observation |
| Sustained Effective | Effect achieved and persists for required duration | Proceed toward resolution |
| Ineffective | Required effect not achieved | Further response / escalate |
| Unknown | Evidence insufficient | Gather evidence / extend observation |
| Reassessment Required | Conditions changed or evidence invalidated | Reassess |
| Rejected | Determination not accepted by authority | Correct evidence / re-determine |

## Completion vs Effectiveness vs Resolution
```text
COMPLETION
→ WAS THE ACTION PERFORMED?

EFFECTIVENESS
→ DID THE ACTION PRODUCE THE REQUIRED EFFECT?

RESOLUTION
→ HAS THE GOVERNED CONDITION BEEN BROUGHT TO REQUIRED STATE?
```

## Required Duration
Some conditions require sustained observation. A response shall not be declared sustained effective merely because the condition temporarily improved.

## Residual Risk
Effectiveness determination shall explicitly identify remaining exposure or residual condition and determine whether it is within authorized limits.

```text
REQUIRED EFFECT
↓
RESIDUAL RISK
├── ACCEPTABLE → CONTINUE
└── UNACCEPTABLE → FURTHER RESPONSE / ESCALATE
```

## Secondary Regression
The effectiveness assessment shall examine whether the response introduced new or secondary regression elsewhere.

```text
RESPONSE
↓
PRIMARY CONDITION IMPROVED?
+
SECONDARY REGRESSION?
↓
OVERALL EFFECTIVENESS
```

## Temporary Effectiveness
Temporary effectiveness shall remain a distinct status until required persistence is demonstrated.

## Criteria Integrity
Effectiveness criteria shall remain versioned and shall not be lowered after execution merely to produce a successful determination.

## Evidence Sufficiency
Where evidence is insufficient, the correct outcome is unknown or reassessment-required, not effective.

## Effectiveness Rejection
A determination may be rejected where evidence is inadequate, criteria were misapplied, authority was insufficient or material residual conditions were ignored.

## Handoff to Resolution
Only an effectiveness determination meeting required criteria may support the next resolution-governance layer.

```text
EFFECTIVE / SUSTAINED EFFECTIVE
↓
RESOLUTION ASSESSMENT

INEFFECTIVE / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN
↓
FURTHER RESPONSE / REASSESSMENT
```

## Anti-Gaming
Effectiveness shall not be declared through selective evidence, shortened observation periods, lowered criteria, ignored secondary effects or redefinition of the required outcome.

## Relationship to Reassessment
A failed or uncertain effectiveness determination feeds back into reassessment and further response rather than allowing premature closure.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes the mandatory response-effectiveness determination layer beneath response execution and above resolution and reassessment. It does not replace governance, authority, mandate, role, responsibility, accountability, outcome, criteria, success, success conditions, mandatory conditions, non-negotiable requirements, applicability, state, verification, evidence, measurement, comparison, deviation detection, classification, alerting, acknowledgement, response initiation, escalation, authority transfer, response execution, reassessment, revalidation, reacceptance, reliance restoration, baseline establishment, monitoring, resolution, closure, post-closure monitoring or regression detection layers.
