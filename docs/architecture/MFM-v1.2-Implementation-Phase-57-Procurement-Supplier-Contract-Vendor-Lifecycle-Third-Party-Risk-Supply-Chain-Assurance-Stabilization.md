# 1. Purpose

This document defines the fifty-seventh implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-56 – Document & Records Management, Information Lifecycle, Filing, Retention, Search, Archiving & Records Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled procurement and third-party management capability covering procurement planning, sourcing, supplier onboarding, supplier master data, tendering, evaluation, contracting, contract obligations, supplier performance, third-party risk, supply-chain risk, vendor compliance, supplier assurance, renewal and termination.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage suppliers and third parties through a controlled lifecycle connecting business need, procurement, due diligence, selection, contracting, delivery, performance, risk, assurance, renewal and exit.**

# 143. Final Procurement Principle

> **Procurement must connect organizational need, value, competition, authorization, supplier selection and accountable contracting through one controlled lifecycle.**

---

# 144. Final Supplier Principle

> **Every material supplier relationship must have a clear owner, defined obligations, appropriate risk classification and measurable performance expectations.**

---

# 145. Final Contract Principle

> **Contracts must be treated as active governance instruments containing obligations, financial commitments, performance expectations, risk controls and exit requirements.**

---

# 146. Final Risk Principle

> **Third-party risk must be assessed according to criticality, dependency, impact and control strength, not merely supplier size.**

---

# 147. Final Security Principle

> **Supplier access to MFM information and services must be governed by least privilege, appropriate security controls and continuous risk-based oversight.**

---

# 148. Final Privacy Principle

> **Third parties processing personal information must be subject to appropriate privacy, contractual and operational controls.**

---

# 149. Final Continuity Principle

> **Critical suppliers must have proportionate continuity, recovery and exit arrangements so that supplier failure does not become uncontrolled organizational failure.**

---

# 150. Final Performance Principle

> **Supplier performance must be measured against agreed outcomes, service levels, quality, delivery and compliance requirements.**

---

# 151. Final Assurance Principle

> **Third-party assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that material suppliers meet contractual, operational, security, privacy and compliance expectations.**

---

# 152. Final Exit Principle

> **Supplier relationships must be capable of controlled termination, including access removal, data handling, asset return, knowledge transfer, financial settlement and record retention.**

---

# 153. Final Integration Principle

> **Procurement and supplier management must integrate with finance, contracts, projects, grants, services, security, privacy, documents, configuration, risk and reporting.**

---

# 154. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage procurement and third parties through a controlled lifecycle connecting demand, sourcing, due diligence, selection, contracting, onboarding, performance, risk, assurance, renewal and exit.**
