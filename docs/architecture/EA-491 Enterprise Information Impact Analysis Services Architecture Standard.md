# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Impact Analysis Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed assessment of impacts caused by changes to Enterprise information, systems, processes, integrations and business capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Impact Analysis Services shall

- provide centralized impact analysis capabilities
- identify information dependencies
- evaluate change consequences
- support informed decision-making
- improve change management
- strengthen governance visibility
- support risk assessment
- support compliance evaluation
- support privacy impact assessment
- support security impact assessment
- support quality impact assessment
- support lifecycle governance
- support lineage-driven analysis
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure impact traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Impact Analysis Services shall provide trusted and reusable impact assessment capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Impact Analysis Services Principles

Enterprise Information Impact Analysis Services shall follow these principles.

## Impact Awareness by Design

Impact analysis capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, change processes, applications, services and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Dependency-Driven Analysis

Enterprise decisions shall consider information, system, process and business dependencies.

---

## Evidence-Based Assessment

Impact analysis shall be based on trusted lineage, metadata, governance information and operational evidence.

---

## Proactive Risk Identification

Potential impacts shall be identified before changes are implemented.

---

## Business and Technical Alignment

Impact analysis shall connect business consequences with technical dependencies and implementation details.

By integrating information dependency analysis, data change impact analysis, system impact analysis, application impact analysis, integration impact analysis, process impact analysis, business impact analysis, regulatory impact analysis, privacy impact analysis, security impact analysis, quality impact analysis, risk impact analysis, change assessment services, dependency mapping, lineage integration, metadata integration, master information integration, reference data integration, governance integration, compliance integration, audit integration, event integration, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables informed, controlled and policy-driven change management while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.
