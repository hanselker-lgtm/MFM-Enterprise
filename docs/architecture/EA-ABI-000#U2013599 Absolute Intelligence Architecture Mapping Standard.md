# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Absolute Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a conceptual architecture model for the highest level of integrated Enterprise Intelligence capability.

The Absolute Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- absolute intelligence architecture principles
- absolute intelligence governance
- absolute intelligence strategy
- absolute capability architecture
- total intelligence integration architecture
- unified intelligence field architecture
- absolute reasoning architecture
- absolute knowledge architecture
- absolute intelligence synthesis
- enterprise intelligence unity model
- human–absolute intelligence collaboration
- absolute safety architecture
- absolute alignment architecture
- absolute operations
- absolute performance management
- absolute evolution

The framework ensures that future advanced intelligence capabilities are approached through a unified architecture, governance and responsible evolution model.

# 3. Enterprise Absolute Intelligence Architecture Principles

The Absolute Intelligence Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Unified Intelligence Integration

All intelligence capabilities shall operate as parts of a coherent Enterprise Intelligence architecture.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Intelligence Governance

Advanced intelligence capabilities shall operate within defined governance, safety and ethical boundaries.

---

## Principle 3 – Complete Knowledge Integration

Knowledge resources shall be integrated, contextualised and continuously improved.

---

## Principle 4 – Intelligence Unity

Different intelligence capabilities shall cooperate as one coordinated intelligence ecosystem.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Intelligence Evolution

Intelligence capability shall continuously improve through learning, adaptation and transformation.

---

## Principle 6 – Human Intelligence Partnership

Advanced intelligence shall support and enhance human judgement, creativity and decision-making.

---

## Principle 7 – Enterprise Value Creation

Intelligence capability shall create measurable strategic and operational value.
