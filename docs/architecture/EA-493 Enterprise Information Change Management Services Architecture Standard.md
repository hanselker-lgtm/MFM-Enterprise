# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Change Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of changes affecting Enterprise information, data, metadata, systems, integrations, processes and business capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Change Management Services shall

- provide centralized change management capabilities
- control information-related changes
- establish change governance
- support change assessment
- integrate impact analysis
- integrate dependency analysis
- integrate lineage analysis
- improve change decision-making
- reduce operational risks
- support compliance requirements
- support audit requirements
- protect information quality
- support lifecycle governance
- support master information governance
- support reference information governance
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure change traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Change Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable change management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Change Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Change Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Controlled Change by Design

Enterprise information changes shall be managed through defined, governed and repeatable change processes.

---

## Impact-Aware Change Management

All significant changes shall consider dependencies, lineage, quality, security, privacy and business impacts.

---

## Approved Change Execution

Changes shall only be implemented after appropriate review, approval and governance validation.

---

## Traceable Change History

All information changes shall maintain complete history, ownership and auditability.

---

## Business and Technical Alignment

Change management shall connect business requirements with technical implementation activities.
