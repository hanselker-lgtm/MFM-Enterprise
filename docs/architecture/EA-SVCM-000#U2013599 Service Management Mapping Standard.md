# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Service Management Framework is to establish a structured approach for designing, managing, delivering and improving Enterprise services across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture environment.

The Service Management Framework shall provide:

- service principles
- service governance
- service lifecycle management
- service ownership
- service performance management
- service improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise services continuously deliver measurable value to stakeholders.

---

# 2. Scope

The Service Management Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture service domains:

- Enterprise Services
- Business Services
- Capability Services
- Application Services
- Technology Services
- Infrastructure Services
- Information Services
- Data Services
- Knowledge Services
- Security Services
- Support Services
- Transformation Services

The framework applies to:

- service strategy
- service design
- service transition
- service operation
- service improvement
- service evolution

---

# 3. Enterprise Service Management Principles

The Service Management Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Service Value Orientation

All services shall create measurable value for stakeholders.

---

## Principle 2 – Service Ownership

Every service shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Principle 3 – Lifecycle Management

Services shall be managed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Principle 4 – Business Alignment

Services shall support Enterprise capabilities and objectives.

---

## Principle 5 – Quality Management

Services shall meet defined quality and performance expectations.

---

## Principle 6 – Transparency

Service information, ownership and performance shall be visible.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

Services shall continuously evolve based on measurement and feedback.

---
