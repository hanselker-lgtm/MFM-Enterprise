# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Blockchain & Distributed Ledger Architecture governing decentralized ledgers, trusted transactions, smart contracts, digital assets and distributed trust services across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Blockchain Architecture shall

- establish standardized distributed ledger services
- provide trusted transaction processing
- support immutable audit trails
- enable decentralized business processes
- support secure digital asset management
- integrate with Enterprise applications
- support Digital Twin trust models
- support Enterprise IoT ecosystems
- improve transparency
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Blockchain shall function as a reusable Enterprise capability supporting trusted digital collaboration across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- blockchain platforms
- distributed ledgers
- smart contracts
- digital assets
- enterprise tokenization
- trust services
- distributed consensus
- blockchain integration
- cryptographic validation
- decentralized applications

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or blockchain technology.

# 3. Enterprise Blockchain Principles

Enterprise Blockchain Architecture shall be governed by the following principles.

## Trust by Design

Enterprise Blockchain solutions shall establish verifiable trust through cryptographic mechanisms and distributed validation.

---

## Immutable Transactions

Approved ledger transactions shall remain immutable and traceable throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Enterprise Governance

All Blockchain implementations shall remain under Enterprise governance and policy control.

---

## Interoperability

Blockchain services shall integrate with Enterprise applications, services and data platforms through standardized interfaces.

---

## Secure Cryptography

All Blockchain platforms shall comply with Enterprise Cryptography and PKI Architecture standards.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Blockchain Architecture shall remain independent of specific blockchain vendors, frameworks and cloud providers.
