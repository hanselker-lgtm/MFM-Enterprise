# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Orchestration Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed coordination, composition, automation and execution of Enterprise information services across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Orchestration Management Services shall

- coordinate Enterprise information services

- improve interoperability
- reduce operational complexity

# 3. Enterprise Information Service Orchestration Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Orchestration Management Services shall follow these principles.

Enterprise information services shall be designed to support controlled orchestration and coordinated execution.

---

## Governed Service Composition

Service compositions shall be created, maintained and executed through approved governance processes.

---

## Automated Coordination

Enterprise orchestration shall use automation capabilities where appropriate to improve consistency and efficiency.

---

## Traceable Execution

All orchestration activities shall maintain complete visibility of execution flow, decisions and outcomes.

## Reusable Orchestration Capabilities

Orchestration patterns, workflows and capabilities shall be reusable across Enterprise domains.
