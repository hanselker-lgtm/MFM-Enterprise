# 124. Final Privacy Principle

> **Privacy must be considered throughout the complete information lifecycle rather than only when an incident occurs.**

---

# 125. Final Purpose Principle

> **Personal information should be collected and processed only for defined and legitimate purposes.**

---

# 126. Final Minimization Principle

> **MFM should avoid collecting, duplicating, displaying or retaining personal information that is not necessary for the approved purpose.**

---

# 127. Final Transparency Principle

> **Individuals should receive appropriate information about how their personal information is used where required.**

---

# 128. Final Rights Principle

> **Applicable information rights must be supported through controlled, traceable and appropriately verified processes.**

---

# 129. Final Consent Principle

> **Where consent is relied upon, consent must be appropriately recorded and withdrawal must be actionable.**

---

# 130. Final Risk Principle

> **Privacy risk must be assessed according to the potential impact on individuals and the organization.**

---

# 131. Final Design Principle

> **Privacy should be built into architecture, workflows, data structures and interfaces from the beginning.**

---

# 132. Final Retention Principle

> **Personal information should not be retained longer than justified by applicable purpose, legal, regulatory or business requirements.**

---

# 133. Final Deletion Principle

> **Eligible personal information must be securely and deliberately disposed of while respecting valid retention and legal-hold requirements.**

---

# 134. Final Third-Party Principle

> **Third parties processing personal information must remain within MFM's governed privacy and security boundary according to applicable requirements.**

---

# 135. Final Incident Principle

> **Privacy incidents must be detected, assessed, contained, documented and remediated through a disciplined response process.**

---

# 136. Final Records Principle

> **Records must preserve required evidence and integrity without creating uncontrolled or unjustified retention of personal information.**

---

# 137. Final Assurance Principle

> **Privacy controls must be supported by evidence from assessments, testing, monitoring, rights handling and governance reviews.**

---

# 138. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage personal and regulated information through privacy-by-design, controlled information rights, accountable processing, risk-based protection, governed retention and secure lifecycle disposal.**
