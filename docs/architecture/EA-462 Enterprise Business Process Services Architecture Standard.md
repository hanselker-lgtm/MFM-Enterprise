# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Business Process Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Business Process Architecture principles are inherited from EA-345.

Enterprise Workflow Services principles are inherited from EA-461.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Business Process Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Business Process Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and scalable business process capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Business Process Services shall

- provide standardized business process services
- support enterprise process execution
- enable process automation
- improve operational efficiency
- strengthen interoperability
- support reusable business process capabilities
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- enable continuous optimization
- improve business agility

Enterprise Business Process Services shall provide trusted and reusable process capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- business process services
- business process execution
- process orchestration
- process modeling
- process optimization
- workflow integration
- rule-based processes
- cloud services
- hybrid environments
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, process technology or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Business Process Services Principles

Enterprise Business Process Services shall follow these principles.

## Business Process First

Enterprise Business Process Services shall standardize, automate and optimize business processes across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Secure by Default

Every business process execution shall be authenticated, authorized, protected and continuously monitored.

---

## Standardized Business Process Services

Enterprise Business Process Services shall expose standardized business process interfaces supporting interoperability throughout the Enterprise.

---

## Metadata Driven Processes

Business process services shall leverage Enterprise metadata for orchestration, execution, governance, lifecycle management and optimization.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Business Process Services shall remain independent of vendors, BPM platforms, workflow engines, cloud providers and infrastructure technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Business Process Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed business process capabilities across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating process modeling, process execution, monitoring, optimization, workflow integration, event-driven processing, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled business process execution while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Business Process Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise business process capabilities across the Enterprise.
