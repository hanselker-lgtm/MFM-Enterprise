# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundred-and-sixth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-105 – Security Architecture, Identity & Access Management, Zero Trust, Vulnerability, Security Operations, Threat Management & Cybersecurity Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled information governance capability covering privacy governance, data protection, privacy principles, information rights, data subject and information requests, privacy impact assessment, records management, information classification, retention schedules, legal hold, information disposal, archiving, records integrity, information access, privacy incident management, data breach response, privacy monitoring, information governance and privacy and records assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage personal, confidential and business-critical information lawfully, responsibly and throughout its lifecycle while preserving rights, evidential integrity, appropriate retention, controlled access and defensible disposal.**

# 132. Definition of Ready

An information governance work item is Ready when:

- Information, processing activity or record category is identified.
- Ownership is established.
- Purpose, classification and applicable requirements are understood.
- Relevant privacy, rights, security, retention and records requirements are identified.
- Dependencies, repositories and affected systems are known.
- Assurance and evidence requirements are defined.

# 134. Final Privacy Principle

> **Personal information must be processed for defined purposes with appropriate lawful authority, transparency, minimization, security and accountability.**

---

# 135. Final Rights Principle

> **Information rights must be handled through controlled, timely, secure and traceable processes that protect the requester and other affected parties.**

---

# 136. Final Records Principle

> **Records required as evidence must remain authentic, reliable, usable, protected and retrievable throughout their required lifecycle.**

---

# 137. Final Retention Principle

> **Information must be retained for justified periods and disposed of only when retention and preservation obligations have been satisfied.**

---

# 138. Final Legal Hold Principle

> **Legal and formally controlled preservation requirements must override ordinary disposal until authorized release.**

---

# 139. Final Disposal Principle

> **Disposal must be authorized, secure, traceable and supported by sufficient evidence.**

---

# 140. Final Privacy Incident Principle

> **Privacy and data-breach incidents must be detected, assessed, contained, reported where required, remediated and reviewed with appropriate evidence.**

---

# 141. Final Assurance Principle

> **Information governance assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that privacy, records, rights, retention, legal hold and disposal controls operate as intended.**

---

# 142. Final Integration Principle

> **Information Governance must integrate with Data Governance, Security, Business Process, Service Management, Enterprise Architecture, Risk, Compliance, Legal, Supplier, Document, Membership, Project and Grant governance.**

---

# 143. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should govern information through a controlled lifecycle connecting privacy, rights, records, classification, retention, legal hold, archiving, access, sharing, disposal, incidents and assurance.**
