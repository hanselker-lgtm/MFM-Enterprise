# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Operating Model Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive operating model for managing, operating and evolving Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Operating Model Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence operating model principles
- operating model governance
- organisation architecture
- role architecture
- responsibility model
- operating processes
- service operations
- capability operations
- resource management
- performance management
- operational analytics
- maturity management
- operating model evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are transformed into operationally effective, governed and continuously improving capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Operating Model Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture operating model domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Operating Model
- Operating Model Governance
- Organisation Architecture
- Role Architecture
- Responsibility Model
- Intelligence Centres of Excellence
- Operating Processes
- Service Management
- Capability Management
- Resource Management
- Performance Management
- Operational Governance
- Operating Model Maturity
- Operating Model Roadmaps
- Operating Model Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- digital operating environments
- future adaptive enterprises

The Intelligence Operating Model Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.
