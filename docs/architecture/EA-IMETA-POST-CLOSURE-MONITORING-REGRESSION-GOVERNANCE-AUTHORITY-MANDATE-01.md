## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandate layer beneath governance authority, defining why an authority exists, what it governs, what it is responsible for, what decisions it may make, what boundaries and conditions apply, and how the mandate is reviewed.

## Core Principle
A mandate defines the governed purpose and boundaries of an authority. It converts organizational intent into explicit responsibilities, decision rights, limits, conditions and accountability.

```text
AUTHORITY
   ↓
MANDATE
   ↓
PURPOSE
   ↓
SCOPE
   ↓
RESPONSIBILITIES
   ↓
DECISION RIGHTS
   ↓
BOUNDARIES
   ↓
CONDITIONS
   ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
   ↓
REVIEW
```

## Mandate Structure
| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Why the authority exists |
| Scope | Services, domains, lifecycle states and decisions covered |
| Responsibilities | Outcomes and obligations owned |
| Decision Rights | Decisions the authority may make |
| Boundaries | Decisions and activities explicitly excluded |
| Conditions | Preconditions that must remain true |
| Accountability | Role accountable for mandate performance |
| Delegation | Permitted delegation and constraints |
| Escalation | Conditions requiring higher authority |
| Evidence | Records proving mandate validity and operation |
| Review | Trigger, frequency and approving authority |

## Mandate Purpose
The purpose statement shall describe the business, operational, security, resilience, compliance or governance outcome the authority exists to protect or enable. Purpose shall not be defined solely as a job title or organizational convenience.

## Mandate Scope
```text
MANDATE SCOPE
 ├── ORGANIZATION
 ├── SERVICE
 ├── PROCESS
 ├── DATA
 ├── TECHNOLOGY
 ├── LIFECYCLE STATE
 ├── RISK DOMAIN
 └── DECISION DOMAIN```

Scope shall be sufficiently precise to prevent both authority gaps and uncontrolled expansion.

## Responsibilities
Responsibilities describe what the mandate holder must ensure, not merely what they are permitted to do.

```text
MANDATE
  ↓
RESPONSIBILITY
  ↓
CONTROL
  ↓
EVIDENCE
  ↓
OUTCOME```

## Decision Rights
Decision rights shall identify the decisions that may be made under the mandate and distinguish recommendation, approval, authorization and execution.

```text
RECOMMEND
   ≠
REVIEW
   ≠
APPROVE
   ≠
AUTHORIZE
   ≠
EXECUTE```

## Mandate Boundaries
Every material mandate shall define explicit exclusions. Out-of-scope decisions shall be escalated rather than inferred.

```text
PROPOSED ACTION
      ↓
WITHIN MANDATE?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → STOP / ESCALATE```

## Mandate Conditions
Conditions are facts that must remain true for the mandate to remain valid, such as organizational role, certification, risk threshold, independence, regulatory status, service criticality or delegated authority.

```text
MANDATE ACTIVE
      ↓
CONDITIONS STILL VALID?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → SUSPEND / REVIEW / REASSIGN```

## Mandate Accountability
One accountable role shall own the continued validity and performance of each material mandate. Multiple contributors do not eliminate the accountable owner.

## Delegation
Delegation may transfer defined decision rights but shall remain within the original mandate. Delegation shall identify scope, duration, conditions, reporting and revocation.

```text
MANDATE OWNER
      ↓
DELEGATE
      ↓
DEFINED RIGHT
      ↓
LIMITS / CONDITIONS
      ↓
REPORT / REVIEW```

## Independence
Where independent challenge or assurance is required, the mandate shall explicitly prohibit conflicts that would compromise that independence.

## Escalation
Mandates shall define thresholds for escalation based on materiality, risk, authority limits, security, resilience, regulatory impact and systemic consequences.

## Risk Mandate
Risk-related mandates shall specify what risk may be assessed, treated, accepted, escalated or rejected and the limits within which the authority may act.

## Security and Resilience Mandate
Security and resilience mandates shall explicitly cover the authority to protect critical services, respond to material exposure, invoke escalation, approve containment and coordinate recovery where applicable.

## Data Mandate
Data mandates shall define authority over relevant data domains, including integrity, access, lineage, retention, classification and permitted decision use.

## AI and Agent Mandate
AI and agent mandates shall define identity, permitted purpose, data scope, tool scope, autonomy boundaries, human oversight, stop conditions and accountability.

```text
AI / AGENT MANDATE
      ↓
PURPOSE
      ↓
DATA / TOOL SCOPE
      ↓
AUTONOMY LIMITS
      ↓
STOP CONDITIONS
      ↓
HUMAN / GOVERNED ACCOUNTABILITY```

## Compliance Mandate
Compliance mandates shall identify applicable obligations and define who may interpret, approve, attest, waive where lawful, or escalate compliance conditions.

## Emergency Mandate
Emergency mandates shall define the exceptional conditions under which accelerated authority may be exercised and the mandatory retrospective review.

```text
EMERGENCY CONDITION
      ↓
MANDATE ACTIVATED
      ↓
CONTROLLED ACTION
      ↓
STABILIZE
      ↓
RETROSPECTIVE REVIEW```

## Mandate Evidence
Mandate evidence shall establish purpose, scope, approving authority, effective period, conditions, responsible role, delegation and changes.

## Mandate Review
Review shall be triggered by organizational change, regulatory change, architecture change, repeated regression, material incidents, authority change, scope change or expiry.

```text
MANDATE ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
ASSESS CONTINUED VALIDITY
      ↓
RENEW / MODIFY / SUSPEND / RETIRE```

## Mandate Failure
```text
MANDATE UNCLEAR / INVALID
        ↓
STOP MATERIAL AUTHORITY USE
        ↓
IDENTIFY PROPER MANDATE
        ↓
ASSIGN / ESCALATE
        ↓
RECORD```

## Governance Learning
Repeated mandate failures shall trigger review of organizational design, authority boundaries, decision rights, accountability, delegation and escalation thresholds.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes mandate within post-closure regression governance authority. It does not replace the overarching governance or authority layers or the stage-specific regression lifecycle controls.
