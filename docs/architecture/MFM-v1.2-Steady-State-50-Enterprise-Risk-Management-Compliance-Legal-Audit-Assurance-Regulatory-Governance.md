# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the fiftieth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-49 – Enterprise Privacy, Personal Data Protection, Records Management, Information Governance & Digital Trust.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for enterprise risk management, risk governance, risk appetite, risk identification, assessment, treatment, monitoring, compliance management, regulatory obligations, legal governance, contract compliance, audit management, assurance, control testing, findings, exceptions, remediation, regulatory engagement, policy compliance, evidence management, governance reporting, risk metrics, compliance metrics, audit planning, assurance planning, risk maturity and continual governance improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain a disciplined, integrated and evidence-based approach to risk, compliance, legal obligations, audit and assurance so that material risks are understood, owned, treated, monitored and reported, and the organization remains capable of demonstrating that its obligations and controls are effectively managed.**

# 3. Enterprise Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify, assess, treat and monitor risks that could materially affect MFM objectives, services, people, information, finances, reputation or obligations.**

---

# 4. Compliance Objective

# 124. Definition of Ready

A risk, compliance obligation, regulatory change, legal matter, audit, assurance activity, finding, exception, remediation action or improvement work item is Ready when:

- The requirement, risk, matter, finding or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, scope, materiality, impact, obligations, dependencies, deadlines and applicable criteria are known.
- Assessment, control, evidence, testing, approval, remediation, reporting and acceptance requirements are defined.

---

# 125. Definition of Done

A risk, compliance, legal, audit or assurance work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement / Risk / Matter Identified
        ↓
Owner / Team Assigned
        ↓
Assessment / Control / Action Completed
        ↓
Testing / Legal / Compliance Validation Completed
        ↓
Evidence Captured
        ↓
Findings / Exceptions Addressed
        ↓
Reporting / Approval Completed
        ↓
Outcome Accepted
```

# 126. Final Risk Principle

> **Material risks must be identified, owned, assessed, treated, monitored and reported throughout their lifecycle.**

---

# 127. Final Risk Appetite Principle

> **Risk-taking must remain within approved appetite and tolerance or be explicitly accepted by an appropriately authorized owner.**

---

# 128. Final Compliance Principle

> **Applicable obligations must be identified, assigned, implemented, tested, evidenced and continuously monitored.**

---

# 129. Final Legal Principle

> **Material legal obligations, matters, contracts, claims and disputes must be governed with appropriate expertise, confidentiality, evidence and accountability.**

---

# 130. Final Audit Principle

> **Audit must provide objective, risk-based and evidence-supported assessment of governance, risk and control effectiveness.**

---

# 131. Final Assurance Principle

> **Assurance must provide proportionate and credible confidence that material risks, obligations and controls are effectively managed.**

---

# 132. Final Evidence Principle

> **Governance decisions, compliance status, audit conclusions and assurance results must be supported by reliable, traceable and appropriately protected evidence.**

---

# 133. Final Findings Principle

> **Material findings must result in owned, prioritized, time-bound remediation and independent or appropriate validation before closure.**

---

# 134. Final Exception Principle

> **Exceptions must be explicit, risk-assessed, authorized, time-bounded where appropriate and periodically reviewed.**

---

# 135. Final Regulatory Principle

> **Regulatory change and reporting must be actively managed so that MFM remains aware of applicable requirements and can demonstrate timely implementation and compliance.**

---

# 136. Final Improvement Principle

> **Risk events, compliance failures, legal matters, audit findings, assurance results and regulatory changes must continuously improve MFM governance and control capability.**

---

# 137. Final Integration Principle

> **Risk, Compliance, Legal, Audit and Assurance must integrate with Enterprise Architecture, Data Management, Cybersecurity, Privacy, Application Management, Integration, Service Management, Business Continuity, Financial Management, Procurement, Supplier Management, People Management and Project Management.**

---

# 138. Final Steady-State Governance Principle

> **MFM must maintain a disciplined, integrated and evidence-based approach to risk, compliance, legal obligations, audit and assurance so that material risks are understood, owned, treated, monitored and reported, and the organization remains capable of demonstrating that its obligations and controls are effectively managed.**
