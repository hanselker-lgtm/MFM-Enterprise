# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities are developed, operated and evolved in a sustainable, responsible and long-term viable manner.

The Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Framework shall provide:

- sustainability architecture principles
- sustainable intelligence frameworks
- resource optimisation models
- energy efficiency structures
- capability sustainability models
- sustainability integration structures
- sustainability governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through sustainable and responsible ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Sustainability Model
- Sustainable Intelligence Architecture
- Resource Sustainability Architecture
- Energy Efficient Intelligence Architecture
- Capability Sustainability Model
- Sustainability Architecture Integration
- Sustainability Governance Model
- Sustainability Evolution Framework
- Sustainability Metrics
- Sustainability Maturity Model
- Sustainability Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Long-Term Sustainability

Intelligence capabilities shall support long-term viability and continuous operation.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Resource Utilisation

Resources shall be used efficiently and responsibly.

---

## Principle 3 – Energy Efficiency

Intelligence capabilities shall optimise energy consumption and operational efficiency.

---

## Principle 4 – Sustainable Development

Intelligence evolution shall consider long-term impact and responsibility.

---

## Principle 5 – Capability Preservation

Critical Intelligence capabilities shall remain sustainable over time.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Sustainability Evolution

Sustainability capability shall continuously improve.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Sustainability Governance

Sustainability mechanisms shall maintain transparency, accountability and controlled development.
