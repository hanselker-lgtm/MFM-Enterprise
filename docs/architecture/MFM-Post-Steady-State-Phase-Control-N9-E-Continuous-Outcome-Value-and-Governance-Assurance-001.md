# 1. Purpose

N9-E establishes the continuous assurance layer for:

```text
Outcome
Benefit
Value
Evidence
Governance Effectiveness
Condition Management
Risk
Traceability
Change
Decision Quality
```

The purpose is to determine whether enterprise changes remain aligned with intended outcomes and value, and whether the governance mechanisms remain effective.

N9-E does not certify business outcomes or financial value unless the applicable authority explicitly provides such certification.

> **N9-E establishes the Continuous Outcome, Value & Governance Assurance layer of the authorized N9 workstream.
