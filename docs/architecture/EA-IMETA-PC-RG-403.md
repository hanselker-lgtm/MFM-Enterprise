## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory procedural verification layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-403, determining whether the substantive validation of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 was correctly performed, evidenced, authorized, decided, recorded and implemented.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 determines whether RG-401 verification is substantively effective. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-403 verifies that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402's validation of that verification was correctly performed and implemented.

```text
RG-400 → VALIDATE
RG-401 → VERIFY
RG-402 → VALIDATE
RG-403 → VERIFY
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-403 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
WAS EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 CORRECTLY PERFORMED?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 validation SHALL NOT automatically prove that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 was correctly verified.

## Key Verification Tests

### EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 Validation Trigger
The verifier SHALL confirm that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 was initiated on a valid, applicable and timely basis.

### Correct Basis
The verifier SHALL confirm that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 used the correct RG-401 verification as its basis and preserved upstream traceability.

### Current Baseline
The verifier SHALL confirm that the current reality used by EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 was actually current and sufficiently evidenced.

### Validation Effect
The verifier SHALL confirm that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 correctly assessed whether RG-401 detected material weaknesses in RG-400.

```text
MATERIAL DEFECT
        ↓
RG-401 DETECTION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 VALIDATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-403 VERIFICATION
        ↓
CORRECT?
├── YES → VERIFIED
└── NO → VERIFICATION FAILURE
```

### Current Outcome
The verifier SHALL confirm that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 correctly assessed the actual current assurance outcome.

### Validation Integrity
The verifier SHALL confirm that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 correctly assessed the integrity of RG-401 and did not confuse procedural completeness with substantive effectiveness.

### Verification Effectiveness
The verifier SHALL confirm that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 used appropriate evidence and methods to determine actual effectiveness.

### Controls and Risk
Material controls and residual risk determinations SHALL be traceable to current evidence and criteria.

### Dependencies and Obligations
Material dependencies and continuing obligations SHALL be verified for correct treatment.

### Conditions and Persistence
Conditions, restrictions and persistence requirements SHALL be verified for correct assessment and implementation.

### Invalidating Conditions
Material contradictions and failures SHALL be correctly identified and acted upon.

### Evidence, Authority, Scope and Criteria
Evidence SHALL be current, relevant, sufficient and traceable. Authority, scope and criteria SHALL match the governed requirements.

### Decision, Recording, Communication and Implementation
The recorded, communicated and implemented state SHALL match the EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 validation decision.

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-402 DECISION
        ↓

        ↓
COMMUNICATED STATE
        ↓
IMPLEMENTED STATE
        ↓
ALL MATCH?
├── YES → VERIFIED
└── NO → CORRECTION REQUIRED
```
