# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the seventy-fourth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-73 – Enterprise Cybersecurity, Information Security, Security Operations, Threat Management, Vulnerability & Security Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for data protection, privacy, information governance, records management, data classification, information handling, retention, disposal, legal holds, data lifecycle management, personal data governance, privacy risk, data subject rights, privacy incidents, data sharing, third-party data processing, international data considerations, information ownership, data quality, data lineage, data access governance, data security coordination, records assurance, data findings, data exceptions, remediation, data protection metrics, information governance dashboards, maturity and continual data protection improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must protect, govern, retain and dispose of information appropriately throughout its lifecycle while respecting privacy, legal, regulatory, contractual and business requirements.**

# 3. Data Protection Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Protect information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss and inappropriate retention or disposal.**

# 4. Privacy Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, transparently and appropriately throughout its lifecycle.**

# 5. Information Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure information is created, classified, used, shared, retained and disposed of according to business, legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.**

# 6. Records Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain authoritative records with appropriate retention, integrity, accessibility, preservation and disposition controls.**

# 7. Data Lifecycle Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Govern information from creation and acquisition through use, sharing, retention, archival and secure disposal.**

# 88. Definition of Ready

A data domain, privacy assessment, data-sharing arrangement, retention rule, legal hold, data subject request, disposal action, data incident, data exception or remediation item is Ready when purpose, owner, data scope, classification, legal or business requirements, dependencies, risk, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
