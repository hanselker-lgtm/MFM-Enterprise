# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Consumption Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Delivery principles are inherited from EA-451.

All Enterprise Data Consumption Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Consumption Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and scalable consumption of Enterprise information by business applications, services, analytics platforms, AI solutions and external consumers.

Enterprise Data Consumption Services shall

- provide standardized consumption interfaces
- simplify consumer integration
- improve interoperability
- strengthen security
- improve scalability
- optimize performance
- enable governed access
- improve operational efficiency
- support automation
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Consumption Services shall provide reusable capabilities for trusted Enterprise information consumption.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise applications
- business services
- APIs
- event consumers
- streaming consumers
- analytics platforms
- AI platforms
- reporting platforms
- cloud services
- external consumers

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or implementation technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Consumption Services Principles

Enterprise Data Consumption Services shall follow these principles.

## Consumer Centric Design

Consumption Services shall provide simple, consistent and reusable interfaces designed around consumer needs while preserving Enterprise governance.

---

## Secure by Default

Every consumption service shall enforce Enterprise authentication, authorization, encryption and auditing.

---

## Metadata Driven Consumption

Consumption Services shall use Enterprise metadata for discovery, routing, policy enforcement and lifecycle management.

---

## Standardized Service Interfaces

All consumption capabilities shall expose standardized Enterprise service interfaces to ensure interoperability and reuse.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Consumption Services shall remain independent of programming languages, middleware products, cloud providers and infrastructure technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Consumption Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed consumption of Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating standardized consumer services, service interfaces, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled consumption of authoritative Enterprise information while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Consumption Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise consumption capabilities across the Enterprise.
