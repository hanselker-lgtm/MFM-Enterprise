## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-closure layer defining when a resolved material condition may formally leave active response governance, how closure is authorized and evidenced, and how post-closure monitoring preserves the ability to detect regression and reopen the matter.

## Core Principle
Closure is a governed lifecycle transition, not an administrative status change. A condition may be closed only when resolution is verified, closure criteria are satisfied, residual risk is explicitly governed, evidence is complete, authority is established and post-closure obligations are transferred to the appropriate monitoring regime.

```text
RESOLVED CONDITION
      ↓
CLOSURE CRITERIA
      ↓
CLOSURE EVIDENCE / VERIFICATION
      ↓
RESIDUAL RISK / FOLLOW-ON OBLIGATIONS
      ↓
AUTHORIZED CLOSURE DECISION
      ↓
CLOSED
      ↓
POST-CLOSURE MONITORING
      ↓
REGRESSION? → REOPEN / REASSESS / REVALIDATE
```

## Closure Quality Test
```text
VALID RESOLUTION
+
VERIFIED CLOSURE CRITERIA
+
COMPLETE EVIDENCE
+
AUTHORIZED DECISION
+
ACCOUNTABILITY
+
RESIDUAL RISK DISPOSITION
+
POST-CLOSURE HANDOVER
+
REOPENING RULES
=
VALID GOVERNED CLOSURE
```

## Closure Status Model
```text
OPEN
READY FOR CLOSURE
UNDER CLOSURE REVIEW
CLOSED
CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS
CLOSED WITH RESIDUAL RISK
CLOSURE REJECTED
CLOSURE UNDETERMINED
REOPENED
SUPERSEDED
UNDER POST-CLOSURE MONITORING
```

```text
CLOSURE SHALL REQUIRE EXPLICIT CRITERIA

```

```text
UNDETERMINED SHALL NOT BE TREATED AS CLOSED
```

```text
ADMINISTRATIVE TASK COMPLETION SHALL NOT BY ITSELF CONSTITUTE CLOSURE
```

```text
CLOSURE SHALL PRESERVE THE COMPLETE TRIGGER, ALERT, ESCALATION, RESOLUTION AND VERIFICATION HISTORY
```

```text
POST-CLOSURE MONITORING SHALL BE ESTABLISHED WHERE MATERIAL REGRESSION IS POSSIBLE
```

```text
SECURITY, RESILIENCE, SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE CLOSURE SHALL RECEIVE APPROPRIATE GOVERNANCE
```

```text
AI AND AGENT CLOSURE SHALL CONFIRM THAT THE GOVERNED STATE AND BOUNDARIES ARE STABLE OR EXPLICITLY CONTROLLED
```

```text
CLOSURE SHALL NOT PREVENT REOPENING WHEN NEW MATERIAL EVIDENCE INVALIDATES THE CLOSURE BASIS
```

```text
REPEATED REOPENING SHALL TRIGGER GOVERNANCE LEARNING AND POSSIBLE SYSTEMIC REMEDIATION
```

## Closure Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Resolved Condition | Condition eligible for closure |
| Criteria | Requirements for closure |
| Evidence | Proof supporting closure |
| Verification | Confirmation of criteria |
| Authority | Authorized closure decision maker |
| Accountability | Accountable role |
| Residual Risk | Remaining governed exposure |
| Follow-on | Monitoring or other obligations |
| Decision | Closure determination |
| Reopening | Conditions invalidating closure |

## Closure Objective

The objective is to formally transition a verified resolved condition from active response governance into an appropriate closed and monitored state without losing accountability, evidence, residual-risk visibility or the ability to respond to regression.

## Closure Definition

Closure is the authorized lifecycle transition that confirms the material condition has satisfied resolution and closure criteria and that all required follow-on controls have been established.

## Closure Scope

Scope shall identify the affected systems, services, controls, processes, data, environments, users, dependencies and lifecycle boundaries covered by closure.

## Closure Authority

Authority shall define who may approve closure, reject closure, accept residual risk where authorized, assign post-closure obligations and reopen the matter.

## Closure Criteria

Closure criteria shall be explicit, current and aligned with the original condition and resolution.

```text
RESOLUTION VERIFIED
↓
CLOSURE CRITERIA SATISFIED?
├── YES → CLOSURE ELIGIBLE
└── NO → CONTINUE / REJECT / ESCALATE
```

## Closure Evidence

Closure evidence shall include resolution results, verification, relevant measurements, decision records, residual-risk disposition and required follow-on monitoring references.

## Closure Accountability

Accountability shall remain identifiable through closure and transition. Closure shall not erase ownership of post-closure obligations.

## Closure Timing

Closure shall occur only after required evidence and verification are available. Premature closure shall be treated as a governance defect.

## Closure Verification

Verification shall confirm that the resolution remains valid at the time of closure and that required closure conditions are satisfied.

```text
READY FOR CLOSURE
↓
VERIFY
↓
CRITERIA SATISFIED?
├── YES → CLOSE
└── NO → REJECT / CONTINUE
```

## Closure Decision

The closure decision shall explicitly classify the outcome.

```text
CLOSED
CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS
CLOSED WITH RESIDUAL RISK
CLOSURE REJECTED
CLOSURE UNDETERMINED
```

## Closure State

The resulting state shall be explicit and distinguish ordinary closure from conditional closure, residual-risk closure, suspension or reopening.

## Security Closure

Security closure shall confirm that the relevant security condition has been resolved or adequately controlled and that required monitoring or follow-on controls are active.

## Resilience Closure

Resilience closure shall confirm restoration or controlled operation of required continuity, recovery, capacity and dependency conditions.

## Compliance Closure

Compliance closure shall confirm that applicable non-conformance or exposure has been addressed to the required standard and that supporting evidence is retained.

## Data Closure

Data closure shall confirm required integrity, quality, lineage, access, retention and authorized-use conditions or explicitly governed residual exposure.

## AI and Agent Closure

AI and agent closure shall confirm that authority, policy, data, tool, autonomy and behavioural boundaries are restored or explicitly constrained and monitored.

```text
AI / AGENT RESOLUTION VERIFIED
↓
CLOSURE CRITERIA
↓
BOUNDARIES STABLE / CONTROLLED?
├── YES → CLOSE + POST-CLOSURE MONITORING
└── NO → REJECT / REOPEN / SUSPEND / ESCALATE
```

## Closure Failure

Closure failure occurs when criteria are not met, evidence is insufficient, verification fails, residual risk is unmanaged or required authority is unavailable.

```text
CLOSURE FAILURE
↓
REMAIN OPEN / REOPEN
↓
REMEDIATE / REASSESS
↓
REVERIFY
```

## Closure Reopening

A closed condition shall be reopened when new evidence demonstrates regression, recurrence, ineffective resolution, material scope expansion or invalidation of the closure basis.

```text
CLOSED
↓
REGRESSION / NEW MATERIAL EVIDENCE
↓
REOPEN
↓
REASSESS / REVALIDATE
↓
REMEDIATE / RE-CLOSE
```

## Closure Review and Learning

Closure outcomes shall be reviewed for premature closure, weak criteria, incomplete evidence, unmanaged residual risk, repeated reopening and inadequate post-closure monitoring.

## Closure Determination Model
```text
RESOLUTION VERIFIED?
├── NO → NOT READY FOR CLOSURE
└── YES
     ↓
CLOSURE CRITERIA SATISFIED?
├── NO → CONTINUE / REJECT / ESCALATE
└── YES
     ↓
RESIDUAL RISK GOVERNED?
├── NO → RESOLVE / ACCEPT AUTHORIZED RISK / REJECT
└── YES
     ↓
POST-CLOSURE OBLIGATIONS ESTABLISHED?
├── NO → NOT READY
└── YES
     ↓
AUTHORIZED DECISION?
├── NO → ESCALATE
└── YES → CLOSE
```

## Closure and Residual Risk
Residual risk shall be explicit. Where residual risk remains, the closure record shall identify its owner, treatment, acceptance authority where required, monitoring and review conditions.

```text
RESOLUTION
↓
RESIDUAL RISK IDENTIFIED?
├── NO → NORMAL CLOSURE
└── YES
     ↓
AUTHORIZED AND CONTROLLED?
     ├── NO → CONTINUE / ESCALATE
     └── YES → CONDITIONAL / RESIDUAL-RISK CLOSURE
```

## Post-Closure Handover
Closure shall transfer all required monitoring, alerting, escalation and reopening obligations into the post-closure governance model. No material obligation shall disappear because the primary finding has been closed.

```text
CLOSURE
↓
POST-CLOSURE OBLIGATIONS
├── MONITORING
├── ALERTING
├── ESCALATION
├── REASSESSMENT
└── REOPENING
↓
ACTIVE POST-CLOSURE GOVERNANCE
```

## Closure Anti-Gaming Control
Closure shall not be used to improve metrics, remove findings from reporting, satisfy deadlines or avoid escalation while the governed condition remains materially unresolved. The actual state and closure criteria remain controlling.

## Closure Change Control
Changes to closure criteria, verification, authority, residual-risk rules, monitoring handover or reopening conditions shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated.

```text
CURRENT CLOSURE MODEL
↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
↓
NEW VERSION
↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

Historical closure decisions, evidence, verification and reopening events shall remain preserved according to applicable retention and governance requirements.

This document specializes the mandatory-closure layer beneath mandatory resolution. It does not replace governance, authority, mandate, role, responsibility, accountability, outcome, criteria, success, success conditions, mandatory conditions, non-negotiable requirements, applicability, state, verification, evidence, measurement, threshold, classification, consequence, response, effectiveness, reassessment, revalidation, acceptance, reliance, monitoring, alerting, escalation or resolution layers.
