# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Reasoning Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities analyse information, establish relationships, perform inference, evaluate alternatives and generate reasoning-based decisions.

The Intelligence Reasoning Architecture Framework shall provide:

- reasoning architecture principles
- analytical reasoning models
- inference capability models
- evaluation frameworks
- decision reasoning mechanisms
- reasoning governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through reasoning, inference and analytical decision support.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Reasoning Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Reasoning Model
- Reasoning Intelligence Architecture
- Reasoning Knowledge Architecture
- Reasoning Capability Model
- Reasoning Architecture Integration
- Reasoning Governance Model
- Reasoning Evolution Framework
- Reasoning Metrics
- Reasoning Maturity Model
- Reasoning Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

# 3. Intelligence Reasoning Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Reasoning Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Analytical Understanding

Intelligence capability shall analyse information to create structured conclusions.

---

## Principle 2 – Logical Inference

Reasoning shall establish relationships and derive meaningful conclusions.

---

## Principle 3 – Evidence-Based Evaluation

Reasoning processes shall be based on reliable information and knowledge.

---

## Principle 4 – Contextual Reasoning

Reasoning shall consider context, dependencies and relationships.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Learning

Reasoning capability shall improve through experience and feedback.

---

## Principle 6 – Adaptive Reasoning

Reasoning shall evolve according to changing conditions.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Reasoning

Reasoning development shall maintain transparency, governance and accountability.
