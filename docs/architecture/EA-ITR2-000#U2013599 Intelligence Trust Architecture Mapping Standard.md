# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Trust Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how trust is created, maintained, verified and governed across Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Trust Architecture Framework shall provide:

- trust architecture principles
- trusted intelligence frameworks
- transparency models
- explainability structures
- integrity mechanisms
- verification frameworks
- assurance models
- trust governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through trusted, responsible and verifiable ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Trust Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Trust Model
- Trusted Intelligence Architecture
- Explainable Intelligence Architecture
- Integrity Architecture
- Verification Architecture
- Assurance Architecture
- Trust Architecture Integration
- Trust Governance Model
- Trust Evolution Framework
- Trust Metrics
- Trust Maturity Model
- Trust Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Trust Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Trust Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Transparency

Intelligence capabilities shall provide visibility into purpose, operation and outcomes.

---

## Principle 2 – Explainability

Intelligence decisions and recommendations shall be understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 3 – Integrity

Intelligence capabilities shall maintain accuracy, consistency and reliability.

---

## Principle 4 – Verification

Intelligence outputs shall be validated and assessed.

---

## Principle 5 – Assurance

Trust shall be supported through continuous control and evaluation.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Trust Evolution

Trust capability shall continuously improve.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Trust Governance

Trust mechanisms shall maintain accountability, ethics and controlled development.
