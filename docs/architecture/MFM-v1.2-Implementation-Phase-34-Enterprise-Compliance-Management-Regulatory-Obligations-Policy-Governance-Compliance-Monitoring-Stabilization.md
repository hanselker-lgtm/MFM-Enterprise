The purpose of this phase is to establish the enterprise compliance-management, regulatory-obligation, policy-governance and compliance-monitoring baseline for MFM.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain a controlled relationship between applicable obligations, policies, controls, owners, evidence, compliance assessments, exceptions and management reporting so that compliance status remains visible, attributable, reviewable and actionable.**

# 129. Final Compliance Principle

> **Every material compliance obligation must be identifiable, owned, mapped to requirements and controls, supported by evidence and subject to review.**

---

# 130. Final Policy Principle

> **Policies must translate governance expectations into controlled and reviewable organizational behavior.**

---

# 131. Final Regulatory Principle

> **Regulatory change must be identified early enough to assess its impact and implement required changes before applicable deadlines.**

---

# 132. Final Control Principle

> **Compliance is not demonstrated merely by having a policy; material requirements must be connected to operating controls and evidence.**

---

# 133. Final Evidence Principle

> **Compliance conclusions must be supported by reliable, traceable and appropriately retained evidence.**

---

# 134. Final Exception Principle

> **Exceptions must be explicit, risk-assessed, approved and reviewed rather than becoming invisible permanent deviations.**

---

# 135. Final Monitoring Principle

> **Compliance monitoring should provide actionable early warning of material deviations and approaching obligations.**

---

# 136. Final Incident Principle

> **Compliance incidents must be assessed for regulatory, contractual, financial, security and privacy consequences and handled through controlled response.**

---

# 137. Final Assurance Principle

> **Compliance assurance should challenge the adequacy of requirements, controls, evidence and assessments rather than merely confirm their existence.**

---

# 138. Final Integration Principle

> **Compliance must connect risks, controls, policies, processes, data, incidents, changes, vendors and evidence across MFM rather than operate as an isolated register.**

---

# 139. Final Lifecycle Principle

> **Obligations, policies, controls, assessments and evidence must remain historically traceable as requirements evolve.**

---

# 140. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should operate compliance as an integrated governance capability in which obligations become actionable requirements, requirements map to controls, controls produce evidence, evidence supports assessments, and deviations trigger governed remediation.**
