# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Architecture governing spatial information, mapping services, geospatial analytics, location intelligence and geographic data management across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise GIS Architecture shall

- establish standardized geospatial services
- support enterprise-wide location intelligence
- provide consistent spatial data management
- enable real-time geographic visualization
- support operational decision-making
- integrate with Enterprise Digital Twins
- support Enterprise IoT
- improve situational awareness
- ensure interoperability
- remain technology independent

Enterprise GIS shall function as a reusable Enterprise capability supporting all Business and Technology Domains.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- geographic information systems
- spatial databases
- mapping platforms
- coordinate systems
- location services
- geospatial analytics
- raster data
- vector data
- marine geographic systems
- enterprise mapping services

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or GIS software vendor.

# 3. Enterprise GIS Principles

Enterprise Geographic Information Systems shall be governed by the following principles.

## Single Source of Geographic Truth

Enterprise geographic information shall be maintained consistently across all systems.

---

## Standardized Spatial Data

Spatial information shall follow Enterprise-approved data models and standards.

---

## Location Intelligence Everywhere

Enterprise applications shall be capable of using geographic context where business value exists.

---

## Real-Time Geographic Awareness

GIS services shall support real-time operational awareness whenever practical.

---

## Secure Geographic Information

Spatial information shall comply with Enterprise Security Architecture throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise GIS Architecture shall remain independent of individual GIS vendors, mapping providers and cloud platforms.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Architecture establishes the standardized foundation for spatial information management, mapping services, geospatial analytics and enterprise-wide location intelligence across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

By integrating Enterprise Digital Twins, Internet of Things, Artificial Intelligence, Enterprise Automation, Observability and Edge Computing, the Enterprise enables secure, scalable and interoperable geographic services that support operational planning, situational awareness and intelligent decision-making.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise GIS initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing geographic information systems across the Enterprise.
