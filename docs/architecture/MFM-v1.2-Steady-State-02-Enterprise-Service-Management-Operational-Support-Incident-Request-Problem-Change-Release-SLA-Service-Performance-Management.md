# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the second document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-01 – Enterprise Steady-State Operating Model, Operational Governance, Service Ownership, Lifecycle Management & Continuous Improvement Foundation.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent service-management operating model for MFM, covering service ownership, service catalog management, operational support, incident management, service requests, problem management, change enablement, release management, service levels, operational performance, monitoring, escalation, knowledge and service assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must provide stable, measurable and supportable services through controlled service management, clear ownership, defined operational processes, measurable service levels and continuous operational assurance.**

A service-management work item is Ready when:

- Service, incident, request, problem, change, release or performance requirement is clearly identified.
