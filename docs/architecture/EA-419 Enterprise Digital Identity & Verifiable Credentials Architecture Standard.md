# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Digital Identity & Verifiable Credentials Architecture governing decentralized identities, verifiable credentials, trusted identity ecosystems and privacy-preserving identity services across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Digital Identity Architecture shall

- establish trusted digital identities
- support decentralized identity models
- enable verifiable credentials
- strengthen digital trust
- improve identity interoperability
- support privacy by design
- integrate with Enterprise Zero Trust Architecture
- support trusted business collaboration
- reduce identity fraud
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Digital Identity shall function as a reusable Enterprise capability supporting trusted digital interactions across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- decentralized identities (DIDs)
- verifiable credentials
- digital identity wallets
- credential issuers
- credential holders
- credential verifiers
- trust registries
- digital signatures
- identity federation
- enterprise trust services

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, cloud provider or identity technology platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Digital Identity Principles

Enterprise Digital Identity Architecture shall be governed by the following principles.

## Identity by Design

Enterprise identities shall be established as trusted digital assets from creation through retirement.

---

## User-Controlled Identity

Individuals and organizations shall retain appropriate control over their digital identities and credentials.

---

## Verifiable Trust

Identity assertions shall be cryptographically verifiable.

---

## Privacy by Design

Digital Identity solutions shall minimize disclosure of personal information.

---

## Enterprise Interoperability

Identity services shall integrate using standardized Enterprise interfaces and open identity standards.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Digital Identity Architecture shall remain independent of specific identity vendors, blockchain technologies and cloud providers.
