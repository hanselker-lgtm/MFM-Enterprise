# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise business continuity throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise business continuity ensures that critical business functions continue operating during and after disruptive events through standardized planning, continuity strategies, crisis management and governance while preserving operational effectiveness and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Business Continuity Planning
- Critical Business Functions
- Continuity Strategies
- Crisis Management
- Communication Planning
- Continuity Testing
- Business Impact Analysis
- Governance

All enterprise business continuity implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

## BC-001

## BC-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Business Continuity Principles

Enterprise business continuity shall follow these principles.

- Continuity by Design
- Business Impact Driven
- Coordinated Response
- Operational Continuity
- Standardized Planning
- Governance by Default
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Business continuity implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Business Continuity Domains

Enterprise business continuity shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Business Continuity Planning
- Business Impact Analysis
- Critical Business Functions
- Crisis Management
- Communication Management
- Continuity Testing
- Recovery Coordination
- Operational Readiness

Additional business continuity domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Business Continuity Ownership

Each business continuity domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the business continuity lifecycle.

---

# 7. Business Continuity Governance

Enterprise business continuity governance shall define

- business continuity governance
- continuity approval
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- continuity verification
- governance reporting

Business continuity governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Business Continuity Responsibilities

Enterprise business continuity shall provide controlled management of critical business operations.

Business continuity responsibilities shall

- separate business continuity from business execution
- coordinate continuity ownership
- ensure operational continuity
- validate continuity objectives
- preserve continuity traceability
- support enterprise operational resilience

Business continuity implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Business Impact Analysis

Enterprise business continuity shall implement standardized business impact analysis.

Business impact analysis shall

- identify critical business functions
- classify operational impact
- classify financial impact
- classify regulatory impact
- preserve analysis history
- maintain analysis traceability

Business impact analysis shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Critical Business Functions

Enterprise business continuity shall identify and document critical business functions.

Critical business functions shall

- define operational priorities
- define service dependencies
- define recovery priorities
- preserve function history
- maintain function traceability
- support operational planning

Critical business functions shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Continuity Strategies

Enterprise business continuity shall implement standardized continuity strategies.

Continuity strategies shall

- support operational continuity
- support alternate operating procedures
- support resource prioritization
- preserve strategy history
- maintain strategy traceability
- support resilience diagnostics

Continuity strategies shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Crisis Management

Enterprise business continuity shall implement standardized crisis management.

Crisis management shall

- support coordinated incident response
- support escalation procedures
- support executive decision making
- preserve crisis history
- maintain crisis traceability
- support operational resilience

Crisis management shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Business Continuity Dependencies

Enterprise business continuity shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- disaster recovery capabilities
- recovery infrastructure
- communication platforms
- operational support services
- monitoring systems
- enterprise governance

Business continuity implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Business Continuity Documentation

Each business continuity domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- continuity objectives
- ownership information
- business impact analyses
- continuity strategies
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Business Continuity Lifecycle

Enterprise business continuity shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Planned
- Designed
- Classified
- Implemented
- Verified
- Operational
- Tested
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Retired

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Business Continuity Quality Attributes

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- reliability
- availability
- resilience
- recoverability
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability
- operational readiness

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the business continuity lifecycle.

---

# 17. Business Continuity Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized business continuity registry.

The registry shall contain

- continuity identifiers
- ownership assignments
- business impact analyses
- lifecycle status
- continuity strategies
- crisis management configurations
- documentation references
- governance status

The business continuity registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise business continuity.

---

# 18. Business Continuity Reviews

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- continuity quality
- business impact analysis completeness
- continuity strategy effectiveness
- crisis management readiness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Business Continuity Metrics

Enterprise business continuity shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- continuity plan coverage
- business impact analysis completion
- continuity testing frequency
- recovery readiness
- crisis response effectiveness
- operational disruption duration
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous business continuity improvement.

---

# 20. Business Continuity Verification

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm continuity objectives
- verify business impact analyses
- verify continuity strategies
- verify crisis management procedures
- verify continuity testing
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve operational readiness

Business continuity verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Business Continuity Improvement

Enterprise business continuity shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve continuity readiness
- improve crisis response
- improve operational continuity
- improve organizational resilience
- strengthen governance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall handle business continuity-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify continuity planning failures
- classify crisis management failures
- classify communication failures
- classify continuity testing failures
- classify operational readiness failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Business continuity exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational continuity or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Business continuity implementations may depend upon

- approved disaster recovery capabilities
- approved recovery infrastructure
- approved communication platforms
- approved operational support services
- approved monitoring systems
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Business continuity implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external business continuity services

Business continuity capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A business continuity implementation is compliant when

- Business continuity responsibilities are documented.
- Business impact analyses are completed.
- Critical business functions are identified.
- Continuity strategies are documented.
- Crisis management procedures are implemented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Business Continuity Registry is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Continuity verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Business Impact Analysis

Enterprise business continuity shall never operate without documented Business Impact Analysis (BIA).

---

## Undefined Critical Business Functions

Critical business functions shall never remain undocumented or unprioritized.

---

## Untested Continuity Plans

Business continuity plans shall never remain untested for critical enterprise operations.

---

## Missing Crisis Communication

Enterprise continuity plans shall never omit documented crisis communication procedures.

---

## Undocumented Business Continuity Dependencies

Business continuity implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Business Continuity Outside Governance

Business continuity implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- continuity quality
- business impact analysis completeness
- continuity strategy effectiveness
- crisis management readiness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational continuity
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Business Continuity Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing business continuity throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and business operations implement standardized business continuity planning, business impact analysis, continuity strategies, crisis management, verification and governance through controlled lifecycle management and continuous improvement while preserving operational continuity, organizational resilience and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise business continuity implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
