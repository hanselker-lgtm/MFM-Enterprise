# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational assurance architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities, processes, information and outcomes are verified, validated, controlled and continuously improved.

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- assurance operating principles
- quality assurance frameworks
- verification structures
- validation structures
- control assurance models
- compliance assurance models
- performance assurance models
- reliability assurance structures
- outcome assurance models
- audit assurance structures
- assurance monitoring structures
- assurance automation structures
- assurance evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for reliable, controlled and continuously improving Intelligence operations.

# 3. Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Assurance by Design

Assurance shall be integrated into Intelligence capabilities from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Verification

Intelligence capabilities shall be verified against defined requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Validation

Intelligence outcomes shall be validated against expected results.

---

## Principle 4 – Evidence-Based Assurance

Assurance decisions shall be supported by evidence.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Control

Controls shall continuously ensure expected behaviour.

---

## Principle 6 – Independent Assessment

Assurance activities shall provide objective evaluation where required.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Assurance Evolution

Assurance capability shall continuously improve.
