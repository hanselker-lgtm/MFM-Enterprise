# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how information and data are transformed into structured knowledge, contextual understanding and intelligent capability.

The Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Framework shall provide:

- knowledge architecture principles
- knowledge representation frameworks
- knowledge capability models
- knowledge integration structures
- knowledge governance model
- knowledge evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through trusted, contextual and continuously evolving knowledge ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Knowledge Model
- Knowledge Intelligence Architecture
- Knowledge Representation Architecture
- Knowledge Capability Model
- Knowledge Architecture Integration
- Knowledge Governance Model
- Knowledge Evolution Framework
- Knowledge Metrics
- Knowledge Maturity Model
- Knowledge Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Knowledge Value Alignment

Knowledge shall support enterprise objectives and Intelligence capability development.

---

## Principle 2 – Contextual Understanding

Knowledge shall include meaning, relationships and operational context.

---

## Principle 3 – Knowledge Reliability

Knowledge shall maintain accuracy, relevance and trustworthiness.

---

## Principle 4 – Knowledge Integration

Knowledge shall connect data, experience, models and intelligence capabilities.

---

## Principle 5 – Knowledge Accessibility

Knowledge shall be available to authorised Intelligence capabilities.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Knowledge Evolution

Knowledge shall improve through learning, experience and adaptation.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Knowledge Governance

Knowledge shall be managed with accountability, transparency and ethical alignment.
