# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the seventy-second document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-71 – Enterprise Project, Programme, Portfolio, PMO, Delivery Governance, Benefits & Change Portfolio Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for business continuity, operational resilience, disaster recovery, crisis management, emergency response, continuity planning, recovery planning, business impact analysis, critical service identification, recovery objectives, recovery dependencies, recovery strategies, recovery procedures, crisis governance, crisis communications, emergency decision-making, technology recovery, application recovery, infrastructure recovery, data recovery, workplace recovery, supplier continuity, recovery testing, exercises, continuity assurance, resilience findings, exceptions, remediation, continuity metrics, resilience dashboards, maturity and continual resilience improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must remain capable of protecting people, maintaining critical services and recovering important business operations following disruptive events, within approved risk, continuity and recovery objectives.**

# 3. Business Continuity Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain critical business services and recover disrupted operations within approved continuity objectives.**

# 4. Operational Resilience Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure critical services can continue operating within approved tolerance levels despite disruption.**

# 5. Disaster Recovery Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore technology, data and supporting capabilities within approved recovery objectives.**

# 6. Crisis Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide coordinated leadership, decision-making, communication and response during significant disruptive events.**

# 7. Recovery Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore critical operations in a prioritized and controlled sequence using tested recovery capabilities.**

# 89. Definition of Ready

A continuity plan, recovery procedure, crisis exercise, disaster-recovery test, business impact analysis, resilience initiative, supplier continuity assessment, exception or remediation item is Ready when scope, owner, critical service, dependencies, impact, recovery objectives, roles, assumptions, communications, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
