# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Ecosystem Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational ecosystem architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities operate across organisations, users, services, platforms, partners, technologies and external actors.

The Intelligence Ecosystem Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- ecosystem operating principles
- ecosystem governance frameworks
- ecosystem lifecycle models
- ecosystem management models
- partner models
- collaboration models
- integration models
- service ecosystem models
- platform ecosystem models
- data exchange models
- trust models
- security models
- resilience models
- monitoring models
- improvement models
- evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for a connected, trusted and continuously evolving Intelligence ecosystem.

## Principle 4 – Controlled Ecosystem Integration

---

## Principle 5 – Secure Ecosystem Operation

Ecosystem activities shall maintain appropriate security controls.

## Principle 6 – Resilient Ecosystem Capability

The ecosystem shall maintain continuity under changing conditions.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Ecosystem Evolution

The ecosystem shall continuously improve and adapt.
