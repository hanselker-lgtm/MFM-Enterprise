This standard defines the Enterprise Data Archiving Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

All Enterprise Data Archiving Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Archiving Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed long-term preservation of Enterprise data across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Archiving Services shall

- provide centralized archive management
- standardize archival processes
- support long-term data preservation
- ensure data integrity over time
- support regulatory requirements
- support business continuity
- reduce operational storage complexity
- strengthen data lifecycle governance
- support privacy requirements
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure archive traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Archiving Services shall provide trusted and reusable archival capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Data Archiving Services Principles

Enterprise Data Archiving Services shall follow these principles.

## Archiving by Design

Archiving capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Preservation by Default

Enterprise data requiring long-term preservation shall be archived according to approved retention, compliance and business requirements.

---

## Data Integrity Preservation

Archived data shall maintain authenticity, integrity, availability and traceability throughout the archive lifecycle.

---

## Controlled Accessibility

Archived data shall remain accessible only through approved authorization, governance and retrieval processes.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Archiving Services shall remain independent of vendors, archive platforms, storage providers, cloud providers and implementation technologies.
