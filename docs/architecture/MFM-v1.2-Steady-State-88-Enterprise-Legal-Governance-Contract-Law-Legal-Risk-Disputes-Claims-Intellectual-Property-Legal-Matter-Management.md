# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eighty-eighth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-87 – Enterprise Compliance Management, Regulatory Obligations, Policy Compliance, Control Framework & Compliance Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for legal governance, legal obligations, legal risk, contract governance, contract lifecycle management, legal review coordination, contractual commitments, contractual rights, disputes, claims, litigation coordination, legal matters, legal advice management, outside counsel, intellectual property, licensing, confidentiality, legal records, legal holds, evidence preservation, regulatory legal matters, legal entity coordination, delegated authority, legal approvals, legal assurance, legal findings, legal exceptions, legal remediation, legal metrics, legal dashboards, legal maturity and continual enterprise legal governance improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage legal obligations, contracts, legal risks, disputes, claims and intellectual property through accountable, controlled and traceable legal governance.**

# 3. Legal Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish clear authority, accountability and control over material legal matters and obligations.**

# 4. Contract Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure contracts are reviewed, approved, executed, stored, monitored and terminated through controlled lifecycle management.**

# 5. Legal Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and manage legal risks before they create unacceptable business, financial, regulatory or reputational consequences.**

# 6. Dispute Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Manage disputes and claims through coordinated assessment, evidence, strategy, decision-making and resolution.**

# 7. Intellectual Property Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Protect, govern and appropriately exploit enterprise intellectual property and associated rights.**

# 8. Legal Matter Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain a controlled and traceable record of material legal matters, advice, actions, decisions and outcomes.**

# 115. Definition of Ready

A contract, legal matter, dispute, claim, legal advice request, legal hold, IP matter, regulatory legal matter, legal exception, remediation or assurance review is Ready when scope, parties, issue, owner, authority, risk, dependencies, deadlines, decision criteria, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
