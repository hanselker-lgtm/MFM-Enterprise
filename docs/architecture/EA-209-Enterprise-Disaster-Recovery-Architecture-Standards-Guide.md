# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Disaster Recovery throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Disaster Recovery ensures that critical enterprise systems, infrastructure and data can be restored within agreed recovery objectives following major disruptions while preserving operational resilience, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Disaster Recovery Planning
- Recovery Site Strategy
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- Backup and Restore
- Disaster Recovery Testing
- Recovery Verification
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## DR-001

Provide standardized enterprise disaster recovery.

---

## DR-002

Protect critical enterprise systems and data.

---

## DR-003

Support rapid restoration following disasters.

---

## DR-004

Strengthen enterprise resilience.

---

## DR-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Disaster Recovery Principles

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall follow these principles.

- Recovery by Design
- Risk-Based Recovery Planning
- Verified Recovery Capability
- Continuous Preparedness
- Complete Traceability
- Operational Resilience
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Disaster Recovery implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

---

# 5. Disaster Recovery Responsibilities

Enterprise Disaster Recovery shall provide

- disaster recovery planning
- recovery site management
- RTO management
- RPO management
- backup and restore management
- disaster recovery testing
- governance reporting
- compliance verification

Additional Disaster Recovery responsibilities shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

# 8. Disaster Recovery Planning

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall implement standardized disaster recovery planning.

Disaster recovery planning shall

- identify critical recovery scenarios
- identify recovery priorities
- identify recovery resources
- identify recovery dependencies
- preserve planning traceability
- maintain planning consistency

Disaster recovery planning shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 9. Recovery Site Strategy

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall implement standardized recovery site strategies.

Recovery site strategies shall

- define primary recovery sites
- define secondary recovery sites
- define infrastructure redundancy
- define failover capabilities
- preserve strategy traceability
- maintain strategy consistency

Recovery site strategies shall align with approved business continuity requirements.

---

# 10. Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall implement standardized Recovery Time Objective management.

RTO management shall

- define recovery time objectives
- classify service recovery priorities
- verify recovery timelines
- preserve RTO traceability
- maintain RTO consistency
- support operational resilience

Recovery Time Objectives shall be approved by business stakeholders.

---

# 11. Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall implement standardized Recovery Point Objective management.

RPO management shall

- define acceptable data loss
- classify backup frequency
- verify recovery point achievement
- preserve RPO traceability
- maintain RPO consistency
- support enterprise data protection

Recovery Point Objectives shall align with enterprise data governance requirements.

---

# 12. Backup and Restore

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall implement standardized backup and restore management.

Backup and restore management shall

- define backup schedules
- verify backup integrity
- verify restore capability
- preserve backup traceability
- maintain backup consistency
- support disaster recovery readiness

Backup and restore procedures shall be tested regularly.

---

# 13. Recovery Verification

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall implement standardized recovery verification.

Recovery verification shall

- verify recovery capability
- verify RTO achievement
- verify RPO achievement
- preserve verification traceability
- maintain verification consistency
- support operational resilience

Recovery verification shall be performed following disaster recovery testing.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall handle disaster recovery-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify disaster recovery planning failures
- classify recovery site failures
- classify Recovery Time Objective failures
- classify Recovery Point Objective failures
- classify backup and restore failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Disaster Recovery exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational resilience, recovery capability, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations may depend upon

- approved continuity management services
- approved availability management services
- approved backup infrastructure
- approved monitoring platforms
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external disaster recovery providers

Disaster Recovery capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementation is compliant when

- Disaster recovery planning is documented.
- Recovery site strategies are maintained.
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) are defined.
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) are defined.
- Backup and restore procedures are implemented.
- Recovery verification is performed.
- Disaster recovery testing is completed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Disaster Recovery Plan

Critical enterprise systems shall never operate without an approved disaster recovery plan.

---

## Untested Recovery Procedures

Recovery procedures shall never remain untested beyond the approved testing interval.

---

## Undefined RTO or RPO

Business-critical systems shall never operate without documented Recovery Time Objectives or Recovery Point Objectives.

---

## Unverified Backups

Backups shall never be considered valid without documented restore verification.

---

## Obsolete Recovery Documentation

Disaster recovery documentation shall never remain outdated following approved infrastructure or architectural changes.

---

## Disaster Recovery Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent disaster recovery mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Disaster Recovery services.

---

Architecture Review shall verify

- governance compliance
- compliance with enterprise standards

The Enterprise Disaster Recovery Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Disaster Recovery throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

All Enterprise Disaster Recovery implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
