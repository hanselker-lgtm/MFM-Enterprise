## Purpose
Establish the authoritative authority model for post-closure regression governance, defining mandates, roles, decision rights, delegation, limits, conditions, accountability, independence, escalation and evidence requirements.

## Core Principle
Authority is the governed right to make or authorize a decision within a defined scope. Authority shall be explicit, attributable, bounded, current and proportionate to risk and materiality.

```text
GOVERNANCE
   ↓
AUTHORITY
   ↓
ROLE
   ↓
MANDATE
   ↓
DECISION RIGHTS
   ↓
DELEGATION
   ↓
LIMITS / CONDITIONS
   ↓
ESCALATION
   ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
```

## Authority Quality Test
```text
VALID MANDATE
+
IDENTIFIED ROLE
+
EXPLICIT DECISION RIGHT
+
DEFINED SCOPE
+
CURRENT AUTHORITY
+
NO MATERIAL CONFLICT
+
TRACEABLE ACCOUNTABILITY
=
VALID GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY
```

## Authority Status Model
```text
DEFINED
ACTIVE
DELEGATED
CONDITIONAL
SUSPENDED
EXPIRED
REVOKED
ESCALATED
UNDER REVIEW
```

## Authority Decision-Rights Matrix
| Decision | Minimum authority | Additional requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Signal classification | Monitoring authority | Materiality escalation |
| Regression assessment | Assessment authority | Independent challenge where material |
| Regression decision | Designated decision authority | Risk and scope confirmation |
| Execution authorization | Action authority | Approved scope and controls |
| Verification conclusion | Verification authority | Independence where required |
| Re-validation conclusion | Re-validation authority | Current evidence and risk |
| Re-acceptance | Governance authority | Formal challenge and rationale |
| Re-closure | Closure authority | Closure criteria and evidence |
| Reopening | Designated governance authority | Materiality and impact review |

## Mandate Model
A mandate defines why an authority exists, what it may decide, what it must not decide, which lifecycle states it governs and which escalation paths apply.

```text
MANDATE
 ├── PURPOSE
 ├── SCOPE
 ├── DECISION RIGHTS
 ├── LIMITS
 ├── CONDITIONS
 ├── ACCOUNTABILITY
 └── ESCALATION```

## Role Model
Authority belongs to an accountable role rather than an informal individual preference.

```text
ROLE
 ↓
MANDATE
 ↓
DECISION RIGHTS
 ↓
RESPONSIBILITIES
 ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
```

## Delegation Model
```text
PRIMARY AUTHORITY
      ↓
DELEGATION
      ↓
DELEGATED AUTHORITY
      ↓
DEFINED SCOPE / LIMITS / EXPIRY
      ↓
DECISION
      ↓
REPORT BACK
```

Delegation shall identify delegator, delegate, authority scope, effective period, conditions, limitations and reporting requirements. The delegator remains accountable unless governance explicitly defines otherwise.

## Authority Limits
Authority limits may be based on risk, financial impact, regulatory significance, security impact, operational criticality, geographic scope, data sensitivity, customer impact or architectural consequences.

```text
DECISION PROPOSED
      ↓
WITHIN LIMITS?
 ├── YES → DECIDE
 └── NO  → ESCALATE
```

## Conditional Authority
Conditional authority is valid only while its stated conditions remain true. Material change in conditions shall trigger reassessment or suspension of the authority.

## Conflict of Interest
Anyone exercising material authority shall disclose relevant conflicts. Where independence is required, the conflicted role shall not provide the final independent decision.

## Independence
```text
OPERATIONAL OWNER       ↓
PROPOSED ACTION       ↓
INDEPENDENT REVIEW       ↓
AUTHORIZED DECISION```

Independence shall be proportionate to materiality, risk and regulatory significance.

## Escalation Authority
```text
DECISION
  ↓
OUTSIDE AUTHORITY?
 ├── NO → EXECUTE / RECORD
 └── YES → ESCALATE
             ↓
       HIGHER AUTHORITY
             ↓
       DECIDE / EXCEPTION```

## Risk Acceptance Authority
Risk acceptance authority shall be aligned with risk appetite and materiality. A role shall not accept risk beyond its authorized threshold.

## Security and Resilience Authority
Security and resilience decisions affecting critical services, material exposure, recovery capability or systemic dependencies shall use explicitly designated authority and escalation paths.

## Compliance Authority
Compliance-related decisions shall respect applicable policy, regulatory obligations, audit requirements and designated compliance authority. An operational owner shall not waive a mandatory obligation without lawful and explicitly governed authority.

## AI and Agent Authority
AI and agents may recommend, analyze or execute only within explicit technical and governance boundaries.

```text
AI / AGENT
   ↓
IDENTITY
   ↓
GRANTED AUTHORITY
   ↓
TOOL / DATA SCOPE
   ↓
ACTION LIMITS
   ↓
STOP CONDITIONS
   ↓
HUMAN / GOVERNED ACCOUNTABILITY```

An AI system shall never infer governance authority merely from access to a tool, data source or workflow.

## Emergency Authority
Emergency authority may permit accelerated action to protect life, safety, security, resilience or critical operations. It shall remain bounded, logged and subject to retrospective governance review.

```text
EMERGENCY
 ↓
EMERGENCY AUTHORITY
 ↓
IMMEDIATE ACTION
 ↓
STABILIZE
 ↓
RETROSPECTIVE REVIEW
 ↓
FORMAL GOVERNANCE RECORD```

## Authority Evidence
Authority evidence shall demonstrate who had authority, under what mandate, during which period, within which scope and under which conditions.

## Authority Review
Authority shall be periodically reviewed for continued relevance, especially following organizational change, regulatory change, architecture change, material incidents, repeated regressions or delegation changes.

## Authority Failure
```text
INVALID / UNCLEAR AUTHORITY
        ↓
STOP MATERIAL DECISION
        ↓
IDENTIFY PROPER AUTHORITY
        ↓
ESCALATE / ASSIGN
        ↓
DECIDE
        ↓
RECORD```

## Governance Learning
Repeated authority failures shall trigger review of role design, delegation, limits, escalation thresholds, segregation of duties and governance structure.
