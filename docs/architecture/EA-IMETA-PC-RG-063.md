## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory reliance-restoration layer defining how an explicitly reaccepted state is safely and traceably returned to operational reliance within the accepted scope, authority, conditions and residual-risk limits.

## Core Principle
Reacceptance establishes formal acceptance; reliance restoration establishes operational permission to rely. Restoration shall therefore remain a separate controlled transition and shall never be inferred merely from reacceptance.

```text
REACCEPTED STATE
      ↓
RESTORATION SCOPE + READINESS
      ↓
CONDITIONS + CONTROLS CONFIRMED
      ↓
AUTHORIZED ACTIVATION
├── FULL → RESTORED
├── CONDITIONAL → RESTORED WITH LIMITS
├── PARTIAL → SELECTED SCOPE
├── RESTRICTED → LIMITED RELIANCE
└── NOT READY → HOLD / REOPEN
```

## Restoration Quality Test
```text
REACCEPTED STATE
+
EXPLICIT RELIANCE SCOPE
+
AUTHORIZED CONSUMERS
+
READINESS EVIDENCE
+
ACTIVE CONDITIONS
+
RESIDUAL-RISK CONFIRMATION
+
MONITORING + RESPONSE READINESS
+
TRACEABLE ACTIVATION
=
VALID GOVERNED RELIANCE RESTORATION
```

```text
RELIANCE SHALL NOT BE RESTORED WITHOUT REQUIRED REACCEPTANCE
```

```text
PARTIAL OR CONDITIONAL RESTORATION SHALL BE EXPLICIT
```

```text
RESTORATION SHALL NOT CREATE NEW AUTHORITY OR EXPAND MANDATE
```

```text
RESTORATION SHALL BE TRACEABLE TO THE REACCEPTANCE DECISION
```

```text
RESTORED RELIANCE SHALL REMAIN REVOCABLE
```

```text
SECURITY, RESILIENCE, SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE RESTORATION SHALL RECEIVE APPROPRIATE RIGOR
```

```text
AI AND AGENT RESTORATION SHALL RECONFIRM AUTHORITY, POLICY, TOOLS, DATA, AUTONOMY AND BEHAVIOURAL LIMITS
```

```text
INITIAL POST-RESTORATION MONITORING SHALL BE READY WHERE MATERIALITY WARRANTS IT
```

```text
FAILED RESTORATION SHALL NOT BE TREATED AS ACCEPTANCE FAILURE WITHOUT SEPARATE DETERMINATION
```

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Reaccepted State | Current formally accepted state |
| Reliance Scope | Scope authorized for restored reliance |
| Consumer | Authorized relying party |
| Readiness | Preconditions for activation |
| Conditions | Acceptance limits |
| Residual Risk | Authorized remaining exposure |
| Activation | Controlled transition |
| Outcome | Restoration status |

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Objective

Return an accepted state to operational reliance without exceeding accepted scope, authority, conditions or residual-risk limits.

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Definition

Reliance restoration is the governed transition from formal reacceptance to operationally authorized reliance within explicit boundaries.

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Scope

Scope shall identify systems, services, users, data, decisions, dependencies, environments and boundaries for which reliance is restored, including exclusions.

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Authority

Authority shall define who authorizes restoration, who may enable reliance, who may restrict and who may revoke restored reliance.

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Criteria

Criteria shall distinguish ready, restored, conditional, partial, restricted, suspended and failed states.

```text
REACCEPTED?
├── NO → NOT READY
└── YES
     ↓
SCOPE + CONSUMERS CONFIRMED?
├── NO → HOLD
└── YES
     ↓
READINESS + CONDITIONS CONFIRMED?
├── NO → HOLD / CONDITIONAL
└── YES
     ↓
RESIDUAL RISK WITHIN LIMIT?
├── NO → RESTRICT / ESCALATE
└── YES → RESTORE
```

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Preconditions

Preconditions include current reacceptance, defined scope, authorized consumers, readiness evidence, active conditions, monitoring and response capability and residual-risk confirmation.

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Evidence

Evidence shall connect restoration to reacceptance, scope, authority, readiness checks, conditions, activation actions and effective time.

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Method

Methods may include staged activation, controlled release, phased scope restoration, consumer enablement, feature activation and guarded transition.

```text
REACCEPTED
↓
PRE-FLIGHT
↓
STAGED / CONTROLLED ACTIVATION
↓
OBSERVE READINESS
↓
AUTHORIZED RELIANCE
```

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Decision

Decisions shall distinguish full, conditional, partial, restricted, suspended and failed restoration.

```text
FULL → NORMAL CONTROLLED RELIANCE
CONDITIONAL → RELIANCE WITH LIMITS
PARTIAL → SELECTED SCOPE
RESTRICTED → LIMITED RELIANCE
FAILED → HOLD / REOPEN
```

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Accountability

Accountability shall remain explicit for authorization, transition execution, consumer enablement, monitoring readiness and revocation readiness.

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Timing

Restoration shall occur while reacceptance and its conditions remain current. Material delay or change before activation may require renewed revalidation or reacceptance.

## Security Reacceptance Reliance Restoration

Restore security reliance only after access, exposure, authentication, authorization, monitoring and security-boundary conditions are confirmed.

## Resilience Reacceptance Reliance Restoration

Restore resilience reliance only after availability, capacity, recovery, continuity and dependency readiness are confirmed.

## Compliance Reacceptance Reliance Restoration

Restore compliance reliance only after current obligations, controls, evidence and reporting conditions are confirmed.

## Data Reacceptance Reliance Restoration

Restore data reliance only after integrity, quality, lineage, access, retention and authorized-use conditions are confirmed.

## AI and Agent Reacceptance Reliance Restoration

Restore AI/agent reliance only after current authority, policy, tools, data boundaries, autonomy and behavioural controls are confirmed.

```text
REACCEPTED AI / AGENT
↓
BOUNDARIES + AUTHORITY CONFIRMED
↓
CONTROLLED RESTORATION
↓
MONITORING READY
↓
NORMAL RELIANCE IF STABLE
```

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Failure

Failure includes readiness gaps, scope mismatch, unauthorized consumer, unmet condition, monitoring gap or residual risk outside limit.

```text
RESTORATION FAILURE
↓
NO UNCONTROLLED RELIANCE
↓
HOLD / RESTRICT
↓
IDENTIFY GAP
↓
REMEDIATE / REVALIDATE / REACCEPT AS REQUIRED
```

Where materiality requires it, transition authorization or initial restoration review shall receive independent challenge or assurance.

## Reacceptance Reliance Restoration Review and Learning

Reviews shall identify transition failures, scope errors, consumer readiness problems, condition breaches, monitoring gaps and recurring restoration weaknesses.

```text
     ↓
```

## Consumer Authorization
Only identified and authorized consumers may rely on the restored state. Consumer authorization shall be scoped and revocable.

## Transition Integrity
Restoration shall be traceable from the reacceptance decision through activation to the effective restoration state.

```text
REACCEPTANCE
↓
RESTORATION AUTHORIZATION
↓
ACTIVATION
↓
EFFECTIVE TIME
↓
MONITORING
```

## Initial Post-Restoration Monitoring
Where materiality warrants it, heightened monitoring shall immediately follow restoration to detect transition defects, latent failures, changed dependencies and unexpected downstream effects.

## Scope Integrity
Reliance shall not be restored beyond the accepted scope. Any expansion requires additional governed assessment and authorization.

## Restoration Revocation
Restored reliance shall be restricted, suspended or revoked when material invalidating conditions arise.

```text
RESTORED RELIANCE
↓
INVALIDATING CONDITION?
├── NO → CONTINUE
└── YES → RESTRICT / SUSPEND / REVOKE
```

## Restoration Change Control
Changes to scope, consumers, conditions, authority, activation method, monitoring readiness or residual-risk limits shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated.

```text
CURRENT RESTORATION MODEL
↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
↓
NEW VERSION
↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

## Restoration Anti-Gaming Control
Reliance shall not be restored merely to remove restrictions, close governance actions or improve operational metrics. All mandatory readiness and acceptance conditions remain controlling.

Historical restoration decisions, pre-flight checks, consumer authorizations, transition evidence, conditions, monitoring readiness, restrictions, suspensions and revocations shall remain preserved according to applicable retention and governance requirements.
