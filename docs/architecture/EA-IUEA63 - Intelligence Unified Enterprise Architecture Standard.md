# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, and intelligence capabilities required for an **Intelligence Unified Enterprise Architecture (IUEA)**.

While EA-IAEA62 enables Enterprise Architecture to autonomously execute approved enterprise activities within governed boundaries, EA-IUEA63 enables Enterprise Intelligence to function as **one unified intelligence ecosystem**, where every architectural domain, knowledge source, intelligent service, autonomous agent, business capability, governance function, and operational process is continuously synchronized.

The objective is to eliminate architectural fragmentation by establishing a permanently unified enterprise intelligence capable of coordinated reasoning, synchronized execution, continuous learning, and enterprise-wide optimization.

Enterprise Architecture shall therefore evolve from autonomous operational intelligence into a unified enterprise intelligence platform.
