## Purpose
Establish the authoritative accountability layer beneath responsibility, defining who is answerable for outcomes, decisions, controls, evidence, performance, risk, escalation and lifecycle integrity within post-closure regression governance.

## Core Principle
Accountability is the obligation to answer for an outcome or governed decision. It remains explicit even when work, authority, execution or evidence production is delegated to other roles.

```text
RESPONSIBILITY
      ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
      ↓
OUTCOME OWNERSHIP
      ↓
DECISION ACCOUNTABILITY
      ↓
CONTROL ACCOUNTABILITY
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
PERFORMANCE
      ↓
ESCALATION
      ↓
ASSURANCE
      ↓
REVIEW
```

## Accountability Quality Test
```text
DEFINED OUTCOME

```

## Accountability Structure
| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Outcome | Result for which accountability exists |
| Accountable Owner | Role answerable for the outcome |
| Responsibility | Work and obligations supporting the outcome |
| Authority | Right to make relevant decisions |
| Control | Mechanism governing the outcome |
| Evidence | Proof supporting performance and decisions |
| Performance | Measures and acceptance criteria |
| Risk | Risk owned, monitored or escalated |
| Escalation | Conditions requiring intervention |
| Assurance | Independent or governed challenge |
| Review | Assessment of continued accountability |

## Accountability vs Responsibility
```text
RESPONSIBILITY = WHAT MUST BE DONE / ENSURED
ACCOUNTABILITY  = WHO ANSWERS FOR THE OUTCOME```

A responsibility may be distributed among several roles. Accountability shall remain explicit and shall identify the role that answers for the final outcome.

## Accountability vs Authority
```text
ACCOUNTABILITY
      ≠
AUTHORITY
      ≠
EXECUTION```

A person may be accountable without possessing every operational decision right. Conversely, authority to approve a decision does not automatically make the authority holder accountable for every operational consequence.

## Outcome Accountability
```text
EXPECTED OUTCOME
      ↓
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
      ↓
MEASUREMENT
      ↓
ACTUAL RESULT
      ↓
VARIANCE
      ↓
EXPLANATION / CORRECTION / ESCALATION```

## Decision Accountability
Decision accountability requires traceability of the decision owner, authority basis, evidence considered, rationale, material assumptions and resulting disposition.

```text
DECISION
   ↓
WHO DECIDED?
   ↓
UNDER WHAT AUTHORITY?
   ↓
USING WHAT EVIDENCE?
   ↓
WITH WHAT RATIONALE?
   ↓
WHAT RESULT?```

## Control Accountability
Every material control shall have an accountable owner responsible for its continued design adequacy, operation, evidence and remediation where control failure occurs.

## Evidence Accountability
Evidence accountability identifies who must ensure evidence is generated, retained, protected, attributable, accessible and sufficient for the relevant lifecycle decision.

## Performance Accountability
Performance accountability shall distinguish completion of activity from achievement of the intended outcome.

```text
ACTIVITY COMPLETE
      ≠
CONTROL OPERATING
      ≠
OUTCOME EFFECTIVE```

## Risk Accountability
Risk accountability shall identify who owns the risk position, who may accept risk, who must treat it and who must escalate it.

```text
RISK IDENTIFIED
      ↓
RISK OWNER
      ↓
TREAT / MONITOR
      ↓
WITHIN ACCEPTANCE LIMIT?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → ESCALATE```

## Security Accountability
Material security exposure shall have explicit accountability for prevention, detection, response, containment, remediation, verification and escalation as applicable.

## Resilience Accountability
Resilience accountability shall cover service continuity, recovery capability, dependency risk, restoration objectives and post-recovery verification.

## Data Accountability
Data accountability shall identify ownership for integrity, quality, lineage, access, classification, retention and authorized use where applicable.

## AI and Agent Accountability
AI and agent actions shall always map to an accountable human or governed organizational owner.

```text
AI / AGENT ACTION
      ↓
GOVERNED RESPONSIBILITY
      ↓
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
      ↓
CONTROL / OVERSIGHT
      ↓
EVIDENCE
```

Technical autonomy, model capability or automated execution shall never be treated as a substitute for organizational accountability.

## Compliance Accountability
Compliance accountability shall identify who is answerable for maintaining conformity, evidence, escalation and remediation of material non-conformity.

## Escalation Accountability
When a responsibility fails, risk increases, authority is exceeded or a material regression emerges, the accountable owner shall ensure escalation occurs even if another role performs the escalation activity.

```text
CONDITION CHANGES
      ↓
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
      ↓
ESCALATION REQUIRED?
 ├── NO  → CONTINUE
 └── YES → ESCALATE / RECORD```

## Delegated Accountability
Delegation may transfer work or decision rights, but accountability shall remain with the accountable owner unless a formally governed accountability transfer occurs.

```text
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
      ↓
DELEGATE WORK
      ↓
EXECUTION
      ↓
REVIEW
      ↓
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER ANSWERS FOR OUTCOME```

## Shared Accountability
Where several organizations or functions contribute materially, the governance model shall identify a lead accountable owner and define supporting accountability.

```text
LEAD ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
       ├── SUPPORTING OWNER A
       ├── SUPPORTING OWNER B
       ├── CONTROL OWNER
       └── ASSURANCE```

Shared accountability shall not be used to eliminate ownership.

## Independent Accountability
Independent assurance, verification or audit roles shall retain accountability for the independence and integrity of their own assurance activity without assuming operational ownership of the process under review.

## Accountability Interfaces
Cross-functional lifecycle stages shall define accountability handoffs explicitly.

```text
ASSESSMENT OWNER      ↓
DECISION OWNER      ↓
EXECUTION OWNER      ↓
VERIFICATION OWNER      ↓
RE-VALIDATION OWNER      ↓
RE-ACCEPTANCE OWNER      ↓
RE-CLOSURE OWNER```

## Accountability Continuity
Material accountability shall have succession and backup arrangements. A vacancy shall trigger reassignment or escalation rather than an accountability gap.

```text
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER UNAVAILABLE
        ↓
ACTIVATE SUCCESSOR / DELEGATE
        ↓
CONFIRM AUTHORITY
        ↓
CONFIRM ACCOUNTABILITY
        ↓
CONTINUE / ESCALATE```

## Accountability Failure
```text
NO ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
        ↓
STOP / ESCALATE MATERIAL DECISION
        ↓
ASSIGN ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
        ↓
CONFIRM MANDATE / ROLE
        ↓
RESTORE CONTROL```

## Accountability Evidence
Evidence shall demonstrate accountable ownership, mandate mapping, decisions, control performance, risk treatment, escalations, handovers and reviews.

## Accountability Review
Accountability shall be reviewed following material regression, repeated control failure, organizational change, role change, mandate change, significant risk change or lifecycle re-opening.

```text
ACCOUNTABILITY ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER STILL VALID?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → TRANSFER / REASSIGN / ESCALATE```

## Governance Learning
Repeated accountability failures shall trigger review of role design, mandate clarity, authority allocation, responsibility allocation, interfaces, segregation of duties and escalation structures.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes accountability beneath responsibility, role, mandate and authority for post-closure regression governance. It does not replace those layers or the stage-specific regression lifecycle controls.
