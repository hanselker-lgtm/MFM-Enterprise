# 1. Purpose

N9-D establishes the integrated governance layer for cross-domain dependencies, enterprise risks and material change impacts identified through N9-A, N9-B and N9-C.

The purpose is to prevent isolated decisions from creating uncontrolled enterprise-wide consequences.

The controlled model is:

```text
CHANGE
  ↓
DEPENDENCY
  ↓
IMPACT
  ↓
RISK
  ↓
DECISION
  ↓
AUTHORIZATION
  ↓
CHANGE
  ↓
VALIDATION
```

N9-D does not itself grant authority that belongs to another governance domain.

# 46. Final N9-D Statement

> **N9-D establishes the Cross-Domain Dependency, Risk & Change Governance layer of the authorized N9 workstream. It integrates material dependencies, enterprise risks, change impacts, conflicts, escalation, decision authority and control effectiveness across strategy, business, capability, architecture, portfolio, investment, operations and other relevant domains. N9-D prevents isolated decisions from being treated as isolated effects and requires material cross-domain impacts to be explicitly assessed, owned, governed and evidenced. N9-D does not itself grant authority belonging to other governance domains.**
