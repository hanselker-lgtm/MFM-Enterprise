# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Orchestration Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed orchestration capabilities for coordinating services, processes, workflows, automation and interactions across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Orchestration Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem orchestration capabilities
- support service orchestration
- support process orchestration
- support workflow coordination
- support cross-enterprise orchestration
- support partner orchestration
- support supplier orchestration
- support event-driven orchestration
- support automation orchestration
- support AI-supported orchestration
- support orchestration governance
- support orchestration lifecycle management
- support orchestration monitoring
- support orchestration optimization
- improve ecosystem coordination
- improve ecosystem responsiveness
- improve ecosystem scalability
- improve ecosystem operational consistency

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Orchestration Services shall provide trusted and reusable orchestration capabilities enabling coordinated ecosystem operations.

# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Orchestration Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Orchestration Services shall follow these principles.

## Orchestration by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall be designed with orchestration capabilities integrated from the beginning.

---

## Coordinated Execution

Services, processes and activities shall be coordinated through governed orchestration mechanisms.

---

## Event-Driven Orchestration

Where appropriate, ecosystem orchestration shall use events to enable responsive and adaptive execution.

---

## Secure Orchestration

All orchestration activities shall operate according to Enterprise security, identity and authorization requirements.

---

## Continuous Orchestration Improvement

Orchestration capabilities shall continuously evolve according to business requirements and technology development.
