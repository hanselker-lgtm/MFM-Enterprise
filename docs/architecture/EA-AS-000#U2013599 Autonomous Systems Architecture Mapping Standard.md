# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Autonomous Systems Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise Autonomous Systems capabilities.

The Autonomous Systems Architecture Framework shall provide:

- autonomous systems principles
- autonomous governance
- autonomous strategy
- autonomous capability architecture
- intelligent agent architecture
- autonomous decision architecture
- autonomous robotics architecture
- autonomous vehicle architecture
- agent collaboration architecture
- autonomous data architecture
- autonomous AI architecture
- autonomous integration architecture
- autonomous security architecture
- human-autonomous collaboration architecture
- autonomous operations
- autonomous performance management
- autonomous evolution

The framework ensures that autonomous technologies and intelligent self-operating capabilities are designed, governed and operated as integrated Enterprise capabilities.

---

---

# 3. Enterprise Autonomous Systems Architecture Principles

The Autonomous Systems Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Responsible Autonomy

Autonomous capabilities shall operate according to defined governance, ethics and operational requirements.

---

## Principle 2 – Human-Centered Autonomy

Autonomous systems shall support human decision-making and improve human capabilities.

---

## Principle 3 – Intelligent Decision Capability

Autonomous systems shall use appropriate intelligence to analyze situations and execute actions.

---

## Principle 4 – Controlled Autonomy

Autonomous behaviour shall be governed through defined rules, limits and responsibilities.

---

## Principle 5 – Secure Autonomous Operation

Autonomous systems shall include security controls throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 6 – Adaptive Intelligence

Autonomous systems shall be capable of learning and adapting according to approved requirements.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Autonomous Evolution

Autonomous capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise needs and technology development.

---
