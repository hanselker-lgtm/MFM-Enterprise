# 1. Purpose

N9-B establishes the controlled governance layer for enterprise architecture evolution following material changes identified through N9-A.

The purpose is to determine:

```text
What changed?
        ↓
What architecture is affected?
        ↓
How is it affected?
        ↓
What capability is affected?
        ↓
What transition is required?
        ↓
What decision is required?
        ↓
What evidence validates the decision?
```

N9-B does not automatically approve architecture changes or implementation.
