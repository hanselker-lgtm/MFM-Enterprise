# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the forty-second document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-41 – Enterprise Privacy, Personal Data Protection, Records Management, Legal Information Governance & Regulatory Data Compliance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for enterprise risk management, risk governance, business continuity, operational resilience, disaster recovery, crisis management, emergency management, recovery planning, resilience testing, dependency management, scenario analysis, crisis communications, business impact analysis, continuity exercises, recovery assurance and continual resilience improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must identify, understand, treat and continuously monitor enterprise risks while maintaining the capability to continue, recover and adapt critical business services during disruption, crisis or adverse conditions.**

# 3. Risk Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure that material risks are identified, assessed, owned, treated, monitored and reported in accordance with organizational strategy and approved risk appetite.**

---

# 4. Business Continuity Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain continuity of critical business services at an acceptable level during disruption and restore normal operations in a controlled manner.**

---

# 5. Disaster Recovery Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore critical technology, applications, data and infrastructure within defined recovery requirements following major disruption.**

---

# 6. Crisis Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide coordinated leadership, decision-making, communication and response during significant events that threaten people, services, assets, reputation or organizational objectives.**

---

# 7. Organizational Resilience Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable MFM to anticipate, withstand, respond to, recover from and adapt to disruptive events while maintaining critical outcomes.**

# 117. Definition of Ready

A risk, continuity, recovery, crisis, resilience, exercise, finding, exception or improvement work item is Ready when:

- The risk, critical service, process, dependency, scenario, recovery requirement, crisis event, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, impact, criticality, dependencies, RTO/RPO, resources, communications, security, regulatory and business requirements are known.
- Assessment, treatment, planning, testing, evidence, approval, validation and acceptance requirements are defined.

---

# 121. Final Continuity Principle

> **Critical business services must have practical continuity arrangements that protect essential outcomes during disruption.**

---

# 122. Final Recovery Principle

> **Recovery objectives must be defined, implemented and periodically tested against business requirements.**

---

# 123. Final Crisis Principle

> **Significant crises require clear authority, rapid escalation, disciplined decision-making and coordinated communication.**

---

# 124. Final Resilience Principle

> **Organizational resilience must extend across people, processes, technology, data, facilities, suppliers and external dependencies.**

---

# 125. Final Exercise Principle

> **Continuity and recovery plans are not considered effective until they have been exercised or otherwise validated at an appropriate level.**

---

# 126. Final Supplier Principle

> **Critical supplier dependencies must be understood, monitored, tested and supported by appropriate continuity and exit arrangements.**

---

# 127. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material risk, continuity and resilience controls must be supported by evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

---

# 128. Final Improvement Principle

> **Risk events, incidents, exercises, recovery tests, crisis reviews, emerging risks and operational lessons must continuously improve MFM resilience.**

---

# 129. Final Integration Principle

> **Risk and Resilience Management must integrate with Enterprise Architecture, Application Management, Infrastructure, Data Management, Cybersecurity, Privacy, Service Management, Financial Management, Procurement, Supplier Management, People Management, Compliance, Legal and Project Management.**

---

# 130. Final Steady-State Resilience Principle

> **MFM must identify, understand, treat and continuously monitor enterprise risks while maintaining the capability to continue, recover and adapt critical business services during disruption, crisis or adverse conditions.**
