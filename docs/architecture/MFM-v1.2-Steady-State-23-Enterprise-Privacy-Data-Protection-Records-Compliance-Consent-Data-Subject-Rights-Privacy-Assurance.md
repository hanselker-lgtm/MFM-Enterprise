# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the twenty-third document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-22 – Enterprise Data Management, Data Governance, Data Quality, Master Data, Metadata, Records, Retention & Information Lifecycle.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for privacy and data protection, ensuring that personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, transparently, securely and for defined purposes throughout its lifecycle.

The central objective is:

> **MFM personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly and transparently, for defined purposes, with appropriate minimization, accuracy, security, retention and accountability controls.**

# 108. Definition of Ready

A privacy work item is Ready when:

- The processing activity, personal-data issue, consent, rights request, DPIA, processor, transfer, breach, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, purpose, lawful basis, data subjects, data categories, recipients, risk and applicable requirements are known.
- Privacy, security, retention, evidence, notification, remediation and acceptance requirements are defined.

---

# 109. Definition of Done

A privacy work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement Identified
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Processing / Rights / Consent / Contract / Retention Records Updated
        ↓

        ↓
Exceptions / Findings Addressed
        ↓
Outcome Accepted
```

# 110. Final Privacy Principle

> **Privacy must be embedded into the normal operation and design of MFM rather than treated only as a compliance activity.**

---

# 111. Final Lawfulness Principle

> **Every material personal-data processing activity must have an appropriate and documented legal basis where required.**

---

# 112. Final Transparency Principle

> **Individuals must receive clear and appropriate information about how their personal data is processed.**

---

# 113. Final Minimization Principle

> **MFM should collect and process only the personal data necessary for defined purposes.**

---

# 114. Final Rights Principle

> **Applicable data-subject rights must be supported through controlled, timely and evidence-based processes.**

---

# 115. Final Consent Principle

> **Where consent is used, it must be meaningful, demonstrable, purpose-specific and capable of withdrawal.**

---

# 116. Final Processor Principle

> **Third parties processing personal data on behalf of MFM must be governed through proportionate due diligence, contractual controls and ongoing oversight.**

---

# 117. Final Breach Principle

> **Personal-data breaches must be assessed promptly and managed through coordinated privacy, security, legal and business processes.**

---

# 118. Final Retention Principle

> **Personal data and privacy records must be retained only for justified periods and securely disposed of when obligations end.**

---

# 119. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material privacy controls must be supported by evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

---

# 120. Final Improvement Principle

> **Privacy incidents, rights requests, findings, assessments and regulatory developments must continuously improve MFM privacy practices.**

---

# 121. Final Integration Principle

> **Privacy must integrate with Data Management, Cybersecurity, Technical Operations, Service Management, Legal, Compliance, Risk, Procurement, Supplier Management, Business Continuity, Architecture and People Management.**

---

# 122. Final Steady-State Privacy Principle

> **MFM personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly and transparently, for defined purposes, with appropriate minimization, accuracy, security, retention and accountability controls.**
