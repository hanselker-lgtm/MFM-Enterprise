# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Digital Trust Architecture governing trust relationships, trust frameworks, trust validation, trust governance and continuous trust evaluation across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Digital Trust Architecture shall

- establish Enterprise-wide trust models
- support trusted digital interactions
- strengthen organizational confidence
- enable verifiable trust relationships
- support Zero Trust principles
- integrate with Enterprise Digital Identity
- improve interoperability
- support trusted business ecosystems
- reduce operational risk
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Digital Trust shall function as a reusable Enterprise capability supporting secure collaboration across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- trust frameworks
- trust domains
- trust anchors
- trust chains
- trust federation
- identity trust
- device trust
- application trust
- data trust
- enterprise trust governance

The standard applies regardless of technology platform, infrastructure provider or deployment model.

---

# 3. Enterprise Digital Trust Principles

Enterprise Digital Trust Architecture shall be governed by the following principles.

## Trust by Design

Trust mechanisms shall be embedded into Enterprise architecture from the beginning rather than added later.

---

## Continuous Verification

Trust shall be continuously evaluated throughout operational lifecycles.

---

## Least Trust Required

Enterprise solutions shall grant only the minimum trust necessary for each interaction.

---

## Verifiable Evidence

Trust decisions shall be based upon verifiable evidence rather than assumptions.

---

## Federated Trust

Trust relationships shall support collaboration across organizational boundaries while preserving Enterprise governance.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Digital Trust Architecture shall remain independent of specific trust technologies, vendors and cloud providers.
