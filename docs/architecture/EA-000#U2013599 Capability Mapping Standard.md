# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Capability Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise capabilities across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Capability Reference Model shall provide:

- capability identification
- capability classification
- capability ownership
- capability relationships
- capability maturity alignment
- capability evolution mapping
- architecture traceability

The model shall provide a common language for understanding how Enterprise capabilities support strategy, operations and continuous evolution.

---

# 2. Scope

The Capability Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture domains:

- Enterprise Architecture Foundation
- Business Architecture
- Information Architecture
- Data Architecture
- Application Architecture
- Technology Architecture
- Security Architecture
- Governance Architecture
- Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Architecture
- Enterprise Evolution Architecture

The model applies to:

- strategic capabilities
- business capabilities
- information capabilities
- technology capabilities
- governance capabilities
- ecosystem capabilities
- evolution capabilities

---

# 3. MFM Capability Architecture Principles

The Capability Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Capability-Centric Architecture

Enterprise Architecture shall be managed through capabilities rather than isolated systems or technologies.

---

## Business Alignment

All capabilities shall support Enterprise objectives and strategic outcomes.

---

## Reusability

Capabilities shall be designed as reusable Enterprise assets.

---

## Evolution Support

Capabilities shall continuously evolve according to Enterprise requirements.

---

## Traceability

Every capability shall maintain traceability to architecture standards, services and outcomes.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Capabilities shall be measured and improved throughout their lifecycle.

---
