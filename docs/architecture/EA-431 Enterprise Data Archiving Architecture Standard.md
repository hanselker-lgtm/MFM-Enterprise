# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Archiving Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Migration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-430.

All Enterprise Data Archiving implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Archiving Architecture governing secure, compliant and long-term preservation of Enterprise information throughout its lifecycle.

Enterprise Data Archiving shall

- preserve Enterprise information
- support regulatory compliance
- protect historical records
- optimize operational storage
- reduce infrastructure costs
- support business continuity
- maintain information integrity
- strengthen governance
- enable secure retrieval
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Archiving shall provide a standardized architectural framework for trusted long-term information preservation.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- structured data archives
- unstructured data archives
- document archives
- database archives
- application archives
- cloud archives
- storage archives
- immutable archives
- legal archives
- historical information repositories

The standard applies regardless of storage technology, cloud provider, archive platform or deployment model.

---

# 3. Enterprise Data Archiving Principles

Enterprise Data Archiving shall be governed by the following principles.

## Preservation by Design

Enterprise information shall remain complete, authentic and accessible throughout its retention period.

---

## Compliance by Design

Archiving shall satisfy applicable legal, regulatory and business retention requirements.

---

## Secure Archiving

Archived information shall remain protected against unauthorized access, alteration and destruction.

---

## Metadata-Driven Archiving

Archive management shall be governed through Enterprise metadata.

---

## Reusable Archive Services

Archive services shall be reusable across Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Archiving shall remain independent of archive technologies, storage vendors and infrastructure providers.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Archiving Architecture establishes the standardized framework for secure, compliant and long-term preservation of Enterprise information throughout its lifecycle.

By integrating Enterprise Data Governance, Metadata Management, Information Lifecycle Management, Security, Observability, Automation and Compliance, the Enterprise ensures trusted archive services that preserve information integrity, satisfy regulatory obligations and optimize long-term storage across all Business and Technology Domains.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Archiving initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing Enterprise archive capabilities.

The Enterprise Data Archiving Architecture shall continuously evolve to support AI-assisted archive classification, autonomous retention optimization, intelligent archive indexing, predictive storage management, self-validating archive integrity, policy-driven compliance automation, quantum-resistant archive protection and emerging cloud-native archival technologies.
