# 1. Purpose

This document defines the seventy-eighth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-77 – Enterprise Risk Management, Compliance, Internal Controls, Audit Management, Policy Governance & Regulatory Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled supplier, procurement and third-party governance capability covering sourcing, supplier lifecycle, due diligence, third-party risk, contracts, obligations, supplier performance, service levels, incidents, changes, renewals, termination, subcontractors, supply-chain resilience and evidence-based supplier assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that suppliers and third parties are selected, governed, monitored and retired according to business need, risk, contractual obligations, service expectations, security, privacy, resilience and assurance requirements.**

# 123. Final Supplier Principle

> **Suppliers must be governed according to their business value, criticality, risk and dependency throughout the complete relationship lifecycle.**

---

# 124. Final Procurement Principle

> **Procurement must translate business need into transparent, appropriately competitive and risk-aware sourcing decisions.**

---

# 125. Final Contract Principle

> **Material contracts must make obligations, responsibilities, service expectations, protection requirements and exit conditions clear and traceable.**

---

# 126. Final Performance Principle

> **Supplier performance must be measured against meaningful service, quality, cost and business outcomes rather than contractual activity alone.**

---

# 127. Final Third-Party Risk Principle

> **Third-party risk must consider direct supplier exposure together with critical dependencies, subcontractors, concentration and supply-chain effects.**

---

# 128. Final Security Principle

> **Suppliers with material access to systems, services or information must meet security requirements proportionate to the associated risk.**

---

# 129. Final Privacy Principle

> **Suppliers processing personal data must be governed according to applicable privacy, contractual and security requirements throughout the processing lifecycle.**

---

# 130. Final Resilience Principle

> **Critical supplier relationships must have proportionate continuity, recovery and exit arrangements so that supplier failure does not create unmanaged operational dependency.**

---

# 131. Final Exit Principle

> **Critical supplier relationships should be capable of controlled transition, termination or replacement without unacceptable loss of service, data, knowledge or control.**

---

# 132. Final Assurance Principle

> **Supplier assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that material third parties meet defined contractual, operational, security, privacy, resilience and compliance expectations.**

---

# 133. Final Integration Principle

> **Supplier Governance must integrate with Procurement, Contract, Financial, Security, Privacy, Risk, Service, Change, Incident, Data, Architecture and Enterprise Assurance governance.**

---

# 134. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage third-party relationships through a controlled lifecycle connecting business need, sourcing, due diligence, risk, contracts, performance, incidents, changes, subcontractors, resilience, exit and continuous assurance.**
