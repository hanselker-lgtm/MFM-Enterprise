# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Transcendent Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a conceptual architecture model for future advanced intelligence ecosystems beyond traditional intelligence boundaries.

The Transcendent Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- transcendent intelligence architecture principles
- transcendent intelligence governance
- transcendent intelligence strategy
- transcendent capability architecture
- beyond-singularity intelligence architecture
- universal reasoning expansion architecture
- advanced learning evolution concepts
- intelligence self-organisation architecture
- collective intelligence evolution architecture
- human-transcendent intelligence collaboration
- transcendent safety architecture
- transcendent alignment architecture
- transcendent operations
- transcendent performance management
- transcendent evolution

The framework ensures that future advanced intelligence capabilities are approached through structured architecture, governance and responsible evolution.
