# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Platform Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities are enabled through platforms, infrastructures, technologies, models, data and integrated service capabilities.

The Intelligence Platform Architecture Framework shall provide:

- platform architecture principles
- platform modelling frameworks
- platform capability models
- infrastructure alignment models
- technology integration mechanisms
- platform governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through scalable and adaptive platform ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Platform Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Platform Model
- Platform Intelligence Architecture
- Platform Knowledge Architecture
- Platform Capability Model
- Platform Architecture Integration
- Platform Governance Model
- Platform Evolution Framework
- Platform Metrics
- Platform Maturity Model
- Platform Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Platform Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Platform Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Platform Capability Alignment

Intelligence platforms shall align with enterprise capabilities and strategic objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Scalable Platform Design

Platforms shall support growth, expansion and changing requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Integrated Platform Ecosystem

Platforms shall connect data, models, services, applications and infrastructure.

---

## Principle 4 – Adaptive Platform Evolution

Platforms shall evolve according to technological and operational changes.

---

## Principle 5 – Platform Reliability

Platforms shall maintain availability, resilience and operational continuity.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Platform Improvement

Platforms shall improve through monitoring, learning and optimisation.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Platform Governance

Platforms shall maintain security, accountability and ethical alignment.
