# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Authorization Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Identity Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-471.

Enterprise Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-467.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Authorization Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Authorization Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, policy-driven and continuously governed authorization decisions across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Authorization Services shall

- provide centralized authorization management
- standardize authorization decisions
- strengthen access governance
- reduce authorization-related risks
- support compliance verification
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure authorization traceability
- improve organizational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Authorization Services shall provide trusted and reusable authorization capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise authorization services
- authorization decision services
- authorization policy services
- policy decision points
- policy enforcement points
- fine-grained authorization
- attribute-based access control
- role-based access control
- context-aware authorization
- enterprise users and workloads

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Authorization Services Principles

Enterprise Authorization Services shall follow these principles.

## Authorization by Design

Authorization capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, business processes, applications, APIs, services and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every authorization request shall be authenticated, authorized, audited and continuously monitored.

---

## Least Privilege Enforcement

Authorization decisions shall enforce least privilege using centralized policies, verified identities and approved business rules.

---

## Metadata Driven Authorization

Authorization policies, attributes, roles, entitlements and authorization decisions shall be classified and governed using Enterprise metadata to ensure traceability, discoverability and consistent reporting.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Authorization Services shall remain independent of vendors, authorization platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Authorization Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and policy-driven Enterprise authorization across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating authorization decision services, authorization policy services, policy decision points, policy enforcement points, fine-grained authorization, attribute-based access control, role-based access control, context-aware authorization, governance, identity governance, risk, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables consistent, transparent and scalable authorization while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Authorization Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise authorization capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 34. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Authorization Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted authorization decisions, autonomous policy optimization, predictive authorization analytics, semantic-aware policy intelligence, adaptive trust evaluation, continuous authorization, decentralized authorization models and quantum-safe Enterprise authorization infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Authorization Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven authorization capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
