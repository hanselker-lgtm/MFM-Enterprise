# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Recovery Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed recovery capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Recovery Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem recovery capabilities
- support recovery governance
- support recovery strategy
- support recovery objectives
- support recovery planning
- support service recovery
- support application recovery
- support data recovery
- support information recovery
- support integration recovery
- support partner recovery
- support supplier recovery
- support security recovery
- support automation recovery
- support orchestration recovery
- support recovery testing
- support recovery monitoring
- support recovery lifecycle management
- improve ecosystem recovery capability
- improve ecosystem availability
- improve ecosystem operational confidence

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Recovery Services shall provide trusted and reusable recovery capabilities enabling rapid restoration of ecosystem operations.
