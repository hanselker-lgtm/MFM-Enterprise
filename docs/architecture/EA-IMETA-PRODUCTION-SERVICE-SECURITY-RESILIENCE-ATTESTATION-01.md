# 1. PURPOSE

EA-IMETA-PRODUCTION-SERVICE-SECURITY-RESILIENCE-ATTESTATION-01 establishes the formal attestation capability through which an authorized accountable party makes a documented statement about a defined production security, resilience, governance or control condition.

Attestation converts accountable ownership into a traceable statement supported by evidence.

The attestation chain is:

```text
REQUIREMENT
 ↓
CONTROL
 ↓
IMPLEMENTATION
 ↓
OPERATION
 ↓
EVIDENCE
 ↓
ASSESSMENT
 ↓
ACCOUNTABLE STATEMENT
 ↓
SIGN-OFF
 ↓
VALIDITY
 ↓
MONITORING
 ↓
RE-ATTESTATION
```

---

# 2. ATTESTATION PRINCIPLE

> EA-IMETA SHALL ACCEPT AN ATTESTATION ONLY WHEN THE ATTESTER, SCOPE, CRITERIA, EVIDENCE, PERIOD, ACCOUNTABILITY AND SIGN-OFF AUTHORITY ARE EXPLICITLY IDENTIFIED AND TRACEABLE.

---

# 37. NOT ATTESTED

The condition cannot responsibly be attested within the defined scope.

---

# 38. EXPIRED

The attestation has reached the end of its defined validity period.

---

# 39. WITHDRAWN

The attestation has been withdrawn because its basis is no longer reliable or the statement is no longer valid.

# 41. POINT-IN-TIME ATTESTATION

A point-in-time attestation applies only to the defined state at the specified time or assessment period.

---

A period attestation covers a defined operational period and requires evidence representative of that period.

---

# 43. CONTINUOUS ATTESTATION

Where supported, continuous attestation may use continuously collected evidence to maintain confidence in a defined condition.

---

# 44. CONTINUOUS ATTESTATION LIMIT
