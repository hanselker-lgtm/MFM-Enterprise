# 1. Purpose

N9-A establishes a controlled mechanism for identifying, classifying and assessing material changes in the strategic and external environment that may affect the MFM enterprise architecture, capabilities, portfolio, investment priorities, risks, outcomes or value.

The objective is not to monitor everything.

The objective is to identify changes material enough to require controlled enterprise assessment.

# 3. Strategic Change Model

The primary monitoring chain is:

```text
ENVIRONMENTAL CHANGE
        ↓
CHANGE DETECTION
        ↓
MATERIALITY ASSESSMENT
        ↓
ENTERPRISE IMPACT
        ↓
ARCHITECTURE IMPACT
        ↓
CAPABILITY IMPACT
        ↓
PORTFOLIO / INVESTMENT IMPACT
        ↓
OUTCOME / VALUE IMPACT
        ↓
GOVERNANCE DECISION
```

A detected change does not automatically become an approved change.

# 43. Final N9-A Statement

> **N9-A establishes the Continuous Strategic & Environmental Change Monitoring layer of the authorized N9 workstream. It provides controlled detection, classification, materiality assessment and escalation of changes that may affect strategy, business, capabilities, architecture, portfolio, investment, risk, outcomes or value. N9-A does not itself approve changes, investments, funding or implementation. Material architecture impacts are escalated to N9-B, while portfolio and investment impacts are escalated to N9-C. All material claims remain evidence-dependent and all scope expansion remains subject to explicit authorization.**
