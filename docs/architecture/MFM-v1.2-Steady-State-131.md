# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the one-hundred-and-thirty-first document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-130 – Enterprise Identity & Access Management Architecture, IAM Governance, Authentication, Authorization, Privileged Access, Identity Lifecycle, Federation, Directory Services, Access Governance, Identity Security & IAM Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for service-management strategy, ITSM governance, service portfolio, service catalogue, service ownership, service-level management, incident management, major incident management, problem management, request fulfilment, change management, release management, configuration management, asset management, knowledge management, event management, service monitoring, service continuity, service reporting, ITSM suppliers, ITSM compliance, ITSM exceptions, remediation, ITSM assurance, metrics, dashboards, maturity and continual enterprise service-management capability improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must govern enterprise services through an integrated service-management capability that provides clear ownership, defined service outcomes, controlled service operation, reliable support, disciplined change, accurate configuration and asset information, effective knowledge, resilient service continuity and evidence-based continual improvement.**

# 3. Service Management Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish clear authority, ownership, service definitions, processes, controls, performance expectations and assurance for enterprise services.**

# 4. Service Architecture Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide coherent service structures linking business capabilities, services, applications, data, infrastructure, cloud, identity, suppliers and operational processes.**

# 5. Service Portfolio Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain an accurate portfolio of services, their owners, consumers, criticality, dependencies, lifecycle, cost, risk and strategic position.**

# 6. Service Catalogue Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide a controlled and accessible catalogue of approved services, service options, service levels, support arrangements and request mechanisms.**

# 7. Service Operations Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Operate services reliably through disciplined incident, request, problem, event, change, release, configuration and performance management.**

# 8. Service Continuity Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure critical services can withstand disruption and recover within defined business and service requirements.**

# 9. ITSM Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide evidence that service-management processes, controls and service outcomes operate effectively and support business, operational, security and compliance requirements.**
