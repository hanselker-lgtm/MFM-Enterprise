## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-regression-revalidation-acceptance layer defining when a revalidated state may be formally accepted as restored, compliant, controlled and suitable for progression toward reliance, resolution and re-closure.

## Core Principle
Acceptance is an authorized governance decision based on successful revalidation; it is not a substitute for revalidation. Acceptance shall confirm that required criteria are met, evidence is sufficient, residual risk is understood and the accepting authority is entitled to make the decision.

```text
REVALIDATED STATE
      ↓
ACCEPTANCE PRECONDITIONS
      ↓
CRITERIA + EVIDENCE + RESIDUAL RISK
      ↓
AUTHORITY REVIEW
      ↓
ACCEPT / CONDITIONALLY ACCEPT / REJECT / DEFER
      ↓
RELIANCE / RESOLUTION / RE-CLOSURE
```

## Acceptance Quality Test
```text
VALID REVALIDATION
+
CURRENT ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
+
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
+
RESIDUAL RISK ASSESSED
+
NO UNRESOLVED MATERIAL GAP
+
AUTHORIZED ACCEPTANCE AUTHORITY
+
TRACEABLE DECISION
=
VALID GOVERNED ACCEPTANCE
```

## Acceptance Status Model
```text
NOT READY
READY FOR ACCEPTANCE
UNDER ACCEPTANCE REVIEW
ACCEPTED
CONDITIONALLY ACCEPTED
REJECTED
DEFERRED
ACCEPTANCE CHALLENGED
ACCEPTANCE REVOKED
REOPENED
ESCALATED
SUPERSEDED
```

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Revalidation | Proof of restored state |
| Criteria | Conditions for acceptance |
| Evidence | Supporting proof |
| Residual Risk | Remaining exposure |
| Authority | Authorized decision maker |
| Decision | Acceptance result |
| Conditions | Obligations attached to acceptance |
| Review Point | Future confirmation where required |
| Follow-on | Reliance / resolution / closure |

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Objective

Formally determine whether the revalidated state is sufficiently restored and governed to permit the next lifecycle state without concealing unresolved material risk.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Definition

Acceptance is the authorized determination that the revalidated state satisfies applicable criteria and is suitable for the permitted level of reliance, resolution or closure.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Scope

Scope shall include all revalidated controls, outcomes, boundaries, dependencies, residual risks and conditions relevant to the acceptance decision.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Authority

Authority shall define who may accept, conditionally accept, reject, defer, challenge or revoke acceptance.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Criteria

Criteria shall be explicit and current.

```text
REVALIDATED STATE
↓
ALL MANDATORY CRITERIA MET?
├── NO → REJECT / REMEDIATE
└── YES
     ↓
MATERIAL RESIDUAL RISK ACCEPTABLE?
├── NO → REJECT / ESCALATE
└── YES
     ↓
AUTHORIZED ACCEPTANCE?
├── NO → DEFER / ESCALATE
└── YES → ACCEPT
```

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Preconditions

Preconditions include completed required revalidation, current evidence, approved criteria, identified residual risk, unresolved-gap review and appropriate authority.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Evidence

Evidence shall demonstrate satisfaction of acceptance criteria and preserve the basis for the decision, including exceptions and conditions.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Decision

The decision shall be explicit: accepted, conditionally accepted, rejected or deferred.

```text
ACCEPTED → PROGRESS
CONDITIONALLY ACCEPTED → CONTROL CONDITIONS
REJECTED → REMEDIATE / REVALIDATE
DEFERRED → COMPLETE GAPS / ESCALATE
```

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Accountability

Accountability for acceptance shall remain with the authorized accepting role. Delegated evidence collection shall not transfer decision accountability.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Residual Risk

Residual risk shall be identified, assessed and treated. Acceptance of residual risk shall require appropriate authority and shall not override mandatory requirements.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Timing

Acceptance shall occur only after required revalidation and within the validity period of the evidence and criteria. Material delay or changed conditions may require renewed reassessment or revalidation.

## Security Regression Revalidation Acceptance

Acceptance shall confirm restored security controls, exposure boundaries, access conditions and required protective capabilities.

## Resilience Regression Revalidation Acceptance

Acceptance shall confirm restored resilience, recovery, continuity, capacity and dependency conditions.

## Compliance Regression Revalidation Acceptance

Acceptance shall confirm that applicable compliance and control requirements are met and sufficiently evidenced for authorized reliance.

## Data Regression Revalidation Acceptance

Acceptance shall confirm data integrity, quality, lineage, access, retention and authorized-use conditions.

## AI and Agent Regression Revalidation Acceptance

Acceptance shall confirm that AI/agent authority, policy, tool permissions, data boundaries, autonomy and behaviour satisfy the approved governed state.

```text
REVALIDATED AI / AGENT STATE
↓
BOUNDARIES SATISFY CRITERIA?
├── NO → REJECT / LIMIT / REMEDIATE
└── YES → AUTHORIZED ACCEPTANCE
             ↓
        RELIANCE / RESOLUTION / CLOSURE
```

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Failure

Failure to reach valid acceptance, invalid evidence, unresolved material gaps or unauthorized acceptance shall trigger remediation, escalation, reassessment or revalidation.

```text
ACCEPTANCE FAILURE
↓
PREVENT PREMATURE RELIANCE / CLOSURE
↓
IDENTIFY GAP
↓
REMEDIATE / REVALIDATE
↓
REASSESS ACCEPTANCE
```

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Escalation

Escalation shall occur when acceptance authority is insufficient, residual risk exceeds authority, material criteria conflict, acceptance is disputed or repeated conditional acceptance persists.

## Regression Revalidation Acceptance Review and Learning

Reviews shall examine rejected, deferred, conditional and revoked acceptance for systemic issues in criteria, evidence, revalidation, risk treatment and authority design.

## Conditional Acceptance
Conditional acceptance shall define every condition, owner, due date or review point, evidence requirement and consequence of failure. It shall not be used as an indefinite substitute for required remediation.

```text
CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE
↓
CONDITIONS + OWNER + DEADLINE
↓
MONITOR
↓
CONDITIONS SATISFIED?
├── YES → FULL ACCEPTANCE
└── NO → REOPEN / ESCALATE / REVALIDATE
```

## Acceptance Revocation
Acceptance shall be revocable when material evidence changes, a condition is breached, required state regresses, the acceptance basis becomes invalid or the authority determines that reliance is no longer justified.

## Acceptance Independence
Where materiality warrants it, acceptance shall be separated from the role responsible for executing remediation so that the remediation owner does not become the sole authority confirming acceptance.

## Acceptance Change Control
Changes to acceptance criteria, authority, residual-risk limits, conditions, evidence requirements or review periods shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated.

```text
CURRENT ACCEPTANCE MODEL
↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
↓
NEW VERSION
↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

## Acceptance Anti-Gaming Control
Acceptance shall not be granted to meet reporting targets, preserve closure metrics, avoid escalation or conceal residual risk. The approved criteria and authorized governance decision remain controlling.

Historical acceptance decisions, conditions, exceptions, revocations, evidence and authority records shall remain preserved according to applicable retention and governance requirements.
