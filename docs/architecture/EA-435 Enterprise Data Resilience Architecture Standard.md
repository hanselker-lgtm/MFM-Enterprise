# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Resilience Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Recovery principles are inherited from EA-434.

All Enterprise Data Resilience implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Resilience Architecture that enables continuous protection, availability, integrity and recoverability of Enterprise information throughout its complete lifecycle.

Enterprise Data Resilience shall

- ensure continuous business operations
- protect Enterprise information
- minimize operational disruption
- strengthen cyber resilience
- support business continuity
- improve availability
- increase operational stability
- reduce recovery risks
- enable proactive resilience
- support enterprise-wide governance

Enterprise Data Resilience shall provide a standardized architectural framework that enables the Enterprise to withstand, respond to and recover from operational disruptions while maintaining trusted information services.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise information
- databases
- applications
- cloud services
- infrastructure platforms
- storage systems
- integration platforms
- data pipelines
- operational services
- disaster recovery environments

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure provider, storage technology or cloud platform.

# 3. Enterprise Data Resilience Principles

Enterprise Data Resilience shall be governed by the following principles.

## Resilience by Design

Enterprise information and supporting services shall be designed for continuous resilience from inception.

---

## Continuous Availability

Critical Enterprise information shall remain continuously available according to approved service objectives.

---

## Defense in Depth

Multiple independent resilience mechanisms shall protect Enterprise information against failures and cyber threats.

---

## Recovery Readiness

Enterprise recovery capabilities shall remain continuously prepared for execution.

---

## Automation First

Resilience capabilities shall maximize automation while minimizing manual operational intervention.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Resilience Architecture shall remain independent of infrastructure vendors, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Resilience Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework that protects Enterprise information through continuous availability, redundancy, cyber resilience, recovery readiness and operational resilience.

By integrating Enterprise Backup, Replication, Recovery, Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Security, Observability and Automation capabilities into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform achieves trusted, scalable and resilient information services capable of supporting mission-critical business operations.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Resilience initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing resilience capabilities across the Enterprise.

The Enterprise Data Resilience Architecture shall evolve to support autonomous resilience platforms, AI-assisted resilience optimization, predictive failure analytics, self-healing infrastructure, policy-driven cyber recovery, quantum-resistant protection mechanisms, adaptive workload resilience, digital twin resilience simulation and cloud-native autonomous recovery services.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Resilience continues to provide secure, interoperable, scalable and business-driven protection for Enterprise information assets across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
