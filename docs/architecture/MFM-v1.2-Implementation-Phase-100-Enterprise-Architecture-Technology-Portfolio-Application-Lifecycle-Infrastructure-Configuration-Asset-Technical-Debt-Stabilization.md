# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundredth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-99 – Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Operational Resilience, Crisis Management & Recovery Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise architecture and technology portfolio capability covering business capability architecture, application portfolio, technology portfolio, application lifecycle, infrastructure lifecycle, configuration management, asset management, CMDB governance, dependency mapping, technology standards, architecture principles, technical debt, legacy technology, technology risk, architecture exceptions, technology roadmaps, platform governance, infrastructure standards, application rationalization and technology lifecycle assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain an intentional, governed and evidence-based technology landscape in which business capabilities, applications, infrastructure, configurations and assets remain aligned with organizational objectives, risk requirements, lifecycle expectations and long-term architectural direction.**

# 125. Definition of Ready

An architecture or technology portfolio work item is Ready when:

- The business capability or service context is identified.
- Ownership is established.
- Current-state information is available.
- Target-state requirements are understood.
- Dependencies and lifecycle considerations are identified.
- Security, privacy, resilience, data and integration requirements are known.
- Cost, risk and technical debt implications are understood.
