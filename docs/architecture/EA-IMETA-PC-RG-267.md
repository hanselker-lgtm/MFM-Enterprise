## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory procedural verification layer for RG-267, determining whether the substantive validation of RG-266 verification was correctly performed, evidenced, authorized, decided, recorded and implemented.

## Core Principle

RG-266 determines whether the RG-265 verification is substantively effective. RG-267 verifies that RG-266's validation of that verification was correctly performed and implemented.

```text
RG-264 → VALIDATE
RG-265 → VERIFY
RG-266 → VALIDATE
RG-267 → VERIFY
```

```text
RG-266 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
RG-267 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
WAS RG-266 CORRECTLY PERFORMED?
```

A positive RG-266 validation SHALL NOT automatically prove that RG-266 was correctly verified.
