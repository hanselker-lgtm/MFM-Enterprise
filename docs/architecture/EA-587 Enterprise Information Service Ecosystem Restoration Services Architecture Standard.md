# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Restoration Services Architecture supporting the controlled restoration of Enterprise capabilities, services, operations and ecosystem functions following disruption, degradation or loss of operational state across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Restoration Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem restoration services
- support restoration governance
- support restoration strategy
- support restoration objectives
- support restoration planning
- support service restoration
- support capability restoration
- support business restoration
- support operational restoration
- support technology restoration
- support application restoration
- support infrastructure restoration
- support data restoration
- support information restoration
- support integration restoration
- support security restoration
- support ecosystem restoration
- support operational state restoration
- support service availability restoration
- support restoration validation
- support restoration measurement
- support restoration improvement
- support restoration maturity development
- support continuous restoration capability development

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Restoration Services shall provide trusted and reusable restoration capabilities enabling Enterprise services and capabilities to return to a stable and approved operational state.
