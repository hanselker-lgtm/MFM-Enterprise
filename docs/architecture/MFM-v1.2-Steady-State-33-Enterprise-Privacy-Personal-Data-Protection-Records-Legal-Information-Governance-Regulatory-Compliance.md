# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the thirty-third document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-32 – Enterprise Cybersecurity, Information Security, Identity & Access Management, Security Operations, Vulnerability & Security Governance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for privacy governance, personal-data protection, records and information governance, legal information management, regulatory compliance, data-subject rights, processing governance, retention, legal holds, compliance obligations, regulatory monitoring and continual compliance improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must process personal data and controlled information lawfully, fairly, transparently, securely and only for legitimate purposes while maintaining appropriate records, legal controls and evidence of regulatory compliance throughout the information lifecycle.**

# 3. Privacy Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure that personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, transparently, securely and consistently with approved purposes and applicable requirements.**

# 4. Records Governance Objective

> **Ensure that important records remain authentic, reliable, available, protected, retrievable and appropriately retained or disposed of throughout their required lifecycle.**

---

# 5. Regulatory Compliance Objective

# 124. Definition of Ready

A privacy, records, legal-information or compliance work item is Ready when:

- The processing activity, personal-data issue, rights request, DPIA, record, legal hold, obligation, regulatory change, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, purpose, applicability, risk, classification, retention, security, privacy and legal requirements are known.
- Assessment, approval, evidence, implementation, response and acceptance requirements are defined.

---

# 125. Definition of Done

        ↓

        ↓
