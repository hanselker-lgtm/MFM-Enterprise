# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational sustainability architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities can be developed, operated and maintained through responsible resource utilisation, sustainable technology practices and long-term capability preservation.

The Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- sustainability operating principles
- sustainability governance frameworks
- sustainability lifecycle structures
- sustainability management models
- resource sustainability models
- operational sustainability models
- technology sustainability models
- environmental sustainability models
- sustainability validation models
- sustainability security models
- sustainability monitoring structures
- sustainability automation models
- sustainability evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for sustainable, responsible and continuously improving Intelligence capabilities.

# 3. Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Sustainability by Design

Sustainability capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Resource Utilisation

Intelligence capability shall use resources efficiently and responsibly.

---

## Principle 3 – Long-Term Capability Preservation

Critical Intelligence capabilities shall be maintained for long-term value creation.

---

## Principle 4 – Sustainable Technology Management

Technology choices shall support lifecycle sustainability.

---

## Principle 5 – Operational Sustainability

Operational processes shall support stable and efficient long-term operation.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Sustainability Improvement

Sustainability capability shall continuously improve through measurement and learning.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolution Through Sustainability

Sustainability shall support continuous Intelligence evolution.
