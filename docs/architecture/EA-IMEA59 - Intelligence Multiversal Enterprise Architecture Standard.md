# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, and intelligence capabilities required for an **Intelligence Multiversal Enterprise Architecture (IMEA)**.

While EA-IIEA58 enables continuously expanding Enterprise Intelligence within an unlimited semantic continuum, EA-IMEA59 extends Enterprise Architecture into a **Multiversal Intelligence Architecture**, capable of operating across multiple independent knowledge universes, semantic realities, governance models, reasoning systems, and contextual dimensions.

The objective is to coordinate architectural intelligence across multiple simultaneously existing knowledge realities without compromising explainability, governance, enterprise purpose, sustainability, or human accountability.

Enterprise Architecture shall evolve beyond one continuous knowledge space into an architecture capable of integrating multiple autonomous realities into one coherent intelligence framework.
