# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Knowledge Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational knowledge architecture that ensures Intelligence knowledge is created, governed, managed, represented, shared, validated, protected and continuously improved.

The Intelligence Knowledge Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- knowledge operating principles
- knowledge governance frameworks
- knowledge lifecycle structures
- knowledge management models
- knowledge representation models
- knowledge sharing structures
- knowledge validation models
- knowledge security models
- knowledge monitoring structures
- knowledge automation models
- knowledge evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for trusted, reusable and continuously improving Intelligence knowledge capabilities.

# 3. Intelligence Knowledge Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Knowledge Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Knowledge by Design

Knowledge capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Knowledge Governance

Knowledge shall be managed according to defined governance principles.

---

## Principle 3 – Knowledge Trust

Knowledge shall be validated and maintained with confidence.

---

## Principle 4 – Knowledge Accessibility

Authorised users shall have access to relevant knowledge.

---

## Principle 5 – Knowledge Preservation

Important knowledge shall be maintained and protected.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Knowledge Improvement

Knowledge capability shall continuously improve through learning and experience.

---

## Principle 7 – Knowledge Evolution

Knowledge shall adapt to changing requirements and environments.
