# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Availability Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Resilience principles are inherited from EA-435.

All Enterprise Data Availability implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Availability Architecture ensuring that Enterprise information remains continuously accessible, reliable, consistent and available across all Business and Technology Domains.

Enterprise Data Availability shall

- ensure continuous information access
- maximize service availability
- minimize service interruptions
- improve operational continuity
- support business-critical workloads
- strengthen resilience
- optimize user experience
- enable proactive operations
- improve service reliability
- support enterprise-wide governance

Enterprise Data Availability shall provide a standardized architectural framework for delivering trusted access to Enterprise information regardless of infrastructure failures, maintenance activities or changing workload demands.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise databases
- applications
- APIs
- cloud services
- storage platforms
- integration platforms
- analytical platforms
- reporting services
- operational services
- digital business services

The standard applies across all deployment models including on-premises, hybrid and cloud-native environments.

# 3. Enterprise Data Availability Principles

Enterprise Data Availability shall be governed by the following principles.

## Availability by Design

Enterprise information services shall be designed to remain continuously available throughout their operational lifecycle.

---

## Continuous Accessibility

Authorized users, applications and services shall have reliable access to Enterprise information whenever required.

---

## Fault Tolerance

Enterprise Data Availability shall tolerate infrastructure, platform and application failures without unacceptable service disruption.

---

## Operational Simplicity

Availability mechanisms shall minimize operational complexity while maximizing service reliability.

---

## Automated Availability

Availability capabilities shall maximize automation for detection, failover, recovery and service restoration.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Availability Architecture shall remain independent of specific infrastructure technologies, cloud providers and software vendors.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Availability Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework that ensures Enterprise information remains continuously accessible, reliable and resilient across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating High Availability, Load Distribution, Capacity Management, Security, Observability, Automation and Business Continuity into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform delivers trusted information services capable of supporting mission-critical operations while minimizing service disruption and maximizing user experience.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Availability initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing availability capabilities across the Enterprise.
