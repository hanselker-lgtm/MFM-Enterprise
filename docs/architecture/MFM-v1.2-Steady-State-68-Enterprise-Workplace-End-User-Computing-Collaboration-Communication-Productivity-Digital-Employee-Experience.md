# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the sixty-eighth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-67 – Enterprise Infrastructure, Cloud, Network, Platform, Compute, Storage, Backup, Endpoint & Infrastructure Operations.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for workplace services, end-user computing, employee devices, digital workplace platforms, collaboration, communication, productivity, meeting technology, messaging, email, document productivity, file sharing, remote work, mobility, printing, user onboarding, workplace support, endpoint experience, digital employee experience, workplace security coordination, workplace continuity, workplace lifecycle, workplace configuration, workplace monitoring, workplace incidents, workplace requests, workplace problems, workplace changes, workplace releases, workplace assurance, workplace findings, exceptions, remediation, workplace metrics, workplace dashboards, workplace maturity and continual workplace improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must provide secure, reliable, accessible, productive and user-centered workplace capabilities that enable people to communicate, collaborate, access information and perform their responsibilities effectively.**

# 3. Workplace Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure employees have secure, reliable, accessible and fit-for-purpose workplace capabilities.**

The primary objective is:

# 95. Definition of Ready

A workplace service, device, collaboration capability, communication service, productivity capability, onboarding item, release, incident, request, improvement, exception or remediation item is Ready when purpose, owner, users, dependencies, security, support, lifecycle, experience requirements, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
