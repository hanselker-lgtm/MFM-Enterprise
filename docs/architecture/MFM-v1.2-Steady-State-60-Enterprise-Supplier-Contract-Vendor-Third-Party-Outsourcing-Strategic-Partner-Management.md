# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the sixtieth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-59 – Enterprise Financial Management, Budgeting, Accounting, Procurement, Cost Management & Financial Control.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for supplier management, vendor management, third-party governance, contract management, outsourcing, strategic partnerships, supplier due diligence, onboarding, performance management, service levels, commercial management, financial controls, information security, privacy, resilience, concentration risk, exit planning, supplier incidents, supplier changes, supplier assurance, supplier findings, exceptions, remediation, supplier metrics, supplier reporting, supplier maturity and continual supplier improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage suppliers, vendors, third parties, outsourced services and strategic partners throughout their lifecycle so that external dependencies remain controlled, performant, secure, compliant, resilient, commercially appropriate and aligned with MFM objectives.**

# 3. Supplier Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain controlled, transparent and performance-oriented relationships with suppliers and third parties throughout their lifecycle.**

# 4. Third-Party Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify, assess, treat and monitor risks arising from external dependencies.**

# 5. Contract Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure contracts define enforceable, measurable and appropriately controlled commercial, operational, security, privacy, continuity and governance obligations.**

# 6. Outsourcing Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure outsourced services remain appropriately governed, controlled, monitored and recoverable.**

# 7. Strategic Partner Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Govern strategic partnerships so that mutual objectives, responsibilities, dependencies, risks and value remain aligned.**

A supplier, contract, outsourcing arrangement, strategic partnership, supplier risk, supplier incident, assurance review, renewal, change or exit activity is Ready when scope, owner, criticality, commercial requirements, security, privacy, continuity, dependencies, obligations and acceptance criteria are defined.

# 98. Final Supplier Principle

> **MFM must maintain controlled, transparent and performance-oriented relationships with suppliers and third parties throughout their lifecycle.**

# 99. Final Risk Principle

> **External dependencies must be identified, assessed, owned, treated and monitored according to their materiality and risk.**

# 100. Final Contract Principle

> **Material contracts must clearly define obligations, responsibilities, service levels, security, privacy, continuity, commercial terms, change and exit requirements.**

# 101. Final Performance Principle

> **Supplier performance must be measurable against agreed service levels, quality, cost and business outcomes.**

# 102. Final Security Principle

> **Supplier access to MFM systems, services and information must be authorized, least-privileged, monitored and removed when no longer required.**

# 103. Final Continuity Principle

> **Critical supplier dependencies must have proportionate continuity, recovery and exit arrangements.**

# 104. Final Exit Principle

> **MFM must retain sufficient capability to manage material supplier termination, transition or failure without unacceptable disruption.**

# 105. Final Assurance Principle

> **Critical and high-risk suppliers must provide sufficient evidence that relevant contractual, security, privacy, continuity and control requirements are operating effectively.**

# 106. Final Improvement Principle

> **Supplier performance, incidents, findings, commercial outcomes and lessons learned must continuously improve MFM's third-party management capability.**

# 107. Final Integration Principle

> **Supplier and Third-Party Management must integrate with Procurement, Financial Management, Legal, Enterprise Risk, Cybersecurity, Privacy, Data Management, Applications, Infrastructure, Service Management, Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery and Enterprise Architecture.**

# 108. Final Steady-State Supplier Principle

> **MFM must manage suppliers, vendors, third parties, outsourced services and strategic partners throughout their lifecycle so that external dependencies remain controlled, performant, secure, compliant, resilient, commercially appropriate and aligned with MFM objectives.**
