# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Continuity Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed continuity capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Continuity Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem continuity capabilities
- support business continuity integration
- support operational continuity
- support service continuity
- support partner continuity
- support supplier continuity
- support information continuity
- support data continuity
- support integration continuity
- support automation continuity
- support orchestration continuity
- support crisis management alignment
- support continuity planning
- support continuity testing
- support continuity monitoring
- support continuity lifecycle management
- improve ecosystem stability
- improve ecosystem recovery capability
- improve ecosystem operational confidence

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Continuity Services shall provide trusted and reusable continuity capabilities enabling sustainable ecosystem operations.
