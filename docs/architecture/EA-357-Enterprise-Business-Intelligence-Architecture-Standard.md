# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Business Intelligence Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Infrastructure Layer principles are inherited from EA-320.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-356.

All Enterprise Business Intelligence implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Business Intelligence Architecture governing reporting, dashboards, analytical presentation and business insight delivery across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Business Intelligence Architecture shall

- provide trusted business reporting
- support operational, tactical and executive decision-making
- standardize dashboard architecture
- enable self-service analytics within governed boundaries
- improve information accessibility
- ensure reporting consistency
- remain technology independent

Business Intelligence shall present governed Enterprise information through standardized architectural principles.

---

# 17. Enterprise Business Intelligence Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Duplicate Reports

The same business report shall never exist in multiple conflicting versions.

Authoritative reports shall be centrally governed.

---

## Conflicting KPI Visualizations

Enterprise KPIs shall never be visualized differently across departments without documented business justification.

Visualization standards shall remain consistent.

---

## Unmanaged Self-Service BI

Self-Service BI shall never bypass Enterprise Governance or access unmanaged data sources.

All self-service capabilities shall consume governed semantic models.

---

## Embedded Business Logic

Business rules shall never be permanently embedded inside reports or dashboards.

Business logic shall reside within governed analytical or semantic services.

---

## Spreadsheet-Based Enterprise Reporting

Mission-critical Enterprise reporting shall never depend solely upon unmanaged spreadsheets.

Enterprise reporting platforms shall remain authoritative.

---

## Vendor Lock-In

Business Intelligence Architecture shall remain independent of proprietary reporting platforms and visualization technologies.

Business Intelligence services shall remain portable and technology independent.
