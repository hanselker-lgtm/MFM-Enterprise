# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Capacity Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed capacity capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Capacity Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem capacity capabilities
- support capacity governance
- support capacity strategy
- support business capacity management
- support service capacity management
- support application capacity
- support platform capacity
- support infrastructure capacity
- support data capacity
- support information capacity
- support integration capacity
- support partner capacity
- support supplier capacity
- support security capacity
- support intelligence capacity
- support analytics capacity
- support automation capacity
- support orchestration capacity
- support capacity monitoring
- support capacity forecasting
- support capacity optimization
- support capacity lifecycle management
- improve ecosystem scalability
- improve ecosystem efficiency
- improve ecosystem operational confidence

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Capacity Services shall provide trusted and reusable capacity capabilities enabling scalable ecosystem operations.
