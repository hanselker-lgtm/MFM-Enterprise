# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Distribution Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Sharing principles are inherited from EA-447.

All Enterprise Data Distribution implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Distribution Architecture supporting secure, scalable and governed distribution of Enterprise information across internal systems, business domains, cloud platforms, partners and external consumers.

Enterprise Data Distribution shall

- enable efficient information delivery
- support multiple distribution models
- improve scalability
- reduce distribution latency
- strengthen interoperability
- optimize information availability
- improve operational efficiency
- support regulatory compliance
- strengthen governance
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Distribution shall provide standardized mechanisms for delivering trusted Enterprise information wherever it is required.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise applications
- APIs
- event platforms
- integration platforms
- business domains
- cloud environments
- analytics platforms
- external partners
- suppliers
- public authorities

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or implementation technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Distribution Principles

Enterprise Data Distribution shall follow these principles.

## Distribution by Design

Enterprise information shall be designed for controlled and efficient distribution throughout the Enterprise.

---

## Authoritative Distribution

Only authoritative Enterprise information shall be distributed.

---

## Secure Distribution

Every distribution channel shall implement authentication, authorization, encryption and auditing.

---

## Metadata Driven Distribution

Distribution services shall use Enterprise metadata to manage routing, policies and lifecycle.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Distribution Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, messaging platforms, cloud providers and infrastructure technologies.

---

## Operational Transparency

Distribution services shall be fully observable, measurable and continuously optimized.
