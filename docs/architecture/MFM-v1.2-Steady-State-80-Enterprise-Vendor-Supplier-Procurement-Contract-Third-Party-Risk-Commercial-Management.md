# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eightieth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-79 – Enterprise IT Financial Management, Technology Cost Management, Budgeting, Forecasting, Chargeback, Showback, Investment & Value Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for vendor management, supplier management, procurement, strategic sourcing, supplier selection, due diligence, contract lifecycle management, commercial governance, supplier performance management, service-provider governance, third-party risk management, supplier security coordination, supplier privacy coordination, supplier continuity, supplier financial health, licensing and commercial optimization, contract renewals, supplier disputes, supplier remediation, supplier findings, supplier exceptions, supplier exit management, transition management, supplier concentration management, fourth-party risk coordination, supplier assurance, supplier metrics, supplier dashboards, supplier maturity and continual supplier improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage suppliers and third parties throughout their lifecycle so that externally provided products and services deliver agreed value, quality, security, resilience, compliance and commercial outcomes.**

# 3. Vendor Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure vendors and suppliers are selected, governed, measured and exited in a controlled manner that protects MFM's business interests.**

# 4. Procurement Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Acquire products and services through transparent, competitive, compliant and value-oriented procurement processes.**

# 5. Contract Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure contractual commitments, rights, obligations, commercial terms and service requirements are understood, monitored and controlled throughout the contract lifecycle.**

# 6. Supplier Performance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure suppliers consistently deliver agreed quality, service, security, availability, value and compliance outcomes.**

# 7. Third-Party Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and manage risks arising from dependence on external parties and their extended supply chains.**

# 8. Commercial Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Optimize commercial value while maintaining appropriate quality, resilience, security, compliance and service outcomes.**

# 9. Supplier Exit Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable controlled supplier termination and transition without unacceptable business, operational, security, data or continuity impact.**

# 116. Definition of Ready

A procurement initiative, supplier selection, contract, supplier onboarding, performance review, risk assessment, renewal, commercial optimization, supplier change, transition, exit, assurance review or remediation item is Ready when purpose, owner, scope, supplier, service, dependencies, commercial baseline, risk, security, privacy, continuity requirements, decision criteria, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
