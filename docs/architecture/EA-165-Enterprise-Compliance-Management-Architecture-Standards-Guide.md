# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise compliance management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise compliance management ensures that legal, regulatory, contractual and internal policy requirements are identified, implemented, monitored, documented and continuously improved through standardized enterprise processes while preserving governance, auditability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Compliance Management
- Regulatory Requirements
- Internal Policies
- Compliance Controls
- Compliance Monitoring
- Compliance Reporting
- Compliance Audits
- Governance

All enterprise compliance management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Compliance Management Principles

Enterprise compliance management shall follow these principles.

- Compliance by Design
- Regulatory Transparency
- Continuous Monitoring
- Governance by Default
- Traceability
- Auditability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Compliance management implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Compliance Management Domains

Enterprise compliance management shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Regulatory Compliance
- Legal Compliance
- Contractual Compliance
- Internal Policy Compliance
- Information Security Compliance
- Privacy Compliance
- Financial Compliance
- Operational Compliance

Additional compliance domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Compliance Management Ownership

Each compliance management domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the compliance management lifecycle.

---

# 7. Compliance Management Governance

Enterprise compliance management governance shall define

- compliance governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- compliance verification
- governance reporting

Compliance management governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Compliance Management Responsibilities

Enterprise compliance management shall provide controlled management of enterprise compliance obligations.

Compliance management responsibilities shall

- separate compliance management from business execution
- coordinate compliance ownership
- ensure regulatory conformity
- validate compliance objectives
- preserve compliance traceability
- support enterprise governance

Compliance management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Regulatory Requirements

Enterprise compliance management shall implement standardized management of regulatory requirements.

Regulatory requirements shall

- identify applicable legislation
- identify regulatory obligations
- identify contractual obligations
- identify internal policy requirements
- preserve requirement history
- maintain requirement traceability

Regulatory requirements shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Compliance Controls

Enterprise compliance management shall implement standardized compliance controls.

Compliance controls shall

- enforce regulatory requirements
- enforce internal policies
- support preventive controls
- support detective controls
- preserve control history
- maintain control traceability

Compliance controls shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Compliance Monitoring

Enterprise compliance management shall implement standardized compliance monitoring.

Compliance monitoring shall

- verify regulatory compliance
- verify policy compliance
- identify compliance deviations
- support corrective actions
- preserve monitoring history
- maintain monitoring traceability

Compliance monitoring shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Compliance Reporting

Enterprise compliance management shall implement standardized compliance reporting.

Compliance reporting shall

- support management reporting
- support regulatory reporting
- support audit reporting
- preserve reporting history
- maintain reporting traceability
- support governance decisions

Compliance reporting shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Compliance Management Dependencies

Enterprise compliance management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- risk management capabilities
- security capabilities
- monitoring systems
- reporting platforms
- enterprise infrastructure

Compliance management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Compliance Management Documentation

Each compliance management domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- compliance registers
- ownership information
- regulatory requirements
- compliance controls
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Compliance Management Lifecycle

Enterprise compliance management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Identified
- Classified
- Implemented
- Verified
- Monitored
- Audited
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Improved
- Retired

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Compliance Quality Attributes

Enterprise compliance management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- reliability
- governance compliance
- regulatory effectiveness

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the compliance management lifecycle.

---

# 17. Compliance Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized compliance register.

The compliance register shall contain

- compliance identifiers
- ownership assignments
- regulatory requirements
- implemented controls
- lifecycle status
- audit references
- documentation references
- governance status

The compliance register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise compliance.

---

# 18. Compliance Reviews

Enterprise compliance management implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- regulatory requirement completeness
- compliance control effectiveness
- monitoring quality
- reporting quality
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Compliance Metrics

Enterprise compliance management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- implemented compliance controls
- regulatory compliance coverage
- monitoring completion
- audit findings
- policy compliance
- unresolved compliance issues
- corrective action completion
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous compliance improvement.

---

# 20. Compliance Verification

Enterprise compliance management implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm regulatory compliance
- verify implemented controls
- verify monitoring procedures
- verify reporting accuracy
- verify ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- confirm governance compliance
- approve operational readiness

Compliance verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Compliance Improvement

Enterprise compliance management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve regulatory compliance
- improve governance
- improve monitoring quality
- improve reporting quality
- improve audit readiness
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise compliance management implementations shall handle compliance-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify regulatory compliance failures
- classify compliance control failures
- classify compliance monitoring failures
- classify reporting failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Compliance exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, regulatory obligations or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Compliance management implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved risk management capabilities
- approved security capabilities
- approved monitoring systems
- approved reporting platforms
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Compliance management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external compliance management services

Compliance management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A compliance management implementation is compliant when

- Compliance management responsibilities are documented.
- Regulatory requirements are identified.
- Compliance controls are implemented.
- Compliance monitoring is operational.
- Compliance reporting is documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Compliance Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Compliance verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Regulatory Requirements

Enterprise compliance management shall never operate without documented regulatory requirements.

---

## Undefined Compliance Ownership

Compliance obligations shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Missing Compliance Controls

Regulatory or internal policy requirements shall never remain without implemented compliance controls.

---

## Missing Compliance Monitoring

Enterprise compliance shall never operate without continuous monitoring of implemented controls.

---

## Undocumented Compliance Dependencies

Compliance management implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Compliance Management Outside Governance

Compliance management implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise compliance management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- compliance management quality
- regulatory requirement completeness
- compliance control effectiveness
- monitoring quality
- reporting quality
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Compliance Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise compliance management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and business operations implement standardized compliance identification, regulatory requirement management, compliance controls, monitoring, reporting, verification and governance through controlled lifecycle management and continuous improvement while preserving regulatory conformity, auditability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise compliance management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
