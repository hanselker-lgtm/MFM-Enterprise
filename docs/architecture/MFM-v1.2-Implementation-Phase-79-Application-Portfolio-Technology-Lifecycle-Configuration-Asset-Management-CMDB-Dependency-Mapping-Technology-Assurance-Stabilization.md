# 1. Purpose

This document defines the seventy-ninth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-78 – Supplier, Vendor, Procurement, Contract Management, Third-Party Risk & Supply-Chain Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled technology and configuration governance capability covering application portfolio management, technology lifecycle management, asset management, configuration management, CMDB governance, dependency mapping, application lifecycle, technology lifecycle, end-of-life management, technical debt, architecture standards, ownership, licensing, configuration compliance, technology risk and technology assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain accurate knowledge of its applications, technology assets, configurations and dependencies throughout their lifecycle so that technology remains supportable, secure, cost-effective, resilient and aligned with enterprise requirements.**

# 111. Definition of Ready

A technology governance work item is Ready when:

- Technology or application scope is defined.
- Owner is assigned.

# 113. Final Application Principle

> **Every material application must have a clear purpose, owner, criticality, technology context and lifecycle status.**

---

# 114. Final Asset Principle

> **Material technology assets must be identifiable, owned, governed and traceable throughout acquisition, operation, maintenance and disposal.**

---

# 115. Final Configuration Principle

> **Configuration information must be sufficiently accurate and controlled to support operational, security, change, risk and assurance decisions.**

---

# 116. Final CMDB Principle

> **The CMDB must provide trusted configuration and relationship information where that information materially supports service management, risk, change, security or continuity.**

---

# 117. Final Dependency Principle

> **Critical service dependencies must be sufficiently understood to identify material failure, concentration and recovery risks.**

---

# 118. Final Lifecycle Principle

> **Technology must not remain indefinitely outside approved lifecycle boundaries without explicit risk assessment, ownership and remediation planning.**

---

# 119. Final Technical Debt Principle

> **Technical debt must be visible, risk-assessed and prioritized so that accumulated technology complexity does not become unmanaged operational or security exposure.**

---

# 120. Final Licensing Principle

> **Software licensing must be governed against contractual entitlements, actual usage and applicable restrictions.**

---

# 121. Final Assurance Principle

> **Technology assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that applications, assets, configurations, dependencies and lifecycle controls remain fit for purpose and appropriately governed.**

---

# 122. Final Integration Principle

> **Technology Governance must integrate with Enterprise Architecture, Security, Privacy, Data, Service, Change, Incident, Supplier, Financial, Risk and Continuity governance.**

---

# 123. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage technology through a controlled lifecycle connecting applications, assets, configurations, CMDB relationships, dependencies, standards, licensing, lifecycle risk, technical debt and continuous assurance.**
