# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory enterprise standards governing Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML) and Intelligent Automation throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The guide ensures that AI solutions are secure, transparent, explainable, governed and aligned with enterprise architecture principles.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Intelligent Automation
- Model Management
- Responsible AI
- AI Security
- AI Monitoring
- AI Governance
- AI Risk Management
- Compliance

All enterprise AI capabilities shall comply with this guide.

# 4. AI Principles

Enterprise AI shall follow these principles.

- Responsible AI
- Human Oversight
- Transparency
- Explainability
- Fairness
- Privacy by Design
- Security by Design
- Continuous Improvement

AI architecture shall support trustworthy enterprise decision-making.

# 5. AI Categories

Enterprise AI governance shall support standardized categories.

AI categories shall include

- Predictive Models

- Generative AI
- Intelligent Automation
- Decision Support
- AI Monitoring
- AI Governance
- AI Risk Management

Additional AI categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

Every enterprise AI capability shall have an assigned owner.

Ownership shall define

- business responsibility
- technical responsibility
- model responsibility
- monitoring responsibility
- lifecycle responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the AI lifecycle.

# 7. AI Governance

Enterprise AI governance shall define

- AI governance
- model governance
- automation governance
- risk governance
- compliance responsibilities
- governance reporting

AI governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Machine Learning Lifecycle

Enterprise Machine Learning capabilities shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

The lifecycle shall include

- data preparation
- feature engineering
- model training
- model validation
- model deployment
- model monitoring

Each lifecycle stage shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 9. Intelligent Automation

Enterprise intelligent automation shall support controlled business automation.

Automation capabilities shall

- automate repetitive business processes
- support human oversight
- provide auditability
- validate business rules
- support exception handling
- allow controlled intervention

Automation shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 10. Model Management

Enterprise AI models shall be centrally managed.

Model management shall

- maintain model inventory
- support version control
- manage deployment history
- manage model ownership
- support model retirement
- document model lineage

Model governance shall ensure traceability throughout the model lifecycle.

---

# 11. Responsible AI

Enterprise AI shall comply with Responsible AI principles.

Responsible AI shall

- support explainability
- minimize bias
- promote fairness
- protect privacy
- ensure accountability
- support human review of significant decisions

Responsible AI requirements shall apply throughout the entire AI lifecycle.

---

# 12. Audit Integration

AI governance shall integrate with Enterprise Audit Trail Architecture.

Audit records shall include

- model deployments
- model updates
- governance approvals
- automation policy changes
- risk assessments
- AI exceptions

Audit records shall remain immutable.

---

# 13. Dependency Rules

AI infrastructure may depend upon

- Enterprise Configuration
- Enterprise Logging
- Enterprise Monitoring
- Enterprise Security
- Enterprise Infrastructure
- Approved AI Infrastructure

AI infrastructure shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Domain business rules
- Repository implementations
- Persistence models
- Workflow orchestration
- Unapproved AI technologies

AI governance shall remain independent of business functionality.

---

# 14. AI Documentation

Enterprise AI capabilities shall be documented.

Documentation shall include

- model documentation
- training datasets
- validation reports
- deployment procedures
- monitoring procedures
- operational runbooks

AI documentation shall remain synchronized with enterprise governance.

Enterprise AI capabilities shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Proposed
- Approved
- Designed
- Trained
- Validated
- Deployed
- Monitored
- Retired

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

# 16. AI Monitoring

Enterprise AI capabilities shall be continuously monitored.

Monitoring shall

- monitor prediction quality
- monitor model drift
- monitor inference latency
- monitor operational availability
- detect abnormal behavior
- support automated alerting

Monitoring shall integrate with Enterprise Observability Architecture.

---

# 17. AI Risk Management

Enterprise AI shall implement standardized risk management.

Risk management shall

- identify AI risks
- assess operational impact
- assess ethical impact
- assess security risks
- define mitigation plans
- monitor residual risks

Risk management shall remain continuously active throughout the AI lifecycle.

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized AI registry.

The registry shall contain

- model inventory
- ownership assignments
- lifecycle state
- deployment history
- validation history
- monitoring status

The AI registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise AI governance.

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized AI governance registry.

The governance registry shall contain

- approved AI standards
- approved Responsible AI policies
- approved model governance standards
- approved monitoring standards
- governance approvals
- compliance status

The governance registry shall remain synchronized with enterprise architecture documentation.

# 20. Continuous AI Improvement

Enterprise AI governance shall support continuous improvement.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve model quality
- improve prediction accuracy
- improve monitoring effectiveness
- improve governance maturity
- improve operational reliability
- improve Responsible AI practices

Continuous improvement shall be an ongoing enterprise activity.

# 21. Human Oversight

Enterprise AI shall support meaningful human oversight.

Human oversight shall

- allow review of significant AI decisions
- support manual intervention
- support decision overrides
- document human review activities
- preserve accountability
- ensure business ownership of AI-assisted decisions

Human oversight shall remain mandatory for high-impact enterprise decisions.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise AI failures shall be handled consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify model failures
- classify prediction failures
- classify automation failures
- classify monitoring failures
- preserve correlation identifiers
- notify monitoring systems

AI failures shall never compromise enterprise security, operational stability or business accountability.

AI processes shall never depend upon

- Persistence models

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An AI implementation is compliant when

- AI governance is documented.
- Responsible AI principles are implemented.
- Model lifecycle is managed.
- Human oversight is defined.
- AI monitoring is operational.
- AI risk management is implemented.
- AI registry is maintained.
- Governance requirements are enforced.
- Audit logging is enabled.
- Continuous AI improvement is demonstrated.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Unvalidated AI Models

Enterprise AI models shall never be deployed without documented validation and approval.

---

## Unmonitored Model Drift

AI models shall never remain in production without continuous monitoring for model drift and prediction quality.

---

## Missing Human Oversight

High-impact AI decisions shall never be executed without appropriate human oversight and accountability.

---

## Unmanaged AI Risk

Enterprise AI capabilities shall never operate without documented risk assessment and mitigation plans.

---

## Undocumented Model Changes

Model updates shall never be introduced into production without documented governance, version control and audit records.

---

## Uncontrolled Intelligent Automation

Automation shall never bypass enterprise governance, business rules or security controls.

---

Architecture Review shall verify

- Responsible AI implementation

- AI monitoring
- AI risk management
- auditability
- governance compliance
- operational readiness
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Artificial Intelligence & Intelligent Automation Architecture Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Intelligent Automation throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise AI capabilities remain secure, transparent, explainable, governed and aligned with enterprise architecture principles through standardized lifecycle management, Responsible AI practices, human oversight and continuous monitoring.

All AI and Intelligent Automation implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
