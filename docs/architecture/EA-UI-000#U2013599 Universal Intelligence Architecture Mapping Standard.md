# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Universal Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a unified architecture model for integrating all Enterprise intelligence capabilities into a coherent intelligence ecosystem.

The Universal Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- universal intelligence architecture principles
- universal intelligence governance
- universal intelligence strategy
- universal intelligence capability architecture
- universal reasoning architecture
- universal learning architecture
- universal knowledge architecture
- universal intelligence coordination
- universal intelligence federation
- universal intelligence ecosystem architecture
- universal human intelligence collaboration
- universal safety architecture
- universal alignment architecture
- universal operations
- universal performance management
- universal evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise intelligence capabilities operate as an integrated, governed and continuously evolving intelligence ecosystem.

---

# 3. Enterprise Universal Intelligence Architecture Principles

The Universal Intelligence Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Integrated Intelligence

Universal Intelligence shall integrate multiple intelligence capabilities into one coherent Enterprise capability.

---

## Principle 2 – Intelligence Accessibility

Intelligence capabilities shall be available where required to support Enterprise objectives.

---

## Principle 3 – Knowledge-Centered Intelligence

Universal Intelligence shall be based on trusted, contextual and continuously evolving knowledge.

---

## Principle 4 – Collaborative Intelligence

Universal Intelligence shall support cooperation between humans, systems and intelligent entities.

---

## Principle 5 – Responsible Intelligence

Universal Intelligence shall operate according to governance, safety and ethical principles.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Intelligence Evolution

Universal Intelligence shall continuously improve through learning, feedback and adaptation.

---

## Principle 7 – Enterprise Value Creation

Universal Intelligence shall create measurable value through improved understanding, decisions and outcomes.
