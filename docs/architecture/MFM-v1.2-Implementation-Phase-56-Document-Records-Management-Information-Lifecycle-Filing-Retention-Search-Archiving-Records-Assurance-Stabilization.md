# 1. Purpose

This document defines the fifty-sixth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-55 – Grant Management, Funding Lifecycle, Eligibility, Application, Award, Compliance & Grant Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled document and records management capability covering information lifecycle, document classification, metadata, filing structures, version control, ownership, search and retrieval, retention, legal and regulatory holds, archiving, disposal, access, integrity and records assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage documents and records through a controlled information lifecycle that preserves authenticity, integrity, availability, context, accessibility, retention and defensible disposition.**
