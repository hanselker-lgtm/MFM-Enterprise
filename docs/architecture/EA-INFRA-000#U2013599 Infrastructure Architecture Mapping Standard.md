# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Infrastructure Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise infrastructure as the operational foundation for applications, data, technology and services.

The Infrastructure Architecture Framework shall provide:

- infrastructure principles
- infrastructure governance
- infrastructure structures
- infrastructure platforms
- infrastructure lifecycle management
- infrastructure operations
- infrastructure improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise infrastructure supports business capabilities, applications, information, technology platforms and operational requirements.

---

# 2. Scope

The Infrastructure Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture infrastructure domains:

- Enterprise Infrastructure Model
- Infrastructure Governance
- Infrastructure Strategy
- Infrastructure Domains
- Infrastructure Platforms
- Compute Architecture
- Storage Architecture
- Network Architecture
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Infrastructure Security Alignment
- Infrastructure Operations
- Infrastructure Monitoring
- Capacity Management
- Availability Management
- Disaster Recovery Alignment
- Infrastructure Modernization
- Infrastructure Evolution

The framework applies to:

- physical infrastructure
- virtual infrastructure
- cloud infrastructure
- computing platforms
- storage platforms
- network platforms
- operational infrastructure services
- transformation initiatives

---

# 3. Enterprise Infrastructure Architecture Principles

The Infrastructure Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Business Alignment

Infrastructure shall support Enterprise capabilities, applications, services and strategic objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Infrastructure Standardization

Infrastructure shall be managed through defined standards and approved architecture patterns.

---

## Principle 3 – Reliability by Design

Infrastructure solutions shall provide stable and dependable operation.

---

## Principle 4 – Security by Design

Infrastructure shall include security requirements throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 – Resilience by Design

Infrastructure shall support availability, recovery and continuity requirements.

---

## Principle 6 – Efficient Resource Usage

Infrastructure shall optimize performance, capacity and operational cost.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

Infrastructure shall continuously evolve according to business and technology requirements.

---

# 9. Infrastructure Domain Architecture

The Infrastructure Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise infrastructure is structured into logical domains to support governance, standardization and effective operational management.

Infrastructure domains provide a common structure for organizing Enterprise infrastructure capabilities and assets.

---

# 9.1 Infrastructure Domain Principles

Infrastructure domains shall ensure:

- clear infrastructure ownership
- logical infrastructure organization
- architecture alignment
- controlled infrastructure usage
- consistent infrastructure management
