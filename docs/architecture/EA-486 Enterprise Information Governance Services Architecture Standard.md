# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Governance Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of Enterprise information across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Governance Services shall

- provide centralized information governance
- establish information ownership
- define accountability structures
- standardize information policies
- manage information standards
- improve information decision-making
- strengthen information quality governance
- support regulatory compliance
- support business objectives
- reduce information-related risks
- support lifecycle governance
- support privacy and security requirements
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure governance traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Governance Services shall provide trusted and reusable governance capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Governance Services Principles

Enterprise Information Governance Services shall follow these principles.

## Governance by Design

Information governance capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, services, processes and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Clear Ownership and Accountability

All Enterprise information shall have clearly defined ownership, responsibility and accountability.

---

## Policy-Driven Governance

Information management activities shall be governed through approved Enterprise policies, standards and decision frameworks.

---

## Business-Aligned Governance

Information governance shall support business objectives, operational requirements, regulatory obligations and strategic priorities.

---

## Transparency and Traceability

Information governance decisions, responsibilities and controls shall be visible, documented and traceable.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Information Governance Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed management of Enterprise information governance across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating information ownership, information stewardship, information policy governance, information standards management, information accountability management, information decision governance, information quality governance, lifecycle governance integration, data governance integration, metadata governance integration, privacy governance integration, security governance integration, compliance governance integration, risk governance integration, audit governance integration, identity integration, authorization integration, event integration, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables controlled, transparent and policy-driven information governance while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Information Governance Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise information governance capabilities across the Enterprise.
