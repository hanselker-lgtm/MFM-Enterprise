# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Decision Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise decision intelligence capabilities.

The Decision Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- decision intelligence principles
- decision governance
- decision strategy
- decision capability architecture
- decision modeling architecture
- decision analytics architecture
- decision automation architecture
- decision knowledge architecture
- decision support architecture
- human decision collaboration architecture
- decision data architecture
- decision AI architecture
- decision security architecture
- decision operations
- decision performance management
- decision evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise decisions are supported by trusted data, intelligence, knowledge, analytical models and appropriate human judgement.

---

# 3. Enterprise Decision Intelligence Architecture Principles

The Decision Intelligence Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Decision Value Creation

Decision intelligence shall create measurable value by improving decision quality, speed and effectiveness.

---

## Principle 2 – Data-Driven Decisions

Decisions shall be supported by reliable, relevant and trusted information.

---

## Principle 3 – Context-Aware Decision Making

Decision systems shall consider business context, operational conditions and relevant knowledge.

---

## Principle 4 – Transparent Decisions

Decision logic, assumptions and outcomes shall be understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 5 – Human-Centered Decision Intelligence

Decision intelligence shall enhance human judgement and support responsible decision-making.

---

## Principle 6 – Secure Decision Capability

Decision systems shall protect information, models and decision processes.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Decision Improvement

Decision capabilities shall continuously improve through feedback, learning and performance measurement.
