# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Operation Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities operate, coordinate, monitor and optimise intelligent operations across enterprise environments.

The Intelligence Operation Architecture Framework shall provide:

- operation architecture principles
- operation modelling frameworks
- operational capability models
- coordination mechanisms
- monitoring frameworks
- optimisation mechanisms
- operation governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through intelligent operational management.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Operation Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Operation Model
- Operation Intelligence Architecture
- Operation Knowledge Architecture
- Operation Capability Model
- Operation Architecture Integration
- Operation Governance Model
- Operation Evolution Framework
- Operation Metrics
- Operation Maturity Model
- Operation Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

The Intelligence Operation Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

Intelligence operations shall align with enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Continuous Operational Awareness

Operations shall maintain continuous understanding of operational conditions.

---

## Principle 3 – Integrated Operations

Operations shall connect people, processes, systems and resources.

---

## Principle 4 – Adaptive Operations

Operations shall adjust according to changing environments.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Optimisation

Operations shall improve through monitoring, learning and feedback.

---

## Principle 6 – Operational Resilience

Operations shall maintain reliability and continuity.

## Principle 7 – Responsible Operations
