# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Trust Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational trust architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities are transparent, explainable, accountable and reliable.

The Intelligence Trust Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- trust operating principles
- transparency frameworks
- explainability structures
- accountability models
- provenance models
- reliability models
- ethical trust structures
- human trust models
- stakeholder confidence models
- trust monitoring structures
- trust automation structures
- trust evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for trusted, responsible and continuously improving Intelligence operations.

# 3. Intelligence Trust Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Trust Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Trust by Design

Trust shall be integrated into Intelligence capabilities from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Transparency

Intelligence operation and outcomes shall be understandable.

---

## Principle 3 – Explainability

Intelligence decisions shall support explanation where required.

---

## Principle 4 – Accountability

Responsibility for Intelligence outcomes shall be clearly defined.

---

## Principle 5 – Provenance

Intelligence information shall maintain traceable origin and history.

---

## Principle 6 – Reliability

Intelligence capability shall deliver consistent and dependable results.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Trust Evolution

Trust capability shall continuously improve.
