# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the forty-seventh document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-46 – Enterprise Data Management, Data Governance, Master Data, Data Quality, Metadata & Data Lifecycle Management.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for analytics, business intelligence, management information, data science, statistical analysis, machine learning, artificial intelligence, predictive analytics, decision intelligence, analytical models, dashboards, reporting, experimentation, model lifecycle management, AI risk management, model validation, model monitoring, explainability, human oversight, responsible AI, analytical data products, analytical platforms, analytical security, analytical privacy, analytical continuity, AI/ML suppliers, model retirement and continual analytical improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must use analytics, artificial intelligence and decision intelligence in a governed, transparent, secure and measurable manner so that analytical and AI-enabled decisions improve business outcomes while remaining explainable, accountable, proportionate and aligned with enterprise risk, data, security, privacy and ethical requirements.**

# 119. Definition of Ready

An analytics product, dashboard, model, AI system, experiment, analytical change, model release, AI deployment, validation activity, finding, exception or improvement work item is Ready when:

- The business question, use case, analytical product, model, AI system, experiment, change, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, data, criticality, risk, security, privacy, dependencies, decision impact and business requirements are known.
- Methodology, validation, testing, human oversight, deployment, monitoring, rollback, evidence and acceptance requirements are defined.
