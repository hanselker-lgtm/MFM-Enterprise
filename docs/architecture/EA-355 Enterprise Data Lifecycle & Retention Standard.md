# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to define the Enterprise Architecture governing the lifecycle, retention and disposal of Enterprise information throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Data Lifecycle Architecture shall

- establish standardized lifecycle management
- define retention requirements
- strengthen regulatory compliance
- improve information governance
- support business continuity
- reduce information risk
- remain technology independent

Enterprise information shall be governed consistently from creation through secure disposal.

# 18. Enterprise Data Lifecycle Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Undefined Retention

Enterprise information shall never exist without an approved retention policy.

Retention schedules shall always be documented.

---

## Premature Disposal

Enterprise information shall never be disposed of before retention obligations have expired.

Disposal shall require governance approval where applicable.

---

## Permanent Retention Without Justification

Enterprise information shall not be retained indefinitely without documented legal or business justification.

Retention shall remain proportional to business and regulatory requirements.

---

## Missing Legal Hold

Information subject to litigation, investigation or regulatory review shall never bypass Legal Hold procedures.

Legal Hold shall take precedence over normal disposal schedules.

---

## Uncontrolled Archiving

Enterprise information shall not be archived outside approved Enterprise archival solutions.

Archived information shall remain governed and searchable.

---

## Unverified Disposal

Enterprise information shall never be destroyed without documented authorization and audit evidence.

Secure disposal shall be verifiable and repeatable.
