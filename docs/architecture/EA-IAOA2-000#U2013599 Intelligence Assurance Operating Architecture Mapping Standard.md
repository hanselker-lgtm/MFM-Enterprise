# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational assurance architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities deliver reliable, validated, verified and trustworthy outcomes.

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- assurance operating principles
- quality management frameworks
- validation structures
- verification structures
- evidence management models
- confidence measurement models
- assessment structures
- review mechanisms
- continuous assurance models
- assurance automation structures
- assurance evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for reliable and trustworthy Intelligence operations.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Assurance Operating Model
- Intelligence Quality Management Architecture
- Intelligence Validation Architecture
- Intelligence Verification Architecture
- Intelligence Evidence Management Architecture
- Intelligence Confidence Management Architecture
- Intelligence Assessment Architecture
- Intelligence Review Architecture
- Intelligence Continuous Assurance Architecture
- Intelligence Assurance Automation Architecture
- Intelligence Assurance Evolution Architecture
- Assurance Metrics
- Assurance Maturity Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all Intelligence operational capabilities
- all Intelligence services
- all Intelligence ecosystem participants
- all future Intelligence assurance development

---

# 3. Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Quality by Design

Assurance shall be integrated from the beginning of Intelligence capability development.

---

## Principle 2 – Evidence-Based Assurance

All assurance conclusions shall be supported by evidence.

---

## Principle 3 – Continuous Validation

Intelligence capability shall be continuously validated.

---

## Principle 4 – Independent Verification

Critical Intelligence outputs shall support independent verification.

---

## Principle 5 – Confidence Transparency

Confidence levels shall be measurable and visible.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Improvement

Assurance capability shall continuously improve.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolutionary Assurance

Assurance methods shall evolve with Intelligence capability.
