# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Compliance Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise compliance as a strategic governance capability.

The Compliance Architecture Framework shall provide:

- compliance principles
- compliance governance
- compliance structures
- regulatory management
- policy management
- control management
- audit management
- compliance improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise activities, solutions and operations remain aligned with applicable laws, regulations, standards, policies and internal requirements.

---

# 2. Scope

The Compliance Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture compliance domains:

- Enterprise Compliance Model
- Compliance Governance
- Compliance Strategy
- Compliance Domains
- Regulatory Management
- Policy Management
- Control Management
- Audit Management
- Evidence Management
- Compliance Monitoring
- Compliance Reporting
- Compliance Risk Management
- Compliance Performance
- Compliance Evolution

The framework applies to:

- business compliance
- information compliance
- data compliance
- security compliance
- technology compliance
- operational compliance
- regulatory obligations
- transformation initiatives

---

# 3. Enterprise Compliance Architecture Principles

The Compliance Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Compliance as an Enterprise Capability

Compliance shall be managed as an integrated Enterprise capability supporting business objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Compliance by Design

Compliance requirements shall be incorporated into architecture, processes, solutions and operations from the beginning.

---

## Principle 3 – Risk-Based Compliance

Compliance activities shall be prioritized according to business impact, regulatory importance and risk exposure.

---

## Principle 4 – Accountability and Ownership

All compliance requirements shall have defined ownership and responsibility.

---

## Principle 5 – Evidence-Based Compliance

Compliance shall be supported by documented evidence, measurements and traceability.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Monitoring

Compliance status shall be continuously monitored and evaluated.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

Compliance capabilities shall continuously evolve according to changing requirements.

---

# 9. Compliance Domain Architecture

The Compliance Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise compliance is structured into logical domains to support governance, assurance and effective compliance management.

Compliance domains provide a common structure for organizing Enterprise obligations, requirements, controls and responsibilities.

---

# 9.1 Compliance Domain Principles

Compliance domains shall ensure:

- clear compliance ownership
- logical compliance organization
- requirement traceability
- controlled compliance management
- consistent assurance practices

---

# 9.2 Compliance Domain Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Compliance Domain | Logical grouping of compliance requirements |
| Compliance Owner | Accountability for compliance domain |
| Requirements | Defines obligations and expectations |
| Controls | Provides compliance assurance |
| Evidence | Supports compliance verification |

---

# 15. Compliance Monitoring Management

The Compliance Monitoring Management Model defines how Enterprise compliance status, obligations, controls and activities are continuously monitored.

Compliance Monitoring ensures visibility of compliance effectiveness and enables timely corrective actions.

---

# 15.1 Compliance Monitoring Principles

- continuous compliance visibility
- early identification of deviations
- effective control oversight
- transparent reporting
- continuous improvement
