# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Platform Engineering Architecture governing the design, delivery, operation and continuous evolution of the Internal Developer Platform (IDP) across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Platform Engineering Architecture shall

- standardize developer platforms
- improve Developer Experience (DevEx)
- enable self-service capabilities
- accelerate software delivery
- improve operational consistency
- strengthen governance
- improve automation
- support cloud-native operations
- reduce cognitive load for development teams
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Platform Engineering shall function as the shared engineering platform for all Enterprise development teams.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- Internal Developer Platforms (IDP)
- platform engineering teams
- self-service platforms
- platform APIs
- developer portals
- golden paths
- platform automation
- infrastructure automation
- platform services
- developer tooling

The standard applies regardless of cloud provider, infrastructure platform or development technology.

---

# 3. Enterprise Platform Engineering Principles

Enterprise Platform Engineering Architecture shall be governed by the following principles.

## Self-Service by Default

Approved platform capabilities shall be delivered through secure self-service interfaces.

---

## Developer Experience First

Platform capabilities shall optimize Developer Experience while maintaining Enterprise governance.

---

## Golden Paths

Standardized implementation paths shall be provided for common development scenarios.

---

## Platform as a Product

The Internal Developer Platform shall be managed as a product with defined owners, roadmap and lifecycle.

---

## Automation First

Platform provisioning, deployment and operations shall be automated wherever practical.

---

## Technology Independence

Platform Engineering Architecture shall remain independent of individual tooling vendors.

# 23. Enterprise Platform Engineering Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Manual Platform Provisioning

Production platform resources shall not be manually provisioned outside approved automation.

---

## Inconsistent Developer Experience

Development teams shall not receive significantly different platform capabilities without approved architectural justification.

---

## Platform Sprawl

Multiple overlapping platform capabilities providing the same function shall be avoided.

---

## Unmanaged Automation

Automation workflows shall not operate outside Enterprise governance and auditing.

---

## Undocumented Golden Paths

Approved implementation patterns shall always be documented and maintained.

---

## Missing Platform Ownership

Every Platform capability shall have documented business and technical ownership.
