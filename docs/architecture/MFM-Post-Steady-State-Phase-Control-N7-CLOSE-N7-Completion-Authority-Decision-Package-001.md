# 10. N7 Closure Conditions

The following conditions shall remain active after N7 closure:

```text
COND-N7-01
Architecture evolution shall remain evidence-based.

COND-N7-02
Target-state status shall remain distinguishable from implementation status.

COND-N7-03
Material changes shall remain subject to impact assessment.

COND-N7-04
Material decisions shall retain authority and rationale traceability.

COND-N7-05
Exceptions shall retain owner, authority, risk and lifecycle information.

COND-N7-06
Material dependencies shall retain ownership and status.

COND-N7-07
Operational feedback shall not automatically become architecture change.

COND-N7-08
Continuous improvement shall remain controlled and shall not create automatic scope expansion.

COND-N7-09
Architecture, implementation, operation and effectiveness shall remain distinct claims.

COND-N7-10
Future material changes shall be assessed for impact on the established N7 governance baseline.
```

These conditions are carry-forward controls and do not invalidate N7 closure.
