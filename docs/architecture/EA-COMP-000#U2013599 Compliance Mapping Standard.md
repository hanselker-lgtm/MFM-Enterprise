# 9. Policy Management

The Policy Management Model defines how Enterprise policies are created, approved, maintained and enforced.

Policies establish the mandatory direction and requirements for Enterprise behavior.

---

Enterprise policies shall ensure:

- clear requirements
- defined ownership
- consistent application
- traceability to obligations
- continuous review

# 21. Compliance Monitoring Management

The Compliance Monitoring Management Model defines how Enterprise compliance status is continuously monitored, measured and improved.

Compliance Monitoring ensures ongoing visibility of compliance conditions, control effectiveness and improvement requirements.

---

# 21.1 Compliance Monitoring Principles

Compliance monitoring shall ensure:

- continuous visibility
- timely detection of deviations
- measurable compliance status
- evidence-based reporting
- continuous improvement

---

| Area | Purpose |

| Control Monitoring | Validate control effectiveness |
| Policy Monitoring | Verify policy adoption |
| Standard Monitoring | Verify standard compliance |
| Risk Monitoring | Track compliance-related risks |
| Audit Monitoring | Follow audit findings |
