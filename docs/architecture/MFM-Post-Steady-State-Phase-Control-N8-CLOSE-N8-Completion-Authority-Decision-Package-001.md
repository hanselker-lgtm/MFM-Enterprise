# 1. Purpose

N8-CLOSE provides the formal completion authority package for N8.

It consolidates the controlled completion assessment from:

```text
N8-A
Strategy, Outcome & Capability Alignment

N8-B
Architecture Roadmap & Transition Portfolio Governance

N8-C
Investment, Prioritization & Portfolio Traceability

N8-D
Value, Outcome & Realization Governance

N8-E
Evidence, Validation & Completion Assessment
```

N8-CLOSE does not automatically close N8.

A separate explicit completion authority decision is required.

---

# 2. Closure Boundary

The controlled distinction is:

```text
N8-E COMPLETE
≠
N8-CLOSE APPROVED

N8-CLOSE APPROVED
≠
N8 AUTOMATICALLY CERTIFIED

N8 CLOSED
≠
ALL FUTURE CONDITIONS ELIMINATED
```

Closure means the authorized N8 workstream has completed its defined scope and has passed the required completion decision.

It does not retroactively certify every business, financial, operational, compliance or value claim.

# 7. Recommended Decision Framework

The authority should consider:

```text
1.
Was the authorized N8 scope completed?

2.
Were N8-A through N8-E completed?

3.
Are material findings resolved, accepted or formally carried forward?

4.
Are N5/N6/N7 conditions preserved?

5.
Is traceability sufficiently established?

6.
Are material claims supported by appropriate evidence?

7.
Are unresolved issues explicitly recorded?

8.
Has N8 remained within authorized scope?

9.
Are any remaining issues appropriate for post-N8 governance?

10.
Is closure preferable to continued N8 execution?
```

# 16. N8 Closure Effects

If N8 is approved for closure:

```text
N8 execution authority terminates.
N8 work-package execution status becomes CLOSED.
N8 conditions remain active where explicitly carried forward.
N8 evidence remains part of the controlled architecture record.
N8 decisions remain part of the decision record.
N8 findings remain traceable.
```

Closure does not delete historical evidence.
