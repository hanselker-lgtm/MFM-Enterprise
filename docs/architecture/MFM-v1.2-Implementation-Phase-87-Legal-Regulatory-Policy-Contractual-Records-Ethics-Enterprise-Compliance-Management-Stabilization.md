# 1. Purpose

This document defines the eighty-seventh implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-86 – Communications, Marketing, Outreach, Public Information, Brand Governance, Campaign Management & Communication Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise compliance capability covering legal governance, regulatory obligations, policy governance, contractual obligations, compliance registers, legal matter management, policy lifecycle, regulatory change monitoring, compliance assessments, compliance controls, ethics governance, conflicts of interest, protected reporting, records and evidence, compliance monitoring, compliance findings, remediation and compliance assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that legal, regulatory, contractual, policy, ethical and records obligations are identified, owned, interpreted, implemented, monitored and evidenced so that the organization can operate lawfully, responsibly and with demonstrable compliance.**

# 140. Final Compliance Principle

> **Compliance must be managed as an end-to-end lifecycle from obligation identification through implementation, evidence, monitoring, remediation and assurance.**

---

# 141. Final Obligation Principle

> **Every material obligation must have sufficient applicability analysis, ownership, control and evidence.**

---

# 142. Final Regulatory Principle

> **Regulatory change must be identified early enough to assess impact and implement required changes before applicable deadlines.**

---

# 143. Final Policy Principle

> **Policies must have clear ownership, approval, publication, implementation, review and retirement arrangements.**

---

# 144. Final Control Principle

> **Compliance controls must be proportionate to the obligation and risk and must produce sufficient evidence to demonstrate operation.**

---

# 145. Final Evidence Principle

> **Compliance without reliable evidence cannot provide sufficient assurance of sustained control effectiveness.**

---

# 146. Final Ethics Principle

> **Ethical governance must support integrity, transparency, fairness and responsible decision-making beyond minimum legal compliance.**

---

# 147. Final Conflict Principle

> **Material conflicts of interest must be disclosed, assessed and appropriately mitigated before affected decisions are made.**

---

# 148. Final Protected Reporting Principle

> **Serious concerns must have an appropriately protected reporting path that supports confidentiality, impartial assessment and protection against retaliation, subject to applicable requirements.**

---

# 149. Final Records Principle

> **Records and evidence required to demonstrate legal, regulatory, contractual or policy compliance must be preserved, protected and retrievable for the required period.**

---

# 150. Final Remediation Principle

> **Compliance findings must address root causes and must not be closed solely because an action was planned or performed without evidence of effectiveness.**

---

# 151. Final Assurance Principle

> **Compliance assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that material obligations are understood, controlled, monitored and demonstrably satisfied.**

---

# 152. Final Integration Principle

> **Enterprise Compliance Management must integrate with Legal, Risk, Security, Privacy, Finance, HR, Procurement, Contract, Records, Service, Process, Change and Enterprise Assurance governance.**

---

# 153. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage compliance through a controlled lifecycle connecting legal and regulatory obligations, policies, contracts, controls, evidence, assessments, ethics, protected reporting, records, findings, remediation and continuous assurance.**
