# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Rules Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Decision Services principles are inherited from EA-463.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Rules Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Rules Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, reusable and governed business rule capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Rules Services shall

- provide centralized rule management
- standardize business rule execution
- support reusable rule libraries
- improve decision consistency
- strengthen governance
- enable automation
- support interoperability
- improve operational efficiency
- reduce implementation complexity
- support continuous optimization

Enterprise Rules Services shall provide trusted and reusable business rule capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- business rule services
- rule repositories
- rule authoring services
- rule validation services
- rule execution services
- rule distribution services
- rule lifecycle management
- cloud services
- hybrid environments
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Rules Services Principles

Enterprise Rules Services shall follow these principles.

## Centralized Rule Governance

Business rules shall be centrally governed, version controlled and managed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Secure by Default

Every business rule shall be protected through authentication, authorization, auditing and continuous monitoring.

---

## Reusable Rule Services

Business rules shall be reusable across workflows, business processes, decision services, APIs and Enterprise applications.

---

## Metadata Driven Rules

Business rules shall be described, classified and governed using Enterprise metadata.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Rules Services shall remain independent of vendors, rule engines, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Rules Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed management of business rules across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating rule authoring, validation, repositories, version management, execution, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables consistent, transparent and reusable business rule execution while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Rules Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise rule capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Rules Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted rule authoring, autonomous rule optimization, semantic-aware rule discovery, adaptive policy enforcement, self-validating rule repositories, decentralized rule ecosystems, intelligent compliance verification and quantum-safe Enterprise rule infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Rules Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven rule capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
