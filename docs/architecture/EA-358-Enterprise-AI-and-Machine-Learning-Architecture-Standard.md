# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise AI & Machine Learning Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Infrastructure Layer principles are inherited from EA-320.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-357.

All Enterprise AI & Machine Learning implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Architecture governing Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and intelligent decision support across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise AI Architecture shall

- provide standardized AI capabilities
- support trustworthy automation
- enable responsible AI adoption
- improve business decision-making
- enable predictive and prescriptive intelligence
- strengthen governance
- remain technology independent

Artificial Intelligence shall augment human decision-making while preserving accountability, transparency and Enterprise Governance.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to all Enterprise AI and Machine Learning capabilities.

The scope includes

- machine learning
- generative AI
- predictive analytics
- prescriptive analytics
- large language models
- intelligent agents
- recommendation engines
- computer vision
- natural language processing
- decision intelligence
- AI governance
- MLOps

The standard applies regardless of AI platform, cloud provider or model technology.

# 3. Enterprise AI Definition

Enterprise Artificial Intelligence is the controlled application of intelligent computational capabilities that assist, augment or automate business processes while operating within Enterprise Governance.

Enterprise AI encompasses

- machine learning
- deep learning
- generative AI
- natural language understanding
- intelligent assistants
- intelligent automation
- predictive modeling
- optimization
- anomaly detection
- decision intelligence

Enterprise AI shall always operate on governed Enterprise information and governed analytical models.

# 18. Enterprise AI Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Unapproved AI Models

Production AI models shall never be deployed without formal governance approval.

---

## Unmanaged Training Data

AI models shall never be trained using undocumented or ungoverned datasets.

Training data shall remain traceable and governed.

---

## Black Box Decision Making

High-impact business decisions shall not rely solely upon AI models that cannot provide an appropriate level of explanation.

Human accountability shall always be preserved.

---

## Shadow AI

Business units shall not deploy unmanaged AI services outside Enterprise Governance.

All Enterprise AI capabilities shall be centrally governed.

---

## Vendor Lock-In

Enterprise AI Architecture shall remain independent of individual AI vendors, model providers or cloud platforms.

Architectural portability shall always be preserved.

---

## Missing Monitoring

Production AI shall never operate without continuous monitoring of performance, quality, security and governance.
