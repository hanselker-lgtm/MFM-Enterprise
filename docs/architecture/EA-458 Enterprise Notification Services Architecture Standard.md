# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Notification Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Messaging Architecture principles are inherited from EA-342.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Notification Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Notification Services Architecture supporting secure, scalable and policy-governed notification capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Notification Services shall

- provide standardized notification services
- support multi-channel communication
- enable event-driven notifications
- improve interoperability
- strengthen scalability
- ensure reliable notification delivery
- support reusable notification capabilities
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- enable continuous optimization

Enterprise Notification Services shall provide trusted and reusable notification capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- notification publishers
- notification consumers
- notification gateways
- notification brokers
- messaging platforms
- email services
- SMS services
- push notification services
- collaboration platforms
- external notification providers

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, notification channel or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Notification Services Principles

Enterprise Notification Services shall follow these principles.

## Notification First

Enterprise notifications shall be delivered using standardized notification services supporting consistent communication across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Secure by Default

Every notification shall be authenticated, authorized, encrypted where applicable and continuously monitored.

---

## Standardized Notification Services

Enterprise Notification Services shall expose standardized interfaces supporting interoperability across the Enterprise.

---

## Metadata Driven Notifications

Notification services shall leverage Enterprise metadata for routing, prioritization, personalization, lifecycle management and governance.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Notification Services shall remain independent of vendors, communication providers, notification platforms and infrastructure technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Notification Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed notification capabilities across all Business and Technology Domains.
