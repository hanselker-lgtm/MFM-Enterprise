# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, architectural models, governance mechanisms, and intelligence capabilities required for an **Omniscient Enterprise Architecture**.

Previous standards progressively enabled Enterprise Architecture to:

- Observe
- Learn
- Self-organize
- Federate
- Collaborate
- Emerge
- Evolve
- Adapt
- Become Collectively Conscious
- Improve its own evolution
- Become Universally Integrated
- Transcend organizational limitations
- Evaluate multiple enterprise realities
- Maintain a persistent Enterprise Consciousness

EA-IOEA56 introduces the next capability:

**Omniscient Enterprise Intelligence.**

Omniscient Enterprise Intelligence is the governed capability of the enterprise to continuously discover, correlate, understand, validate, and utilize all relevant enterprise knowledge, context, experience, and intelligence required for optimal decision-making.

The objective is no longer only maintaining Enterprise Consciousness.

The objective is maintaining Enterprise Consciousness with continuously expanding Enterprise Knowledge and Enterprise Wisdom.
