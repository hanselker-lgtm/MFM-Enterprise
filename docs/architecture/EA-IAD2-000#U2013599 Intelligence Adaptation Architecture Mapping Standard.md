# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities dynamically adjust, transform and optimise themselves according to changing environments, experience, feedback, learning and strategic requirements.

The Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Framework shall provide:

- adaptation architecture principles
- adaptive intelligence frameworks
- context adaptation models
- behaviour adaptation models
- capability adaptation models
- adaptation integration structures
- adaptation governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through adaptive and continuously evolving ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Adaptation Model
- Adaptive Intelligence Architecture
- Context Adaptation Architecture
- Behaviour Adaptation Architecture
- Capability Adaptation Model
- Adaptation Architecture Integration
- Adaptation Governance Model
- Adaptation Evolution Framework
- Adaptation Metrics
- Adaptation Maturity Model
- Adaptation Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Dynamic Adaptation

Intelligence capabilities shall adjust according to changing conditions and requirements.

---

## Principle 2 – Context Awareness

Adaptation shall be based on understanding of environmental and operational context.

---

## Principle 3 – Learning-Based Adaptation

Adaptation shall use experience, feedback and knowledge.

---

## Principle 4 – Controlled Evolution

Adaptation shall occur through governed and measurable processes.

---

## Principle 5 – Capability Optimisation

Adaptation shall improve performance, effectiveness and resilience.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Adaptation

Intelligence capability shall continuously adjust and evolve.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Adaptation Governance

Adaptation shall maintain transparency, accountability and ethical alignment.

---
