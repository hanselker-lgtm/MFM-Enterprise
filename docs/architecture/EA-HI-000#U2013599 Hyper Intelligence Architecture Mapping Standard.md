# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Hyper Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a conceptual architecture model for future large-scale interconnected intelligence capabilities.

The Hyper Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- hyper intelligence architecture principles
- hyper intelligence governance
- hyper intelligence strategy
- hyper intelligence capability architecture
- hyper reasoning architecture
- hyper learning architecture
- hyper knowledge architecture
- hyper intelligence federation
- hyper intelligence networks
- hyper intelligence ecosystem architecture
- hyper human intelligence collaboration
- hyper safety architecture
- hyper alignment architecture
- hyper operations
- hyper performance management
- hyper evolution

The framework ensures that future advanced intelligence capabilities are designed, governed and operated as responsible interconnected intelligence ecosystems.
