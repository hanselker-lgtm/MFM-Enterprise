# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eighteenth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-17 – Enterprise Procurement, Supplier, Contract, Vendor Risk, Third-Party Governance & Commercial Operations.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for people, workforce, organization, roles, responsibilities, competence, learning, performance, workforce planning, employee lifecycle and people operations.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain an appropriately structured, competent, supported and accountable workforce whose roles, responsibilities, capabilities and performance are aligned with organizational objectives and operational requirements.**

# 136. Definition of Ready

A people or workforce work item is Ready when:

- The role, employee lifecycle event, workforce requirement, competence requirement, training, performance activity, organizational change, finding or assurance activity is clearly identified.
- Ownership, authority, workforce impact, privacy, security and applicable requirements are known.
- Acceptance, evidence, communication and control requirements are defined.

---

# 137. Definition of Done

A people or workforce work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement Identified
        ↓
Owner Assigned
        ↓
People / Workforce Action Completed
        ↓
Role / Competence / Access / Policy Requirements Validated
        ↓
Records / Data / Training Updated Where Required
        ↓
Evidence Captured
        ↓
Exceptions / Findings Addressed
        ↓
Outcome Accepted
```
