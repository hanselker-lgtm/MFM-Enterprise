## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory substantive validation layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-280, determining whether the procedural verification of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-279 is itself substantively true, effective, complete and supportable in the actual current operating state.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-279 verifies that RG-278 validation was correctly performed and implemented. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-280 validates whether the EA-IMETA-PC-RG-279 verification remains substantively effective and provides reliable assurance.

```text
RG-278 → VALIDATE
RG-279 → VERIFY
RG-280 → VALIDATE
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-279 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-280 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
IS THE EA-IMETA-PC-RG-279 VERIFICATION ACTUALLY EFFECTIVE AND TRUE?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-279 verification SHALL NOT automatically establish substantive current effectiveness.
