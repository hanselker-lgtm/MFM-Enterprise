## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory measurement-and-observation layer that continuously or periodically captures the actual post-closure state against the accepted baseline, preserving enough quality, context and traceability to support reliable comparison and regression detection.

## Core Principle
Observation is not merely data collection. A governed post-closure observation shall be relevant to the baseline, sufficiently accurate, timely, contextualized and traceable so that later deviation and regression decisions are defensible.

```text
POST-CLOSURE BASELINE ACTIVE
      ↓
DEFINE MEASURES + OBSERVATION OBJECTIVES
      ↓
COLLECT / OBSERVE CURRENT STATE
      ↓
VALIDATE OBSERVATION QUALITY
      ↓
CONTEXTUALIZE + RECORD
      ↓
COMPARE WITH BASELINE
      ↓
DEVIATION / REGRESSION ANALYSIS
```

## Measurement Observation Quality Test
```text
DEFINED MEASURE
+
RELEVANT OBSERVATION
+
VALID SOURCE
+
TIMELY CAPTURE
+
KNOWN CONTEXT
+
QUALITY CHECK
+
TRACEABLE RECORD
+
BASELINE LINK
=
VALID GOVERNED POST-CLOSURE OBSERVATION
```

## Measurement vs Observation vs Comparison
```text
MEASUREMENT
→ QUANTIFIED OR QUALIFIED REPRESENTATION OF A STATE / PROPERTY

OBSERVATION
→ CAPTURED EVIDENCE ABOUT CURRENT CONDITION

COMPARISON
→ EVALUATION OF CURRENT OBSERVATION AGAINST REFERENCE STATE
```

## Observation State Model
```text
SCHEDULED
COLLECTING
CAPTURED
VALIDATING
VALID
INVALID
MISSING
DELAYED
ANOMALOUS
CONTEXTUALIZED
PUBLISHED / AVAILABLE
```

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Objective

Provide reliable current-state evidence that allows meaningful comparison against the accepted post-closure baseline and timely identification of material deviation.

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Definition

Measurement and observation are governed activities for capturing current state, properties, events, conditions and evidence required for post-closure monitoring.

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Scope

Scope shall identify systems, services, users, data, decisions, dependencies, environments, consumers, measures and observation boundaries.

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Authority

Authority shall define who owns measurement definitions, observation sources, validation, exceptions, sampling changes and data-quality decisions.

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Criteria

Criteria shall define expected frequency, accuracy, completeness, timeliness, coverage, source quality and contextual requirements.

```text
OBSERVATION
↓
SOURCE VALID?
├── NO → INVALID / INVESTIGATE
└── YES
     ↓
TIMELY + COMPLETE ENOUGH?
├── NO → DELAYED / MISSING / QUALIFY
└── YES
     ↓
CONTEXT SUFFICIENT?
├── NO → CONTEXTUALIZE
└── YES → VALID OBSERVATION
```

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Preconditions

Preconditions include active baseline, defined measures, observation sources, collection method, frequency, quality criteria and storage/traceability mechanisms.

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Evidence

Evidence shall preserve raw or authoritative observation, source identity, collection time, processing steps, quality status, context and linkage to the baseline.

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Method

Methods may include automated telemetry, controlled sampling, inspection, testing, logs, event records, surveys, operational measures and human observation.


Decision shall explicitly classify observations as valid, invalid, missing, delayed, anomalous or otherwise qualified.

```text
OBSERVATION
├── VALID → USE
├── QUALIFIED → USE WITH CONTEXT
├── INVALID → EXCLUDE / INVESTIGATE
└── MISSING → DETECT GAP / COMPENSATE
```

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Accountability

Accountability shall remain explicit for measure definitions, observation quality, source integrity, collection and exception handling.

## Post-Closure Measurement Observation Timing

Observation frequency shall be proportionate to materiality, volatility, time-to-impact and required regression-detection sensitivity.

## Security Post-Closure Measurement Observation

Security observation shall monitor material access, exposure, control state, configuration, detection and threat-related conditions.

## Resilience Post-Closure Measurement Observation

Resilience observation shall monitor availability, recovery, capacity, continuity, dependency and stability conditions.

## Compliance Post-Closure Measurement Observation

Compliance observation shall monitor required controls, obligations, evidence, reporting status and relevant exceptions.

## Data Post-Closure Measurement Observation

## Measurement Definition Integrity
Every material measure shall have stable semantics including what is measured, unit or scale, source, frequency, interpretation and limitations.

## Observation Context
Context may include operating mode, workload, environment, dependency state, configuration, user population, threat conditions, maintenance or other factors that materially affect interpretation.

## Observation Gaps
Missing or delayed observations shall be observable as conditions themselves. A monitoring system shall not silently treat absence of data as evidence of normality.

```text
NO DATA
≠
NO DEVIATION
```

## Source Integrity
Observation source identity and trustworthiness shall be maintained. Changes to collection systems shall be governed because they can alter apparent state without changing the underlying condition.

## Sampling
Sampling shall be representative for the intended purpose. Sampling changes shall be documented where they could affect comparison or regression detection.

## Outliers
Outliers shall not be automatically discarded. They shall be investigated, contextualized or governed according to explicit rules.

## Measurement Drift
Changes in instrumentation, definitions, collection logic or processing can create apparent deviation. Such drift shall be detected and governed.

## Observation History
Historical observations shall remain available for trend analysis, retrospective investigation and reconstruction of regression events.

## AI and Agent Observation Integrity
Where AI/agent behaviour is material, observation shall capture sufficient context to distinguish model/configuration change, policy change, tool change, data change and behavioural change.

## Observation Anti-Gaming
Measurement and observation shall not be selectively suppressed, delayed, filtered or redefined to reduce apparent deviations or avoid regression detection.
