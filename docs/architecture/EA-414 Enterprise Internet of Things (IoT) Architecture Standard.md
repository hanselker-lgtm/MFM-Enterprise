# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Internet of Things (IoT) Architecture governing connected devices, sensors, edge computing, telemetry collection, device lifecycle management and secure integration across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Internet of Things (IoT) Architecture shall

- establish standardized IoT architecture
- support secure device connectivity
- enable real-time telemetry
- improve operational visibility
- integrate with Enterprise Digital Twins
- support predictive analytics
- enable intelligent automation
- strengthen operational resilience
- support scalable deployments
- remain technology independent

Enterprise IoT shall function as a reusable Enterprise capability supporting intelligent operations and digital transformation.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- connected devices
- sensors
- actuators
- industrial IoT
- edge computing
- telemetry collection
- gateways
- fleet management
- firmware management
- device lifecycle management

The standard applies regardless of communication technology, infrastructure platform or cloud provider.

# 3. Enterprise IoT Principles

Enterprise Internet of Things Architecture shall be governed by the following principles.

## Secure by Design

All IoT solutions shall implement Enterprise Security Architecture from the beginning.

---

## Standardized Connectivity

IoT devices shall communicate through standardized Enterprise integration mechanisms.

---

## Event-Driven Communication

IoT devices shall exchange operational information using Enterprise Event Architecture wherever practical.

---

## Device Lifecycle Governance

IoT devices shall be governed throughout their complete operational lifecycle.

---

## Edge Intelligence

Edge processing shall be used where operational efficiency, latency or resilience requires local decision-making.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Internet of Things Architecture shall remain independent of specific IoT hardware vendors, cloud platforms and communication technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Internet of Things Architecture establishes the standardized foundation for connecting, managing and securing Enterprise devices, sensors, gateways and edge computing environments across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

By integrating Enterprise Security, Event Architecture, Digital Twins, Artificial Intelligence, Observability, Automation and Analytics, the Enterprise enables intelligent, scalable and resilient IoT capabilities that improve operational awareness, predictive maintenance, asset management and business decision-making.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Internet of Things initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing IoT implementations across the Enterprise.
