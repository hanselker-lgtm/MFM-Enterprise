# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Secrets Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of secrets across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Secrets Management Services shall

- provide centralized secrets management
- standardize secret lifecycle management
- strengthen application security
- reduce secret exposure risks
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure secret traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Secrets Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable secrets management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Secrets Management Services Principles

Enterprise Secrets Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Secrets Security by Design

Secrets management capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every secret shall be securely created, stored, accessed, monitored and retired according to approved Enterprise security requirements.

---

## Secret Least Privilege

Secrets shall only be accessible by approved identities, applications, services and workloads requiring authorized usage.

---

## Secret Lifecycle Governance

Secrets shall be continuously managed throughout creation, activation, usage, rotation, suspension and retirement.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Secrets Management Services shall remain independent of vendors, secrets platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.
