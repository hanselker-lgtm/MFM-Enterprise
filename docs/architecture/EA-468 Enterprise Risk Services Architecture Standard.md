# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Risk Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-467.

Enterprise Compliance Services principles are inherited from EA-466.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Risk Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Risk Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and proactive risk management across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Risk Services shall

- provide centralized risk management
- standardize enterprise risk processes
- support enterprise-wide risk identification
- improve operational resilience
- strengthen governance
- enable enterprise automation
- support interoperability
- improve organizational transparency
- reduce enterprise risk exposure
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Risk Services shall provide trusted and reusable risk management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise risk services
- risk management
- risk identification
- risk assessment
- risk analysis
- risk monitoring
- risk mitigation
- risk registers
- risk lifecycle management
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Risk Services Principles

Enterprise Risk Services shall follow these principles.

## Risk by Design

Risk identification, assessment and mitigation shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, business processes, technology platforms and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every risk management activity shall be authenticated, authorized, audited and continuously monitored.

---

## Continuous Risk Management

Enterprise risks shall be continuously identified, evaluated, monitored and managed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Metadata Driven Risk Management

Enterprise risks shall be described, classified and governed using Enterprise metadata to ensure traceability, discoverability and consistent reporting.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Risk Services shall remain independent of vendors, risk management platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Risk Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed Enterprise risk management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating risk management, risk identification, assessment, analysis, monitoring, mitigation, governance, compliance, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables proactive, transparent and scalable management of Enterprise risks while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Risk Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise risk capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Risk Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted risk identification, autonomous risk assessment, predictive risk analytics, semantic-aware risk intelligence, adaptive mitigation planning, self-optimizing governance, digital twin risk simulation and quantum-safe Enterprise risk management infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Risk Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven risk capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
