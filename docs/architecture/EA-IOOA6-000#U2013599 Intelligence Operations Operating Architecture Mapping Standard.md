# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Operations Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities can be planned, delivered, operated, monitored, maintained and continuously improved.

The Intelligence Operations Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- operations operating principles
- operations governance frameworks
- operations lifecycle models

- data operations models
- model operations models

- incident operations models

- performance operations models
- monitoring operations models
- automation operations models
- improvement operations models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for stable, controlled and continuously improving Intelligence operations.
