# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the seventieth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-69 – Enterprise IT Financial Management, Technology Cost Management, Budgeting, FinOps, Licensing, Procurement & Asset Economics.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for supplier management, vendor management, third-party management, contract management, outsourcing, managed services, supplier selection, supplier onboarding, supplier risk, third-party cybersecurity, data protection, supplier due diligence, commercial governance, contract performance, service levels, supplier performance, supplier reporting, supplier incidents, supplier problems, supplier changes, supplier continuity, supplier resilience, supplier exit, transition, concentration risk, fourth-party risk, supplier financial risk, supplier compliance, supplier assurance, supplier findings, supplier exceptions, supplier remediation, supplier metrics, supplier dashboards, supplier maturity and continual supplier improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must govern suppliers and third parties throughout their lifecycle so that externally provided products, services, technology and capabilities remain aligned with business requirements, contractual obligations, security, resilience, financial expectations and acceptable risk.**

# 3. Supplier Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure suppliers deliver agreed products and services at the required quality, cost, security, resilience and performance levels.**

# 4. Vendor Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain effective relationships with vendors while protecting MFM's commercial, operational, security and strategic interests.**

# 5. Contract Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure contractual obligations, rights, deliverables, pricing, service levels, controls and termination provisions are actively governed throughout the contract lifecycle.**

# 6. Third-Party Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and manage risks arising from dependence on external organizations and services.**

# 7. Outsourcing Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure outsourced services remain controlled, measurable, resilient and aligned with MFM requirements.**

# 8. Supplier Performance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Measure supplier performance objectively and drive timely corrective action and continual improvement.**

# 93. Definition of Ready

A supplier, contract, third-party relationship, outsourcing arrangement, renewal, supplier change, performance issue, security finding, continuity plan, exit, exception or remediation item is Ready when purpose, owner, scope, criticality, supplier dependency, contractual requirements, risk, security, financial implications, service requirements, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.

> **Suppliers with access to MFM systems, services or data must meet applicable security requirements proportionate to their risk.**

# 100. Final Continuity Principle

> **Critical supplier dependencies must have proportionate resilience, continuity and exit arrangements.**

> **Supplier economics must remain transparent, controlled and aligned with contracted value and business requirements.**
