# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the nineteenth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-18 – Enterprise Human Resources, Workforce, Organization, Roles, Competence, Learning, Performance & People Operations.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for service management and service operations, covering service definition, service ownership, service catalogue, service levels, service requests, incidents, problems, changes, configuration, service availability, capacity, continuity, monitoring, operational procedures, service reporting and continual service improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM services must be clearly defined, owned, supported, measured, controlled and continuously improved so that users and business processes receive predictable, reliable and fit-for-purpose services.**

# 135. Definition of Ready

A service-management work item is Ready when:

- The service, incident, request, problem, change, release, configuration item, service level, operational procedure or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, impact, criticality and dependencies are known.
- Support, authorization, evidence, communication, recovery and acceptance requirements are defined.
