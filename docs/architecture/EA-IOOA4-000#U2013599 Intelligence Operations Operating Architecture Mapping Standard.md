# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Operations Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities are planned, coordinated, executed, monitored and continuously improved.

The Intelligence Operations Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- operations operating principles
- operational planning frameworks
- process management structures
- workflow models
- service operation models
- resource operation structures
- performance operation models
- operational control models
- monitoring structures
- automation models
- evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for efficient, controlled and continuously improving Intelligence operations.

# 3. Intelligence Operations Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Operations Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Operations by Design

Operational capability shall be integrated into Intelligence design from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Operational Effectiveness

Operations shall deliver expected Intelligence outcomes efficiently.

---

## Principle 3 – Process Discipline

Operations shall follow defined and controlled processes.

---

## Principle 4 – Resource Optimisation

Operational resources shall be managed effectively.

---

## Principle 5 – Operational Visibility

Operational activities shall be measurable and transparent.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Improvement

Operations shall continuously improve through learning and optimisation.

---

## Principle 7 – Operational Evolution

Operations shall adapt to changing requirements and environments.
