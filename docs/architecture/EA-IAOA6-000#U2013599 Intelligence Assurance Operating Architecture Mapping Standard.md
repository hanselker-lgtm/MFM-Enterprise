# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational assurance architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities continuously meet defined requirements for quality, correctness, security, compliance, reliability and performance.

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- assurance operating principles
- assurance governance frameworks
- assurance lifecycle structures
- assurance management models
- quality assurance models
- compliance assurance models
- performance assurance models
- data assurance models
- model assurance models
- validation assurance models
- assurance security models
- assurance monitoring structures
- assurance automation models
- assurance evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for assured, controlled and continuously improving Intelligence capabilities.

# 3. Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Assurance by Design

Assurance capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Quality Assurance

Intelligence capability shall meet defined quality requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Evidence-Based Assurance

Assurance decisions shall be based on measurable evidence.

---

## Principle 4 – Continuous Verification

Intelligence capability shall be continuously verified.

---

## Principle 5 – Compliance Assurance

Intelligence capability shall operate according to defined policies, standards and requirements.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Assurance Improvement

Assurance capability shall continuously improve through measurement and feedback.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolution Through Assurance

Assurance shall support continuous Intelligence evolution.
