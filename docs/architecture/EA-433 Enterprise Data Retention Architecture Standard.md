# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Retention Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Backup & Recovery Architecture principles are inherited from EA-432.

All Enterprise Data Retention implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Retention Architecture governing the controlled retention, preservation and timely disposal of Enterprise information throughout its lifecycle while ensuring regulatory compliance, operational efficiency and information governance.

Enterprise Data Retention shall

- ensure regulatory compliance
- support business requirements
- preserve critical information
- control retention periods
- reduce information risk
- optimize storage utilization
- strengthen governance
- support secure disposal
- enable automation
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Retention shall provide a standardized architectural framework for managing Enterprise information throughout its retention lifecycle.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- structured information
- unstructured information
- application data
- database records
- documents
- electronic communications
- cloud repositories
- archive repositories
- backup repositories
- enterprise information stores

The standard applies regardless of technology platform, storage medium, cloud provider or deployment model.

# 3. Enterprise Data Retention Principles

Enterprise Data Retention shall be governed by the following principles.

## Lifecycle by Design

Enterprise information shall be managed according to approved lifecycle and retention requirements.

---

## Compliance by Design

Retention shall satisfy legal, regulatory, contractual and business obligations.

---

## Secure Retention

Retained information shall remain protected against unauthorized access, modification and destruction.

---

## Metadata-Driven Retention

Retention management shall be governed through Enterprise metadata and standardized policies.

---

## Reusable Retention Services

Retention capabilities shall be reusable across Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Retention shall remain independent of storage technologies, retention platforms and infrastructure providers.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Retention Architecture establishes the standardized framework for controlled retention, preservation and secure disposal of Enterprise information throughout its lifecycle.

By integrating Enterprise Data Governance, Information Lifecycle Management, Metadata Management, Security, Observability, Automation and Compliance capabilities, the Enterprise ensures trusted retention services that satisfy legal obligations, reduce information risk, optimize storage utilization and support business continuity across all Business and Technology Domains.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Retention initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing Enterprise retention capabilities.
