# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Learning Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational learning architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities continuously learn, improve, adapt and evolve.

The Intelligence Learning Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- learning operating principles
- learning governance frameworks
- learning lifecycle structures
- learning management models
- learning experience structures
- knowledge transfer models
- learning assessment models
- learning security models
- learning monitoring structures
- learning automation models
- learning evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for adaptive, learning-driven and continuously improving Intelligence capabilities.

# 3. Intelligence Learning Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Learning Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Learning by Design

Learning capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Continuous Learning

Intelligence capability shall continuously improve through experience and feedback.

---

## Principle 3 – Knowledge-Driven Learning

Learning shall build upon validated knowledge and operational experience.

---

## Principle 4 – Learning Transparency

Learning processes and outcomes shall be measurable and understandable.

---

## Principle 5 – Adaptive Learning

Learning capability shall adjust to changing environments and requirements.

---

## Principle 6 – Learning Improvement

Learning capability shall continuously improve through evaluation and feedback.

---

## Principle 7 – Learning Evolution

Learning structures shall develop according to future needs.
