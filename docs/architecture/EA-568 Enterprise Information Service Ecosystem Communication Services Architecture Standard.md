# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Communication Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Communication Services shall follow these principles.

## Communication by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include communication considerations from initial design through operational lifecycle.

---

## Controlled Communication

Communication shall only occur through approved governance, security and communication mechanisms.

---

## Reliable Information Exchange

Communication capabilities shall ensure reliable, accurate and timely exchange of information.

---

## Communication Ownership

Every communication capability shall have defined ownership, responsibility and accountability.

---

## Communication Lifecycle Management

Communication channels shall be established, maintained, monitored, evolved and retired through controlled lifecycle processes.

---

## Governed Communication Management

Communication activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.

---

## Continuous Communication Improvement

Communication capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
