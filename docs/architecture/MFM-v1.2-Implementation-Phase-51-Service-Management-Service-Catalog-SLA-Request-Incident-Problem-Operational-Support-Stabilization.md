# 1. Purpose

This document defines the fifty-first implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-50 – Business Process Management, Process Automation, Case Management & Operational Workflow Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise service management capability covering service ownership, service catalog, service design, service levels, SLA / OLA governance, service requests, incident management, major incident management, problem management, knowledge management, operational support and service performance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must operate services through clearly defined ownership, service expectations, controlled requests, effective incident and problem management, usable knowledge, measurable performance and continuous operational improvement.**
