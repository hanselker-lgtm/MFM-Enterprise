# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Governance Framework is to establish the governing principles, structures, responsibilities and controls required to manage the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture environment.

The Governance Framework shall provide:

- governance principles
- governance structures
- decision authority
- ownership models
- compliance management
- risk management
- change governance
- architecture assurance
- continuous governance improvement

The framework establishes the foundation for controlled Enterprise Architecture evolution.

---

# 2. Scope

The Governance Framework applies to all MFM Enterprise Architecture domains:

- Architecture Governance
- Business Governance
- Information Governance
- Data Governance
- Application Governance
- Technology Governance
- Infrastructure Governance
- Security Governance
- Knowledge Governance
- Decision Governance
- Outcome Governance
- Value Governance
- Evolution Governance

The framework applies to:

- strategic governance
- operational governance
- technical governance
- compliance governance
- transformation governance
- continuous improvement governance

---

# 3. Enterprise Governance Principles

The MFM Governance Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Governance Alignment

All governance activities shall align with Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Clear Accountability

Every architecture capability shall have defined ownership and responsibility.

---

## Principle 3 – Decision Transparency

Architecture decisions shall be documented, traceable and governed.

---

## Principle 4 – Controlled Evolution

All Enterprise changes shall follow defined governance processes.

---

## Principle 5 – Risk-Based Governance

Governance shall focus on managing Enterprise risks and opportunities.

---

## Principle 6 – Compliance Assurance

Enterprise Architecture shall comply with approved standards and requirements.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

Governance processes shall continuously improve through feedback and learning.

---
