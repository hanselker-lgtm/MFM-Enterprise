## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-regression-response-effectiveness layer defining how executed responses are assessed to determine whether they actually achieved their intended objective, reduced or controlled the regression, restored the required state, and justified progression toward resolution and re-closure.

## Core Principle
Executing a response is not evidence that the response was effective. Effectiveness shall be demonstrated through defined criteria, relevant measurements, sufficient evidence, independent or appropriately separated verification where required, and a decision that distinguishes effective, partially effective and ineffective outcomes.

```text
RESPONSE EXECUTED
      ↓
RESPONSE OBJECTIVE
      ↓
EXPECTED EFFECT
      ↓
MEASURE / OBSERVE
      ↓
COMPARE AGAINST CRITERIA
      ↓
EFFECTIVE / PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE / INEFFECTIVE / UNKNOWN
      ↓
RESOLVE / CONTINUE / REPLAN / ESCALATE / REOPEN
```

## Effectiveness Quality Test
```text
DEFINED RESPONSE OBJECTIVE
+
DEFINED SUCCESS CRITERIA
+
RELEVANT MEASUREMENT
+
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
+
VALID COMPARISON
+
APPROPRIATE VERIFICATION
+
AUTHORIZED DECISION
=
VALID GOVERNED RESPONSE EFFECTIVENESS
```


## Effectiveness Invariants

```text
EVERY MATERIAL RESPONSE SHALL HAVE AN EXPLICIT EFFECTIVENESS DETERMINATION
```

```text
EFFECTIVENESS SHALL BE ASSESSED AGAINST THE RESPONSE OBJECTIVE AND REQUIRED STATE
```

```text
RESPONSE COMPLETION SHALL NOT BE USED AS A SUBSTITUTE FOR EFFECTIVENESS
```

```text
MEASUREMENTS SHALL BE RELEVANT TO THE EFFECT THAT THE RESPONSE WAS INTENDED TO PRODUCE
```

```text
EVIDENCE SHALL BE SUFFICIENT TO SUPPORT THE EFFECTIVENESS DECISION
```

```text
PARTIAL EFFECTIVENESS SHALL REMAIN DISTINCT FROM FULL EFFECTIVENESS
```

```text
UNKNOWN SHALL NOT BE TREATED AS EFFECTIVE
```

```text
INEFFECTIVE RESPONSE SHALL TRIGGER REASSESSMENT, REPLANNING, ESCALATION OR REOPENING AS REQUIRED
```

```text
EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA SHALL NOT BE WEAKENED SOLELY TO DECLARE A RESPONSE SUCCESSFUL
```

```text
SECURITY, RESILIENCE, SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE RESPONSE EFFECTIVENESS SHALL RECEIVE APPROPRIATE RIGOR
```

```text
AI AND AGENT RESPONSE EFFECTIVENESS SHALL CONFIRM THAT REQUIRED GOVERNANCE BOUNDARIES AND BEHAVIOURAL OUTCOMES ARE RESTORED
```

```text
EFFECTIVENESS SHALL CONSIDER UNINTENDED CONSEQUENCES AND NEW REGRESSION
```

```text
REPEATED PARTIAL OR INEFFECTIVE RESPONSES SHALL TRIGGER SYSTEMIC REVIEW
```

```text
HISTORICAL EFFECTIVENESS DECISIONS SHALL REMAIN TRACEABLE
```

```text
ACCEPTANCE OF EFFECTIVENESS SHALL NOT ERASE MATERIAL RESIDUAL RISK
```

## Response Effectiveness Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Response | Executed action |
| Objective | Intended result |
| Criteria | Conditions for success |
| Measurement | Observable result |
| Evidence | Supporting proof |
| Verification | Confirmation of result |
| Decision | Effectiveness determination |
| Residual Risk | Remaining exposure |
| Follow-on | Replan / escalate / reopen / close |

## Response Effectiveness Objective

Determine whether the response materially achieved its intended objective and restored or protected the required governed state.

## Response Effectiveness Definition

Response effectiveness is the evidence-based determination that an executed response produced the required outcome to the degree specified by approved criteria.

## Response Effectiveness Scope

Scope shall include the response actions, affected systems, controls, services, processes, data, dependencies, environments and governance boundaries relevant to the objective.

## Response Effectiveness Authority

Authority shall define who may verify effectiveness, approve the determination, reject a claimed outcome and require additional response or reopening.

## Response Effectiveness Criteria

Criteria shall be defined before or, for emergency response, as soon as practicable after execution.

```text
RESPONSE
↓
OBJECTIVE
↓
SUCCESS CRITERIA
↓
MEASURE RESULT
↓
CRITERIA SATISFIED?
├── YES → EFFECTIVE
├── PARTLY → PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE
└── NO → INEFFECTIVE
```

## Response Effectiveness Measurement

Measurements shall reflect the intended effect, relevant time horizon, materiality and detection requirements. Measurements shall not be selected merely because they are easy to obtain.

## Response Effectiveness Evidence

Evidence shall link actions to observed outcomes and preserve timestamps, sources, measurement versions, criteria versions and relevant context.

## Response Effectiveness Timing

Effectiveness may require immediate, short-term and sustained assessment. A response shall not be declared fully effective when the required outcome has not yet persisted for the defined validation period.

## Response Effectiveness Validation

Validation shall confirm that measured outcomes are attributable enough to the response and that no material regression or unintended consequence invalidates the conclusion.

```text
MEASURED IMPROVEMENT
↓
VALIDATION
├── SUFFICIENT → EFFECTIVENESS DECISION
└── INSUFFICIENT → UNKNOWN / REASSESS
```

## Response Effectiveness Decision

The determination shall explicitly classify the result.

```text
EFFECTIVE
PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE
INEFFECTIVE
UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED
```

## Response Effectiveness Accountability

Accountability for the effectiveness decision shall remain explicit and shall not automatically transfer to the execution team merely because they performed the response.

## Security Regression Response Effectiveness

Confirm that security response actions actually restored required security controls, reduced exposure and prevented recurrence to the degree defined by the criteria.

## Resilience Regression Response Effectiveness

Confirm that resilience response restored required availability, capacity, recovery, continuity and dependency conditions and that recovery remains stable for the required period.

## Compliance Regression Response Effectiveness

Confirm that corrective actions actually restored required compliance or control conditions and that supporting evidence is sufficient for reliance.

## Data Regression Response Effectiveness

Confirm that data remediation restored required integrity, quality, lineage, access and authorized-use conditions and did not introduce new material defects.

## AI and Agent Regression Response Effectiveness

Confirm that AI/agent response restored required authority, policy adherence, tool and data boundaries, autonomy controls and behavioural outcomes.

```text
RESPONSE EXECUTED
↓
AI / AGENT BEHAVIOUR OBSERVED
↓
REQUIRED BOUNDARIES RESTORED?
├── YES → EFFECTIVE / SUSTAINED VALIDATION
└── NO → INEFFECTIVE / LIMIT / REOPEN / REVALIDATE
```

## Regression Response Effectiveness Failure

Failure to establish effectiveness, unreliable measurement, invalid evidence or false success claims shall be treated as a governed control failure.

```text
EFFECTIVENESS UNKNOWN / FAILED
↓
PROTECT REQUIRED STATE
↓
REASSESS RESPONSE
↓
REPLAN / REEXECUTE / ESCALATE
```

## Regression Response Effectiveness Escalation

Escalation shall occur when effectiveness cannot be established within the required window, residual impact remains material, response failure repeats, or verification authority is insufficient.

## Regression Response Effectiveness Review and Learning

Effectiveness reviews shall identify weak objectives, poor criteria, misleading metrics, delayed effects, unintended consequences and recurring response failure patterns.

## Sustained Effectiveness
Where the required state must remain stable over time, effectiveness shall be assessed over a defined validation window rather than inferred from a single successful observation.

```text
INITIAL IMPROVEMENT
↓
STABILITY WINDOW
↓
REPEATED VALIDATION
↓
SUSTAINED EFFECTIVENESS?
├── YES → ACCEPT
└── NO → PARTIAL / INEFFECTIVE / REOPEN
```

## Unintended Consequences
Effectiveness assessment shall consider whether the response introduced new material security, resilience, compliance, data, operational or governance risks.

## Effectiveness Change Control
Changes to effectiveness criteria, metrics, validation windows, evidence standards or authority shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated.

```text
CURRENT EFFECTIVENESS MODEL
↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
↓
NEW VERSION
↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

## Effectiveness Anti-Gaming Control
A response shall not be declared effective because a task was completed, a ticket was closed, a metric temporarily improved or a deadline was met. The required governed outcome remains the controlling test.

Historical effectiveness determinations, evidence, measurements, validations, disputes and rework shall remain preserved according to applicable retention and governance requirements.
