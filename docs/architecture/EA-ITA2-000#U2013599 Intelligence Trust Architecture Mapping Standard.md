# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Trust Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive trust architecture for ensuring that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are understandable, verifiable, accountable and trusted by humans, organisations and connected systems.

The Intelligence Trust Architecture Framework shall provide:

- trust principles
- trust governance
- trusted intelligence architecture
- explainable intelligence
- verifiable intelligence
- AI assurance integration
- human trust architecture
- digital identity trust
- data trust
- model trust
- decision trust
- trust measurement
- trust maturity
- trust evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence operates with transparency, reliability and accountability.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Trust Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture trust domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Trust Model
- Trust Governance
- Trusted Intelligence Architecture
- Explainable Intelligence
- Verifiable Intelligence
- AI Assurance
- Human Trust Architecture
- Digital Identity Trust
- Data Trust
- Model Trust
- Decision Trust
- Trust Metrics
- Trust Maturity
- Trust Roadmaps
- Trust Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- autonomous systems
- decision-support environments
- future Intelligence ecosystems

---

# 3. Intelligence Trust Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Trust Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Transparency

Enterprise Intelligence shall provide appropriate visibility into operation and outcomes.

---

## Principle 2 – Explainability

Intelligence decisions shall be understandable where required.

---

## Principle 3 – Verifiability

Intelligence capability shall be capable of validation and confirmation.

---

## Principle 4 – Accountability

Responsibility for Intelligence outcomes shall be clearly defined.

---

## Principle 5 – Reliability

Trusted Intelligence shall provide consistent and dependable behaviour.

---

## Principle 6 – Security and Protection

Trust shall be supported by secure design and operation.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Trust Improvement

Trust capability shall continuously evolve through measurement and learning.
