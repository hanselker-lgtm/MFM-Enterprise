# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Portfolio Management Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise portfolios as strategic investment capabilities.

The Portfolio Management Architecture Framework shall provide:

- portfolio principles
- portfolio governance
- portfolio strategy
- portfolio structures
- investment management
- initiative management
- program and project portfolio management
- value management
- portfolio improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise investments and initiatives are aligned with strategic objectives, architecture direction and expected business value.

---

# 2. Scope

The Portfolio Management Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture portfolio domains:

- Enterprise Portfolio Model
- Portfolio Governance
- Portfolio Strategy
- Portfolio Domains
- Investment Management
- Initiative Management
- Program Portfolio Management
- Project Portfolio Management
- Resource Portfolio Management
- Architecture Portfolio Management
- Value Management
- Portfolio Performance
- Portfolio Risk Management
- Portfolio Maturity
- Portfolio Evolution

The framework applies to:

- strategic investments
- transformation initiatives
- programs
- projects
- architecture initiatives
- capability development
- resource allocation
- business improvement activities

---

# 3. Enterprise Portfolio Management Architecture Principles

The Portfolio Management Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategy Alignment

Portfolio decisions shall directly support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Value-Based Investment

Portfolio decisions shall be based on expected business value and measurable outcomes.

---

## Principle 3 – Prioritization and Selection

Initiatives shall be evaluated and prioritized according to strategic importance and available resources.

---

## Principle 4 – Transparency

Portfolio information shall provide visibility of investments, progress, risks and outcomes.

---

## Principle 5 – Balanced Portfolio Management

The portfolio shall maintain balance between operational needs, innovation, transformation and strategic development.

---

## Principle 6 – Architecture Alignment

Portfolio decisions shall align with Enterprise Architecture principles and target states.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Optimization

The portfolio shall continuously evolve according to changing business requirements.

---

# 9. Portfolio Domain Architecture

The Portfolio Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise investments and initiatives are structured into logical portfolio domains to support governance, prioritization and strategic alignment.

Portfolio domains provide a common structure for managing investments, capabilities and transformation activities.

---

# 9.1 Portfolio Domain Principles

Portfolio domains shall ensure:

- strategic alignment
- investment transparency
- clear ownership
- balanced prioritization
- measurable value delivery
