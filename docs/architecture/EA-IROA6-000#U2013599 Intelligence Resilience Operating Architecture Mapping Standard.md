# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational resilience architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities can withstand disruption, maintain critical functions, recover from incidents and continuously improve resilience.

The Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- resilience operating principles
- resilience governance frameworks
- resilience lifecycle structures
- resilience management models
- continuity structures
- recovery models
- crisis response models
- resilience validation models
- resilience security models
- resilience monitoring structures
- resilience automation models
- resilience evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for resilient, reliable and continuously improving Intelligence capabilities.

# 3. Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Resilience Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Resilience by Design

Resilience capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Continuous Availability

Critical Intelligence capabilities shall maintain operational availability.

---

## Principle 3 – Controlled Recovery

Recovery activities shall follow defined governance and control processes.

---

## Principle 4 – Risk-Based Resilience

Resilience decisions shall be based on identified risks and operational impact.

---

## Principle 5 – Adaptive Resilience

Resilience capability shall adapt to changing conditions and threats.

---

## Principle 6 – Learning Through Disruption

Resilience capability shall improve through experience and incidents.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolution Through Resilience

Resilience shall support continuous Intelligence evolution.
