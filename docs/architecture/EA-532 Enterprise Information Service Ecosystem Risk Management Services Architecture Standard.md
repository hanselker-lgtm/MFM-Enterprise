# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Risk Management Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed risk identification, assessment, treatment and monitoring capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Risk Management Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem risk management capabilities
- support strategic ecosystem risk management
- support operational ecosystem risk management
- support partner risk management
- support supplier risk management
- support integration risk management
- support information risk management
- support data risk management
- support security risk management
- support AI risk management
- support automation risk management
- support orchestration risk management
- support compliance risk management
- support risk assessment
- support risk monitoring
- support risk reporting
- support risk lifecycle management
- improve ecosystem resilience
- improve ecosystem transparency
- improve ecosystem decision-making

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Risk Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable risk management capabilities enabling resilient ecosystem operations.

# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Risk Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Risk Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Risk Management by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include risk management considerations from the beginning.

---

## Proactive Risk Management

Risks shall be identified and managed before they create unacceptable impact.

---

## Evidence-Based Risk Decisions

Risk decisions shall be supported by documented analysis and measurable information.

---

## Integrated Risk Management

Risk management shall be integrated across governance, compliance, security, operations and technology domains.

---

## Continuous Risk Improvement

Risk management capabilities shall continuously evolve according to ecosystem changes.
