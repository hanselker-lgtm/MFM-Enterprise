# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, and intelligence capabilities required for an **Intelligence Convergent Enterprise Architecture (ICEA)**.

Where **EA-IUEA63** establishes a unified Enterprise Intelligence in which all architectural domains operate in permanent synchronization, **EA-ICEA64** advances the framework further by enabling those domains to **converge into a single cognitive enterprise intelligence**.

Convergence represents more than integration or synchronization.

It is the continuous fusion of enterprise capabilities into one intelligent operational ecosystem where knowledge, governance, reasoning, planning, execution, learning, sustainability, Digital Twins, Human Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, and Enterprise Wisdom function as inseparable components of one Enterprise Intelligence.

The objective is to remove the remaining conceptual boundaries between architectural disciplines and establish a continuously converging enterprise intelligence capable of holistic enterprise cognition.
