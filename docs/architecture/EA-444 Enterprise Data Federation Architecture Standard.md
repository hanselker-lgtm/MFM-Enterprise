This standard defines the Enterprise Data Federation Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

All Enterprise Data Federation implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Federation Architecture enabling coordinated, secure and governed access to distributed Enterprise information across multiple independent information domains through standardized federation services.

Enterprise Data Federation shall

- unify distributed information access
- coordinate federated information services
- improve interoperability
- reduce unnecessary information duplication
- enable cross-domain information sharing
- strengthen governance
- improve operational agility
- support regulatory compliance
- optimize Enterprise information utilization
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Federation shall establish a consistent architectural framework for federated information management across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise databases
- master data platforms
- transactional systems
- data warehouses
- lakehouses
- cloud-native data platforms
- APIs
- event-driven systems
- analytical environments
- external information providers

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform, cloud provider or information technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Federation Principles

Enterprise Data Federation shall be governed by the following principles.

## Federation by Design

Enterprise information shall be made available through standardized federation mechanisms before physical consolidation is considered.

---

## Unified Information Access

Enterprise users and services shall access federated information through standardized federation services.

---

## Distributed Ownership

Information ownership shall remain with the authoritative Business Domain responsible for the underlying information assets.

---

## Coordinated Federation

Federated information services shall coordinate distributed information sources using standardized Enterprise policies.

---

## Metadata Driven Federation

Enterprise Data Federation shall utilize centralized metadata, semantic definitions and governance policies to coordinate distributed information assets.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Federation Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, database technologies, middleware platforms and cloud providers.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Federation Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for coordinated, secure and governed access to distributed Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating federated data services, distributed query coordination, metadata management, semantic consistency, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted information sharing, reduced complexity, improved interoperability and operational excellence while preserving authoritative ownership of Enterprise information.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Federation initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing federation capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Data Federation Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted federation optimization, autonomous federation orchestration, semantic reasoning engines, adaptive workload distribution, intelligent policy enforcement, self-optimizing federation services, digital twin-enabled federation management and post-quantum protected federation infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Federation continues to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven coordination of distributed Enterprise information across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
