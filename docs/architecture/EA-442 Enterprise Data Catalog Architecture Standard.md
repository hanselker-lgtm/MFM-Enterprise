# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Catalog Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Metadata Architecture principles are inherited from EA-353.

All Enterprise Data Catalog implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Catalog Architecture providing a centralized, trusted and governed inventory of all Enterprise information assets, metadata, business definitions and technical relationships throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Catalog shall

- establish a single authoritative catalog
- improve Enterprise information discoverability
- strengthen metadata management
- improve governance
- support business understanding
- enable information reuse
- support regulatory compliance
- improve operational transparency
- strengthen Enterprise trust
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Catalog shall provide a standardized architectural framework for governing Enterprise information assets throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise databases
- master data
- transactional systems
- data warehouses
- lakehouses
- APIs
- event streams
- integration platforms
- analytical platforms
- archived Enterprise information

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, cloud provider, storage technology or infrastructure platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Data Catalog Principles

Enterprise Data Catalog shall be governed by the following principles.

## Catalog by Design

Every Enterprise information asset shall be registered in the Enterprise Data Catalog.

---

## Single Source of Metadata

Enterprise metadata shall be centrally governed and consistently managed.

---

## Business and Technical Integration

Business definitions and technical metadata shall remain continuously synchronized.

---

## Automated Discovery

Enterprise information assets shall be automatically discovered and cataloged wherever technically feasible.

---

## Continuous Governance

Enterprise Data Catalog shall be continuously maintained through Enterprise governance processes.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Catalog Architecture shall remain independent of catalog vendors, cloud providers, database technologies and implementation platforms.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Catalog Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized registration, governance, discovery and management of Enterprise information assets across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating metadata management, business glossary services, asset registration, search capabilities, lineage, provenance, observability, security and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted information discovery, operational transparency, regulatory compliance and enterprise-wide information governance.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Catalog initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing catalog capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Data Catalog Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted metadata enrichment, autonomous asset discovery, semantic search, knowledge graph integration, intelligent business glossary management, digital twin-based information catalogs, predictive metadata analytics and post-quantum protected metadata repositories.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Catalog continues to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven information discovery and governance across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
