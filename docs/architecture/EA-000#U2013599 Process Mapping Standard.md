# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Process Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise Processes across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Process Reference Model shall provide:

- process identification
- process classification
- process ownership
- process relationships
- process capability mapping
- process service mapping
- process lifecycle management
- architecture traceability

The model shall establish a common Enterprise language for defining, managing and evolving processes.

---

# 2. Scope

The Process Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture process domains:

- Architecture Processes
- Business Processes
- Information Processes
- Data Processes
- Application Processes
- Technology Processes
- Security Processes
- Governance Processes
- Ecosystem Processes
- Evolution Processes

The model applies to:

- strategic processes
- management processes
- operational processes
- supporting processes
- transformation processes
- evolution processes

---

# 3. Enterprise Process Architecture Principles

The Process Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Process Orientation

Enterprise capabilities shall be realized through managed processes.

---

## Capability Alignment

Every process shall support one or more Enterprise capabilities.

---

## Service Enablement

Processes shall enable delivery of Enterprise Services.

---

## Standardization

Processes shall follow common Enterprise process standards.

---

## Ownership

Every process shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Processes shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
