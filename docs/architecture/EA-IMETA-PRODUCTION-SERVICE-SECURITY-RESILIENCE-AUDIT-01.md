# 1. PURPOSE

EA-IMETA-PRODUCTION-SERVICE-SECURITY-RESILIENCE-AUDIT-01 establishes the formal independent audit capability above Assurance.

Audit provides independent examination of whether:

```text
GOVERNANCE
CONTROLS
SECURITY
RESILIENCE
ADAPTATION
AI
AGENTS
RISK MANAGEMENT
RECOVERY
ASSURANCE
```

operate according to approved requirements and authority.

The audit chain is:

```text
AUDIT UNIVERSE
 ↓
RISK ASSESSMENT
 ↓
AUDIT PLAN
 ↓
ENGAGEMENT
 ↓
EVIDENCE
 ↓
TEST
 ↓
FINDING
 ↓
MANAGEMENT RESPONSE
 ↓
REMEDIATION
 ↓
FOLLOW-UP
 ↓
AUDIT OPINION
 ↓
GOVERNANCE REPORTING
```

# 2. AUDIT PRINCIPLE

> EA-IMETA SHALL MAINTAIN AN INDEPENDENT, OBJECTIVE AND RISK-BASED AUDIT CAPABILITY THAT PROVIDES GOVERNANCE WITH EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSIONS ON THE EFFECTIVENESS OF SECURITY, RESILIENCE, ADAPTATION AND CONTROL ENVIRONMENTS.

---

# 60. EFFECTIVE

Evidence supports that the audited area is operating effectively within scope.

---

# 61. EFFECTIVE WITH CONDITIONS

The area is generally effective but has defined material limitations or conditions.

---

# 62. PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE

Material weaknesses exist but significant portions of the control environment remain effective.

---

# 63. INEFFECTIVE

Material weaknesses prevent reasonable reliance on the audited control environment.

---

# 64. UNABLE TO CONCLUDE

Evidence is insufficient to support a reliable audit conclusion.

```text

=

=
```
