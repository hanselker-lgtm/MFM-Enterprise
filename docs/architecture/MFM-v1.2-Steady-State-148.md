This document establishes the one-hundred-and-forty-eighth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

# 101. Final Infrastructure Governance Principle

> **MFM must govern enterprise infrastructure as a controlled, secure, standardized, observable, performant, resilient and lifecycle-managed foundation that provides reliable compute, storage, operating-system, virtualization and cloud capabilities for business applications, data, integration and services.**

# 102. Final Architecture Principle

> **Infrastructure architecture must provide standardized, scalable, secure, observable, maintainable and recoverable foundations aligned with enterprise architecture and service requirements.**

# 103. Final Compute Principle

> **Compute resources must be appropriately sized, securely configured, monitored, resilient and lifecycle-managed according to workload and business criticality.**

# 104. Final Storage Principle

> **Storage must be governed according to data criticality, performance, availability, security, capacity, retention and recovery requirements.**

# 105. Final Operating System Principle

> **Operating systems must remain supported, securely configured, patched, monitored and lifecycle-managed, with unsupported systems subject to controlled remediation or exception.**

# 106. Final Automation Principle

> **Infrastructure provisioning and configuration should be automated where appropriate to improve consistency, repeatability, security and operational efficiency.**

# 107. Final Security Principle

> **Infrastructure security must integrate identity, least privilege, hardening, patching, vulnerability management, secrets, encryption, logging and continuous monitoring.**

# 108. Final Operations Principle

> **Infrastructure operations must maintain reliable service through disciplined monitoring, performance, capacity, availability, incident, problem, change, configuration and recovery management.**

# 109. Final Resilience Principle

> **Critical infrastructure must have appropriate redundancy, backup, resilience and tested recovery capabilities aligned with business criticality, dependencies, RTO and RPO.**

# 110. Final Technical Debt Principle

> **Material infrastructure technical debt must be visible, owned, risk-assessed, prioritized and progressively reduced where its risk, cost or operational impact justifies remediation.**

# 111. Final Lifecycle Principle

> **Infrastructure must be actively managed from design and provisioning through operation, modernization and secure retirement, including data, access, monitoring, backup, licensing and asset cleanup.**

# 113. Final Improvement Principle
