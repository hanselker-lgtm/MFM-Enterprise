# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Information Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise Information capabilities across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Information Reference Model shall provide:

- information identification
- information classification
- information ownership
- information relationships
- information service mapping
- information process mapping
- data governance
- information lifecycle management
- architecture traceability

The model shall establish a common Enterprise language for defining, managing and evolving information assets.

---

# 2. Scope

The Information Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture information domains:

- Architecture Information
- Business Information
- Operational Information
- Master Information
- Transaction Information
- Analytical Information
- Knowledge Information
- Security Information
- Governance Information
- Ecosystem Information
- Evolution Information

The model applies to:

- strategic information
- operational information
- supporting information
- analytical information
- knowledge assets
- transformation information
- evolution information

---

# 3. Enterprise Information Architecture Principles

The Information Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Information Value

Information shall be managed as an Enterprise asset.

---

## Information Ownership

Every information asset shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Information Quality

Information shall be accurate, complete, consistent and reliable.

---

## Information Governance

Information shall be controlled according to Enterprise policies and standards.

---

## Information Security

Information shall be protected throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Information Accessibility

Authorized users shall have appropriate access to required information.

---

## Information Lifecycle Management

Information shall be managed from creation to disposal.

---

## Continuous Evolution

Information capabilities shall continuously evolve according to Enterprise requirements.

---
