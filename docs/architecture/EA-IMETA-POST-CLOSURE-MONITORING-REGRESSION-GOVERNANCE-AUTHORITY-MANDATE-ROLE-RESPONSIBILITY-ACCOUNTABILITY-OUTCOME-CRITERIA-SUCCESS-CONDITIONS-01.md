## Purpose
Establish the authoritative success-conditions layer beneath success, defining the mandatory and conditional conditions that must exist for an outcome to qualify as successful, including required state, observability, verification, evidence, measurement, pass/fail, security, resilience, compliance, escalation and review requirements.

## Core Principle
A success condition is a specific condition that must be satisfied for success to be recognized. Conditions shall be explicit enough to permit consistent verification and shall distinguish mandatory conditions from conditional or supporting conditions.

```text
SUCCESS
   ↓
SUCCESS CONDITIONS
   ↓
MANDATORY CONDITIONS
   ↓
CONDITIONAL CONDITIONS
   ↓
REQUIRED STATE
   ↓
VERIFICATION
   ↓
EVIDENCE
   ↓
PASS / FAIL
   ↓
ACCEPTANCE
```

## Success Condition Structure
| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Condition | Specific requirement for success |
| Classification | Mandatory, conditional or supporting |
| Activation | When the condition applies |
| Required State | State that must exist |
| Verification | Method used to verify satisfaction |
| Evidence | Proof supporting determination |
| Measurement | Applicable metric or assessment |
| Pass Rule | Condition for satisfaction |
| Failure Rule | Condition for non-satisfaction |
| Tolerance | Permitted deviation where allowed |
| Exception | Governed exception mechanism |
| Escalation | Required response to material failure |
| Review | Reassessment trigger and frequency |

## Mandatory Conditions
Mandatory conditions are conditions that must be satisfied for full success. They may arise from business requirements, architecture, security, resilience, safety, compliance, contractual obligations or governance decisions.

```text
MANDATORY CONDITION
       ↓
SATISFIED?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → NO FULL SUCCESS / ESCALATE / EXCEPTION```

## Conditional Conditions
Conditional conditions apply only when their activation criteria are met. The activation rule shall be explicit and evidence-based.

```text
CONDITION
   ↓
ACTIVATED?
 ├── NO  → NOT APPLICABLE
 └── YES → VERIFY SATISFACTION```

## Required State
The required state defines what must actually exist for the condition to be satisfied. It shall be distinguishable from the activity performed to create that state.

```text
ACTIVITY COMPLETED
      ≠
REQUIRED STATE EXISTS```

## Observable Conditions
Material conditions shall be observable or objectively assessable. Where direct observation is impossible, an approved proxy or assurance method shall be defined.

## Verification Conditions
Verification conditions specify how satisfaction is established, who performs verification, what evidence is required and what independence is necessary.

```text
CONDITION
   ↓
VERIFICATION METHOD
   ↓
VERIFY
   ↓
RESULT
   ↓
PASS / FAIL```

## Evidence Conditions
Evidence conditions define the minimum evidence required to demonstrate satisfaction. Evidence shall be attributable, relevant, sufficiently complete, current and protected against inappropriate alteration.

## Measurement Conditions
Measurement conditions shall define data source, method, frequency, quality, uncertainty and interpretation where measurement is used.

```text
DATA SOURCE
   ↓
DATA VALIDATION
   ↓
MEASUREMENT
   ↓
CONDITION TEST
   ↓
RESULT```

## Pass Conditions
Pass requires satisfaction of every applicable mandatory condition and all applicable conditional conditions required by the approved success definition.

## Failure Conditions
Failure includes an unsatisfied mandatory condition, an unacceptable conditional condition, insufficient mandatory evidence, unsafe state, mandatory non-conformity or performance outside approved limits.

```text
CONDITION RESULT
      ↓
MANDATORY?
 ├── YES → FAILURE MAY BLOCK SUCCESS
 └── NO  → ASSESS IMPACT / PARTIAL SUCCESS```

## Tolerance Conditions
Tolerance may be applied only where explicitly permitted by the parent criteria. Tolerance shall define boundaries, measurement rules and consequences.

## Threshold Conditions
Thresholds shall identify boundaries that trigger pass, warning, escalation, failure or another governed disposition.

```text
CONDITION VALUE
      ↓
WITHIN PASS LIMIT?
 ├── YES → PASS
 └── NO
      ↓
WITHIN TOLERANCE?
 ├── YES → CONDITIONAL / AT RISK
 └── NO  → FAIL / ESCALATE```

## Security Conditions
Security conditions shall address applicable confidentiality, integrity, availability, authentication, authorization, vulnerability, threat, incident and protective-control requirements. Mandatory security conditions shall not be waived by operational convenience.

## Resilience Conditions
Resilience conditions shall address continuity, recovery, restoration, dependency behavior, capacity, degraded-mode operation and recovery verification where applicable.

## Data Conditions
Data conditions may include accuracy, completeness, integrity, timeliness, lineage, availability, access control, retention and authorized use.

## AI and Agent Conditions
AI and agent success conditions shall include applicable correctness, policy compliance, authority boundaries, autonomy limits, tool restrictions, data-access limits, safety controls and human oversight.

```text
AI / AGENT ACTION
      ↓
CONDITION APPLIES?
      ↓
WITHIN AUTHORITY?
      ↓
POLICY COMPLIANT?
      ↓
REQUIRED RESULT ACHIEVED?
      ↓
NO UNACCEPTABLE SIDE EFFECT?
      ↓
PASS / FAIL```

## Compliance Conditions
Compliance conditions shall reflect mandatory obligations. A success state shall not be accepted where a mandatory compliance condition is violated.

## Escalation Conditions
```text
CONDITION FAILURE
      ↓
MATERIAL?
 ├── NO  → CORRECT / MONITOR
 └── YES → ESCALATE```

Escalation shall also occur where authority is insufficient, the condition failure is systemic, or the failure threatens a material outcome.

## Exception Conditions
An exception shall be governed, authorized, time-bounded where appropriate, risk-assessed and supported by compensating controls and evidence.

```text
CONDITION NOT SATISFIED
      ↓
EXCEPTION ALLOWED?
 ├── NO  → FAIL / REMEDIATE
 └── YES → GOVERNED EXCEPTION → CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE```

## Condition Determination
```text
CONDITION
   ↓
APPLICABLE?
 ├── NO  → NOT APPLICABLE
 └── YES
      ↓
EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT?
       ├── NO  → UNDETERMINED / ESCALATE
       └── YES
            ↓
SATISFIED?
             ├── YES → PASS
             └── NO  → FAIL```

## Condition Aggregation
Success conditions shall be aggregated according to the approved parent criteria. Mandatory failures shall not be hidden by a high number of satisfied supporting conditions.

```text
MANDATORY CONDITIONS
       ↓
ALL SATISFIED?
 ├── YES → EVALUATE CONDITIONAL CONDITIONS
 └── NO  → NO FULL SUCCESS```

## Condition Change Control
Changes shall be approved, versioned, effective-dated and traceable. Historical determinations shall remain linked to the condition version that was active at the time.

```text
CURRENT CONDITION
      ↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
      ↓
IMPACT ASSESSMENT
      ↓
AUTHORIZATION
      ↓
NEW VERSION
      ↓
EFFECTIVE DATE```

## Condition Failure and Revalidation
Material failure shall lead to correction, revalidation and a new determination before the success state is restored.

```text
CONDITION FAIL
      ↓
CONTAIN / REMEDIATE
      ↓
RE-VERIFY
      ↓
RE-ESTABLISH EVIDENCE
      ↓
PASS / FAIL```

## Condition Review
Conditions shall be reviewed following material regression, repeated failure, criteria change, baseline change, architecture change, risk change, regulatory change or evidence that the condition no longer measures the intended success state.

```text
CONDITION ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
STILL VALID?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → MODIFY / SUPERSEDE / RETIRE```

## Governance Learning
Repeated condition failures shall trigger review of the success definition, outcome criteria, controls, architecture, responsibilities, measurement methods and governance assumptions.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes the success-conditions layer beneath success for post-closure regression governance. It does not replace governance, authority, mandate, role, responsibility, accountability, outcome, criteria or success layers.
