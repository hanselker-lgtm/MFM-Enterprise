# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise information management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise information management ensures that information is identified, classified, governed, protected, shared and maintained through standardized enterprise processes while preserving information quality, governance, traceability, operational effectiveness and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Information Management
- Information Classification
- Information Governance
- Information Quality
- Information Sharing
- Information Protection
- Information Accessibility
- Continuous Improvement

All enterprise information management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 4. Information Management Principles

Enterprise information management shall follow these principles.

- Information by Design
- Governance by Default
- Information Quality
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Accountability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Information management implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Information Management Domains

Enterprise information management shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Business Information
- Operational Information
- Technical Information
- Financial Information
- Compliance Information
- Security Information
- Reporting Information
- Reference Information

Additional information domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Information Management Ownership

Each information management domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- information stewardship

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the information lifecycle.

---

# 7. Information Management Governance

Enterprise information management governance shall define

- information governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- information verification
- governance reporting

Information management governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Information Management Responsibilities

Enterprise information management shall provide controlled management of enterprise information assets.

Information management responsibilities shall

- separate information management from business execution
- coordinate information ownership
- ensure information consistency
- validate information objectives
- preserve information traceability
- support enterprise governance

Information management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Information Classification

Enterprise information management shall implement standardized information classification.

Information classification shall

- define information categories
- define confidentiality levels
- define accessibility requirements
- define ownership
- preserve classification history
- maintain classification traceability

Information classification shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Information Quality

Enterprise information management shall ensure standardized information quality.

Information quality shall

- define quality requirements
- validate completeness
- validate consistency
- validate accuracy
- preserve quality history
- maintain quality traceability

Information quality shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Information Sharing

Enterprise information management shall implement standardized information sharing.

Information sharing shall

- distribute approved information
- support authorized collaboration
- ensure controlled accessibility
- preserve sharing history
- maintain sharing traceability
- support enterprise operations

Information sharing shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Information Protection

Enterprise information management shall implement standardized information protection.

Information protection shall

- protect confidentiality
- protect integrity
- protect availability
- preserve protection history
- maintain protection traceability
- support regulatory compliance

Information protection shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Information Management Dependencies

Enterprise information management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- document management systems
- collaboration platforms
- communication platforms
- enterprise repositories
- enterprise infrastructure

Information management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Information Documentation

Each information management domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- information registers
- ownership information
- classifications
- quality requirements
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Information Lifecycle

Enterprise information management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Identified
- Classified
- Approved
- Published
- Maintained
- Reviewed
- Updated
- Protected
- Retired
- Archived

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Information Quality Attributes

Enterprise information management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- accessibility
- governance compliance
- information reliability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the information lifecycle.

---

# 17. Information Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized information register.

The information register shall contain

- information identifiers
- ownership assignments
- classifications
- lifecycle status
- publication status
- documentation references
- review schedules
- governance status

The information register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise information.

---

# 18. Information Reviews

Enterprise information management implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- information completeness
- information consistency
- classification accuracy
- publication integrity
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Information Metrics

Enterprise information management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- published information assets
- information assets under review
- overdue information reviews
- information quality score
- information accessibility
- information revisions
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous information improvement.

---

# 20. Information Verification

Enterprise information management implementations shall undergo formal verification before publication and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm information completeness
- verify classifications
- verify ownership
- verify publication readiness
- verify accessibility
- verify documentation completeness
- confirm governance compliance
- approve publication

Information verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Information Improvement

Enterprise information management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve information quality
- improve governance
- improve information accessibility
- improve operational effectiveness
- improve regulatory compliance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise information management implementations shall handle information-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify information classification failures
- classify information quality failures
- classify information sharing failures
- classify information protection failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Information management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, information integrity, governance or regulatory obligations.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Information management implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved document management systems
- approved collaboration platforms
- approved communication platforms
- approved enterprise repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Information management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external information management services

Information management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An information management implementation is compliant when

- Information management responsibilities are documented.
- Information classifications are documented.
- Information quality requirements are implemented.
- Information sharing procedures are implemented.
- Information protection mechanisms are documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Information Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Information verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Information Ownership

Enterprise information shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Missing Information Classification

Enterprise information shall never be managed without an approved classification.

---

## Missing Information Quality Controls

Enterprise information shall never be published without defined quality validation.

---

## Information Silos

Critical enterprise information shall never remain isolated within individuals, departments or systems.

---

## Undocumented Information Dependencies

Information management implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Information Management Outside Governance

Information management implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise information management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- information management quality
- information completeness
- classification integrity
- information quality
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational effectiveness
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Information Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise information management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise information is identified, classified, governed, protected, shared and continuously improved through standardized lifecycle management while preserving information quality, governance, traceability, accountability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise information management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
