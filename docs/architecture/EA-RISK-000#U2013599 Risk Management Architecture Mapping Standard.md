# 9. Risk Domain Architecture

The Risk Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise risks are structured into logical domains to support identification, assessment, treatment and governance.

Risk domains provide a common structure for organizing risk exposure, ownership and mitigation activities.

---

# 9.1 Risk Domain Principles

Risk domains shall ensure:

- comprehensive risk coverage
- clear risk ownership
- consistent risk assessment
- effective mitigation
- transparent risk management

---

# 9.2 Risk Domain Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Risk Domain | Logical grouping of risks |
| Risk Owner | Accountability for risk area |
| Risk Categories | Defines risk types |
| Risk Indicators | Measures risk exposure |
| Risk Controls | Defines mitigation mechanisms |

# 15. Risk Integration Architecture

The Risk Integration Architecture Model defines how Enterprise risk management connects with all MFM Enterprise Architecture domains and management frameworks.

Risk integration ensures that risk considerations are embedded into strategic planning, architecture decisions, operational activities and transformation initiatives.

---

# 15.1 Risk Integration Principles

Risk integration shall ensure:

- enterprise-wide risk visibility
- consistent risk management
- architecture alignment
- informed decisions
- improved resilience

# 21. Risk Governance Operating Model

The Risk Governance Operating Model defines how Enterprise risk decisions, responsibilities and controls are governed.

Risk Governance ensures that Enterprise risks remain aligned with strategic objectives, architecture principles, operational requirements and acceptable risk exposure.

---

# 21.1 Risk Governance Principles

Risk Governance shall ensure:

- clear risk ownership
- controlled risk decisions
- strategic alignment
- transparent risk reporting
- continuous risk improvement
