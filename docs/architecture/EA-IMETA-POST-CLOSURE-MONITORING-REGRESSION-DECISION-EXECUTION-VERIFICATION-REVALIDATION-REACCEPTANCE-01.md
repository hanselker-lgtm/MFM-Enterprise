## Purpose
Establish the authoritative re-acceptance layer after successful post-closure regression re-validation, ensuring that evidence, current residual risk, open conditions, authority, independent challenge and decision rationale support an explicit re-acceptance before re-closure.

## Core Principle
Re-validation establishes that the current state satisfies the applicable criteria. Re-acceptance is the separate governance decision that authorizes the organization to treat that state as accepted and proceed toward re-closure.

```text
RE-VALIDATION PASSED
        ↓
EVIDENCE PACKAGE
        ↓
CURRENT RESIDUAL RISK
        ↓
OPEN CONDITIONS
        ↓
AUTHORITY REVIEW
        ↓
INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE
        ↓
RE-ACCEPTANCE DECISION
        ↓
RE-CLOSURE / CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE / DEFERRAL
```

## Re-Acceptance Quality Test
```text
VALID RE-VALIDATION
+
COMPLETE EVIDENCE
+
CURRENT RESIDUAL RISK
+
CONDITIONS GOVERNED
+
APPROPRIATE AUTHORITY
+
INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE
+
TRACEABLE RATIONALE
=
GOVERNED POST-CLOSURE RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

## Re-Acceptance Status Model
```text
NOT READY
PACKAGE PREPARATION
EVIDENCE REVIEW
RISK REVIEW
CONDITION REVIEW
AUTHORITY REVIEW
UNDER CHALLENGE
READY FOR DECISION
ACCEPTED
CONDITIONALLY ACCEPTED
DEFERRED
REJECTED
ESCALATED
READY FOR RE-CLOSURE
```

## Re-Acceptance Invariants

```text
RE-VALIDATION ≠ RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
NO VALID RE-VALIDATION → NO POSITIVE RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
NO CURRENT EVIDENCE → NO POSITIVE RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
CURRENT RESIDUAL RISK SHALL BE EXPLICIT
```

```text
MATERIAL OPEN CONDITIONS SHALL BLOCK UNCONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE SHALL HAVE OWNER, DUE DATE AND EXIT CRITERIA
```

```text
DEFERRED ACCEPTANCE SHALL NOT BE TREATED AS ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
AUTHORITY SHALL MATCH MATERIALITY AND RISK
```

```text
CHALLENGE SHALL BE TRACEABLE
```

```text
RE-ACCEPTANCE SHALL CREATE A NEW IMMUTABLE GOVERNANCE EVENT
```

```text
ORIGINAL ACCEPTANCE AND CLOSURE HISTORY SHALL NOT BE OVERWRITTEN
```

```text
AI RECOMMENDATION ≠ RE-ACCEPTANCE AUTHORITY
```

```text
AGENT ACTION ≠ RE-ACCEPTANCE AUTHORITY
```

```text
POSITIVE RE-ACCEPTANCE → RE-CLOSURE READINESS
```

## Re-Acceptance Decision Matrix
| Dimension | States |
|---|---|
| Re-Validation | Passed / Partial / Failed |
| Evidence | Missing / Partial / Sufficient / Invalid |
| Residual Risk | Within Appetite / Elevated / Above Appetite / Critical |
| Conditions | None / Open / Verified / Overdue |
| Authority | Confirmed / Delegated / Insufficient / Escalated |
| Challenge | Complete / Open / Material Objection |
| Decision | Accept / Conditional Accept / Defer / Reject / Escalate |
| Next State | Re-Close / Monitor / Remediate / Re-Validate / Reopen |

## Re-Acceptance Record Model
| Record | Minimum information |
|---|---|
| Re-Acceptance Record | ID, re-validation, evidence, risk, conditions, authority, decision, rationale, timestamp |
| Evidence Package | source, version, integrity, completeness, sufficiency, traceability |
| Residual Risk Record | current risk, appetite, treatment, owner, acceptance rationale |
| Condition Record | condition, owner, due date, expiry, monitoring, exit criteria |
| Challenge Record | challenger, issue, evidence, response, resolution |
| Authority Record | decision maker, role, authority level, delegation, validity |
| Decision Record | outcome, rationale, constraints, conditions, effective date |
| Re-Closure Handoff | accepted state, remaining conditions, baseline, monitoring requirements |

```text

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓
DECIDE

 ↓
RE-CLOSURE READY?
 ├─ YES → RE-CLOSURE
 └─ NO → CONDITIONAL / DEFERRED / REJECTED / REMEDIATION
```

## Conditional Re-Acceptance
Conditional re-acceptance may be used only where remaining conditions are explicitly defined, risk-assessed, owned, time-bounded where appropriate, monitored and linked to measurable exit criteria.

```text
CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE
 ↓
CONDITION
 ↓
OWNER
 ↓
DUE DATE / REVIEW POINT
 ↓
MONITOR
 ↓
VERIFY EXIT CRITERIA
 ↓
CLOSE / EXTEND / REJECT
```

## Deferred Re-Acceptance
Deferral is a governance state, not an acceptance state. The record shall identify why a decision cannot yet be made, interim controls, decision owner, target date and the risk created by delay.

## Residual Risk Re-Acceptance
Re-acceptance shall use the current residual risk established during re-validation. Material risk above appetite requires additional treatment, escalation or an explicitly authorized exception.

## Authority and Independent Challenge
```text

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓
RESPONSE / RESOLUTION
 ↓
```

## Security and Resilience Re-Acceptance
Security and resilience shall be assessed as current operating properties. A successful remediation execution does not automatically demonstrate that current exposure, dependencies, recovery capability or threat conditions are acceptable.

## AI and Agent Re-Acceptance
AI may synthesize evidence, identify gaps, compare risk indicators and recommend a disposition. The formal re-acceptance decision shall remain with the designated governance authority and shall be supported by verified evidence.

```text
AI / AGENT RECOMMENDATION
        ↓
EVIDENCE VERIFICATION
        ↓
GOVERNED REVIEW
        ↓
AUTHORIZED RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

## Re-Acceptance Rejection / Deferral
```text
RE-ACCEPTANCE NOT APPROVED
        ↓
IDENTIFY GAP
        ↓
ASSESS RISK / IMPACT
        ↓
REMEDIATE / ADD EVIDENCE / ESCALATE
        ↓
RE-VALIDATE
        ↓
RE-ACCEPT
```

## Re-Closure Boundary
Positive re-acceptance establishes authorization to proceed toward re-closure; it does not itself close the lifecycle.

```text
RE-ACCEPTED
      ↓
RE-CLOSURE PACKAGE
      ↓
CLOSURE CRITERIA
      ↓
AUTHORIZED RE-CLOSURE
```

## Monitoring Continuation
Where re-acceptance confirms that the lifecycle may remain closed, the decision shall hand off explicit monitoring thresholds, metrics, owners, review frequency and escalation criteria.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes re-acceptance for post-closure regression re-validation. It does not replace the generic regression re-acceptance or re-closure architecture.
