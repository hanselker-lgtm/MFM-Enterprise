# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Consistency Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Integrity principles are inherited from EA-437.

All Enterprise Data Consistency implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Consistency Architecture ensuring that Enterprise information remains synchronized, logically consistent and semantically aligned across all Business and Technology Domains.

Enterprise Data Consistency shall

- ensure consistent Enterprise information
- maintain synchronization across systems
- preserve semantic consistency
- support distributed business operations
- improve operational reliability
- reduce conflicting information
- strengthen business trust
- improve regulatory compliance
- enable enterprise interoperability
- support continuous governance

Enterprise Data Consistency shall provide a standardized architectural framework for maintaining trusted and synchronized Enterprise information regardless of platform, location or processing model.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise databases
- distributed databases
- master data
- transactional systems
- analytical platforms
- APIs
- event-driven systems
- messaging platforms
- cloud services
- enterprise integrations

The standard applies across on-premises, hybrid, multi-cloud and cloud-native environments.

# 3. Enterprise Data Consistency Principles

Enterprise Data Consistency shall be governed by the following principles.

## Consistency by Design

Enterprise information shall be designed to remain logically consistent throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Synchronization First

Enterprise information shall remain synchronized across all authoritative systems according to approved consistency objectives.

---

## Single Business Meaning

Enterprise information shall maintain identical semantic meaning regardless of storage location or consuming application.

---

## Controlled Distribution

Distributed Enterprise information shall remain governed through standardized consistency mechanisms.

---

## Continuous Validation

Consistency shall be continuously validated through automated Enterprise controls.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Consistency Architecture shall remain independent of database technologies, messaging platforms, cloud providers and infrastructure vendors.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Consistency Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework that ensures Enterprise information remains synchronized, semantically aligned and logically consistent across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating synchronization, distributed transaction coordination, conflict detection, reconciliation, metadata management, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform delivers trusted and consistent information supporting business continuity, operational excellence and regulatory compliance.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Consistency initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing consistency capabilities across the Enterprise.
