# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Records Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Records Management ensures that enterprise records are created, classified, maintained, protected, retained and disposed of according to business requirements, legal obligations and governance policies while preserving authenticity, integrity, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Record Classification
- Record Lifecycle Management
- Record Preservation
- Record Access Control
- Record Retention
- Record Disposal
- Records Management Reporting
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Records Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Records Management implementations shall handle records management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify record classification failures
- classify lifecycle management failures
- classify record preservation failures
- classify access control failures
- classify record retention failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Records Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, record authenticity, information integrity, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Records Management implementations may depend upon

- approved data retention services
- approved data governance services
- approved security services
- approved storage infrastructure
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Records Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external records management providers

Records Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Records Management implementation is compliant when

- Record classification is documented.
- Record lifecycle management is implemented.
- Record preservation procedures are documented.
- Record access control is enforced.
- Record retention requirements are maintained.
- Record verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Record Classification

Enterprise records shall never exist without an approved record classification.

---

## Undefined Record Lifecycle

Enterprise records shall never be managed without documented lifecycle procedures.

---

## Uncontrolled Record Preservation

Enterprise records shall never be preserved outside approved preservation procedures.

---

## Unauthorized Record Disposal

Enterprise records shall never be destroyed without documented authorization and verification.

---

## Inadequate Access Control

Enterprise records shall never be accessible without approved access control and auditing.

---

## Records Management Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent records management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Records Management services.

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Records Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Records Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise records are created, maintained, protected, retained and securely disposed of according to business requirements, legal obligations and governance policies while preserving authenticity, integrity, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Records Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
