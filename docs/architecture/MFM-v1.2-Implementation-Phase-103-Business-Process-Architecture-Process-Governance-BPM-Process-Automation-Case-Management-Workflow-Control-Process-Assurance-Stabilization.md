# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundred-and-third implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-102 – Integration Governance, API Management, Event Architecture, Interoperability, Workflow Orchestration & Integration Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled business process capability covering business process architecture, process ownership, process hierarchy, process taxonomy, process mapping, process modeling, BPM governance, process lifecycle, process performance, process controls, process automation, workflow control, case management, business rules, decision management, process exceptions, process variants, process optimization, process mining, process monitoring and process assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain business processes that are explicitly owned, consistently modeled, appropriately controlled, measurable, automatable where beneficial and continuously improved without losing accountability or operational resilience.**

# 126. Definition of Ready

A process work item is Ready when:

- Business purpose and outcome are defined.
- Process ownership is established.
- Scope and boundaries are known.
- Inputs, outputs and dependencies are identified.
- Applicable controls, risks, data, systems and roles are understood.
- Performance and assurance requirements are identified.
- Automation or workflow requirements are known where applicable.

---

# 128. Final Process Principle

> **Important business processes must have clear ownership, defined outcomes, visible controls and measurable performance.**

---

# 129. Final Modeling Principle

> **Process models must represent actual responsibilities, decisions, dependencies, controls and exception paths sufficiently to support operation and governance.**

---

# 130. Final Automation Principle

> **Automation should simplify and strengthen a well-understood process rather than conceal poor process design.**

---

# 131. Final Workflow Principle

> **Workflow execution must make ownership, state, deadlines, escalation and exceptions visible and controllable.**

---

# 132. Final Case Principle

> **Case management must preserve accountability, evidence, decisions and outcome traceability throughout the case lifecycle.**

---

# 133. Final Decision Principle

> **Material decisions must have defined authority, traceable inputs and controlled decision logic.**

---

# 134. Final Exception Principle

> **Process exceptions must be visible, owned and analyzed so that recurring exceptions become opportunities for structural improvement.**

---

# 135. Final Assurance Principle

> **Process assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that important processes operate according to approved design, controls, performance expectations and governance requirements.**

---

# 136. Final Integration Principle

> **Process Governance must integrate with Enterprise Architecture, Data Governance, Integration, Security, Privacy, Service Management, Risk, Resilience, Project, Grant, Membership and Enterprise Assurance.**

---

# 137. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should govern business processes through a controlled lifecycle connecting architecture, ownership, modeling, controls, workflow, case management, rules, decisions, automation, monitoring, optimization and assurance.**
