# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Exchange Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-426.

All Enterprise Data Exchange implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Exchange Architecture governing secure, standardized and interoperable exchange of information between internal systems, external organizations, customers, suppliers, business partners and public authorities.

Enterprise Data Exchange shall

- standardize information exchange
- ensure interoperability
- improve data consistency
- strengthen security
- support regulatory compliance
- reduce integration complexity
- improve scalability
- enable digital collaboration
- support automation
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Exchange shall provide trusted information exchange across the entire Enterprise ecosystem.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- internal data exchange
- external data exchange
- B2B integration
- partner integration
- supplier integration
- customer integration
- government data exchange
- metadata exchange
- document exchange
- machine-to-machine communication

The standard applies regardless of communication technology, infrastructure platform or deployment model.

# 3. Enterprise Data Exchange Principles

Enterprise Data Exchange shall be governed by the following principles.

## Standardization First

Enterprise information exchange shall utilize standardized exchange formats and protocols whenever possible.

---

## Secure by Design

Security controls shall be integrated into every Enterprise Data Exchange mechanism.

---

## Interoperability by Design

Exchange mechanisms shall maximize interoperability across Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Metadata Driven

Exchange definitions shall be governed through standardized Enterprise metadata.

---

## Reusable Exchange Services

Exchange services shall be reusable across multiple Enterprise integration scenarios.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Exchange shall remain independent of vendors, middleware products and transport technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Exchange Architecture establishes the standardized framework for secure, governed and interoperable information exchange across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

By integrating Enterprise APIs, Event Streaming, Data Integration, Data Pipelines, Data Orchestration, Data Fabric, Data Mesh, Lakehouse, Metadata Management, Security, Observability and Automation, the Enterprise enables trusted communication between internal systems, external organizations, customers, suppliers, business partners and public authorities.
