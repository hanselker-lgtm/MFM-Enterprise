# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Knowledge Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Knowledge Management ensures that organizational knowledge is identified, captured, structured, validated, shared, preserved and continuously improved according to business objectives, governance requirements and Enterprise Architecture principles.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Knowledge Capture
- Knowledge Classification
- Knowledge Validation
- Knowledge Sharing
- Knowledge Preservation
- Knowledge Discovery
- Knowledge Reuse
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Knowledge Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## KM-001

Provide standardized enterprise knowledge management.

---

## KM-002

Preserve organizational knowledge.

---

## KM-003

Promote knowledge sharing and reuse.

---

## KM-004

Support governance and regulatory compliance.

---

## KM-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Knowledge Management Principles

Enterprise Knowledge Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Knowledge by Design
- Structured Knowledge
- Verified Knowledge
- Controlled Accessibility
- Knowledge Reuse
- Complete Traceability
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Knowledge Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Knowledge Management implementations shall handle knowledge management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify knowledge classification failures
- classify knowledge capture failures
- classify knowledge validation failures
- classify knowledge sharing failures
- classify knowledge preservation failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Knowledge Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, knowledge integrity, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Knowledge Management implementations may depend upon

- approved information lifecycle services
- approved records management services
- approved data governance services
- approved security services
- approved storage infrastructure
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Knowledge Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external knowledge management providers

Knowledge Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Knowledge Management implementation is compliant when

- Knowledge classification is documented.
- Knowledge capture procedures are implemented.
- Knowledge validation is performed.
- Knowledge sharing is controlled.
- Knowledge preservation procedures are documented.
- Knowledge discovery capabilities are implemented.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Knowledge Classification

Enterprise knowledge shall never exist without an approved knowledge classification.

---

## Unvalidated Knowledge

Knowledge shall never be published without documented validation.

---

## Uncontrolled Knowledge Sharing

Enterprise knowledge shall never be shared outside approved governance and security procedures.

---

## Knowledge Loss

Critical organizational knowledge shall never be lost due to missing preservation procedures.

---

## Inadequate Knowledge Accessibility

Enterprise knowledge shall never be stored in a manner that prevents authorized discovery and reuse.

---

## Knowledge Management Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent knowledge management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Knowledge Management services.

---

# 26. Governance

Architecture Review shall verify

- knowledge preservation compliance

- operational readiness
- governance compliance
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Knowledge Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Knowledge Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that organizational knowledge is identified, captured, validated, shared, preserved and continuously improved according to business objectives, governance requirements and Enterprise Architecture while maintaining integrity, traceability and compliance.

All Enterprise Knowledge Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
