# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the twenty-eighth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-27 – Enterprise Human Resources, Workforce Management, Roles, Competence, Training, Performance & People Governance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for business continuity, operational resilience, disaster recovery, crisis management, emergency response, recovery planning, continuity testing and post-event improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must remain capable of protecting critical services, people, information, assets and organizational objectives during disruption and must recover critical capabilities within defined and approved recovery requirements.**

# 112. Definition of Ready

A continuity, resilience, crisis or recovery work item is Ready when:

- The critical service, process, disruption scenario, dependency, recovery requirement, plan, exercise, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, criticality, RTO, RPO, MBCO, dependencies, risks and applicable requirements are known.
- Response, recovery, communications, evidence, validation and acceptance requirements are defined.

---

# 113. Definition of Done

A continuity, resilience, crisis or recovery work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement Identified
        ↓
Owner / Authority Assigned
        ↓
Continuity / Recovery Action Completed
        ↓
Operational / Technical / Security / Supplier Validation Completed
        ↓
Plans / Records / Recovery Evidence Updated
        ↓
Exercise / Test Results Recorded Where Applicable
        ↓
Exceptions / Findings Addressed
        ↓
Outcome Accepted
```
