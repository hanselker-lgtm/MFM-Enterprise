# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Transformation Management Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise transformation as a strategic capability.

The Transformation Management Architecture Framework shall provide:

- transformation principles
- transformation governance
- transformation structures
- change management
- transformation portfolio management
- transition management
- benefits management
- transformation improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise transformations are aligned with strategic objectives, architecture direction and business value.

---

# 2. Scope

The Transformation Management Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture transformation domains:

- Enterprise Transformation Model
- Transformation Governance
- Transformation Strategy
- Transformation Domains
- Change Management
- Transformation Portfolio
- Program Management
- Project Management Alignment
- Transition Architecture
- Transformation Roadmaps
- Benefits Management
- Transformation Performance
- Transformation Risk Management
- Transformation Maturity
- Transformation Evolution

The framework applies to:

- strategic transformations
- business transformations
- digital transformations
- organizational changes
- technology transformations
- process improvements
- architecture transitions

---

# 3. Enterprise Transformation Management Architecture Principles

The Transformation Management Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategy Driven Transformation

Transformation initiatives shall be directly connected to Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Value-Based Transformation

Transformation shall be managed according to expected business value and measurable outcomes.

---

## Principle 3 – Controlled Change

All transformation activities shall follow structured governance and change management practices.

---

## Principle 4 – Architecture Alignment

Transformation initiatives shall align with Enterprise Architecture principles and target states.

---

## Principle 5 – Stakeholder Engagement

Transformation shall actively involve affected stakeholders and organizational participants.

---

## Principle 6 – Measurable Benefits

Transformation success shall be evaluated through measurable benefits and outcomes.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

Transformation capability shall continuously mature according to changing Enterprise needs.

---

# 9. Transformation Domain Architecture

The Transformation Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise transformation activities are structured into logical domains to support governance, planning and execution.

Transformation domains provide a common structure for organizing change activities, responsibilities and transformation capabilities.

---

# 9.1 Transformation Domain Principles

Transformation domains shall ensure:

- clear transformation ownership
- structured change management
- strategic alignment
- controlled execution
- measurable outcomes

---

# 9.2 Transformation Domain Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Transformation Domain | Logical grouping of transformation activities |
| Transformation Owner | Accountability for transformation area |
| Transformation Objectives | Defines expected outcomes |
| Transformation Capabilities | Defines required abilities |
| Transformation Measures | Evaluates progress |

---

# 9.3 Transformation Domain Lifecycle

```text

        |

        |

Assign Transformation Ownership

        |

        |

        |

# 15. Transformation Roadmap Management

Transformation roadmaps provide visibility of the journey from current Enterprise state to desired future state.

- strategic alignment
