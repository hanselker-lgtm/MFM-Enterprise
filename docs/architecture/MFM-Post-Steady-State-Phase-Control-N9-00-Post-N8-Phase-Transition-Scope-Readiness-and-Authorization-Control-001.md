The following N8 closure conditions remain protected:

```text
CLOSE-N8-01

CLOSE-N8-02
N8 authorization conditions remain applicable to residual N8-derived activity.

CLOSE-N8-03
Material strategy, architecture, portfolio or investment changes shall use the applicable future governance mechanism.

CLOSE-N8-08

CLOSE-N8-09
N8 closure shall not automatically authorize a future phase.

CLOSE-N8-10
Any future phase requires separate scope, authorization and completion criteria.
```

# 5. N9 Purpose

N9 is not automatically defined by N8 closure.

N9-00 therefore establishes a controlled readiness gate before any N9 scope is approved.

The purpose is to determine:

```text
Whether N9 is required
What N9 should accomplish
What N9 should not include
Which N8 outputs are relevant
Which N8 conditions carry forward
What authority is required
What evidence is required
What completion criteria are required
```
