## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory procedural verification layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-363, determining whether the substantive validation of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-362 was correctly performed, evidenced, authorized, decided, recorded and implemented.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-362 determines whether RG-361 verification is substantively effective. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-363 verifies that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-362's validation of that verification was correctly performed and implemented.

```text
RG-360 → VALIDATE
RG-361 → VERIFY
RG-362 → VALIDATE
RG-363 → VERIFY
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-362 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-363 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
WAS EA-IMETA-PC-RG-362 CORRECTLY PERFORMED?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-362 validation SHALL NOT automatically prove that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-362 was correctly verified.
