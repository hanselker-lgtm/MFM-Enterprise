This standard defines the Enterprise Data Provenance Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

All Enterprise Data Provenance implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Provenance Architecture ensuring that the origin, authenticity, ownership, custody and historical evolution of Enterprise information can be continuously documented, verified and trusted throughout its lifecycle.

Enterprise Data Provenance shall

- establish trusted information origin
- verify authenticity
- document ownership
- maintain complete change history
- support chain of custody
- improve regulatory compliance
- strengthen governance
- increase information trust
- support forensic investigations
- enable continuous verification

Enterprise Data Provenance shall provide the architectural foundation for trusted Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise master data
- transactional systems
- operational databases
- data warehouses
- lakehouses
- integration platforms
- APIs
- event streams
- analytical platforms
- archived Enterprise information

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, cloud provider, storage technology or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Data Provenance Principles

Enterprise Data Provenance shall be governed by the following principles.

## Provenance by Design

Every Enterprise information asset shall have documented provenance from creation through disposal.

---

## Trusted Origin

The original source of Enterprise information shall be identifiable and verifiable.

---

## Immutable History

Historical provenance records shall be protected against unauthorized modification.

---

## Metadata Driven

Provenance information shall be maintained through standardized Enterprise metadata.

---

## Continuous Verification

Enterprise information provenance shall be continuously validated throughout the information lifecycle.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Provenance Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, cloud providers, databases and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Provenance Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for documenting, protecting and verifying the origin, ownership, authenticity and historical evolution of Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating provenance capture, trusted source authentication, ownership management, authenticity verification, metadata management, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform delivers trusted information suitable for governance, regulatory compliance, operational excellence and informed decision-making.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Provenance initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing provenance capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Data Provenance Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted provenance verification, autonomous trust assessment, decentralized provenance registries, cryptographic chain-of-custody verification, distributed ledger-based provenance evidence, semantic provenance intelligence, digital twin integration and post-quantum protected authenticity validation.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Provenance continues to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven trust management across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
