# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Ecosystem Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how humans, organisations, processes, data, technologies, platforms, Artificial Intelligence capabilities and external ecosystem participants operate together as one integrated Intelligence ecosystem.

The Intelligence Ecosystem Architecture Framework shall provide:

- ecosystem architecture principles
- Intelligence ecosystem frameworks
- ecosystem capability models
- human Intelligence integration models
- Artificial Intelligence integration models
- cognitive collaboration models
- Intelligence network structures
- partner ecosystem structures
- marketplace structures
- platform ecosystem models
- governance ecosystem models
- assurance ecosystem models
- security ecosystem models
- evolution ecosystem models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for an interconnected Intelligence ecosystem where all participants contribute to shared Intelligence capability development.

# 3. Intelligence Ecosystem Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Ecosystem Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Ecosystem Integration

All Intelligence participants and capabilities shall operate as a connected ecosystem.

---

## Principle 2 – Human and Artificial Intelligence Collaboration

Human expertise and Artificial Intelligence capability shall complement each other.

---

## Principle 3 – Shared Intelligence Value

The ecosystem shall create shared value through cooperation.

---

## Principle 4 – Open Capability Development

The ecosystem shall support scalable capability growth.

---

## Principle 5 – Secure Ecosystem Operation

All ecosystem interactions shall operate within defined security boundaries.

---

## Principle 6 – Trusted Ecosystem Relationships

Ecosystem participants shall operate through trust-based relationships.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Ecosystem Evolution

The Intelligence ecosystem shall continuously adapt and develop.
