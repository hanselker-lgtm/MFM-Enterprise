# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Decision Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities transform knowledge, analysis, context and reasoning outcomes into controlled, explainable and strategically aligned decisions.

The Intelligence Decision Architecture Framework shall provide:

- decision architecture principles
- intelligence decision frameworks
- decision capability models
- decision intelligence structures
- cognitive decision models
- strategic decision models
- operational decision models
- autonomous decision models
- human-AI collaboration models
- decision governance model
- decision assurance framework
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for advanced Intelligence decision capabilities through controlled, explainable and continuously improving decision ecosystems.

---

The Intelligence Decision Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Decision Model

# 3. Intelligence Decision Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Decision Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Decision Transparency

Decision processes shall be understandable and explainable.

---

## Principle 2 – Evidence-Based Decisions

Decisions shall be based on available knowledge, evidence and analysis.

---

## Principle 3 – Strategic Alignment

Decisions shall support enterprise objectives.

---

## Principle 4 – Human Responsibility

Human accountability shall remain defined where required.

---

## Principle 5 – Adaptive Decision Capability

Decision capability shall improve through learning and experience.

---

## Principle 6 – Decision Assurance

Decision quality shall be continuously evaluated.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Decision Evolution

Decision capability shall continuously develop.
