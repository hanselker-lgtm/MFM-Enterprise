# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise records management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise records management ensures that organizational records are created, classified, retained, protected, archived and disposed of through standardized enterprise processes while preserving governance, accountability, auditability, legal compliance and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Records Management
- Record Creation
- Record Classification
- Record Retention
- Record Protection
- Record Archiving
- Record Disposal
- Records Governance

All enterprise records management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Records Management Principles

Enterprise records management shall follow these principles.

- Records by Design
- Governance by Default
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Auditability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Records management implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Records Management Domains

Enterprise records management shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Business Records
- Financial Records
- Operational Records
- Technical Records
- Compliance Records
- Security Records
- Project Records
- Support Records

Additional records domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Records Management Ownership

Each records management domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- records stewardship

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the records lifecycle.

---

# 7. Records Management Governance

Enterprise records management governance shall define

- records governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- records verification
- governance reporting

Records management governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Records Management Responsibilities

Enterprise records management shall provide controlled management of enterprise records.

Records management responsibilities shall

- separate records management from business execution
- coordinate records ownership
- ensure records consistency
- validate records objectives
- preserve records traceability
- support enterprise governance

Records management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Record Creation

Enterprise records management shall implement standardized record creation.

Record creation shall

- define record purpose
- identify record ownership
- classify record type
- establish record metadata
- preserve creation history
- maintain creation traceability

Record creation shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Record Classification

Enterprise records management shall classify all enterprise records.

Record classifications shall

- define record category
- define confidentiality level
- define retention requirements
- define ownership
- preserve classification history
- maintain classification traceability

Record classifications shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Record Retention

Enterprise records management shall implement standardized record retention.

Record retention shall

- define retention periods
- preserve business records
- preserve compliance records
- preserve audit records
- maintain retention traceability
- support legal compliance

Record retention shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Record Protection

Enterprise records management shall implement standardized record protection.

Record protection shall

- protect record integrity
- protect confidentiality
- protect availability
- preserve protection history
- maintain protection traceability
- support regulatory compliance

Record protection shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Records Management Dependencies

Enterprise records management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- document management systems
- storage platforms
- archival systems
- enterprise repositories
- enterprise infrastructure

Records management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Records Documentation

Each records management domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- records registers
- ownership information
- record classifications
- retention schedules
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Records Lifecycle

Enterprise records management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Created
- Classified
- Approved
- Active
- Protected
- Retained
- Archived
- Reviewed
- Disposed
- Destroyed

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Records Quality Attributes

Enterprise records management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- integrity
- governance compliance
- records reliability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the records lifecycle.

---

# 17. Records Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized records register.

The records register shall contain

- record identifiers
- ownership assignments
- classifications
- lifecycle status
- retention status
- documentation references
- review schedules
- governance status

The records register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise records.

---

# 18. Records Reviews

Enterprise records management implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- records completeness
- records consistency
- classification accuracy
- retention compliance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Records Metrics

Enterprise records management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- active records
- archived records
- overdue record reviews
- retention compliance
- disposal compliance
- records revisions
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous records management improvement.

---

# 20. Records Verification

Enterprise records management implementations shall undergo formal verification before operational use and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm records completeness
- verify classifications
- verify ownership
- verify retention schedules
- verify protection mechanisms
- verify documentation completeness
- confirm governance compliance
- approve operational use

Records verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Records Management Improvement

Enterprise records management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve records quality
- improve governance
- improve record protection
- improve regulatory compliance
- improve operational continuity
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise records management implementations shall handle records-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify record creation failures
- classify record classification failures
- classify record retention failures
- classify record protection failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Records management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, regulatory compliance, governance or auditability.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Records management implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved document management systems
- approved storage platforms
- approved archival systems
- approved enterprise repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Records management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external records management services

Records management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A records management implementation is compliant when

- Records management responsibilities are documented.
- Record creation procedures are implemented.
- Record classifications are documented.
- Record retention policies are implemented.
- Record protection mechanisms are documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Records Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Records verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Record Ownership

Enterprise records shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Missing Record Classification

Enterprise records shall never be stored without an approved classification.

---

## Missing Retention Schedule

Enterprise records shall never exist without documented retention requirements.

---

## Unauthorized Record Disposal

Enterprise records shall never be deleted, destroyed or disposed of without approved governance procedures.

---

## Undocumented Records Dependencies

Records management implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Records Management Outside Governance

Records management implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise records management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- records management quality
- records completeness
- classification integrity
- retention compliance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational continuity
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Records Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise records management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise records are created, classified, retained, protected, archived and disposed of through standardized lifecycle management while preserving governance, accountability, legal compliance, auditability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise records management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
