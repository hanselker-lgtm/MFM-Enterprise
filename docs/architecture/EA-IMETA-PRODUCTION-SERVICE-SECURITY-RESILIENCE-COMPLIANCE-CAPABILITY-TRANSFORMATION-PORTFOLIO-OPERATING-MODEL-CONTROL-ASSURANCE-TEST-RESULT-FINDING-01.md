# 1. PURPOSE

Establish the authoritative finding-management architecture for deficiencies identified through portfolio operating-model assurance testing, including classification, severity, materiality, root cause, risk, ownership, remediation, acceptance, escalation, retesting and closure.

# 2. FINDING PRINCIPLE

EA-IMETA SHALL TREAT EACH MATERIAL FINDING AS A GOVERNED, TRACEABLE RISK OBJECT LINKED TO ITS TEST RESULT, CONTROL, RISK, EVIDENCE, OWNER, REMEDIATION AND CLOSURE.

# 3. SCOPE

The finding model covers control failures, design deficiencies, evidence deficiencies, authority breaches, coverage gaps, timeliness failures, systemic issues, isolated exceptions, residual-risk issues and recurring findings.

# 4. FINDING INDEPENDENCE

Material findings shall be reviewed by an appropriately independent authority. Finding closure shall not be solely self-certified by the party responsible for remediation where independence is required.

# 5. FINDING LIFECYCLE

IDENTIFY → VALIDATE → CLASSIFY → RATE → ASSIGN → CONTAIN → REMEDIATE → EVIDENCE → RETEST → ACCEPT → CLOSE.

# 6. FINDING CLASSIFICATION

Findings shall distinguish design deficiency, operating failure, evidence deficiency, authority breach, coverage gap, timeliness failure, systemic issue, isolated exception and residual-risk issue.

# 7. SEVERITY

Severity shall reflect potential or actual impact, likelihood, scope, recurrence, control criticality, regulatory significance and residual risk.

# 8. MATERIALITY

Materiality shall determine escalation, management attention, remediation priority, independence and acceptance authority.

# 9. ROOT CAUSE

Findings shall seek systemic root causes rather than treating symptoms as sufficient remediation targets.

# 10. REMEDIATION

Every material finding shall have an accountable owner, corrective action, target date, evidence requirement and validation method.

# 11. RISK ACCEPTANCE

Residual risk may only be accepted by authorized governance within defined risk appetite and for an explicit period where applicable.

# 12. ESCALATION

Critical or materially overdue findings shall be escalated according to defined thresholds and governance authority.

# 13. RETEST

Finding closure requires evidence that corrective action has been implemented and that the relevant control or process is effective.

# 14. RECURRING FINDINGS

Repeated or reopened findings shall trigger systemic analysis and may require portfolio-level intervention.

# 15. AI FINDING PRINCIPLE

AI may assist in finding detection, classification, correlation or summarization, but authoritative finding creation, severity and closure require governed validation.

# 16. AGENT FINDING PRINCIPLE

Agent-generated findings shall preserve agent identity, authority, tools, data sources, execution context, evidence and validation status.

# 17. AUDITABILITY

Finding history shall preserve creation, classification, decisions, changes, remediation, evidence, retesting and closure sufficiently to reconstruct the full lifecycle.
