# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Assurance Evolution Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive assurance evolution architecture for ensuring that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are continuously validated, monitored, improved and aligned with organisational requirements.

The Intelligence Assurance Evolution Architecture Framework shall provide:

- assurance principles
- assurance governance
- continuous assurance architecture
- Intelligence quality assurance
- AI validation architecture
- compliance assurance
- performance assurance
- security assurance integration
- trust assurance integration
- resilience assurance integration
- assurance measurement
- assurance maturity
- assurance evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities remain reliable, controlled, measurable and continuously improving.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Assurance Evolution Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture assurance evolution domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Assurance Evolution Model
- Assurance Governance
- Continuous Assurance Architecture
- Intelligence Quality Assurance
- AI Validation Architecture
- Compliance Assurance
- Performance Assurance
- Security Assurance Integration
- Trust Assurance Integration
- Resilience Assurance Integration
- Assurance Metrics
- Assurance Maturity

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- autonomous systems
- decision intelligence environments
- future Intelligence ecosystems

# 3. Intelligence Assurance Evolution Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Assurance Evolution Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Continuous Assurance

Enterprise Intelligence shall be continuously evaluated and improved.

---

## Principle 2 – Evidence-Based Assurance

Assurance activities shall be based on measurable evidence.

---

## Principle 3 – Integrated Assurance

Assurance shall integrate governance, security, trust, resilience and sustainability.

---

## Principle 4 – Quality by Design

Quality requirements shall be incorporated from the beginning.

---

## Principle 5 – Independent Validation

Critical Intelligence capabilities shall be validated objectively.

---

## Principle 6 – Transparent Assurance

Assurance results shall be understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

Assurance capability shall continuously improve through learning and experience.
