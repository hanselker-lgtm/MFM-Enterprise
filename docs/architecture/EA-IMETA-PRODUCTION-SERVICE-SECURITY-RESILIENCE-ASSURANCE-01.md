# 1. PURPOSE

EA-IMETA-PRODUCTION-SERVICE-SECURITY-RESILIENCE-ASSURANCE-01 establishes the assurance layer above Security-Resilience Governance.

It determines whether the governed capabilities:

```text
EXIST
ARE IMPLEMENTED
OPERATE
ARE EFFECTIVE
REMAIN WITHIN AUTHORITY
REDUCE RISK
SUPPORT RESILIENCE
ADAPT SAFELY
PRODUCE EVIDENCE
```

The assurance chain is:

```text
REQUIREMENT
 ↓
CONTROL
 ↓
IMPLEMENTATION
 ↓
OPERATION
 ↓
EVIDENCE
 ↓
TEST
 ↓
ASSESSMENT
 ↓
FINDING
 ↓
REMEDIATION
 ↓
VERIFICATION
 ↓
ASSURANCE
```

# 2. ASSURANCE PRINCIPLE

> EA-IMETA SHALL PROVIDE RISK-BASED, EVIDENCE-DRIVEN AND SUFFICIENTLY INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE THAT SECURITY, RESILIENCE AND ADAPTIVE CONTROLS ARE DESIGNED, IMPLEMENTED, OPERATING AND EFFECTIVE.
