# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise capacity management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Capacity management ensures that enterprise resources are planned, monitored, forecasted and optimized in a consistent manner while maintaining performance, scalability, operational stability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture principles.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Capacity Management
- Capacity Planning
- Capacity Forecasting
- Resource Utilization
- Performance Thresholds
- Capacity Monitoring
- Capacity Optimization
- Capacity Governance
- Compliance

All enterprise capacity management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## CAP-001

Provide standardized enterprise capacity management processes.

---

## CAP-002

Ensure sufficient capacity for current and future enterprise workloads.

---

## CAP-003

Optimize utilization of enterprise resources.

---

## CAP-004

Support proactive capacity planning and scalability.

---

## CAP-005

Maintain full compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Capacity Management Principles

Enterprise capacity management shall follow these principles.

- Capacity as a Managed Enterprise Resource
- Proactive Planning
- Continuous Monitoring
- Forecast Before Expansion
- Resource Optimization
- Performance by Design
- Governance by Default
- Continuous Improvement

Capacity management shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Capacity Categories

Enterprise capacity shall be organized into standardized categories.

Categories shall include

- Compute Capacity
- Storage Capacity
- Network Capacity
- Database Capacity
- Application Capacity
- Integration Capacity
- Operational Capacity
- Security Capacity

Additional capacity categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Capacity Ownership

Each enterprise capacity domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- planning responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the capacity lifecycle.

---

# 7. Capacity Governance

Enterprise capacity governance shall define

- capacity governance
- planning governance
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- capacity approval
- governance reporting

Capacity governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Capacity Responsibilities

Enterprise capacity management shall provide controlled coordination of enterprise capacity planning.

Capacity responsibilities shall

- separate capacity management from operational execution
- coordinate capacity ownership
- ensure capacity consistency
- validate capacity planning
- preserve capacity traceability
- support enterprise scalability

Capacity management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Capacity Planning

Enterprise capacity shall be planned using standardized methodologies.

Capacity planning shall

- evaluate current utilization
- identify future demand
- establish capacity targets
- support scalability planning
- optimize resource allocation
- preserve planning traceability

Capacity planning shall remain consistent across the enterprise.

---

# 10. Capacity Forecasting

Enterprise capacity management shall maintain controlled capacity forecasting.

Capacity forecasting shall

- predict workload growth
- estimate future resource requirements
- evaluate seasonal demand
- identify scaling requirements
- support budgeting
- maintain forecasting history

Capacity forecasts shall remain under governance control.

---

# 11. Resource Utilization

Enterprise resource utilization shall be continuously evaluated.

Resource utilization shall

- measure compute utilization
- measure storage utilization
- measure network utilization
- measure database utilization
- identify bottlenecks
- support optimization initiatives

Resource utilization shall ensure efficient enterprise operations.

---

# 12. Capacity Monitoring

Enterprise capacity management shall maintain continuous monitoring.

Capacity monitoring shall

- monitor utilization trends
- monitor threshold violations
- detect capacity risks
- support proactive scaling
- preserve monitoring history
- provide enterprise reporting

Capacity monitoring shall remain continuously active.

---

# 13. Capacity Dependencies

Enterprise capacity management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- infrastructure management
- configuration management
- release management
- observability services
- monitoring platforms
- enterprise governance

Capacity management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Capacity Documentation

Each enterprise capacity domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- capacity descriptions
- ownership information
- planning assumptions
- forecasting history
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Capacity Lifecycle

Enterprise capacity management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Identified
- Assessed
- Planned
- Forecasted
- Approved
- Implemented
- Monitored
- Optimized
- Reviewed
- Retired

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Capacity Quality Attributes

Enterprise capacity management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- scalability
- reliability
- availability
- consistency
- traceability
- auditability
- predictability
- maintainability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the capacity lifecycle.

---

# 17. Capacity Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized capacity registry.

The registry shall contain

- capacity identifiers
- ownership assignments
- capacity categories
- lifecycle status
- utilization history
- forecasting history
- documentation references
- governance status

The capacity registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise capacity management.

---

# 18. Capacity Reviews

Enterprise capacity shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- capacity planning quality
- forecasting accuracy
- utilization efficiency
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment
- scalability readiness

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Capacity Metrics

Enterprise capacity management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- utilization rate
- forecast accuracy
- resource efficiency
- capacity growth
- threshold violations
- audit findings
- operational stability
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous capacity improvement.

---

# 20. Capacity Verification

Enterprise capacity implementations shall undergo formal verification before production use and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm planning accuracy
- verify utilization measurements
- verify governance compliance
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve operational readiness

Capacity verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Capacity Improvement

Enterprise capacity management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve forecasting accuracy
- improve utilization efficiency
- reduce capacity bottlenecks
- strengthen governance
- improve scalability
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise capacity management shall handle capacity management exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify capacity planning failures
- classify forecasting inaccuracies
- classify monitoring failures
- classify threshold violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Capacity management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational stability or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Capacity management implementations may depend upon

- approved infrastructure management systems
- approved configuration management systems
- approved monitoring platforms
- approved observability platforms
- approved governance repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Capacity management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external capacity management services

Capacity management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A capacity management implementation is compliant when

- Capacity responsibilities are documented.
- Capacity planning follows enterprise standards.
- Capacity forecasting has been completed.
- Capacity monitoring is implemented.
- Resource utilization is continuously evaluated.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Capacity Registry is maintained.
- Capacity verification has been completed.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Audit logging is enabled where applicable.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Reactive Capacity Planning

Capacity planning shall never rely solely on operational failures before action is taken.

---

## Missing Forecasting

Capacity expansions shall never occur without documented forecasting and analysis.

---

## Ignored Threshold Violations

Capacity threshold violations shall never remain unresolved without documented evaluation.

---

## Incomplete Capacity Documentation

Capacity plans shall never exist without sufficient documentation supporting planning assumptions and governance.

---

## Uncontrolled Resource Growth

Enterprise resources shall never grow without governance approval and documented capacity planning.

---

## Missing Monitoring

Production capacity shall never operate without continuous monitoring of utilization and thresholds.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise capacity management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- planning quality
- forecasting accuracy
- monitoring effectiveness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- scalability readiness
- operational stability
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Capacity Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing capacity management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise capacity is consistently planned, forecasted, monitored, optimized and governed while preserving scalability, operational stability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise capacity management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
