This standard defines the Enterprise Identity Governance Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-467.

All Enterprise Identity Governance Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Identity Governance Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed identity lifecycle and access governance across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Identity Governance Services shall

- provide centralized identity governance
- standardize identity lifecycle management
- strengthen access governance
- reduce identity-related risks
- support compliance verification
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure identity traceability
- improve organizational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Identity Governance Services shall provide trusted and reusable identity governance capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise identity governance services
- identity lifecycle management
- identity provisioning
- access governance
- role management
- privileged access governance
- segregation of duties
- access certification
- identity compliance
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Identity Governance Services Principles

Enterprise Identity Governance Services shall follow these principles.

## Identity Governance by Design

Identity governance capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, business processes, technology platforms and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every identity governance activity shall be authenticated, authorized, audited and continuously monitored.

---

## Least Privilege

Users, applications, services and workloads shall receive only the minimum access privileges required to perform authorized business functions.

---

## Metadata Driven Identity Governance

Identities, roles, entitlements, certifications and governance decisions shall be classified and governed using Enterprise metadata to ensure traceability, discoverability and consistent reporting.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Identity Governance Services shall remain independent of vendors, identity governance platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Identity Governance Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed identity lifecycle and access governance across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating identity lifecycle management, identity provisioning, access governance, role management, privileged access governance, segregation of duties, access certification, identity compliance, governance, risk, audit, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables transparent, scalable and policy-driven identity governance while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Identity Governance Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise identity governance capabilities across the Enterprise.

# 34. Future Evolution
