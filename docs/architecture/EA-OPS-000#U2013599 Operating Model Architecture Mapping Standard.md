# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Operating Model Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for defining how the Enterprise operates, delivers value and executes strategy.

The Operating Model Architecture Framework shall provide:

- operating model principles
- operating model governance
- organizational structures
- roles and responsibilities
- process operating models
- capability operating models
- service operating models
- performance operating models
- continuous improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise operating capabilities support strategic objectives, business architecture, services, technology and operational execution.

---

# 2. Scope

The Operating Model Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture operating model domains:

- Enterprise Operating Model
- Operating Model Governance
- Operating Model Strategy
- Organizational Model
- Roles and Responsibilities
- Process Operating Model
- Capability Operating Model
- Service Operating Model
- Decision Operating Model
- Performance Operating Model
- Operational Governance
- Continuous Improvement
- Operating Model Evolution

The framework applies to:

- organizational structures
- operating processes
- business capabilities
- service delivery models
- decision structures
- operational management
- transformation initiatives

---

# 3. Enterprise Operating Model Architecture Principles

The Operating Model Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategy Alignment

The operating model shall directly support Enterprise strategy and business objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Clear Accountability

All operating activities shall have defined ownership, responsibility and authority.

---

## Principle 3 – Capability-Based Organization

The Enterprise shall be structured around required capabilities and value delivery.

---

## Principle 4 – Process Excellence

Processes shall be designed, managed and improved to deliver consistent outcomes.

---

## Principle 5 – Integrated Operations

Operating activities shall work together across organizational boundaries.

---

## Principle 6 – Decision Effectiveness

Decision structures shall enable timely and informed decisions.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

The operating model shall continuously evolve according to changing business requirements.

---

# 9. Organizational Model Architecture

The Organizational Model Architecture defines how Enterprise organizational structures, responsibilities and relationships are designed to support strategic objectives and operational execution.

The organizational model establishes how people, teams and functions collaborate to deliver Enterprise value.

---

# 9.1 Organizational Model Principles

The organizational model shall ensure:

- clear organizational accountability
- effective collaboration
- defined authority structures
- capability alignment
- operational efficiency
