# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the twenty-fourth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-23 – Enterprise Privacy, Data Protection, Records Compliance, Consent, Data Subject Rights & Privacy Assurance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for governance, risk management, compliance, internal controls, audit, assurance, findings, remediation and continual improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must operate within defined governance, risk and compliance boundaries, with effective internal controls, evidence-based assurance and accountable remediation of identified weaknesses.**

# 114. Definition of Ready

A governance, risk, compliance, control, audit or assurance work item is Ready when:

- The risk, obligation, control, audit scope, finding, exception, remediation action, decision or assurance activity is clearly identified.
- Ownership, authority, impact, priority and applicable requirements are known.
- Evidence, validation, reporting, escalation and acceptance requirements are defined.

---

# 115. Definition of Done

A governance, risk, compliance, control, audit or assurance work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement Identified
        ↓
Owner / Authority Assigned
        ↓
Action Completed
        ↓
Assessment / Control / Audit / Compliance Validation Completed
        ↓
Evidence Captured
        ↓
Findings / Exceptions Addressed
        ↓
Decision / Closure Approved
        ↓
Records Updated
```

# 116. Final Governance Principle

> **Governance must make accountability, authority and decision rights explicit for material enterprise activities.**

---

# 117. Final Risk Principle

> **Risk management must identify uncertainty early enough to enable informed decisions and proportionate treatment.**

---

# 118. Final Control Principle

> **Internal controls must be designed around defined risks and objectives and supported by sufficient evidence.**

---

# 119. Final Compliance Principle

> **Applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and organizational obligations must be identified, owned, mapped and monitored.**

---

# 120. Final Audit Principle

> **Audit should provide independent, objective and evidence-based assurance over material governance, risk and control areas.**

---

# 121. Final Assurance Principle

> **Assurance should provide decision-makers with credible evidence about the effectiveness of governance, risk management and controls.**

---

# 122. Final Finding Principle

> **Findings must address underlying causes and remain open until remediation is evidenced and validated.**

---

# 123. Final Exception Principle

> **Exceptions must be explicit, risk-assessed, approved, time-bounded and periodically reviewed.**

---

# 124. Final Evidence Principle

> **Material governance, risk, compliance and control decisions must be supported by reliable, traceable and appropriately retained evidence.**

---

# 125. Final Improvement Principle

> **Risk events, control failures, audit findings, compliance issues and assurance results must continuously improve MFM governance and control effectiveness.**

---

# 126. Final Integration Principle

> **Enterprise Governance, Risk, Compliance, Internal Controls, Audit and Assurance must integrate with Cybersecurity, Privacy, Data Management, Technical Operations, Service Management, Finance, Procurement, Supplier Management, Business Continuity, Architecture and People Management.**

---

# 127. Final Steady-State Governance Principle

> **MFM must operate within defined governance, risk and compliance boundaries, with effective internal controls, evidence-based assurance and accountable remediation of identified weaknesses.**
