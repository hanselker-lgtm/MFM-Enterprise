# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Privilege Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Privilege Services shall follow these principles.

## Privilege by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include privilege considerations from initial design through operational lifecycle.

---

## Least Privilege Authority

Privileges shall provide only the authority required to perform approved activities.

---

## Controlled Privilege Assignment

Privileges shall be assigned through approved governance processes.

---

## Privilege Ownership

Every privilege shall have defined ownership, responsibility and accountability.

---

## Privilege Lifecycle Management

Privileges shall be created, assigned, reviewed, modified and removed through controlled lifecycle processes.

---

## Governed Privilege Management

Privilege activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.

---

## Continuous Privilege Improvement

Privilege capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
