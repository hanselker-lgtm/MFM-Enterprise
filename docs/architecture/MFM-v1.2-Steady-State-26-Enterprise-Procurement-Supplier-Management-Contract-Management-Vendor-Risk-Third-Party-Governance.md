# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the twenty-sixth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-25 – Enterprise Financial Management, Budgeting, Accounting, Procurement, Cost Management & Financial Control.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for procurement, supplier management, contract management, vendor risk, third-party access, supplier assurance, performance management and supplier lifecycle governance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM third-party relationships must be selected, contracted, onboarded, governed, monitored, assured and exited according to business need, risk, value, security, privacy, compliance and contractual requirements.**

# 3. Procurement Objective

# 4. Third-Party Governance Objective

# 118. Definition of Ready

A procurement, supplier or contract work item is Ready when:

- The requirement, supplier, sourcing event, contract, risk, performance issue, access, finding, exception, renewal or exit is clearly identified.
- Ownership, criticality, budget, authority, risk, dependencies and applicable obligations are known.
- Procurement, contract, security, privacy, continuity, evidence and acceptance requirements are defined.

---
