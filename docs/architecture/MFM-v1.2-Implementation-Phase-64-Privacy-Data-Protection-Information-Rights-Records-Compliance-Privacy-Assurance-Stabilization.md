# 1. Purpose

This document defines the sixty-fourth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-63 – Security Operations, Identity, Access, Vulnerability, Threat Detection & Cybersecurity Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled privacy, data protection, information rights, records compliance and privacy assurance capability.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must process personal and protected information lawfully, fairly, transparently and securely throughout its lifecycle while maintaining controlled information rights, retention, deletion, records governance and evidence-based privacy assurance.**

# 148. Final Privacy Principle

> **Personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly, transparently and only to the extent necessary for defined purposes.**

---

# 149. Final Data Minimization Principle

> **MFM should collect and retain only the personal information required to achieve legitimate and defined purposes.**

---

# 150. Final Rights Principle

> **Information rights must be supported through controlled, traceable and timely processes that protect both the rights of individuals and legitimate organizational requirements.**

---

# 151. Final DPIA Principle

> **Material privacy risks must be identified and addressed during design and before high-risk processing becomes operational.**

---

# 152. Final Breach Principle

> **Privacy incidents and personal data breaches must be assessed rapidly, contained appropriately and managed according to applicable notification and remediation requirements.**

---

# 153. Final Retention Principle

> **Personal information and records must not be retained longer than justified by defined purposes, legal obligations or documented preservation requirements.**

---

# 154. Final Records Principle

> **Records must remain authentic, reliable, retrievable, protected and appropriately retained or disposed of throughout their lifecycle.**

---

# 155. Final Assurance Principle

> **Privacy assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that privacy requirements, controls, rights processes and data lifecycle mechanisms function as intended.**

---

# 156. Final Integration Principle

> **Privacy and Data Protection must integrate with Security, Data Governance, Service Management, Architecture, Projects, Procurement, Suppliers, Records Management, Risk, Compliance and Enterprise Assurance.**

---

# 157. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage privacy through a controlled lifecycle connecting processing purpose, lawful basis, transparency, data minimization, rights, risk assessment, data sharing, retention, deletion, incident response and continuous privacy assurance.**
