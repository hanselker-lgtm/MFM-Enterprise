## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-verification layer defining how the required state of an applicable mandatory condition is independently or appropriately verified, evidenced, measured and determined. The layer governs verification objectives, scope, authority, independence, planning, execution, evidence, thresholds, failure, revalidation and learning.

## Core Principle
Verification is the controlled act of determining whether the defined required state actually exists. Verification shall test the state and applicable criteria, not merely confirm that an activity was performed.

```text
MANDATORY STATE
      ↓
VERIFICATION OBJECTIVE
      ↓
SCOPE / METHOD / AUTHORITY
      ↓
PRECONDITIONS
      ↓
VERIFICATION EXECUTION
      ↓
EVIDENCE / MEASUREMENT
      ↓
PASS / FAIL / UNDETERMINED
      ↓
REVALIDATION / ESCALATION
```

DEFINED REQUIRED STATE

## Verification Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Required State | State being verified |
| Objective | What verification must establish |
| Scope | Boundaries of verification |
| Method | Approved verification technique |
| Authority | Authorized verifier |
| Independence | Required level of separation |
| Preconditions | Conditions required before execution |
| Evidence | Evidence produced or examined |
| Measurement | Measurement performed |
| Result | Pass, fail, partial or undetermined |
| Determination | Formal verification conclusion |
| Revalidation | Trigger and method for repeat verification |

## Verification Objective

The verification objective shall state precisely what the verification is intended to establish.

```text
REQUIRED STATE
      ↓
VERIFICATION OBJECTIVE
      ↓
WHAT MUST BE PROVEN?
```

## Verification Scope

Scope shall define the systems, services, processes, controls, data, environments, time period and conditions included in the verification. Scope exclusions shall be explicit and justified.

## Verification Method

Verification methods may include inspection, observation, test, measurement, sampling, reconciliation, independent assessment, automated validation or other approved techniques. The method shall be appropriate to the risk and required state.

## Verification Authority

The verifier shall have authority to perform the verification and record the determination. Authority to verify shall not automatically imply authority to alter the requirement being verified.

## Verification Independence

Independence shall be proportionate to materiality. Critical requirements shall receive sufficient separation from the person or mechanism that created or operated the state where independence is required.

## Verification Planning

Verification planning shall identify objective, scope, method, evidence, resources, timing, dependencies, risks, acceptance criteria and escalation paths before execution where planning is material.

## Verification Preconditions

Required preconditions shall be satisfied before execution. If a material precondition is absent, verification shall be blocked or explicitly classified as incomplete rather than falsely passed.

## Verification Execution

Execution shall follow the approved method and preserve sufficient records to reconstruct what was tested, when, by whom, under which conditions and with what result.

```text
PLAN
 ↓
READY CHECK
 ↓
EXECUTE
 ↓
CAPTURE EVIDENCE
 ↓
ANALYZE
 ↓
DETERMINE

## Verification Evidence

Evidence shall be attributable, relevant, complete enough for the determination, current and protected against inappropriate alteration. Evidence shall reference the requirement and state versions used.

## Verification Measurement

Quantitative verification shall define data source, measurement method, calibration or validation where relevant, sampling, uncertainty and interpretation.

## Verification Thresholds

Thresholds shall define pass, warning, failure and escalation boundaries where quantitative or categorical thresholds are required.

```text
RESULT
 ↓
PASS THRESHOLD
 ↓
WARNING / AT-RISK
 ↓
FAILURE THRESHOLD
```

## Security Verification

Security verification shall assess applicable security state, controls, configuration, access, vulnerabilities, events and protective measures according to the relevant mandatory requirements.

## Resilience Verification

Resilience verification shall test applicable continuity, recovery, restoration, dependency, capacity and degraded-operation requirements.

## Compliance Verification

Compliance verification shall assess actual conformity with applicable mandatory obligations. Policy existence alone shall not be treated as proof of operational compliance.

## Data Verification

Data verification shall assess applicable accuracy, completeness, integrity, lineage, timeliness, availability, classification and authorized-use requirements.

## AI and Agent Verification

AI and agent verification shall assess required output/state plus authority, autonomy, policy, safety, tool and data-access constraints.

```text
AI / AGENT RESULT
      ↓
CORRECT / REQUIRED STATE?
      ↓
WITHIN AUTHORITY?
      ↓
POLICY / SAFETY COMPLIANT?
      ↓
VERIFIED PASS / FAIL
```

## Verification Failure

A verification failure means the required state has not been demonstrated or a verification requirement has not been satisfied. Material failure shall trigger the defined blocking, containment, remediation and escalation response.

```text
VERIFICATION FAIL
      ↓
BLOCK / CONTAIN
      ↓
ASSESS IMPACT
      ↓
REMEDIATE
      ↓
RE-VERIFY
```

## Verification Revalidation

Revalidation shall use an appropriate verification method after remediation or material change and shall create new evidence. The original verification result shall remain preserved.

## Verification Review and Learning

Verification methods shall be reviewed after repeated false positives, false negatives, disputed results, material failures, architecture changes, criteria changes, technology changes or evidence that the method no longer adequately tests the required state.

## Verification Determination Model
```text
VERIFICATION
   ↓
PRECONDITIONS SATISFIED?
 ├── NO → BLOCKED / INCOMPLETE
 └── YES
      ↓
EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT?
 ├── NO → UNDETERMINED
 └── YES
      ↓
REQUIRED STATE SATISFIED?
 ├── YES → PASS
 └── NO  → FAIL
```

        ↓
