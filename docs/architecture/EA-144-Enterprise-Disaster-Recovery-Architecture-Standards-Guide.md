# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise disaster recovery throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Disaster recovery ensures that enterprise infrastructure, services and data can be restored within approved recovery objectives following major disruptions while preserving operational resilience, business continuity and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Disaster Recovery Planning
- Recovery Architecture
- Recovery Sites
- Backup and Restore Strategy
- Recovery Verification
- Disaster Recovery Testing
- Governance
- Compliance

All enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## DR-001

Provide standardized enterprise disaster recovery.

---

## DR-002

Ensure reliable recovery of enterprise infrastructure and services.

---

## DR-003

Support controlled disaster recovery planning.

---

## DR-004

Maintain operational resilience during catastrophic events.

---

## DR-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 4. Disaster Recovery Principles

Enterprise disaster recovery shall follow these principles.

- Recovery by Design
- Risk-Based Recovery Planning
- Recovery Objective Compliance
- Verified Recovery Capability
- Complete Traceability
- Governance by Default
- Operational Resilience
- Continuous Improvement

Disaster recovery shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Disaster Recovery Categories

Enterprise disaster recovery shall be organized into standardized categories.

Categories shall include

- Infrastructure Recovery
- Platform Recovery
- Database Recovery
- Application Recovery
- Network Recovery
- Storage Recovery
- Backup Recovery
- Disaster Recovery Testing

Additional disaster recovery categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Disaster Recovery Ownership

Each disaster recovery domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- recovery responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the disaster recovery lifecycle.

---

# 7. Disaster Recovery Governance

Enterprise disaster recovery governance shall define

- recovery governance
- disaster recovery approval
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- recovery verification
- governance reporting

Disaster recovery governance shall remain technology independent.

---

# 8. Disaster Recovery Responsibilities

Enterprise disaster recovery shall provide controlled coordination of disaster recovery preparation and execution.

Disaster recovery responsibilities shall

- separate disaster recovery management from operational execution
- coordinate recovery ownership
- ensure recovery consistency
- validate recovery objectives
- preserve recovery traceability
- support enterprise operational resilience

Disaster recovery implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Recovery Architecture

Enterprise disaster recovery shall implement standardized recovery architectures.

Recovery architecture shall

- define recovery topology
- identify recovery infrastructure
- establish recovery dependencies
- validate recovery capabilities
- preserve architecture history
- support resilient recovery operations

Recovery architecture shall remain consistent across the enterprise.

---

# 10. Recovery Sites

Enterprise disaster recovery shall define standardized recovery sites.

Recovery sites shall

- identify primary recovery locations
- identify secondary recovery locations
- define site activation procedures
- preserve site readiness
- support geographical resilience
- maintain recovery traceability

Recovery sites shall remain under governance control.

---

# 11. Backup and Restore Strategy

Enterprise disaster recovery shall support standardized backup and restore procedures.

Backup and restore strategy shall

- define backup frequency
- define retention policies
- validate restore procedures
- support recovery objectives
- preserve backup history
- maintain restore traceability

Backup strategies shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 12. Disaster Recovery Testing

Enterprise disaster recovery shall implement standardized testing procedures.

Disaster recovery testing shall

- validate recovery procedures
- verify recovery objectives
- identify recovery weaknesses
- document test results
- preserve testing history
- maintain testing traceability

Testing shall ensure predictable recovery capability.

---

# 13. Disaster Recovery Dependencies

Enterprise disaster recovery shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- business continuity
- operational readiness
- infrastructure management
- environment management
- monitoring services
- enterprise governance

Disaster recovery implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Disaster Recovery Documentation

Each disaster recovery domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- recovery objectives
- ownership information
- recovery architecture
- backup procedures
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Disaster Recovery Lifecycle

Enterprise disaster recovery shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Planned
- Assessed
- Designed
- Implemented
- Verified
- Approved
- Operational
- Exercised
- Reviewed
- Improved

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Disaster Recovery Quality Attributes

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- resilience
- recoverability
- availability
- reliability
- consistency
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the disaster recovery lifecycle.

---

# 17. Disaster Recovery Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized disaster recovery registry.

The registry shall contain

- recovery identifiers
- ownership assignments
- recovery classifications
- lifecycle status
- recovery objectives
- recovery architecture references
- documentation references
- governance status

The disaster recovery registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise disaster recovery.

---

# 18. Disaster Recovery Reviews

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- recovery quality
- recovery architecture completeness
- backup strategy effectiveness
- recovery objective compliance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Disaster Recovery Metrics

Enterprise disaster recovery shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- recovery success rate
- Recovery Time Objective achievement
- Recovery Point Objective achievement
- backup success rate
- restore success rate
- audit findings
- recovery exercise results
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous disaster recovery improvement.

---

# 20. Recovery Verification

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm recovery objectives
- verify recovery procedures
- verify backup integrity
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve disaster recovery readiness

Recovery verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Disaster Recovery Improvement

Enterprise disaster recovery shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve recovery capability
- improve recovery architecture
- reduce operational risk
- strengthen governance
- improve recovery performance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall handle disaster recovery exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify recovery architecture failures
- classify backup failures
- classify restore failures
- classify disaster recovery testing failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Disaster recovery exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational resilience or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Disaster recovery implementations may depend upon

- approved business continuity systems
- approved operational readiness systems
- approved infrastructure management systems
- approved environment management systems
- approved monitoring systems
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Disaster recovery implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external disaster recovery services

Disaster recovery capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A disaster recovery implementation is compliant when

- Disaster recovery responsibilities are documented.
- Recovery architecture is approved.
- Recovery sites are documented.
- Backup and restore strategies are approved.
- Disaster recovery testing has been completed.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Disaster Recovery Registry is maintained.
- Recovery verification has been completed.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Audit logging is enabled where applicable.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Recovery Architecture

Critical enterprise systems shall never operate without documented disaster recovery architecture.

---

## Untested Recovery Procedures

Recovery procedures shall never be considered valid without documented disaster recovery exercises.

---

## Incomplete Backup Strategy

Enterprise systems shall never rely upon undocumented or unverified backup procedures.

---

## Missing Recovery Verification

Recovery capability shall never be assumed without documented verification and governance approval.

---

## Undocumented Recovery Dependencies

Disaster recovery implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure, application or operational dependencies.

---

## Recovery Outside Governance

Disaster recovery activities shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- recovery quality
- recovery architecture completeness
- backup strategy effectiveness
- recovery objective compliance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational resilience
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Disaster Recovery Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing disaster recovery throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, applications and data can be restored through standardized recovery architectures, backup strategies, verification, governance and continuous improvement while preserving operational resilience and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise disaster recovery implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.

End of Document.
