# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-028 establishes the controlled mechanism for ensuring that the governance system can absorb disruption, maintain critical functions, adapt under changing conditions and recover from systemic disturbance.

The purpose is to move beyond risk identification and establish controlled resilience and adaptive capacity.

The operating sequence is:

```text
SYSTEMIC EXPOSURE
↓
PREPAREDNESS
↓
ABSORPTION
↓
ADAPTATION
↓
CONTINUITY
↓
RECOVERY
↓
RESTORATION
↓
LESSON
↓
CAPABILITY IMPROVEMENT
↓
REASSESSMENT
```

---

The following distinctions are mandatory:

```text
RESILIENCE
≠
PREVENTION

CONTINUITY
≠
NORMAL OPERATION
```
