# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Digital Twin Architecture governing digital representations of business entities, physical assets, operational environments, infrastructure, products, services and business processes across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Digital Twin Architecture shall

- establish standardized digital representations
- improve operational visibility
- enable predictive analysis
- support simulation and optimization
- improve decision-making
- strengthen lifecycle management
- integrate real-time operational data
- support artificial intelligence
- enable enterprise-wide interoperability
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Digital Twins shall function as reusable Enterprise capabilities supporting intelligent operations and digital transformation.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- digital asset twins
- business object twins
- infrastructure twins
- process twins
- equipment twins
- IoT device twins
- operational twins
- simulation models
- lifecycle models
- real-time synchronization

The standard applies regardless of business domain, infrastructure platform or cloud provider.

---

# 3. Enterprise Digital Twin Principles

Enterprise Digital Twin Architecture shall be governed by the following principles.

## Digital Representation by Design

Enterprise assets and business entities shall be designed with standardized digital representations wherever practical.

---

## Real-Time Synchronization

Digital Twins shall maintain synchronized operational state using standardized Enterprise integration mechanisms.

---

## Standardized Information Models

Digital Twins shall use standardized Enterprise data models and metadata definitions.

---

## Event-Driven Updates

Digital Twin state changes shall be propagated through Enterprise Event Architecture wherever practical.

---

## Lifecycle Governance

Digital Twins shall be governed throughout their complete operational lifecycle.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Digital Twin Architecture shall remain independent of individual Digital Twin platforms and vendors.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Digital Twin Architecture establishes the standardized foundation for creating, synchronizing and governing digital representations of Enterprise assets, business entities, infrastructure and operational environments.

By integrating Enterprise Data Architecture, Event Architecture, Artificial Intelligence, Observability, Automation and Analytics, the Enterprise enables intelligent digital models that improve operational awareness, predictive capabilities, decision support and lifecycle management.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Digital Twin initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing Digital Twin implementations across the Enterprise.
