# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Decision Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Business Process Architecture principles are inherited from EA-345.

Enterprise Business Process Services principles are inherited from EA-462.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Decision Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Decision Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and scalable decision capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Decision Services shall

- provide standardized decision services
- support enterprise business rules
- enable intelligent decision execution
- improve operational efficiency
- strengthen interoperability
- support reusable decision capabilities
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- enable continuous optimization
- improve business agility

Enterprise Decision Services shall provide trusted and reusable decision capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- decision services
- business rule services
- decision engines
- decision models
- rule repositories
- decision execution services
- AI-assisted decision services
- cloud services
- hybrid environments
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, decision technology or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Decision Services Principles

Enterprise Decision Services shall follow these principles.

## Decision First

Enterprise Decision Services shall centralize, standardize and automate business decisions across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Secure by Default

Every decision execution shall be authenticated, authorized, protected and continuously monitored.

---

## Standardized Decision Services

Enterprise Decision Services shall expose standardized decision interfaces supporting interoperability throughout the Enterprise.

---

## Metadata Driven Decisions

Decision services shall leverage Enterprise metadata for rule evaluation, decision execution, governance, lifecycle management and optimization.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Decision Services shall remain independent of vendors, rule engines, decision engines, AI platforms, cloud providers and infrastructure technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Decision Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed decision capabilities across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating business rules, decision engines, decision models, AI-assisted decision support, event-driven decision processing, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled decision execution while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Decision Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise decision capabilities across the Enterprise.
