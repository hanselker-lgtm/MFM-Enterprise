# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational service architecture that ensures Intelligence services are designed, delivered, managed, monitored and continuously improved.

The Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- service operating principles
- service design frameworks
- service delivery structures
- service management models
- service catalogue structures
- service level models
- service support structures
- service performance models
- service monitoring structures
- service automation models
- service evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for reliable, measurable and continuously improving Intelligence service delivery.

---

The Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Service Operating Model

# 3. Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Service by Design

Services shall be designed according to defined business and operational requirements.

---

## Principle 2 – Service Value Delivery

Services shall create measurable value for stakeholders.

---

## Principle 3 – Service Reliability

Services shall provide dependable and consistent delivery.

---

## Principle 4 – Service Transparency

Service capabilities and performance shall be visible.

---

## Principle 5 – Service Ownership

Every service shall have clear ownership and responsibility.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Service Improvement

Services shall continuously improve through measurement and learning.

---

## Principle 7 – Service Evolution

Services shall adapt to changing requirements and environments.
