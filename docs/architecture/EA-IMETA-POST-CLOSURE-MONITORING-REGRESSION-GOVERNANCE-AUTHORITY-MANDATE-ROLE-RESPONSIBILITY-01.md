## Purpose
Establish the authoritative responsibility layer beneath role, defining the outcomes, obligations, ownership, interfaces, performance, evidence, risk duties, escalation and review requirements assigned to roles participating in post-closure regression governance.

## Core Principle
Responsibility defines what a role must ensure or deliver under its mandate. Responsibility is distinct from authority: a role may be responsible for an outcome while another role retains the authority to approve or authorize the relevant decision.

```text
ROLE
   ↓
RESPONSIBILITY
   ↓
OUTCOME
   ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
   ↓
CONTROL
   ↓
EVIDENCE
   ↓
PERFORMANCE
   ↓
ESCALATION
   ↓
REVIEW
```

## Responsibility Structure
| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Responsibility | What must be ensured or delivered |
| Outcome | Result for which the responsibility exists |
| Accountable Owner | Role accountable for the outcome |
| Control | Mechanism used to govern the responsibility |
| Evidence | Proof that the responsibility is being performed |
| Performance | Measures and acceptance criteria |
| Interface | Dependencies on other roles or functions |
| Escalation | Conditions requiring intervention |
| Continuity | Backup and handover requirements |
| Review | Frequency and triggers for reassessment |

## Responsibility vs Authority
```text
RESPONSIBLE
    ≠
AUTHORIZED
    ≠
APPROVER
    ≠
EXECUTOR
    ≠
AUDITOR```

A role may be responsible for preparing a decision without possessing authority to approve it. Conversely, an authority holder may approve an outcome while operational responsibility remains with another role.

## Outcome Ownership
Every material responsibility shall identify the outcome it exists to protect or produce.

```text
RESPONSIBILITY
      ↓
EXPECTED OUTCOME
      ↓
MEASURE
      ↓
ACTUAL OUTCOME
      ↓
VARIANCE
      ↓
ESCALATE / CORRECT / ACCEPT```

## Accountability
Accountability is the obligation to answer for the outcome of a responsibility. Delegating execution does not automatically remove accountability from the accountable owner.

## Responsibility Allocation
Responsibilities shall be allocated according to mandate, role competence, independence requirements, lifecycle stage and risk. Allocation shall avoid both gaps and uncontrolled duplication.

```text
REQUIRED OUTCOME
      ↓
IDENTIFY RESPONSIBILITY
      ↓
ASSIGN ROLE
      ↓
CONFIRM COMPETENCE
      ↓
CONFIRM ACCOUNTABILITY
```

## Responsibility Interfaces
Cross-functional responsibilities shall define handoffs, dependencies, inputs, outputs, service levels and escalation points.

```text
ROLE A
  ↓ INPUT
ROLE B
  ↓ OUTPUT
ROLE C
  ↓ ASSURANCE
ROLE D```

## Performance
Material responsibilities shall have measurable performance criteria appropriate to the lifecycle stage. Performance shall distinguish activity completion from outcome effectiveness.

```text
ACTION COMPLETE
     ≠
OUTCOME ACHIEVED
     ≠
CONTROL EFFECTIVE```

## Control Ownership
Every material responsibility shall identify the controls that support its outcome and who owns those controls. Control ownership shall remain explicit through lifecycle transitions.

## Evidence Ownership
Evidence responsibility shall identify who must create, preserve, validate and provide evidence. Evidence ownership shall not be assumed merely because a system generates a record.

## Risk Responsibility
Risk responsibilities shall specify who identifies, assesses, treats, monitors and escalates risk. Risk acceptance remains subject to the applicable authority and mandate.

```text
RISK IDENTIFY
     ↓
ASSESS
     ↓
TREAT
     ↓
MONITOR
     ↓
ESCALATE / ACCEPT UNDER AUTHORITY```

## Security and Resilience Responsibility
Security and resilience responsibilities shall cover relevant prevention, detection, response, recovery, continuity and escalation duties. Systemic risks shall be escalated beyond local responsibility where required.

## Data Responsibility
Data responsibilities shall define ownership for integrity, quality, lineage, access, classification, retention and authorized use where applicable.

## AI and Agent Responsibility
AI and agent operations shall always map to an accountable human or governed organizational role.

```text
AI / AGENT ACTION
      ↓
RESPONSIBILITY ASSIGNED
      ↓
CONTROL APPLIED
      ↓
EVIDENCE CREATED
      ↓
HUMAN / GOVERNED ACCOUNTABILITY```

Technical autonomy does not create independent organizational accountability.

## Compliance Responsibility
Compliance responsibilities shall identify who monitors obligations, maintains evidence, raises non-conformity and escalates material compliance risk.

## Escalation Responsibility
Roles shall have explicit responsibility to escalate when outcomes are at risk, controls fail, materiality thresholds are exceeded, authority is insufficient or conditions change.

```text
RESPONSIBILITY ACTIVE
      ↓
EXPECTED OUTCOME AT RISK?
 ├── NO  → CONTINUE / MONITOR
 └── YES → ESCALATE / CORRECT / RECOVER```

## Delegation and Transfer
Delegated or transferred responsibilities shall define the effective date, scope, receiving role, handover evidence, outstanding actions and return conditions.

```text
CURRENT OWNER
      ↓
TRANSFER DECISION
      ↓
HANDOVER
      ↓
RECEIVING OWNER
      ↓
CONFIRM ACCEPTANCE
      ↓
UPDATE RECORD```

## Responsibility Continuity
Material responsibilities shall have backup coverage and succession arrangements sufficient to prevent governance or operational gaps during absence, vacancy or organizational change.

## Responsibility Failure
```text
RESPONSIBILITY UNASSIGNED / FAILED
        ↓
ASSESS IMPACT
        ↓
CONTAIN / ESCALATE
        ↓
ASSIGN QUALIFIED OWNER
        ↓
RECOVER OUTCOME
        ↓
REVIEW ROOT CAUSE```

## Responsibility Evidence
Evidence shall demonstrate assignment, scope, outcome criteria, performance, control operation, handoffs, escalation and review.

## Responsibility Review
Review shall be triggered by material regression, organizational change, mandate change, role change, repeated performance failure, control failure or changes in risk.

```text
RESPONSIBILITY ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
VALID / EFFECTIVE?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → MODIFY / REASSIGN / ESCALATE```

## Governance Learning
Repeated responsibility failures shall trigger review of role design, mandate clarity, competence, workload, control ownership, interfaces, escalation and accountability.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes responsibility beneath role, mandate and authority for post-closure regression governance. It does not replace those layers or the stage-specific regression lifecycle controls.
