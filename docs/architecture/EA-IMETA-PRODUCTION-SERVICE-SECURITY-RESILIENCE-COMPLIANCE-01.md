# 1. PURPOSE

EA-IMETA-PRODUCTION-SERVICE-SECURITY-RESILIENCE-COMPLIANCE-01 establishes the authoritative compliance capability for production security, resilience, governance, AI, agents, data and service obligations.

Compliance connects external and internal obligations to executable controls and evidence.

The compliance chain is:

```text
OBLIGATION
 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓
COMPLIANCE STATUS
 ↓
CONTINUOUS MONITORING
```

# 47. COMPENSATING CONTROL
