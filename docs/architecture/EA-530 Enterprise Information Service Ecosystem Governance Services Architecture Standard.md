This standard defines the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Governance Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Governance Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed decision-making, accountability and control mechanisms across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Governance Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem governance capabilities
- support ecosystem decision governance
- support partner governance
- support supplier governance
- support collaboration governance
- support integration governance
- support intelligence governance
- support analytics governance
- support automation governance
- support orchestration governance
- support compliance governance
- support risk governance
- support audit governance
- support internal control governance
- support governance lifecycle management
- improve ecosystem transparency
- improve ecosystem accountability
- improve ecosystem coordination
- improve ecosystem value realization

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Governance Services shall provide trusted and reusable governance capabilities enabling controlled ecosystem operations.

# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Governance Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Governance Services shall follow these principles.

## Governance by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include governance mechanisms from the beginning.

---

## Clear Accountability

All ecosystem activities shall have clearly defined ownership, responsibility and accountability.

---

## Transparent Decision-Making

Ecosystem decisions shall be documented, traceable and governed.

---

## Risk-Aware Governance

Governance activities shall consider operational, security, compliance and business risks.

---

## Continuous Governance Improvement

Governance capabilities shall continuously evolve according to ecosystem requirements.
