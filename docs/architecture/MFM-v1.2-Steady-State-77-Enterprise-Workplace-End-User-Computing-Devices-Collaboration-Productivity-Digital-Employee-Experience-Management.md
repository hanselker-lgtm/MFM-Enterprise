# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the seventy-seventh document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-76 – Enterprise Infrastructure, Compute, Network, Cloud, Storage, Platform & Infrastructure Operations Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for workplace services, end-user computing, desktops, laptops, mobile devices, peripherals, endpoint lifecycle, device provisioning, configuration, patching, workplace applications, productivity services, collaboration platforms, communication tools, digital employee experience, workplace support, service desk integration, endpoint monitoring, endpoint security coordination, identity coordination, workplace continuity, remote work, accessibility, employee enablement, workplace incidents, workplace problems, workplace changes, workplace releases, device refresh, asset management, software distribution, workplace assurance, workplace findings, workplace exceptions, workplace remediation, workplace metrics, workplace dashboards, workplace maturity and continual workplace improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must provide secure, reliable, usable and productive digital workplace services that enable employees to perform their roles effectively across office, remote and mobile working environments.**

# 3. Workplace Management Objective

The primary objective is:

# 88. Definition of Ready

A workplace service, device profile, workplace application, collaboration service, device deployment, refresh, change, release, incident, problem, continuity action, experience improvement, assurance review or remediation item is Ready when purpose, owner, scope, user group, dependencies, security, privacy, support, financial implications, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
