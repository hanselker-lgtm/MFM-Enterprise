## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory substantive validation layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290, determining whether the procedural verification of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-289 is itself substantively true, effective, complete and supportable in the actual current operating state.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-289 verifies that RG-288 validation was correctly performed and implemented. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290 validates whether the EA-IMETA-PC-RG-289 verification remains substantively effective and provides reliable assurance.

```text
RG-288 → VALIDATE
RG-289 → VERIFY
RG-290 → VALIDATE
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-289 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
IS THE EA-IMETA-PC-RG-289 VERIFICATION ACTUALLY EFFECTIVE AND TRUE?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-289 verification SHALL NOT automatically establish substantive current effectiveness.
