# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Credential Management Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Identity Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-471.

All Enterprise Credential Management Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Credential Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of credentials across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Credential Management Services shall

- provide centralized credential management
- standardize credential lifecycle management
- strengthen authentication security
- reduce credential-related risks
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure credential traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Credential Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable credential management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

This standard applies to

- enterprise credential management services
- credential lifecycle management

- credential protection
- credential rotation
- secret management
- certificate credentials
- cryptographic keys
- passwords
- privileged credentials

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Credential Management Services Principles

Enterprise Credential Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Credential Security by Design

Credential management capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every credential shall be securely created, stored, protected, monitored and retired according to approved Enterprise security requirements.

---

## Least Credential Privilege

Credentials shall provide only the minimum privileges, scope and lifetime required for authorized activities.

---

## Credential Lifecycle Governance

Credentials shall be continuously managed throughout creation, activation, usage, rotation, suspension and retirement.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Credential Management Services shall remain independent of vendors, credential platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Credential Management Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed credential management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating credential lifecycle management, credential issuance, credential storage, credential protection, credential rotation, secret management, certificate credentials, cryptographic keys, password management, privileged credentials, identity integration, authentication integration, authorization integration, risk, audit, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables secure, scalable and policy-driven credential handling while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Credential Management Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise credential management capabilities across the Enterprise.
