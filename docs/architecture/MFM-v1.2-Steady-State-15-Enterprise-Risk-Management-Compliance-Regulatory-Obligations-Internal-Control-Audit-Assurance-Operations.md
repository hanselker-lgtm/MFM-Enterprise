# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the fifteenth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-14 – Enterprise Privacy, Data Protection, Information Rights, Consent, Personal Data Governance & Privacy Operations.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for risk management, regulatory obligations, compliance, internal control, audit, assurance, findings, remediation, exceptions and governance reporting.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must identify, assess, manage, monitor and assure material risks and obligations through an accountable internal-control framework supported by evidence, independent challenge and continual improvement.**

# 130. Definition of Ready

A risk, compliance or assurance work item is Ready when:

- The risk, obligation, control, audit, finding, exception, assessment or assurance activity is clearly identified.
- Ownership, scope, criticality and applicable requirements are known.
- Criteria, evidence, acceptance, escalation and reporting requirements are defined.

---

# 132. Final Risk Principle

> **MFM must actively manage material risk rather than merely record it.**

---

# 133. Final Appetite Principle

> **Risk acceptance must remain within approved appetite, tolerance and delegated authority.**

---

# 134. Final Compliance Principle

> **Applicable obligations must be identified, assigned, translated into requirements and supported by effective controls and evidence.**

---

# 135. Final Control Principle

> **Material risks and obligations must be supported by appropriately designed, operated and tested controls.**

---

# 136. Final Evidence Principle

> **MFM must be able to demonstrate control execution, compliance and assurance through reliable evidence.**

---

# 137. Final Independence Principle

> **Where independent assurance is required, assessment must maintain sufficient objectivity and independence from the activity being assessed.**

---

# 138. Final Finding Principle

> **Findings must address underlying causes where practical and remain open until implementation and effectiveness have been appropriately validated.**

---

# 139. Final Exception Principle

> **Exceptions must be risk-assessed, approved, time-bounded and periodically reviewed.**

---

# 140. Final Improvement Principle

> **Risk events, control failures, audit findings, compliance assessments and assurance results must continuously strengthen MFM governance and controls.**

---

# 141. Final Integration Principle

> **Risk, Compliance and Assurance must integrate with Service Management, Cybersecurity, Privacy, Data Management, Technical Operations, Business Continuity, Architecture, Supplier Management and Financial Operations.**

---

# 142. Final Steady-State Risk and Assurance Principle

> **MFM must identify, assess, manage, monitor and assure material risks and obligations through an accountable internal-control framework supported by evidence, independent challenge and continual improvement.**
