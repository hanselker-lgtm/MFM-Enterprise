# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Analytics Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed analytical capabilities for collecting, processing, analysing and interpreting ecosystem information across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Analytics Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem analytics capabilities
- support ecosystem data analysis
- support ecosystem performance analytics
- support relationship analytics
- support partner analytics
- support supplier analytics
- support ecosystem trend analysis
- support predictive analytics
- support decision support capabilities
- support analytical dashboards
- support analytical reporting
- support analytical governance
- support analytical lifecycle management
- support AI and machine learning analytics integration
- improve ecosystem understanding
- improve ecosystem transparency
- improve ecosystem decision-making
- improve ecosystem optimization
- support continuous ecosystem improvement

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Analytics Services shall provide trusted and reusable analytical capabilities enabling data-driven ecosystem management.
