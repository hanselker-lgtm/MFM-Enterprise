## Purpose
Establish the overarching governance framework for post-closure regression, defining mandate, scope, policy authority, decision rights, accountability, lifecycle ownership, independent oversight, escalation, risk, evidence and reporting requirements across the complete regression lifecycle.

## Core Principle
Post-closure regression governance determines who may decide, who is accountable, what evidence is required, when escalation is mandatory and how the organization preserves lifecycle integrity from detection through re-closure.

```text
POST-CLOSURE REGRESSION GOVERNANCE
        ↓
MANDATE / POLICY
        ↓
AUTHORITY / ACCOUNTABILITY
        ↓
DECISION RIGHTS
        ↓
CONTROL ORCHESTRATION
        ↓
INDEPENDENT OVERSIGHT
        ↓
ESCALATION
        ↓
ASSURANCE / REPORTING
        ↓
CONTINUOUS GOVERNANCE LEARNING
```

## Governance Decision-Rights Matrix
| Decision area | Primary authority | Required challenge / escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Signal classification | Monitoring owner | Materiality escalation where required |
| Regression assessment | Assessment authority | Independent challenge for material cases |
| Regression decision | Designated decision authority | Risk / governance escalation |
| Execution authorization | Action authority | Scope and risk challenge |
| Verification acceptance | Verification authority | Independence where material |
| Re-validation conclusion | Re-validation authority | Independent review where required |
| Re-acceptance | Governance authority | Formal challenge / escalation |
| Re-closure | Closure authority | Independent closure review where required |
| Reopening | Designated governance authority | Material risk escalation |

## Accountability Model
```text
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
       │
       ├── DECISION AUTHORITY
       ├── EXECUTION OWNER
       ├── ASSURANCE / VERIFICATION
       ├── RISK OWNER
       ├── SECURITY / RESILIENCE OWNER
       └── AUDIT / OVERSIGHT
```

Every material regression shall have one identifiable accountable owner, even when execution and assessment responsibilities are distributed.

## Independence Model
```text
OPERATING TEAM
      ↓
ASSESSMENT
      ↓
INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE
      ↓
GOVERNANCE DECISION
      ↓
ASSURANCE / OVERSIGHT
```

Independence requirements shall increase with materiality, risk, regulatory significance and organizational impact.

## Escalation Governance
```text
REGRESSION EVENT
      ↓
RISK / MATERIALITY      ↓
WITHIN AUTHORITY?
 ├── YES → GOVERNED DECISION
 └── NO  → ESCALATE
              ↓
        HIGHER AUTHORITY
              ↓
        DECISION / EXCEPTION
```

Escalation transfers decision authority where required; it does not erase the originating owner's accountability or evidence obligations.

## Risk Governance
Current risk shall drive governance intensity. Historical risk ratings may inform the assessment but shall not substitute for current-state risk evaluation.

```text
CURRENT RISK
    ↓
RISK APPETITE
    ↓
MATERIALITY
    ↓
GOVERNANCE LEVEL
```

## Evidence Governance
Evidence shall be attributable, time-stamped, integrity-protected, relevant, sufficient and traceable to the lifecycle stage and decision it supports.

## Baseline Governance
Any change to the post-closure baseline shall have a defined owner, rationale, effective date, approval and traceability. Baseline changes shall not be used to conceal regression.

## Security and Resilience Governance
Security and resilience governance shall have explicit ownership and escalation paths for material regression. Cross-service and systemic conditions shall not be contained solely within a local operational team when broader impact exists.

## Data Governance
Data-related regression shall be governed according to data ownership, integrity, lineage, access, retention, privacy and regulatory obligations applicable to the affected state.

## AI and Agent Governance
AI and autonomous or semi-autonomous agents shall operate under explicit governance for identity, authority, tool access, data access, action scope, logging, human oversight, stop conditions and accountability.

## Compliance and Audit Governance
Material regression events shall remain reconstructable for audit. Governance records shall show what happened, who decided, what evidence was used, what actions occurred and why the lifecycle was re-closed or escalated.

## Reporting and Transparency
Governance reporting shall distinguish at minimum: open regression, suspected regression, confirmed regression, containment, reopening, new finding, re-validation, re-acceptance, re-closure and recurring regression.

## Governance Learning
Repeated regression shall trigger governance review, not merely repeated remediation. The organization shall assess whether authority, policy, controls, architecture, monitoring, baselines or accountability need modification.
