# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Autonomous Decision & Adaptive Systems Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing autonomous and adaptive Enterprise capabilities.

The Autonomous Decision & Adaptive Systems Architecture Framework shall provide:

- autonomous decision principles
- adaptive systems governance
- autonomous decision strategy
- adaptive capability architecture
- autonomous decision modeling
- self-learning system architecture
- feedback loop architecture
- adaptive process architecture
- autonomous agent coordination
- dynamic knowledge architecture
- real-time decision architecture
- adaptive data architecture
- autonomous security architecture
- adaptive operations
- adaptive performance management
- adaptive evolution

The framework ensures that autonomous and adaptive systems are designed, governed and operated as intelligent Enterprise capabilities.

---

---

# 3. Enterprise Autonomous Decision & Adaptive Systems Architecture Principles

The Autonomous Decision & Adaptive Systems Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Autonomous Value Creation

Autonomous capabilities shall create measurable value through intelligent decision-making and adaptive behaviour.

---

## Principle 2 – Controlled Autonomy

Autonomous decisions shall operate within defined governance, security and responsibility boundaries.

---

## Principle 3 – Continuous Adaptation

Systems shall improve and adapt through feedback, learning and changing conditions.

---

## Principle 4 – Human Responsibility

Human accountability shall remain clearly defined for autonomous capabilities.

---

## Principle 5 – Real-Time Intelligence

Adaptive systems shall use relevant information to respond effectively to changing situations.

---

## Principle 6 – Secure Autonomous Operation

Autonomous systems shall include protection mechanisms throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolution Through Learning

Adaptive capabilities shall continuously evolve through experience and performance feedback.
