# 1. Purpose

This document defines the eighty-third implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-82 – Organizational Change Management, Adoption, Training, Communications, Stakeholder Engagement & Transformation Readiness Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled organizational knowledge and competence capability covering knowledge management, knowledge lifecycle, knowledge ownership, knowledge domains, knowledge repositories, knowledge articles, lessons learned, organizational learning, competence management, skills management, role capability, workforce enablement, communities of practice, expert knowledge, knowledge transfer, succession knowledge, learning metrics, competence assurance and knowledge assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that critical organizational knowledge is identified, captured, maintained, transferred, accessible and continuously improved, while people have the competence and skills required to perform their roles safely, effectively and consistently.**

# 118. Definition of Ready

A knowledge or competence work item is Ready when:

- Purpose is defined.
- Knowledge or capability scope is known.

# 120. Final Knowledge Principle

> **Critical organizational knowledge must not depend solely on individual memory or undocumented practice.**

---

# 121. Final Ownership Principle

> **Material knowledge assets and knowledge domains must have clear ownership and defined lifecycle responsibility.**

---

# 122. Final Quality Principle

> **Knowledge must be accurate, understandable, current, findable and appropriate for its intended audience.**

---

# 123. Final Lifecycle Principle

> **Knowledge must be actively managed from identification and capture through validation, use, review, archival and retirement.**

---

# 124. Final Lessons Principle

> **Lessons learned create organizational value only when they are converted into changes in behavior, process, standards, training or decision-making.**

---

# 125. Final Competence Principle

> **People performing material roles must possess and demonstrate the knowledge, skills and behaviors required for those roles.**

---

# 126. Final Skills Principle

> **Skill gaps must be visible and managed before they become operational, service, quality or resilience risks.**

---

# 127. Final Knowledge Transfer Principle

> **Critical knowledge must be transferable so that organizational capability does not depend on a single individual.**

---

# 128. Final Succession Principle

> **Critical roles must have sufficient knowledge transfer and succession arrangements to preserve operational capability through personnel changes.**

---

# 129. Final Learning Principle

> **Organizational learning must convert experience and evidence into measurable improvements in competence, capability and performance.**

---

# 130. Final Workforce Enablement Principle

> **People must have the knowledge, skills, tools, access, authority and support required to perform their responsibilities effectively.**

---

# 131. Final Assurance Principle

> **Knowledge and competence assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that critical knowledge is available and people performing material roles remain appropriately competent.**

---

# 132. Final Integration Principle

> **Knowledge Management must integrate with Change, Training, HR, Service, Process, Project, Product, Security, Privacy, Data, Document, Risk and Enterprise Assurance governance.**

---

# 133. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage organizational knowledge and competence through a controlled lifecycle connecting knowledge capture, repositories, lessons learned, learning, competence, skills, workforce enablement, expert knowledge, knowledge transfer, succession and continuous assurance.**

**Service Management Authority:** Enterprise Service Management / Service Catalog / SLA / Request / Incident / Problem / Operational Support  
**Financial Governance Authority:** Financial Management / Budgeting / Cost Control / Accounting / Procurement / Financial Assurance  
**Membership Governance Authority:** Membership / Member Experience / Communications / Engagement / Relationship Management  
**Project Governance Authority:** Project & Portfolio Management / Planning / Resource / Milestone / Delivery / Project Assurance  
**Grant Governance Authority:** Grant Management / Funding Lifecycle / Eligibility / Application / Award / Compliance / Grant Assurance  
**Document Governance Authority:** Document & Records Management / Information Lifecycle / Filing / Retention / Search / Archiving / Records Assurance  
**Procurement Governance Authority:** Procurement / Supplier / Contract / Vendor Lifecycle / Third-Party Risk / Supply-Chain Assurance  
**Enterprise Assurance Authority:** Risk / Compliance / Internal Control / Audit / Policy / Enterprise Assurance  
**Configuration Governance Authority:** Configuration Management / Asset Management / CMDB / Dependency Mapping / Technology Lifecycle Assurance
