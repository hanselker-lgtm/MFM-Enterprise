# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the ninth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-08 – Enterprise Risk Management, Compliance, Regulatory Obligations, Internal Control, Audit, Assurance & Governance Risk Operations.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise service-management operating model for MFM, covering service ownership, service catalog, incident management, service requests, problem management, change enablement, release management, service levels, knowledge management, service continuity, service reporting and continual improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM services must remain reliable, supportable, measurable, governed and continuously improved throughout steady-state operation.**
