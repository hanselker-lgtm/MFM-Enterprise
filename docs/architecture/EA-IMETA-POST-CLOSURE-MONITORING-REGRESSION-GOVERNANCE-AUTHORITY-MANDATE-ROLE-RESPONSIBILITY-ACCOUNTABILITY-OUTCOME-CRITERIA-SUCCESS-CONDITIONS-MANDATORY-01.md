## Short File ID
`EA-IMETA-POST-CLOSURE-MONITORING-REGRESSION-GOVERNANCE-AUTHORITY-MANDATE-ROLE-RESPONSIBILITY-ACCOUNTABILITY-OUTCOME-CRITERIA-SUCCESS-CONDITIONS-MANDATORY-01`

### Version 1.0
### Status: POST-CLOSURE REGRESSION GOVERNANCE MANDATORY SUCCESS CONDITIONS BASELINE
### Governing Architecture: EA-IMETA-MASTER-01

## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-conditions layer beneath success conditions, defining non-negotiable requirements that must be satisfied for full success, including applicability, required state, verification, evidence, measurement, blocking failure, security, resilience, compliance, escalation, exception authority and review.

## Core Principle
A mandatory condition is a condition that must be satisfied whenever applicable for full success to be recognized. It cannot be treated as optional merely because other conditions are satisfied.

```text
SUCCESS CONDITIONS
        ↓
MANDATORY CONDITIONS
        ↓
NON-NEGOTIABLE REQUIREMENT
        ↓
APPLICABILITY
        ↓
REQUIRED STATE
        ↓
VERIFICATION
        ↓
EVIDENCE
        ↓
PASS / FAIL
        ↓
BLOCKING FAILURE
        ↓
ESCALATION / EXCEPTION AUTHORITY
```

## Mandatory Condition Quality Test
```text
DEFINED SUCCESS CONDITION
+
EXPLICIT MANDATORY CLASSIFICATION
+
VALID APPLICABILITY
+
REQUIRED STATE
+
OBJECTIVE VERIFICATION
+
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
+
BLOCKING FAILURE RULE
+
ESCALATION PATH
+
AUTHORIZED EXCEPTION RULE
=
VALID GOVERNED MANDATORY CONDITION
```

## Mandatory Condition Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Condition | Non-negotiable requirement |
| Source | Governance, mandate, policy, law, contract or approved criterion |
| Applicability | Circumstances under which the condition applies |
| Required State | State that must exist |
| Verification | Method used to establish satisfaction |
| Evidence | Required proof |
| Pass Rule | Conditions for satisfaction |
| Blocking Rule | Consequence of failure |
| Exception Rule | Conditions for authorized exception |
| Escalation | Required response to material failure |
| Effective Version | Version governing the determination |
| Review | Reassessment requirements |

## Non-Negotiable Requirements

Mandatory conditions shall represent requirements that cannot be downgraded to optional status through operational preference, convenience or local interpretation.

```text
MANDATORY
   ↓
APPLICABLE?
 ├── NO  → NOT APPLICABLE
 └── YES
      ↓
SATISFIED?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → BLOCK / ESCALATE / GOVERNED EXCEPTION
```

## Mandatory Applicability

Applicability shall be explicit. A mandatory condition shall not be marked not applicable without satisfying its defined applicability rule and retaining evidence of the determination.

```text
CONDITION
   ↓
APPLICABILITY TEST
   ↓
APPLICABLE?
 ├── NO  → RECORD N/A
 └── YES → MANDATORY TEST
```

## Mandatory State

The required state shall be defined so that a verifier can determine whether the condition exists.

```text
REQUIRED STATE
      ↓
OBSERVABLE / ASSESSABLE
      ↓
VERIFY
```

## Mandatory Verification

Verification shall be independent enough for the materiality of the condition. Critical mandatory conditions shall not rely solely on unverified self-attestation where independent evidence is required.

## Mandatory Evidence

Evidence shall be attributable, relevant, current, sufficiently complete and protected from inappropriate alteration. Evidence shall identify the condition version and verification context.

## Mandatory Measurement

Where a mandatory condition is quantitative, the measurement method, source, frequency, quality controls and threshold shall be defined.

```text
DATA
 ↓
VALIDATE
 ↓
MEASURE
 ↓
COMPARE WITH MANDATORY LIMIT
 ↓
PASS / FAIL
```

## Blocking Conditions

Failure of a mandatory condition blocks full success unless an explicitly authorized exception mechanism applies.

```text
MANDATORY FAILURE
      ↓
BLOCK FULL SUCCESS
      ↓
CONTAIN / ESCALATE
      ↓
REMEDIATE OR GOVERN EXCEPTION
```
Blocking shall remain effective until the condition is restored or an authorized exception is active.

## Mandatory Security

Mandatory security conditions may include confidentiality, integrity, availability, authentication, authorization, vulnerability management, incident response and protective-control requirements.

Operational performance shall not override a mandatory security requirement.

## Mandatory Resilience

Mandatory resilience conditions may include continuity, recovery capability, restoration objectives, dependency controls, capacity and recovery verification.

## Mandatory Data

Mandatory data conditions may include integrity, accuracy, completeness, authorized access, classification, retention, lineage and availability requirements.

## Mandatory AI and Agent

AI and agent mandatory conditions shall define hard boundaries that cannot be exceeded by autonomous execution.

```text
AI / AGENT ACTION
      ↓
MANDATORY CONDITION
      ↓
WITHIN AUTHORITY?
 ├── NO  → BLOCK
 └── YES
      ↓
POLICY / SAFETY CONDITION SATISFIED?
 ├── NO  → BLOCK / ESCALATE
 └── YES → CONTINUE
```
Model capability or technical autonomy shall never override a mandatory governance condition.

## Mandatory Compliance

Mandatory compliance conditions shall reflect obligations that cannot be waived by operational preference. Where an authorized legal or governance exception mechanism exists, it shall be explicit and evidence-based.

## Mandatory Escalation

```text
MANDATORY CONDITION FAILED
      ↓
LOCAL AUTHORITY SUFFICIENT?
 ├── NO  → ESCALATE
 └── YES → APPLY APPROVED RESPONSE
```
Material failure, systemic failure or uncertainty about authority shall trigger escalation.

## Mandatory Exception Authority

An exception to a mandatory condition shall require explicit authority, documented rationale, impact and risk assessment, compensating controls where applicable, effective period and review or expiry.

```text
MANDATORY FAILURE
      ↓
EXCEPTION ELIGIBLE?
 ├── NO  → BLOCK / REMEDIATE
 └── YES
      ↓
AUTHORIZED?
 ├── NO  → BLOCK / ESCALATE
 └── YES → GOVERNED EXCEPTION
```
An exception shall not be inferred from silence, delay or operational necessity.

## Mandatory Override Control

No local role, system or agent shall override a mandatory condition without the explicitly defined authority to do so.

```text
LOCAL OVERRIDE REQUEST
      ↓
AUTHORIZED BY MANDATE?
 ├── NO  → REJECT
 └── YES → GOVERNED EXCEPTION / OVERRIDE
```

## Mandatory Dependency

Where a mandatory condition depends on another service, system, data source or external party, the dependency shall be identified and its failure behavior governed.

## Mandatory Continuity

Critical mandatory conditions shall have continuity arrangements so that organizational absence or service disruption does not silently remove the requirement.

```text
OWNER UNAVAILABLE
      ↓
SUCCESSOR / BACKUP
      ↓
MANDATORY CONDITION REMAINS ACTIVE
      ↓
VERIFY / ESCALATE
```

## Mandatory Determination

```text
MANDATORY CONDITION
      ↓
APPLICABLE?
 ├── NO  → N/A + RECORD
 └── YES
      ↓
EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT?
       ├── NO  → UNDETERMINED / BLOCK / ESCALATE
       └── YES
            ↓
SATISFIED?
             ├── YES → PASS
             └── NO  → BLOCK / FAIL
```
Absence of sufficient evidence shall not be treated as proof of compliance.

## Mandatory Failure and Remediation

```text
MANDATORY CONDITION FAIL
      ↓
BLOCK FULL SUCCESS
      ↓
CONTAIN
      ↓
ROOT / CONTRIBUTING CAUSE
      ↓
REMEDIATE
      ↓
RE-VERIFY
      ↓
RESTORE / EXCEPTION / REJECT
```

## Mandatory Condition Revalidation

After remediation, the mandatory condition shall be revalidated using the applicable verification method. Revalidation shall create new evidence rather than overwrite the original failure record.

## Mandatory Condition Change Control

Changes to mandatory conditions shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated. The historical condition definition shall remain preserved.

```text
CURRENT MANDATORY CONDITION
          ↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
          ↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
          ↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
          ↓
NEW VERSION
          ↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

## Mandatory Condition Review

Review shall be triggered by material regression, repeated failure, architecture change, regulatory change, risk change, mandate change or evidence that the mandatory condition is no longer adequate.

```text
MANDATORY CONDITION ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
STILL VALID / REQUIRED?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → SUPERSEDE / RETIRE THROUGH GOVERNANCE
```

## Governance Learning

Repeated mandatory-condition failures shall trigger review of architecture, controls, responsibility, competence, measurement, criteria, resilience and governance assumptions.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes the mandatory-condition layer beneath success conditions for post-closure regression governance. It does not replace governance, authority, mandate, role, responsibility, accountability, outcome, criteria or success layers.
