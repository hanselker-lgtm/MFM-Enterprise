## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory procedural verification layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-291, determining whether the substantive validation of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290 was correctly performed, evidenced, authorized, decided, recorded and implemented.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290 determines whether RG-289 verification is substantively effective. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-291 verifies that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290's validation of that verification was correctly performed and implemented.

```text
RG-288 → VALIDATE
RG-289 → VERIFY
RG-290 → VALIDATE
RG-291 → VERIFY
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-291 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
WAS EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290 CORRECTLY PERFORMED?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290 validation SHALL NOT automatically prove that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-290 was correctly verified.
