# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the fifth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-04 – Enterprise Security Operations, Identity, Access, Vulnerability, Threat Management, Security Monitoring, Incident Response & Cyber Resilience.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent privacy, data-protection and information-governance operating model for MFM, covering personal data processing, information rights, privacy risk, records, retention, deletion, data lifecycle, privacy incidents, third-party processing, data transfers and privacy assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must process, protect, retain, disclose and dispose of information in a controlled manner that respects privacy, information rights, legal and governance obligations, operational needs and defined retention requirements throughout the information lifecycle.**

# 125. Definition of Ready

A privacy or information-governance work item is Ready when:

- The processing activity, data, rights request, retention issue, record, privacy risk, incident or assurance requirement is clearly identified.
- Ownership and affected information are known.
- Risk, purpose, retention and acceptance criteria are defined.
- Required evidence, rights, security and disposal requirements are understood.

# 127. Final Privacy Principle

> **Privacy must remain an active operational discipline throughout the complete information lifecycle.**

---

# 128. Final Data Minimization Principle

> **MFM should not collect, use or retain more personal information than is reasonably necessary for defined purposes.**

---

# 129. Final Transparency Principle

> **Individuals should receive appropriate and understandable information about relevant processing and applicable rights.**

---

# 130. Final Rights Principle

> **Information rights must be supported through controlled, traceable and timely operational processes.**

---

# 131. Final Retention Principle

> **Information should be retained only for as long as justified by applicable purpose, legal, contractual, operational or evidentiary requirements.**

---

# 132. Final Records Principle

> **Material records must remain authentic, protected, retrievable and appropriately disposed of or preserved.**

---

# 133. Final Deletion Principle

> **When information is no longer required and no valid hold or other requirement applies, it should be securely disposed of through controlled processes.**

---

# 134. Final Incident Principle

> **Privacy incidents and potential data breaches must be rapidly assessed, contained, documented and escalated according to risk and applicable requirements.**

---

# 135. Final Third-Party Principle

> **Third parties processing personal data must remain subject to appropriate contractual, security, privacy and assurance controls.**

---

# 136. Final Assurance Principle

> **Privacy and information-governance controls must remain testable, evidenced and subject to continuous assurance.**

---

# 137. Final Integration Principle

> **Privacy Operations must integrate with Security, Data, Records, Service Management, Technical Operations, Architecture, Risk, Compliance, Supplier and Enterprise Assurance governance.**

---

# 138. Final Steady-State Privacy Principle

> **MFM information must remain governed from collection through use, sharing, retention, archiving and final disposition.**
