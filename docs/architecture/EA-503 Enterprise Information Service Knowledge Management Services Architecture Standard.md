# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Knowledge Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management, sharing, preservation and utilization of Enterprise knowledge derived from information services, metadata, semantics, experience and organizational learning across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Knowledge Management Services shall

- provide centralized knowledge management capabilities
- establish knowledge governance
- manage knowledge capabilities

# 3. Enterprise Information Service Knowledge Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Knowledge Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Knowledge by Design

Enterprise information services shall be designed to create, capture and maintain reusable knowledge assets.

---

## Governed Knowledge

All Enterprise knowledge capabilities shall be managed through approved governance processes.

---

## Trusted Knowledge Sources

Knowledge assets shall originate from trusted, validated and governed information sources.

---

## Knowledge Preservation

Enterprise knowledge shall be preserved throughout its lifecycle to prevent loss of critical organizational capability.

---

## Knowledge Sharing and Reuse

Knowledge assets shall be made available for appropriate reuse across Enterprise domains.
