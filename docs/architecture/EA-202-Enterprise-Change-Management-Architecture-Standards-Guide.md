# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Change Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Change Management ensures that changes affecting applications, infrastructure, services and enterprise capabilities are planned, assessed, approved, implemented, validated and documented in a controlled and repeatable manner while preserving operational stability, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Change Identification
- Change Classification
- Change Risk Assessment
- Change Approval
- Change Scheduling
- Change Implementation
- Change Validation
- Change Rollback
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Change Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Change Management Principles

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Risk-Based Decision Making
- Controlled Change
- Standardized Approval
- Complete Traceability
- Operational Stability
- Repeatable Processes
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Change Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

Enterprise Change Management shall provide

- change identification
- change classification
- risk assessment
- approval coordination
- implementation planning
- implementation governance
- validation management
- rollback planning
- governance reporting
- compliance verification

Additional Change Management responsibilities shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

- operational ownership
- governance responsibility

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall define

# 8. Change Identification

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall implement standardized change identification.

Change identification shall

- identify proposed enterprise changes
- identify affected business capabilities
- identify affected applications
- identify affected infrastructure
- preserve identification traceability
- maintain identification consistency

Change identification shall remain centrally governed.

# 9. Change Classification

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall implement standardized change classification.

Change classification shall

- classify standard changes
- classify normal changes
- classify emergency changes
- classify major changes
- preserve classification traceability
- maintain classification consistency

Change classification policies shall remain centrally governed.

# 10. Change Risk Assessment

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall implement standardized risk assessment.

Risk assessment shall

- evaluate business impact
- evaluate technical impact
- evaluate operational risk
- evaluate security implications
- preserve assessment traceability
- maintain assessment consistency

Risk assessment shall support informed approval decisions.

---

# 11. Change Approval

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall implement standardized approval procedures.

Change approval shall

- assign approval authority
- document approval decisions
- verify prerequisite completion
- preserve approval traceability
- maintain approval consistency
- support governance compliance

Change approval shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Change Scheduling

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall implement standardized scheduling.

Change scheduling shall

- coordinate implementation windows
- minimize operational disruption
- avoid conflicting changes
- preserve scheduling traceability
- maintain scheduling consistency
- support enterprise operations

Change scheduling shall align with Enterprise Operations standards.

---

# 13. Change Implementation

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall implement standardized implementation procedures.

Change implementation shall

- execute approved changes
- follow documented implementation plans
- monitor implementation progress
- preserve implementation traceability
- maintain implementation consistency
- support controlled execution

Change implementation shall follow approved governance procedures.

Dependencies shall include

- approved monitoring platforms

- approved configuration repositories

- governance services

# 15. Change Validation

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall implement standardized change validation.

Change validation shall

- verify successful implementation
- verify expected business outcomes
- verify technical correctness
- verify operational stability
- preserve validation traceability
- maintain validation consistency

Change validation shall be completed before change closure.

# 16. Change Rollback

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall implement standardized rollback procedures.

Rollback procedures shall

- define rollback criteria
- define rollback responsibilities
- restore previous operational state
- preserve rollback traceability
- minimize operational disruption
- support business continuity

Rollback procedures shall be documented before implementation begins.

- record change requests

- record validation activities
- record rollback activities where applicable
- preserve audit evidence
- maintain audit traceability

Audit information shall support compliance verification.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall handle change management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify change request failures
- classify approval failures
- classify implementation failures
- classify validation failures
- classify rollback failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Change Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational stability, traceability, governance, compliance or business continuity.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Change Management implementations may depend upon

- approved monitoring platforms
- approved incident management services
- approved problem management services
- approved configuration repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external change management providers

Change Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Change Management implementation is compliant when

- Change identification is implemented.
- Change classification follows enterprise policy.
- Risk assessment is completed.
- Approval requirements are fulfilled.
- Scheduling is coordinated.
- Implementation follows approved procedures.
- Validation is completed.
- Rollback procedures are documented.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Unauthorized Changes

Enterprise changes shall never be implemented without the required approvals.

---

## Missing Risk Assessment

Changes shall never proceed without documented risk assessment unless explicitly defined as approved standard changes.

---

## Missing Rollback Plan

Changes affecting production environments shall never be implemented without an approved rollback strategy where rollback is technically feasible.

---

## Unverified Change Completion

Changes shall never be marked as completed before validation confirms successful implementation.

---

## Emergency Changes Without Documentation

Emergency changes shall never bypass documentation and post-implementation review requirements.

---

## Change Management Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent change management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Change Management services.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise Change Management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- change identification compliance

- risk assessment compliance
- approval compliance
- implementation compliance
- validation compliance
- dependency compliance
- documentation completeness
- operational readiness
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Change Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Change Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise changes are consistently identified, assessed, approved, implemented, validated and documented while preserving operational stability, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Change Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
