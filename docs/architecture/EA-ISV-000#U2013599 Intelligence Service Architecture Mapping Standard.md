# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Service Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive service architecture for designing, delivering, managing and evolving Enterprise Intelligence services.

The Intelligence Service Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence service principles
- service governance
- intelligence service catalogue
- service design architecture
- service delivery architecture
- service lifecycle management
- service level management
- service performance management
- service integration architecture
- service experience architecture
- service improvement framework
- service evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are delivered as structured, measurable and valuable services.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Service Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture intelligence service domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Service Model
- Service Governance
- Service Catalogue Architecture
- Service Design Architecture
- Service Delivery Architecture
- Service Lifecycle Architecture
- Service Level Management
- Service Performance Management
- Service Integration
- Service Experience
- Service Improvement
- Service Maturity
- Service Roadmaps
- Service Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- intelligent operating environments
- future adaptive enterprises
