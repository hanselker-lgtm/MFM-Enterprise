# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational governance architecture that ensures the complete Intelligence landscape is governed, controlled, prioritised, audited and continuously improved.

The Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- governance operating principles
- governance control structures
- policy management frameworks
- decision authority models
- compliance management structures
- risk management frameworks
- audit structures
- ethics frameworks
- accountability models
- transparency frameworks
- governance automation structures
- governance evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for responsible, controlled and continuously evolving Intelligence governance.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Governance Operating Model
