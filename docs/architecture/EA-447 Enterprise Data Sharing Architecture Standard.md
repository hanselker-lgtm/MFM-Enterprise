# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Sharing Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Access principles are inherited from EA-445.

Enterprise Data Consumption principles are inherited from EA-446.

All Enterprise Data Sharing implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Sharing Architecture enabling secure, governed and standardized sharing of Enterprise information across Business Domains, Technology Domains, external organizations, strategic partners and public authorities.

Enterprise Data Sharing shall

- enable trusted information sharing
- improve interoperability
- strengthen collaboration
- reduce information duplication
- improve operational efficiency
- strengthen governance
- support regulatory compliance
- optimize information availability
- improve business agility
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Sharing shall establish a unified architectural framework for controlled information sharing throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- internal business domains
- enterprise applications
- APIs
- event-driven platforms
- cloud services
- business intelligence platforms
- external organizations
- strategic partners
- suppliers
- government authorities

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform, cloud provider or implementation technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Sharing Principles

Enterprise Data Sharing shall be governed by the following principles.

## Sharing by Design

Enterprise information shall be designed for secure and governed sharing whenever business value justifies controlled distribution.

---

## Authoritative Information Sources

Shared Enterprise information shall originate from authoritative information sources.

---

## Controlled Information Distribution

Enterprise information shall only be shared through approved Enterprise sharing mechanisms.

---

## Secure Sharing

Information sharing shall be authenticated, authorized, encrypted and continuously monitored.

---

## Metadata Driven Sharing

Enterprise Data Sharing shall utilize metadata, semantic definitions and governance policies to standardize information sharing.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Sharing Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, cloud providers, middleware technologies and implementation platforms.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Sharing Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed sharing of Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating standardized sharing services, internal and external information exchange, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted collaboration, efficient interoperability and business-driven information exchange while maintaining governance, compliance and authoritative ownership of Enterprise information.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Sharing initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise information sharing capabilities across the Enterprise.
