# 1. Purpose

This document defines the sixty-eighth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-67 – Business Process Integration, Workflow Orchestration, Process Intelligence, Case Management & Cross-System Automation Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise service architecture capability covering service definition, service ownership, service portfolio management, service catalog management, service design, service dependencies, service lifecycle, service onboarding, service retirement, service experience and service assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage services as explicit, owned and governed capabilities with defined consumers, outcomes, dependencies, service levels, lifecycle controls and evidence-based assurance.**

**Procurement Governance Authority:** Procurement / Supplier / Contract / Vendor Lifecycle / Third-Party Risk / Supply-Chain Assurance  
**Enterprise Assurance Authority:** Risk / Compliance / Internal Control / Audit / Policy / Enterprise Assurance  
**Configuration Governance Authority:** Configuration Management / Asset Management / CMDB / Dependency Mapping / Technology Lifecycle Assurance  
**Principle:** MFM must manage services as explicit, owned and governed capabilities with defined consumers, outcomes, dependencies, service levels, lifecycle controls and evidence-based assurance
