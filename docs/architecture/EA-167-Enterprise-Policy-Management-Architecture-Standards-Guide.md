# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise policy management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise policy management ensures that internal policies are created, approved, classified, distributed, maintained, reviewed and continuously improved through standardized enterprise processes while preserving governance, consistency, accountability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Policy Management
- Policy Development
- Policy Classification
- Policy Approval
- Policy Distribution
- Policy Maintenance
- Policy Compliance
- Governance

All enterprise policy management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

## PM-001

Provide standardized enterprise policy management.

---

## PM-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Policy Management Principles

Enterprise policy management shall follow these principles.

- Policy by Design
- Governance by Default
- Consistency
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Accountability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Policy management implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Policy Management Domains

Enterprise policy management shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Enterprise Policies
- Information Security Policies
- Operational Policies
- Compliance Policies
- Risk Policies
- Privacy Policies
- HR Policies
- Technical Policies

Additional policy domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Policy Management Ownership

Each policy management domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the policy lifecycle.

---

# 7. Policy Management Governance

Enterprise policy management governance shall define

- policy governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- policy verification
- governance reporting

Policy management governance shall remain technology independent.

---

# End of Part 1

# 8. Policy Management Responsibilities

Enterprise policy management shall provide controlled management of enterprise policies.

Policy management responsibilities shall

- separate policy management from business execution
- coordinate policy ownership
- ensure policy consistency
- validate policy objectives
- preserve policy traceability
- support enterprise governance

Policy management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Policy Development

Enterprise policy management shall implement standardized policy development.

Policy development shall

- define policy objectives
- identify applicable requirements
- identify affected business areas
- identify implementation responsibilities
- preserve development history
- maintain development traceability

Policy development shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Policy Classification

Enterprise policy management shall classify all enterprise policies.

Policy classifications shall

- define policy category
- define confidentiality level
- define applicability
- define ownership
- preserve classification history
- maintain classification traceability

Policy classifications shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Policy Approval

Enterprise policy management shall implement standardized policy approval.

Policy approval shall

- validate policy completeness
- verify governance compliance
- verify stakeholder approval
- preserve approval history
- maintain approval traceability
- support policy integrity

Policy approval shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Policy Distribution

Enterprise policy management shall implement standardized policy distribution.

Policy distribution shall

- distribute approved policies
- notify affected stakeholders
- support policy accessibility
- preserve distribution history
- maintain distribution traceability
- support governance communication

Policy distribution shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Policy Management Dependencies

Enterprise policy management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- compliance capabilities
- audit capabilities
- document management systems
- communication platforms
- enterprise infrastructure

Policy management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Policy Documentation

Each policy management domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- policy registers
- ownership information
- policy classifications
- approval records
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Policy Lifecycle

Enterprise policy management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Drafted
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Published
- Implemented
- Verified
- Monitored
- Revised
- Retired
- Archived

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Policy Quality Attributes

Enterprise policy management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- readability
- governance compliance
- policy effectiveness

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the policy lifecycle.

---

# 17. Policy Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized policy register.

The policy register shall contain

- policy identifiers
- ownership assignments
- classifications
- approval status
- lifecycle status
- documentation references
- review schedules
- governance status

The policy register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise policies.

---

# 18. Policy Reviews

Enterprise policy management implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- policy completeness
- policy consistency
- classification accuracy
- approval integrity
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Policy Metrics

Enterprise policy management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- approved policies
- policies under review
- overdue policy reviews
- policy compliance rate
- policy exceptions
- policy revisions
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous policy improvement.

---

# 20. Policy Verification

Enterprise policy management implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm policy completeness
- verify classifications
- verify approvals
- verify distribution
- verify ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- confirm governance compliance
- approve publication

Policy verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Policy Improvement

Enterprise policy management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve policy quality
- improve governance
- improve policy accessibility
- improve policy consistency
- improve organizational compliance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise policy management implementations shall handle policy-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify policy development failures
- classify policy approval failures
- classify policy distribution failures
- classify policy review failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Policy management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, governance, policy integrity or regulatory obligations.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Policy management implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved compliance capabilities
- approved audit capabilities
- approved document management systems
- approved communication platforms
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Policy management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external policy management services

Policy management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A policy management implementation is compliant when

- Policy management responsibilities are documented.
- Policy development procedures are implemented.
- Policy classifications are documented.
- Policy approval procedures are implemented.
- Policy distribution is documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Policy Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Policy verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Policy Ownership

Enterprise policies shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Missing Policy Approval

Policies shall never be published without formal approval.

---

## Missing Policy Distribution

Approved policies shall never remain unavailable to affected stakeholders.

---

## Outdated Policies

Enterprise policies shall never remain active beyond their scheduled review period without documented reapproval or revision.

---

## Undocumented Policy Dependencies

Policy management implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Policy Management Outside Governance

Policy management implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise policy management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- policy management quality
- policy completeness
- approval integrity
- distribution effectiveness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- policy consistency
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Policy Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise policy management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and business operations implement standardized policy development, approval, classification, distribution, verification and governance through controlled lifecycle management and continuous improvement while preserving consistency, accountability, regulatory alignment and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise policy management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
