# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Validation Services Architecture supporting structured verification, confirmation and acceptance that Enterprise capabilities, services, controls, information and operational outcomes meet approved requirements across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Validation Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem validation services
- support validation governance
- support validation strategy
- support validation objectives
- support validation planning
- support requirement validation
- support architecture validation
- support service validation
- support capability validation
- support operational validation
- support technology validation
- support application validation
- support infrastructure validation
- support data validation
- support information validation
- support security validation
- support compliance validation
- support quality validation
- support performance validation
- support acceptance validation
- support evidence validation
- support validation measurement
- support validation improvement
- support validation maturity development
- support continuous validation capability development

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Validation Services shall provide trusted and reusable validation capabilities enabling Enterprise stakeholders to confirm that Enterprise capabilities and services satisfy defined requirements.
