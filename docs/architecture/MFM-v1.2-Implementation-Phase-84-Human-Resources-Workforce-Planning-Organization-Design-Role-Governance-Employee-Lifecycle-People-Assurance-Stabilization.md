# 1. Purpose

This document defines the eighty-fourth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-83 – Knowledge Management, Learning, Competence, Organizational Capability, Workforce Enablement & Knowledge Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled people-management capability covering human resources governance, workforce planning, organization design, organizational structure, role governance, position management, employee lifecycle, recruitment, onboarding, internal mobility, performance management, development, offboarding, workforce capacity, workforce risk, people data governance and people assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain a clear, governed and sustainable relationship between organizational structure, roles, workforce capacity, employee lifecycle, competence requirements and organizational objectives while protecting people data and maintaining appropriate people assurance.**

# 130. Definition of Ready

A people-management work item is Ready when:

- Organizational or workforce need is defined.
- Relevant role or position is known.
- Ownership is assigned.
- Applicable people-data requirements are understood.
- Competence and capacity implications are identified.
- Required approvals are known.

---

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

# 139. Final Performance Principle

> **Employee departures must protect organizational security, assets, knowledge, records and operational continuity.**

# 144. Final Assurance Principle
