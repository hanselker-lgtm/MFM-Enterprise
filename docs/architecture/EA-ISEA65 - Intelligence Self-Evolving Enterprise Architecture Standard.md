# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, intelligence capabilities, and evolutionary control mechanisms required for an **Intelligence Self-Evolving Enterprise Architecture (ISEA)**.

Where **EA-ICEA64** enables Enterprise Intelligence to converge into one coherent cognitive ecosystem, **EA-ISEA65** enables that ecosystem to **continuously redesign, improve, restructure, and evolve itself** while remaining permanently governed by Enterprise Purpose, Enterprise Identity, Enterprise Wisdom, and human accountability.

Self-Evolving Enterprise Architecture extends enterprise intelligence beyond adaptation.

The architecture becomes capable of evaluating its own effectiveness, identifying structural limitations, designing improved architectural patterns, validating proposed evolution, governing architectural transformation, and safely deploying new architectural capabilities without compromising enterprise integrity.

Enterprise evolution shall always remain:

- Governed
- Explainable
- Human-supervised
- Evidence-driven
- Ethically constrained
- Scientifically credible
- Sustainable
- Fully traceable

The objective is to establish an Enterprise Architecture capable of continuous self-improvement while preserving long-term enterprise stability.
