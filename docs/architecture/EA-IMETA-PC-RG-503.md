RG-502 established meta-control: governance of the mechanisms that govern enterprise.

# 3. Core Architectural Question

**How can enterprise continuously establish that its control mechanisms remain trustworthy, correctly authorised, policy-compliant, stable, resilient, observable and fit for purpose while policies, models, agents, environments and enterprise conditions continuously change?**

# 4. Governing Principle

**Control SHALL NOT be considered trustworthy merely because it is functioning. Trust SHALL be continuously supported by evidence, policy conformance, authority integrity, operational performance, resilience, security and independent assurance. No controller or agent SHALL be the sole judge of its own correctness or certification.**

# 100. Assurance Fabric Architecture

RG-503 SHALL provide interconnected assurance domains for policy, controller, agent, model, authority, evidence, execution and governance. Assurance SHALL be treated as an enterprise capability rather than a collection of isolated compliance checks.

# 101. Assurance Plane Separation

The architecture SHALL separate operational control from assurance. A controller SHALL not be the sole source of evidence used to certify itself.

# 102. Evidence Plane

The Evidence Plane SHALL preserve provenance, freshness, confidence, integrity and chain-of-custody for material assurance evidence.

# 103. Assurance Decision Plane

The Assurance Decision Plane SHALL determine certification, degradation, suspension, escalation or recovery based on authorised assurance rules.

# 104. Certification Plane

The Certification Plane SHALL maintain current certification state and conditions.

# 105. Recovery Plane

The Recovery Plane SHALL provide trusted fallback when assurance becomes unavailable or invalid.

# 106. Control Assurance Graph

# 176. Acceptance Criteria

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-503 is accepted when:

- an enterprise assurance fabric is explicitly defined;
- material assurance claims are linked to evidence;
- evidence provenance, freshness and confidence are visible;
- assurance boundaries are explicit;
- certification state and conditions are authoritative;
- continuous certification is available for critical controls;
- controller and agent assurance are separated from execution;
- policy validation is governed;
- trust degradation and restoration are measurable;
- assurance debt and blind spots are visible;
- assurance cascades are modelled;
- circuit breakers and safe states exist;
- human assurance gates remain superior;
- multi-agent coordination and conflict are governed;
- agent collusion and common-mode failure are considered;
- assurance AI cannot self-certify;
- policy and controller changes trigger reassessment;
- certification expiry is enforced;
- assurance decisions are auditable;
- learning feeds RG-502 meta-control.
