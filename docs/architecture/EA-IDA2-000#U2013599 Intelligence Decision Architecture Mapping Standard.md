# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Decision Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities transform perception, awareness, cognition and reasoning into informed decisions and adaptive actions.

The Intelligence Decision Architecture Framework shall provide:

- decision architecture principles
- decision modelling frameworks
- decision evaluation models
- decision optimisation mechanisms
- autonomous decision support
- decision governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through intelligent decision-making.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Decision Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Decision Model
- Decision Intelligence Architecture
- Decision Knowledge Architecture
- Decision Capability Model
- Decision Architecture Integration
- Decision Governance Model
- Decision Evolution Framework
- Decision Metrics
- Decision Maturity Model
- Decision Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Decision Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Decision Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Informed Decision-Making

Intelligence capability shall create decisions based on knowledge, reasoning and evidence.

---

## Principle 2 – Context-Based Decisions

Decisions shall consider objectives, constraints, dependencies and consequences.

---

## Principle 3 – Decision Transparency

Decision processes shall be understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 4 – Adaptive Decisions

Decision capability shall respond to changing conditions.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Improvement

Decision quality shall improve through learning and feedback.

---

## Principle 6 – Responsible Decisions

Decision development shall maintain governance, ethics and accountability.

---

## Principle 7 – Future Decision Capability

The architecture shall support emerging autonomous decision technologies.

---
