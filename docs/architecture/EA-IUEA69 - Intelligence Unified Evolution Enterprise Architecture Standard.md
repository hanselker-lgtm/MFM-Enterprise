# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, intelligence capabilities, and enterprise coordination framework required for an **Intelligence Unified Evolution Enterprise Architecture (IUEEA)**.

Where **EA-ISEA68** establishes a permanently unified Enterprise Mind, **EA-IUEEA69** establishes an Enterprise Intelligence that continuously evolves as a single cognitive organism without losing its coherence, governance, identity, explainability, or human accountability.

The objective is no longer only cognitive unity.

The objective is **continuous governed evolution**.

Enterprise Architecture shall become a continuously improving intelligence capable of redesigning itself while preserving Enterprise Purpose.
