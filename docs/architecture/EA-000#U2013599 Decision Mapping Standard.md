# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Decision Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise Decision capabilities across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Decision Reference Model shall provide:

- decision identification
- decision classification
- decision ownership
- decision relationships
- decision knowledge mapping
- decision data mapping
- decision lifecycle management
- decision governance
- architecture traceability

The model shall establish a common Enterprise language for defining, managing and evolving decision capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

The Decision Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture decision domains:

- Architecture Decisions
- Business Decisions
- Operational Decisions
- Information Decisions
- Data Decisions
- Technology Decisions
- Security Decisions
- Governance Decisions
- Ecosystem Decisions
- Evolution Decisions

The model applies to:

- strategic decisions
- tactical decisions
- operational decisions
- automated decisions
- assisted decisions
- transformation decisions
- evolutionary decisions

---

# 3. Enterprise Decision Architecture Principles

The Decision Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Decision Value

Decisions shall create measurable Enterprise value.

---

## Decision Ownership

Every decision shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Decision Governance

Decisions shall follow defined governance structures and approval processes.

---

## Decision Transparency

Decision rationale, assumptions and consequences shall be documented.

---

## Decision Intelligence

Decisions shall use trusted information, data and knowledge.

---

## Decision Security

Sensitive decisions shall be protected throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Decision Lifecycle Management

Decisions shall be managed from identification to review.

---

## Continuous Decision Improvement

Decision capabilities shall continuously evolve through learning and feedback.
