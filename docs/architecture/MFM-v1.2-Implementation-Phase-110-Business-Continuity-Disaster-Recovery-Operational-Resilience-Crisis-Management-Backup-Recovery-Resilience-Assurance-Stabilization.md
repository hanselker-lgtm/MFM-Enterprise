# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundred-and-tenth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-109 – Supplier, Vendor, Contract, Procurement Lifecycle, Third-Party Risk, Service Provider Governance & Supply-Chain Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled resilience capability covering business continuity governance, business impact analysis, critical services, recovery objectives, disaster recovery, backup governance, restore testing, recovery procedures, operational resilience, crisis management, emergency response, crisis communications, dependency resilience, supplier continuity, technology resilience, recovery testing, exercise management and resilience assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must remain capable of protecting critical services, information, people and operations during disruption and must recover within approved objectives through tested, governed and continuously improved resilience arrangements.**
