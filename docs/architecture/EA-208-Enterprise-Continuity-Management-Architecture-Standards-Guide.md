# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Continuity Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Continuity Management ensures that critical business functions, services and supporting resources remain available or can be restored within agreed recovery objectives while preserving operational resilience, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Business Continuity Planning
- Business Impact Analysis
- Recovery Strategy Management
- Continuity Testing
- Recovery Verification
- Continuity Reporting
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Continuity Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Continuity Management Principles

Enterprise Continuity Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Business Continuity by Design
- Risk-Based Recovery Planning
- Continuous Preparedness
- Verified Recovery Capability
- Complete Traceability
- Operational Resilience
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Continuity Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

---

# 5. Continuity Management Responsibilities

Enterprise Continuity Management shall provide

- continuity planning
- business impact analysis
- recovery strategy management
- continuity testing
- recovery verification
- reporting
- governance reporting
- compliance verification

Additional Continuity Management responsibilities shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

# 8. Business Impact Analysis

Enterprise Continuity Management implementations shall implement standardized Business Impact Analysis.

Business Impact Analysis shall

- identify critical business functions
- identify maximum acceptable downtime
- identify recovery priorities
- identify business dependencies
- preserve analysis traceability
- maintain analysis consistency

Business Impact Analysis shall remain centrally governed.

# 9. Continuity Planning

Enterprise Continuity Management implementations shall implement standardized continuity planning.

Continuity planning shall

- define recovery procedures

- define communication procedures
- define escalation procedures
- preserve planning traceability
- maintain planning consistency

Continuity planning policies shall remain centrally governed.

Enterprise Continuity Management implementations shall implement standardized recovery strategy management.

Recovery strategies shall

- define recovery objectives

- define recovery resources
- define recovery dependencies
- preserve strategy traceability
- maintain strategy consistency

Recovery strategies shall align with approved business continuity requirements.

- preserve testing traceability

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Continuity Management implementations shall handle continuity management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify Business Impact Analysis failures
- classify continuity planning failures
- classify recovery strategy failures
- classify continuity testing failures
- classify recovery verification failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Continuity Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational resilience, recovery capability, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Continuity Management implementations may depend upon

- approved availability management services
- approved capacity management services
- approved incident management services
- approved monitoring platforms
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Continuity Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external continuity management providers

Continuity Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Continuity Management implementation is compliant when

- Business Impact Analysis is documented.
- Continuity plans are maintained.
- Recovery strategies are documented.
- Continuity testing is performed.
- Recovery verification is completed.
- Continuity improvement activities are documented.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Business Impact Analysis

Critical business functions shall never exist without an approved Business Impact Analysis.

---

## Untested Continuity Plans

Continuity plans shall never remain untested beyond the approved testing interval.

---

## Undefined Recovery Objectives

Recovery objectives shall never remain undocumented for business-critical services.

---

## Incomplete Recovery Verification

Recovery capability shall never be assumed without documented verification.

---

## Obsolete Continuity Documentation

Continuity documentation shall never remain outdated following approved organizational or technical changes.

---

## Continuity Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent continuity management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Continuity Management services.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise Continuity Management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- Business Impact Analysis compliance
- continuity planning compliance
- recovery strategy compliance
- testing compliance
- recovery verification compliance
- dependency compliance
- documentation completeness
- operational readiness
- governance compliance
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Continuity Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Continuity Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that critical business functions can continue or be restored within agreed recovery objectives while preserving operational resilience, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Continuity Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
