# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Quantum Computing Readiness Architecture governing future adoption of quantum computing technologies, hybrid computing models, quantum-enabled services and enterprise preparedness across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Quantum Computing Readiness Architecture shall

- establish Enterprise quantum readiness
- support hybrid classical-quantum computing
- prepare Enterprise systems for quantum integration
- enable future quantum-enabled business capabilities
- support quantum-safe architecture evolution
- integrate with Enterprise AI platforms
- support advanced computational workloads
- ensure technology independence
- minimize future migration risk
- strengthen long-term Enterprise innovation

Enterprise Quantum Computing Readiness shall function as a strategic Enterprise capability supporting future technological evolution.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- quantum computing readiness
- hybrid computing architectures
- quantum workloads
- quantum services
- quantum algorithms
- quantum APIs
- quantum integration
- post-quantum preparedness
- enterprise governance
- future technology planning

The standard applies regardless of quantum hardware vendor, cloud provider or quantum computing platform.

# 3. Enterprise Quantum Computing Principles

Enterprise Quantum Computing Readiness shall be governed by the following principles.

## Future Ready

Enterprise architectures shall be designed to accommodate future quantum technologies without significant architectural redesign.

---

## Hybrid Computing

Quantum computing shall complement—not replace—classical enterprise computing.

---

## Business Driven

Quantum capabilities shall only be adopted where measurable business value exists.

---

## Secure Evolution

Enterprise transition toward quantum technologies shall preserve security, compliance and operational stability.

---

## Standards-Based Integration

Quantum services shall integrate using standardized Enterprise interfaces and architectural principles.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Quantum Architecture shall remain independent of individual quantum hardware vendors, cloud providers and programming frameworks.
