# 2. Architectural Position

RG-486 established systemic economic adaptation, shock propagation, resilience and continuous re-equilibration.

RG-487 moves one level earlier in the causal chain. Instead of waiting for a shock or regime transition to become material, the enterprise develops a governed capability to detect weak signals, identify predictive discontinuities, evaluate alternative futures and prepare options before the transition becomes unavoidable.

```text
RG-485 ECONOMIC EQUILIBRIUM
        ↓
RG-486 SYSTEMIC ADAPTATION
        ↓
RG-487 REGIME INTELLIGENCE
        ↓
PREDICTIVE DISCONTINUITY
        ↓
PRE-EMPTIVE OPTIONS
        ↓
STRATEGIC INTERLOCK
        ↓
PRE-EMPTIVE GOVERNANCE
```

# 3. Core Architectural Question

The central question is:

> **How can the enterprise recognise that the economic regime may be changing before the change is fully observable, prepare credible response options, connect those signals to strategic decision-making and act pre-emptively within strict authority boundaries?**

The architecture SHALL treat prediction as decision-support evidence rather than autonomous truth.

# 4. Core Principle

# 9. Weak Signals and Signal Convergence

Weak signals MAY be individually insignificant but collectively important.

Signal convergence exists when independent indicators, models or domains point toward a materially similar future state.

Signal convergence SHOULD increase investigation and scenario coverage. It SHALL NOT automatically grant execution authority.

# 10. Forecast Ensemble

Material discontinuity decisions SHOULD use an ensemble of:

- statistical forecasts;
- causal models;
- simulation models;
- expert assessments;
- scenario analysis;
- external dependency indicators;
- historical analogues.

The ensemble SHALL preserve model provenance and disagreement.

# 11. Scenario Ensemble

A scenario ensemble SHALL represent multiple plausible futures rather than a single predicted future.

```text
BASELINE
 ├── REGIME A
 ├── REGIME B
 ├── REGIME C
 └── DISCONTINUITY D
```

Scenario probabilities are estimates, not preferences. Scenario selection SHALL not be manipulated to justify a desired action.
