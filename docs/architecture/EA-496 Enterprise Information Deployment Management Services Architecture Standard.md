# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Deployment Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed implementation, distribution, activation and monitoring of Enterprise information releases, configurations, changes and information-based capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Deployment Management Services shall

- provide centralized deployment management capabilities
- establish controlled deployment processes
- support deployment governance
- coordinate information deployment activities
- integrate release management
- integrate configuration management
- integrate change management
- integrate dependency management
- integrate lineage management
- improve deployment quality
- reduce operational risks
- support compliance requirements
- support audit requirements
- protect information integrity
- support lifecycle governance
- support master information deployment
- support reference information deployment
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure deployment traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Deployment Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable deployment capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Deployment Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Deployment Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Controlled Deployment by Design

Enterprise information deployments shall be planned, authorized and executed through defined governance processes.

---

## Automated and Repeatable Deployment

Deployment activities shall use standardized and repeatable processes supported by automation where appropriate.

---

## Deployment Traceability

All deployments shall maintain complete records of ownership, approval, execution and verification.

---

## Integrated Deployment Management

Deployment activities shall integrate with release, change, configuration, dependency and lineage management capabilities.

---

## Business and Technical Alignment

Deployment management shall connect business requirements with technical execution and operational activities.
