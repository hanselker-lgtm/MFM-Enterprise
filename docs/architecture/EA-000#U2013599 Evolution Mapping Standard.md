# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Evolution Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise Evolution capabilities across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Evolution Reference Model shall provide:

- evolution identification
- evolution classification
- evolution ownership
- evolution relationships
- evolution planning
- evolution execution
- evolution measurement
- evolution governance
- architecture traceability

The model shall establish a common Enterprise language for defining, managing and evolving Enterprise capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

The Evolution Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture evolution domains:

- Architecture Evolution
- Business Evolution
- Information Evolution
- Digital Evolution
- Security Evolution
- Governance Evolution
- Ecosystem Evolution
- Enterprise Evolution

The model applies to:

- strategic evolution
- operational evolution
- technological evolution
- organizational evolution
- transformation evolution
- adaptive evolution
- sustainable evolution
- future evolution

---

# 3. Enterprise Evolution Architecture Principles

The Evolution Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Continuous Evolution

Enterprise capabilities shall continuously improve and adapt.

---

## Evolution Alignment

Evolution activities shall align with Enterprise strategy, value and outcomes.

---

## Evolution Ownership

Every evolution initiative shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Evolution Governance

Evolution shall be governed through defined decision structures.

---

## Evolution Measurement

Evolution progress and impact shall be measured.

---

## Evolution Adaptability

The Enterprise shall be able to respond to internal and external changes.

---

## Evolution Sustainability

Evolution shall create sustainable long-term value.

---

## Continuous Learning

Evolution shall be supported by knowledge, experience and feedback.
