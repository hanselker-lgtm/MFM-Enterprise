```text

+
DEFINED ACCOUNTABILITY
+
DELEGATED RESPONSIBILITY
+
CONTROLLED DECISION-MAKING
+
```

# 56. Final N5-D Statement

> **N5-D establishes the controlled assessment of Risk, Compliance, Assurance and Change Governance within the MFM Post-Steady-State Governance & Control Architecture. It connects risk and compliance obligations to authority, controls, evidence, assurance, findings, remediation, change and validation. No risk acceptance, compliance state, assurance conclusion or change authorization is inferred without appropriate evidence and authority.**
