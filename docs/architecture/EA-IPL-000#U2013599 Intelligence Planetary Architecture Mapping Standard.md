# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Planetary Architecture Framework is to establish a future-oriented architecture describing how Enterprise Intelligence interacts with planetary systems, Earth processes, natural ecosystems and global environmental challenges.

The Intelligence Planetary Architecture Framework shall provide:

- planetary Intelligence principles
- Earth system Intelligence models
- planetary Intelligence ecosystem architecture
- planetary governance
- planetary sustainability
- planetary resilience
- planetary knowledge networks
- planetary innovation evolution
- planetary resource Intelligence
- climate Intelligence
- biodiversity Intelligence
- planetary trust development
- planetary measurement
- planetary maturity
- planetary evolution

The framework ensures that Intelligence development contributes to planetary balance, environmental responsibility and long-term global sustainability.
