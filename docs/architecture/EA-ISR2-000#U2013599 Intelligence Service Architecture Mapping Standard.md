# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Service Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities are delivered as intelligent services, reusable capabilities and adaptive service ecosystems.

The Intelligence Service Architecture Framework shall provide:

- service architecture principles
- service modelling frameworks
- service capability models
- service delivery mechanisms
- service integration models
- service governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through intelligent service delivery.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Service Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Service Model
- Service Intelligence Architecture
- Service Knowledge Architecture
- Service Capability Model
- Service Architecture Integration
- Service Governance Model
- Service Evolution Framework
- Service Metrics
- Service Maturity Model
- Service Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Service Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Service Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Service Value Alignment

Intelligence services shall create measurable value aligned with enterprise objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Service Integration

Services shall integrate capabilities, systems, processes and resources.

---

## Principle 3 – Service Reusability

Intelligence services shall support reuse across domains and environments.

---

## Principle 4 – Adaptive Service Delivery

Services shall adjust according to changing requirements and conditions.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Service Improvement

Services shall improve through monitoring, learning and feedback.

---

## Principle 6 – Service Reliability

Services shall maintain availability, quality and operational continuity.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Service Governance

Services shall maintain accountability, security and ethical alignment.
