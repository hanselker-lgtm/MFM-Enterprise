# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Capability Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive capability architecture for identifying, managing, developing and evolving Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Capability Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence capability principles
- capability governance
- capability architecture
- intelligence capability domains
- capability mapping
- capability assessment
- capability maturity
- capability dependency management
- capability value management
- capability investment management
- capability roadmaps
- capability evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are managed as strategic assets supporting continuous organisational development.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Capability Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture capability domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Capability Model
- Capability Governance
- Capability Architecture
- Intelligence Capability Domains
- Capability Mapping
- Capability Assessment
- Capability Dependencies
- Capability Value Management
- Capability Investment Management
- Capability Maturity
- Capability Roadmaps
- Capability Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- intelligent operating environments
- future adaptive enterprises

---

# 3. Intelligence Capability Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Capability Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Capability Alignment

Intelligence capabilities shall support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Capability as a Strategic Asset

Capabilities shall be managed as long-term Enterprise assets.

---

## Principle 3 – Capability Transparency

Capability status, ownership and maturity shall be visible.

---

## Principle 4 – Capability Evolution

Capabilities shall continuously develop according to future requirements.

---

## Principle 5 – Capability Integration

Capabilities shall operate as connected Enterprise capabilities.

---

## Principle 6 – Capability Value

Capabilities shall create measurable Enterprise value.

---

## Principle 7 – Capability Governance

Capabilities shall be controlled through defined governance structures.

# 15. Intelligence Capability Maturity Architecture

The Intelligence Capability Maturity Architecture defines how Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are evaluated, developed and evolved through structured maturity levels.

The architecture ensures that capability development is measurable, controlled and aligned with Enterprise objectives.

---

# 15.1 Capability Maturity Principles

Capability maturity shall ensure:

- measurable capability development
- structured improvement
- transparent capability status
- strategic capability alignment
- continuous evolution

---

# 15.2 Intelligence Capability Maturity Model

## Level 1 – Capability Awareness

Characteristics:

- basic understanding of required capabilities
- initial capability identification
- limited capability governance
- informal capability management

---

## Level 2 – Defined Capability

Characteristics:

- documented capability structures
- defined capability ownership
- established capability models
- basic capability management processes

---

## Level 3 – Managed Capability

Characteristics:

- operational capability management
- capability measurement
- capability improvement processes
- controlled capability development

---

## Level 4 – Integrated Capability

Characteristics:

- enterprise-wide capability integration
- capability optimisation
- dependency management
- strategic capability planning

---

## Level 5 – Adaptive Intelligence Capability

- continuously evolving capabilities
- intelligent capability optimisation

- adaptive Enterprise capability development
