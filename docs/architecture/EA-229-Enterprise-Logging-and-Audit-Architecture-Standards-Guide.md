# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Logging & Audit throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Logging & Audit provides standardized mechanisms for operational logging, audit logging, security event recording and centralized log management while preserving traceability, integrity, accountability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Operational Logging
- Audit Logging
- Structured Logging
- Security Event Logging
- Centralized Log Collection
- Log Retention
- Log Integrity
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Logging & Audit implementations shall comply with this guide.

- Unapproved external logging providers

Enterprise Logging & Audit capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Unstructured Log Data

Enterprise systems shall never generate logs in formats that prevent automated processing, correlation or analysis where structured logging is required.

---

## Missing Audit Trails

Security-sensitive or business-critical operations shall never occur without appropriate audit logging.

---

## Log Tampering

Enterprise log data shall never be modified, deleted or overwritten outside approved retention and governance procedures.

---

## Excessive Logging of Sensitive Data

Enterprise logging shall never expose passwords, cryptographic secrets, authentication tokens or other sensitive information unless explicitly approved and protected.

---

## Decentralized Log Storage

Enterprise log data shall never remain permanently isolated within individual applications when centralized logging is required.

---

## Business Logic Inside Logging Infrastructure

Enterprise Logging & Audit implementations shall never contain business logic that belongs within the appropriate Domain or Capability implementation.
