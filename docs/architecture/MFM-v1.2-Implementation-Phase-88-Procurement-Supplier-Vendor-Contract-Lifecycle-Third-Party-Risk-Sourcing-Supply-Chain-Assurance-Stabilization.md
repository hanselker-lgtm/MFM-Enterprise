# 1. Purpose

This document defines the eighty-eighth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-87 – Legal, Regulatory, Policy, Contractual, Records, Ethics & Enterprise Compliance Management Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled procurement and third-party management capability covering procurement governance, sourcing, supplier management, vendor management, contract lifecycle management, supplier onboarding, due diligence, third-party risk, supplier performance, supplier assurance, contract obligations, renewals, commercial governance, purchase-to-pay interfaces, supply-chain resilience, supplier incidents, supplier offboarding, procurement analytics and procurement assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that procurement and third-party relationships are selected, contracted, governed, monitored and concluded through a controlled lifecycle that balances value, risk, compliance, service quality, resilience and organizational objectives.**

# 139. Final Procurement Principle

> **Procurement must begin with a defined organizational need and end with controlled delivery, evidence and assurance.**

---

# 140. Final Sourcing Principle

> **Sourcing decisions must use proportionate, transparent and evidence-based evaluation criteria.**

---

# 141. Final Supplier Principle

> **Suppliers must be governed according to their criticality, dependency, risk and organizational impact.**

---

# 142. Final Due Diligence Principle

> **Material suppliers must be appropriately assessed before the organization accepts significant third-party exposure.**

---

# 143. Final Contract Principle

> **Every material contract must have clear ownership, obligations, terms, lifecycle controls and renewal or exit arrangements.**

---

# 144. Final Performance Principle

> **Supplier performance must be measured against agreed expectations and converted into corrective action where required.**

---

# 145. Final Assurance Principle

> **Third-party assurance must provide evidence that critical suppliers satisfy material security, privacy, continuity, compliance, service and contractual requirements.**

---

# 146. Final Commercial Principle

> **Procurement decisions should consider total cost, commercial risk, contractual value and long-term sustainability rather than purchase price alone.**

---

# 147. Final Resilience Principle

> **Critical supply dependencies must have sufficient visibility, risk treatment and recovery or alternative-sourcing strategies.**

---

# 148. Final Offboarding Principle

> **Supplier exit must protect continuity, security, assets, data, knowledge, financial closure and organizational records.**

---

# 149. Final Integration Principle

> **Procurement Management must integrate with Finance, Legal, Contract, Risk, Security, Privacy, Service, Asset, Configuration, Project, Data, Records and Enterprise Assurance governance.**

---

# 150. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage procurement and third-party relationships through a controlled lifecycle connecting demand, sourcing, due diligence, supplier selection, contracting, performance, risk, assurance, renewal, resilience and offboarding.**
