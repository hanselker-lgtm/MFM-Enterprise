## Purpose
Establish the authoritative non-negotiable requirements layer beneath mandatory conditions. This document defines requirements that, when applicable, shall not be downgraded, bypassed or locally reinterpreted through operational convenience, undocumented delegation, technical autonomy or informal decision-making.

## Core Principle
A non-negotiable requirement is an obligation whose mandatory character remains in force whenever its applicability conditions are satisfied. Local preference, schedule pressure, system behavior or convenience shall not remove the requirement.

```text
MANDATORY CONDITIONS
        ↓
NON-NEGOTIABLE REQUIREMENTS
        ↓
SOURCE OF OBLIGATION
        ↓
ABSOLUTE REQUIREMENT
        ↓
APPLICABILITY
        ↓
NO LOCAL OVERRIDE
        ↓
VERIFICATION
        ↓
BLOCKING FAILURE
        ↓
AUTHORIZED EXCEPTION ONLY
```

## Requirement Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Requirement | Non-negotiable obligation |
| Source | Authority, mandate, policy, law, contract or approved criterion |
| Applicability | Conditions under which it applies |
| Required State | State required for conformity |
| Authority | Role authorized to govern the requirement |
| Verification | Method used to test conformity |
| Evidence | Required proof |
| Failure Effect | Blocking, escalation or other defined consequence |
| Exception | Explicit authorized exception mechanism |
| Effective Version | Version applicable to the determination |
| Review | Reassessment requirements |

## Source of Obligation

Every non-negotiable requirement shall identify why the requirement exists and which authoritative source establishes it.

```text
SOURCE
  ↓
OBLIGATION
  ↓
MANDATORY STATUS
  ↓
APPLICABILITY
```

Sources may include governance decisions, mandates, policies, regulatory obligations, contractual commitments, architecture principles, security requirements, resilience requirements or approved outcome criteria.

## Absolute Requirements

An absolute requirement is one for which the applicable condition must be satisfied without local weakening.

```text
ABSOLUTE REQUIREMENT
        ↓
APPLICABLE?
 ├── NO  → NOT APPLICABLE
 └── YES
      ↓
SATISFIED?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → BLOCK / ESCALATE / AUTHORIZED EXCEPTION```

## Applicability

Applicability shall be determined using explicit rules. A requirement shall not be marked not applicable merely because compliance is inconvenient or difficult.

```text
REQUIREMENT
   ↓
APPLICABILITY TEST
   ↓
APPLICABLE?
 ├── NO  → RECORD N/A + EVIDENCE
 └── YES → ENFORCE```

## No Local Override

No local role, service owner, operator, developer, administrator, system or AI agent may override a non-negotiable requirement without explicit authority established by the governing architecture.

```text
LOCAL OVERRIDE REQUEST
        ↓
AUTHORIZED BY GOVERNANCE?
 ├── NO  → REJECT
 └── YES → GOVERNED EXCEPTION / OVERRIDE```

## Mandatory Authority

Authority to modify, suspend or except a non-negotiable requirement shall be explicit. Responsibility for execution shall not be confused with authority to change the requirement.

## Mandatory Security

Non-negotiable security requirements may include confidentiality, integrity, availability, authentication, authorization, vulnerability handling, incident response, protective controls and security monitoring.

```text
SECURITY REQUIREMENT
        ↓
BREACH
        ↓
NON-NEGOTIABLE FAILURE
        ↓
BLOCK / CONTAIN / ESCALATE```

## Mandatory Resilience

Non-negotiable resilience requirements may include continuity, recovery, restoration, dependency controls, degraded operation, recovery testing and service restoration.

## Mandatory Compliance

Mandatory compliance requirements shall remain binding where applicable. Operational objectives shall not be used to justify an unauthorized non-conformity.

## Mandatory Safety

Where safety is applicable, safety requirements shall remain non-negotiable and shall take precedence over convenience, schedule or throughput objectives.

## Mandatory Data

Non-negotiable data requirements may include integrity, authorized access, classification, retention, lineage, accuracy, completeness, availability and lawful or authorized use.

## Mandatory AI and Agent

AI and agents shall operate within non-negotiable governance constraints.

```text
AI / AGENT ACTION
      ↓
NON-NEGOTIABLE REQUIREMENT
      ↓
WITHIN AUTHORITY?
 ├── NO  → BLOCK
 └── YES
      ↓
REQUIREMENT SATISFIED?
 ├── NO  → BLOCK / ESCALATE
 └── YES → CONTINUE```

An agent's ability to execute an action shall never be interpreted as authority to violate a non-negotiable requirement.

## Mandatory Control

Controls protecting non-negotiable requirements shall have identifiable owners, defined operating expectations and evidence requirements.

## Mandatory Evidence

Evidence shall be attributable, relevant, sufficiently complete, current and protected against inappropriate alteration. Evidence shall identify the requirement version and verification context.

## Mandatory Verification

Verification shall be proportionate to materiality. Critical non-negotiable requirements shall receive appropriate independent assurance where required.

```text
REQUIREMENT
   ↓
VERIFICATION METHOD
   ↓
TEST / ASSESS
   ↓
RESULT
   ↓
CONFORM / NON-CONFORM```

## Blocking Effect

Failure of an applicable non-negotiable requirement shall produce the defined blocking effect and shall prevent an affected success state from being represented as fully compliant.

```text
NON-NEGOTIABLE FAILURE
        ↓
BLOCK AFFECTED SUCCESS
        ↓
CONTAIN
        ↓
ESCALATE
        ↓
REMEDIATE / AUTHORIZED EXCEPTION```

## Exception Boundary

An exception is not a local waiver. It is a governed authorization with defined scope, rationale, risk, compensating controls, authority, effective period and review.

```text
FAILURE
  ↓
EXCEPTION ELIGIBLE?
 ├── NO  → BLOCK / REMEDIATE
 └── YES
      ↓
AUTHORIZED?
 ├── NO  → BLOCK / ESCALATE
 └── YES → CONTROLLED EXCEPTION```

## Override Prevention

Systems and processes shall be designed so that routine operational paths cannot silently bypass non-negotiable requirements.

Override attempts shall be logged, attributable and subject to review where material.

## Escalation

```text
NON-NEGOTIABLE BREACH
        ↓
LOCAL AUTHORITY SUFFICIENT?
 ├── YES → APPLY APPROVED RESPONSE
 └── NO  → ESCALATE TO AUTHORIZED GOVERNANCE```

Uncertainty about authority shall result in escalation rather than assumption.

## Review and Learning

Non-negotiable requirements shall be reviewed following material breach, repeated exception, architecture change, regulatory change, risk change, mandate change or evidence that the requirement is no longer adequate.

```text
REQUIREMENT ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
STILL VALID?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → SUPERSEDE / RETIRE THROUGH GOVERNANCE```

Repeated breaches shall trigger analysis of control effectiveness, architecture, responsibility, competence, measurement and governance assumptions.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes the non-negotiable-requirements layer beneath mandatory success conditions. It does not replace governance, authority, mandate, role, responsibility, accountability, outcome, criteria, success, success conditions or mandatory-condition layers.
