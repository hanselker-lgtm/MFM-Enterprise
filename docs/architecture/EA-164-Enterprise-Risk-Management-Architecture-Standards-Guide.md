# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise risk management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise risk management ensures that business, operational, technical, financial and regulatory risks are identified, evaluated, mitigated, monitored and governed using standardized enterprise processes while preserving operational resilience, informed decision-making and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Enterprise Risk Management
- Risk Identification
- Risk Classification
- Risk Assessment
- Risk Treatment
- Risk Monitoring
- Risk Reporting
- Governance

All enterprise risk management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## RM-001

Provide standardized enterprise risk management.

---

## RM-002

Identify and classify enterprise risks.

---

## RM-003

Support informed decision-making.

---

## RM-004

Ensure complete risk traceability.

---

## RM-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Risk Management Principles

Enterprise risk management shall follow these principles.

- Risk-Based Decision Making
- Continuous Risk Assessment
- Proactive Risk Mitigation
- Governance by Default
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Risk management implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Risk Management Domains

Enterprise risk management shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Strategic Risks
- Operational Risks
- Financial Risks
- Regulatory Risks
- Technology Risks
- Information Security Risks
- Project Risks
- Third-Party Risks

Additional risk domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Risk Management Ownership

Each risk management domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the risk management lifecycle.

---

# 7. Risk Management Governance

Enterprise risk management governance shall define

- risk governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- risk verification
- governance reporting

Risk management governance shall remain technology independent.

---

# 8. Risk Management Responsibilities

Enterprise risk management shall provide controlled management of enterprise risks.

Risk management responsibilities shall

- separate risk management from business execution
- coordinate risk ownership
- ensure consistent risk evaluation
- validate mitigation objectives
- preserve risk traceability
- support enterprise resilience

Risk management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Risk Identification

Enterprise risk management shall implement standardized risk identification.

Risk identification shall

- identify business risks
- identify operational risks
- identify technology risks
- identify regulatory risks
- preserve identification history
- maintain identification traceability

Risk identification shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Risk Classification

Enterprise risk management shall classify all identified risks.

Risk classifications shall

- define risk category
- define likelihood
- define business impact
- define technical impact
- preserve classification history
- maintain classification traceability

Risk classifications shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Risk Assessment

Enterprise risk management shall implement standardized risk assessment.

Risk assessment shall

- evaluate likelihood
- evaluate impact
- determine risk level
- prioritize mitigation
- preserve assessment history
- maintain assessment traceability

Risk assessments shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Risk Treatment

Enterprise risk management shall implement standardized risk treatment.

Risk treatment shall

- reduce enterprise risks
- support mitigation planning
- support risk acceptance
- support risk transfer where applicable
- preserve treatment history
- maintain treatment traceability

Risk treatment shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Risk Management Dependencies

Enterprise risk management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- compliance capabilities
- security capabilities
- monitoring systems
- reporting platforms
- enterprise infrastructure

Risk management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Risk Management Documentation

Each risk management domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- risk registers
- ownership information
- risk assessments
- treatment plans
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Risk Management Lifecycle

Enterprise risk management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Identified
- Classified
- Assessed
- Planned
- Mitigated
- Verified
- Monitored
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Closed

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Risk Management Quality Attributes

Enterprise risk management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- reliability
- governance compliance
- operational effectiveness

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the risk management lifecycle.

---

# 17. Risk Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized risk register.

The risk register shall contain

- risk identifiers
- ownership assignments
- classifications
- assessment results
- treatment plans
- lifecycle status
- documentation references
- governance status

The risk register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise risks.

---

# 18. Risk Reviews

Enterprise risk management implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- risk identification completeness
- classification accuracy
- assessment quality
- treatment effectiveness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Risk Metrics

Enterprise risk management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- identified risks
- assessed risks
- mitigated risks
- accepted risks
- unresolved risks
- overdue treatments
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous risk management improvement.

---

# 20. Risk Verification

Enterprise risk management implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm risk identification
- verify classifications
- verify assessments
- verify treatment plans
- verify ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- confirm governance compliance
- approve operational readiness

Risk verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Risk Management Improvement

Enterprise risk management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve risk visibility
- improve mitigation effectiveness
- improve governance
- improve enterprise resilience
- improve decision support
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise risk management implementations shall handle risk management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify risk identification failures
- classify risk assessment failures
- classify risk treatment failures
- classify risk monitoring failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Risk management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational resilience or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Risk management implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved compliance capabilities
- approved security capabilities
- approved monitoring systems
- approved reporting platforms
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Risk management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external risk management services

Risk management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A risk management implementation is compliant when

- Risk management responsibilities are documented.
- Risk identification procedures are implemented.
- Risk classifications are documented.
- Risk assessments are completed.
- Risk treatment plans are maintained.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Risk Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Risk verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Risk Identification

Enterprise risk management shall never operate without systematic risk identification.

---

## Undefined Risk Ownership

Enterprise risks shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Untreated High Risks

High and critical risks shall never remain without documented treatment or approved acceptance.

---

## Missing Risk Monitoring

Enterprise risks shall never remain without ongoing monitoring and periodic reassessment.

---

## Undocumented Risk Dependencies

Risk management implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Risk Management Outside Governance

Risk management implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise risk management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- risk management quality
- identification completeness
- assessment quality
- treatment effectiveness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- enterprise resilience
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Risk Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise risk management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and business operations implement standardized risk identification, classification, assessment, treatment, monitoring, verification and governance through controlled lifecycle management and continuous improvement while preserving operational resilience, informed decision-making and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise risk management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
