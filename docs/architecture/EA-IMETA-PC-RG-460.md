# 2. Purpose

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-460 establishes the anticipatory governance layer above the predictive decision-support architecture defined by RG-459.

RG-459 determines whether evidence, forecasts and scenarios justify anticipatory action.

RG-460 determines how competing scenarios are arbitrated and how multiple enterprise responses are coordinated when a future condition could affect several domains simultaneously.

The architecture SHALL answer:

> **How does the enterprise convert competing predictions into one coherent, proportionate and governed pre-emptive response without creating conflicting actions, excessive commitment, uncontrolled automation or avoidable resilience degradation?**

The architecture SHALL distinguish:

```text
ANTICIPATORY GOVERNANCE
= GOVERNANCE THAT CONVERTS CREDIBLE FUTURE CONDITIONS INTO CONTROLLED PREPARATION, RESERVATION OR INTERVENTION

SCENARIO ARBITRATION
= GOVERNED COMPARISON OF COMPETING FUTURE SCENARIOS FOR THE PURPOSE OF SELECTING A COHERENT ENTERPRISE RESPONSE

SCENARIO PRIORITY
= RELATIVE GOVERNANCE PRIORITY ASSIGNED TO A SCENARIO

SCENARIO WEIGHT
= GOVERNED REPRESENTATION OF THE RELATIVE IMPORTANCE OF A SCENARIO

SCENARIO CONFLICT
= CONDITION WHERE DIFFERENT SCENARIOS REQUIRE INCOMPATIBLE OR COMPETING RESPONSES

SCENARIO ARBITRATION RULE
= DEFINED RULE FOR RESOLVING COMPETING SCENARIO IMPLICATIONS

RESILIENCE ACTIVATION
= CONTROLLED INCREASE IN ENTERPRISE READINESS OR RESPONSE CAPABILITY

PRE-EMPTIVE RESILIENCE
= RESILIENCE ACTION TAKEN BEFORE A MATERIAL DISRUPTION OCCURS

READINESS STATE
= CURRENT LEVEL OF PREPARATION FOR A DEFINED FUTURE CONDITION

READINESS ESCALATION
= CONTROLLED MOVEMENT TO A HIGHER PREPARATION STATE

READINESS DE-ESCALATION
= CONTROLLED RETURN TO A LOWER PREPARATION STATE

RESILIENCE POSTURE
= GOVERNED SET OF CAPABILITIES, RESERVES, CONTROLS AND AUTHORITIES AVAILABLE FOR A FUTURE condition

INTERVENTION ORCHESTRATION
= COORDINATION OF MULTIPLE ACTIONS SO THAT THEY FORM ONE COHERENT RESPONSE

INTERVENTION DEPENDENCY
= CONDITION WHERE ONE INTERVENTION REQUIRES ANOTHER INTERVENTION

INTERVENTION COLLISION
= CONDITION WHERE TWO ACTIONS COMPETE OR CREATE CONFLICT

INTERVENTION SEQUENCING
= ORDERING OF ACTIONS TO MAXIMISE EFFECT AND MINIMISE HARM

INTERVENTION BOUNDARY
= LIMIT ON SCOPE, MAGNITUDE OR DURATION OF AN ACTION

BOUNDED AUTONOMY
= AUTOMATED OR DELEGATED ACTION WITH EXPLICIT POLICY, AUTHORITY, LIMITS AND OVERRIDE

RESILIENCE RESERVE
= CAPACITY, CAPITAL, PEOPLE OR OTHER RESOURCES DELIBERATELY PROTECTED FOR DISRUPTION RESPONSE

READINESS DEBT
= REQUIRED PREPARATION THAT HAS NOT BEEN COMPLETED

INTERVENTION DEBT
= APPROVED OR REQUIRED ANTICIPATORY ACTION THAT REMAINS UNRESOLVED

SCENARIO DEBT
= IMPORTANT FUTURE CONDITION THAT HAS NOT BEEN ADEQUATELY ASSESSED

ARBITRATION DEBT
= UNRESOLVED CONFLICT BETWEEN COMPETING SCENARIOS OR RESPONSE strategies

RESPONSE SATURATION
= CONDITION WHERE THE AGGREGATE NUMBER OR INTENSITY OF RESPONSES EXCEEDS ORGANISATIONAL ABSORPTION CAPACITY

RESILIENCE CONFLICT
= CONDITION WHERE AN ACTION INTENDED TO INCREASE RESILIENCE DEGRADES ANOTHER CRITICAL CAPABILITY

PRE-EMPTIVE VALUE
= VALUE CREATED BY ACTING BEFORE A DISRUPTION BECOMES MATERIAL

PRE-EMPTIVE COST
= COST CREATED BY PREPARATION OR EARLY INTERVENTION

OPTIONALITY
= ABILITY TO RETAIN MULTIPLE VIABLE FUTURE RESPONSE PATHS

RESPONSE REVERSIBILITY
= ABILITY TO WITHDRAW OR MODIFY A PRE-EMPTIVE RESPONSE WITHOUT MATERIAL LOSS

SCENARIO DOMINANCE
= CONDITION WHERE ONE SCENARIO HAS SUFFICIENT GOVERNANCE WEIGHT TO DRIVE THE PRIMARY RESPONSE

ROBUST RESPONSE
= RESPONSE THAT PERFORMS ACCEPTABLY ACROSS MULTIPLE PLAUSIBLE SCENARIOS

NO-REGRET ACTION
= ACTION THAT PROVIDES BENEFIT ACROSS MOST MATERIAL SCENARIOS WITH LIMITED DOWNSIDE

CONTINGENT RESPONSE
= PREPARED RESPONSE THAT ACTIVATES ONLY WHEN A DEFINED CONDITION OCCURS

RESPONSE GATE
= GOVERNANCE POINT AT WHICH READINESS OR INTERVENTION MAY ESCALATE

RESILIENCE TRIGGER
= OBSERVABLE CONDITION THAT ACTIVATES A DEFINED RESPONSE

DEACTIVATION TRIGGER
= CONDITION THAT JUSTIFIES REDUCING OR TERMINATING AN ACTIVE response

RECOVERY HANDOVER
= CONTROLLED TRANSFER FROM ANTICIPATORY RESPONSE TO RECOVERY GOVERNANCE

ANTICIPATORY LEARNING
= CONVERSION OF PRE-EMPTIVE RESPONSE EXPERIENCE INTO IMPROVED FUTURE GOVERNANCE


# 3. Core Principle

> **The enterprise SHALL arbitrate competing future conditions using evidence, materiality, resilience impact, reversibility and strategic value, and SHALL coordinate pre-emptive actions within explicit authority boundaries so that the response is robust across plausible futures without creating unnecessary instability.**

The governing chain is:

```text
PREDICT
  ↓
COMPARE SCENARIOS
  ↓
ARBITRATE
  ↓
SELECT RESPONSE POSTURE
  ↓
ACTIVATE READINESS
  ↓
ORCHESTRATE
  ↓
INTERVENE
  ↓
VERIFY
  ↓
DE-ESCALATE / RECOVER
  ↓
LEARN
```

# 227. Acceptance Criteria

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-460 is accepted when:

- competing scenarios can be represented and compared;
- arbitration criteria are explicit and traceable;
- scenario probability is separated from scenario certainty;
- robust and no-regret actions can be identified;
- readiness states and transitions are governed;
- resilience reserves are visible and protected;
- intervention dependencies and collisions are detectable;
- pre-emptive actions have explicit boundaries and authorities;
- response saturation and change load are visible;
- bounded autonomy is enforceable;
- human authority remains explicit for material decisions;
- cross-domain impacts are assessed;
- intervention effectiveness is verified;
- deactivation and recovery handover are governed;
- readiness, scenario and arbitration debt remain visible;
- AI assistance is bounded, explainable and auditable;
- manual fallback exists;
- historical reconstruction is possible;
- negative and scenario tests prevent unsupported anticipatory activation.


# 228. Next Step

The next logical artifact is the enterprise **resilience response mesh, multi-domain coordination, crisis transition and adaptive command orchestration model**, because RG-460 establishes scenario arbitration and bounded pre-emptive intervention, while the next layer should govern coordinated execution when several domains must respond simultaneously.


# 229. Governing Principle

> **Enterprise anticipation SHALL become coordinated resilience only when competing futures can be arbitrated, readiness can be escalated proportionately and interventions can be orchestrated within explicit boundaries; therefore no predictive signal alone SHALL dictate enterprise action, and every material pre-emptive response SHALL remain evidence-based, authorised, bounded, reversible where practical and measurable through outcome feedback.**

The PC-RG architecture SHALL consequently evolve from predictive decision support into coordinated anticipatory resilience governance.
