# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise master data management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise master data management ensures that master data is identified, governed, standardized, synchronized, protected and maintained through standardized enterprise processes while preserving consistency, integrity, interoperability, governance and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Master Data Management
- Master Data Governance
- Master Data Ownership
- Master Data Stewardship
- Master Data Quality
- Master Data Synchronization
- Master Data Repositories
- Continuous Improvement

All enterprise master data management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Master Data Management Principles

Enterprise master data management shall follow these principles.

- Master Data by Design
- Governance by Default
- Single Source of Truth
- Standardization
- Traceability
- Accountability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Master data management implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Master Data Domains

Enterprise master data management shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Person Master Data
- Organization Master Data
- Address Master Data
- Geographic Master Data
- Financial Master Data
- Asset Master Data
- Reference Master Data
- System Master Data

Additional master data domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Master Data Management Ownership

Each master data domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- master data stewardship

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the master data lifecycle.

---

# 7. Master Data Management Governance

Enterprise master data management governance shall define

- master data governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- master data verification
- governance reporting

Master data management governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Master Data Management Responsibilities

Enterprise master data management shall provide controlled management of enterprise master data assets.

Master data management responsibilities shall

- separate master data management from business execution
- coordinate master data ownership
- ensure master data consistency
- validate master data objectives
- preserve master data traceability
- support enterprise governance

Master data management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Master Data Identification

Enterprise master data management shall implement standardized master data identification.

Master data identification shall

- define unique identifiers
- identify authoritative data sources
- identify ownership
- identify lifecycle status
- preserve identification history
- maintain identification traceability

Master data identification shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Master Data Standardization

Enterprise master data management shall implement standardized master data definitions.

Master data standardization shall

- define canonical data models
- define mandatory attributes
- define optional attributes
- define validation rules
- preserve standards history
- maintain standards traceability

Master data standardization shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Master Data Synchronization

Enterprise master data management shall implement standardized synchronization.

Synchronization shall

- synchronize approved master data
- preserve authoritative sources
- prevent conflicting updates
- preserve synchronization history
- maintain synchronization traceability
- support enterprise interoperability

Synchronization shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Master Data Quality

Enterprise master data management shall implement standardized master data quality.

Master data quality shall

- validate completeness
- validate consistency
- validate uniqueness
- validate accuracy
- preserve quality history
- maintain quality traceability

Master data quality shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Master Data Management Dependencies

Enterprise master data management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- master data repositories
- integration platforms
- metadata repositories
- enterprise repositories
- enterprise infrastructure

Master data management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Master Data Documentation

Each master data domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- master data registers
- ownership information
- standard definitions
- synchronization specifications
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Master Data Lifecycle

Enterprise master data management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Identified
- Defined
- Approved
- Published
- Synchronized
- Maintained
- Reviewed
- Updated
- Retired
- Archived

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Master Data Quality Attributes

Enterprise master data management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- interoperability
- governance compliance
- master data reliability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the master data lifecycle.

---

# 17. Master Data Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized master data register.

The master data register shall contain

- master data identifiers
- ownership assignments
- standard definitions
- lifecycle status
- synchronization status
- documentation references
- review schedules
- governance status

The master data register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise master data.

---

# 18. Master Data Reviews

Enterprise master data management implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- master data completeness
- master data consistency
- standards compliance
- synchronization integrity
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Master Data Metrics

Enterprise master data management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- managed master data entities
- synchronized master data entities
- overdue master data reviews
- master data quality score
- synchronization success rate
- master data revisions
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous master data improvement.

---

# 20. Master Data Verification

Enterprise master data management implementations shall undergo formal verification before operational use and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm master data completeness
- verify ownership
- verify standard definitions
- verify synchronization readiness
- verify documentation completeness
- verify interoperability
- confirm governance compliance
- approve operational use

Master data verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Master Data Improvement

Enterprise master data management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve master data quality
- improve governance
- improve interoperability
- improve enterprise consistency
- improve regulatory compliance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise master data management implementations shall handle master data-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify master data identification failures
- classify master data standardization failures
- classify synchronization failures
- classify master data quality failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Master data management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, data integrity, interoperability, governance or regulatory obligations.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Master data management implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved master data repositories
- approved metadata repositories
- approved integration platforms
- approved enterprise repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Master data management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external master data management services

Master data management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A master data management implementation is compliant when

- Master data management responsibilities are documented.
- Master data identification procedures are implemented.
- Master data standards are documented.
- Master data synchronization is implemented.
- Master data quality requirements are implemented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Master Data Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Master data verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Master Data Ownership

Enterprise master data shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Multiple Sources of Truth

Enterprise master data shall never have multiple competing authoritative sources.

---

## Missing Master Data Standards

Enterprise master data shall never be managed without approved standard definitions.

---

## Unsynchronized Master Data

Enterprise master data shall never diverge between enterprise systems without approved synchronization governance.

---

## Undocumented Master Data Dependencies

Master data management implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Master Data Management Outside Governance

Master data management implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

Enterprise master data management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- master data management quality
- master data completeness
- standards compliance
- synchronization integrity
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- enterprise interoperability
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Master Data Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise master data management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise master data is identified, governed, standardized, synchronized, protected and continuously improved through standardized lifecycle management while preserving consistency, integrity, interoperability, governance, accountability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise master data management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
