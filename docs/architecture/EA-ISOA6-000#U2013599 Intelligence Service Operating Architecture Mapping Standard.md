# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational service architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities are delivered as controlled, measurable and value-generating services to users, organisations and ecosystems.

The Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- service operating principles
- service governance frameworks
- service lifecycle models
- service management models
- service design models
- service delivery models
- service catalogue models
- service level models
- service support models
- service experience models
- service integration models
- service monitoring models
- service automation models
- service improvement models
- service evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for reliable, measurable and continuously improving Intelligence services.

# 3. Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Service Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

## Principle 1 – Service by Design

Service capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Value-Oriented Services

Services shall deliver measurable value to users and stakeholders.

## Principle 3 – Controlled Service Delivery

Services shall operate through defined processes and controls.

## Principle 4 – Service Quality

## Principle 5 – User-Centred Experience
