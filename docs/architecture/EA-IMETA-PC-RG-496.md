# 2. Architectural Position

RG-495 established multi-horizon strategic value systems, horizon balancing, option preservation, resilience balancing and autonomous strategic value orchestration.

RG-496 extends the architecture into the **strategic option network**: future choices are no longer treated as isolated investments but as an interconnected portfolio of options, triggers, dependencies, capabilities, scenarios and resilience positions.

```text
RG-494 OUTCOME-STRATEGY ALIGNMENT
        ↓
RG-495 MULTI-HORIZON STRATEGIC VALUE
        ↓
RG-496 STRATEGIC OPTION NETWORKS
        ↓
FUTURE-STATE INVESTMENT
        ↓
DISCONTINUITY READINESS
        ↓
ADAPTIVE RESILIENCE PORTFOLIOS
        ↓
AUTONOMIC STRATEGIC OPTION ORCHESTRATION
```

# 3. Core Architectural Question

**How can the enterprise continuously preserve, develop, connect and selectively exercise strategic options so that it remains capable of responding to discontinuities without sacrificing current resilience, liquidity, strategic coherence or human authority?**

# 4. Governing Principle

**Strategic options SHALL be treated as governed enterprise assets. Their preservation, readiness, exercise and retirement SHALL be based on explicit triggers, value, cost, resilience, dependencies and authority. Autonomous systems MAY identify and coordinate option actions within approved envelopes, but SHALL never independently redefine strategic intent or create new strategic decision rights.**

```text
STRATEGIC INTENT
      ↓
OPTION NETWORK
      ↓
FUTURE-STATE PATHS
      ↓
READINESS
      ↓
TRIGGER
      ↓
EXERCISE
      ↓
VALUE / RESILIENCE
      ↓
LEARNING
      ↺
```

# 7. Strategic Option Network Architecture

The option network SHALL represent:

```text
OPTION A ─────→ OPTION B
   │              │
   ↓              ↓
FUTURE STATE A → FUTURE STATE B
   │              │
   └──────→ ENTERPRISE VALUE
```

Relationships SHALL distinguish dependencies, synergies, conflicts, cascades and substitutions.

# 12. Option Trigger Architecture

Triggers SHALL be:

- measurable;
- attributable;
- time-bounded where appropriate;
- resistant to manipulation;
- associated with decision authority.

Weak or ambiguous signals SHALL trigger assessment rather than automatic exercise.

# 13. Trigger Integrity

Trigger measurements SHALL preserve provenance and confidence.

An autonomous system SHALL not manufacture or selectively suppress trigger evidence.

# 14. Partial and Staged Exercise

Options MAY be exercised partially or in stages to preserve flexibility.

- stage limits;
- stop conditions;
- evidence requirements;
- escalation points.
