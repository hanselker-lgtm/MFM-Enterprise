# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Technology Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise technology as a strategic foundation for business capabilities, applications, information and services.

The Technology Architecture Framework shall provide:

- technology principles
- technology governance
- technology structures
- technology standards
- technology lifecycle management
- technology integration
- technology improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise technology supports business objectives, application services, information management and operational requirements.

---

# 2. Scope

The Technology Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture technology domains:

- Enterprise Technology Model
- Technology Governance
- Technology Strategy
- Technology Domains
- Technology Standards
- Technology Platforms
- Technology Components
- Technology Lifecycle Management
- Technology Integration
- Technology Security Alignment
- Technology Performance
- Technology Rationalization
- Technology Modernization
- Technology Evolution

The framework applies to:

- technology platforms
- infrastructure technologies
- software platforms
- integration technologies
- cloud technologies
- digital technologies
- transformation initiatives

---

# 3. Enterprise Technology Architecture Principles

The Technology Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Business Alignment

Technology shall support Enterprise capabilities, services, applications and strategic objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Technology Standardization

Technology shall be managed through defined standards and approved patterns.

---

## Principle 3 – Technology Lifecycle Management

Technology shall be managed throughout its complete lifecycle.

---

## Principle 4 – Security by Design

Technology solutions shall include security requirements from the beginning.

---

## Principle 5 – Integration Capability

Technology shall support controlled interoperability and integration.

---

## Principle 6 – Reuse and Optimization

Technology shall promote reuse, efficiency and reduced complexity.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

Technology shall continuously evolve according to business and technical requirements.

---

# 9. Technology Domain Architecture

The Technology Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise technology is structured into logical domains to support governance, standardization and effective management.

Technology domains provide a common structure for organizing Enterprise technology capabilities and assets.

---

# 9.1 Technology Domain Principles

Technology domains shall ensure:

- clear technology ownership
- logical technology organization
- architecture alignment
- controlled technology usage
- consistent technology management
