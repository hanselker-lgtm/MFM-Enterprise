## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-regression-consequence layer defining how a classified regression determines the required protection, containment, response, escalation, reopening, remediation, risk treatment and governance action.

## Core Principle
Classification states what the regression is; consequence determines what must happen because of it. Consequence shall be proportionate to materiality, severity, scope, confidence and potential impact, and shall never be selected merely for administrative convenience.

```text
DETECTED REGRESSION
      ↓
CLASSIFICATION
      ↓
MATERIALITY + SEVERITY + SCOPE + CONFIDENCE
      ↓
CONSEQUENCE MAPPING
      ↓
PROTECT / CONTAIN / ALERT / ESCALATE / REOPEN / REMEDIATE
      ↓
VERIFY EFFECTIVENESS
```

VALID CLASSIFICATION

```

CONSEQUENCE SHALL DEFINE WHEN REOPENING IS MANDATORY
```

CONSEQUENCE CHANGES SHALL BE VERSIONED AND JUSTIFIED

SECURITY, RESILIENCE, SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE CONSEQUENCES SHALL RECEIVE APPROPRIATE GOVERNANCE

AI AND AGENT CONSEQUENCES SHALL PROTECT HUMAN AND GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY WHEN BOUNDARIES ARE EXCEEDED

DISPUTED CONSEQUENCE SHALL NOT DELAY NECESSARY PROTECTIVE ACTION

## Regression Consequence Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Classification | Regression meaning |
| Materiality | Governance significance |
| Severity | Potential consequence level |
| Scope | Affected boundary |
| Consequence | Required treatment |
| Authority | Decision authority |
| Action | Protection / containment / remediation |
| Evidence | Decision basis |
| Reassessment | Follow-on governance path |

## Regression Consequence Objective

Ensure every material regression produces a timely and proportionate governance consequence that protects the required state and drives the condition toward controlled resolution.

## Regression Consequence Definition

A regression consequence is the governed determination of what must occur because a detected and classified regression affects the accepted state.

## Regression Consequence Scope

Scope shall identify affected services, systems, controls, processes, data, environments, dependencies, users and governance boundaries.

## Regression Consequence Authority

Authority shall define who may determine consequence, authorize protective action, accept residual risk where permitted, require reopening and invoke emergency governance.

## Regression Consequence Criteria

Criteria shall map classification to mandatory treatment.

```text
CLASSIFICATION
↓
MATERIALITY / SEVERITY
├── LOW → MONITOR / LOCAL CONTROL
├── MODERATE → CONTROLLED REMEDIATION
├── HIGH → ESCALATE / REOPEN / REMEDIATE
└── CRITICAL → PROTECT / CONTAIN / IMMEDIATE ESCALATION
```

## Regression Consequence Severity

Severity shall describe the potential effect if the regression persists or worsens and shall drive urgency and authority.

## Regression Consequence Materiality

Materiality shall consider effect on required state, accepted risk, security, resilience, compliance, data integrity, safety and governance outcomes.

## Regression Consequence Timing

Timing shall define when protective action, escalation, reopening and remediation must occur. Critical consequences shall not wait for routine governance cycles.

## Regression Consequence Evidence

Evidence shall preserve classification, rationale, authority, consequence mapping, decision timestamp and resulting actions.

## Regression Consequence Mapping

Consequence mappings shall be explicit, versioned and tested so that materially similar regressions receive consistent treatment.

## Regression Consequence Decision

The decision shall explicitly select one or more governed treatments.

```text
PROTECT
CONTAIN
ALERT
ESCALATE
REOPEN
REMEDIATE
INCREASE MONITORING
AUTHORIZED RISK ACCEPTANCE
```

## Security Regression Consequence

Security regression may require access restriction, containment, exposure reduction, incident response, escalation, reopening and renewed validation.

## Resilience Regression Consequence

Resilience regression may require capacity protection, service limitation, contingency activation, recovery action, escalation and reopening.

## Compliance Regression Consequence

Compliance regression may require corrective action, control restoration, formal escalation, reporting where required and reopening of the underlying condition.

## Data Regression Consequence

Data regression may require access restriction, correction, quarantine, lineage investigation, retention action and renewed validation.

## AI and Agent Regression Consequence

AI and agent regression may require limiting or suspending autonomy, restricting tools or data, human takeover, escalation, reopening and revalidation.

```text
REGRESSION
↓
BOUNDARY IMPACT?
├── NO → CONTROL / MONITOR
└── YES → LIMIT / SUSPEND AUTONOMY
          ↓
       HUMAN / GOVERNANCE CONTROL
          ↓
       REOPEN / REASSESS / REMEDIATE
```

## Regression Consequence Failure

Failure to apply a required consequence is itself a control failure and shall trigger immediate assessment and, where necessary, fallback or higher authority action.

```text
REQUIRED CONSEQUENCE
↓
APPLIED?
├── YES → CONTINUE
└── NO → CONTROL FAILURE
          ↓
       FALLBACK / ESCALATE / PROTECT
```

## Regression Consequence Escalation

Escalation shall occur when consequence exceeds local authority, required action is blocked, materiality increases, risk remains uncontrolled or the selected consequence is ineffective.

## Regression Consequence Review and Learning

Consequence performance shall be reviewed for underreaction, overreaction, inconsistent mapping, delayed action, ineffective remediation and repeated reopening.

## Regression Consequence Determination Model
```text
CLASSIFIED REGRESSION
↓
MATERIALITY / SEVERITY VALID?
├── NO → REASSESS CLASSIFICATION
└── YES
     ↓
MANDATORY CONSEQUENCE MAPPING EXISTS?
├── NO → GOVERNANCE GAP / ESCALATE
└── YES
     ↓
PROTECTIVE ACTION REQUIRED?
├── YES → PROTECT / CONTAIN
└── NO → CONTINUE GOVERNED RESPONSE
     ↓
REOPENING REQUIRED?
├── YES → REOPEN / REASSESS
└── NO → REMEDIATE / MONITOR
     ↓
EFFECTIVE?
├── NO → ESCALATE / CHANGE CONSEQUENCE
└── YES → CONTINUE / RESOLVE
```

## Consequence Dispute
A disputed consequence shall be resolved through defined authority. Protective action shall continue where credible material risk exists.

## Consequence Anti-Gaming Control
Consequences shall not be weakened, delayed or reclassified to preserve closure metrics, avoid reopening, reduce reporting or transfer accountability. The governed risk and required state remain controlling.

Historical consequence decisions, mapping versions, changes and actions shall remain preserved according to applicable retention and governance requirements.
