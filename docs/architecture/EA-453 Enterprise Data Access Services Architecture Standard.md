# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Access Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Access principles are inherited from EA-445.

Enterprise Data Consumption Services principles are inherited from EA-452.

All Enterprise Data Access Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Access Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and policy-driven access to authoritative Enterprise information for applications, services, analytics platforms, AI solutions and external consumers.

Enterprise Data Access Services shall

- provide standardized access services
- enforce authorization policies
- strengthen security
- improve interoperability
- simplify consumer integration
- optimize scalability
- improve operational efficiency
- enable automation
- support governance
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Access Services shall provide reusable capabilities ensuring trusted access to Enterprise information.

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise applications
- business services
- APIs
- event consumers
- streaming consumers
- analytics platforms
- AI platforms
- reporting platforms
- cloud services
- external consumers

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or implementation technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Access Services Principles

Enterprise Data Access Services shall follow these principles.

## Secure by Default

Every access request shall be authenticated, authorized, encrypted and audited before Enterprise information is made available.

---

## Least Privilege Access

Access Services shall grant only the minimum level of information access required to perform approved business functions.

---

## Policy Driven Access

Enterprise access decisions shall be governed by centrally managed Enterprise policies.

---

## Metadata Driven Access

Access Services shall use Enterprise metadata to determine authorization, routing, classification and lifecycle management.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Access Services shall remain independent of vendors, middleware products, cloud providers and infrastructure technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Access Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed access to Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating standardized access services, authorization mechanisms, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled access to authoritative Enterprise information while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Access Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise access capabilities across the Enterprise.
