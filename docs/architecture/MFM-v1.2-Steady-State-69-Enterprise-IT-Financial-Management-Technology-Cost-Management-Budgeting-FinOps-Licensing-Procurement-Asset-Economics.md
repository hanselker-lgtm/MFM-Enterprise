# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the sixty-ninth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-68 – Enterprise Workplace, End-User Computing, Collaboration, Communication, Productivity & Digital Employee Experience.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for IT financial management, technology cost management, IT budgeting, forecasting, financial planning, FinOps, cloud economics, technology licensing, software licensing, subscription management, procurement economics, technology asset economics, total cost of ownership, business cases, investment governance, cost allocation, chargeback and showback, vendor economics, contract economics, financial controls, technology asset lifecycle economics, depreciation coordination, financial assurance, financial findings, financial exceptions, remediation, technology cost metrics, financial dashboards, economic maturity and continual technology-financial improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage technology expenditure, investment and asset economics transparently and responsibly so that technology spending is aligned with business value, risk, service requirements, lifecycle needs and available financial resources.**

# 3. IT Financial Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure technology financial resources are planned, controlled, transparent and aligned with strategic and operational priorities.**

# 4. Technology Cost Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Understand and manage the full cost of technology services, assets, platforms, applications and capabilities.**

# 5. Budget Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish realistic budgets and forecasts that support sustainable technology operations and investment.**

# 6. FinOps Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Optimize cloud and consumption-based technology expenditure while preserving required business value, performance, security and resilience.**

# 7. Licensing Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure technology licenses and subscriptions are economically justified, compliant, appropriately utilized and lifecycle-controlled.**

# 8. Procurement Economics Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure technology procurement decisions consider price, value, lifecycle, risk, dependencies, contractual obligations and total cost of ownership.**

# 9. Asset Economics Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Manage technology assets throughout their lifecycle with visibility of acquisition, operating, support, renewal and retirement economics.**

# 10. Investment Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Direct technology investment toward initiatives that provide appropriate business value, risk reduction, regulatory compliance, resilience or strategic capability.**

# 97. Definition of Ready

A technology budget, forecast, procurement, license renewal, cloud optimization initiative, investment, business case, cost-allocation model, asset decision, exception or remediation item is Ready when purpose, owner, financial scope, cost drivers, assumptions, dependencies, risk, expected value, approval requirements and evidence requirements are defined.
