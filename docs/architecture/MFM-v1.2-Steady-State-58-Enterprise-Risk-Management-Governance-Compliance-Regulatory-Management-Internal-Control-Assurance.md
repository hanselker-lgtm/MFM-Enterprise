# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the fifty-eighth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-57 – Enterprise Privacy, Personal Data Protection, Records, Information Governance, Legal Hold & Data Rights.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for enterprise risk management, governance, compliance management, regulatory management, internal control, control ownership, risk appetite, risk assessment, risk treatment, risk registers, compliance obligations, regulatory change, policy management, control testing, assurance, internal audit coordination, findings, exceptions, remediation, management review, governance reporting, risk metrics, compliance metrics, control maturity and continual governance improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain an accountable, risk-based and evidence-driven governance system that identifies and manages enterprise risks, fulfills applicable obligations, maintains effective internal controls and provides reliable assurance to management and stakeholders.**

# 3. Enterprise Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify, assess, manage and monitor risks that could prevent MFM from achieving its objectives.**

# 4. Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure clear accountability, decision rights, oversight and escalation across MFM.**

# 5. Compliance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify applicable obligations and maintain effective processes and controls to meet them.**

# 6. Regulatory Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify, assess and implement relevant changes in laws, regulations and regulatory expectations.**

# 7. Internal Control Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain controls that reduce material risks to acceptable levels and provide evidence of effective operation.**

# 8. Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide objective and evidence-based confidence that governance, risk, compliance and control arrangements operate effectively.**

# 119. Definition of Ready

A risk, compliance obligation, regulatory change, control, finding, exception, assurance activity or remediation item is Ready when scope, ownership, requirement, risk, priority, dependencies, evidence requirements, acceptance criteria and applicable deadlines are defined.

# 121. Final Risk Principle

> **MFM must understand and manage risk in proportion to its potential impact on objectives, stakeholders and obligations.**

# 122. Final Governance Principle

> **Accountability, decision rights, oversight and escalation must be explicit and traceable.**

# 123. Final Compliance Principle

> **Material obligations must be identified, owned, mapped to controls and supported by evidence of compliance.**

# 124. Final Regulatory Principle

> **Regulatory change must be identified early, assessed for impact and implemented within applicable requirements and deadlines.**

# 125. Final Control Principle

> **Controls must be designed around material risks, operated consistently, tested appropriately and supported by reliable evidence.**

# 126. Final Assurance Principle

> **Assurance must provide objective, risk-based and evidence-supported confidence in governance, risk, compliance and control effectiveness.**

# 127. Final Finding Principle

> **Material findings must be risk-rated, assigned, remediated, independently or objectively validated where appropriate and formally closed.**

# 128. Final Exception Principle

> **Exceptions must be explicit, risk-assessed, approved, time-bound and periodically reviewed.**

# 129. Final Improvement Principle

> **Risk events, control failures, findings, regulatory changes, assurance results and lessons learned must continuously improve MFM governance and control capability.**

# 130. Final Integration Principle

> **Enterprise Risk, Governance, Compliance and Assurance must integrate with Enterprise Architecture, Cybersecurity, Data, Privacy, Applications, Infrastructure, Service Management, Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Suppliers, Finance, People and Project Management.**

# 131. Final Steady-State Governance Principle

> **MFM must maintain an accountable, risk-based and evidence-driven governance system that identifies and manages enterprise risks, fulfills applicable obligations, maintains effective internal controls and provides reliable assurance to management and stakeholders.**
