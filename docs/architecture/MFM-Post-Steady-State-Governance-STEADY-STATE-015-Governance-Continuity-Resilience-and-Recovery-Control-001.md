# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-015 establishes the controlled mechanism for maintaining governance continuity during disruption, loss of key personnel, unavailable systems, evidence loss, major incidents, organizational change and other conditions that could impair normal governance operation.

The purpose is not merely to keep operations running. It is to preserve the ability to make, authorize, record, validate and assure governance decisions under degraded conditions.

The operating sequence is:

```text
DISRUPTION
↓
DETECT
↓
ASSESS
↓
ACTIVATE CONTINUITY
↓
ESTABLISH GOVERNANCE MINIMUM
↓
OPERATE UNDER CONTROL
↓
RECOVER
↓
RECONCILE
↓
VALIDATE
↓
RETURN TO NORMAL GOVERNANCE
```

---

# 2. Core Principle

Governance continuity means preserving governance integrity, not merely preserving activity.

The following distinctions are mandatory:

```text
SYSTEM AVAILABLE
≠
GOVERNANCE AVAILABLE

PERSON AVAILABLE
≠
AUTHORITY AVAILABLE

BACKUP EXISTS
≠
RECOVERY IS PROVEN

RECOVERY COMPLETED
≠
GOVERNANCE STATE RECONCILED

CONTINUITY ACTIVATED
≠
NORMAL GOVERNANCE RESTORED
```

# 38. Resilience Boundary

Resilience does not mean that every governance function must remain fully operational during every disruption.

Instead:

```text
CRITICAL GOVERNANCE
= PRESERVE

= PRIORITIZE / DEFER
```

according to defined materiality and risk.

# 55. Final STEADY-STATE-015 Statement

> **STEADY-STATE-015 establishes the controlled continuity, resilience and recovery mechanism for steady-state governance. It preserves critical decision authority, accountability, records, evidence, baseline integrity, risk visibility and escalation capability during disruption. It distinguishes system availability from governance availability and recovery completion from governance reconciliation. Temporary and emergency mechanisms remain controlled and do not automatically become permanent governance. N8 and N9 remain CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS, while N10 remains NOT DEFINED and NOT AUTHORIZED.**
