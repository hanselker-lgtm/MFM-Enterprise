# 1. Purpose

This document defines the fifty-ninth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-58 – Risk, Compliance, Internal Control, Audit, Policy & Enterprise Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled Service Management and Operational Assurance capability covering service definition, service requests, incidents, major incidents, problems, changes, releases, deployments, service levels, operational support, knowledge and service continuity.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage operational services through a controlled lifecycle connecting service definition, request fulfillment, incident recovery, problem prevention, change enablement, release control, service levels, operational support, continuity and measurable assurance.**

# 173. Final Request Principle

> **Service requests must be standardized, authorized, fulfilled, measured and closed with evidence of the intended outcome.**

---

# 174. Final Incident Principle

> **Incident Management must restore service quickly while preserving sufficient information to understand impact, recovery and recurrence risk.**

---

# 175. Final Major Incident Principle

> **Major incidents require clear command, coordinated communication, rapid recovery, traceable decisions and proportionate post-incident review.**

---

# 176. Final Problem Principle

> **Problem Management must address underlying causes and recurring operational weaknesses rather than repeatedly treating symptoms.**

---

# 177. Final Change Principle

> **Change must be enabled through proportionate risk assessment, authorization, scheduling, implementation, validation and controlled recovery.**

---

# 178. Final Release Principle

> **Releases must move tested and approved service changes into operation through controlled deployment and post-release validation.**

---

# 179. Final SLA Principle

> **Service levels must be measurable, transparent and linked to customer expectations, operational capabilities and corrective action.**

---

# 180. Final Continuity Principle

> **Critical services must have proportionate recovery requirements, plans, dependencies, testing and improvement.**

---

# 181. Final Operational Assurance Principle

> **Operational assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that services are available, controlled, supported, recoverable and performing according to defined requirements.**

---

# 182. Final Integration Principle

> **Service Management must integrate with business processes, applications, configuration, suppliers, contracts, security, privacy, risk, finance, projects, grants, documents and enterprise assurance.**

---

# 183. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should operate services through a controlled lifecycle connecting service definition, requests, incidents, problems, changes, releases, service levels, support, knowledge, continuity, monitoring and operational assurance.**
