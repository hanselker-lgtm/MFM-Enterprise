# 1. Purpose

This document defines the sixty-second implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-61 – Monitoring, Event Management, Observability, Alerting, Capacity & Operational Telemetry Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled capability for incident response, major incident command, problem investigation, root cause analysis, operational recovery and resilience.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must detect, coordinate, contain, recover from and learn from operational incidents through a controlled response lifecycle that protects services, users, data, continuity and organizational outcomes.**

# 129. Definition of Ready

An incident-response or recovery work item is Ready when:

- Incident or scenario is identified.
- Service and impact are understood sufficiently.
- Owner or command role is assigned.
- Priority and escalation criteria are known.
- Dependencies are identified.
- Required recovery or response resources are known.

# 131. Final Incident Principle

> **Incident response must prioritize protection, containment, restoration and clear accountability while preserving the evidence required for learning and assurance.**

---

# 132. Final Major Incident Principle

> **Major incidents require a single coordinated command structure, clear decision authority, controlled communication and an explicit focus on recovery.**

---

# 133. Final Problem Principle

> **Problem Management must reduce recurrence by addressing underlying causes rather than repeatedly treating incident symptoms.**

---

# 134. Final Root Cause Principle

> **Root cause conclusions must be evidence-based and must clearly distinguish observed facts, hypotheses, assumptions and confirmed causes.**

---

# 135. Final Recovery Principle

> **Recovery is complete only when the restored service or capability has been validated against defined operational, security, data and business requirements.**

---

# 136. Final Resilience Principle

> **Resilience must be designed around critical services, critical dependencies, realistic disruptive scenarios and tested recovery capabilities.**

---

# 137. Final Learning Principle

> **Material incidents must generate actionable learning that is converted into owned and verified improvement actions.**

---

# 138. Final Assurance Principle

> **Incident and recovery assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that response, recovery and learning mechanisms work as intended.**

---

# 139. Final Integration Principle

> **Incident Response and Operational Recovery must integrate with Monitoring, Service Management, Problem, Change, Configuration, Security, Privacy, Continuity, Suppliers, Risk and Enterprise Assurance.**

---

# 140. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage operational incidents through a controlled lifecycle connecting detection, triage, command, containment, recovery, validation, problem investigation, root cause analysis, resilience improvement and post-incident learning.**
