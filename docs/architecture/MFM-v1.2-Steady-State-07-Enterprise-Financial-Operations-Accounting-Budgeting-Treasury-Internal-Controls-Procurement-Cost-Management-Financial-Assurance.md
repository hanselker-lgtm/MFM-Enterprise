# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the seventh document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-06 – Enterprise Data Governance, Data Quality, Master Data, Metadata, Lineage, Data Architecture, Analytics & Information Lifecycle Management.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent financial-management operating model for MFM, covering accounting, financial transactions, budgeting, forecasting, treasury, cash management, procurement, purchasing, cost management, financial controls, reconciliation, reporting, financial close, audit evidence and financial assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain financially controlled, transparent, traceable and auditable operations in which transactions, budgets, commitments, cash, costs, approvals, reconciliations and financial reporting remain governed throughout steady-state operation.**

# 131. Definition of Ready

A financial-management work item is Ready when:

- The transaction, budget, forecast, procurement, cost, reconciliation, control, financial risk or reporting requirement is clearly identified.
- Ownership and financial impact are known.
- Approval, accounting, evidence and acceptance criteria are defined.
- Required controls and dependencies are understood.
