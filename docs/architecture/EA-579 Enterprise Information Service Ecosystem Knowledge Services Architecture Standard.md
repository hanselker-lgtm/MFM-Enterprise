# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Knowledge Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Knowledge Services shall follow these principles.

## Knowledge by Design

Enterprise knowledge shall be deliberately created, captured, governed and maintained.

---

## Knowledge Ownership

Every critical knowledge domain shall have defined ownership and stewardship.

---

## Knowledge Accessibility

Enterprise knowledge shall be accessible to authorized users and processes.

---

## Knowledge Protection

Enterprise knowledge shall be protected according to classification, security and governance requirements.

---

## Knowledge Reusability

Enterprise knowledge shall be structured for reuse across business and technology domains.

---

## Knowledge Evolution

Enterprise knowledge shall continuously evolve through learning, experience and improvement.

---

## Knowledge Measurement

Knowledge capabilities shall be evaluated through measurable indicators and evidence.

---

## Governed Knowledge Management

Knowledge activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.
