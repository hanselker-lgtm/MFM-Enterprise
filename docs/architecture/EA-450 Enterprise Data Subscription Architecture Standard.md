# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Subscription Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Publication principles are inherited from EA-449.

All Enterprise Data Subscription implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Subscription Architecture supporting secure, scalable and governed subscription to authoritative Enterprise information through APIs, events, streams, messaging platforms and other subscription-based mechanisms.

Enterprise Data Subscription shall

- enable controlled information subscriptions
- improve information availability
- support real-time information delivery
- strengthen interoperability
- improve operational efficiency
- optimize information consumption
- strengthen governance
- improve scalability
- support regulatory compliance
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Subscription shall provide standardized mechanisms enabling trusted consumers to receive Enterprise information automatically.

This standard applies to

- regulatory authorities

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or implementation technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Subscription Principles

Enterprise Data Subscription shall follow these principles.

## Subscription by Design

Enterprise information shall support secure and reusable subscription capabilities wherever business value exists.

---

## Authoritative Information Sources

Subscribers shall receive information only from authoritative Enterprise information sources.

---

## Secure Subscription

All subscriptions shall implement authentication, authorization, encryption and continuous monitoring.

---

## Metadata Driven Subscription

Subscription services shall use Enterprise metadata to manage routing, policies, discovery and lifecycle.

---

## Standardized Subscription Services

Enterprise subscriptions shall utilize approved Enterprise subscription services and standardized interfaces.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Subscription Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, middleware platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.
