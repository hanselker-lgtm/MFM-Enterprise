# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Collaboration Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Communication Services principles are inherited from EA-459.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Collaboration Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Collaboration Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and scalable collaboration capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Collaboration Services shall

- provide standardized collaboration services
- support enterprise teamwork
- enable secure communication and cooperation
- improve organizational productivity
- strengthen interoperability
- support internal and external collaboration
- support reusable collaboration capabilities
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- enable continuous optimization

Enterprise Collaboration Services shall provide trusted and reusable collaboration capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- collaboration services
- enterprise collaboration platforms
- team workspaces
- chat platforms
- meeting platforms
- document collaboration services
- cloud services
- hybrid environments
- external collaboration providers
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, collaboration technology or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Collaboration Services Principles

Enterprise Collaboration Services shall follow these principles.

## Collaboration First

Enterprise Collaboration Services shall support efficient, secure and standardized collaboration across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Secure by Default

Every collaboration activity shall be authenticated, authorized, protected and continuously monitored.

---

## Standardized Collaboration Services

Enterprise Collaboration Services shall expose standardized collaboration capabilities supporting interoperability throughout the Enterprise.

---

## Metadata Driven Collaboration

Collaboration services shall leverage Enterprise metadata for participant management, workspace governance, content organization, lifecycle management and automation.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Collaboration Services shall remain independent of vendors, collaboration platforms, cloud providers and infrastructure technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Collaboration Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed collaboration capabilities across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating team collaboration, chat, meetings, document collaboration, workspaces, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled collaboration while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Collaboration Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise collaboration capabilities across the Enterprise.
