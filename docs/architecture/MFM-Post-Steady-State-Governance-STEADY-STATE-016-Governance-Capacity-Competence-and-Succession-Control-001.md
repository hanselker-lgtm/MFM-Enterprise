# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-016 establishes the controlled mechanism for ensuring that governance has sufficient capacity, competence, role coverage, knowledge continuity and succession capability to operate sustainably over time.

The purpose is to prevent governance dependence on unavailable individuals, insufficient capability, undocumented knowledge or unfilled critical roles.

The operating sequence is:

```text
GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENT
↓
CAPACITY / COMPETENCE ASSESSMENT
↓
GAP IDENTIFICATION
↓
RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
RESPONSE
↓
DEVELOP / DELEGATE / RECRUIT / CROSS-TRAIN
↓
VALIDATE
↓
MONITOR
↓
SUCCESSION / CONTINUITY
```

---

# 2. Core Principle

Governance capability must be sufficient for the authority and complexity it carries.

The following distinctions are mandatory:

```text
ROLE EXISTS
≠
ROLE IS CAPABLE

PERSON IS AVAILABLE
≠
PERSON IS COMPETENT FOR THE ROLE

COMPETENCE EXISTS
≠
CAPACITY IS SUFFICIENT

DOCUMENTED KNOWLEDGE
≠
TRANSFERRED KNOWLEDGE

SUCCESSOR IDENTIFIED
≠
SUCCESSOR READY

TRAINING COMPLETED
≠
COMPETENCE VALIDATED
```

# 59. Final STEADY-STATE-016 Statement

> **STEADY-STATE-016 establishes the controlled capacity, competence and succession mechanism for steady-state governance. It ensures that critical governance responsibilities are supported by sufficient capacity, demonstrated competence, appropriate authority, knowledge continuity and credible succession. It distinguishes role existence from role capability, training from validated competence and successor identification from successor readiness. Material capability gaps become visible governance risks rather than hidden dependencies. N8 and N9 remain CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS, while N10 remains NOT DEFINED and NOT AUTHORIZED.**
