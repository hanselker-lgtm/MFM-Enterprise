# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Privacy Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Data Protection Services principles are inherited from EA-480.

Enterprise Encryption Services principles are inherited from EA-479.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Data Privacy Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Privacy Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of privacy requirements across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Privacy Services shall

- provide centralized privacy management
- standardize privacy controls
- protect personal and sensitive data
- support regulatory compliance
- strengthen privacy governance
- reduce privacy-related risks
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure privacy traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Privacy Services shall provide trusted and reusable privacy management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise data privacy services
- privacy governance services

- personal data management
- data subject management
- consent management
- privacy impact assessment integration
- privacy risk management
- data minimization services
- purpose limitation services

- data pseudonymization services

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Data Privacy Services Principles

Enterprise Data Privacy Services shall follow these principles.

## Privacy by Design

Privacy capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Privacy by Default

Personal and sensitive data shall be protected according to approved privacy requirements, business policies and regulatory obligations.

---

## Data Minimization

Enterprise data collection, storage and processing shall be limited to information required for approved business purposes.

---

## Purpose Limitation

Personal data shall only be processed for approved, documented and governed purposes.

---

## Privacy Governance

Privacy requirements shall be governed through approved Enterprise policies, controls, accountability structures and review processes.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Privacy Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed privacy management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating privacy governance, privacy policy management, personal data management, data subject management, consent management, privacy impact assessment integration, privacy risk management, data minimization, purpose limitation, data anonymization, data pseudonymization, privacy protection controls, data protection integration, encryption integration, identity integration, authentication integration, authorization integration, risk, audit, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables secure, transparent and policy-driven privacy management while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Privacy Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise privacy capabilities across the Enterprise.
