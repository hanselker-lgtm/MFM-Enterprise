# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Consumption Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Access principles are inherited from EA-445.

Enterprise Data Federation principles are inherited from EA-444.

All Enterprise Data Consumption implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Consumption Architecture enabling secure, governed and standardized utilization of Enterprise information by business users, applications, analytics platforms, artificial intelligence solutions, automation services and external consumers.

Enterprise Data Consumption shall

- provide standardized information consumption
- improve business decision support
- enable self-service analytics
- strengthen governance
- improve interoperability
- support regulatory compliance
- optimize information utilization
- improve operational efficiency
- support business agility
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Consumption shall establish a unified architectural framework for Enterprise information utilization throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- business applications
- reporting platforms
- business intelligence platforms
- dashboards
- analytical services
- artificial intelligence platforms
- machine learning platforms
- automation platforms
- mobile applications
- external information consumers

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform, cloud provider or implementation technology.

# 3. Enterprise Data Consumption Principles

Enterprise Data Consumption shall be governed by the following principles.

## Consumption by Design

Enterprise information shall be consumed through standardized and reusable Enterprise consumption services.

---

## Business Value First

Enterprise information consumption shall prioritize measurable business value and operational outcomes.

---

## Standardized Consumption Interfaces

Consumers shall utilize standardized Enterprise interfaces for information consumption.

---

## Secure Consumption

Enterprise information consumption shall be authenticated, authorized, monitored and protected throughout the complete consumption lifecycle.

---

## Metadata Driven Consumption

Enterprise Data Consumption shall utilize Enterprise metadata, semantic definitions and governance policies to simplify and standardize information utilization.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Consumption Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, cloud providers, analytics platforms and implementation technologies.
