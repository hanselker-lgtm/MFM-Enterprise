# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Authentication Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Identity Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-471.

Enterprise Authorization Services principles are inherited from EA-472.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Authentication Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Authentication Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed authentication capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Authentication Services shall

- provide centralized authentication management
- standardize identity verification
- strengthen security controls
- reduce authentication-related risks
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure authentication traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Authentication Services shall provide trusted and reusable authentication capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise authentication services
- identity verification services
- multi-factor authentication
- passwordless authentication
- certificate-based authentication
- federated authentication
- single sign-on services
- session management
- credential management
- user authentication
- application authentication
- service and workload authentication

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Authentication Services Principles

Enterprise Authentication Services shall follow these principles.

## Authentication by Design

Authentication capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every authentication request shall be validated, protected, monitored and audited.

---

## Strong Identity Verification

Authentication mechanisms shall provide appropriate assurance levels based on identity risk, business requirements and security classification.

---

## Continuous Authentication

Authentication trust shall be continuously evaluated throughout user, application and workload interactions.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Authentication Services shall remain independent of vendors, authentication platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Authentication Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed authentication across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating identity verification, multi-factor authentication, passwordless authentication, certificate-based authentication, federated authentication, single sign-on, session management, credential management, identity governance, authorization integration, governance, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, scalable and policy-driven authentication while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Authentication Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise authentication capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 34. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Authentication Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted identity verification, autonomous authentication optimization, predictive authentication risk analytics, semantic-aware identity intelligence, adaptive trust evaluation, continuous authentication assurance, decentralized identity models and quantum-safe Enterprise authentication infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Authentication Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven authentication capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
