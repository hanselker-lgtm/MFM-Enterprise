# 1. PURPOSE

EA-IMETA-PRODUCTION-SERVICE-SECURITY-RESILIENCE-COMPLIANCE-REMEDIATION-01 establishes the controlled capability for correcting compliance deficiencies and restoring an acceptable, evidenced and governed state after findings, monitoring events, response actions, audit observations, control failures, regulatory changes or other compliance-impacting conditions.

The remediation chain is:

```text
DEFICIENCY
 ↓
CLASSIFY
 ↓
ROOT CAUSE
 ↓

 ↓
REMEDIATION PLAN
 ↓
CORRECTIVE ACTION
 ↓
IMPLEMENT
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
RETEST
 ↓
VALIDATE
 ↓
CLOSE
 ↓
LEARN
 ↓
IMPROVE
```

# 2. REMEDIATION PRINCIPLE

> EA-IMETA SHALL REMEDIATE MATERIAL COMPLIANCE DEFICIENCIES THROUGH TRACEABLE, RISK-BASED, TIME-BOUND AND VALIDATED ACTIONS THAT ADDRESS ROOT CAUSE, RESTORE CONTROL EFFECTIVENESS AND PREVENT UNCONTROLLED RECURRENCE.
