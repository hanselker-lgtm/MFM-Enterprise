# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Incident Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Incident Management ensures that operational, security and business incidents are consistently identified, classified, prioritized, managed, resolved and reviewed while preserving operational resilience, traceability, governance and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Incident Classification
- Incident Prioritization
- Incident Lifecycle
- Incident Response
- Root Cause Analysis
- Post-Incident Review
- Knowledge Capture
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Incident Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Incident Management Principles

Enterprise Incident Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Rapid Detection
- Risk-Based Prioritization
- Standardized Response
- Complete Traceability
- Continuous Learning
- Operational Resilience
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Incident Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Incident Management implementations shall handle incident management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify incident registration failures
- classify incident response failures
- classify recovery failures
- classify communication failures
- classify reporting failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Incident Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational resilience, traceability, governance, compliance or service recovery.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Incident Management implementations may depend upon

- approved monitoring platforms
- approved alert management services
- approved logging platforms
- approved incident repositories
- approved notification services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Incident Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external incident management providers

Incident Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Incident Management implementation is compliant when

- Incident classification is implemented.
- Incident prioritization follows enterprise policy.
- Incident lifecycle is fully documented.
- Incident response procedures are operational.
- Root cause analysis is performed where required.
- Post-incident reviews are completed.
- Operational recovery is verified.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Unclassified Incidents

Incidents shall never remain unclassified after registration.

---

## Delayed Response

Critical incidents shall never wait for manual prioritization when approved automated prioritization is available.

---

## Missing Root Cause Analysis

Major incidents shall never be closed without documented root cause analysis where required.

---

## Undocumented Recovery

Operational recovery shall never be considered complete without documented verification.

---

## Missing Lessons Learned

Major incidents shall never be closed without capturing lessons learned and improvement actions.

---

## Incident Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent incident management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Incident Management services.

---

- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Incident Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Incident Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that operational, security and business incidents are consistently identified, classified, prioritized, managed, resolved, reviewed and continuously improved while preserving operational resilience, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Incident Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
