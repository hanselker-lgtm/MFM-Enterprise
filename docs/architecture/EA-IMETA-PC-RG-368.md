## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory substantive validation layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-368, determining whether the procedural verification of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-367 is substantively true, effective, complete and supportable in the actual current operating state.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-367 verifies that RG-366 validation was correctly performed and implemented. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-368 validates whether the EA-IMETA-PC-RG-367 verification remains substantively effective and provides reliable assurance.

```text
RG-366 → VALIDATE
RG-367 → VERIFY
RG-368 → VALIDATE
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-367 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-368 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
IS THE EA-IMETA-PC-RG-367 VERIFICATION ACTUALLY EFFECTIVE AND TRUE?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-367 verification SHALL NOT automatically establish substantive current effectiveness.
