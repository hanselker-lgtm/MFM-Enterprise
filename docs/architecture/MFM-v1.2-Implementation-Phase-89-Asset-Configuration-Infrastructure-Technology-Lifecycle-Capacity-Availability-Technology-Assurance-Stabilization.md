# 1. Purpose

This document defines the eighty-ninth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-88 – Procurement, Supplier, Vendor, Contract Lifecycle, Third-Party Risk, Sourcing & Supply-Chain Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled technology and asset management capability covering asset management, configuration management, CMDB governance, configuration items, infrastructure inventory, technology lifecycle, hardware lifecycle, software lifecycle, capacity management, availability management, technology dependencies, infrastructure resilience, technology standards, technical debt, technology obsolescence, end-of-life management, technology refresh, configuration assurance, asset assurance and technology lifecycle assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must maintain accurate visibility and governance over technology assets, configuration items, infrastructure, dependencies, lifecycle status, capacity and availability so that technology remains supportable, secure, resilient, cost-effective and aligned with organizational needs.**

A technology-management work item is Ready when:

# 143. Final Lifecycle Principle

> **Technology must be actively managed from introduction through operation, refresh and retirement rather than allowed to become unmanaged legacy.**

---

# 144. Final Capacity Principle

> **Capacity management must anticipate future demand rather than respond only after capacity constraints occur.**

---

# 145. Final Availability Principle

> **Availability objectives must reflect business criticality, service requirements, risk and the cost of achieving the required level.**

---

# 146. Final Dependency Principle

> **Material technology dependencies must be visible enough to support impact analysis, incident response, change, resilience and lifecycle decisions.**

---

# 147. Final Resilience Principle

> **Critical technology must have appropriate redundancy, recovery capability and evidence of recovery readiness.**

---

# 148. Final Technical Debt Principle

> **Technical debt must be visible, risk-assessed and actively managed rather than hidden within normal operational complexity.**

---

# 149. Final Assurance Principle

> **Technology assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that assets, configurations, capacity, availability and lifecycle controls operate as intended.**

---

# 150. Final Integration Principle
