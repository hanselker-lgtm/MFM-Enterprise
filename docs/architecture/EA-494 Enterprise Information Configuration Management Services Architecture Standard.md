# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Configuration Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed identification, registration, control and monitoring of configuration items related to Enterprise information, data, metadata, systems, applications, integrations and technology components across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Configuration Management Services shall

- provide centralized configuration management capabilities
- identify enterprise configuration items
- maintain configuration information
- establish configuration relationships
- support change management
- support dependency management
- support impact analysis
- improve operational visibility
- strengthen governance
- support compliance requirements
- support audit requirements
- protect information quality
- support lifecycle governance
- support master information governance
- support reference information governance
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure configuration traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Configuration Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable configuration management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Configuration Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Configuration Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Configuration Control by Design

Enterprise information configurations shall be identified, documented and governed as an integrated part of Enterprise Architecture.

---

## Trusted Configuration Information

Configuration records shall be accurate, validated and maintained through controlled governance processes.

---

## Complete Configuration Visibility

Critical configuration items and relationships shall remain visible across information, technology and business domains.

---

## Controlled Configuration Changes

Configuration changes shall be managed through approved change management processes.

---

## Lifecycle-Based Configuration Management

Configuration items shall be governed from creation through modification, retirement and preservation.
