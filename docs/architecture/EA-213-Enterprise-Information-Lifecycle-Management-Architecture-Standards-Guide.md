# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management ensures that enterprise information is created, classified, stored, used, shared, archived and disposed of according to business requirements, legal obligations and governance policies while preserving integrity, availability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Information Creation
- Information Classification
- Information Storage
- Information Usage
- Information Sharing
- Information Archiving
- Information Disposal
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## ILM-001

Provide standardized enterprise information lifecycle management.

---

## ILM-002

Protect enterprise information throughout its lifecycle.

---

## ILM-003

Ensure information integrity, availability and traceability.

---

## ILM-004

Support regulatory and legal compliance.

---

## ILM-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Information Lifecycle Principles

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Lifecycle by Design
- Information Integrity
- Controlled Accessibility
- Information Availability
- Complete Traceability
- Regulatory Compliance
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Information Lifecycle Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management implementations shall handle information lifecycle-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify information classification failures
- classify information storage failures
- classify information usage failures
- classify information sharing failures
- classify information archiving failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Information Lifecycle Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, information integrity, availability, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management implementations may depend upon

- approved records management services
- approved data retention services
- approved data governance services
- approved storage infrastructure
- approved security services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external information lifecycle providers

Information Lifecycle Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management implementation is compliant when

- Information classification is documented.
- Information storage procedures are implemented.
- Information usage policies are maintained.
- Information sharing is controlled.
- Information archiving procedures are documented.
- Information verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Information Classification

Enterprise information shall never exist without an approved information classification.

---

## Undefined Storage Management

Enterprise information shall never be stored without documented storage requirements.

---

## Uncontrolled Information Sharing

Enterprise information shall never be shared outside approved governance procedures.

---

## Unauthorized Information Disposal

Enterprise information shall never be destroyed without documented authorization and verification.

---

## Inadequate Information Protection

Enterprise information shall never be stored or shared without approved security controls.

---

## Information Lifecycle Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent information lifecycle mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management services.

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise information is created, classified, stored, used, shared, archived and securely disposed of according to business requirements, legal obligations and governance policies while preserving integrity, availability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
