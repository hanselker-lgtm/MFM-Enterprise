# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Compliance Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Policy Services principles are inherited from EA-465.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Compliance Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Compliance Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, automated and governed compliance capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Compliance Services shall

- provide centralized compliance management
- standardize compliance controls
- support regulatory compliance
- improve governance
- strengthen operational transparency
- enable enterprise automation
- support interoperability
- improve audit readiness
- reduce compliance risk
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Compliance Services shall provide trusted and reusable compliance capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise compliance services
- compliance management
- compliance monitoring
- compliance validation
- compliance assessment
- compliance reporting
- compliance evidence management
- compliance lifecycle management
- cloud services
- hybrid environments
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, regulatory framework or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Compliance Services Principles

Enterprise Compliance Services shall follow these principles.

## Compliance by Design

Compliance requirements shall be incorporated into Enterprise Architecture, business processes, applications and technology solutions from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every compliance activity shall be authenticated, authorized, audited and continuously monitored.

---

## Continuous Compliance

Enterprise Compliance Services shall continuously evaluate compliance status across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Metadata Driven Compliance

Compliance requirements, controls and evidence shall be described, classified and governed using Enterprise metadata.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Compliance Services shall remain independent of vendors, compliance platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Compliance Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed compliance management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating compliance management, continuous monitoring, validation, assessment, evidence management, policy integration, rules integration, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables transparent, auditable and continuously verifiable compliance while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Compliance Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise compliance capabilities across the Enterprise.

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Compliance Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted compliance assessments, autonomous regulatory monitoring, semantic-aware compliance mapping, adaptive control optimization, self-validating compliance evidence repositories, decentralized compliance ecosystems, intelligent regulatory impact analysis and quantum-safe Enterprise compliance infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Compliance Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven compliance capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
