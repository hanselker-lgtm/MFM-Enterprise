# 1. Purpose

This document defines the sixty-first implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-60 – Configuration Management, Asset Management, CMDB, Dependency Mapping & Technology Lifecycle Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled monitoring, event management, observability, alerting, capacity and operational telemetry capability.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must obtain timely, reliable and actionable visibility into service health, system behavior, performance, capacity, dependencies and operational events so that issues can be detected, understood, prioritized and resolved before they create uncontrolled business impact.**

# 142. Final Monitoring Principle

> **Monitoring must exist to support a defined operational, business, security, continuity or compliance objective.**

---

# 143. Final Observability Principle

> **Observability must provide sufficient metrics, logs, traces and contextual events to understand system behavior and diagnose material conditions.**

---

# 144. Final Alert Principle

> **Alerts must be actionable, appropriately prioritized, context-rich and routed to accountable responders.**

---

# 145. Final Noise Principle

> **Monitoring quality is measured not by the number of alerts generated but by the quality of meaningful signals delivered to the people or systems that must act.**

---

# 146. Final Capacity Principle

> **Capacity must be managed proactively through utilization, headroom, demand trends, forecasting and defined action thresholds.**

---

# 147. Final Performance Principle

> **Performance monitoring must identify meaningful deviation from defined expectations and connect observed degradation to changes, dependencies, incidents and capacity.**

---

# 148. Final Monitoring Health Principle

> **The monitoring capability itself must be monitored so that absence of telemetry is not incorrectly interpreted as absence of operational problems.**

---

# 149. Final Security Principle

> **Monitoring infrastructure and telemetry must be protected against unauthorized access, tampering, disclosure and disruption.**

---

# 150. Final Privacy Principle

> **Telemetry collection must minimize unnecessary personal information and protect information that is legitimately collected.**

---

# 151. Final Integration Principle

> **Monitoring and observability must integrate with Service Management, Incident, Problem, Change, Release, Configuration, Security, Capacity, Continuity and Enterprise Assurance.**

---

# 152. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should operate monitoring and observability through a controlled lifecycle connecting telemetry collection, event evaluation, alerting, operational response, capacity planning, performance analysis and assurance.**
