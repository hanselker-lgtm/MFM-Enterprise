# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-018 establishes the controlled mechanism for ensuring that governance remains aligned with strategic direction and can adapt to material internal and external changes without uncontrolled drift.

The purpose is to ensure that changes in strategy, environment, regulation, technology, organization, risk or operating conditions are translated into controlled governance assessment and, where required, authorized change.

The operating sequence is:

```text
STRATEGIC / EXTERNAL CHANGE
↓
DETECT
↓
ASSESS
↓
MATERIALITY
↓
IMPACT ANALYSIS
↓
ALIGNMENT DECISION
↓
AUTHORIZED CHANGE WHERE REQUIRED
↓
IMPLEMENT
↓
VALIDATE
↓
REASSESS
```

---

# 2. Core Principle

Adaptability must not become uncontrolled governance drift.

The following distinctions are mandatory:

```text
CHANGE DETECTED
≠
CHANGE MATERIAL

CHANGE MATERIAL
≠
CHANGE AUTHORIZED

CHANGE AUTHORIZED
≠
CHANGE IMPLEMENTED

CHANGE IMPLEMENTED
≠
CHANGE VALIDATED

ADAPTATION
≠
BASELINE DRIFT

STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT
≠
AUTOMATIC AUTHORIZATION
```

# 58. Final STEADY-STATE-018 Statement

> **STEADY-STATE-018 establishes the controlled mechanism for strategic alignment, adaptability and external change. It ensures that material strategic and environmental changes are detected, assessed for materiality and impact, routed through appropriate authority, implemented under control and validated without allowing adaptation to become uncontrolled governance drift. It preserves baseline integrity, accountability, evidence, authority and traceability while enabling governance to evolve with its environment. N8 and N9 remain CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS, while N10 remains NOT DEFINED and NOT AUTHORIZED.**
