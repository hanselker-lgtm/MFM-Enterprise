# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Key Management Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Secrets Management Services principles are inherited from EA-476.

Enterprise Credential Management Services principles are inherited from EA-475.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Key Management Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Key Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of cryptographic keys across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Key Management Services shall

- provide centralized key management
- standardize cryptographic key lifecycle management
- strengthen encryption security
- reduce cryptographic risks
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure key traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Key Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable cryptographic key management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Key Management Services Principles

Enterprise Key Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Key Security by Design

Key management capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every cryptographic key shall be securely generated, stored, protected, distributed, monitored and retired according to approved Enterprise security requirements.

---

## Least Key Privilege

Cryptographic keys shall only be accessible by approved identities, applications, services and workloads requiring authorized usage.

---

## Cryptographic Lifecycle Governance

Keys shall be continuously managed throughout generation, activation, usage, rotation, suspension and destruction.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Key Management Services shall remain independent of vendors, key management platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Key Management Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed cryptographic key management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating cryptographic key lifecycle management, key generation, key storage, key distribution, key rotation, key revocation, Hardware Security Module services, encryption services integration, certificate services integration, secrets management integration, credential management integration, identity integration, authentication integration, authorization integration, risk, audit, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables secure, scalable and policy-driven cryptographic protection while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Key Management Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise key management capabilities across the Enterprise.
