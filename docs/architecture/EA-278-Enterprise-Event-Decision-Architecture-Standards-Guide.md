# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Event Decision Architecture throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Event Decision Architecture provides standardized mechanisms for defining, executing, governing and tracing enterprise event-driven decisions while preserving consistency, accountability, transparency and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Event Decisions
- Decision Models
- Decision Lifecycle
- Decision Execution
- Decision Governance
- Decision Traceability
- Compliance

All Enterprise Event Decision implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## EDEC-001

Provide standardized Enterprise Event Decision Architecture.

## EDEC-003

---

## EDEC-004

Support governance and regulatory compliance.

# 4. Enterprise Event Decision Principles

Enterprise Event Decision implementations shall follow these principles.

- Standardized Decision Models
- Controlled Decision Lifecycle
- Traceable Decision Execution
- Consistent Decision Governance
- Technology Independence
- Explicit Decision Ownership
- Centralized Governance
- Complete Decision Traceability

Enterprise Event Decision implementations shall remain independent of presentation, persistence and unrelated business workflow concerns.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Event Decision implementations shall handle Event Decision-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify decision model failures
- classify decision lifecycle failures
- classify decision execution failures
- classify decision traceability failures
- classify infrastructure failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Enterprise Event Decision exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, business integrity, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Event Decision implementations may depend upon

- approved governance services
- approved compliance services
- approved audit services
- approved reporting services
- approved monitoring services
- approved configuration services
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Enterprise Event Decision implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Reporting implementations
- Query implementations
- Command implementations outside approved interfaces
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Unapproved external decision frameworks

Enterprise Event Decision capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Event Decision implementation is compliant when

- Decision models are implemented.
- Decision lifecycle management is implemented.
- Decision execution is implemented.
- Decision traceability is implemented.
- Decision management is implemented.
- Decision verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Compliance coordination is operational.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Undefined Decision Models

Enterprise event capabilities shall never execute business decisions without approved decision models.

---

## Uncontrolled Decision Changes

Decision models shall never be modified outside the approved governance and lifecycle management process.

---

## Missing Decision Traceability

Enterprise decisions shall never be executed without preserving complete traceability from inputs to outcomes.

---

## Hidden Decision Dependencies

Enterprise implementations shall never introduce undocumented runtime or infrastructure dependencies.

---

## Cross-Capability Decision Access

Decision implementations shall never access internal capability components except through approved enterprise interfaces.

---

## Unapproved Decision Overrides

Decision execution shall never be bypassed or overridden without explicit governance approval.

---

- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Event Decision Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Event Decision Architecture throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that decision modeling, decision lifecycle management, decision execution, decision traceability and governance are implemented consistently while preserving maintainability, scalability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Event Decision implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
