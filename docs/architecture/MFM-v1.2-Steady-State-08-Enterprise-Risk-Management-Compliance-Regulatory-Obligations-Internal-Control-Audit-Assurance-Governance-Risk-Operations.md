# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eighth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-07 – Enterprise Financial Operations, Accounting, Budgeting, Treasury, Internal Controls, Procurement, Cost Management & Financial Assurance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise risk, compliance, regulatory, internal-control, audit and assurance operating model for MFM.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must continuously identify, assess, treat, monitor and assure enterprise risks and obligations so that governance decisions remain informed, accountable, compliant, evidence-based and aligned with approved risk appetite.**

# 125. Definition of Ready

A risk, compliance, control, audit or assurance work item is Ready when:

- The requirement, risk, control, obligation, finding or assurance need is clearly identified.
- Ownership and authority are known.
- Risk, scope, evidence and acceptance criteria are defined.
- Dependencies and escalation requirements are understood.

---

```text

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
```

# 127. Final Risk Principle

> **MFM must actively manage uncertainty that may affect its objectives, people, services, finances, information, compliance or resilience.**

---

# 128. Final Appetite Principle

> **Risk acceptance must remain within approved appetite, tolerance and delegated authority.**

---

# 129. Final Control Principle

> **Material risks must be supported by controls that are appropriately designed, owned, executed, evidenced and tested.**

---

# 130. Final Compliance Principle

> **Applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, policy and standard-based obligations must be identified, owned, assessed and evidenced.**

---

# 131. Final Audit Principle

> **Audit activities must provide credible evidence regarding governance, risk and control effectiveness within defined scope and criteria.**

---

# 132. Final Assurance Principle

> **Assurance must provide sufficient confidence that important risks and controls are being managed effectively.**

---

# 133. Final Finding Principle

> **Material findings must remain owned, actioned, evidenced, validated and closed only when their underlying risk has been appropriately addressed.**

---

# 134. Final Governance Principle

> **Material governance decisions must be based on relevant evidence, understood risk, defined authority and traceable follow-up.**

---

# 135. Final Integration Principle

> **Risk, Compliance, Controls, Audit and Assurance must integrate with Security, Privacy, Data, Financial Operations, Service Management, Technical Operations, Architecture, Supplier Management and Resilience.**

---

# 136. Final Steady-State Governance Principle

> **MFM governance must continuously provide visibility, accountability and assurance over the risks, obligations and controls that affect the enterprise.**
