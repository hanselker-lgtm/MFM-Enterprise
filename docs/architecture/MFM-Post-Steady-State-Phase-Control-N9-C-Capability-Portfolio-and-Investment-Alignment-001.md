# 1. Purpose

N9-C establishes the controlled alignment layer connecting:

```text
Strategy
   ↓
Outcome
   ↓
Capability
   ↓
Architecture
   ↓
Roadmap
   ↓
Portfolio
   ↓
Investment
   ↓
Execution
   ↓
Outcome / Value
```

The purpose is to identify and govern misalignment between enterprise direction, required capabilities, architecture evolution, portfolio priorities and investment decisions.

N9-C does not itself approve budgets, funding or investments unless the applicable authority explicitly grants such authority.
