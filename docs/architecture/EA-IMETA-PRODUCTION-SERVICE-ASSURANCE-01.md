# 1. PURPOSE

EA-IMETA-PRODUCTION-SERVICE-ASSURANCE-01 establishes the assurance layer above the production control framework.

It provides a structured way to determine whether:

```text
GOVERNANCE
CONTROLS
OPERATIONS
SERVICE MANAGEMENT
SECURITY
DATA
ARCHITECTURE
```

are operating effectively and remain fit for purpose.

---

# 2. ASSURANCE PRINCIPLE

> ASSURANCE SHALL PROVIDE OBJECTIVE, EVIDENCED AND TRACEABLE CONFIDENCE THAT EA-IMETA IS OPERATING WITHIN ITS APPROVED GOVERNANCE, CONTROL, SECURITY, DATA AND SERVICE BOUNDARIES.

---

# 3. ASSURANCE OBJECTIVES

Assurance shall determine:

```text
ARE CONTROLS DESIGNED CORRECTLY?
ARE CONTROLS OPERATING?
IS EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT?
ARE RISKS CONTROLLED?
ARE EXCEPTIONS MANAGED?
IS SERVICE PERFORMANCE ACCEPTABLE?
IS ARCHITECTURE INTEGRITY PRESERVED?
IS DATA TRUSTWORTHY?
IS SECURITY EFFECTIVE?
```

# 5. ASSURANCE LAYERS

```text
LEVEL 1 — MANAGEMENT CONTROL
LEVEL 2 — MANAGEMENT / GOVERNANCE REVIEW
LEVEL 3 — INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE
LEVEL 4 — FORMAL AUDIT
```

---

# 6. LEVEL 1 — MANAGEMENT CONTROL

Control owners execute and monitor controls.

---

# 7. LEVEL 2 — MANAGEMENT / GOVERNANCE REVIEW

Service, security, data, architecture and governance authorities review control performance and risk.

---

# 8. LEVEL 3 — INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE

An independent reviewer assesses selected controls and service domains without owning the controls being reviewed.

---

# 9. LEVEL 4 — FORMAL AUDIT

Formal audit provides the highest independent assurance level where required.

---

# 10. INDEPENDENCE PRINCIPLE

> THE PERSON PROVIDING ASSURANCE SHOULD NOT BE THE SOLE OWNER OF THE CONTROL BEING ASSURED.
