# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture DevOps Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise DevOps capabilities.

The DevOps Architecture Framework shall provide:

- DevOps principles
- DevOps governance
- DevOps strategy
- DevOps operating model
- CI/CD architecture
- pipeline architecture
- infrastructure as code
- automation architecture
- release architecture
- quality engineering
- DevSecOps architecture
- monitoring and observability
- performance management
- DevOps evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise software delivery, operations and continuous improvement are integrated through controlled, automated and measurable practices.

---

# 2. Scope

The DevOps Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture DevOps domains:

- Enterprise DevOps Model
- DevOps Governance
- DevOps Strategy
- DevOps Operating Model
- CI/CD Architecture
- Pipeline Architecture
- Infrastructure as Code
- Automation Architecture
- Release Architecture
- Quality Engineering
- DevSecOps Architecture
- Monitoring and Observability
- DevOps Performance
- DevOps Maturity
- DevOps Roadmaps
- DevOps Evolution

The framework applies to:

- software development teams
- operations teams
- platform teams
- cloud-native environments
- application delivery
- infrastructure delivery
- automated deployment processes
- continuous improvement practices

---

# 3. Enterprise DevOps Architecture Principles

The DevOps Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Collaboration

Development and operations shall work together as integrated teams.

---

## Principle 2 – Automation First

Manual activities shall be reduced through automation where appropriate.

---

## Principle 3 – Continuous Delivery

Software and infrastructure changes shall be delivered through controlled continuous processes.

---

## Principle 4 – Quality by Design

Quality shall be integrated throughout the development lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 – Security by Design

Security controls shall be embedded throughout delivery processes.

---

## Principle 6 – Measurement and Feedback

DevOps activities shall be measured and continuously improved.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

DevOps capabilities shall evolve according to Enterprise needs.

---

# 9. DevOps Operating Model Architecture

The DevOps Operating Model Architecture defines how Enterprise development, operations, platform and support capabilities are organized to enable continuous delivery and reliable service operation.

The operating model establishes responsibilities, collaboration structures and operational practices required for successful DevOps adoption.

The DevOps operating model shall ensure:

- shared responsibility
- cross-functional collaboration
- clear ownership
- operational transparency
- continuous improvement

# 15. Quality Engineering Architecture

The Quality Engineering Architecture defines how Enterprise quality activities are integrated throughout the DevOps lifecycle to ensure reliable, secure and maintainable solutions.

Quality Engineering moves quality from a final validation activity into an integrated capability across development, delivery and operation.

---

# 15.1 Quality Engineering Principles

Quality Engineering shall ensure:

- quality by design
- automated validation
- continuous feedback
- defect prevention
- measurable quality improvement
