# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundred-and-seventeenth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-116 – Privacy, Information Rights, Records Compliance, Data Protection, Consent, Retention, Data Subject Rights & Privacy Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise assurance capability covering enterprise risk management, risk taxonomy, risk registers, control frameworks, internal controls, compliance obligations, regulatory mapping, policy governance, control ownership, control testing, audit management, findings, remediation, risk acceptance, compliance monitoring, assurance planning, evidence management, control effectiveness, enterprise assurance and governance quality gates.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must establish a coherent enterprise assurance framework in which risks, obligations, policies, controls, tests, findings, remediation and evidence are continuously governed and traceable from requirement through operational outcome.**

# 146. Definition of Ready

- Risk, obligation, policy, control, audit, finding or assurance requirement is identified.

- Testing, evidence, remediation and closure requirements are defined.
