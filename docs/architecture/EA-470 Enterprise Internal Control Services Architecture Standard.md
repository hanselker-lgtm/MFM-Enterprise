# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Internal Control Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-467.

Enterprise Risk Services principles are inherited from EA-468.

Enterprise Audit Services principles are inherited from EA-469.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Internal Control Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Internal Control Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously monitored internal controls across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Internal Control Services shall

- provide centralized control management
- standardize internal control processes
- strengthen governance
- reduce operational risk
- support compliance verification
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure control traceability
- improve organizational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Internal Control Services shall provide trusted and reusable internal control capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise internal control services
- control framework management
- control design
- control implementation
- control monitoring
- control testing
- control effectiveness management
- control deficiencies
- corrective control actions
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Internal Control Services Principles

Enterprise Internal Control Services shall follow these principles.

## Control by Design

Internal controls shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, business processes, technology platforms and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every internal control activity shall be authenticated, authorized, audited and continuously monitored.

---

## Continuous Control Monitoring

Internal controls shall be continuously monitored, evaluated and improved throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Metadata Driven Control Management

Control definitions, testing, monitoring, deficiencies and corrective actions shall be classified and governed using Enterprise metadata to ensure traceability, discoverability and consistent reporting.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Internal Control Services shall remain independent of vendors, control platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Internal Control Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed Enterprise internal control management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating control framework management, control design, implementation, monitoring, testing, effectiveness management, deficiency management, corrective actions, governance, risk, audit, compliance, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables transparent, measurable and scalable internal control operations while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Internal Control Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise internal control capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 34. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Internal Control Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted control design, autonomous control testing, predictive control analytics, semantic-aware control intelligence, adaptive control optimization, continuous assurance capabilities, self-healing control frameworks and quantum-safe Enterprise internal control infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Internal Control Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven internal control capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
