# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Conscious Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a conceptual architecture model for managing future advanced reflective intelligence capabilities within the Enterprise architecture.

The Conscious Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- conscious intelligence architecture principles
- conscious intelligence governance
- conscious intelligence strategy
- conscious capability architecture
- metacognition architecture
- self-awareness architecture
- reflective intelligence architecture
- intentional intelligence architecture
- self-model architecture
- conscious knowledge architecture
- conscious decision architecture
- human-conscious intelligence collaboration
- conscious safety architecture
- conscious alignment architecture
- conscious operations
- conscious performance management
- conscious evolution

The framework ensures that advanced reflective intelligence capabilities are designed, governed and operated as responsible Enterprise capabilities.

# 3. Enterprise Conscious Intelligence Architecture Principles

The Conscious Intelligence Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Responsible Intelligence

Conscious Intelligence capabilities shall operate according to ethical, secure and governed principles.

---

## Principle 2 – Self-Monitoring Capability

Intelligent systems shall support monitoring and evaluation of their own behaviour and performance.

---

## Principle 3 – Reflective Intelligence

Conscious Intelligence capabilities shall support reflection, evaluation and improvement.

---

## Principle 4 – Human Collaboration

Conscious Intelligence shall enhance human capability and support responsible cooperation.

---

## Principle 5 – Knowledge-Based Understanding

Conscious Intelligence shall use structured knowledge and contextual understanding.

---

## Principle 6 – Safety and Alignment

Conscious Intelligence systems shall remain aligned with defined objectives, values and governance requirements.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

Conscious Intelligence capabilities shall continuously improve through learning, feedback and controlled adaptation.
