# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the thirty-fourth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-33 – Enterprise Privacy, Personal Data Protection, Records, Legal Information Governance & Regulatory Compliance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for financial governance, accounting, budgeting, forecasting, treasury, cash management, financial planning, financial controls, financial reporting, accounts payable, accounts receivable, purchasing controls, asset accounting, project accounting, financial risk, audit support and continual financial improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM financial management must provide accurate, timely, controlled and transparent financial information while protecting financial resources through effective governance, accounting, budgeting, treasury and internal financial controls.**

---

# 5. Budget Objective

# 124. Definition of Ready

A financial-management work item is Ready when:

- The transaction, budget, forecast, payment, invoice, receivable, reconciliation, asset, project, finding, exception or financial-control activity is clearly identified.
- Ownership, authority, amount, budget, risk, accounting treatment, supporting evidence and applicable requirements are known.
- Approval, implementation, validation, reporting and acceptance requirements are defined.

---
