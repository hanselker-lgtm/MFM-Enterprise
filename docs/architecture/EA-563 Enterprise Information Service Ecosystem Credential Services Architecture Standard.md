# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Credential Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Credential Services shall follow these principles.

## Credential by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include credential considerations from initial design through operational lifecycle.

---

## Trusted Credential Representation

Credentials shall provide recognized and reliable evidence of identity, capability, authorization or qualification.

---

## Credential Ownership

Every credential shall have defined ownership, responsibility and governance.

---

## Credential Lifecycle Management

Credentials shall be created, issued, maintained, reviewed, renewed and retired through controlled lifecycle processes.

---

## Governed Credential Management

Credential activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.

---

## Continuous Credential Improvement

Credential capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
