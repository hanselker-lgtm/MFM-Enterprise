# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Operations Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed operation, monitoring, management and optimization of Enterprise information services after implementation across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Operations Management Services shall

- provide centralized information operations capabilities
- establish operational governance
- support information service management
- monitor information service availability
- manage operational performance
- support capacity management
- support incident management
- support problem management
- support event management
- support operational analytics
- improve operational resilience
- support continuity requirements
- support compliance requirements
- support audit requirements
- protect information quality
- support lifecycle governance
- support master information operations
- support reference information operations
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure operational traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Operations Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable operational management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.
