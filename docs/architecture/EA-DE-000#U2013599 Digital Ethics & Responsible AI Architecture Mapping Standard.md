# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Digital Ethics & Responsible AI Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing responsible artificial intelligence capabilities.

The Responsible AI Architecture Framework shall provide:

- responsible AI principles
- digital ethics governance
- responsible AI strategy
- ethical AI capability architecture
- AI transparency architecture
- explainable AI architecture
- AI fairness architecture
- AI risk architecture
- human oversight architecture
- AI compliance architecture
- AI security ethics architecture
- responsible data architecture
- AI audit architecture
- responsible AI operations
- responsible AI performance management
- responsible AI evolution

The framework ensures that artificial intelligence capabilities are designed, developed and operated according to ethical, transparent, secure and responsible principles.

---

# 3. Enterprise Responsible AI Architecture Principles

The Responsible AI Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence

AI systems shall support human capabilities and remain aligned with human values and organizational objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Transparency and Explainability

AI decisions and recommendations shall be understandable, traceable and explainable where required.

---

## Principle 3 – Fairness and Non-Discrimination

AI systems shall be designed to minimize unfair outcomes and support equitable treatment.

---

## Principle 4 – Responsible AI Governance

AI capabilities shall be governed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 – Security and Privacy by Design

AI systems shall include security, privacy and protection mechanisms from the beginning.

---

## Principle 6 – Human Oversight

Critical AI decisions shall include appropriate human control and accountability.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Responsible AI Evolution

AI capabilities shall continuously improve according to ethical, regulatory and Enterprise requirements.

---

# 21. Responsible AI Governance Operating Model
