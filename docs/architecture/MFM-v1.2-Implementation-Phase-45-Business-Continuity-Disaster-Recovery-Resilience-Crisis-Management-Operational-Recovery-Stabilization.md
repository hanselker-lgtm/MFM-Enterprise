# 1. Purpose

This document defines the forty-fifth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-44 – Vendor, Supplier, Contract, Procurement & Third-Party Service Governance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish an integrated business continuity, disaster recovery, resilience, crisis management and operational recovery capability for MFM.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must remain sufficiently resilient to continue critical operations, recover material services within approved objectives and coordinate controlled response to disruptive events while protecting people, information, assets, services and organizational trust.**

# 5. Business Continuity

Business continuity is the capability to continue or restore critical business activities following disruption.

---

# 6. Critical Business Activity

A critical business activity is an activity whose disruption could create unacceptable impact.

---

# 7. Business Impact Analysis

# 10. Impact Over Time

Impact should be considered across time because disruption consequences may increase as outage duration increases.

---

# 11. Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption

The maximum tolerable period of disruption identifies the maximum period a critical activity can remain unavailable before unacceptable impact occurs.

---

# 12. Recovery Time Objective

Recovery Time Objective (RTO) defines the target time within which a service or activity should be restored.

---

# 13. Recovery Point Objective

Recovery Point Objective (RPO) defines the acceptable amount of data loss measured in time.

---

# 109. Definition of Ready

A resilience work item is Ready when:

- Critical service or activity is identified.
- Impact is understood.
- Recovery objectives are defined.
- Dependencies are known.
- Recovery owner is assigned.
- Test or implementation approach is defined.

# 111. Final Continuity Principle

> **Continuity planning must be based on business impact and critical services rather than technology alone.**

---

# 112. Final Recovery Principle

> **Recovery objectives must be measurable, approved and tested against actual capabilities.**

---

# 113. Final Resilience Principle

> **Resilience is demonstrated through the ability to withstand, respond to, recover from and learn from disruption.**

---

# 114. Final Testing Principle

> **A recovery procedure that has never been tested cannot be treated as proven recovery capability.**

---

# 115. Final Dependency Principle

> **Recovery plans must account for dependencies and the possibility that supporting dependencies fail during the same event.**

---

# 116. Final Crisis Principle

> **Crisis management requires clear authority, coordinated communication, disciplined decisions and a controlled transition back to normal operations.**

---

# 117. Final Data Principle

> **Recovery must protect data integrity, confidentiality, completeness and auditability throughout the recovery lifecycle.**

---

# 118. Final Improvement Principle

> **Every recovery test, exercise and disruptive event should produce actionable resilience learning.**

---

# 119. Final Integration Principle

> **Business continuity, disaster recovery, resilience and crisis management must integrate with incident, security, risk, vendor, configuration, monitoring, service and change governance.**

---

# 120. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should operate a tested resilience capability that connects business impact, recovery objectives, technical recovery, crisis coordination and continual improvement into one controlled operational model.**
