# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Awareness Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities detect, interpret, understand and respond to internal and external conditions.

The Intelligence Awareness Architecture Framework shall provide:

- awareness architecture principles
- perception and detection models
- knowledge awareness management
- situational understanding capability
- adaptive response mechanisms
- awareness governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing Intelligence capabilities through continuous awareness and adaptation.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Awareness Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Awareness Model
- Awareness Intelligence Architecture
- Awareness Knowledge Architecture
- Awareness Capability Model
- Awareness Architecture Integration
- Awareness Governance Model
- Awareness Evolution Framework
- Awareness Metrics
- Awareness Maturity Model
- Awareness Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

# 3. Intelligence Awareness Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Awareness Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Continuous Awareness

Intelligence capability shall continuously observe and understand relevant conditions.

---

## Principle 2 – Contextual Understanding

Awareness shall be based on context, relationships and meaning.

---

## Principle 3 – Adaptive Response

Intelligence capability shall adapt according to changing conditions.

---

## Principle 4 – Integrated Perception

Information from multiple sources shall be combined into unified awareness.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Learning

Awareness capability shall improve through experience and learning.

---

## Principle 6 – Responsible Awareness

Awareness development shall maintain governance and responsibility.

---

## Principle 7 – Future Readiness

The architecture shall support emerging Intelligence capabilities.
