# 1. Purpose

This document defines the ninety-first implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-90 – Data Platform, Master Data, Information Architecture, Data Lifecycle, Data Quality & Enterprise Information Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled analytics and reporting capability covering reporting governance, management information, business intelligence, analytics governance, KPI governance, metric definitions, dashboards, reporting lifecycle, report ownership, data-to-report lineage, decision support, forecasting, trend analysis, operational analytics, management reporting, executive reporting, performance analytics, reporting quality and reporting assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that reporting, analytics and management information are trusted, timely, understandable, traceable and aligned with decision needs so that management and operational users can make informed, evidence-based decisions.**

- The business or decision need is defined.
- Audience is identified.

# 149. Final Lineage Principle

> **Reporting quality must be actively governed across accuracy, completeness, timeliness, consistency and validity.**

---

# 151. Final Analytics Principle

> **Analytics must make assumptions, methods, limitations and interpretation sufficiently visible to support responsible decision-making.**

---

# 152. Final Forecast Principle

> **Forecasts are decision-support information and must be understood in terms of assumptions, uncertainty, confidence and changing evidence.**

---

# 153. Final Decision Principle

> **Material decisions should be supported by traceable evidence, explicit options, documented rationale and appropriate authority.**

---

# 154. Final Assurance Principle

> **Reporting and analytics assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that information is reliable and fit for its intended decision or governance purpose.**

# 155. Final Integration Principle
