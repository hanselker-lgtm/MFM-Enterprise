# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the thirty-sixth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-35 – Enterprise Procurement, Supplier Management, Contract Management, Third-Party Governance & Vendor Risk.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for people governance, workforce planning, organizational capability, recruitment, onboarding, personnel administration, roles and responsibilities, competence management, learning and development, performance management, succession, workforce risk, employee lifecycle, people data governance, employee relations, health and wellbeing, workforce continuity and continual organizational capability improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that people, roles, competencies, workforce capacity and organizational capability are planned, governed, developed and maintained so that the enterprise can reliably execute its strategy, services, controls and obligations.**

# 125. Definition of Ready

A people, workforce or organizational-capability work item is Ready when:

- The role, position, recruitment activity, competence requirement, training need, performance issue, workforce risk, succession activity, employee lifecycle event, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, business need, authority, competence, privacy, security, financial and continuity requirements are known.
- Approval, implementation, evidence, validation and acceptance requirements are defined.
