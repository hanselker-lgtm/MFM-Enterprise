# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Assurance Services Architecture supporting confidence, verification, transparency and evidence-based confirmation that Enterprise capabilities, services, controls and operations perform according to approved requirements across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Assurance Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem assurance services
- support assurance governance
- support assurance strategy
- support assurance objectives
- support assurance planning
- support operational assurance
- support service assurance

- support compliance assurance
- support quality assurance
- support performance assurance
- support reliability assurance

- support evidence assurance

- support assurance measurement
- support assurance improvement
- support assurance maturity development
- support continuous assurance capability development

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Assurance Services shall provide trusted and reusable assurance capabilities enabling Enterprise stakeholders to have confidence in Enterprise operations, services and capabilities.

Enterprise capabilities shall include assurance requirements from initial design.

---

## Continuous Assurance

Enterprise capabilities shall be continuously evaluated and monitored.

---

## Transparent Assurance

Assurance results shall be visible to authorized stakeholders.

---

Assurance activities shall prioritize areas of greatest Enterprise importance.

---

## Controlled Assurance

Assurance activities shall follow approved governance and control processes.

---

## Continuous Assurance Improvement

Assurance capabilities shall continuously evolve through learning and experience.
