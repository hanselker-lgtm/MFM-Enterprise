# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Integrity Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Availability principles are inherited from EA-436.

All Enterprise Data Integrity implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Integrity Architecture ensuring that Enterprise information remains accurate, complete, consistent, authentic and trustworthy throughout its entire lifecycle.

Enterprise Data Integrity shall

- preserve information accuracy
- maintain information consistency
- ensure data authenticity
- protect against unauthorized modification
- strengthen regulatory compliance
- support trusted decision-making
- improve operational reliability
- enable secure information exchange
- strengthen enterprise governance
- support continuous business operations

Enterprise Data Integrity shall provide a standardized architectural framework ensuring confidence in Enterprise information across all Business and Technology Domains.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise databases
- master data
- transactional data
- analytical data
- application data
- APIs
- event streams
- integration platforms
- cloud services
- archived information

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, storage technology, infrastructure platform or cloud provider.

# 3. Enterprise Data Integrity Principles

Enterprise Data Integrity shall be governed by the following principles.

## Integrity by Design

Enterprise information shall be designed, stored, transmitted and processed in a manner that preserves integrity throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Authenticity

Enterprise information shall be verifiable with regard to origin, ownership and authorized modification.

---

## Consistency

Enterprise information shall remain logically and structurally consistent across all Enterprise systems.

---

## Validation First

Integrity controls shall continuously validate Enterprise information before, during and after processing.

---

## Traceability

Every modification affecting Enterprise information shall be fully traceable through standardized audit mechanisms.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Integrity Architecture shall remain independent of implementation technologies, storage platforms and infrastructure vendors.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Integrity Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework that ensures Enterprise information remains accurate, complete, authentic, consistent and trustworthy throughout its entire lifecycle.

By integrating validation, reconciliation, cryptographic protection, security, observability, automation and governance into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform provides trusted information that supports reliable business operations, regulatory compliance and high-quality decision-making across all Business and Technology Domains.
