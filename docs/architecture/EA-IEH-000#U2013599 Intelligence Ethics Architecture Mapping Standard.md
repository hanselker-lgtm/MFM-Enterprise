# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Ethics Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive ethical architecture for ensuring that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are developed, deployed and operated responsibly according to human values, ethical principles and organisational responsibility.

The Intelligence Ethics Architecture Framework shall provide:

- ethical principles
- ethics governance
- responsible Intelligence architecture
- ethical AI architecture
- fairness management
- bias management
- human values alignment
- moral decision support
- ethical compliance
- social responsibility
- ethics measurement
- ethics maturity
- ethics evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence creates value while respecting human dignity, fairness, responsibility and societal expectations.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Ethics Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture ethics domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Ethics Model
- Ethics Governance
- Responsible Intelligence Architecture
- Ethical AI Architecture
- Fairness Architecture
- Bias Management Architecture
- Human Values Alignment
- Moral Decision Architecture
- Ethical Compliance
- Social Responsibility
- Ethics Metrics
- Ethics Maturity
- Ethics Roadmaps
- Ethics Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- autonomous decision environments
- human-machine collaboration systems
- future Intelligence ecosystems

---

# 3. Intelligence Ethics Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Ethics Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Human-Centred Intelligence

Enterprise Intelligence shall support and enhance human capability.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsibility

Organisations shall remain responsible for Intelligence outcomes.

---

## Principle 3 – Fairness

Intelligence systems shall operate fairly and avoid unjust discrimination.

---

## Principle 4 – Transparency

Intelligence behaviour and decisions shall be understandable.

---

## Principle 5 – Human Values Alignment

Intelligence capabilities shall align with human and organisational values.

---

## Principle 6 – Ethical Decision Support

Intelligence shall support responsible decision-making.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Ethical Improvement

Ethical capability shall continuously evolve through learning and evaluation.

# 9. Intelligence Ethics Architecture
