# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Monitoring and Observability throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability ensures that applications, services, infrastructure and integrations provide sufficient operational visibility to detect, diagnose and resolve issues while preserving reliability, traceability, resilience and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Metrics Collection
- Distributed Tracing
- Health Monitoring
- Alert Management
- Dashboards
- Service Level Indicators (SLI)
- Service Level Objectives (SLO)
- Operational Visibility
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Monitoring and Observability Principles

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall follow these principles.

- Observability by Design
- Centralized Monitoring
- Complete Traceability
- Real-Time Visibility
- Technology Independence
- Operational Resilience
- Continuous Improvement
- Centralized Governance

Monitoring implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

# 5. Monitoring Responsibilities

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability shall provide

- metrics collection
- distributed tracing
- health monitoring
- alert management
- dashboard visualization
- operational reporting
- governance reporting
- compliance verification

Additional Monitoring responsibilities shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

# 8. Metrics Collection

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall implement standardized metrics collection.

Metrics collection shall

- collect operational metrics
- collect infrastructure metrics
- collect application metrics
- preserve metric traceability
- support real-time analysis
- maintain metric consistency

Metrics collection shall remain centrally governed.

# 9. Distributed Tracing

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall implement standardized distributed tracing.

Distributed tracing shall

- trace end-to-end requests
- preserve correlation identifiers
- support dependency visualization
- enable root cause analysis
- maintain trace consistency
- support enterprise interoperability

Distributed tracing shall remain technology independent.

# 10. Health Monitoring

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall implement standardized health monitoring.

Health monitoring shall

- monitor application health
- monitor service health
- monitor infrastructure health
- monitor dependency health
- preserve monitoring history
- support automated health verification

Health monitoring shall support proactive operations.

---

# 11. Alert Management

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall implement standardized alert management.

Alert management shall

- detect operational anomalies
- notify responsible teams
- prioritize alerts
- support automated escalation
- preserve alert traceability
- maintain alert consistency

Alert policies shall remain centrally governed.

- visualize operational metrics

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall handle monitoring-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify metrics collection failures
- classify distributed tracing failures
- classify health monitoring failures
- classify alert management failures
- classify dashboard availability failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Monitoring exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, observability, traceability, governance, compliance, resilience or operational reliability.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations may depend upon

- approved monitoring platforms
- approved logging platforms
- approved metrics storage services
- approved tracing services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external monitoring providers

Monitoring capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementation is compliant when

- Metrics collection is implemented.
- Distributed tracing is operational.
- Health monitoring is implemented.
- Alert management follows enterprise policy.
- Dashboards provide operational visibility.
- Service Level Indicators are defined.
- Service Level Objectives are documented.
- Monitoring governance is implemented.
- Compliance requirements are fulfilled.
- Operational verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Monitoring Without Metrics

Enterprise services shall never operate in production without collecting approved operational metrics.

---

## Missing Distributed Tracing

Distributed requests shall never execute across enterprise services without trace correlation where applicable.

---

## Unmanaged Alerts

Critical operational events shall never occur without approved alert generation and escalation.

---

## Dashboard-Only Monitoring

Operational decisions shall never rely solely on dashboards without supporting metrics, logs and traces.

---

## Undefined Service Levels

Business-critical services shall never operate without documented Service Level Indicators and Service Level Objectives.

---

## Monitoring Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent monitoring mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Monitoring and Observability services.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- metrics collection compliance
- distributed tracing compliance
- health monitoring compliance
- alert management compliance
- dashboard compliance
- SLI compliance
- SLO compliance
- dependency compliance
- documentation completeness
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Monitoring and Observability Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Monitoring and Observability throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise systems provide consistent operational visibility through standardized metrics, distributed tracing, health monitoring, alerting and governance while preserving reliability, traceability, resilience and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Monitoring and Observability implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
