# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the one-hundred-and-fifteenth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-114 – Enterprise Cybersecurity Architecture, Security Governance, Security Operations, Threat Management, Vulnerability Management & Cybersecurity Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for IT service strategy, service governance, service catalog, service ownership, service levels, service requests, incident management, major incident management, problem management, change enablement, release coordination, configuration management, asset integration, knowledge management, service continuity, service monitoring, service reporting, continual service improvement, ITSM risk, exceptions, remediation, assurance, metrics, dashboards, maturity and continual enterprise service management capability improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must govern and operate IT services as controlled enterprise capabilities, ensuring that services are clearly owned, defined, measurable, supportable, secure, resilient, observable and continuously improved throughout their lifecycle.**

# 3. IT Service Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish clear authority, accountability, service ownership, governance standards, performance requirements and assurance for enterprise IT services.**

# 4. Service Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide reliable and measurable IT services aligned with business requirements, user needs, security requirements and enterprise architecture.**

# 5. Service Catalog Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain a clear and authoritative view of available IT services, their owners, consumers, service levels, dependencies and support arrangements.**

# 6. Incident Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore normal service as quickly and safely as practical while minimizing business impact.**

# 7. Problem Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and eliminate underlying causes of recurring or significant incidents and reduce future service disruption.**

# 8. Change Enablement Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable necessary changes while controlling risk to service availability, security, resilience and business operations.**

# 9. Configuration Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain trustworthy information about configuration items, relationships and service dependencies to support effective operational decisions.**

# 10. ITSM Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide evidence that IT service management processes, controls, service levels and operational responsibilities operate effectively.**

# 116. Definition of Ready

A service, service catalog entry, SLA, service request, incident process, major incident, problem, change, release, configuration item, knowledge article, recovery procedure, exception, remediation or assurance review is Ready when purpose, owner, scope, business impact, dependencies, security requirements, service requirements, approval authority and acceptance criteria are defined.

# 117. Definition of Done

An ITSM work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement / Service Event Identified
        ↓
Owner Assigned
        ↓
ITSM Action Completed
        ↓
Service / User / Security / Operational Validation Completed where Required
        ↓
Service / Catalog / Incident / Problem / Change / Configuration / Knowledge Records Updated
        ↓
Evidence Captured
        ↓
Findings / Exceptions Addressed
        ↓
Outcome Accepted
```
