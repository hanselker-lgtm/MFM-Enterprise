# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Event Subscription Architecture throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Event Subscription Architecture provides standardized mechanisms for registering, validating, maintaining and governing event subscriptions while preserving architectural integrity, scalability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Event Subscription
- Subscription Registration
- Subscription Lifecycle
- Subscription Filtering
- Subscription Validation
- Subscription Management
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Event Subscription implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Enterprise Event Subscription Principles

Enterprise Event Subscription implementations shall follow these principles.

- Explicit Subscription Registration
- Controlled Subscription Lifecycle
- Deterministic Subscription Filtering

- Technology Independence
- Traceable Subscription Management

Enterprise Event Subscription implementations shall remain independent of presentation, persistence and business rule concerns.

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Uncontrolled Subscription Registration

Subscriptions shall never be registered without approved governance and ownership validation.

---

## Invalid Subscription Filters

Subscription filtering shall never permit undefined or unsupported event selection criteria.

---

## Missing Subscription Validation

Subscriptions shall never become active without successful validation of configuration and compatibility.

---

## Hidden Subscription Dependencies

Enterprise implementations shall never introduce undocumented subscription infrastructure or runtime dependencies.

---

## Cross-Capability Subscription Bypass

Capabilities shall never subscribe directly to internal implementation events outside approved enterprise contracts.

---

## Unmanaged Subscription Lifecycle

Subscriptions shall never remain active, modified or retired outside approved lifecycle management procedures.

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Event Subscription Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Event Subscription Architecture throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that subscription registration, lifecycle management, filtering, validation and governance are implemented consistently while preserving maintainability, scalability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Event Subscription implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
