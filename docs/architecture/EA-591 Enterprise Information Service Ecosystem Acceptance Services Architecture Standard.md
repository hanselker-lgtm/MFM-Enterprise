# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Acceptance Services Architecture supporting formal approval, ownership transfer and operational acceptance of Enterprise capabilities, services, solutions and outcomes across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Acceptance Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem acceptance services
- support acceptance governance
- support acceptance strategy
- support acceptance objectives
- support acceptance planning
- support acceptance criteria management
- support business acceptance
- support operational acceptance
- support service acceptance
- support capability acceptance
- support technology acceptance
- support application acceptance
- support infrastructure acceptance
- support data acceptance
- support information acceptance
- support security acceptance
- support compliance acceptance
- support quality acceptance
- support performance acceptance
- support stakeholder acceptance
- support approval management
- support acceptance evidence management
- support acceptance measurement
- support acceptance improvement
- support acceptance maturity development
- support continuous acceptance capability development

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Acceptance Services shall provide trusted and reusable acceptance capabilities enabling Enterprise stakeholders to formally approve that capabilities and services are ready for operational use.

## Stakeholder Acceptance

---

---

Acceptance shall continue throughout the Enterprise capability lifecycle.

---

## Continuous Acceptance Improvement

Acceptance capabilities shall continuously evolve through learning and experience.
