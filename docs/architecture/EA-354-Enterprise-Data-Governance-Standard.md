# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to define the Enterprise Architecture governing Data Governance throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Data Governance Architecture shall

- establish Enterprise-wide governance principles
- define ownership and accountability
- standardize decision-making
- strengthen regulatory compliance
- improve data quality
- improve transparency
- support Enterprise Analytics
- support Enterprise AI
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Governance shall ensure that Enterprise information is managed consistently, responsibly and in accordance with business objectives.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to all Enterprise Data assets.

It governs

- governance organization

- ownership

- decision authorities

- audit governance

The standard applies independently of implementation technology, organizational structure or cloud provider.

# 18. Enterprise Data Governance Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Undefined Ownership

Enterprise Data shall never exist without documented ownership.

Every Enterprise Data Domain shall have one accountable Data Owner.

---

## Governance Without Authority

Governance decisions shall never be made without documented decision authority.

Decision responsibilities shall remain explicit.

---

## Missing Stewardship

Enterprise Data Domains shall never operate without assigned Data Stewards.

Operational governance responsibilities shall always be defined.

---

## Inconsistent Governance Policies

Conflicting governance policies across Enterprise Data Domains are prohibited.

Enterprise Governance shall remain consistent across the organization.

---

## Reactive Governance

Governance shall not rely solely on reacting to incidents.

Preventive governance activities shall be continuously performed.

---

## Governance Outside Enterprise Architecture

Enterprise Data Governance shall never operate independently of Enterprise Architecture.

Governance shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture principles and standards.
