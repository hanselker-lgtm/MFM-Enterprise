# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, and intelligence capabilities required for an **Intelligence Synthetic Evolution Enterprise Architecture (ISEA)**.

While EA-IMEA59 enables Enterprise Architecture to coordinate intelligence across multiple autonomous semantic realities, EA-ISEA60 enables Enterprise Intelligence to **autonomously create new architectural knowledge**, generate original semantic structures, evolve governance concepts, and synthesize enterprise capabilities that have never previously existed.

The objective is not merely to discover or integrate knowledge, but to **safely generate governed, explainable, and valuable new knowledge** that extends Enterprise Architecture beyond its inherited conceptual boundaries.

Enterprise Architecture shall therefore evolve from knowledge integration to knowledge synthesis.
