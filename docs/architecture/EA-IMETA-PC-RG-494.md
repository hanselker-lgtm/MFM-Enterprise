# 2. Architectural Position

RG-493 established enterprise value-outcome networks, benefit realisation orchestration, cross-value-stream optimisation, value resilience and autonomous outcome governance.

RG-494 moves one level higher: it establishes the **strategic orchestration layer** that ensures outcome and benefit portfolios across domains converge on authorised enterprise strategy rather than independently optimising local objectives.

```text
RG-492 VALUE REALISATION
        ↓
RG-493 OUTCOME NETWORKS
        ↓
RG-494 STRATEGIC OUTCOME ALIGNMENT
        ↓
BENEFIT PORTFOLIO CONVERGENCE
        ↓
CROSS-DOMAIN VALUE SYNCHRONISATION
        ↓
STRATEGIC BENEFIT RESILIENCE
        ↓
AUTONOMIC ENTERPRISE OUTCOME ORCHESTRATION
```

# 3. Core Architectural Question

**How can enterprise strategy be translated into a coherent, continuously aligned network of outcomes and benefit portfolios across domains, while preserving strategic optionality, preventing local optimisation from fragmenting enterprise value, and allowing autonomous orchestration only within explicitly authorised boundaries?**

# 4. Governing Principle

**Strategy SHALL remain the authoritative source of enterprise direction. Outcome and benefit portfolios SHALL demonstrate traceable strategic contribution, material conflicts SHALL be surfaced rather than hidden, cross-domain dependencies SHALL be governed, and autonomous orchestration SHALL optimise only within approved strategic, governance, security, resilience, liquidity and authority boundaries.**

```text
STRATEGY
   ↓
STRATEGIC OUTCOMES
   ↓
BENEFIT PORTFOLIOS
   ↓
CROSS-DOMAIN CONVERGENCE
   ↓
VALUE SYNCHRONISATION
   ↓
EXECUTION
   ↓
REALISED BENEFIT
   ↓
STRATEGIC FEEDBACK
   ↺
```

# 8. Alignment Model

Alignment SHALL consider:

- strategic contribution;
- timing;
- value;
- resilience;
- dependency;
- resource consumption;
- optionality;
- confidence.

A high-value outcome with weak strategic fit SHALL not be treated as automatically strategic.

# 9. Strategic Coherence

The enterprise SHALL evaluate the portfolio as a system.

```text
OUTCOME A ─┐
OUTCOME B ─┼→ ENTERPRISE STRATEGY
OUTCOME C ─┘
```

Coherence SHALL account for synergies, conflicts and shared constraints.

# 10. Strategic Drift

Strategic drift SHALL be detected when:

- priorities change;
- assumptions become invalid;
- outcomes no longer contribute materially;
- external conditions alter strategic value;
- portfolios continue following obsolete objectives.

Drift SHALL trigger reassessment rather than silent continuation.

# 11. Alignment Debt

Alignment debt is accumulated work or investment that continues despite weakening strategic relevance.

Alignment debt SHALL be measurable and owned.

# 12. Strategic Assumptions

Material assumptions SHALL be explicit.

Examples:

- market assumptions;
- resource assumptions;
- technology assumptions;
- regulatory assumptions;
- capacity assumptions;
- timing assumptions.

Changes in high-sensitivity assumptions SHALL trigger review.

# 14. Benefit Portfolio Convergence

Multiple benefit portfolios SHALL be evaluated for convergence.

```text

DOMAIN C BENEFITS ─┘
```

Convergence SHALL not imply identical execution; it means alignment of contribution and strategic direction.
