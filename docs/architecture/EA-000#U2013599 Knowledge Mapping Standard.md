# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Knowledge Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise Knowledge capabilities across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Knowledge Reference Model shall provide:

- knowledge identification
- knowledge classification
- knowledge ownership
- knowledge relationships
- knowledge information mapping
- knowledge data mapping
- knowledge lifecycle management
- knowledge governance
- architecture traceability

The model shall establish a common Enterprise language for defining, managing and evolving knowledge assets.

---

# 2. Scope

The Knowledge Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture knowledge domains:

- Architecture Knowledge
- Business Knowledge
- Operational Knowledge
- Technical Knowledge
- Information Knowledge
- Data Knowledge
- Security Knowledge
- Governance Knowledge
- Ecosystem Knowledge
- Evolution Knowledge

The model applies to:

- strategic knowledge
- operational knowledge
- technical knowledge
- organizational knowledge
- experiential knowledge
- transformation knowledge
- evolutionary knowledge

---

# 3. Enterprise Knowledge Architecture Principles

The Knowledge Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Knowledge as an Enterprise Asset

Knowledge shall be managed as a strategic Enterprise asset.

---

## Knowledge Ownership

Every knowledge asset shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Knowledge Governance

Knowledge shall be managed according to Enterprise policies and standards.

---

## Knowledge Quality

Knowledge shall be accurate, relevant, current and usable.

---

## Knowledge Accessibility

Authorized users shall have appropriate access to required knowledge.

---

## Knowledge Security

Sensitive knowledge shall be protected throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Knowledge Lifecycle Management

Knowledge shall be managed from creation to preservation.

---

## Continuous Learning

Knowledge capabilities shall continuously evolve through experience and improvement.

---
