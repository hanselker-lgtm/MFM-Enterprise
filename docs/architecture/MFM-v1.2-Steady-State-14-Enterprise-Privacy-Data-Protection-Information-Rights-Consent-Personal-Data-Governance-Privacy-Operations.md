# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the fourteenth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-13 – Enterprise Data Management, Data Governance, Data Quality, Master Data, Metadata, Records, Information Lifecycle & Analytics Operations.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise privacy and personal-data governance operating model for MFM, covering privacy governance, personal-data inventories, lawful processing, purpose limitation, data minimization, transparency, consent, information rights, privacy-by-design, privacy impact assessments, data protection controls, data sharing, processors, international transfers, retention, deletion, privacy incidents, privacy assurance and continual improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must process personal data lawfully, fairly, transparently, securely and only for defined purposes, while respecting applicable information rights and maintaining accountable privacy governance throughout the personal-data lifecycle.**
