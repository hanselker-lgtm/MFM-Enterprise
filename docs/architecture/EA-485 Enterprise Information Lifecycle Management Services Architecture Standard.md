# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of Enterprise information throughout its complete lifecycle.

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management Services shall

- provide centralized lifecycle management
- standardize information lifecycle processes
- manage information from creation to disposal
- strengthen information governance
- support regulatory requirements
- improve information value management
- support retention and preservation requirements
- reduce unnecessary information complexity
- support privacy and protection requirements
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure lifecycle traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable lifecycle management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Lifecycle Management by Design

Information lifecycle capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Complete Information Lifecycle Control

Enterprise information shall be governed from creation through active usage, retention, preservation and final disposal.

---

## Value-Based Information Management

Information lifecycle decisions shall consider business value, regulatory requirements, operational needs and risk exposure.

---

## Governed Information Evolution

Information shall be managed consistently as it moves through different lifecycle stages.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management Services shall remain independent of vendors, lifecycle platforms, storage providers, cloud providers and implementation technologies.
