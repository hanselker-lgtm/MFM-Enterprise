# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Civilization Architecture Framework is to establish a future-oriented architecture for understanding how Enterprise Intelligence capabilities influence, support and integrate with advanced civilisation structures.

The Intelligence Civilization Architecture Framework shall provide:

- civilization intelligence principles
- human–Intelligence civilization models
- global Intelligence ecosystem architecture
- intelligent civilization governance
- civilization sustainability
- civilization resilience
- civilization knowledge evolution
- civilization innovation evolution
- civilization economic evolution
- civilization cultural evolution
- civilization trust development
- civilization measurement
- civilization maturity
- civilization evolution

The framework ensures that Intelligence evolution contributes to responsible, sustainable and human-aligned civilization development.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Civilization Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture civilization domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Civilization Model

- future knowledge-based economies
- emerging civilization models

# 3. Intelligence Civilization Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Civilization Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Human-Centred Civilization Evolution

Intelligence development shall enhance human civilisation while preserving human values and identity.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Intelligence Advancement

Civilization-level Intelligence shall develop responsibly and ethically.

---

## Principle 3 – Sustainable Civilization Development

Intelligence evolution shall support long-term environmental, social and economic balance.

---

## Principle 4 – Global Intelligence Cooperation

Civilization advancement shall enable cooperation across communities and ecosystems.

---

## Principle 5 – Knowledge Expansion

Intelligence shall support continuous knowledge creation and discovery.

---

## Principle 6 – Civilization Resilience

Future civilizations shall maintain adaptability and continuity.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Civilization Evolution

Civilization shall continuously evolve through learning, innovation and responsible Intelligence.
