# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the seventy-ninth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-78 – Enterprise Service Management, ITSM, Service Desk, Incident, Request, Problem, Change, Release & Service Level Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for IT financial management, technology cost management, financial governance, technology budgeting, forecasting, cost allocation, service costing, chargeback, showback, technology investment management, business cases, portfolio economics, technology value realization, financial controls, cost optimization, cloud financial management, FinOps coordination, supplier economics, licensing economics, total cost of ownership, technology asset economics, financial reporting, variance management, investment prioritization, benefits realization, financial assurance, financial findings, financial exceptions, financial remediation, technology financial metrics, financial dashboards, maturity and continual technology financial improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage technology costs, investments and value transparently so that technology resources are financially sustainable, appropriately prioritized and aligned with business outcomes.**

# 3. IT Financial Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure technology financial resources are planned, governed, measured and optimized in alignment with business priorities.**

# 4. Technology Cost Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide transparent understanding of technology costs, cost drivers, ownership and trends.**

# 5. Budget Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish realistic and controlled technology budgets aligned with business and service requirements.**

# 6. Forecast Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide forward-looking financial visibility to support timely decisions and corrective actions.**

# 7. Investment Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Prioritize technology investments according to business value, risk, strategic alignment, cost and expected benefits.**

# 8. Value Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Demonstrate that technology expenditure contributes to measurable business outcomes, service quality, risk reduction or strategic capability.**

# 92. Definition of Ready

A technology budget, forecast, investment, business case, cost allocation model, chargeback model, showback model, savings initiative, financial exception, benefits plan, supplier renewal or assurance review is Ready when purpose, owner, scope, financial baseline, assumptions, dependencies, risks, decision criteria, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
