# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise data governance throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise data governance ensures that enterprise data is governed, owned, managed, protected and continuously improved through standardized governance processes while preserving accountability, compliance, consistency, quality and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Data Governance
- Data Ownership
- Data Stewardship
- Data Policies
- Data Decision Authority
- Data Quality Governance
- Data Compliance
- Continuous Improvement

All enterprise data governance implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Data Governance Principles

Enterprise data governance shall follow these principles.

- Governance by Design
- Accountability
- Data Ownership
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Compliance
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Data governance implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Data Governance Domains

Enterprise data governance shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Master Data Governance
- Reference Data Governance
- Metadata Governance
- Operational Data Governance
- Analytical Data Governance
- Compliance Data Governance
- Security Data Governance
- Reporting Data Governance

Additional governance domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Data Governance Ownership

Each data governance domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- data stewardship

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the governance lifecycle.

---

# 7. Data Governance Organization

Enterprise data governance shall define

- governance committees
- approval authority
- stewardship responsibilities
- architecture review responsibilities
- governance verification
- governance reporting

Data governance organization shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Data Governance Responsibilities

Enterprise data governance shall provide controlled governance of enterprise data assets.

Data governance responsibilities shall

- separate governance from business execution
- coordinate data ownership
- ensure governance consistency
- validate governance objectives
- preserve governance traceability
- support enterprise governance

Data governance implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Data Ownership

Enterprise data governance shall implement standardized data ownership.

Data ownership shall

- assign accountable owners
- define ownership responsibilities
- define ownership boundaries
- define approval authority
- preserve ownership history
- maintain ownership traceability

Data ownership shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Data Stewardship

Enterprise data governance shall implement standardized data stewardship.

Data stewardship shall

- manage enterprise data quality
- coordinate data maintenance
- ensure standards compliance
- support governance processes
- preserve stewardship history
- maintain stewardship traceability

Data stewardship shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Data Policies

Enterprise data governance shall implement standardized data policies.

Data policies shall

- define governance standards
- define quality requirements
- define security requirements
- define compliance requirements
- preserve policy history
- maintain policy traceability

Data policies shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Data Decision Authority

Enterprise data governance shall implement standardized decision authority.

Decision authority shall

- approve governance decisions
- resolve ownership conflicts
- approve governance exceptions
- preserve decision history
- maintain decision traceability
- support enterprise accountability

Decision authority shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Data Governance Dependencies

Enterprise data governance shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- master data repositories
- metadata repositories
- policy management
- enterprise repositories
- enterprise infrastructure

Data governance implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Data Governance Documentation

Each data governance domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- governance registers
- ownership information
- stewardship assignments
- governance policies
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

- validate accuracy

Compliance governance shall

- verify regulatory compliance
- verify policy compliance

- maintain compliance traceability

Lifecycle governance shall include

- creation
- approval

- usage

Lifecycle governance shall preserve complete traceability throughout every lifecycle phase.

- policy compliance
- data quality trends
- governance performance

# 21. Continuous Improvement

Enterprise data governance shall continuously improve governance capabilities.

Continuous improvement shall

- evaluate governance maturity
- identify improvement opportunities
- eliminate governance deficiencies
- improve governance processes
- improve governance documentation
- improve enterprise consistency

Continuous improvement shall remain part of normal governance activities.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise data governance implementations shall handle governance-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify governance failures
- classify ownership conflicts
- classify stewardship failures
- classify policy violations
- classify compliance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Data governance exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, accountability, compliance, data quality or regulatory obligations.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Data governance implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved master data repositories
- approved metadata repositories
- approved policy management capabilities
- approved enterprise repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Data governance implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external governance services

Data governance capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A data governance implementation is compliant when

- Governance responsibilities are documented.
- Data ownership is assigned.
- Data stewardship responsibilities are documented.
- Governance policies are approved.
- Decision authority is documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Governance Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Governance verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Data Ownership

Enterprise data shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Undefined Governance Responsibilities

Governance responsibilities shall never remain undefined or ambiguous.

---

## Missing Stewardship

Enterprise data shall never be managed without assigned data stewards.

---

## Governance Outside Approved Policies

Enterprise governance shall never bypass approved enterprise policies.

---

## Undocumented Governance Dependencies

Data governance implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Governance Without Auditability

Enterprise data governance shall never operate without complete auditability and traceability.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise data governance implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- governance quality
- ownership completeness
- stewardship effectiveness
- policy compliance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- enterprise consistency
- compliance with enterprise standards

The Enterprise Data Governance Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise data governance throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

All enterprise data governance implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
