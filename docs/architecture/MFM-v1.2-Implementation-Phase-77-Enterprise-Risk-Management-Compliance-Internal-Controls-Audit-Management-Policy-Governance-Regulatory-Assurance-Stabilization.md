# 1. Purpose

This document defines the seventy-seventh implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-76 – Privacy Governance, Personal Data Protection, Data Subject Rights, Consent, Privacy Operations & Privacy Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise risk, compliance and assurance capability covering risk identification, assessment, treatment, acceptance, compliance obligations, regulatory requirements, internal controls, control testing, audit management, findings, corrective actions, policy governance, compliance monitoring and regulatory assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must identify and manage material enterprise risks, maintain awareness of applicable obligations, operate effective internal controls, govern policies and demonstrate evidence-based compliance and assurance.**

# 135. Definition of Ready

An enterprise assurance work item is Ready when:

- Scope is defined.
- Applicable risk or obligation is identified.
- Owner is assigned.
- Relevant criteria are known.
- Existing controls are identified.
- Evidence requirements are understood.
- Expected outcome is defined.

# 137. Final Risk Principle

> **Material risks must be visible, owned, assessed, treated and monitored against approved risk appetite and tolerance.**

---

# 138. Final Compliance Principle

> **Compliance must be managed as an operational capability connecting obligations to owners, controls, evidence, assessment and remediation.**

---

# 139. Final Control Principle

> **Controls must have clear objectives, accountable ownership, appropriate evidence and periodic assessment of design and operating effectiveness.**

---

# 140. Final Audit Principle

> **Audit should provide independent or appropriately objective evidence regarding governance, risk, control and compliance effectiveness.**

---

# 141. Final Policy Principle

> **Policies must be controlled through an explicit lifecycle from drafting and approval through publication, operation, review and retirement.**

---

# 142. Final Regulatory Principle

> **Regulatory change must be translated into actionable requirements, controls, implementation activities and evidence.**

---

# 143. Final Corrective Action Principle

> **Findings and corrective actions remain open until appropriate evidence demonstrates that the underlying issue has been addressed and, where required, effectiveness has been verified.**

---

# 144. Final Assurance Principle

> **Enterprise assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that risks, obligations, controls, policies and regulatory requirements are understood and effectively governed.**

---

# 145. Final Integration Principle

> **Enterprise Assurance must integrate with Security, Privacy, Data, Financial, Service, Supplier, Project, Change, Continuity, Architecture and Executive Governance.**

---

# 146. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage enterprise assurance through a controlled lifecycle connecting risk, compliance, controls, audit, policy, regulatory change, findings, corrective actions, evidence and continuous improvement.**
