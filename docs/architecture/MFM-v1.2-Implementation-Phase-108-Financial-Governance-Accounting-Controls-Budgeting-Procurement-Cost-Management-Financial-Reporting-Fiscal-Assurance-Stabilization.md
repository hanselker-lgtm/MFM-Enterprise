# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundred-and-eighth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-107 – Risk Management, Compliance, Internal Controls, Audit, Policy Governance, Regulatory Obligations & Enterprise Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled financial management capability covering financial governance, accounting controls, chart of accounts, budgeting, forecasting, financial planning, cost management, procurement, purchase-to-pay, supplier financial controls, invoice management, payments, revenue, asset accounting, financial reporting, financial close, segregation of duties, financial compliance and fiscal assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage financial resources through accurate accounting, controlled budgeting, authorized procurement, reliable payment and revenue processes, transparent cost management, timely reporting and evidence-based fiscal assurance.**

# 142. Definition of Ready

A financial work item is Ready when:

- Financial objective and transaction context are identified.
- Financial authority and owner are established.
- Budget, account, project, grant or cost-centre requirements are known.
- Applicable accounting, procurement, tax and control requirements are identified.
- Required evidence and reporting outcomes are defined.

---

        ↓
