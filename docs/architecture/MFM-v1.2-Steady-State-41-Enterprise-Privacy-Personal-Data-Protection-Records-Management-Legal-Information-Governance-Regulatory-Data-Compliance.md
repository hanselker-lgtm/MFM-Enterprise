# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the forty-first document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-40 – Enterprise Cybersecurity, Information Security, Identity & Access Management, Security Operations & Security Governance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for privacy governance, personal data protection, records management, legal information governance, regulatory data compliance, information rights, data-subject rights, lawful processing, privacy risk, privacy by design, data protection impact assessment, retention, records classification, legal holds, information requests, regulatory reporting, privacy incidents, records integrity, information access, disclosure, archival, defensible deletion and continual privacy and information-governance improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage personal data, records and regulated information lawfully, transparently, securely and throughout their lifecycle, while maintaining the evidence, accountability and governance necessary to demonstrate compliance and protect individual and organizational rights.**

# 121. Definition of Ready

A privacy, records, legal-information or regulatory-compliance work item is Ready when:

- The processing activity, personal-data issue, rights request, DPIA, record, legal hold, regulatory obligation, disclosure, incident, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, purpose, data, legal basis, classification, risk, security, privacy, retention, regulatory and business requirements are known.
- Assessment, processing, preservation, access, disclosure, evidence, approval, validation and acceptance requirements are defined.

# 123. Final Privacy Governance Principle

> **Privacy must be governed as an enterprise accountability capability with clear ownership, lawful processing, transparency, rights protection, evidence and continuous improvement.**

---

# 124. Final Personal Data Principle

> **Personal data must be collected and processed only for defined, justified purposes and protected throughout its lifecycle.**

---

# 125. Final Rights Principle

> **Applicable data-subject rights must be supported through controlled, traceable and appropriately timed processes.**

---

# 126. Final Privacy-by-Design Principle

> **Privacy must be integrated into architecture, procurement, design, development, implementation, operation, change and retirement.**

---

# 127. Final Records Principle

> **Material records must remain authentic, reliable, integral, usable, accessible and appropriately protected throughout their required lifecycle.**

---

# 128. Final Retention Principle

> **Personal data and records must be retained only for justified periods and securely disposed of when no preservation requirement remains.**

---

# 129. Final Legal Hold Principle

> **Records and information subject to legal, regulatory or investigative preservation requirements must be protected from inappropriate alteration or disposal until authorized release.**

---

# 130. Final Regulatory Principle

> **Regulatory information obligations must have clear ownership, reliable source data, appropriate review, timely execution and defensible evidence.**

---

# 131. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material privacy, records and regulatory controls must be supported by evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

---

# 132. Final Improvement Principle

> **Privacy incidents, rights requests, regulatory findings, records issues, audits, legal matters and operational lessons must continuously improve MFM privacy and information-governance capability.**

---

# 133. Final Integration Principle

> **Privacy, Records and Legal Information Governance must integrate with Enterprise Architecture, Data Management, Cybersecurity, Application Management, Infrastructure, Service Management, Financial Management, Procurement, Supplier Management, People Management, Business Continuity, Risk, Compliance and Project Management.**

---

# 134. Final Steady-State Privacy Principle

> **MFM must manage personal data, records and regulated information lawfully, transparently, securely and throughout their lifecycle, while maintaining the evidence, accountability and governance necessary to demonstrate compliance and protect individual and organizational rights.**
