## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory substantive validation layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-358, determining whether the procedural verification of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-357 is substantively true, effective, complete and supportable in the actual current operating state.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-357 verifies that RG-356 validation was correctly performed and implemented. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-358 validates whether the EA-IMETA-PC-RG-357 verification remains substantively effective and provides reliable assurance.

```text
RG-356 → VALIDATE
RG-357 → VERIFY
RG-358 → VALIDATE
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-357 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-358 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
IS THE EA-IMETA-PC-RG-357 VERIFICATION ACTUALLY EFFECTIVE AND TRUE?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-357 verification SHALL NOT automatically establish substantive current effectiveness.
