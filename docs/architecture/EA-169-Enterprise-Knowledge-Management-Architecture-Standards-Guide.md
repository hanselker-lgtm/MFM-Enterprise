# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise knowledge management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise knowledge management ensures that organizational knowledge is identified, captured, classified, shared, maintained, protected and continuously improved through standardized enterprise processes while preserving governance, organizational learning, operational continuity and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Knowledge Management
- Knowledge Capture
- Knowledge Classification
- Knowledge Sharing
- Knowledge Retention
- Knowledge Governance
- Knowledge Repositories
- Continuous Improvement

All enterprise knowledge management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

## KM-001

Provide standardized enterprise knowledge management.

## KM-002

## KM-003

Support enterprise knowledge sharing.

Ensure complete knowledge traceability.

---

## KM-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Knowledge Management Principles

Enterprise knowledge management shall follow these principles.

- Knowledge by Design
- Governance by Default
- Organizational Learning
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Accountability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Knowledge management implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Knowledge Management Domains

Enterprise knowledge management shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Business Knowledge
- Technical Knowledge
- Operational Knowledge
- Architectural Knowledge
- Compliance Knowledge
- Security Knowledge
- Project Knowledge
- Support Knowledge

Additional knowledge domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Knowledge Management Ownership

Each knowledge management domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- knowledge stewardship

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the knowledge lifecycle.

---

# 7. Knowledge Management Governance

Enterprise knowledge management governance shall define

- knowledge governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- knowledge verification
- governance reporting

Knowledge management governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Knowledge Management Responsibilities

Enterprise knowledge management shall provide controlled management of enterprise knowledge assets.

Knowledge management responsibilities shall

- separate knowledge management from business execution
- coordinate knowledge ownership
- ensure knowledge consistency
- validate knowledge objectives
- preserve knowledge traceability
- support enterprise governance

Knowledge management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Knowledge Capture

Enterprise knowledge management shall implement standardized knowledge capture.

Knowledge capture shall

- identify critical knowledge
- capture business knowledge
- capture technical knowledge
- capture operational knowledge
- preserve capture history
- maintain capture traceability

Knowledge capture shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Knowledge Classification

Enterprise knowledge management shall classify all enterprise knowledge.

Knowledge classifications shall

- define knowledge category
- define confidentiality level
- define accessibility
- define ownership
- preserve classification history
- maintain classification traceability

Knowledge classifications shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Knowledge Sharing

Enterprise knowledge management shall implement standardized knowledge sharing.

Knowledge sharing shall

- distribute approved knowledge
- support collaboration
- ensure controlled accessibility
- preserve sharing history
- maintain sharing traceability
- support organizational learning

Knowledge sharing shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Knowledge Retention

Enterprise knowledge management shall implement standardized knowledge retention.

Knowledge retention shall

- preserve critical organizational knowledge
- support long-term accessibility
- define retention periods
- preserve retention history
- maintain retention traceability
- support operational continuity

Knowledge retention shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Knowledge Management Dependencies

Enterprise knowledge management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- document management systems
- collaboration platforms
- communication platforms
- enterprise repositories
- enterprise infrastructure

Knowledge management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Knowledge Documentation

Each knowledge management domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- knowledge registers
- ownership information
- knowledge classifications
- retention records
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Knowledge Lifecycle

Enterprise knowledge management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Identified
- Captured
- Classified
- Approved
- Published
- Maintained
- Reviewed
- Updated
- Retired
- Archived

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Knowledge Quality Attributes

Enterprise knowledge management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- accessibility
- governance compliance
- knowledge effectiveness

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the knowledge lifecycle.

---

# 17. Knowledge Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized knowledge register.

The knowledge register shall contain

- knowledge identifiers
- ownership assignments
- classifications
- lifecycle status
- publication status
- documentation references
- review schedules
- governance status

The knowledge register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise knowledge.

---

# 18. Knowledge Reviews

Enterprise knowledge management implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- knowledge completeness
- knowledge consistency
- classification accuracy
- publication integrity
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Knowledge Metrics

Enterprise knowledge management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- published knowledge assets
- knowledge assets under review
- overdue knowledge reviews
- knowledge accessibility
- knowledge reuse
- knowledge revisions
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous knowledge improvement.

---

# 20. Knowledge Verification

Enterprise knowledge management implementations shall undergo formal verification before publication and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm knowledge completeness
- verify classifications
- verify ownership
- verify publication readiness
- verify accessibility
- verify documentation completeness
- confirm governance compliance
- approve publication

Knowledge verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Knowledge Improvement

Enterprise knowledge management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve knowledge quality
- improve governance
- improve knowledge accessibility
- improve organizational learning
- improve operational continuity
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise knowledge management implementations shall handle knowledge-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify knowledge capture failures
- classify knowledge classification failures
- classify publication failures
- classify knowledge retention failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Knowledge management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, organizational learning, governance or regulatory obligations.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Knowledge management implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved document management systems
- approved collaboration platforms
- approved communication platforms
- approved enterprise repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Knowledge management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external knowledge management services

Knowledge management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A knowledge management implementation is compliant when

- Knowledge management responsibilities are documented.
- Knowledge capture procedures are implemented.
- Knowledge classifications are documented.
- Knowledge sharing procedures are implemented.
- Knowledge retention policies are documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Knowledge Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Knowledge verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Knowledge Ownership

Knowledge assets shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Missing Knowledge Classification

Knowledge assets shall never be stored without an approved classification.

---

## Missing Knowledge Reviews

Knowledge assets shall never remain active beyond their scheduled review period without documented review or revision.

---

## Knowledge Silos

Critical enterprise knowledge shall never remain isolated within individuals, departments or systems.

---

## Undocumented Knowledge Dependencies

Knowledge management implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Knowledge Management Outside Governance

Knowledge management implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise knowledge management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- knowledge management quality
- knowledge completeness
- classification integrity
- publication readiness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- organizational learning support
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Knowledge Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise knowledge management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise knowledge is identified, captured, classified, shared, retained, governed and continuously improved through standardized lifecycle management while preserving organizational learning, operational continuity, governance, accountability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise knowledge management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
