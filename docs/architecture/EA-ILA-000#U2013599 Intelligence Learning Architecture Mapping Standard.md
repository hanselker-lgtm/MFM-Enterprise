# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Learning Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive learning architecture for enabling continuous learning, adaptation and improvement of Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Learning Architecture Framework shall provide:

- learning principles
- learning governance
- learning architecture
- learning systems
- machine learning integration
- human intelligence learning
- organisational learning
- knowledge feedback loops
- experience management
- adaptive learning systems
- learning measurement
- learning maturity
- learning evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities continuously improve through structured learning mechanisms.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Learning Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture learning domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Learning Model
- Learning Governance
- Learning Architecture
- Learning Systems
- Machine Learning Integration
- Human Intelligence Learning
- Organisational Learning
- Knowledge Feedback Loops
- Experience Management
- Adaptive Learning Systems
- Learning Metrics
- Learning Maturity
- Learning Roadmaps
- Learning Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- adaptive enterprises
- learning ecosystems
- continuously improving Intelligence environments

---

# 3. Intelligence Learning Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Learning Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Continuous Learning

Enterprise Intelligence shall continuously learn from experience and information.

---

## Principle 2 – Feedback-Driven Improvement

Learning shall be supported by structured feedback mechanisms.

---

## Principle 3 – Knowledge-Based Evolution

Learning shall transform information and experience into knowledge.

---

## Principle 4 – Adaptive Capability Development

Learning shall enable adaptation to changing requirements.

---

## Principle 5 – Human and Machine Collaboration

Learning shall integrate human and artificial intelligence capabilities.

---

## Principle 6 – Measurable Learning Outcomes

Learning activities shall produce measurable improvements.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Learning

Learning activities shall follow governance, security and ethical requirements.
