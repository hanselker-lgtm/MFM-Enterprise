# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eighty-sixth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-85 – Enterprise Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Operational Resilience, Crisis Management & Recovery Governance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for enterprise risk management, risk governance, risk strategy, risk appetite, risk taxonomy, risk ownership, risk identification, risk assessment, risk analysis, risk treatment, risk acceptance, risk transfer, risk avoidance, risk mitigation, technology risk, information risk, cyber risk coordination, operational risk, service risk, project risk coordination, supplier risk coordination, financial and commercial risk coordination, compliance risk, legal risk, privacy risk coordination, resilience risk coordination, risk aggregation, risk scenarios, emerging risk, risk monitoring, risk reporting, risk escalation, risk assurance, risk findings, risk exceptions, risk remediation, risk metrics, risk dashboards, risk maturity and continual enterprise risk improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must identify, assess, treat, accept, monitor and report enterprise risks in a consistent and risk-based manner so that material risks remain within approved appetite and tolerance.**

# 3. Enterprise Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain enterprise risk within approved appetite and tolerance while enabling informed business decisions.**

# 4. Risk Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish consistent accountability, decision rights, escalation and oversight for enterprise risk.**

# 5. Risk Assessment Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide a consistent understanding of likelihood, impact, exposure, controls and residual risk.**

# 6. Risk Treatment Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Reduce unacceptable risk through proportionate and effective treatment.**

# 7. Risk Appetite Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Define the level and nature of risk the enterprise is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives.**

# 8. Risk Reporting Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide decision-makers with timely, reliable and actionable information about material risk.**

# 99. Definition of Ready

A risk, risk assessment, treatment plan, risk acceptance, KRI, risk scenario, emerging risk, exception, remediation or assurance review is Ready when context, owner, scope, risk statement, dependencies, assessment criteria, appetite, decision authority, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.

# 101. Final Enterprise Risk Principle

> **MFM must identify, assess, treat, accept, monitor and report enterprise risks in a consistent and risk-based manner so that material risks remain within approved appetite and tolerance.**

# 102. Final Ownership Principle

> **Every material risk must have an accountable owner with responsibility for understanding, treating, monitoring and escalating the risk.**

# 103. Final Appetite Principle

> **Risk decisions must remain aligned with approved risk appetite and tolerance, with material breaches escalated promptly.**

# 104. Final Assessment Principle

> **Risk assessment must consider likelihood, impact, controls, control effectiveness and residual exposure using consistent criteria.**

# 105. Final Treatment Principle

> **Risk treatment must reduce unacceptable exposure through proportionate, owned, time-bound and evidence-based actions.**

# 106. Final Acceptance Principle

> **Residual risk must only be accepted by appropriately authorized decision-makers and should be reviewed when circumstances change.**

# 107. Final Monitoring Principle

> **Key risk indicators, thresholds, events and trends must provide timely warning of material changes in risk exposure.**

# 108. Final Scenario Principle

> **Material risks must be considered individually and collectively, including concentration, correlation, common-cause and severe-but-plausible scenarios.**

# 109. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material risk controls, assessments and decisions must be supported by reliable evidence and periodically challenged or assured.**

# 110. Final Improvement Principle

> **Risk events, incidents, assessments, findings, scenarios and emerging signals must continuously improve MFM's enterprise risk capability.**

# 111. Final Integration Principle

> **Enterprise Risk Management must integrate with Cybersecurity, Data, Privacy, Identity, Enterprise Architecture, Applications, Infrastructure, Service Management, Suppliers, Finance, Projects, Business Continuity, Compliance and Legal functions.**

# 112. Final Steady-State Risk Principle

> **MFM must identify, assess, treat, accept, monitor and report enterprise risks in a consistent and risk-based manner so that material risks remain within approved appetite and tolerance.**
