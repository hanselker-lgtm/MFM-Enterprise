## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory procedural verification layer for RG-246, determining whether the substantive validation of RG-245 verification was correctly performed, evidenced, authorized, decided, recorded and implemented.

## Core Principle

RG-246 determines whether the RG-245 verification is substantively effective. RG-247 verifies that RG-246's validation of that verification was correctly performed and implemented.

```text
RG-244 → VALIDATE
RG-245 → VERIFY
RG-246 → VALIDATE
RG-247 → VERIFY
```

```text
RG-246 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
RG-247 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
WAS RG-246 CORRECTLY PERFORMED?
```

A positive RG-246 validation SHALL NOT automatically prove that RG-246 was correctly verified.
