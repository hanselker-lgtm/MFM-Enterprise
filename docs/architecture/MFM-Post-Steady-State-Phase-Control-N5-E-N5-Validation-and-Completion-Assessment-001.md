# 1. Purpose

N5-E is the controlled validation and completion assessment for the N5 Governance & Control Architecture workstream.

Its purpose is to:

```text
Validate N5 Scope
Validate Governance Architecture
Validate Authority / Accountability
Validate Decision Rights
Validate Policy / Standards / Controls
Validate Risk / Compliance / Assurance
Validate Change Governance
Consolidate Findings
Validate Evidence Boundaries
Establish Completion Recommendation
Prepare Authority Decision
```

The original N5 work-package charter defines N5-E as the work package for validation, finding consolidation, completion recommendation and preparation of the authority decision. fileciteturn14file9L1599-L1609

# 27. N5C-1 — COMPLETE

May be recommended only where:

```text
All Required Scope Assessed
AND
Governance Model Coherent
AND
Material Findings Controlled
AND
Evidence Boundaries Preserved
AND
No Blocking Conditions Remain
AND
Completion Authority Can Decide
```

---

# 28. N5C-2 — COMPLETE WITH CONDITIONS

May be recommended where:

```text
Core N5 Scope Is Complete
AND
No Material Governance Blocker Prevents Closure
AND
Residual Conditions Are Explicit
AND
Conditions Have Owners
AND
Conditions Have Review / Follow-Up
```

Conditions shall not be silently converted into closure.

---

# 29. N5C-3 — NOT COMPLETE

May be recommended where:

```text
Material Scope Is Unassessed
OR
Governance Model Is Materially Incoherent
OR
Blocking Findings Remain
OR
Required Authority Decision Cannot Be Prepared
OR
Evidence Boundary Cannot Be Preserved
```

---

# 30. N5C-4 — COMPLETE PENDING EVIDENCE

May be recommended where:

```text
Architecture / Governance Model Is Complete
BUT
Specific Evidence Required for Final Confirmation Is Missing
```

This state shall preserve the distinction:

```text
MISSING EVIDENCE
≠
FAILED CAPABILITY
```

```text

≠
```
