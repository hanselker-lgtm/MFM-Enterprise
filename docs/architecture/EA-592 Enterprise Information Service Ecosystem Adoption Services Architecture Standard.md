# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Adoption Services Architecture supporting successful organizational, operational and technological adoption of Enterprise capabilities, services and solutions across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Adoption Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem adoption services
- support adoption governance
- support adoption strategy
- support adoption objectives
- support adoption planning
- support organizational adoption
- support user adoption
- support business adoption
- support operational adoption
- support service adoption
- support capability adoption
- support technology adoption
- support application adoption
- support infrastructure adoption
- support data adoption
- support information adoption
- support security adoption
- support stakeholder adoption
- support change adoption
- support adoption measurement
- support adoption improvement
- support adoption maturity development
- support continuous adoption capability development

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Adoption Services shall provide trusted and reusable adoption capabilities enabling Enterprise stakeholders to effectively utilize and realize value from Enterprise capabilities and services.
