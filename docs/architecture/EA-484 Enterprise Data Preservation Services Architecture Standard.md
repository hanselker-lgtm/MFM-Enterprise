This standard defines the Enterprise Data Preservation Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Archiving Services principles are inherited from EA-483.

All Enterprise Data Preservation Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Preservation Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed preservation of Enterprise information across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Preservation Services shall

- provide centralized preservation management
- standardize preservation processes
- ensure long-term information availability
- maintain data authenticity
- maintain data integrity
- preserve historical information value
- support regulatory requirements
- support business continuity
- strengthen information lifecycle governance
- support privacy requirements
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure preservation traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Preservation Services shall provide trusted and reusable preservation capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Data Preservation Services Principles

Enterprise Data Preservation Services shall follow these principles.

## Preservation by Design

Preservation capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Long-Term Availability

Enterprise information requiring preservation shall remain accessible, understandable and usable throughout approved preservation periods.

---

## Authenticity Preservation

Preserved information shall maintain evidence of origin, ownership, context and trustworthiness.

---

## Integrity Preservation

Preserved information shall remain complete, accurate and protected against unauthorized alteration.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Preservation Services shall remain independent of vendors, preservation platforms, storage providers, cloud providers and implementation technologies.
