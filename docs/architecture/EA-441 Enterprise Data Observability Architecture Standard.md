# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Observability Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Observability principles are inherited from EA-411.

All Enterprise Data Observability implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Observability Architecture ensuring continuous monitoring, measurement, analysis and optimization of Enterprise information, data pipelines and data services throughout the complete information lifecycle.

Enterprise Data Observability shall

- continuously monitor Enterprise information
- improve operational visibility
- detect anomalies proactively
- improve data quality
- monitor information freshness
- improve service reliability
- strengthen governance
- support regulatory compliance
- enable predictive analytics
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Observability shall provide a standardized architectural framework for ensuring trusted, healthy and continuously monitored Enterprise information.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise databases
- master data
- transactional systems
- data warehouses
- lakehouses
- data pipelines
- integration platforms
- APIs
- event streams
- analytical platforms

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, cloud provider, storage technology or infrastructure platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Data Observability Principles

Enterprise Data Observability shall be governed by the following principles.

## Observability by Design

Every Enterprise information asset shall be observable throughout its complete lifecycle.

---

## Continuous Monitoring

Enterprise information shall be continuously monitored for health, availability, quality and operational performance.

---

## Proactive Detection

Operational anomalies shall be detected before they affect business operations.

---

## Metadata Driven

Observability shall utilize standardized Enterprise metadata for monitoring, diagnostics and analytics.

---

## Automation First

Observability processes shall be automated wherever technically feasible.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Observability Architecture shall remain independent of vendors, monitoring platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Observability Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for continuous monitoring, analysis and optimization of Enterprise information, data services and operational health across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating telemetry collection, health monitoring, anomaly detection, analytics, dashboards, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables proactive operations, improved reliability, regulatory compliance and data-driven operational excellence.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Observability initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing observability capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Data Observability Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted operational monitoring, autonomous anomaly detection, predictive service health analytics, self-healing monitoring platforms, digital twin-based observability, semantic telemetry correlation, autonomous optimization engines and post-quantum protected monitoring infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Observability continues to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven operational visibility across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
