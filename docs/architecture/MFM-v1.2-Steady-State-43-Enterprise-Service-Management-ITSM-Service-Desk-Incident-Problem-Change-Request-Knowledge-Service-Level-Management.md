# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the forty-third document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-42 – Enterprise Risk Management, Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Crisis Management & Organizational Resilience.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for service management, IT service management, service desk operations, incident management, major incident management, problem management, change enablement, service requests, service configuration, service level management, service availability, service continuity, service capacity, knowledge management, service catalogue management, service reporting, service improvement, user experience, service ownership and continual service improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage enterprise services through a consistent, measurable and customer-focused service-management model that maintains service quality, resolves disruption efficiently, controls change, manages demand and continuously improves service outcomes.**

# 125. Definition of Ready

A service, incident, problem, change, request, configuration, knowledge, SLA, availability, capacity, continuity, supplier or improvement work item is Ready when:

- The service requirement, user need, disruption, problem, change, request, configuration item, knowledge requirement, service-level requirement, recovery requirement or improvement opportunity is clearly identified.
- Ownership, impact, priority, dependencies, security, privacy, continuity, business and operational requirements are known.
- Assessment, approval, implementation, validation, evidence and acceptance requirements are defined.

---
