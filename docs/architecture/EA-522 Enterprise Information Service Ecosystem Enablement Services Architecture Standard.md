# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Enablement Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed capabilities that enable digital ecosystem participation, partner connectivity, ecosystem integration, collaboration platforms and external value network execution across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Enablement Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem enablement capabilities
- support ecosystem platform capabilities
- support ecosystem onboarding
- support partner connectivity
- support supplier connectivity
- support ecosystem integration
- support API ecosystem enablement
- support ecosystem collaboration platforms
- support digital marketplace capabilities
- support ecosystem identity federation
- support ecosystem data exchange
- support ecosystem automation
- support ecosystem service connectivity
- support ecosystem technology enablement
- improve ecosystem execution capability
- improve ecosystem scalability
- improve ecosystem interoperability
- improve ecosystem transparency
- support secure ecosystem collaboration
- support continuous ecosystem evolution

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Enablement Services shall provide trusted and reusable capabilities enabling Enterprise ecosystems.

# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Enablement Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Enablement Services shall follow these principles.

## Enable Ecosystem Participation

Enterprise capabilities shall provide secure and standardized mechanisms enabling participation in approved ecosystems.

---

## Secure Connectivity by Design

All ecosystem connectivity shall implement security, identity and authorization requirements from the beginning.

---

## Interoperability First

Ecosystem enablement capabilities shall support open standards, reusable interfaces and scalable integration patterns.

---

## Controlled External Access

External ecosystem participants shall receive controlled and governed access to Enterprise capabilities.

---

## Reusable Enablement Capabilities

Ecosystem enablement services shall be designed for reuse across Business and Technology Domains.
