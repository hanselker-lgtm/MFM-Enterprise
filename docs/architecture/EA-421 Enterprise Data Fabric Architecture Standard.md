# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Fabric Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Infrastructure Architecture principles are inherited from EA-320.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Digital Trust principles are inherited from EA-420.

All Enterprise Data Fabric implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Fabric Architecture governing intelligent, metadata-driven, distributed and policy-based access to Enterprise data across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Data Fabric Architecture shall

- unify Enterprise data
- eliminate unnecessary data silos
- support distributed data access
- enable metadata-driven automation
- simplify data integration
- improve data discoverability
- strengthen governance
- support artificial intelligence
- increase operational agility
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Fabric shall function as a strategic Enterprise capability supporting all Business and Technology Domains.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- Enterprise data platforms
- distributed data environments
- cloud data platforms
- hybrid data environments
- on-premises data platforms
- metadata platforms
- semantic layers
- data virtualization
- data federation
- enterprise analytics

The standard applies regardless of technology platform, cloud provider or deployment model.

---

# 3. Enterprise Data Fabric Principles

Enterprise Data Fabric shall be governed by the following principles.

## Metadata First

Metadata shall drive data discovery, automation, governance and lifecycle management.

---

## Distributed Data

Enterprise data shall remain distributed while appearing logically unified.

---

## Data as a Product

Enterprise data shall be managed as reusable products supporting multiple business capabilities.

---

## Policy Driven

Access, governance and lifecycle management shall be governed through centrally managed policies.

---

## Automation by Default

Automation shall replace manual operational activities wherever practical.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Fabric Architecture shall remain independent of specific vendors and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Fabric Architecture establishes the standardized foundation for intelligent, metadata-driven and policy-based Enterprise data management across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

By integrating Enterprise Metadata, Data Governance, Knowledge Graphs, Artificial Intelligence, Event Streaming, Data Virtualization, Federation, Observability and Automation, the Enterprise provides a unified data ecosystem that enables trusted information access, improved decision-making and accelerated digital transformation.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Fabric initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing Enterprise data platforms.
