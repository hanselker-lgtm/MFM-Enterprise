# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational sustainability architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities can be developed, operated and maintained as a long-term sustainable enterprise capability.

The Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- sustainability operating principles
- sustainable strategy frameworks
- resource sustainability models
- technology sustainability structures
- operational sustainability models
- environmental sustainability structures
- economic sustainability models
- social sustainability structures
- capability sustainability models
- lifecycle sustainability models
- sustainability monitoring structures
- sustainability automation structures
- sustainability evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for responsible, balanced and continuously improving Intelligence operations.

# 3. Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Sustainability by Design

Sustainability shall be integrated into Intelligence capabilities from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Resource Usage

Resources shall be managed efficiently and responsibly.

---

## Principle 3 – Long-Term Value Creation

Intelligence capabilities shall create sustainable value over time.

---

## Principle 4 – Lifecycle Sustainability

Sustainability shall be maintained throughout the entire capability lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 – Balanced Development

Technology, people, processes and resources shall develop in balance.

---

## Principle 6 – Environmental Responsibility

Environmental impact shall be considered in Intelligence development.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Sustainability Evolution

Sustainability capability shall continuously improve.
