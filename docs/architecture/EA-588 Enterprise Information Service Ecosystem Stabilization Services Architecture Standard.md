The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Stabilization Services Architecture supporting the establishment, maintenance and verification of stable Enterprise operational conditions following restoration, response activities or significant changes across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

- provide standardized ecosystem stabilization services
- support stabilization governance
