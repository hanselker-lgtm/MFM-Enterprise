# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Certificate Management Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Key Management Services principles are inherited from EA-477.

Enterprise Secrets Management Services principles are inherited from EA-476.

Enterprise Credential Management Services principles are inherited from EA-475.

Enterprise Authentication Services principles are inherited from EA-473.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Certificate Management Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Certificate Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management of digital certificates across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Certificate Management Services shall

- provide centralized certificate management
- standardize certificate lifecycle management
- strengthen digital trust
- reduce certificate-related risks
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure certificate traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Certificate Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable certificate management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise certificate management services
- certificate lifecycle management
- certificate authority integration
- certificate issuance
- certificate validation
- certificate distribution
- certificate renewal
- certificate revocation
- Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)
- trust management
- digital identity certificates
- application certificates
- service certificates
- workload certificates

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Certificate Management Services Principles

Enterprise Certificate Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Certificate Security by Design

Certificate management capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every certificate shall be securely issued, stored, validated, distributed, monitored and retired according to approved Enterprise security requirements.

---

## Certificate Trust Governance

Digital certificates shall be managed through approved trust models, certificate authorities and governance processes.

---

## Certificate Lifecycle Governance

Certificates shall be continuously managed throughout issuance, activation, usage, renewal, suspension, revocation and retirement.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Certificate Management Services shall remain independent of vendors, certificate platforms, certificate authorities, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Certificate Management Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed certificate management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating certificate lifecycle management, certificate authority integration, certificate issuance, certificate validation, certificate distribution, certificate renewal, certificate revocation, Public Key Infrastructure, trust management, digital identity certificates, application certificates, service certificates, workload certificates, key management integration, secrets management integration, credential management integration, identity integration, authentication integration, governance, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, scalable and policy-driven digital certificate management while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Certificate Management Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise certificate management capabilities across the Enterprise.
