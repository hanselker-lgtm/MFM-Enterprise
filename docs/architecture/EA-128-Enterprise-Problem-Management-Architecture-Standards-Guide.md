# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing problem management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Problem management ensures that underlying causes of incidents are systematically identified, analyzed, documented and eliminated in order to improve operational stability and reduce incident recurrence while maintaining compliance with Enterprise Architecture principles.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Problem Management
- Problem Identification
- Root Cause Analysis
- Problem Classification
- Known Error Management
- Preventive Actions
- Problem Governance
- Problem Lifecycle
- Compliance

All enterprise problem management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## PRB-001

Provide standardized problem management processes.

---

## PRB-002

Support systematic identification of root causes.

---

## PRB-003

Reduce recurring incidents through preventive actions.

---

## PRB-004

Improve operational stability and service quality.

---

## PRB-005

Maintain full compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 4. Problem Management Principles

Enterprise problem management shall follow these principles.

- Root Cause Before Workaround
- Prevention by Default
- Knowledge Sharing
- Risk-Based Prioritization
- Traceability by Design
- Auditability by Design
- Continuous Improvement
- Enterprise Alignment

Problem management shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Problem Categories

Enterprise problems shall be organized into standardized categories.

Categories shall include

- Application Problems
- Infrastructure Problems
- Security Problems
- Configuration Problems
- Database Problems
- Integration Problems
- Performance Problems
- Operational Problems

Additional problem categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Problem Ownership

Each enterprise problem domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- investigation responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the problem lifecycle.

---

# 7. Problem Governance

Enterprise problem governance shall define

- problem governance
- investigation governance
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- problem approval
- governance reporting

Problem governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Problem Responsibilities

Enterprise problem management shall provide controlled coordination of problem investigations.

Problem responsibilities shall

- separate problem management from incident response
- coordinate stakeholder involvement
- ensure investigation consistency
- validate corrective actions
- preserve problem traceability
- support operational stability

Problem management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Problem Identification

Enterprise problems shall be identified using standardized mechanisms.

Problem identification shall

- analyze recurring incidents
- identify operational trends
- identify systemic weaknesses
- identify infrastructure deficiencies
- identify application defects
- preserve investigation traceability

Problem identification shall minimize recurrence of operational incidents.

---

# 10. Root Cause Analysis

Enterprise problems shall undergo standardized root cause analysis.

Root cause analysis shall

- identify technical causes
- identify process deficiencies
- identify organizational factors
- determine contributing factors
- document evidence
- recommend corrective actions

Root cause analysis shall remain objective and evidence-based.

---

# 11. Known Error Management

Enterprise problem management shall maintain a known error repository.

Known error management shall

- document known errors
- document temporary workarounds
- document permanent resolutions
- support operational troubleshooting
- maintain version history
- preserve auditability

Known error records shall remain available throughout the problem lifecycle.

---

# 12. Preventive Actions

Enterprise problem management shall implement preventive actions.

Preventive actions shall

- eliminate root causes
- reduce incident recurrence
- improve operational reliability
- strengthen architectural resilience
- validate corrective effectiveness
- support continuous improvement

Preventive actions shall be verified after implementation.

---

# 13. Problem Dependencies

Enterprise problem management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- incident management
- change management
- release management
- configuration management
- observability services
- enterprise governance

Problem management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Problem Documentation

Each enterprise problem shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- problem description
- investigation results
- root cause analysis
- corrective actions
- preventive actions
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Problem Lifecycle

Enterprise problems shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Identified
- Logged
- Classified
- Assigned
- Investigated
- Root Cause Identified
- Corrective Action Implemented
- Verified
- Reviewed
- Closed

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Problem Quality Attributes

Enterprise problem management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- reliability
- consistency
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability
- repeatability
- effectiveness
- predictability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the problem lifecycle.

---

# 17. Problem Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized problem registry.

The registry shall contain

- problem identifiers
- ownership assignments
- problem categories
- lifecycle status
- dependency information
- investigation history
- documentation references
- governance status

The problem registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise problem management.

---

# 18. Problem Reviews

Enterprise problems shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- investigation quality
- root cause analysis quality
- corrective action effectiveness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment
- operational readiness

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Problem Metrics

Enterprise problem management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- recurring incident reduction
- root cause identification time
- corrective action completion rate
- preventive action effectiveness
- known error resolution rate
- audit findings
- operational stability
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous problem improvement.

---

# 20. Problem Verification

Enterprise problems shall undergo formal verification before closure.

Verification shall

- confirm root cause elimination
- validate corrective actions
- validate preventive actions
- confirm operational stability
- verify documentation completeness
- approve problem closure

Problem verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Problem Improvement

Enterprise problem management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve investigation quality
- improve root cause analysis
- reduce recurring incidents
- strengthen preventive actions
- improve knowledge sharing
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise problem management shall handle problem management exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify investigation failures
- classify root cause analysis failures
- classify corrective action failures
- classify verification failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Problem management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational stability or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Problem management implementations may depend upon

- approved incident management systems
- approved change management systems
- approved release management systems
- approved observability platforms
- approved knowledge management systems
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Problem management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external problem management services

Problem management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A problem management implementation is compliant when

- Problem responsibilities are documented.
- Problem identification follows enterprise standards.
- Root cause analysis has been completed.
- Known error management is implemented.
- Preventive actions are documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Problem Registry is updated.
- Problem verification has been completed.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Audit logging is enabled where applicable.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Superficial Root Cause Analysis

Problems shall never be closed based solely on symptoms without identifying the underlying cause.

---

## Repeated Temporary Workarounds

Temporary workarounds shall never become permanent solutions without documented approval and corrective action planning.

---

## Missing Preventive Actions

Recurring problems shall never be closed without documented preventive actions where prevention is technically feasible.

---

## Undocumented Known Errors

Known errors shall never remain undocumented if they can affect operational stability or future incident response.

---

## Incomplete Investigation

Problem investigations shall never be considered complete without documented evidence supporting the identified root cause.

---

## Missing Knowledge Sharing

Resolved problems shall never be closed without updating the enterprise knowledge base where applicable.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise problem management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- investigation quality
- root cause analysis quality
- corrective action effectiveness
- preventive action implementation
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational readiness
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Problem Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing problem management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise problems are systematically investigated, their root causes eliminated, preventive actions implemented and organizational knowledge continuously improved while maintaining Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise problem management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
