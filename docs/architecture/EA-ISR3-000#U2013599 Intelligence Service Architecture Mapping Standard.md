# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Service Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities are transformed into structured, accessible and managed services.

The Intelligence Service Architecture Framework shall provide:

- service architecture principles
- intelligence service frameworks
- service capability models
- service design structures
- service delivery models
- service management frameworks
- service integration models
- service quality models
- service governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for delivering advanced Intelligence capabilities through reliable, scalable and continuously improving service ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Service Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Service Model
- Intelligence Service Capability Architecture
- Intelligence Service Design Architecture
- Intelligence Service Delivery Architecture
- Intelligence Service Management Architecture
- Intelligence Service Integration Architecture
- Intelligence Service Quality Architecture
- Intelligence Service Lifecycle Architecture
- Service Governance Model
- Service Evolution Framework

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

# 3. Intelligence Service Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Service Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Service Value

Intelligence services shall create measurable value for users and stakeholders.

---

## Principle 2 – Service Availability

Intelligence services shall be available according to defined requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Service Quality

Services shall maintain defined quality standards.

---

## Principle 4 – Service Integration

Services shall integrate effectively across enterprise capabilities.

---

## Principle 5 – Service Lifecycle Management

Services shall be managed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Service Improvement

Services shall continuously improve through feedback and learning.

---

## Principle 7 – Service Evolution

Service capability shall continuously develop according to future requirements.
