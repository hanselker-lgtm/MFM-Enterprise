# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Workflow Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Business Process Architecture principles are inherited from EA-345.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Workflow Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Workflow Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and scalable workflow capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Workflow Services shall

- provide standardized workflow services
- support enterprise process orchestration
- enable workflow automation
- improve operational efficiency
- strengthen interoperability
- support reusable workflow capabilities
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- enable continuous optimization
- improve business agility

Enterprise Workflow Services shall provide trusted and reusable workflow capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- workflow services
- business workflows
- technical workflows
- orchestration services
- approval workflows
- task management services
- process automation platforms
- cloud services
- hybrid environments
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, workflow technology or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Workflow Services Principles

Enterprise Workflow Services shall follow these principles.

## Process First

Enterprise Workflow Services shall automate and orchestrate business and technical processes using standardized workflow capabilities.

---

## Secure by Default

Every workflow execution shall be authenticated, authorized, protected and continuously monitored.

---

## Standardized Workflow Services

Enterprise Workflow Services shall expose standardized workflow interfaces supporting interoperability throughout the Enterprise.

---

## Metadata Driven Workflows

Workflow services shall leverage Enterprise metadata for orchestration, routing, task assignment, lifecycle management and governance.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Workflow Services shall remain independent of vendors, workflow engines, cloud providers and infrastructure technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Workflow Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed workflow capabilities across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating workflow orchestration, business process services, task management, rule execution, approval workflows, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled workflow execution while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Workflow Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise workflow capabilities across the Enterprise.
