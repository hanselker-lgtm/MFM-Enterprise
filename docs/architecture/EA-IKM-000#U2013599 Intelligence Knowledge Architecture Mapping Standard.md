# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive knowledge architecture for creating, managing, sharing and evolving Enterprise Intelligence knowledge capabilities.

The Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence knowledge principles
- knowledge governance
- knowledge architecture
- knowledge domains
- knowledge graph architecture
- semantic architecture
- ontology architecture
- knowledge representation
- knowledge lifecycle management
- knowledge acquisition
- knowledge sharing
- knowledge discovery
- knowledge reasoning
- knowledge security
- knowledge analytics
- knowledge evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are supported by structured, meaningful and reusable knowledge resources.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture knowledge domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Knowledge Model
- Knowledge Governance
- Knowledge Architecture
- Knowledge Domains
- Knowledge Graph Architecture
- Semantic Architecture
- Ontology Architecture
- Knowledge Representation
- Knowledge Lifecycle Management
- Knowledge Acquisition
- Knowledge Sharing
- Knowledge Discovery

- Knowledge Security

- Knowledge Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- intelligent operating environments
- future adaptive enterprises

# 3. Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Knowledge Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Knowledge as a Strategic Asset

Knowledge shall be managed as a strategic Enterprise capability.

---

## Principle 2 – Knowledge Quality

Knowledge shall be accurate, relevant and valuable.

---

## Principle 3 – Semantic Understanding

Knowledge shall maintain meaningful relationships and context.

---

## Principle 4 – Knowledge Reuse

Knowledge shall be reusable across Enterprise capabilities.

---

## Principle 5 – Knowledge Accessibility

Authorised users and intelligent systems shall access relevant knowledge.

---

## Principle 6 – Knowledge Security

Knowledge assets shall be protected throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Knowledge Evolution

Knowledge capability shall continuously improve and adapt.
