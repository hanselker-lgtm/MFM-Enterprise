# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Evolution Transformation Services Architecture supporting controlled execution of strategic Enterprise transformations resulting from approved evolution decisions across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Evolution Transformation Services shall

- provide standardized evolution transformation services
- support transformation governance
- support transformation frameworks
- support transformation strategies
- support transformation planning
- support transformation execution
- support transformation coordination
- support transformation orchestration
- support transformation measurement
- support transformation assurance
- support transformation validation
- support transformation acceptance
- support transformation adoption
- support transformation realization
- support transformation preservation
- support continuous transformation improvement

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Evolution Transformation Services shall provide trusted and reusable transformation capabilities enabling Enterprise stakeholders to convert strategic evolution decisions into measurable Enterprise change.
