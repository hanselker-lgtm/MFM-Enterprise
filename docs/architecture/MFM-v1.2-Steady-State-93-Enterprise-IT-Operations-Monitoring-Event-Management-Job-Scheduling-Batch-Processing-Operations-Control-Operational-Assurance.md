# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the ninety-third document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-92 – Enterprise Service Management, IT Service Governance, Service Catalog, SLA, Incident, Request, Problem & Service Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for IT operations governance, operations strategy, operations ownership, operational control, infrastructure operations, platform operations, application operations, cloud operations, monitoring, observability, event management, alert management, operational dashboards, job scheduling, batch processing, operational procedures, runbooks, operational handover, operations readiness, operational support, maintenance coordination, backup and recovery operations, capacity coordination, availability coordination, operational security coordination, supplier operations coordination, operational reporting, operational assurance, operational findings, operational exceptions, operational remediation, operational metrics, operational maturity and continual enterprise IT operations improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must operate technology services through controlled, observable, repeatable and accountable operational processes that maintain availability, performance, security, resilience and service quality.**

# 3. IT Operations Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish clear accountability, control and operational discipline for technology services and platforms.**

# 4. Operations Strategy Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Align day-to-day technology operations with service commitments, business priorities, resilience requirements, security requirements and enterprise architecture.**

# 5. Monitoring Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide timely and reliable visibility into service, system, infrastructure, application, cloud and security-relevant operational conditions.**

# 6. Event Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Detect, classify, correlate and respond to operational events before they create unacceptable service impact where possible.**

# 7. Operations Control Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure operational activities are performed through approved, repeatable and traceable procedures.**

# 8. Operational Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide evidence that operational processes, controls, monitoring, jobs, procedures and technology environments operate according to defined requirements.**

An operational service, monitoring rule, event process, alert, job, batch process, runbook, operational handover, backup process, recovery procedure, exception, remediation or assurance review is Ready when scope, owner, dependencies, criticality, operating requirements, escalation, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.

# 108. Final Operations Governance Principle

> **MFM must operate technology services through controlled, observable, repeatable and accountable operational processes that maintain availability, performance, security, resilience and service quality.**

# 109. Final Monitoring Principle

> **Critical services and technology components must have sufficient monitoring and observability to detect material degradation, failure and risk.**

# 110. Final Event Principle

> **Operational events and alerts must be actionable, owned, prioritized and integrated with incident and problem management where appropriate.**

# 111. Final Automation Principle

> **Operational automation must improve consistency, reliability, speed and traceability without bypassing required governance and controls.**

# 112. Final Job Principle

> **Material scheduled jobs and batch processes must have accountable ownership, controlled dependencies, defined failure handling and reliable completion evidence.**

# 113. Final Recovery Principle

> **Backup and recovery operations must be monitored, validated and periodically tested according to service criticality and recovery requirements.**

# 114. Final Readiness Principle

> **Services must demonstrate appropriate operational readiness before entering steady-state production operation.**

# 115. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material operational processes and controls must be periodically validated through reliable evidence and risk-based assurance.**

# 116. Final Improvement Principle

> **Operational events, failures, monitoring gaps, job results, recovery tests and assurance findings must continuously improve MFM's operational capability and resilience.**
