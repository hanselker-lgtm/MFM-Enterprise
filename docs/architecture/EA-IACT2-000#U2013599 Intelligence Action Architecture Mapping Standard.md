# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Action Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence decisions are transformed into controlled, coordinated and measurable actions.

The Intelligence Action Architecture Framework shall provide:

- action architecture principles
- intelligence action frameworks
- action capability models
- action planning structures
- action coordination models
- action execution models
- autonomous action models
- human-AI collaboration models
- action monitoring structures
- action optimisation frameworks
- action governance model
- action assurance framework
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for advanced Intelligence action capabilities through controlled, explainable and continuously improving action ecosystems.

# 3. Intelligence Action Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Action Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Controlled Action

Actions shall be performed within defined authority and governance boundaries.

---

## Principle 2 – Decision Alignment

Actions shall directly support approved decisions.

---

## Principle 3 – Action Traceability

Actions shall be traceable from initiation to outcome.

---

## Principle 4 – Operational Effectiveness

Actions shall create measurable value.

---

## Principle 5 – Human Responsibility

Human accountability shall remain defined where required.

---

## Principle 6 – Adaptive Action Capability

Action capability shall improve through learning and experience.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Action Evolution

Action capability shall continuously develop.
