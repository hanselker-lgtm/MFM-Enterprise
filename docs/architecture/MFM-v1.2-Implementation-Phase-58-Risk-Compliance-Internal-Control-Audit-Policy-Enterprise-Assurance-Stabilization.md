# 1. Purpose

This document defines the fifty-eighth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-57 – Procurement, Supplier, Contract, Vendor Lifecycle, Third-Party Risk & Supply-Chain Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise risk, compliance, internal control, audit, policy and assurance capability covering risk identification, risk appetite, compliance obligations, control ownership, policy management, audit planning, evidence, findings, corrective actions, compliance monitoring and enterprise assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage risk, compliance and assurance through a controlled lifecycle connecting obligations, risks, controls, policies, evidence, testing, findings, remediation, verification and management oversight.**
