# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to define the Enterprise principles governing analytics throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Analytics Architecture shall

- provide standardized analytics capabilities
- support operational, tactical and strategic reporting
- enable consistent Business Intelligence
- support evidence-based decision making
- provide reusable analytical services
- establish Enterprise governance
- remain technology independent

The Enterprise Analytics Architecture shall ensure that Enterprise analytics are accurate, explainable, governed and based upon authoritative information sources.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every Enterprise Analytics implementation throughout the Enterprise Platform.

It governs

- reporting
- Business Intelligence
- dashboards
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- visualization
- analytical services
- data marts
- self-service analytics
- governance
- lifecycle management

The standard applies independently of reporting platforms, BI products, visualization tools and infrastructure technologies.

---

# 3. Enterprise Analytics Definition

Enterprise Analytics is the controlled collection, transformation, analysis and presentation of Enterprise information to support operational insight, tactical management and strategic decision making.

Enterprise Analytics encompasses

- reporting
- Business Intelligence
- dashboards
- KPI management
- trend analysis
- performance analysis
- visual analytics
- self-service analytics

Enterprise Analytics shall operate using authoritative Enterprise information under approved governance.

---

# 4. Enterprise Analytics Objectives

The Enterprise Analytics Architecture shall

- improve business visibility
- improve operational transparency
- support decision quality
- enable reusable analytics
- support governance
- preserve semantic consistency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Analytics capabilities shall be available as reusable Enterprise services.

The Enterprise Analytics Architecture is responsible for

- reporting services

# 19. Enterprise Analytics Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Multiple KPI Definitions

The same KPI shall never have multiple conflicting definitions across the Enterprise.

The centralized KPI repository shall remain authoritative.

---

## Spreadsheet-Based Enterprise Reporting

Critical Enterprise reporting shall never depend upon unmanaged spreadsheets as the primary reporting mechanism.

Enterprise reporting shall use approved analytical platforms.

---

## Unapproved Data Sources

Reports and dashboards shall never consume information from ungoverned or unofficial data sources.

Only authoritative Enterprise information sources shall be used.

---

## Duplicate Reports

Multiple reports providing identical business information shall be consolidated whenever practical.

Enterprise reporting shall minimize duplication.

---

## Inconsistent Business Terminology

Reports, dashboards and analytical services shall never use terminology that conflicts with the Enterprise Canonical Business Vocabulary.

Semantic consistency shall be preserved.

---

## Unmonitored Analytical Services

Enterprise Analytics services shall never operate without monitoring of

- availability
- performance
- data freshness
- KPI quality
- governance compliance

Operational issues shall initiate corrective action according to Enterprise governance.
