# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Streaming Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Event Streaming Architecture principles are inherited from EA-343.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Streaming Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Streaming Services Architecture supporting secure, scalable and real-time streaming capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Streaming Services shall

- provide standardized streaming services
- enable continuous data streaming
- support real-time processing
- improve interoperability
- strengthen scalability
- reduce latency
- support event-driven operations
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- enable continuous optimization

Enterprise Streaming Services shall provide trusted and reusable real-time streaming capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- streaming publishers
- streaming consumers
- stream processing engines
- streaming platforms
- messaging infrastructure
- analytics platforms
- enterprise applications
- cloud platforms
- hybrid environments
- external streaming integrations

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, infrastructure platform or streaming technology.

# 3. Enterprise Streaming Services Principles

Enterprise Streaming Services shall follow these principles.

## Real-Time First

Enterprise Streaming Services shall prioritize real-time processing while maintaining reliability, consistency and governance.

---

## Secure by Default

Every streaming service shall be authenticated, authorized, encrypted and continuously monitored.

---

## Standardized Streaming Services

Enterprise Streaming Services shall expose standardized streaming interfaces supporting interoperability across the Enterprise.

---

## Metadata Driven Streaming

Streaming services shall leverage Enterprise metadata for discovery, routing, governance and lifecycle management.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Streaming Services shall remain independent of vendors, cloud providers, streaming engines and messaging technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Streaming Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for secure, scalable and governed real-time streaming across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating standardized streaming services, stream processing, metadata, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables trusted, resilient and policy-controlled real-time data distribution while maintaining governance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Streaming Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise streaming capabilities across the Enterprise.

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Streaming Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted stream optimization, autonomous processing pipelines, semantic-aware streaming, adaptive workload balancing, intelligent stream discovery, self-optimizing streaming infrastructures, edge-native stream processing, decentralized streaming ecosystems and quantum-safe Enterprise streaming platforms.
