# 1. Decision

The MFM architecture is hereby accepted as a **three-layer controlled architecture** consisting of:

```text
LAYER 1
MFM DOCUMENT SERIES
A1.x / MFM-001 → MFM-151

LAYER 2
PHASE CONTROL
N1 → N9 + POST-N9 TRANSITION

LAYER 3
STEADY-STATE GOVERNANCE
CURRENT CONTROL CATALOG
```

The three layers are formally recognized as distinct.

They shall not be treated as one sequential document series.

# 4. Layer 3 Decision

# 9. New-Control Threshold

A new control requires all of the following:

```text
MATERIAL REQUIREMENT
AND
DISTINCT SCOPE
AND
DISTINCT CONTROL OBJECTIVE
AND
DISTINCT AUTHORITY
AND
DISTINCT OUTPUT
AND
NO ADEQUATE EXISTING COVERAGE
AND
FORMAL AUTHORIZATION
```

Failure of any mandatory criterion means a new numbered control shall not be created.

---

# 10. Anti-Runaway Principle

The following principle is permanently adopted:

> **No MFM document, phase, governance control or steady-state control shall create automatic authority for its own successor. Numerical succession has no architectural authority.**

---

# 11. Anti-Recursion Principle

The following additional principle is permanently adopted:

> **No governance control shall create another governance layer merely because a further governance question can be formulated. A new layer requires demonstrated material necessity, distinct scope, distinct authority, distinct outputs and formal authorization.**

# 36. Final Architectural Closure Statement

> **The MFM architecture has reached a controlled architectural endpoint. The original MFM numbered series is formally closed at MFM-151. The N1–N9 phase-control lifecycle and Post-N9 transition are closed with conditions. Continuous governance remains active as an operating control framework. The numbered STEADY-STATE succession is frozen and has no automatic successor authority. Future evolution shall occur through controlled change, revision, extension, merger, supersession, retirement or formal reopening based on demonstrated material need. This preserves both architectural closure and future adaptability while preventing uncontrolled document proliferation, phase proliferation and recursive governance layers.**
