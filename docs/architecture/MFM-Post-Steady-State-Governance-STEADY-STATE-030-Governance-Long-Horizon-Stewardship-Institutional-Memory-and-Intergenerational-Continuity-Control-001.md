# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-030 establishes the controlled mechanism for preserving institutional knowledge, governance intent, decision rationale, critical capabilities, foundational records and long-horizon continuity across leadership changes, organizational restructuring, technological change and generations of system evolution.

The purpose is to ensure that governance remains understandable, accountable and capable of continuity even when the individuals, technologies, structures or operating conditions surrounding it change.

The operating sequence is:

```text
PURPOSE
↓
GOVERNANCE INTENT
↓
INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY
↓
KNOWLEDGE PRESERVATION
↓
KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER
↓
SUCCESSION / TRANSITION
↓
CONTINUITY
↓
REASSESSMENT
↓
ADAPTATION
↓
FUTURE STEWARDSHIP
```

# 4. Stewardship

Stewardship is the responsibility to preserve, maintain and responsibly evolve assets, knowledge, capabilities and governance intent for present and future stakeholders.

Stewardship requires:

```text
Care
Accountability
Traceability
Continuity
Adaptability

```

---

# 5. Stewardship vs Ownership

Ownership establishes accountability for an asset, decision or outcome.

Stewardship establishes responsibility for preserving its integrity and future usefulness.

```text
OWNER
=
ACCOUNTABILITY

STEWARD
=
CONTINUITY + CARE + FUTURE FITNESS
```

One role may hold both responsibilities where appropriate, but the concepts remain distinct.
