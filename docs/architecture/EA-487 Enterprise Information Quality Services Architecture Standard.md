# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Quality Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed management, measurement and improvement of Enterprise information quality across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Quality Services shall

- provide centralized information quality management
- establish common quality standards
- measure information quality
- monitor quality conditions
- identify quality issues
- support quality improvement
- strengthen information governance
- improve decision accuracy
- support regulatory requirements
- reduce information-related risks
- support lifecycle governance
- support privacy and security requirements
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure quality traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Quality Services shall provide trusted and reusable quality management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Information Quality Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed management, measurement and improvement of Enterprise information quality across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating information quality governance, quality rules management, quality measurement, quality profiling, information quality monitoring, quality issue management, quality improvement, quality validation, quality score management, quality reporting, lifecycle integration, data governance integration, metadata governance integration, information governance integration, privacy integration, security integration, compliance integration, risk integration, audit integration, identity integration, authorization integration, event integration, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables reliable, accurate and policy-driven information quality management while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.
