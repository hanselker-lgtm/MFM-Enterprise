# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Governance Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive governance architecture for managing Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Governance Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence governance principles
- governance operating model
- decision rights architecture
- policy management architecture
- risk governance architecture
- ethics and responsibility framework
- compliance architecture
- control framework
- audit architecture
- governance performance management
- governance evolution framework

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are governed responsibly, transparently and effectively.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Governance Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture intelligence governance domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Governance Model
- Governance Operating Model
- Governance Principles
- Decision Rights Architecture
- Policy Management Architecture
- Risk Governance Architecture
- Ethics and Responsibility Framework
- Compliance Architecture
- Control Framework
- Audit Architecture
- Governance Performance
- Governance Maturity
- Governance Roadmaps
- Governance Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- intelligent operating models
- future adaptive enterprises

---

# 3. Intelligence Governance Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Governance Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Responsible Intelligence Governance

All intelligence capabilities shall operate under defined governance structures and responsibilities.

---

## Principle 2 – Clear Accountability

Ownership and responsibility shall be clearly defined for all intelligence capabilities.

---

## Principle 3 – Transparency

Intelligence decisions, controls and outcomes shall be understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 4 – Risk-Based Governance

Governance activities shall consider intelligence risks and operational impact.

---

## Principle 5 – Human Responsibility

Human accountability shall remain central to intelligence governance.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Compliance

Intelligence capabilities shall continuously comply with policies, standards and requirements.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Governance Evolution

Governance structures shall continuously improve as intelligence capabilities evolve.

# 9. Intelligence Decision Rights Architecture

The Intelligence Decision Rights Architecture defines how authority, responsibility and decision-making rights are established across the Enterprise Intelligence Governance environment.

The architecture ensures that intelligence-related decisions are made by appropriate roles with clear accountability.

---

# 9.1 Intelligence Decision Rights Principles

Decision rights shall ensure:

- clear authority assignment
- accountable decision-making
- transparent governance
- appropriate escalation paths
- controlled intelligence decisions
