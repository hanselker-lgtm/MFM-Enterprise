# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise knowledge management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Knowledge management ensures that enterprise knowledge is captured, validated, classified, shared, maintained and continuously improved in a consistent manner while preserving organizational learning and maintaining compliance with Enterprise Architecture principles.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Knowledge Management
- Knowledge Capture
- Knowledge Classification
- Knowledge Validation
- Knowledge Sharing
- Knowledge Repository
- Knowledge Governance
- Knowledge Lifecycle
- Compliance

All enterprise knowledge management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## KM-001

Provide standardized knowledge management processes.

---

## KM-002

Support enterprise-wide knowledge sharing.

---

## KM-003

Ensure validated and trustworthy organizational knowledge.

---

## KM-004

Improve operational efficiency through knowledge reuse.

---

## KM-005

Maintain full compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 4. Knowledge Management Principles

Enterprise knowledge management shall follow these principles.

- Knowledge as an Enterprise Asset
- Capture by Default
- Validation Before Publication
- Reuse Before Recreation
- Controlled Classification
- Traceability by Design
- Auditability by Design
- Continuous Improvement

Knowledge management shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Knowledge Categories

Enterprise knowledge shall be organized into standardized categories.

Categories shall include

- Architectural Knowledge
- Operational Knowledge
- Technical Knowledge
- Security Knowledge
- Process Knowledge
- Support Knowledge
- User Documentation
- Lessons Learned

Additional knowledge categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Knowledge Ownership

Each enterprise knowledge domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- maintenance responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the knowledge lifecycle.

---

# 7. Knowledge Governance

Enterprise knowledge governance shall define

- knowledge governance
- publication governance
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- publication approval
- governance reporting

Knowledge governance shall remain technology independent.

---

# 8. Knowledge Responsibilities

Enterprise knowledge management shall provide controlled coordination of enterprise knowledge.

Knowledge responsibilities shall

- separate knowledge management from operational execution
- coordinate knowledge ownership
- ensure knowledge consistency
- validate published knowledge
- preserve knowledge traceability
- support organizational learning

Knowledge management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Knowledge Capture

Enterprise knowledge shall be captured using standardized processes.

Knowledge capture shall

- capture operational experience
- capture architectural decisions
- capture technical documentation
- capture lessons learned
- capture governance decisions
- preserve source traceability

Knowledge capture shall occur continuously throughout the enterprise lifecycle.

---

# 10. Knowledge Classification

Enterprise knowledge shall follow standardized classification.

Classification shall

- determine knowledge category
- determine confidentiality
- determine ownership
- determine lifecycle status
- determine publication level
- document classification decisions

Knowledge classification shall remain consistent across the enterprise.

---

# 11. Knowledge Validation

Enterprise knowledge shall be validated before publication.

Validation shall

- verify technical accuracy
- verify architectural alignment
- verify governance compliance
- verify documentation quality
- detect outdated information
- support periodic review

Knowledge validation shall ensure trustworthy enterprise knowledge.

---

# 12. Knowledge Sharing

Enterprise knowledge management shall provide standardized sharing mechanisms.

Knowledge sharing shall

- support enterprise collaboration
- support cross-functional reuse
- provide searchable repositories
- preserve version history
- support controlled publication
- encourage organizational learning

Knowledge sharing shall remain secure and governed.

---

# 13. Knowledge Dependencies

Enterprise knowledge management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- architecture repositories
- document management systems
- incident management
- problem management
- governance repositories
- enterprise infrastructure

Knowledge management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Knowledge Documentation

Each enterprise knowledge domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- knowledge description
- ownership information
- validation history
- classification details
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Knowledge Lifecycle

Enterprise knowledge shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Created
- Captured
- Classified
- Validated
- Published
- Maintained
- Reviewed
- Updated
- Archived
- Retired

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Knowledge Quality Attributes

Enterprise knowledge implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- accuracy
- consistency
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability
- completeness
- accessibility
- reliability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the knowledge lifecycle.

---

# 17. Knowledge Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized knowledge registry.

The registry shall contain

- knowledge identifiers
- ownership assignments
- knowledge categories
- lifecycle status
- dependency information
- publication history
- documentation references
- governance status

The knowledge registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise knowledge management.

---

# 18. Knowledge Reviews

Enterprise knowledge shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- knowledge quality
- validation effectiveness
- classification accuracy
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment
- publication readiness

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Knowledge Metrics

Enterprise knowledge management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- publication frequency
- validation success rate
- knowledge reuse rate
- documentation completeness
- review completion rate
- outdated knowledge percentage
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous knowledge improvement.

---

# 20. Knowledge Verification

Enterprise knowledge shall undergo formal verification before publication and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm technical correctness
- verify architectural consistency
- verify governance compliance
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve publication

Knowledge verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Knowledge Improvement

Enterprise knowledge management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve knowledge quality
- improve organizational learning
- improve knowledge reuse
- reduce outdated documentation
- strengthen governance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise knowledge management shall handle knowledge management exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify validation failures
- classify publication failures
- classify repository failures
- classify review failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Knowledge management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, governance or organizational learning.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Knowledge management implementations may depend upon

- approved document management systems
- approved architecture repositories
- approved incident management systems
- approved problem management systems
- approved governance repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Knowledge management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external knowledge management services

Knowledge management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A knowledge management implementation is compliant when

- Knowledge responsibilities are documented.
- Knowledge capture follows enterprise standards.
- Knowledge classification is completed.
- Knowledge validation has been performed.
- Knowledge sharing is governed.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Knowledge Registry is updated.
- Knowledge verification has been completed.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Audit logging is enabled where applicable.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Unvalidated Knowledge

Knowledge shall never be published without documented validation.

---

## Duplicate Documentation

Multiple conflicting versions of the same enterprise knowledge shall never exist without governance approval.

---

## Outdated Knowledge

Knowledge shall never remain published after it has become obsolete without review and update.

---

## Missing Ownership

Knowledge artifacts shall never exist without documented ownership and maintenance responsibility.

---

## Incomplete Documentation

Knowledge shall never be published without sufficient documentation supporting its intended use.

---

## Uncontrolled Publication

Knowledge shall never be shared outside approved governance processes.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise knowledge management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- knowledge quality
- validation effectiveness
- classification accuracy
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- publication readiness
- operational usefulness
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Knowledge Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing knowledge management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise knowledge is consistently captured, validated, classified, maintained and shared while preserving organizational learning, architectural consistency and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise knowledge management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
