# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Resilience Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive resilience architecture for ensuring that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities remain reliable, available, secure and recoverable under changing conditions and disruptive events.

The Intelligence Resilience Architecture Framework shall provide:

- resilience principles
- resilience governance
- resilient architecture
- fault tolerance
- self-healing intelligence
- operational resilience
- cyber resilience
- data resilience
- model resilience
- decision resilience
- continuity management
- resilience measurement
- resilience maturity
- resilience evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities can withstand disruption, recover effectively and continue delivering value.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Resilience Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture resilience domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Resilience Model
- Resilience Governance
- Resilient Architecture
- Fault Tolerance Architecture
- Self-Healing Intelligence
- Operational Resilience
- Cyber Resilience
- Data Resilience
- Model Resilience
- Decision Resilience
- Continuity Management
- Resilience Metrics
- Resilience Maturity
- Resilience Roadmaps
- Resilience Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- autonomous systems
- critical Intelligence platforms
- continuously operating Enterprise environments

---

# 3. Intelligence Resilience Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Resilience Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Continuous Availability

Enterprise Intelligence capabilities shall remain available when required.

---

## Principle 2 – Fault Tolerance

Systems shall continue operating despite failures.

---

## Principle 3 – Recovery Capability

Enterprise Intelligence shall recover effectively after disruption.

---

## Principle 4 – Self-Healing Capability

Intelligence systems should identify and correct failures where possible.

---

## Principle 5 – Security Resilience

Resilience shall include protection against cyber threats.

---

## Principle 6 – Data Integrity

Learning and operational data shall remain accurate and protected.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

Resilience shall continuously evolve through experience and learning.
