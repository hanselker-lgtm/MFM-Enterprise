# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Risk Management Framework is to establish a structured approach for identifying, assessing, managing and reducing risks across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture environment.

The Risk Management Framework shall provide:

- risk principles
- risk governance
- risk identification
- risk assessment
- risk treatment
- risk monitoring
- risk improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise decisions are informed by risk awareness and resilience considerations.

---

# 2. Scope

The Risk Management Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture risk domains:

- Enterprise Risk
- Architecture Risk
- Capability Risk
- Service Risk
- Process Risk
- Application Risk
- Technology Risk
- Infrastructure Risk
- Information Risk
- Data Risk
- Knowledge Risk
- Decision Risk
- Outcome Risk
- Value Risk
- Evolution Risk
- Transformation Risk
- Portfolio Risk
- Security Risk

The framework applies to:

- strategic risk management
- operational risk management
- technology risk management
- information risk management
- transformation risk management
- investment risk management

---

# 3. Enterprise Risk Management Principles

The Risk Management Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Risk Awareness

All Enterprise activities shall consider relevant risks.

---

## Principle 2 – Proactive Risk Management

Risks shall be identified and managed before they become incidents.

---

## Principle 3 – Risk Ownership

Every identified risk shall have a defined owner.

---

## Principle 4 – Risk-Based Decision Making

Enterprise decisions shall consider risk impact and exposure.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Risk Monitoring

Risks shall be continuously monitored and reviewed.

---

## Principle 6 – Balanced Risk Management

Risk management shall balance:

- protection
- opportunity
- value creation
- innovation

---

## Principle 7 – Enterprise Resilience

Risk management shall strengthen Enterprise ability to adapt and recover.

# 9. Architecture Risk Management

The Architecture Risk Management Model defines how risks related to Enterprise Architecture decisions, standards, principles and designs are identified, assessed and controlled.

Architecture Risk Management ensures that Enterprise Architecture remains aligned, secure, sustainable and resilient.

---

# 9.1 Architecture Risk Principles

Architecture risks shall consider:

- strategic alignment
- architecture compliance
- complexity
- dependency management
- scalability
- sustainability
- future adaptability

---

# 9.2 Architecture Risk Categories

| Risk Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Alignment Risk | Risk of misalignment with strategy |
| Compliance Risk | Risk of violating architecture standards |
| Complexity Risk | Risk caused by excessive complexity |
| Dependency Risk | Risk from architecture dependencies |
| Scalability Risk | Risk affecting future growth |
| Evolution Risk | Risk affecting future adaptability |

---

        |

        |

        |

        |

        |
