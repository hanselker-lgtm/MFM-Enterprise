# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Governance Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise governance as a strategic architecture capability.

The Governance Architecture Framework shall provide:

- governance principles
- governance structures
- governance responsibilities
- decision governance
- architecture governance
- policy governance
- control governance
- integrated governance practices
- governance improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise decisions are made consistently, transparently and aligned with strategic objectives, architecture direction and business value.

---

# 2. Scope

The Governance Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture governance domains:

- Enterprise Governance Model
- Governance Principles
- Governance Structures
- Decision Governance
- Architecture Governance
- Policy Governance
- Control Governance
- Risk Governance Integration
- Compliance Governance Integration
- Performance Governance Integration
- Transformation Governance Integration
- Portfolio Governance Integration
- Governance Monitoring
- Governance Maturity
- Governance Evolution

The framework applies to:

- strategic decisions
- architecture decisions
- investment decisions
- risk decisions
- compliance decisions
- transformation decisions
- operational decisions
- performance decisions

---

# 3. Enterprise Governance Architecture Principles

The Governance Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategic Alignment

Governance decisions shall support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Clear Accountability

Every governance decision shall have defined ownership and responsibility.

---

## Principle 3 – Transparent Decision Making

Governance decisions shall be documented, traceable and understandable.

---

## Principle 4 – Architecture Alignment

Governance decisions shall align with Enterprise Architecture principles and target states.

---

## Principle 5 – Integrated Governance

Governance shall connect strategy, risk, compliance, performance, transformation and portfolio management.

---

## Principle 6 – Evidence-Based Decisions

Governance decisions shall be supported by relevant information, analysis and documentation.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Governance Improvement

Governance capability shall continuously evolve according to Enterprise needs.

---

# 9. Governance Domain Architecture

The Governance Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise governance capabilities are structured into logical domains to support decision-making, accountability and control.

Governance domains provide a common structure for organizing authorities, responsibilities, decision processes and governance activities.

---

# 9.1 Governance Domain Principles

Governance domains shall ensure:

- clear authority structures
- defined responsibilities
- consistent decision processes
- transparent governance activities
- continuous governance improvement

---

# 9.2 Governance Domain Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Governance Domain | Logical grouping of governance activities |
| Governance Owner | Accountability for governance area |
| Decision Authority | Defines decision rights |
| Governance Processes | Defines governance activities |
| Governance Measures | Evaluates effectiveness |

---

# 9.3 Governance Domain Lifecycle

```text
Identify Governance Domain

        |

Define Governance Scope

        |

Assign Governance Ownership

        |

Establish Governance Processes

        |

Monitor Governance Effectiveness

        |

Improve Governance Capability
# 15. Risk Governance Integration Architecture

The Risk Governance Integration Architecture defines how Enterprise risk management is integrated into governance structures and decision processes.

Risk governance ensures that Enterprise decisions consider uncertainty, exposure and resilience requirements.

---

# 15.1 Risk Governance Principles

Risk governance shall ensure:

- risk-aware decisions
- defined risk ownership
- transparent risk exposure
- proactive risk management
- continuous resilience improvement

---

# 15.2 Risk Governance Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Risk Governance Framework | Defines risk management approach |
| Risk Authority | Defines risk decision rights |
| Risk Ownership | Defines accountability |
| Risk Reporting | Communicates risk position |
| Risk Review | Evaluates risk effectiveness |
