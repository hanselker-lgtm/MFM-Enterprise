# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundred-and-sixteenth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-115 – Security Architecture, Identity & Access Management, Zero Trust, Privileged Access, Secrets, Vulnerability & Security Control Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled privacy and information-rights capability covering privacy governance, data protection, privacy architecture, records compliance, information rights, consent, lawful processing, data subject rights, privacy impact assessment, data protection impact assessment, retention and deletion, privacy by design, data sharing, international data transfer, privacy incidents, data breach integration, privacy control testing, privacy assurance, privacy exceptions and privacy quality gates.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must govern personal information and privacy obligations throughout the information lifecycle so that processing is purposeful, lawful, transparent, proportionate, secure, traceable and appropriately retained or deleted.**

# 135. Definition of Ready

A privacy work item is Ready when:

- Processing activity, rights request, assessment, incident, retention rule or privacy control is identified.
- Ownership and applicable privacy requirements are established.
- Purpose, data, individuals, risks and lifecycle are understood.
- Required notices, consent, rights, retention, security, contractual and assurance controls are identified.

# 137. Final Privacy Principle

> **MFM must protect privacy through the full lifecycle of personal information, from collection through use, sharing, retention and disposal.**

---

# 138. Final Purpose Principle

> **Personal information must be processed for defined purposes and only to the extent necessary for those purposes.**

---

# 139. Final Transparency Principle

> **Individuals should receive clear and current information about material processing of their personal information.**

---

# 140. Final Rights Principle

> **Applicable information rights must be supported through controlled, traceable and timely processes.**

---

# 141. Final Retention Principle

> **Personal information must not be retained longer than justified by business, legal, regulatory or other legitimate requirements.**

---

# 142. Final Privacy-by-Design Principle

> **Privacy requirements must be embedded into architecture, design, procurement, development, change and operational processes rather than added after implementation.**

---

# 143. Final Incident Principle

> **Potential privacy incidents and data breaches must be assessed, contained, documented and escalated according to risk and applicable obligations.**

---

# 144. Final Assurance Principle

> **Privacy assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that material processing, rights, retention and protection controls operate effectively.**

---

# 145. Final Integration Principle

> **Privacy Governance must integrate with Security, Data, Records, Document, Service, Architecture, Procurement, Supplier, Risk, Compliance and Enterprise Assurance governance.**

---

# 146. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage privacy through a controlled lifecycle connecting purpose, lawful processing, consent, transparency, rights, assessment, sharing, transfer, retention, deletion, incident management and assurance.**
