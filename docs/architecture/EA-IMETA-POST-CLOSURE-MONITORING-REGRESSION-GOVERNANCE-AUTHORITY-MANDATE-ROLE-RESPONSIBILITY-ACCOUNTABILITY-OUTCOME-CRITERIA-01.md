### Status: POST-CLOSURE REGRESSION GOVERNANCE OUTCOME CRITERIA BASELINE
### Governing Architecture: EA-IMETA-MASTER-01

## Purpose
Establish the authoritative criteria layer beneath outcome, defining the conditions, tolerances, thresholds, measurement rules, evidence requirements, acceptance and rejection conditions, materiality, risk, escalation and review rules used to determine whether an outcome is acceptable.

## Core Principle
Outcome criteria convert an intended outcome into an objective and governable determination of success, variance, acceptance, rejection or escalation.

```text
OUTCOME
   ↓
CRITERIA
   ↓
SUCCESS CONDITIONS
   ↓
TOLERANCE / THRESHOLDS
   ↓
MEASUREMENT RULES
   ↓
EVIDENCE
   ↓
ACCEPTANCE / REJECTION
   ↓
ESCALATION
   ↓
REVIEW
```

## Criteria Quality Test
```text
DEFINED OUTCOME
+
CLEAR SUCCESS CONDITIONS
+
MEASURABLE THRESHOLDS
+
DEFINED TOLERANCE
+
VALID MEASUREMENT RULES
+
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
+
EXPLICIT ACCEPTANCE / REJECTION
+
ESCALATION RULES
=
VALID GOVERNED CRITERIA
```

## Criteria Structure
| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Outcome | Result being evaluated |
| Success Condition | State required for achievement |
| Tolerance | Permitted deviation |
| Threshold | Boundary triggering a classification or action |
| Measurement Rule | How the criterion is evaluated |
| Evidence Rule | Evidence required to support determination |
| Acceptance Condition | Requirement for acceptance |
| Rejection Condition | Requirement for rejection |
| Materiality Rule | When variance becomes material |
| Escalation Rule | When higher authority is required |
| Exception Rule | Conditions for governed exception |
| Review Rule | When criteria must be reassessed |

## Success Conditions
Success conditions shall describe observable or verifiable states that demonstrate the intended outcome has been achieved.

```text
OUTCOME
  ↓
SUCCESS CONDITIONS
  ├── SATISFIED
  ├── PARTIALLY SATISFIED
  ├── NOT SATISFIED
  └── UNDETERMINED
```

## Acceptance Criteria
Acceptance criteria define the minimum conditions under which the outcome may be accepted.

```text
MEASURED RESULT
      ↓
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
      ↓
ALL MANDATORY CONDITIONS SATISFIED?
 ├── YES → ACCEPT
 └── NO  → REJECT / CORRECT / CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE / ESCALATE
```

## Rejection Criteria
Rejection criteria shall identify conditions that make the outcome unacceptable, including material failure, unsafe state, regulatory non-conformity, unacceptable risk or insufficient evidence.

## Tolerance
Tolerance defines the permitted deviation from the expected outcome before a different governance response is required.

```text
EXPECTED VALUE / STATE
       ↓
ACCEPTABLE TOLERANCE
       ↓
WARNING ZONE
       ↓
ESCALATION THRESHOLD
       ↓
FAILURE THRESHOLD
```

Tolerance shall not override mandatory security, safety, resilience or compliance requirements.

## Thresholds
Thresholds shall define objective boundaries for classification, monitoring and action. Each threshold shall have an owner and an explicit consequence.

## Measurement Rules
Measurement rules shall define data source, method, frequency, calculation or assessment method, quality requirements and handling of missing or unreliable data.

```text
DATA SOURCE
   ↓
VALIDATE DATA
   ↓
APPLY MEASUREMENT RULE
   ↓
RESULT
   ↓
CLASSIFY
```

## Evidence Criteria
Evidence criteria define what must be available before an outcome determination can be considered sufficiently supported.

Evidence shall be attributable, relevant, current, complete enough for the decision and protected against inappropriate alteration.

## Baseline Criteria
Criteria shall identify the approved baseline against which outcome performance is assessed. Baseline changes shall not silently alter historical determinations.

## Outcome Classification
```text
RESULT
 ↓
CRITERIA
 ↓
ACHIEVED
PARTIALLY ACHIEVED
DEVIATING
FAILED
UNDETERMINED
```

## Materiality Criteria
Materiality shall consider impact, duration, scope, recurrence, affected services, customers, security exposure, resilience impact, regulatory consequences and systemic significance.

```text
VARIANCE
  ↓
MATERIAL?
 ├── NO  → LOCAL CORRECTION / MONITOR
 └── YES → GOVERNED ESCALATION
```

## Risk Criteria
Risk criteria shall define unacceptable risk states and the conditions under which risk may be accepted, treated or escalated.

## Security Criteria
Security criteria shall include applicable confidentiality, integrity, availability, authentication, authorization, vulnerability, threat and incident requirements.

## Resilience Criteria
Resilience criteria shall address continuity, recovery, restoration, dependency behavior, capacity and service recovery requirements where applicable.

## Data Criteria
Data criteria may address accuracy, completeness, integrity, timeliness, lineage, availability, access control and authorized use.

## AI and Agent Criteria
AI and agent criteria shall evaluate task correctness, policy compliance, bounded autonomy, safety, tool use, data access, harmful side effects and required human oversight.

```text
AI / AGENT RESULT
      ↓
CORRECT?
      ↓
POLICY COMPLIANT?
      ↓
WITHIN AUTONOMY LIMITS?
      ↓
SAFE / ACCEPTABLE?
      ↓
ACCEPT / REJECT / ESCALATE
```

## Compliance Criteria
Compliance criteria shall distinguish mandatory requirements from discretionary controls and shall prevent operational convenience from overriding mandatory obligations.

## Escalation Criteria
```text
CRITERION RESULT
      ↓
WITHIN LOCAL AUTHORITY?
 ├── YES → DISPOSITION
 └── NO  → ESCALATE
```
Escalation shall be mandatory where thresholds indicate material risk, mandatory non-conformity, systemic impact or insufficient authority.

## Exception Criteria
Exceptions shall define eligibility, authority, rationale, risk, compensating controls, expiry or review date and evidence requirements.

```text
CRITERION FAILURE
      ↓
EXCEPTION ELIGIBLE?
 ├── NO  → REJECT / REMEDIATE
 └── YES → GOVERNED EXCEPTION
```

## Criteria Change Control
Criteria changes shall be versioned, approved, effective-dated and traceable. Changes shall not be applied retroactively to conceal a previous failure.

```text
CURRENT CRITERIA
      ↓
PROPOSED CHANGE
      ↓
IMPACT ASSESSMENT
      ↓
AUTHORIZATION
      ↓
NEW VERSION
      ↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

## Criteria Failure
```text
CRITERIA INVALID / AMBIGUOUS
        ↓
STOP MATERIAL DETERMINATION
        ↓
CLARIFY / GOVERN
        ↓
RE-ESTABLISH CRITERIA
        ↓
RE-MEASURE
        ↓
DETERMINE
```

## Criteria Review
Criteria shall be reviewed after material regression, repeated variance, baseline change, architecture change, risk change, regulatory change or evidence that the criteria no longer measure the intended outcome.

```text
CRITERIA ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
STILL VALID?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → MODIFY / SUPERSEDE / RETIRE
```

## Governance Learning
Repeated failures against the same criteria shall trigger analysis of whether the outcome definition, controls, architecture, measurement method or criteria themselves are inadequate.

This document specializes the criteria layer beneath outcome for post-closure regression governance. It does not replace governance, authority, mandate, role, responsibility or accountability layers.
