# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the eighty-ninth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-88 – Enterprise Legal Governance, Contract Law, Legal Risk, Disputes, Claims, Intellectual Property & Legal Matter Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for financial governance, financial strategy, financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, IT financial management, cost management, cost transparency, cost allocation, showback, chargeback, investment governance, investment prioritization, business cases, financial approvals, portfolio economics, total cost of ownership, lifecycle cost management, financial controls, procurement-finance coordination, supplier financial management, invoice governance, financial reporting, variance management, financial risk coordination, financial assurance, financial findings, financial exceptions, financial remediation, financial metrics, financial dashboards, financial maturity and continual enterprise financial management improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage enterprise and technology financial resources through transparent planning, budgeting, forecasting, cost control, investment governance and evidence-based financial assurance.**

# 3. Financial Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish clear accountability and control over enterprise financial resources, commitments and decisions.**

# 4. Financial Planning Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Translate strategic priorities and operational requirements into sustainable financial plans and approved budgets.**

# 5. IT Financial Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide transparent understanding of technology costs, investments, consumption and financial value.**

# 6. Cost Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Manage costs against approved plans while maintaining required business outcomes, service quality, resilience and risk posture.**

# 7. Investment Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure investments are evaluated, prioritized, approved and monitored according to strategic value, risk and financial sustainability.**

# 8. Financial Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide reliable evidence that material financial controls, approvals, reporting and commitments operate effectively.**
