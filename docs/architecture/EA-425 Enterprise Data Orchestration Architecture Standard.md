# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Orchestration Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Fabric Architecture principles are inherited from EA-421.

Enterprise Data Mesh Architecture principles are inherited from EA-422.

Enterprise Lakehouse Architecture principles are inherited from EA-423.

Enterprise Data Pipeline Architecture principles are inherited from EA-424.

All Enterprise Data Orchestration implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Orchestration Architecture governing coordination, scheduling, execution and management of Enterprise data workflows across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Orchestration shall

- coordinate Enterprise workflows
- automate data movement
- manage dependencies
- improve operational reliability
- optimize execution
- support event-driven processing
- increase scalability
- strengthen governance
- enhance observability
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Orchestration shall serve as the central coordination capability for Enterprise information processing.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- workflow orchestration
- pipeline orchestration
- task scheduling
- dependency management
- event-driven orchestration
- distributed workflow execution
- metadata-driven orchestration
- operational automation
- workflow monitoring
- orchestration governance

The standard applies across all Enterprise systems, cloud environments and deployment models.

# 3. Enterprise Data Orchestration Principles

Enterprise Data Orchestration shall be governed by the following principles.

## Automation by Default

Workflow execution shall be fully automated wherever practical.

---

## Event-Driven Coordination

Enterprise workflows should be initiated through business events whenever appropriate.

---

## Metadata-Driven Execution

Workflow behavior shall be controlled through standardized Enterprise metadata.

---

## Resilience by Design

Workflow execution shall tolerate failures while maintaining operational continuity.

---

## Reusable Workflow Components

Orchestration services shall be reusable across Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Orchestration shall remain independent of orchestration platforms, workflow engines and cloud vendors.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Orchestration Architecture establishes the standardized framework for coordinating Enterprise workflows, scheduling activities and orchestrating data processing across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

By integrating Enterprise Data Pipelines, Data Fabric, Data Mesh, Lakehouse, Event Streaming, Metadata Management, Security, Observability and Automation, the Enterprise achieves reliable, scalable and policy-driven workflow execution supporting operational systems, analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Orchestration initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing workflow orchestration capabilities.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Data Orchestration Architecture shall continuously evolve to support AI-assisted workflow optimization, autonomous orchestration, intelligent dependency resolution, adaptive scheduling, self-healing workflow execution, predictive workload balancing, policy-driven orchestration and emerging Enterprise automation technologies.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Orchestration continues to provide secure, interoperable, scalable and business-driven workflow coordination across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
