# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the seventeenth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-16 – Enterprise Financial Management, Budgeting, Accounting, Treasury, Procurement, Cost Control & Financial Assurance Operations.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for procurement, supplier management, contracts, vendor risk, third-party governance and commercial operations.

The central objective is:

> **MFM third-party relationships must be selected, contracted, onboarded, monitored, governed, renewed and exited through controlled, risk-based and commercially sound processes that protect service continuity, information, finances and organizational interests.**

# 142. Definition of Ready

A procurement, supplier or contract work item is Ready when:

- The requirement, supplier, contract, sourcing activity, renewal, issue, risk or exit action is clearly identified.
- Ownership, value, criticality, dependencies and applicable requirements are known.
- Evaluation, security, privacy, financial, legal, continuity, approval and evidence requirements are defined.

---

# 144. Final Procurement Principle

> **MFM procurement must obtain appropriate value while maintaining transparency, authorization, competition where appropriate and proportionate risk control.**

---

# 145. Final Supplier Principle

> **Suppliers must be managed according to their criticality, dependency, risk and performance throughout the relationship lifecycle.**

---

# 146. Final Contract Principle

> **Material contractual commitments must be clearly defined, authorized, monitored and traceable from execution through expiry or termination.**

---

# 147. Final Security Principle

> **Third parties with access to MFM systems, services or information must meet appropriate security requirements and remain subject to proportionate oversight.**

---

# 148. Final Privacy Principle

> **Third parties processing personal data must be governed through appropriate privacy, contractual and security controls.**

---

# 149. Final Continuity Principle

> **Critical supplier dependencies must have proportionate resilience, continuity and exit arrangements.**

---

# 150. Final Commercial Principle

> **Supplier relationships must be managed for total value and lifecycle cost, not purchase price alone.**

---

# 151. Final Exit Principle

> **MFM must retain practical control over data, access, assets, knowledge and service continuity when supplier relationships end.**

---

# 152. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material procurement and supplier controls must be supported by evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

---

# 153. Final Improvement Principle

> **Supplier performance, incidents, disputes, commercial results and assurance findings must continuously improve MFM third-party management.**

---

# 154. Final Integration Principle

> **Procurement and Supplier Management must integrate with Financial Management, Risk, Compliance, Cybersecurity, Privacy, Data Management, Technical Operations, Service Management, Business Continuity, Architecture and Legal functions.**

---

# 155. Final Steady-State Supplier Principle

> **MFM third-party relationships must be selected, contracted, onboarded, monitored, governed, renewed and exited through controlled, risk-based and commercially sound processes that protect service continuity, information, finances and organizational interests.**
