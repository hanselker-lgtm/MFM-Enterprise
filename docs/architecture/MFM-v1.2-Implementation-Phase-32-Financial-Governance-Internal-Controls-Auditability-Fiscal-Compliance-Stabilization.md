The purpose of this phase is to establish the financial-governance, internal-control, auditability and fiscal-compliance baseline for MFM.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must provide controlled, traceable and auditable financial processes in which transactions, approvals, reconciliations, reporting and period-close activities preserve financial integrity and clearly demonstrate who performed, approved and changed material financial actions.**

# 146. Final Financial Principle

> **Every material financial transaction must be authorized, traceable, accurately recorded and capable of reconciliation.**

---

# 147. Final Integrity Principle

> **Historical financial evidence must not be silently overwritten; corrections must preserve an auditable relationship to the original state.**

---

# 148. Final Approval Principle

> **Financial authority must be explicit, threshold-based where appropriate and supported by retained approval evidence.**

---

# 149. Final Segregation Principle

> **Conflicting financial responsibilities should be separated where practical, with controlled exceptions where separation cannot be achieved.**

---

# 150. Final Budget Principle

> **Budget controls must distinguish approved plans, actual transactions, commitments and forecasts where those concepts are used.**

---

# 151. Final Reconciliation Principle

> **Material financial records must be reconciled against authoritative sources with differences visible and resolved through controlled processes.**

---

# 152. Final Reporting Principle

> **Financial reports must be traceable to controlled underlying records and preserve the distinction between actual, budget and forecast information.**

---

# 153. Final Close Principle

> **Period close must be a controlled, evidenced process rather than an informal administrative activity.**

---

# 154. Final Compliance Principle

> **Applicable accounting, fiscal, tax and statutory requirements must be identified by the responsible authority and reflected in governed system processes.**

---

# 155. Final Audit Principle

> **Authorized auditors must be able to trace material financial information from report to transaction, approval, source and supporting evidence where required.**

---

# 156. Final Control Principle

> **Financial controls are effective only when they are owned, evidenced, tested and corrected when they fail.**

---

# 157. Final Recovery Principle

> **Financial recovery must restore not only data but also accounting integrity, reconciliation and auditability.**

---

# 158. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should operate financial processes through strong internal controls, explicit approval authority, reliable reconciliation, preserved audit evidence and governed fiscal compliance.**
