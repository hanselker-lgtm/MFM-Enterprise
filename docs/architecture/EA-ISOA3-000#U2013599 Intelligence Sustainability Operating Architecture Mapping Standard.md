# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational sustainability architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities can be developed, operated and maintained responsibly over the long term.

The Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- sustainability operating principles
- resource sustainability frameworks
- energy efficiency structures
- lifecycle sustainability models
- technology sustainability models
- environmental impact models
- operational sustainability structures
- economic sustainability models
- human capability sustainability models
- sustainability monitoring structures
- sustainability automation structures
- sustainability evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for sustainable, responsible and continuously improving Intelligence operations.

# 3. Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Sustainability Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Sustainability by Design

Sustainability shall be integrated into Intelligence capabilities from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Resource Usage

Resources shall be managed responsibly and efficiently.

---

## Principle 3 – Lifecycle Sustainability

Intelligence capabilities shall be sustainable throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 4 – Energy Efficiency

Energy consumption shall be optimised without reducing capability.

---

## Principle 5 – Long-Term Value Creation

Intelligence investments shall create sustainable value.

---

## Principle 6 – Human Capability Sustainability

Human knowledge and capability shall be continuously developed.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Sustainability Evolution

Sustainability capability shall continuously improve.
