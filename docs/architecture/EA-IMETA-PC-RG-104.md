## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory effectiveness-determination layer that evaluates whether an executed post-closure response achieved its required outcome, restored required control conditions, reduced the governed deviation and produced sufficient evidence to support resolution or further action.

## Core Principle
Execution completion is not effectiveness. Effectiveness shall be determined against explicit required outcomes and acceptance criteria, using evidence that distinguishes action completion from actual improvement, control restoration and sustained result.

```text
RESPONSE EXECUTED
↓
REQUIRED OUTCOME DEFINED?
├── NO → DEFINE / ESCALATE
└── YES
     ↓
EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT?
├── NO → MEASURE / OBSERVE / ASSESS
└── YES
     ↓
OUTCOME ACHIEVED?
├── NO → CONTINUE / CORRECT / ESCALATE
└── YES
     ↓
CONTROL STATE RESTORED?
├── NO → FURTHER ACTION / REVALIDATE
└── YES
     ↓
SUSTAINED / RELIABLE?
├── NO → CONTINUE MONITORING / REOPEN
└── YES → EFFECTIVENESS ACCEPTED
```

## Effectiveness Quality Test
```text
COMPLETED RESPONSE
+
DEFINED REQUIRED OUTCOME
+
VALID ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
+
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
+
MEASURED RESULT
+
CONTROL RESTORATION
+
SUSTAINABILITY CONSIDERED
+
TRACEABLE DETERMINATION
=
VALID GOVERNED EFFECTIVENESS DETERMINATION
```

## Completion vs Effectiveness vs Resolution
```text
COMPLETION
→ ACTIONS WERE PERFORMED

EFFECTIVENESS
→ REQUIRED RESULT WAS ACHIEVED

RESOLUTION
→ GOVERNED CONDITION REACHED AN ACCEPTED END STATE

RELIANCE RESTORATION
→ AUTHORIZED RELIANCE CAN BE SAFELY RESTORED
```

## Effectiveness State Model
```text
PENDING
MEASUREMENT REQUIRED
ASSESSING
INEFFECTIVE
PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE
EFFECTIVE
EFFECTIVE WITH CONDITIONS
SUSTAINABILITY PENDING
REASSESSMENT REQUIRED
REOPENING REQUIRED
ACCEPTED
```

```text
EFFECTIVENESS SHALL BE MEASURED AGAINST DEFINED REQUIRED OUTCOMES
```

```text
EXECUTION COMPLETION SHALL NOT BE USED AS PROOF OF EFFECTIVENESS
```

```text
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA SHALL BE EXPLICIT AND VERSIONED
```

```text
EVIDENCE SHALL BE SUFFICIENT TO SUPPORT THE DETERMINATION
```

```text
PARTIAL EFFECTIVENESS SHALL REMAIN VISIBLE
```

```text
INEFFECTIVENESS SHALL TRIGGER A GOVERNED NEXT ACTION
```

```text
SUSTAINABILITY SHALL BE CONSIDERED WHERE TRANSIENT IMPROVEMENT IS INSUFFICIENT
```

```text
CONTROL RESTORATION SHALL BE DISTINGUISHED FROM SYMPTOM REDUCTION
```

```text
UNCERTAINTY SHALL REMAIN VISIBLE
```

```text
SECURITY, RESILIENCE, SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE EFFECTIVENESS SHALL RECEIVE APPROPRIATE RIGOR

```


```text
NEW REGRESSION EVIDENCE SHALL OVERRIDE STALE EFFECTIVENESS ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
EFFECTIVENESS ACCEPTANCE SHALL NOT AUTOMATICALLY MEAN RELIANCE RESTORATION
```

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Executed Response | Actions performed |
| Required Outcome | Expected result |
| Criteria | Acceptance conditions |
| Evidence | Supporting evidence |
| Measurement | Observed result |
| Control State | Restored / not restored |
| Sustainability | Persistence of result |
| Determination | Effectiveness result |

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Objective

Determine whether the executed response achieved its required outcome and restored the relevant control condition sufficiently to support the next governed lifecycle state.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Definition

Effectiveness is the evidence-based determination that a response achieved the required outcome to the defined acceptance criteria. It is distinct from execution completion and from final resolution.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Scope

Scope shall include affected outcomes, controls, dependencies, measurements, observation period, acceptance criteria and residual conditions.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Authority

Authority shall define who may determine, review, accept, reject or reopen an effectiveness result.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Criteria

Criteria shall define required outcome, target state, tolerance, evidence, observation period, sustainability and rejection conditions.
```text
EXECUTED RESPONSE
↓
MEASURE RESULT
↓
COMPARE WITH REQUIRED OUTCOME
↓
CONTROL RESTORED?
├── NO → INEFFECTIVE / FURTHER ACTION
└── YES
     ↓
SUSTAINED?
├── NO → MONITOR / REASSESS
└── YES → EFFECTIVE
```

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Preconditions

Preconditions include completed or sufficiently executed response, defined outcome, valid criteria, measurement capability, evidence and observation period where required.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Evidence

Evidence shall preserve response execution, required outcome, baseline, measurements, observations, criteria version, uncertainty, residual condition and determination.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Method

Methods may include before/after comparison, control-state verification, outcome measurement, trend analysis, independent validation and sustained-observation assessment.
```text
BEFORE STATE
↓
RESPONSE
↓
AFTER STATE
↓
COMPARE
↓
SUSTAINABILITY CHECK
↓
EFFECTIVENESS
```

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Decision

Decision shall determine ineffective, partially effective, effective, effective with conditions, sustainability pending, reassessment required or reopening required.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Accountability

Accountability shall remain explicit for measurement quality, criteria interpretation, acceptance and reopening decisions.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Timing

Effectiveness determination shall occur when evidence is sufficient and within a timeframe proportionate to consequence and required recovery.

## Security Post-Closure Response Effectiveness

Security effectiveness shall verify that exposure, control weakness or compromise has actually been reduced or contained, not merely that a remediation action was executed.

## Resilience Post-Closure Response Effectiveness

Resilience effectiveness shall evaluate restored availability, recovery capability, capacity and dependency stability.

## Compliance Post-Closure Response Effectiveness

Compliance effectiveness shall verify restoration of required controls and obligations with appropriate evidence.

## Data Post-Closure Response Effectiveness

Data effectiveness shall verify integrity, quality, lineage, authorized access and recoverability after response.

## AI and Agent Post-Closure Response Effectiveness

AI/agent effectiveness shall evaluate both output outcomes and restoration of authority, policy, tool, data and autonomy controls.
```text
AI / AGENT RESPONSE
↓
OUTCOME RESTORED?
+
CONTROL STATE RESTORED?
↓
SUSTAINED?
↓
EFFECTIVENESS
```

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Failure

Failure includes false success, insufficient evidence, transient improvement, residual control weakness, measurement error or effectiveness declared before required observation.
```text
EFFECTIVENESS FAILURE
↓
MATERIAL CONDITION REMAINS?
├── NO → CORRECT / REASSESS
└── YES → FURTHER RESPONSE / ESCALATE / REOPEN
```

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Independence

Independent effectiveness validation may be required for high-consequence, irreversible, disputed or reliance-critical responses.

## Post-Closure Response Effectiveness Review and Learning

Reviews shall identify false positives, weak criteria, transient recovery, measurement gaps and recurring ineffective response patterns.

|---|---|---|

## Completion Is Not Effectiveness
A response can execute every planned action and still fail to restore the required outcome.
```text
PLAN COMPLETED
≠
OUTCOME ACHIEVED
```

## Symptom Reduction vs Control Restoration
Temporary symptom reduction shall not be treated as control restoration when the underlying condition remains materially impaired.
```text
SYMPTOM REDUCED
+
CONTROL STILL IMPAIRED
=
NOT EFFECTIVE
```

## Sustainability
Where transient recovery is insufficient, effectiveness requires an observation period demonstrating persistence of the restored condition.

## Residual Condition
Residual conditions shall be explicitly recorded. Effectiveness may be accepted with conditions only where the remaining condition is authorized, understood and governed.

## Self-Reported Success
Self-reported success alone shall not constitute sufficient evidence for material effectiveness determinations where independent measurement is available or required.

## New Regression Evidence
New evidence of deterioration shall supersede stale effectiveness acceptance and trigger reassessment where criteria are met.

## Effectiveness vs Reliance Restoration
Effectiveness does not automatically restore reliance. Reliance restoration requires its own governed determination.
```text
EFFECTIVE
↓
RELIANCE RESTORATION ASSESSMENT
```

## AI and Agent Effectiveness
AI/agent effectiveness must include control-state restoration; acceptable output alone does not prove that authority, policy, tool or data controls were restored.

## Effectiveness Anti-Gaming
Effectiveness shall not be declared merely to close workflow, improve metrics or avoid reopening.

## Relationship to Reliance Restoration
RG-104 determines whether the response worked. The next layer determines whether the system may be reaccepted and reliance restored based on the effectiveness evidence.
```text
EXECUTION → EFFECTIVENESS → REACCEPTANCE → RELIANCE RESTORATION
```
