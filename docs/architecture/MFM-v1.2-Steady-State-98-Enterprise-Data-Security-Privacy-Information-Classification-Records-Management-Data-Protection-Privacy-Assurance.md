# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the ninety-eighth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-97 – Enterprise Data Architecture, Data Governance, Data Quality, Master Data, Metadata, Data Lifecycle & Data Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for information classification, data protection, privacy governance, personal data management, records management, information handling, data access, privacy rights coordination, retention, legal holds, secure disposal, data loss prevention, information protection, privacy risk, records assurance, findings, exceptions, remediation, privacy metrics, dashboards, maturity and continual enterprise information protection and privacy improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must protect information and personal data throughout its lifecycle through proportionate classification, access control, privacy governance, records management, secure handling, retention and evidence-based assurance.**

# 3. Information Protection Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish clear accountability, decision rights, standards and controls for protecting enterprise information and personal data.**

# 4. Privacy Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, transparently, securely and for defined legitimate purposes in accordance with applicable requirements.**

# 5. Classification Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure information receives protection proportionate to its sensitivity, criticality, business value and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.**

# 6. Records Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure authoritative records are identified, retained, protected, accessible and disposed of in accordance with approved requirements.**

# 7. Data Protection Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse and destruction of protected information.**

# 8. Privacy Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide evidence that privacy, information protection and records controls operate effectively and remain aligned with applicable obligations.**

# 111. Final Information Protection Principle

> **MFM must protect information and personal data throughout its lifecycle through proportionate classification, access control, privacy governance, records management, secure handling, retention and evidence-based assurance.**

# 112. Final Classification Principle

> **Information must receive protection proportionate to its sensitivity, criticality, business value and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.**

# 113. Final Privacy Principle

> **Personal data must be processed for defined legitimate purposes with appropriate transparency, minimization, accuracy, protection, retention and accountability.**

# 114. Final Records Principle

> **Authoritative records must remain authentic, reliable, accessible, protected and retained for the required period.**

# 115. Final Access Principle

> **Access to protected information must be based on legitimate purpose, authorization, least privilege and need-to-know.**

# 116. Final Retention Principle

> **Information must not be retained longer than justified by applicable business, legal, regulatory or contractual requirements, subject to valid legal holds.**

# 117. Final Disposal Principle

> **Information must be securely disposed of when retention requirements expire and no legal or legitimate business requirement prevents disposal.**

# 118. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material information protection, privacy and records controls must be supported by reliable evidence and periodically assessed through risk-based assurance.**

# 119. Final Improvement Principle

> **Privacy incidents, DLP events, classification gaps, retention issues, records findings and assurance results must continuously improve MFM's information protection capability.**

# 120. Final Integration Principle

> **Information Protection and Privacy must integrate with Data Management, Cybersecurity, Identity, Enterprise Architecture, Application Management, IT Operations, Service Management, Configuration Management, Asset Management, Finance, Procurement, Suppliers, Risk, Compliance, Legal and Business Continuity.**

# 121. Final Steady-State Information Protection Principle

> **MFM must protect information and personal data throughout its lifecycle through proportionate classification, access control, privacy governance, records management, secure handling, retention and evidence-based assurance.**
