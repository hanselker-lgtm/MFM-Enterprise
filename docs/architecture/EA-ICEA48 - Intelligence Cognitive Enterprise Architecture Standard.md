# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, models, and architectural requirements for introducing **enterprise cognition** into the Enterprise Architecture.

Previous standards enabled the architecture to:

- Observe
- Learn
- Adapt
- Federate
- Collaborate
- Self-organize
- Evolve

EA-ICEA48 introduces the next capability:

**Understand.**

The objective is to enable Enterprise Architecture to reason about itself, its environment, and its objectives before making recommendations or autonomous decisions.

Enterprise cognition transforms architecture from an adaptive system into an intelligent reasoning system.

---

# 2. Vision

The Enterprise Architecture shall evolve into a cognitive enterprise capable of:

- Understanding context
- Understanding intent
- Understanding consequences
- Understanding relationships
- Understanding uncertainty
- Understanding business objectives
- Understanding human decisions
- Understanding its own behavior

The architecture shall become capable of reasoning similarly to expert enterprise architects while maintaining complete governance and explainability.

---

# 3. Enterprise Cognition Philosophy

Enterprise cognition is founded upon eight fundamental principles.

## Principle 1

Understanding precedes optimization.

An architecture shall understand a problem before attempting to improve it.

---

## Principle 2

Knowledge is contextual.

Information has meaning only within context.

---

## Principle 3

Reasoning requires relationships.

Isolated facts cannot produce intelligent decisions.

---

## Principle 4

Every decision has consequences.

Architectural reasoning shall evaluate downstream effects before acting.

---

## Principle 5

Uncertainty is measurable.

Architectural intelligence shall quantify uncertainty rather than ignore it.

---

## Principle 6

Human knowledge remains valuable.

AI augments enterprise architects rather than replacing them.

---

## Principle 7

Enterprise memory improves cognition.

Every architectural experience strengthens future reasoning.

---

## Principle 8

Continuous reasoning creates continuous intelligence.

Enterprise cognition is an ongoing process rather than a one-time analysis.

# 11. Enterprise Cognitive Architecture

The Enterprise Cognitive Architecture (ECA) defines how cognitive capabilities are organized across the enterprise.

Unlike traditional Enterprise Architecture, which primarily models structure, the Enterprise Cognitive Architecture models how the enterprise understands, reasons, and continuously improves itself.

The architecture consists of six interconnected cognitive layers.

```
Enterprise Perception

↓

Enterprise Understanding

↓

Enterprise Reasoning

↓

Enterprise Decision Intelligence

↓

Enterprise Learning

↓

Enterprise Memory
```

These layers operate continuously and exchange information through governed knowledge flows.

# 14. Enterprise Reasoning Layer

Reasoning converts contextual understanding into explainable conclusions.

The Reasoning Layer integrates multiple reasoning techniques.

---

## Deductive Reasoning

Applies enterprise rules to derive conclusions.

Example:

```
Policy A

AND

Policy B

↓

Required Control
```

---

## Inductive Reasoning

Identifies patterns from historical observations.

Example:

```
Repeated Incidents

↓

Pattern Recognition

↓

Predict Future Risk
```

---

## Abductive Reasoning

Determines the most likely explanation for observed events.

Example:

```
Performance Degradation

↓

Possible Causes

↓

Most Probable Root Cause
```

---

## Analogical Reasoning

Compares current situations with previous enterprise experiences.

Example:

```
Current Migration

↓

Historical Migration

↓

Recommended Practices
```

---

## Causal Reasoning

Determines cause-and-effect relationships.

Example:

```
Infrastructure Failure

↓

Service Disruption

↓

Customer Impact

↓

Revenue Loss
```

|------:|------|-----------------|
| 0 | Reactive | Decisions based on isolated observations |
| 1 | Context-Aware | Enterprise context is recognized |
| 2 | Knowledge-Driven | Knowledge guides architectural decisions |

| 4 | Predictive | Future enterprise states are forecast |

Organizations shall periodically assess cognitive maturity and define measurable improvement objectives for progressing toward higher levels.
