# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundred-and-nineteenth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-118 – Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Operational Resilience, Crisis Management, Recovery Planning, Testing & Resilience Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled third-party and supply-chain capability covering procurement governance, supplier governance, vendor lifecycle, contract lifecycle, third-party risk, supplier due diligence, supplier onboarding, supplier performance, SLA / OLA, contract obligations, supplier security, supplier privacy, supply-chain resilience, fourth-party dependencies, vendor incidents, vendor changes, contract renewal, exit planning, supplier assurance, third-party control testing, vendor risk exceptions and supply-chain quality gates.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must govern third-party relationships throughout their lifecycle so that suppliers, vendors, contracts and external dependencies are selected, onboarded, operated, monitored, changed and exited according to business need, risk, performance, security, privacy, resilience and assurance requirements.**

# 120. Definition of Ready

A third-party work item is Ready when:

- Supplier, procurement, contract, dependency, risk or assurance requirement is identified.
- Business owner and procurement ownership are established.
- Scope, criticality, data, security, privacy, resilience and financial exposure are understood.
- Due diligence, contracting, performance, testing, evidence and exit requirements are defined.

> **MFM must acquire external goods and services through controlled procurement that balances value, capability, risk, compliance and lifecycle cost.**

# 123. Final Supplier Principle

# 125. Final Third-Party Risk Principle

> **Third-party risk must be assessed according to the actual business, operational, financial, security, privacy and resilience impact of supplier dependency.**

---

# 126. Final Security Principle

> **Supplier access and supplier services must meet security requirements proportionate to the information, systems and privileges involved.**

---

# 127. Final Privacy Principle

> **Third-party processing of personal information must remain purposeful, controlled, contractually governed and consistent with applicable privacy requirements.**

---

# 128. Final Resilience Principle

> **Critical supplier dependencies must have appropriate continuity, recovery, alternative arrangements and assurance proportional to their impact.**

---

# 129. Final Exit Principle

> **MFM must maintain a realistic ability to transition away from material suppliers without uncontrolled loss of service, data, access or operational capability.**

---

# 130. Final Assurance Principle

> **Third-party assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that supplier controls, contractual commitments and service dependencies operate according to defined requirements.**

---

# 131. Final Integration Principle

> **Third-Party Governance must integrate with Procurement, Finance, Security, Privacy, Risk, Service Management, Architecture, Data, Resilience, Legal, Compliance and Enterprise Assurance governance.**
