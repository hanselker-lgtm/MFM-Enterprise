The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Self-Coordinating Enterprise Architecture Framework is to establish a self-coordinating enterprise architecture model where intelligence, knowledge, learning, governance and enterprise capabilities continuously coordinate their objectives, activities and resources.

- self-coordinating enterprise reference model

- self-coordinating intelligence architecture
- self-coordinating connectivity architecture
