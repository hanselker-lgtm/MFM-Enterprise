# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Availability Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Availability Management ensures that enterprise services remain available, resilient and capable of meeting agreed availability targets while preserving operational stability, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Availability Planning
- Availability Monitoring
- Service Availability
- Availability Targets
- Availability Reporting
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## AVL-001

Provide standardized enterprise availability management.

---

---

## AVL-003

## AVL-004

Support resilient enterprise operations.

---

## AVL-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Availability Management Principles

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Service Availability by Design
- Continuous Monitoring
- Proactive Risk Management
- Resilience
- Complete Traceability
- Operational Stability
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Availability Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

Enterprise Availability Management shall provide

- availability target management
- reporting

- compliance verification

Additional Availability Management responsibilities shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

# 8. Availability Planning

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall implement standardized availability planning.

Availability planning shall

- identify availability requirements
- identify critical business services
- identify availability risks
- identify resilience requirements
- preserve planning traceability
- maintain planning consistency

Availability planning shall remain centrally governed.

# 9. Availability Monitoring

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall implement standardized availability monitoring.

Availability monitoring shall

- monitor service availability
- monitor infrastructure availability
- monitor application availability
- monitor dependency availability
- preserve monitoring traceability
- maintain monitoring consistency

Availability monitoring policies shall remain centrally governed.

# 10. Service Availability

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall implement standardized service availability management.

Service availability management shall

- maintain agreed service availability
- identify availability degradation
- support rapid service restoration
- preserve service availability traceability
- maintain operational consistency
- support business continuity

Service availability shall remain continuously monitored.

# 11. Availability Targets

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall implement standardized availability target management.

Availability target management shall

- define service availability objectives
- define acceptable downtime
- define recovery expectations
- preserve target traceability
- maintain target consistency
- support enterprise service management

Availability targets shall align with approved service level agreements.

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall implement standardized availability verification.

Availability verification shall

- verify service availability
- verify monitoring effectiveness
- verify target achievement
- preserve verification traceability
- maintain verification consistency
- support operational reliability

Availability verification shall be performed regularly.

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall implement standardized availability improvement.

Availability improvement shall

- identify recurring availability issues

- improve monitoring effectiveness
- reduce downtime
- preserve improvement traceability
- support continuous improvement

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall handle availability management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify availability planning failures
- classify monitoring failures
- classify service availability failures
- classify availability target failures
- classify verification failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Availability Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational stability, service resilience, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Availability Management implementations may depend upon

- approved monitoring platforms
- approved alert management services
- approved incident management services
- approved capacity management services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external availability management providers

Availability Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Availability Management implementation is compliant when

- Availability planning is documented.
- Availability monitoring is operational.
- Service availability is continuously monitored.
- Availability targets are maintained.
- Availability verification is performed.
- Availability improvement activities are documented.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Reactive Availability Management

Availability improvements shall never occur only after repeated service failures.

---

## Missing Availability Monitoring

Critical enterprise services shall never operate without approved availability monitoring.

---

## Undefined Availability Targets

Business-critical services shall never operate without documented availability objectives.

---

## Ignored Availability Degradation

Availability degradation shall never remain unresolved without approved corrective actions.

---

## Unverified Availability Reporting

Availability reports shall never be published without verification of measurement accuracy.

---

## Availability Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent availability management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Availability Management services.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise Availability Management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- planning compliance

- documentation completeness
- operational readiness
- governance compliance
- audit compliance
- compliance with enterprise standards

The Enterprise Availability Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Availability Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.
