# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Automation Architecture governing automated business processes, infrastructure automation, operational automation, workflow orchestration and intelligent automation across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Automation Architecture shall

- improve operational efficiency
- eliminate repetitive manual activities
- improve process consistency
- reduce operational risk
- strengthen governance
- improve service delivery
- enable intelligent orchestration
- support continuous optimization
- integrate with Enterprise AI capabilities
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Automation shall function as a strategic Enterprise capability supporting digital transformation.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- business process automation
- workflow automation
- infrastructure automation
- operational automation
- robotic process automation (RPA)
- orchestration
- intelligent automation
- event-driven automation
- scheduled automation
- policy-based automation

The standard applies regardless of application architecture, infrastructure platform or cloud provider.

---

# 3. Enterprise Automation Principles

Enterprise Automation Architecture shall be governed by the following principles.

## Automation by Design

Automation shall be considered during the design of every Enterprise solution.

---

## Standardized Automation

Automation capabilities shall use standardized Enterprise automation models wherever practical.

---

## Event-Driven Automation

Automation shall preferably be initiated by Enterprise events rather than manual intervention.

---

## Policy-Based Execution

Automation shall execute according to approved Enterprise governance policies.

---

## Secure Automation

Automation shall comply with Enterprise Security Architecture and Zero Trust principles.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Automation Architecture shall remain independent of individual automation products and vendors.
