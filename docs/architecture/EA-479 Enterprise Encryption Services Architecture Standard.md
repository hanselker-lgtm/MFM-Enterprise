# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Encryption Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Key Management Services principles are inherited from EA-477.

Enterprise Certificate Management Services principles are inherited from EA-478.

Enterprise Secrets Management Services principles are inherited from EA-476.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Encryption Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Encryption Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed encryption capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Encryption Services shall

- provide centralized encryption management
- standardize encryption capabilities
- strengthen data protection
- reduce data exposure risks
- support regulatory compliance
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure encryption traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Encryption Services shall provide trusted and reusable encryption capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Encryption Services Principles

Enterprise Encryption Services shall follow these principles.

## Encryption by Design

Encryption capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Sensitive data shall be encrypted according to approved Enterprise security requirements throughout storage, processing and transmission.

---

## Data Protection by Design

Encryption shall be applied according to data classification, business requirements, regulatory obligations and security risk.

---

## Cryptographic Governance

Encryption algorithms, cryptographic mechanisms and encryption practices shall be governed through approved Enterprise standards.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Encryption Services shall remain independent of vendors, encryption platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

Enterprise Encryption Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed encryption capabilities across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating data encryption, application encryption, database encryption, file encryption, communication encryption, encryption policy management, encryption algorithm management, key management integration, certificate integration, secrets management integration, credential management integration, data protection integration, identity integration, authentication integration, authorization integration, risk, audit, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables secure, scalable and policy-driven data protection while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.
