# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Data Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise data intelligence capabilities.

The Data Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- data intelligence principles
- data intelligence governance
- data intelligence strategy
- data intelligence capabilities
- data analytics architecture
- business intelligence architecture
- advanced analytics architecture
- predictive analytics architecture
- data science architecture
- knowledge architecture
- data intelligence security
- data intelligence operations
- data intelligence performance management
- data intelligence evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise data is transformed into trusted information, actionable insights and intelligent decision support.

---

# 2. Scope

The Data Intelligence Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture data intelligence domains:

- Enterprise Data Intelligence Model
- Data Intelligence Governance
- Data Intelligence Strategy
- Data Intelligence Capabilities
- Data Analytics Architecture
- Business Intelligence Architecture
- Advanced Analytics Architecture
- Predictive Analytics Architecture
- Data Science Architecture
- Knowledge Architecture
- Data Intelligence Security
- Data Intelligence Operations
- Data Intelligence Performance
- Data Intelligence Maturity
- Data Intelligence Roadmaps
- Data Intelligence Evolution

The framework applies to:

- business intelligence solutions
- analytics platforms
- reporting capabilities
- data science activities
- predictive analytics
- knowledge management
- AI-supported decision systems
- enterprise data insights

---

# 3. Enterprise Data Intelligence Architecture Principles

The Data Intelligence Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Data Driven Decision Making

Enterprise decisions shall be supported by trusted data and meaningful insights.

---

## Principle 2 – Data Value Creation

Data shall be managed as a strategic Enterprise asset.

---

## Principle 3 – Trusted Information

Data intelligence capabilities shall rely on accurate, consistent and governed information.

---

## Principle 4 – Analytical Transparency

Analytics results shall be understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 5 – Business Alignment

Data intelligence capabilities shall support business objectives and Enterprise capabilities.

---

## Principle 6 – Responsible Data Usage

Data intelligence shall respect security, privacy and regulatory requirements.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Intelligence Evolution

Data intelligence capabilities shall continuously evolve according to business and technology needs.

---
