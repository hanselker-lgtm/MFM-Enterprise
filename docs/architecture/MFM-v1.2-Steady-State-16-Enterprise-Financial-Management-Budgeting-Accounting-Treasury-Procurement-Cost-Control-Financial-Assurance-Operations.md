# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the sixteenth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-15 – Enterprise Risk Management, Compliance, Regulatory Obligations, Internal Control, Audit & Assurance Operations.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise financial-management operating model for MFM, covering financial governance, budgeting, accounting, chart of accounts, accounts payable, accounts receivable, cash management, treasury, procurement, purchasing, commitments, cost control, financial reporting, reconciliations, financial controls, financial planning, forecasting and financial assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM financial information must be accurate, complete, timely, controlled, traceable and fit for decision-making, reporting, stewardship and compliance throughout the financial lifecycle.**

# 154. Definition of Ready

A financial-management work item is Ready when:

- The financial process, transaction, budget, forecast, supplier, customer/member, reconciliation, control, audit item or reporting requirement is clearly identified.
- Ownership, authority, financial impact and applicable requirements are known.
- Approval, evidence, accounting, control, reporting and acceptance criteria are defined.
