# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Innovation Enablement Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed capabilities that enable innovation execution, experimentation, prototyping, collaboration and technology adoption across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Innovation Enablement Services shall

- provide centralized innovation enablement capabilities
- provide innovation execution environments
- support innovation platforms
- support innovation laboratories
- support innovation workspaces
- support innovation sandbox environments
- support experimentation capabilities
- support prototype development environments
- support proof-of-concept execution
- support innovation toolchains
- support innovation collaboration platforms
- support innovation development environments
- support innovation DevSecOps capabilities
- support artificial intelligence innovation enablement
- support data innovation enablement
- support automation innovation enablement
- support knowledge innovation enablement
- support innovation measurement platforms
- support innovation environment governance
- improve innovation execution capability
- accelerate innovation delivery
- reduce innovation implementation barriers
- improve experimentation effectiveness
- improve technology adoption
- support secure innovation practices
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Service Innovation Enablement Services shall provide trusted and reusable enablement capabilities supporting Enterprise innovation activities.
