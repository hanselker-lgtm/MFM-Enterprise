# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the seventy-eighth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-77 – Enterprise Workplace, End-User Computing, Devices, Collaboration, Productivity & Digital Employee Experience Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for service management, IT service management, service governance, service desk, incident management, major incident management, service request management, problem management, change enablement, change assessment, change approval, release management, deployment coordination, service configuration management, service catalog management, service level management, service availability management, service continuity coordination, knowledge management, event management coordination, service monitoring coordination, service reporting, service measurement, customer experience, supplier service management, service assurance, service findings, service exceptions, service remediation, service metrics, service dashboards, service maturity and continual service improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must provide reliable, measurable and customer-focused services through standardized service management practices that restore service quickly, fulfill requests consistently, control change and continuously improve service quality.**

# 3. Service Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure business services are delivered consistently, measurably and according to agreed requirements.**

# 4. ITSM Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide a controlled operating framework for planning, delivering, supporting and improving technology-enabled services.**

# 5. Service Desk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide a single, accessible and accountable point of contact for service users and coordinate restoration and fulfillment.**

# 6. Incident Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore normal service operation as quickly as possible while minimizing business impact.**

# 7. Problem Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and address underlying causes of incidents and reduce recurrence and business impact.**

# 8. Change Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable beneficial changes while controlling risk, disruption and unintended consequences.**

# 9. Release Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Coordinate the introduction of new or changed service components into controlled environments and production.**

# 10. Service Level Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Ensure service performance is defined, measured, reviewed and improved against agreed business requirements.**
