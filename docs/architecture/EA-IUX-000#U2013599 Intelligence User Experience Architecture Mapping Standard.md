# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence User Experience Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive user experience architecture for interaction between humans and Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence User Experience Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence user experience principles
- UX governance
- user experience architecture
- human intelligence interaction
- human-AI collaboration
- intelligent user interfaces
- conversational intelligence interfaces
- adaptive experience architecture
- accessibility architecture
- user journey architecture
- experience analytics
- trust and transparency experience
- personalisation architecture
- experience security
- experience performance
- experience evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are delivered through effective, understandable, accessible and trustworthy experiences.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence User Experience Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture experience domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence User Experience Model
- UX Governance
- UX Architecture
- Human Intelligence Interaction
- Human-AI Collaboration
- Intelligent User Interfaces

- Adaptive Experience Architecture
- User Journey Architecture

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- intelligent operating environments
- future adaptive enterprises

# 3. Intelligence User Experience Architecture Principles

The Intelligence User Experience Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Human-Centred Intelligence

Intelligent capabilities shall be designed around human needs and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Trust by Design

User experiences shall promote understanding, confidence and trust.

---

## Principle 3 – Transparency

Intelligent systems shall provide understandable interactions and explanations.

---

## Principle 4 – Accessibility

Experiences shall be available to all authorised users.

---

## Principle 5 – Adaptive Experience

Experiences shall adapt to changing user needs and contexts.

---

## Principle 6 – Responsible Interaction

Human and intelligent system interaction shall be controlled and responsible.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Experience Evolution

User experiences shall continuously improve.
