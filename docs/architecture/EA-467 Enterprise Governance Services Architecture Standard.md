# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Governance Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Policy Services principles are inherited from EA-465.

Enterprise Compliance Services principles are inherited from EA-466.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Governance Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Governance Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, transparent and standardized governance capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Governance Services shall

- provide centralized governance management
- standardize governance processes
- support enterprise-wide decision making
- strengthen accountability
- improve organizational transparency
- enable enterprise automation
- support interoperability
- improve risk management
- strengthen regulatory compliance
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Governance Services shall provide trusted and reusable governance capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise governance services
- governance management
- governance frameworks
- architecture governance
- decision governance
- policy governance
- compliance governance
- risk governance
- control management
- governance lifecycle management

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, organizational structure or infrastructure platform.

# 3. Enterprise Governance Services Principles

Enterprise Governance Services shall follow these principles.

## Governance by Design

Governance requirements shall be embedded into Enterprise Architecture, business capabilities, technology platforms and operational processes from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every governance activity shall be authenticated, authorized, audited and continuously monitored.

---

## Transparent Governance

Governance decisions, approvals, responsibilities and accountability shall be transparent, traceable and documented.

---

## Metadata Driven Governance

Governance information shall be described, classified and governed using Enterprise metadata to ensure discoverability, traceability and consistency.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Governance Services shall remain independent of vendors, governance platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Governance Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed Enterprise governance across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating governance management, governance frameworks, architecture governance, decision governance, policy governance, compliance governance, risk governance, control management, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables transparent, accountable and scalable governance while maintaining compliance, operational excellence and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Governance Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise governance capabilities across the Enterprise.
