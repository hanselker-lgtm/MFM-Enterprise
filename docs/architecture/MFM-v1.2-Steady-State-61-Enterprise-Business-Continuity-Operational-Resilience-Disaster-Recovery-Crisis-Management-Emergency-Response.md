# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the sixty-first document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-60 – Enterprise Supplier, Contract, Vendor, Third-Party, Outsourcing & Strategic Partner Management.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for business continuity, operational resilience, business impact analysis, continuity strategies, continuity plans, disaster recovery, technology recovery, crisis management, emergency response, incident escalation, recovery priorities, dependencies, alternate operating arrangements, communications, exercises, testing, recovery validation, continuity assurance, continuity findings, exceptions, remediation, resilience metrics, continuity reporting, maturity and continual resilience improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must remain capable of delivering critical services and protecting people, information, assets and organizational objectives during disruption, and must recover critical capabilities within approved resilience and recovery requirements.**

# 3. Business Continuity Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Maintain critical business services and activities during disruption and restore them within approved recovery requirements.**

# 4. Operational Resilience Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Enable MFM to anticipate, withstand, respond to, adapt to and recover from disruptive events.**

# 5. Disaster Recovery Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Restore technology, applications, infrastructure and data required for critical services within approved recovery objectives.**

# 6. Crisis Management Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide coordinated leadership, decision-making and communication during major disruptive events.**

# 7. Emergency Response Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Protect people, stabilize the situation and establish controlled response and recovery arrangements during emergencies.**

# 8. Continuity Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide evidence that continuity and recovery capabilities remain effective, tested and aligned with approved requirements.**

# 103. Definition of Ready

A continuity plan, BIA, recovery capability, crisis exercise, disaster-recovery activity, supplier continuity arrangement, finding, exception or improvement item is Ready when scope, criticality, owner, dependencies, recovery requirements, resources, communications, acceptance criteria and evidence requirements are defined.
