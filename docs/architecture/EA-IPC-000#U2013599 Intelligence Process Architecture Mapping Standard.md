# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Process Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive process architecture for designing, managing, optimising and evolving intelligent Enterprise processes.

The Intelligence Process Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence process principles
- process governance
- process architecture
- intelligent process design
- process automation
- process intelligence
- process mining
- adaptive process management
- decision process architecture
- workflow intelligence
- process optimisation
- process performance analytics
- process security
- process evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are transformed into measurable, efficient and adaptive business processes.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Process Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture process domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Process Model
- Process Governance
- Process Architecture
- Intelligent Process Design
- Process Automation Architecture
- Process Intelligence
- Process Mining
- Adaptive Process Management
- Decision Process Architecture
- Workflow Intelligence
- Process Optimisation
- Process Performance Analytics
- Process Security
- Process Maturity
- Process Roadmaps
- Process Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- intelligent operating environments
- future adaptive enterprises

# 3. Intelligence Process Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Process Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Process Value Creation

Intelligent processes shall create measurable Enterprise value.

---

## Principle 2 – Process Intelligence

Processes shall use intelligence to improve effectiveness and efficiency.

---

## Principle 3 – Process Automation

Suitable process activities shall be automated where beneficial.

---

## Principle 4 – Adaptive Processes

Processes shall adapt to changing requirements and conditions.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Improvement

Processes shall continuously evolve and improve.

---

## Principle 6 – Process Transparency

Process activities and decisions shall be visible and understandable.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Process Execution

Intelligent processes shall operate securely and responsibly.
