# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive adaptation architecture for enabling Enterprise Intelligence capabilities to continuously respond, adjust and evolve according to changing internal and external conditions.

The Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Framework shall provide:

- adaptation principles
- adaptation governance
- adaptive architecture
- context awareness
- dynamic capability adaptation
- autonomous adaptation
- environmental intelligence
- adaptive decision models
- adaptation measurement
- adaptation maturity
- adaptation evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities remain responsive, resilient and continuously aligned with changing requirements.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture adaptation domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Adaptation Model
- Adaptation Governance
- Adaptive Architecture
- Context Management
- Dynamic Capability Management
- Autonomous Adaptation
- Environmental Intelligence
- Real-Time Adaptation
- Adaptive Decision Architecture
- Adaptation Metrics
- Adaptation Maturity
- Adaptation Roadmaps
- Adaptation Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- adaptive enterprises
- autonomous systems
- continuously evolving Intelligence environments

---

# 3. Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Adaptation Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Continuous Adaptation

Enterprise Intelligence shall continuously adjust to changing conditions.

---

## Principle 2 – Context Awareness

Adaptation shall be based on understanding operational context.

---

## Principle 3 – Dynamic Capability Evolution

Capabilities shall evolve according to changing requirements.

---

## Principle 4 – Controlled Adaptation

Adaptation activities shall follow governance and control mechanisms.

---

## Principle 5 – Real-Time Responsiveness

Intelligence capabilities shall respond rapidly when required.

---

## Principle 6 – Learning-Based Adaptation

Adaptation shall use knowledge, experience and feedback.

---

## Principle 7 – Sustainable Evolution

Adaptation shall support long-term Enterprise development.
