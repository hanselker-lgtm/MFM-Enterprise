### Governing Architecture: EA-IMETA-MASTER-01

## Purpose
Establish the authoritative role layer beneath mandate, defining the purpose, scope, responsibilities, competence, decision rights, boundaries, accountability, delegation, independence, segregation of duties and continuity requirements for roles participating in post-closure regression governance.

## Core Principle
A role is the governed organizational construct through which a mandate is performed. A role does not create authority by itself; its authority derives from an approved mandate and governance framework.

```text
MANDATE
   ↓
ROLE
   ↓
PURPOSE
   ↓
SCOPE
   ↓
RESPONSIBILITIES
   ↓
COMPETENCE
   ↓
DECISION RIGHTS
   ↓
BOUNDARIES
   ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
   ↓
DELEGATION / CONTINUITY
```

## Role Quality Test
```text
VALID MANDATE
+
IDENTIFIED ROLE
+
DEFINED RESPONSIBILITIES
+
REQUIRED COMPETENCE
+
EXPLICIT DECISION RIGHTS
+
DEFINED BOUNDARIES
+
ACCOUNTABILITY
+
CONTINUITY
=
VALID GOVERNED ROLE
```

## Role Structure
| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Role Purpose | Why the role exists |
| Role Scope | Services, processes, domains and lifecycle states covered |
| Responsibilities | Outcomes and obligations owned |
| Competence | Knowledge, skills, experience and qualifications required |
| Decision Rights | Decisions the role may recommend, approve or authorize |
| Boundaries | Decisions and activities outside the role |
| Conditions | Preconditions for exercising the role |
| Accountability | Outcome owner for the role |
| Delegation | Permitted transfer of defined responsibilities or rights |
| Independence | Required separation from conflicting activities |
| Continuity | Succession and coverage requirements |

## Role Purpose
The role purpose shall describe the outcome the role exists to achieve within the mandate. It shall be expressed as an accountable governance or operational function, not merely as an individual job title.

## Role Scope
```text
ROLE SCOPE
 ├── ORGANIZATION
 ├── SERVICE
 ├── PROCESS
 ├── DATA
 ├── TECHNOLOGY
 ├── RISK DOMAIN
 ├── LIFECYCLE STATE
 └── DECISION DOMAIN```

Scope shall prevent role ambiguity and uncontrolled expansion.

## Role Responsibilities
Responsibilities shall distinguish accountability from participation.

```text
ACCOUNTABLE
    ↓
RESPONSIBLE
    ↓
CONSULTED
    ↓
INFORMED```

Participation in an activity does not automatically create accountability for its outcome.

## Competence and Capability
Material roles shall define required knowledge, skills, experience, training, certification where applicable and practical capability.

```text
ROLE
 ↓
COMPETENCE REQUIREMENTS
 ↓
ASSESS
 ↓
QUALIFIED?
 ├── YES → ASSIGN / CONTINUE
 └── NO  → TRAIN / REASSIGN / ESCALATE```

## Decision Rights
Role decision rights shall remain within mandate boundaries.

```text
RECOMMEND
   ≠
REVIEW
   ≠
APPROVE
   ≠
AUTHORIZE
   ≠
EXECUTE```

## Role Boundaries
Out-of-scope actions shall be stopped or escalated. A role shall not infer authority from access, seniority or technical capability.

## Role Conditions
Conditions may include active appointment, competence status, independence, certification, organizational assignment, risk threshold and valid delegation.

```text
ROLE ACTIVE
   ↓
CONDITIONS VALID?
 ├── YES → OPERATE
 └── NO  → SUSPEND / REASSIGN / REVIEW```

## Accountability
Every material role shall have an accountable owner or accountable organizational function. Shared work does not remove the need for a single accountable outcome owner.

## Delegation
Delegation shall define the specific responsibility or decision right transferred, scope, duration, limits, conditions, reporting and revocation.

```text
ROLE OWNER
   ↓
DELEGATION
   ↓
DEFINED RIGHT / RESPONSIBILITY
   ↓
LIMITS
   ↓
REPORT / REVIEW```

## Independence
Roles performing independent assurance, challenge, audit or verification shall be separated from conflicting operational responsibilities where required by materiality and governance policy.

## Segregation of Duties
Critical combinations of incompatible duties shall be separated where combining them could create unacceptable self-approval, fraud, error, control failure or governance bias.

```text
PROPOSE  ≠APPROVE  ≠VERIFY  ≠AUDIT```

Where organizational scale prevents full separation, compensating controls and independent review shall be defined.

## Escalation
Roles shall know when a decision exceeds their authority, competence, risk threshold or mandate.

```text
ROLE DECISION
      ↓
WITHIN ROLE / MANDATE?
 ├── YES → DECIDE / EXECUTE
 └── NO  → ESCALATE```

## Risk and Materiality
Role authority and independence requirements shall increase with risk, materiality, customer impact, security exposure, resilience impact and regulatory significance.

## Security and Resilience Role
Security and resilience roles shall have clear responsibility for protection, detection, response, recovery, continuity and escalation within their assigned mandate.

## Data Role
Data roles shall define responsibility for data quality, integrity, access, lineage, classification, retention and authorized use as applicable.

## AI and Agent Role
AI and agent roles shall be explicitly defined. Technical autonomy shall not be mistaken for organizational accountability.

```text
AI / AGENT ROLE
      ↓
DEFINED PURPOSE
      ↓
DEFINED AUTHORITY
      ↓
DEFINED TOOL / DATA SCOPE
      ↓
DEFINED AUTONOMY
      ↓
DEFINED STOP CONDITIONS
      ↓
ACCOUNTABLE HUMAN / GOVERNANCE ROLE```

## Compliance Role
Compliance roles shall define responsibility for interpretation, monitoring, advice, evidence, escalation and formal compliance decisions where applicable.

## Succession and Continuity
Material roles shall have continuity arrangements so that absence, vacancy or organizational change does not create uncontrolled governance or operational gaps.

```text
PRIMARY ROLE
   ↓
BACKUP / SUCCESSOR
   ↓
QUALIFICATION CHECK
   ↓
ACTIVATION
   ↓
RETURN / HANDOVER```

## Role Evidence
Role evidence shall demonstrate appointment, mandate mapping, competence, authority, delegation, independence requirements, effective period and relevant changes.

## Role Review
Role design shall be reviewed after organizational change, mandate change, repeated regression, material incident, regulatory change, technology change or loss of competence.

```text
ROLE ACTIVE
   ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
   ↓
VALID?
 ├── YES → RENEW
 └── NO  → MODIFY / REASSIGN / RETIRE```

## Role Failure
```text
ROLE UNCLEAR / VACANT / UNQUALIFIED
        ↓
STOP MATERIAL DECISION WHERE REQUIRED
        ↓
IDENTIFY QUALIFIED ROLE
        ↓
ASSIGN / DELEGATE / ESCALATE
        ↓
RECORD```

## Governance Learning
Repeated role failures shall trigger review of organizational structure, competence requirements, segregation of duties, succession, delegation and accountability.

This document specializes the role layer beneath mandate and authority for post-closure regression governance. It does not replace the governance, authority, mandate or stage-specific regression documents.
