# 1. Purpose

This document defines the eighty-sixth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-85 – Customer, Member, Citizen, User Experience, Engagement, Relationship Management & Experience Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled communications capability covering corporate communications, internal communications, external communications, public information, marketing governance, outreach, campaign management, audience management, message governance, brand governance, content governance, communication channels, media management, digital communications, communication analytics, campaign performance, reputation management and communication assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that organizational communications are purposeful, accurate, consistent, accessible, timely, appropriately governed and measurable, while protecting organizational reputation and maintaining trust across internal and external audiences.**
