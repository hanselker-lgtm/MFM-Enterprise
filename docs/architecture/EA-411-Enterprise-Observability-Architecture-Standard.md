# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Observability Architecture governing telemetry, monitoring, logging, metrics, distributed tracing and operational intelligence across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Observability Architecture shall

- improve operational visibility
- enable proactive monitoring
- support rapid incident response
- improve system reliability
- strengthen operational governance
- enable data-driven operations
- improve service performance
- support continuous optimization
- integrate with Enterprise Security
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Observability shall function as the standardized operational intelligence capability for the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- logging
- metrics
- distributed tracing
- telemetry
- OpenTelemetry
- dashboards
- alerting
- Service Level Indicators (SLIs)
- Service Level Objectives (SLOs)
- operational analytics

The standard applies regardless of application architecture, infrastructure platform or cloud provider.

---

# 3. Enterprise Observability Principles

Enterprise Observability Architecture shall be governed by the following principles.

## Observability by Design

Observability shall be designed into every Enterprise solution from the beginning.

---

## Unified Telemetry

Logs, metrics and traces shall be collected using standardized Enterprise telemetry models.

---

## Open Standards

OpenTelemetry and other open standards shall be preferred wherever practical.

---

## Automation First

Collection, analysis and alerting shall be automated wherever practical.

---

## Actionable Insights

Observability data shall support operational decision-making and rapid remediation.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Observability Architecture shall remain independent of specific observability vendors.
