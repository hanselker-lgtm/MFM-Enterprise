# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-004 establishes the controlled mechanism for taking materially relevant signals from the intake boundary and preparing them for structured governance assessment and decision.

The operating sequence is:

```text
MATERIAL SIGNAL
↓
GOVERNANCE ROUTING
↓
ASSESSMENT
↓
OPTIONS
↓
RECOMMENDATION
↓
DECISION PREPARATION
↓
AUTHORITY ROUTING
```

STEADY-STATE-004 prepares decisions; it does not itself exercise decision authority unless such authority is explicitly assigned through the applicable governance structure.

---

# 2. Scope

The control applies to signals routed from:

```text
STEADY-STATE-003
Governance Signal Intake & Materiality Assessment
```

and may involve:

```text
GB-01 Strategic Monitoring

GB-03 Capability Governance
GB-04 Portfolio Governance
GB-05 Investment Governance
GB-06 Risk Governance
GB-07 Dependency Governance
GB-08 Change Governance
GB-09 Outcome Governance
GB-10 Value Governance
GB-11 Evidence Governance
GB-12 Decision Governance
```

---

# 3. Routing Principle

Routing shall be based on:

```text
Materiality
Domain
Impact
Authority
Urgency
Cross-Domain Effect
Risk
Dependency
```

The principle remains:

```text
NON-MATERIAL
→ Local / Existing Governance

MATERIAL
→ Relevant Domain Governance

CROSS-DOMAIN MATERIAL
→ Integrated Governance

MAJOR / CRITICAL
→ Appropriate Senior Authority
```

---

# 4. Routing Record

Each routed item should retain:

```text
Routing ID
Signal ID
Primary Domain
Affected Domains
Materiality
Urgency
Owner
Required Authority
Recommended Governance Forum
Evidence
Status
```

No routing decision shall invent missing ownership or authority.

---

# 5. Primary and Secondary Domains

The assessment shall identify:

```text
Primary Governance Domain
```

and, where applicable:

```text
Secondary / Affected Governance Domains
```

Where cross-domain impact is material, integrated assessment shall be used.

---

# 6. Routing Authority

Routing authority shall be distinguished from decision authority.

```text
Routing
≠
Decision

Decision
≠
Authorization
```

The routing mechanism may recommend the appropriate authority but shall not assume that recommendation constitutes approval.



# 41. Final STEADY-STATE-004 Statement

> **STEADY-STATE-004 establishes the controlled routing, assessment and decision-preparation mechanism for materially relevant governance signals. It preserves the distinctions between evidence, assumptions, assessment, recommendation, decision and authorization, and ensures that risks, dependencies, options, trade-offs, authority and conditions are visible before a decision is presented to the appropriate authority. It does not itself authorize funding, implementation or a new phase. N8 and N9 remain CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS, while N10 remains NOT DEFINED and NOT AUTHORIZED.**
