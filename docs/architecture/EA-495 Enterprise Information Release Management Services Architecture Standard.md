# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Release Management Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed planning, coordination, validation and delivery of Enterprise information releases across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Release Management Services shall

- provide centralized release management capabilities
- establish controlled release processes
- coordinate information releases
- support release governance
- support release planning
- integrate change management
- integrate configuration management
- integrate dependency management
- integrate lineage management
- improve release quality
- reduce operational risks
- support compliance requirements
- support audit requirements
- protect information quality
- support lifecycle governance
- support master information releases
- support reference information releases
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure release traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Release Management Services shall provide trusted and reusable release management capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

# 3. Enterprise Information Release Management Services Principles

Enterprise Information Release Management Services shall follow these principles.

## Controlled Release by Design

Enterprise information releases shall be planned, approved and executed through defined governance processes.

---

## Quality-Assured Release Management

Information releases shall be validated to ensure accuracy, completeness, integrity and compliance.

---

## Traceable Release History

All releases shall maintain complete records of ownership, approval, execution and verification.

---

## Integrated Release Management

Release activities shall integrate with change, configuration, dependency and lineage management capabilities.

---

## Business and Technical Alignment

Release management shall connect business requirements with technical implementation and operational activities.
