# 1. Purpose

This document defines the ninety-second implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-91 – Analytics, Reporting, Business Intelligence, Management Information, Decision Support & Performance Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise architecture capability covering enterprise architecture governance, business architecture, information architecture alignment, application architecture, technology architecture, solution architecture, architecture principles, architecture standards, application portfolio, technology portfolio, architecture decisions, architecture review, solution review, technical standards, architecture exceptions, application rationalization, technology rationalization, target architecture, transition architecture, architecture roadmap, architecture debt and architecture assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that business, information, application and technology decisions remain architecturally coherent, governed, reusable, supportable and aligned with strategic direction throughout the enterprise lifecycle.**

# 130. Final Architecture Principle

> **Architecture must connect business direction to capabilities, information, applications, technology and change rather than operate as an isolated technical discipline.**

---

# 131. Final Capability Principle

> **Capabilities should provide the stable business context against which application, technology and investment decisions are evaluated.**

---

# 132. Final Application Principle

> **Applications must be actively managed as a portfolio across value, cost, risk, lifecycle, dependency and strategic alignment.**

---

# 133. Final Technology Principle

> **Technology choices must be governed against approved standards, lifecycle considerations, security, resilience, cost and strategic direction.**

---

# 134. Final Solution Principle

> **Every material solution should make its context, options, trade-offs, architecture decisions and consequences sufficiently explicit.**

---

# 135. Final Decision Principle

> **Material architecture decisions must be recorded with sufficient context, rationale, authority and consequences to remain understandable over time.**

---

# 136. Final Exception Principle

> **Architecture exceptions must be explicit, risk-assessed, approved, time-bounded where appropriate and subject to review.**

---

# 137. Final Target-State Principle

> **Target architecture must be achievable through defined transition states, dependencies, milestones and accountable ownership.**

---

# 138. Final Debt Principle

> **Architecture debt must be visible and actively prioritized according to business impact, risk, cost and strategic importance.**

---

# 139. Final Assurance Principle

> **Architecture assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that implemented solutions remain aligned with approved architecture, standards and organizational requirements.**

---

# 140. Final Integration Principle

> **Enterprise Architecture must integrate with Data, Security, Privacy, Procurement, Finance, Service Management, Change, Projects, Risk, Compliance, Asset, Configuration and Enterprise Assurance governance.**

---

# 141. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage architecture through a controlled lifecycle connecting business capabilities, information, applications, technology, solution decisions, standards, portfolios, roadmaps, exceptions, architecture debt and continuous assurance.**
