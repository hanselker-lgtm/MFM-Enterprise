# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Analytics Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Infrastructure Layer principles are inherited from EA-320.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-355.

All Enterprise Analytics implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Architecture governing analytical capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Analytics Architecture shall

- transform Enterprise data into actionable information
- support operational and strategic decision-making
- enable trusted business insights
- provide standardized analytical services
- improve organizational performance
- support predictive capabilities
- remain technology independent

Analytics shall be based on governed Enterprise information and standardized architectural principles.

# 17. Enterprise Analytics Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Duplicate KPI Definitions

The same KPI shall never be calculated differently across multiple systems.

Enterprise KPI definitions shall be centrally governed.

---

## Shadow Analytics

Business units shall not establish unmanaged analytical solutions outside Enterprise Governance.

Analytical capabilities shall be implemented using approved Enterprise platforms.

---

## Spreadsheet-Driven Analytics

Mission-critical analytics shall never depend solely upon unmanaged spreadsheets.

Enterprise analytical services shall remain authoritative.

---

## Untraceable Calculations

Analytical results shall always be traceable back to governed Enterprise data sources.

Every calculation shall be documented and reproducible.

---

## Inconsistent Metrics

Enterprise Metrics shall never have conflicting definitions across departments.

Metric ownership shall be clearly assigned.

---

## Vendor Lock-In

Enterprise Analytics shall never depend upon proprietary reporting or analytics technologies that prevent architectural portability.

Analytical logic shall remain technology independent.
