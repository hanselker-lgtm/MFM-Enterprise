## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-effectiveness layer defining how the organization determines whether a mandatory response achieved its intended control objective, reduced or contained the relevant risk, and restored or protected the required state.

## Core Principle
Completion of a response does not prove effectiveness. A response is effective only when objective evidence demonstrates that the intended mandatory outcome or protected state has been achieved within approved criteria and thresholds.

```text
MANDATORY RESPONSE
      ↓
EFFECTIVENESS OBJECTIVE
      ↓
EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA
      ↓
MEASURE / OBSERVE
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
COMPARE WITH BASELINE / THRESHOLD
      ↓
EFFECTIVE / PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE / INEFFECTIVE / UNDETERMINED
      ↓
REASSESS / ESCALATE / CLOSE
```

## Effectiveness Quality Test
```text
DEFINED RESPONSE
+
DEFINED CONTROL OBJECTIVE
+
EXPLICIT EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA
+
VALID MEASUREMENT
+
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
+
APPROPRIATE BASELINE / THRESHOLD
+
TIMELY ASSESSMENT
+
ATTRIBUTABLE RESULT
+
DEFINED CONSEQUENCE OF INEFFECTIVENESS
=
VALID GOVERNED EFFECTIVENESS DETERMINATION
```

## Effectiveness Status Model
```text
NOT ASSESSED
PLANNED
ASSESSING
PARTIALLY ASSESSED
EFFECTIVE
PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE
INEFFECTIVE
UNDETERMINED
REQUIRES REASSESSMENT
ESCALATED
SUPERSEDED
UNDER REVIEW
```

## Effectiveness Invariants

```text
EVERY MATERIAL MANDATORY RESPONSE SHALL HAVE DEFINED EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA
```

```text
RESPONSE COMPLETION SHALL NOT AUTOMATICALLY MEAN EFFECTIVENESS
```

```text
EFFECTIVENESS SHALL BE ASSESSED AGAINST THE INTENDED CONTROL OBJECTIVE
```

```text
EFFECTIVENESS SHALL BE SUPPORTED BY TRACEABLE EVIDENCE
```

```text
EFFECTIVENESS MEASUREMENT SHALL BE APPROPRIATE TO THE RESPONSE AND RISK
```

```text
BASELINES AND THRESHOLDS SHALL BE EXPLICIT WHERE REQUIRED
```

```text
TIMING SHALL ALLOW SUFFICIENT OBSERVATION OF THE RESPONSE EFFECT
```

```text
PARTIAL EFFECTIVENESS SHALL NOT BE SILENTLY TREATED AS FULL EFFECTIVENESS
```

```text
INEFFECTIVENESS SHALL TRIGGER DEFINED ESCALATION OR ALTERNATIVE RESPONSE
```

```text
UNDETERMINED EFFECTIVENESS SHALL NOT BE TREATED AS EFFECTIVE
```

```text
SECURITY, RESILIENCE, SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE EFFECTIVENESS SHALL RECEIVE APPROPRIATE RIGOR
```

```text
AI AND AGENT EFFECTIVENESS SHALL INCLUDE GOVERNANCE-BOUNDARY EFFECTS WHERE APPLICABLE
```

```text
HISTORICAL EFFECTIVENESS DETERMINATIONS SHALL REMAIN PRESERVED
```

```text
CHANGES TO EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA SHALL BE GOVERNED AND VERSIONED
```

```text
REPEATED INEFFECTIVENESS SHALL TRIGGER GOVERNANCE LEARNING
```

## Effectiveness Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Response | Response being assessed |
| Objective | Intended control objective |
| Criteria | What effective means |
| Measurement | How effectiveness is assessed |
| Evidence | Proof of effect |
| Baseline | Reference condition |
| Threshold | Effectiveness boundary |
| Timing | Assessment window |
| Attribution | Basis for linking response to result |
| Determination | Effective / partial / ineffective / undetermined |
| Consequence | Required action if ineffective |

## Effectiveness Objective

The effectiveness objective shall state what the response was intended to achieve, prevent, contain, restore or protect.

## Effectiveness Definition

Effectiveness shall be defined in terms of observable or measurable achievement of the intended control objective, not merely completion of planned actions.

## Effectiveness Scope

Scope shall identify affected systems, services, controls, processes, data, environments, users, dependencies and observation period.

## Effectiveness Authority

The authority responsible for accepting the effectiveness determination shall be identifiable and distinct from operational convenience.

## Effectiveness Criteria

Criteria shall define the conditions that constitute effective, partially effective, ineffective and undetermined results.

```text
OBJECTIVE
↓
CRITERIA
├── EFFECTIVE
├── PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE
├── INEFFECTIVE
└── UNDETERMINED
```

## Effectiveness Measurement

Measurements shall be appropriate to the control objective and may include performance, risk, state, recovery, security, compliance, quality or behavioural indicators.

## Effectiveness Evidence

Evidence shall demonstrate that the response produced the intended effect and shall be traceable to the response, state, criteria and observation period.

## Effectiveness Baseline

A baseline shall establish the reference condition against which the response effect is evaluated. Historical baseline versions shall remain preserved.

## Effectiveness Thresholds

Effectiveness thresholds shall define decision boundaries and shall be versioned, authorized and effective-dated.

```text
MEASURED EFFECT
↓
THRESHOLD
├── EFFECTIVE
├── PARTIAL
└── INEFFECTIVE
```

## Effectiveness Timing

The assessment window shall be long enough to determine whether the response effect is real and stable. Immediate post-action success shall not automatically prove sustained effectiveness.

## Effectiveness Attribution

Where causality matters, the assessment shall consider whether the observed improvement can reasonably be attributed to the response rather than unrelated changes.

## Security Effectiveness

Security response effectiveness may include restored protection, reduced exposure, controlled access, remediated vulnerability or reduced incident impact as applicable.

## Resilience Effectiveness

Resilience response effectiveness may include achieved recovery objectives, restored service, improved continuity capability, stable recovery performance or controlled dependency effects.

## Compliance Effectiveness

Compliance response effectiveness shall demonstrate actual restoration of compliant state where required and shall not rely solely on administrative completion.

## Data Effectiveness

Data response effectiveness may include restored accuracy, integrity, completeness, lineage, authorized access or other required data state.

## AI and Agent Effectiveness

AI and agent response effectiveness shall assess whether the response restored the intended governed behaviour and authority boundary.

```text
AI / AGENT RESPONSE
↓
EFFECT OBSERVED
↓
WITHIN REQUIRED AUTHORITY / SAFETY / POLICY?
├── YES → EFFECTIVE
└── NO  → INEFFECTIVE / ESCALATE
```

## Effectiveness Failure

Ineffective or materially partial response shall trigger the defined alternative response, remediation or escalation.

```text
INEFFECTIVE
↓
PROTECT REQUIRED STATE
↓
ESCALATE
↓
ALTERNATIVE RESPONSE
↓
REASSESS
```

## Effectiveness Escalation

Escalation shall occur when effectiveness is absent, disputed, unstable, materially partial, delayed or insufficiently evidenced.

## Effectiveness Review and Learning

Effectiveness models shall be reviewed after repeated ineffective responses, unexpected outcomes, recurring regressions, incidents, changed risks or evidence that criteria no longer represent the intended objective.

## Effectiveness Determination Model
```text
RESPONSE COMPLETED
↓
CRITERIA VALID?
├── NO → GOVERNANCE GAP
└── YES
     ↓
EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT?
├── NO → UNDETERMINED
└── YES
     ↓
OBJECTIVE ACHIEVED?
├── YES → EFFECTIVE
├── PARTLY → PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE
└── NO → INEFFECTIVE
```

## Effectiveness Anti-Gaming Control
Effectiveness shall not be declared solely because an action was completed, a ticket was closed, a procedure was followed or a short-term metric improved. Criteria, evidence and observation period shall support the determination.

## Effectiveness Change Control
Changes to effectiveness criteria, metrics, baselines, thresholds or observation windows shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated. Historical determinations shall remain linked to the versions used at the time.

```text
CURRENT EFFECTIVENESS MODEL
↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
↓
NEW VERSION
↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```
