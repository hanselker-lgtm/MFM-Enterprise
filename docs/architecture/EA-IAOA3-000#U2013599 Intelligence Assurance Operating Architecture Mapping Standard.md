# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational assurance architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities are continuously validated, verified, controlled and improved.

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- assurance operating principles
- quality assurance frameworks
- compliance assurance structures
- validation models
- verification models
- control assurance structures
- audit frameworks
- performance assurance models
- risk assurance structures
- assurance monitoring structures
- assurance automation structures
- assurance evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for reliable, controlled and continuously improving Intelligence operations.

# 3. Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Assurance Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Assurance by Design

Assurance shall be integrated into Intelligence capabilities from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Continuous Validation

Intelligence capabilities shall be continuously validated.

---

## Principle 3 – Independent Verification

Critical Intelligence functions shall support independent verification.

---

## Principle 4 – Evidence-Based Assurance

Assurance decisions shall be based on documented evidence.

---

## Principle 5 – Controlled Operation

Intelligence operations shall operate within defined controls.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Improvement

Assurance capability shall continuously improve.

---

## Principle 7 – Transparent Assurance

Assurance results shall be understandable and traceable.
