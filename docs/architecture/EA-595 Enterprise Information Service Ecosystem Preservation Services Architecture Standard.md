# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Preservation Services Architecture supporting the protection, conservation and long-term maintenance of Enterprise capabilities, services, information, knowledge and achieved business value across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Preservation Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem preservation services
- support preservation governance
- support preservation strategy
- support preservation objectives
- support preservation planning
- support capability preservation
- support service preservation
- support technology preservation
- support application preservation
- support infrastructure preservation
- support data preservation
- support information preservation
- support knowledge preservation
- support architecture preservation
- support process preservation
- support organizational preservation
- support stakeholder preservation
- support value preservation
- support benefit preservation
- support outcome preservation
- support investment preservation
- support historical preservation
- support decision preservation
- support continuous preservation capability development

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Preservation Services shall provide trusted and reusable preservation capabilities enabling Enterprise stakeholders to protect achieved value, maintain critical capabilities and preserve strategic knowledge.
