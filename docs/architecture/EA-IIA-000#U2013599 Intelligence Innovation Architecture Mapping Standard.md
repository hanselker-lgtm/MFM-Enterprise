# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Innovation Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive innovation architecture for identifying, developing, validating and scaling new Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Innovation Architecture Framework shall provide:

- innovation principles
- innovation governance
- innovation strategy
- innovation architecture
- innovation portfolio management
- research and development management
- experimentation frameworks
- prototype management
- emerging technology management
- innovation laboratories
- innovation ecosystem management
- innovation measurement
- innovation maturity
- innovation evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence innovation is managed as a structured, measurable and continuously evolving capability.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Innovation Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture innovation domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Innovation Model
- Innovation Governance
- Innovation Strategy
- Innovation Architecture
- Innovation Portfolio Management
- Research & Development Architecture
- Experimentation Framework
- Prototype Management
- Emerging Technology Management
- Innovation Labs
- Innovation Ecosystem
- Innovation Metrics
- Innovation Maturity
- Innovation Roadmaps
- Innovation Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- research environments
- innovation programmes
- future adaptive enterprises

---

# 3. Intelligence Innovation Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Innovation Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategic Innovation Alignment

Innovation shall support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Controlled Experimentation

Innovation activities shall be tested and evaluated through structured approaches.

---

## Principle 3 – Value-Oriented Innovation

Innovation shall create measurable Enterprise value.

---

## Principle 4 – Open Innovation

Innovation shall leverage internal and external knowledge sources.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Exploration

The Enterprise shall continuously explore emerging opportunities.

---

## Principle 6 – Responsible Innovation

Innovation shall consider security, ethics, governance and sustainability.

---

## Principle 7 – Scalable Innovation

Successful innovation shall be capable of enterprise-wide adoption.
