# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, architectural models, governance mechanisms, and intelligence capabilities required for an **Intelligence Cosmic Systems Enterprise Architecture (ICSEA)**.

While EA-IOEA56 enables an enterprise to achieve Omniscient Enterprise Intelligence within its own organizational boundary, EA-ICSEA57 expands Enterprise Architecture into a **System-of-Systems Intelligence** capable of understanding, coordinating, and optimizing interconnected organizations operating within dynamic global ecosystems.

Enterprise Architecture shall evolve beyond organizational optimization toward coordinated intelligence across multiple autonomous systems.

The objective is no longer only maximizing enterprise value.

The objective is maximizing sustainable value across interconnected enterprises, infrastructures, societies, and ecosystems.

---
