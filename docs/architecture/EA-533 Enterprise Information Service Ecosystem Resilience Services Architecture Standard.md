# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Resilience Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed resilience capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Resilience Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem resilience capabilities
- support business continuity
- support operational resilience
- support partner resilience
- support supplier resilience
- support service resilience
- support integration resilience
- support information resilience
- support data resilience
- support security resilience
- support intelligence resilience
- support analytics resilience
- support automation resilience
- support orchestration resilience
- support disaster recovery alignment
- support resilience monitoring
- support resilience testing
- support resilience lifecycle management
- improve ecosystem reliability
- improve ecosystem adaptability
- improve ecosystem recovery capability

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Resilience Services shall provide trusted and reusable resilience capabilities enabling stable and sustainable ecosystem operations.
