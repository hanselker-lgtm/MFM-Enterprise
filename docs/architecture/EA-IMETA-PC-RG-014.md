## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-revalidation layer defining how a reassessed mandatory state is independently or formally confirmed as still valid, restored, or requiring further action before it can be accepted as current.

## Core Principle
Reassessment determines the current condition; revalidation confirms that the reassessment, evidence, criteria, determination and resulting state are sufficiently valid for continued governance reliance. Revalidation is therefore a controlled confirmation, not a repetition without purpose.

```text
REASSESSMENT RESULT
      ↓
REVALIDATION TRIGGER
      ↓
REVIEW BASIS / EVIDENCE
      ↓
CONFIRM METHOD / AUTHORITY
      ↓
REVALIDATE DETERMINATION
      ↓
VALID / INVALID / CONDITIONAL / UNDETERMINED
      ↓
ACCEPT / REMEDIATE / ESCALATE
```

## Revalidation Quality Test
```text
VALID REASSESSMENT
+ CURRENT EVIDENCE
+ CURRENT CRITERIA
+ AUTHORIZED REVALIDATOR
+ TRACEABLE DECISION
+ INDEPENDENCE WHERE REQUIRED
+ EXPLICIT ACCEPTANCE RULE
= VALID GOVERNED REVALIDATION
```

## Revalidation Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Reassessment | Result being revalidated |
| Trigger | Reason revalidation is required |
| Scope | What is confirmed |
| Authority | Authorized revalidation authority |
| Criteria | Acceptance criteria |
| Method | Revalidation method |
| Evidence | Supporting evidence |
| Independence | Required separation |
| Decision | Valid / invalid / conditional / undetermined |
| Conditions | Conditions of acceptance |
| Consequence | Required response |

## Revalidation Objective

The objective is to provide formal confidence that a reassessed mandatory state can be relied upon for continued governance, monitoring and operational decisions.

## Revalidation Trigger

Triggers may include material reassessment results, regression remediation, critical findings, disputed determinations, assurance requirements, governance gates, significant changes or conditions requiring formal acceptance.

```text
REASSESSMENT
↓
REVALIDATION REQUIRED?
├── NO → CONTINUE GOVERNED MONITORING
└── YES → REVALIDATE
```

## Revalidation Scope

Scope shall cover the basis necessary to confirm the reassessment determination and shall expand where material uncertainty or risk exists.

## Revalidation Authority

The authority shall define who may perform, approve, reject or conditionally accept revalidation. Authority to revalidate shall not automatically imply authority to waive mandatory requirements.

## Revalidation Criteria

Criteria shall define what must be true for the reassessment to be accepted as valid. Criteria shall be versioned and current.

## Revalidation Method

Methods may include independent evidence review, repeat testing, sampling, inspection, challenge testing, control review, observation or other appropriate assurance techniques.

## Revalidation Evidence

Evidence shall be sufficient to confirm the reassessment basis, current state and determination. Evidence lineage shall remain traceable to source and version.

## Revalidation Independence

Where assurance independence is required, the revalidator shall have sufficient separation from the original assessment or response execution to provide credible challenge.

## Revalidation Baseline

Revalidation shall use the approved baseline applicable to the current state. Historical baselines shall remain available for comparison and shall not be silently replaced.

## Revalidation Thresholds

Acceptance thresholds shall be explicit and governed. Threshold changes shall not be applied retrospectively to convert an adverse revalidation into acceptance.

## Revalidation Decision

Decisions shall be explicit.

```text
REVALIDATE
├── VALID → ACCEPT
├── INVALID → REJECT / REMEDIATE
├── CONDITIONAL → ACCEPT WITH GOVERNED CONDITIONS
└── UNDETERMINED → ESCALATE / FURTHER EVIDENCE
```

## Security Revalidation

Security revalidation shall confirm that required protective controls, boundaries and current evidence remain valid after remediation or material change.

## Resilience Revalidation

Resilience revalidation shall confirm that recovery, continuity, dependency and degraded-operation capabilities meet current requirements.

## Compliance Revalidation

Compliance revalidation shall confirm that the required compliant state is actually restored and supported by current evidence.

## Data Revalidation

Data revalidation shall confirm that required integrity, quality, lineage, access and authorized-use conditions remain valid.

## AI and Agent Revalidation

AI and agent revalidation shall confirm current authority, policy, data, tools, autonomy and observable behaviour after material change or remediation.

```text
AI / AGENT REASSESSMENT
↓
REVALIDATE GOVERNANCE BOUNDARIES
├── VALID → ACCEPT
├── CONDITIONAL → LIMIT / MONITOR
└── INVALID → BLOCK / SUSPEND / ESCALATE
```

## Revalidation Failure

Failure to establish validity shall prevent normal acceptance where acceptance is mandatory and shall trigger remediation, further evidence or escalation.

```text
REVALIDATION FAILURE
↓
REJECT / LIMIT RELIANCE
↓
REMEDIATE / REASSESS
↓
REVALIDATE AGAIN
```

## Revalidation Escalation

Escalation shall occur when validity is disputed, evidence is insufficient, independence is compromised, acceptance conditions are breached or a material mandatory state cannot be confirmed.

## Revalidation Review and Learning

Revalidation outcomes shall be reviewed for recurring evidence deficiencies, weak assessment methods, unsuitable thresholds, insufficient independence and systemic governance gaps.

## Revalidation Determination Model
```text
REASSESSMENT RESULT
↓
BASIS VALID AND CURRENT?
├── NO → INVALID / FURTHER ASSESSMENT
└── YES
     ↓
EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT?
├── NO → UNDETERMINED / ESCALATE
└── YES
     ↓
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA SATISFIED?
├── YES → VALID / ACCEPT
├── PARTLY → CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE
└── NO → INVALID / REJECT
```


```text
REVALIDATION FAILED
↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

```

## Revalidation Anti-Gaming Control
Revalidation shall not be reduced to a ceremonial approval. Evidence, criteria, independence and decision rationale shall support the result. Acceptance shall not be granted solely because operational stakeholders need closure.

## Revalidation Change Control
Changes to revalidation triggers, scope, authority, independence, criteria, methods, thresholds or acceptance conditions shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated.

```text
CURRENT REVALIDATION MODEL
↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
↓
NEW VERSION
↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

Historical revalidation decisions shall remain preserved and shall not be overwritten by later acceptance or rejection.
