# 2. Purpose

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-472 establishes the execution-control layer following the adaptive strategy architecture of RG-471.

RG-471 governs how strategy changes, scenarios are navigated and strategic options are activated.

RG-472 governs how an approved strategic change is translated into coordinated enterprise execution without losing strategic intent, control of dependencies, resilience floors, benefit accountability or operational stability.

The architecture SHALL answer:

> **How does the enterprise translate an approved strategic adaptation into coordinated cross-domain execution, control the resulting change cascade, protect critical operations, manage dependencies and capacity, and verify that the intended strategic outcome is actually realised?**

# 3. Core Principle

> **A strategic decision SHALL NOT be considered implemented merely because initiatives have started; implementation SHALL be governed from strategic intent through change propagation, execution, stabilisation, measurable outcome and verified benefit realisation.**

# 171. Acceptance Criteria

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-472 is accepted when:

- approved strategic adaptations can be translated into executable change structures;
- current and target states are explicit;
- strategic change cascades can be mapped across enterprise domains;
- critical dependencies and critical paths are visible;
- change collisions and interference can be detected;
- change capacity, contention and saturation are governed;
- readiness and execution gates are evidence-based and owned;
- transition, cutover, rollback, failback and rollforward are governed;
- critical operations and resilience floors are protected;
- operational acceptance and handover are explicit;
- stabilisation and regression monitoring exist;
- benefits have baselines, targets, owners and evidence;
- value leakage and outcome drift are visible;
- execution, decision and escalation latency are measurable;
- change, control and transformation debt remain visible;
- AI assistance remains bounded and explainable;
- manual fallback exists;
- historical execution and benefit evidence remain reconstructable;
- negative and scenario tests prevent uncontrolled execution, unsafe transition and unsupported benefit claims.
