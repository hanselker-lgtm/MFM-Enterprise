# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Collaboration Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Management principles are inherited from EA-512 and EA-521.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Enablement principles are inherited from EA-522.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Integration principles are inherited from EA-523.

Enterprise Information Service Collaboration capabilities are aligned with

- Enterprise Information Service Knowledge Management Services
- Enterprise Information Service Learning Management Services
- Enterprise Information Service Innovation Management Services
- Enterprise Information Service Digital Transformation Management Services

Enterprise Information Governance principles are inherited from EA-486.

Enterprise Information Quality principles are inherited from EA-487.

Enterprise Information Lifecycle Management principles are inherited from EA-485.

Enterprise Data Governance principles are inherited from EA-354.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Collaboration Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Collaboration Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed collaboration capabilities between Enterprise domains, partners, suppliers, communities and external ecosystem participants across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Collaboration Services shall

- provide standardized collaboration capabilities
- support ecosystem collaboration platforms
- support shared collaboration workspaces
- support partner collaboration
- support supplier collaboration
- support community collaboration
- support digital communication channels
- support knowledge sharing collaboration
- support innovation collaboration
- support learning collaboration
- support cross-enterprise collaboration
- support collaboration governance
- support collaboration security
- support collaboration lifecycle management
- improve communication effectiveness
- improve knowledge exchange
- improve teamwork across organizational boundaries
- improve ecosystem value creation
- support continuous ecosystem development

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Collaboration Services shall provide trusted and reusable collaboration capabilities enabling effective ecosystem cooperation.
