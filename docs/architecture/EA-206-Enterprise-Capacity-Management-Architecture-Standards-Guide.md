# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Capacity Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Capacity Management ensures that enterprise resources are planned, monitored, optimized and scaled to meet current and future business demand while preserving operational stability, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Capacity Planning
- Capacity Monitoring
- Demand Forecasting
- Resource Optimization
- Capacity Threshold Management
- Capacity Reporting
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Capacity Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 4. Capacity Management Principles

Enterprise Capacity Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Proactive Capacity Planning
- Continuous Monitoring
- Predictive Forecasting
- Efficient Resource Utilization
- Complete Traceability
- Operational Stability
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Capacity Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Capacity Management implementations shall handle capacity management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify capacity planning failures
- classify monitoring failures
- classify forecasting failures
- classify resource optimization failures
- classify threshold management failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Capacity Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational stability, resource integrity, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Capacity Management implementations may depend upon

- approved monitoring platforms
- approved alert management services
- approved configuration management services
- approved asset management services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Capacity Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external capacity management providers

Capacity Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Capacity Management implementation is compliant when

- Capacity planning is documented.
- Capacity monitoring is operational.
- Demand forecasting is implemented.
- Resource optimization is performed.
- Capacity thresholds are maintained.
- Capacity verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Reactive Capacity Planning

Capacity shall never be expanded only after service degradation has already occurred.

---

## Missing Capacity Monitoring

Enterprise resources shall never operate without approved capacity monitoring.

---

## Undefined Capacity Thresholds

Capacity thresholds shall never be absent for critical enterprise services.

---

## Unverified Forecasts

Demand forecasts shall never be used without periodic verification against actual utilization.

---

## Resource Overallocation

Enterprise resources shall never remain permanently overallocated without approved mitigation plans.

---

## Capacity Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent capacity management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Capacity Management services.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise Capacity Management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- planning compliance
- monitoring compliance
- forecasting compliance
- optimization compliance
- threshold compliance
- dependency compliance
- documentation completeness
- operational readiness
- governance compliance
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Capacity Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Capacity Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise capacity is proactively planned, continuously monitored, efficiently optimized and aligned with business demand while preserving operational stability, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Capacity Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
