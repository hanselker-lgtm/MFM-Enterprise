## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-evidence layer defining what evidence is required to support verification and determination of an applicable mandatory state. The layer governs evidence purpose, scope, source, quality, integrity, authenticity, completeness, timeliness, retention, chain of custody, sufficiency, deficiency, escalation and review.

## Core Principle
Evidence is the basis on which a verification result can be trusted. Evidence shall demonstrate the relevant state or verification result and shall be sufficiently reliable, attributable, complete and current for the materiality of the determination.

```text
MANDATORY VERIFICATION
        ↓
EVIDENCE REQUIREMENT
        ↓
EVIDENCE SOURCE
        ↓
CAPTURE / COLLECT
        ↓
VALIDATE
        ↓
ASSESS QUALITY / INTEGRITY
        ↓
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE?
 ├── YES → DETERMINATION
 └── NO  → DEFICIENCY / UNDETERMINED / ESCALATE
```

## Evidence Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Verification | Verification supported |
| Evidence Requirement | What must be demonstrated |
| Source | Origin of evidence |
| Collection | How evidence is obtained |
| Quality | Fitness for purpose |
| Integrity | Protection against unauthorized alteration |
| Authenticity | Basis for trust in origin |
| Completeness | Required coverage |
| Timeliness | Currency relative to state |
| Retention | Required preservation period |
| Custody | Chain of custody where relevant |
| Sufficiency | Whether evidence supports determination |
| Disposition | Retain, archive or dispose |

## Evidence Objective

The evidence objective shall identify what the evidence must demonstrate and how it relates to the required state and verification objective.

```text
REQUIRED STATE
↓
VERIFICATION OBJECTIVE
↓
EVIDENCE OBJECTIVE
↓
PROOF
```

## Evidence Scope

Evidence scope shall identify the systems, services, controls, data, events, transactions, time periods and environments represented by the evidence.

## Evidence Source

Sources shall be identifiable and appropriate to the determination. Source reliability shall be assessed where material.

Sources may include system records, logs, measurements, configuration records, test results, observations, attestations, approvals, reports or independently generated evidence.

## Evidence Quality

Evidence quality shall be assessed against relevance, reliability, accuracy, completeness, timeliness, consistency and fitness for the intended determination.

## Evidence Integrity

Evidence integrity requires appropriate protection against unauthorized modification, deletion, corruption or substitution. Integrity controls shall be proportionate to materiality.

## Evidence Authenticity

Authenticity shall establish reasonable confidence that evidence originated from the claimed source and represents the claimed event, state or result.

## Evidence Completeness

Completeness shall consider whether required evidence elements, time periods, scope and exceptions are represented. A convenient subset shall not be treated as complete without an approved sampling or coverage rationale.

## Evidence Timeliness

Evidence shall be sufficiently current for the state being verified. Historical evidence may be valid for historical determination but shall not automatically prove current state.

## Evidence Retention

Evidence shall be retained according to applicable governance, legal, regulatory, contractual and architecture requirements. Retention shall preserve the ability to reconstruct material determinations.

## Security Evidence

Security evidence may include access records, configuration state, security tests, monitoring records, incident evidence, vulnerability results and control operation evidence as appropriate. Sensitive evidence shall receive appropriate protection.

## Resilience Evidence

Resilience evidence may include recovery tests, restoration records, continuity exercises, dependency tests, capacity results and degraded-mode evidence.

## Compliance Evidence

Compliance evidence shall demonstrate actual conformity where required. The existence of a policy or procedure shall not automatically prove operational compliance.

## Data Evidence

Data evidence shall preserve relevant lineage, source, transformation, timestamp, quality indicators and access context where needed to support the determination.

## AI and Agent Evidence

AI and agent evidence shall support the result and, where applicable, the governing context: authority, autonomy, tools, data access, policy constraints, decisions and relevant outputs.

```text
AI / AGENT RESULT
↓
RESULT EVIDENCE
↓
AUTHORITY / POLICY EVIDENCE
↓
GOVERNED DETERMINATION
```

## Evidence Chain of Custody

Where evidence is material to an investigation, assurance determination or disputed result, chain of custody shall record acquisition, handling, transfer, storage and access as appropriate.

```text
SOURCE
↓
CAPTURE
↓
TRANSFER
↓
STORE
↓
ACCESS
↓
USE IN DETERMINATION
```

## Evidence Sufficiency

Evidence is sufficient only when it provides a reasonable basis for the verification conclusion given the materiality, risk and required confidence.

```text
EVIDENCE
↓
SUFFICIENT FOR DETERMINATION?
├── YES → SUPPORT RESULT
└── NO  → UNDETERMINED / DEFICIENCY
```

## Evidence Deficiency

Evidence deficiency exists when evidence is missing, unreliable, incomplete, stale, contradictory, unauthenticated or otherwise inadequate to support the intended determination.

```text
EVIDENCE DEFICIENCY
↓
DETERMINATION SAFE?
├── NO → UNDETERMINED / BLOCK
└── YES → DOCUMENT LIMITATION
```

## Evidence Escalation

Material evidence deficiency, suspected manipulation, conflicting evidence or inability to establish authenticity shall trigger escalation according to the applicable governance path.

## Evidence Review and Learning

Evidence requirements shall be reviewed after disputed determinations, repeated deficiencies, false positives, false negatives, incidents, architecture changes, regulatory changes or changes in verification methods.
