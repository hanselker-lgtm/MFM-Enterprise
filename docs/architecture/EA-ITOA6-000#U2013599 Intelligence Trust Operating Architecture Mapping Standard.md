# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Trust Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational trust architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities operate with transparency, accountability, reliability and documented trust throughout their lifecycle.

The Intelligence Trust Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- trust operating principles
- trust governance frameworks
- trust lifecycle structures
- trust management models
- transparency models
- explainability models
- accountability models
- reliability models
- trust validation models
- trust security models
- trust monitoring structures
- trust automation models
- trust evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for trusted, responsible and continuously improving Intelligence capabilities.

# 3. Intelligence Trust Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Trust Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Trust by Design

Trust capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Transparency

Intelligence capability shall provide understandable and traceable behaviour.

---

## Principle 3 – Explainability

Intelligence decisions and outputs shall be explainable where required.

---

## Principle 4 – Accountability

Responsibility for Intelligence outcomes shall be clearly defined.

---

## Principle 5 – Reliability

Intelligence capability shall provide dependable and consistent results.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Trust Improvement

Trust capability shall continuously improve through measurement and feedback.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolution Through Trust

Trust shall support continuous Intelligence evolution.
