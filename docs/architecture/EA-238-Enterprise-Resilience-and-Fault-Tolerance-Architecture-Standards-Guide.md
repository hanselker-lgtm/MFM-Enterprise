# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance provides standardized mechanisms for fault isolation, graceful degradation, recovery, operational continuity and workload protection while preserving scalability, reliability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Fault Tolerance
- Resilience
- Circuit Breakers
- Bulkheads
- Graceful Degradation
- Recovery Strategies
- Operational Continuity
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 8. Fault Isolation

Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance implementations shall implement standardized fault isolation.

Fault isolation shall

- isolate failing components
- prevent cascading failures
- preserve operational traceability
- maintain service availability
- support enterprise governance
- support operational reliability

Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance implementations shall implement standardized circuit breaker mechanisms.

Circuit breakers shall

- detect repeated failures
- suspend failing operations
- allow controlled recovery
- preserve execution traceability
- maintain operational consistency
- support enterprise governance

Circuit breaker implementations shall align with enterprise governance requirements.

- approved resilience infrastructure

- approved logging services
- approved configuration services
- approved reporting services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations

- Domain implementations

- Business Services
- Unapproved external resilience providers

Enterprise Resilience capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Cascading Failures

Enterprise implementations shall never allow failures to propagate across unrelated services without appropriate isolation mechanisms.

---

## Missing Circuit Breakers

Critical external dependencies shall never be invoked continuously after repeated failures without approved circuit breaker protection.

---

## Shared Resource Exhaustion

Enterprise implementations shall never permit a single workload to exhaust shared resources when bulkhead isolation is required.

---

## Recovery Without Validation

Recovery procedures shall never restore services without validating operational readiness.

---

## Hidden Resilience Dependencies

Resilience implementations shall never introduce undocumented recovery infrastructure, monitoring mechanisms or operational dependencies.

---

## Business Logic Inside Resilience Infrastructure

Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance implementations shall never contain business logic that belongs within the appropriate Domain or Capability implementation.

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing resilience, recovery and fault tolerance throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that fault isolation, graceful degradation, recovery strategies and operational continuity are implemented consistently while preserving reliability, scalability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Resilience & Fault Tolerance implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
