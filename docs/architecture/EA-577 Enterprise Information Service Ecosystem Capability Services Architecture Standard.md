# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Capability Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Capability Services shall follow these principles.

## Capability by Design

Enterprise capabilities shall be deliberately designed, governed and managed throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Capability Ownership

Every capability shall have defined ownership, accountability and lifecycle responsibility.

---

## Capability Reusability

Enterprise capabilities shall be reusable across business and technology domains.

---

## Capability Alignment

Capabilities shall align with Enterprise strategy, objectives and operating models.

---

## Capability Measurement

Capabilities shall be evaluated through measurable indicators and evidence.

---

## Continuous Capability Evolution

Capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.

---

## Governed Capability Management

Capability activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.
