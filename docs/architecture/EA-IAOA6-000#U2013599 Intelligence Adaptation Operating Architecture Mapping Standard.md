# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Adaptation Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational adaptation architecture that enables Intelligence capabilities to detect change, evaluate impact, decide adaptation, execute transformation and continuously improve.

The Intelligence Adaptation Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- adaptation operating principles
- adaptation governance frameworks
- adaptation lifecycle structures
- adaptation management models
- change detection structures
- adaptation decision models
- adaptation execution models
- adaptation validation models
- adaptation security models
- adaptation monitoring structures
- adaptation automation models
- adaptation evolution models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for adaptive, resilient and continuously evolving Intelligence capabilities.

# 3. Intelligence Adaptation Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Adaptation Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Adaptation by Design

Adaptation capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

---

## Principle 2 – Continuous Adaptation

Intelligence capability shall continuously adapt to changes and new requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Controlled Adaptation

All adaptation activities shall follow defined governance and control processes.

---

## Principle 4 – Evidence-Based Adaptation

Adaptation decisions shall be based on validated information and analysis.

---

## Principle 5 – Adaptive Resilience

Adaptation capability shall strengthen Intelligence resilience.

---

## Principle 6 – Learning-Driven Adaptation

Adaptation shall incorporate knowledge and operational experience.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolution Through Adaptation

Adaptation shall support continuous Intelligence evolution.
