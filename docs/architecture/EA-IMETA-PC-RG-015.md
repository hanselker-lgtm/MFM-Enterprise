## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-acceptance layer defining the formal decision by which a validated mandatory state is accepted for continued reliance, operation, monitoring or closure.

## Core Principle
Acceptance is a governed decision that establishes reliance. It shall occur only when the required evidence, revalidation, criteria, authority and conditions support the decision. Acceptance shall never be inferred merely from absence of an open finding.

```text
REVALIDATION RESULT
      ↓
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
      ↓
EVIDENCE / CONDITIONS
      ↓
AUTHORIZED ACCEPTANCE DECISION
      ↓
ACCEPT / CONDITIONAL / REJECT / UNDETERMINED
      ↓
RELIANCE / MONITORING / REMEDIATION / ESCALATION
```

## Acceptance Quality Test
```text
VALID REVALIDATION
+ CURRENT EVIDENCE
+ ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
+ AUTHORIZED DECISION MAKER
+ ACCOUNTABILITY
+ CONDITIONS SATISFIED
+ DECISION TRACEABILITY
= VALID GOVERNED ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
NOT READY
READY FOR ACCEPTANCE
UNDER REVIEW
ACCEPTED

REJECTED
UNDETERMINED
EXPIRED
SUSPENDED

UNDER REASSESSMENT
```

## Acceptance Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Revalidation | Validated basis for acceptance |
| Criteria | Conditions required for acceptance |
| Evidence | Supporting proof |
| Authority | Authorized acceptance decision maker |
| Accountability | Person/role accountable for decision |
| Conditions | Conditions or limitations |
| Decision | Accept / conditional / reject / undetermined |
| Effective Date | Start of acceptance |
| Review / Expiry | Required renewal point |
| Consequence | Required action after rejection or expiry |

## Acceptance Objective

The objective is to establish a clear, attributable and defensible decision that the mandatory state may be relied upon within the defined scope and conditions.

## Acceptance Definition

Acceptance shall state what is being accepted, for which scope, under which criteria, with what evidence, authority, conditions and validity period.

## Acceptance Scope

Scope shall explicitly identify systems, services, controls, processes, data, environments, users, dependencies and lifecycle conditions covered by the acceptance.

## Acceptance Authority

Only authorized roles may issue acceptance. Delegation shall be explicit and shall not silently expand authority.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criteria shall define the minimum conditions for acceptance and shall be versioned.

```text
REVALIDATION
↓
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA SATISFIED?
├── YES → ACCEPTANCE ELIGIBLE
└── NO → REJECT / FURTHER ACTION
```

## Acceptance Evidence

Evidence shall be current, sufficient, traceable and relevant to the accepted state. Historical evidence may support context but shall not substitute for current evidence where current validity is required.

## Acceptance Conditions

Conditions may limit scope, duration, reliance, operation or autonomy. Conditions shall be explicit, measurable where possible, assigned and monitored.

```text
CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE
↓
DEFINE LIMITS
↓
ASSIGN OWNER
↓
MONITOR
↓
CONDITION MET?
├── YES → CONTINUE
└── NO → SUSPEND / REVOKE / ESCALATE
```

## Acceptance Decision

The decision shall be explicit and attributable.

```text
ACCEPT
CONDITIONAL ACCEPT
REJECT
UNDETERMINED
```

## Acceptance Accountability

The acceptance decision shall have an accountable owner who remains responsible for ensuring that the acceptance basis, conditions and review obligations remain governed.

## Acceptance Timing

Acceptance shall be effective-dated. Time-limited acceptance shall have explicit expiry or review conditions.

## Acceptance Independence

Where required by risk, governance or assurance, acceptance shall include independent review or approval.

## Security Acceptance

Security acceptance shall confirm that required security controls and boundaries are sufficiently valid for the defined scope and period.

## Resilience Acceptance

Resilience acceptance shall confirm that required continuity, recovery, restoration and dependency conditions are sufficiently valid for reliance.

## Compliance Acceptance

Compliance acceptance shall confirm actual conformity where required. Acceptance shall not itself create an unauthorized legal or regulatory exception.

## Data Acceptance

Data acceptance shall confirm required integrity, quality, lineage, access and authorized-use conditions where applicable.

## AI and Agent Acceptance

AI and agent acceptance shall confirm current authority, policy, data, tools, autonomy and behavioural boundaries.

```text
AI / AGENT REVALIDATION
↓
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
↓
AUTHORITY / SAFETY / POLICY VALID?
├── YES → ACCEPT / CONDITIONAL ACCEPT
└── NO  → REJECT / LIMIT / SUSPEND
```

## Acceptance Failure

Failure to satisfy acceptance criteria shall prevent acceptance or trigger suspension/revocation if already accepted.

```text
ACCEPTANCE FAILURE
↓
REJECT / SUSPEND
↓
REMEDIATE / REASSESS
↓
REVALIDATE
↓
RE-ACCEPT ONLY WHEN VALID
```

## Acceptance Escalation

Escalation shall occur when acceptance is disputed, criteria are unmet, evidence is insufficient, authority is unclear, conditions are breached or the acceptance basis materially changes.

## Acceptance Review and Learning

Acceptance outcomes shall be reviewed for recurring deficiencies, overly weak criteria, unclear authority, inappropriate conditions, premature acceptance or repeated suspension/revocation.

## Acceptance Determination Model
```text
REVALIDATION RESULT
↓
CURRENT AND SUFFICIENT BASIS?
├── NO → NOT READY / UNDETERMINED
└── YES
     ↓
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA SATISFIED?
├── NO → REJECT
└── YES
     ↓
AUTHORIZED DECISION?
├── NO → ESCALATE / NOT VALID
└── YES
     ↓
CONDITIONS?
├── YES → CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE
└── NO  → ACCEPT
```

## Acceptance Suspension and Revocation
Acceptance shall be suspended or revoked when its basis becomes invalid, conditions are breached, mandatory requirements fail, evidence becomes unreliable or a material change invalidates the acceptance.

```text
ACCEPTED STATE
↓
MATERIAL CHANGE / BREACH
↓
SUSPEND / REVOKE
↓
REASSESS
↓
REVALIDATE
↓
RE-ACCEPT OR REMEDIATE
```

## Acceptance Anti-Gaming Control
Acceptance shall not be granted merely to achieve project closure, remove a finding from reporting, satisfy a schedule or create an appearance of compliance. The decision shall be supported by criteria, evidence, authority and accountability.

## Acceptance Change Control
Changes to acceptance criteria, authority, scope, conditions, review periods or decision rules shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated.

```text
CURRENT ACCEPTANCE MODEL
↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
↓
NEW VERSION
↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

Historical acceptance decisions shall remain preserved and shall not be overwritten by later suspension, revocation or re-acceptance.

This document specializes the mandatory-acceptance layer beneath mandatory revalidation. It does not replace governance, authority, mandate, role, responsibility, accountability, outcome, criteria, success, success conditions, mandatory conditions, non-negotiable requirements, applicability, state, verification, evidence, measurement, threshold, classification, consequence, response, effectiveness, reassessment or revalidation layers.
