# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the ninetieth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-89 – Enterprise Financial Governance, IT Financial Management, Budgeting, Cost Management, Investment Governance & Financial Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for procurement governance, procurement strategy, sourcing, procurement planning, category management, supplier identification, supplier selection, tendering, request-for-proposal processes, evaluation, due diligence, procurement approvals, purchasing, supplier onboarding, third-party governance, supplier performance, supplier risk, supplier resilience, procurement controls, contract handover, purchase lifecycle, procurement records, procurement assurance, procurement findings, procurement exceptions, procurement remediation, procurement metrics, procurement dashboards, procurement maturity and continual enterprise procurement improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must acquire goods and services through transparent, competitive, risk-based and controlled procurement processes that achieve required value while protecting the enterprise from unacceptable supplier and third-party risk.**

# 3. Procurement Governance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Establish clear authority, accountability, transparency and control over procurement decisions and supplier relationships.**

# 4. Sourcing Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Select suppliers through fair, structured and risk-based sourcing processes that achieve the required business outcome and value.**

# 5. Supplier Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Manage suppliers throughout their lifecycle according to business criticality, risk, performance and contractual obligations.**

# 6. Third-Party Risk Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Identify and manage material risks arising from external suppliers, service providers and other third parties.**

# 7. Procurement Assurance Objective

The primary objective is:

> **Provide reliable evidence that material procurement decisions, controls and supplier governance processes operate effectively.**

# 94. Definition of Ready

A procurement, sourcing event, supplier assessment, tender, evaluation, onboarding, purchase, supplier review, exception, remediation or assurance review is Ready when need, scope, owner, budget, criticality, risk, requirements, decision authority, evaluation criteria and evidence requirements are defined.

# 96. Final Procurement Governance Principle

> **MFM must acquire goods and services through transparent, competitive, risk-based and controlled procurement processes that achieve required value while protecting the enterprise from unacceptable supplier and third-party risk.**

# 97. Final Sourcing Principle

> **Sourcing decisions must consider business requirements, market conditions, total value, lifecycle cost, risk, supplier capability and appropriate competition.**

# 98. Final Supplier Principle

> **Material suppliers must be governed throughout their lifecycle according to criticality, risk, performance and contractual obligations.**

# 99. Final Due Diligence Principle

> **Supplier due diligence must be proportionate to supplier criticality, access, dependency, data exposure and potential business impact.**

# 100. Final Approval Principle

> **Material procurement decisions must be approved by appropriately authorized decision-makers with appropriate segregation of duties.**

# 101. Final Performance Principle

> **Supplier performance must be measured against agreed requirements, service levels, quality, cost, risk and contractual obligations.**

# 102. Final Exit Principle

> **Critical supplier relationships must have appropriate exit, transition and continuity considerations before termination or material change.**

# 103. Final Evidence Principle

> **Material procurement decisions must be supported by reliable, complete and traceable records and evidence.**

# 104. Final Assurance Principle

> **Procurement governance and supplier controls must be periodically assessed and assured according to risk and materiality.**

# 105. Final Improvement Principle

> **Procurement outcomes, supplier performance, findings, market intelligence and assurance results must continuously improve MFM's procurement capability.**

# 106. Final Integration Principle

> **Procurement Management must integrate with Financial Management, Legal, Compliance, Enterprise Risk, Privacy, Cybersecurity, Data, Enterprise Architecture, Service Management, Business Continuity, Projects and Contract Management.**

# 107. Final Steady-State Procurement Principle

> **MFM must acquire goods and services through transparent, competitive, risk-based and controlled procurement processes that achieve required value while protecting the enterprise from unacceptable supplier and third-party risk.**
