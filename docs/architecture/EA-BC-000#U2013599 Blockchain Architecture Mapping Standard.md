# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Blockchain Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise Blockchain capabilities.

The Blockchain Architecture Framework shall provide:

- blockchain principles
- blockchain governance
- blockchain strategy
- blockchain capability architecture
- distributed ledger architecture
- node architecture
- consensus architecture
- smart contract architecture
- token architecture
- digital identity architecture
- blockchain data architecture
- blockchain integration architecture
- blockchain security architecture
- blockchain operations
- blockchain performance management
- blockchain evolution

The framework ensures that distributed ledger technologies and decentralized trust capabilities are designed, governed and operated as integrated Enterprise capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

The Blockchain Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture blockchain domains:

- Enterprise Blockchain Model
- Blockchain Governance

- Blockchain Capability Architecture
- Distributed Ledger Architecture

The framework applies to:

- trusted transaction services
- multi-party collaboration platforms

---

# 3. Enterprise Blockchain Architecture Principles

The Blockchain Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Distributed Trust

Blockchain capabilities shall establish trusted transactions between multiple parties without unnecessary centralized dependencies.

---

## Principle 2 – Transparency and Traceability

Blockchain solutions shall provide verifiable transaction history and controlled information visibility.

---

## Principle 3 – Business Value First

Blockchain adoption shall be based on measurable Enterprise value.

---

## Principle 4 – Appropriate Decentralization

Blockchain architectures shall use decentralization according to business, security and operational requirements.

---

## Principle 5 – Secure Blockchain by Design

Blockchain environments shall include security controls throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 6 – Controlled Digital Assets

Digital assets, tokens and blockchain records shall be governed throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Blockchain Evolution

Blockchain capabilities shall continuously evolve according to Enterprise needs and technology development.

---
