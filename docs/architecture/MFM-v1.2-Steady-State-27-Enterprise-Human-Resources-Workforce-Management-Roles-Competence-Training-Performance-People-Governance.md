# MFM v1.2-Steady-State-27
## Enterprise Human Resources, Workforce Management, Roles, Competence, Training, Performance & People Governance

**Version:** 1.2  
**Document ID:** MFM-v1.2-Steady-State-27  
**Status:** Steady-State Enterprise People & Workforce Governance Baseline  
**Lifecycle:** Post-Implementation / Steady-State Operation  
**Document Type:** Enterprise Human Resources / Workforce Management / Roles / Competence / Training / Performance / People Governance Document  

---

# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the twenty-seventh document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series. It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-26 – Enterprise Procurement, Supplier Management, Contract Management, Vendor Risk & Third-Party Governance. It establishes the permanent operating model for people governance, workforce management, roles, competence, recruitment, onboarding, training, performance, succession, workforce risk, employee lifecycle and continual people development.

> **MFM must ensure that the right people, skills, responsibilities, authorities and workforce capabilities are available, developed, protected and governed throughout the employee and organizational lifecycle.**

---

# 2. Scope

People Governance; Human Resources Governance; Workforce Planning; Workforce Strategy; Organizational Structure; Roles and Responsibilities; Competence and Skills Management; Recruitment and Selection; Pre-Employment Checks; Onboarding; Personnel Records; Employee Lifecycle; Joiner/Mover/Leaver; Training; Learning and Development; Mandatory and Role-Based Training; Competence Assessment; Certification; Authorization; Performance Management; Objectives; Reviews; Development Plans; Succession; Talent Management; Workforce Capacity and Availability; Workforce Resilience; Key-Person Risk; Separation of Duties; Privileged Roles; Contractor and External Personnel Management; Employee Relations; Conduct; Health, Safety and Wellbeing; Workforce Compliance; Employee Privacy; HR Security; Workforce Records and Retention; People Assurance; Workforce Audit; Findings; Exceptions; Remediation; Metrics; Dashboards; Maturity; Continual Improvement.

---

# 3. People Governance Objective

> **Ensure that people, roles, responsibilities, competence and workforce capacity are aligned with MFM objectives and governed throughout the workforce lifecycle.**

---

# 4. Workforce Management Objective

> **Maintain sufficient qualified and appropriately authorized personnel to perform required MFM activities safely, effectively and consistently.**

---

# 5. People Principles

Accountable; Fair; Competence-Based; Role-Aware; Risk-Based; Transparent; Privacy-Aware; Development-Oriented; Resilient; Continuously Improved.

---

# 6. Workforce Operating Model

Plan → Recruit → Select → Onboard → Assign → Develop → Assess → Perform → Progress → Transfer / Change → Exit

---

# 7. People Authority

Coordinates People Policy, Workforce Planning, Roles, Competence, Recruitment, Onboarding, Training, Performance, Succession, Employee Lifecycle, People Risk, People Assurance and People Improvement.

---

# 8. People Ownership

Material workforce processes must have accountable owners. People Owners are accountable for purpose, role, capacity, competence, authority, performance, risk, compliance, records and improvement.

---

# 9. HR Function

Where established, Human Resources provides appropriate governance, advisory and operational support across the workforce lifecycle.

---

# 10. HR Policy and Standards

HR policies establish mandatory requirements. Standards define common requirements for recruitment, employment, roles, competence, training, performance, records, conduct and separation.

---

# 11. Workforce Planning

Align people capacity with business objectives, services, projects, workload, skills, risk, budget and future demand.

---

# 12. Workforce Strategy

Consider current and future capability, critical skills, capacity, retention, succession, technology change and organizational change.

---

# 13. Organizational Structure

Define appropriate functions, teams, roles, reporting lines, authorities and responsibilities.

---

# 14. Role Definition

Material roles must have clearly defined responsibilities and authorities.

---

# 15. Role Profile

May include role name, purpose, responsibilities, authority, competence, experience, skills, reporting line and dependencies.

---

# 16. Responsibility Mapping

Where appropriate use Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed mappings.

---

# 17. Competence Management

Ensure personnel possess capabilities required for assigned responsibilities.

---

# 18. Competence Framework

Define relevant knowledge, skills, experience, behaviours, qualifications and authorizations.

---

# 19. Skills Inventory

Maintain visibility of material organizational capabilities and identify critical skills.

---

# 20. Competence Assessment

Assess competence against defined requirements and identify gaps between required and actual competence.

---

# 21. Competence Development

Use training, coaching, mentoring, experience, certification, job rotation and supervised practice as appropriate.

---

# 22. Certification and Authorization

Monitor required certifications. Personnel performing controlled, safety-, security-, financial- or compliance-sensitive activities must have appropriate authorization.

---

# 23. Authorization Records

Where applicable record person, role, authority, scope, start date, expiry, approver and status.

---

# 24. Recruitment

Recruitment must be based on defined organizational need and approved workforce requirements.

---

# 25. Selection

Use defined and appropriately documented criteria, retaining evidence of assessment, decision and approval.

---

# 26. Pre-Employment Checks

Where appropriate and lawful, checks may include identity, qualifications, references, right to work and role-specific screening.

---

# 27. Onboarding

Establish readiness before personnel perform assigned responsibilities independently. Include employment, role, policies, security, privacy, systems, training, competence, access and emergency information.

---

# 28. Personnel Records

Personnel records must be accurate, complete, current, protected, access-controlled and retained according to applicable requirements.

---

# 29. Employee Lifecycle

Support recruitment, onboarding, employment, development, promotion, transfer, leave, return and separation where applicable.

---

# 30. Joiner / Mover / Leaver

Workforce changes must trigger coordinated updates to role, organization, training, authorization, systems, access and records.

---

# 31. Access Lifecycle

Personnel access must align with current role and authorization. Joiners receive only approved access; movers trigger timely review; leavers have access revoked according to defined requirements.

---

# 32. Privileged Roles and SoD

Privileged roles require enhanced governance. Conflicting responsibilities must be separated where required to reduce fraud, error or inappropriate activity.

---

# 33. Contractors and External Personnel

Contractors and temporary or external personnel must be governed according to role, access, competence, contractual status and risk.

---

# 34. Training

Training must provide knowledge and skills necessary for assigned responsibilities.

---

# 35. Training Needs Analysis

Identify training needs from role, competence gaps, risk, change, incidents, regulation, performance and career development.

---

# 36. Training Plan

Identify requirement, person/group, training, priority, due date, provider and evidence.

---

# 37. Mandatory and Role-Based Training

Define and monitor mandatory training. Specialized roles receive appropriate role-based training.

---

# 38. Security, Privacy, Compliance and Financial Training

Personnel receive training appropriate to security, privacy, compliance and financial-control responsibilities.

---

# 39. Training Evidence and Effectiveness

Training records should identify person, training, date, provider, result and expiry/renewal where applicable. Material training should be evaluated for effectiveness where justified.

---

# 40. Learning and Development

Development should support current performance and future organizational capability through documented development plans.

---

# 41. Performance Management

Align individual contribution with organizational objectives and role expectations.

---

# 42. Performance Objectives

Objectives should be relevant, clear, measurable where appropriate, time-bound where appropriate and aligned.

---

# 43. Performance Review

Consider objectives, results, behaviour, competence, development, feedback and future priorities.

---

# 44. Performance Improvement

Material gaps should trigger support and improvement actions. Plans should identify gap, expected outcome, support, actions, review dates, evidence and outcome.

---

# 45. Talent and Succession

Identify and develop important capabilities. Critical roles should have proportionate succession arrangements.

---

# 46. Key-Person Risk

Assess dependencies where loss of an individual could materially affect operations, knowledge, decision-making, compliance, security or continuity.

---

# 47. Knowledge Transfer

Critical knowledge should be documented and transferred where dependency risk exists.

---

# 48. Workforce Capacity and Availability

Monitor demand, workload, availability, skills, critical activities, leave, absence, turnover, training, projects and peak demand.

---

# 49. Workforce Resilience

Use cross-training, backup personnel, succession, knowledge transfer, remote capability and emergency coverage as appropriate.

---

# 50. Workforce Risk

Integrate workforce risk with Enterprise Risk Management. Consider capacity, competence, turnover, absence, key-person dependency, skills shortage, conduct, compliance, security and continuity.

---

# 51. Employee Relations and Conduct

Manage employee relations under applicable policies. Personnel should understand professional conduct, respect, integrity, confidentiality, security and compliance expectations.

---

# 52. Workplace Concerns

Workplace concerns should be received, protected, assessed, assigned, handled and closed according to applicable procedures.

---

# 53. Health, Safety and Wellbeing

Integrate relevant workplace health, safety and wellbeing requirements. Safety-critical personnel require appropriate competence and authorization.

---

# 54. Absence Management

Manage absence with appropriate privacy, fairness and continuity considerations.

---

# 55. Workforce Compliance

People processes should comply with applicable employment, labour, health and safety, privacy, security, tax, contractual and regulatory requirements.

---

# 56. Employee Privacy and HR Security

Personnel data must be protected. HR information requires appropriate access control, confidentiality, integrity, retention and monitoring.

---

# 57. Workforce Records and Retention

Workforce records require defined retention and disposition requirements according to applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and organizational requirements.

---

# 58. Employee Separation

Exit should include notice, handover, knowledge transfer, asset return, access revocation, records, final payments and contract closure as applicable.

---

# 59. Workforce Suppliers

External workforce services must integrate with the Third-Party Governance framework.

---

# 60. People Assurance

People Assurance provides confidence that workforce governance and people controls remain effective. Material controls should be tested according to risk.

---

# 61. People Evidence

Evidence may include role profiles, competence records, training records, authorizations, access reviews, performance records, succession plans, workforce assessments and HR controls.

---

# 62. Findings, Exceptions and Remediation

People Findings identify weaknesses. Remediation identifies finding, cause, action, owner, due date, evidence and validation. Exceptions identify requirement, deviation, reason, risk, compensating control, approval and expiry.

---

# 63. People Metrics

Metrics may include headcount, capacity, turnover, absence, critical skills, competence coverage, training completion, certification expiry, performance-review completion, succession coverage, key-person risk and open findings.

---

# 64. People Dashboard

May include workforce, capacity, skills, training, competence, performance, succession, risk, compliance and findings.

---

# 65. Review Cadence

Daily: critical absence, staffing gaps, safety-critical competence and urgent workforce risks. Weekly: capacity, recruitment, training, absence, critical roles and issues. Monthly: workforce, turnover, capacity, competence, training, performance, succession, risk and compliance. Quarterly: workforce strategy, critical skills, key-person risk, succession, talent, training effectiveness and resilience. Annual: organizational capability, competence, training, performance, succession, people risk, compliance, maturity and improvement.

---

# 66. People Maturity

Assess governance, workforce planning, roles, competence, skills, recruitment, onboarding, training, performance, talent, succession, access lifecycle, employee privacy, workforce risk, assurance, records and improvement.

---

# 67. Maturity Levels

1 – Initial; 2 – Repeatable; 3 – Defined; 4 – Managed; 5 – Optimized.

# 78. Definition of Ready

A people or workforce work item is Ready when the role, person, workforce requirement, competence gap, training need, performance issue, authorization, succession requirement, finding, exception or improvement is clearly identified; ownership, role, authority, risk, privacy, compliance and applicable requirements are known; and evidence, assessment, training, approval, access, records and acceptance requirements are defined.

---

# 79. Definition of Done

A people or workforce work item is Done when requirement is identified; owner/authority assigned; people/workforce action completed; HR/business/security/privacy/compliance validation completed; role/competence/training/authorization/records updated; evidence captured; exceptions/findings addressed; and outcome accepted.

---

# 80. Final People Principle

> **People are a critical enterprise capability and must be governed according to role, competence, responsibility, risk and organizational purpose.**

---

# 81. Final Workforce Principle

> **MFM must maintain sufficient qualified and appropriately authorized personnel to perform critical activities effectively and consistently.**

---

# 82. Final Role Principle

> **Material responsibilities and authorities must be explicit, assigned and periodically reviewed.**

---

# 83. Final Competence Principle

> **Personnel must possess or develop the competence required for the responsibilities they perform.**

---

# 84. Final Training Principle

> **Training must be driven by role requirements, competence gaps, risk, change and organizational need.**

---

# 85. Final Authorization Principle

> **Personnel must receive only the authority and access necessary for their assigned responsibilities.**

---

# 86. Final Performance Principle

> **Performance management must align individual contribution, competence and development with organizational objectives.**

---

# 87. Final Resilience Principle

> **Critical workforce dependencies must be supported through cross-training, knowledge transfer, succession and appropriate backup capability.**

---

# 88. Final Privacy Principle

> **Personnel information must be protected throughout the employee lifecycle according to applicable privacy and security requirements.**

---

# 89. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material people controls must be supported by evidence and periodically assessed for effectiveness.**

---

# 90. Final Improvement Principle

> **Workforce data, performance feedback, competence gaps, incidents, findings and organizational change must continuously improve MFM people capability.**

---

# 91. Final Integration Principle

> **People and Workforce Management must integrate with Governance, Risk, Compliance, Financial Management, Procurement, Cybersecurity, Privacy, Data Management, Technical Operations, Service Management, Business Continuity and Architecture.**
