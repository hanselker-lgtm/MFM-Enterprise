# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Resilience Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities maintain availability, reliability, recovery capability and continuous development under disruption, uncertainty and changing conditions.

The Intelligence Resilience Architecture Framework shall provide:

- resilience architecture principles
- continuity frameworks
- recovery models
- fault tolerance structures
- adaptive resilience models
- resilience capability models
- resilience integration structures
- resilience governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through robust, reliable and resilient ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Resilience Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Resilience Model
- Resilient Intelligence Architecture
- Operational Resilience Architecture
- Adaptive Recovery Architecture
- Capability Resilience Model
- Resilience Architecture Integration
- Resilience Governance Model
- Resilience Evolution Framework
- Resilience Metrics
- Resilience Maturity Model
- Resilience Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Resilience Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Resilience Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Continuous Availability

Intelligence capabilities shall remain available and operational under changing conditions.

---

## Principle 2 – Fault Tolerance

Intelligence capabilities shall withstand failures and disruptions.

---

## Principle 3 – Recovery Capability

Intelligence capabilities shall recover effectively after disruption.

---

## Principle 4 – Adaptive Resilience

Resilience shall improve through learning, feedback and adaptation.

---

## Principle 5 – Operational Continuity

Critical Intelligence functions shall maintain continuity.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Resilience Evolution

Resilience capability shall continuously improve.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Resilience Governance

Resilience mechanisms shall maintain transparency, accountability and controlled development.
