# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Information Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise information as a strategic asset.

The Information Architecture Framework shall provide:

- information principles
- information governance
- information structure
- information ownership
- information lifecycle management
- information quality management
- information protection
- information improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise information supports strategy, operations, decisions and value creation.

---

# 2. Scope

The Information Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture information domains:

- Enterprise Information Model
- Information Governance
- Information Strategy
- Information Domains
- Information Assets
- Information Lifecycle
- Information Ownership
- Information Classification
- Information Flows
- Information Quality
- Information Integration
- Information Security Alignment
- Information Compliance
- Information Performance
- Information Evolution

The framework applies to:

- strategic information management
- operational information management
- business information management
- data and information governance
- decision support
- transformation activities

---

# 3. Enterprise Information Architecture Principles

The Information Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Information as a Strategic Asset

Enterprise information shall be managed as a valuable organizational asset.

---

## Principle 2 – Information Ownership

All critical information shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Principle 3 – Information Quality

Information shall be accurate, complete, consistent and reliable.

---

## Principle 4 – Information Accessibility

Information shall be available to authorized users when required.

---

## Principle 5 – Information Protection

Information shall be protected according to value, sensitivity and risk.

---

## Principle 6 – Information Lifecycle Management

Information shall be managed throughout its complete lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 – Information Value

Information management shall support business outcomes and decision-making.

---

# 9. Information Domain Architecture

The Information Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise information is structured into logical domains to enable effective management and governance.

Information domains provide a common structure for organizing Enterprise information assets.

---

# 9.1 Information Domain Principles

Information domains shall ensure:

- clear information ownership
- logical information organization
- business alignment
- controlled information usage
- consistent information management

---

# 9.2 Information Domain Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Information Domain | Logical grouping of information |
| Information Owner | Accountability for information |
| Information Assets | Managed information resources |
| Information Relationships | Connections between information |
| Information Standards | Rules for management |

# 15. Information Quality Management

The Information Quality Management Model defines how Enterprise information quality is measured, maintained and improved.

Information Quality Management ensures that information remains accurate, complete, consistent and reliable for business usage.

---

# 15.1 Information Quality Principles

Information quality shall ensure:

- accuracy
- completeness
- consistency
- timeliness
- validity
- reliability

---

# 15.2 Information Quality Dimensions

| Dimension | Description |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Information correctly represents reality |
| Completeness | Required information is available |
| Consistency | Information is consistent across sources |
| Timeliness | Information is available when needed |
| Validity | Information follows defined rules |
| Reliability | Information can be trusted |
