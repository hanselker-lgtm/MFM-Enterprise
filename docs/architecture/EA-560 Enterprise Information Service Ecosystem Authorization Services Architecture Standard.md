# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Authorization Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Authorization Services shall follow these principles.

## Authorization by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include authorization considerations from initial design through operational lifecycle.

---

## Least Privilege Authorization

Authorization shall provide only the access and permissions required to perform approved activities.

---

## Evidence-Based Authorization

Authorization decisions shall be supported by documented policies, identity information, context and approved evidence.

---

## Governed Authorization

Authorization activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.

---

## Continuous Authorization Improvement

Authorization capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
