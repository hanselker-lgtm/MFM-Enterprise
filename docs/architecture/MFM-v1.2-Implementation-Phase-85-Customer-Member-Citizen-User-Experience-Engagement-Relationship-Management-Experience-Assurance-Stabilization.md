# 1. Purpose

This document defines the eighty-fifth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-84 – Human Resources, Workforce Planning, Organization Design, Role Governance, Employee Lifecycle & People Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled experience and relationship capability covering customer, member, citizen and user governance, stakeholder experience, experience design, user journeys, customer journeys, member journeys, personas, experience requirements, accessibility, service experience, feedback management, satisfaction, engagement, relationship management, communication touchpoints, experience analytics, experience improvement, complaint and compliment insight and experience assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must ensure that people interacting with the organization experience coherent, accessible, understandable, responsive and trustworthy services and relationships, while experience evidence is continuously converted into measurable improvements.**

# 132. Definition of Ready

An experience work item is Ready when:

- Target audience is defined.
- Intended outcome is clear.
- Relevant journey or touchpoint is identified.
- Experience requirements are known.
- Accessibility needs are considered.
- Owner is assigned.
- Measurement approach is defined where appropriate.

# 134. Final Experience Principle

> **Experience must be designed around the needs and outcomes of the people using or receiving the organization's services.**

---

# 135. Final Journey Principle

> **The quality of an experience is determined by the end-to-end journey rather than by isolated touchpoints.**

---

# 136. Final Accessibility Principle

> **Accessibility must be designed into services, journeys and interactions from the beginning rather than treated only as a remediation activity.**

---

# 137. Final Touchpoint Principle

> **Every material touchpoint must have clear purpose, ownership, accessibility, privacy, security and service expectations.**

---

# 138. Final Feedback Principle

> **Feedback is organizational evidence and must be converted into insight and improvement rather than merely collected.**

---

# 139. Final Complaint Principle

> **Complaints must be handled respectfully and transparently while recurring complaints must be used to identify and address underlying causes.**

---

# 140. Final Engagement Principle

> **Engagement should create meaningful participation and measurable relationship value rather than communication volume alone.**

---

# 141. Final Relationship Principle

> **Material relationships require clear ownership, contextual understanding, appropriate interaction and visibility of risks and opportunities.**

---

# 142. Final Analytics Principle

> **Experience analytics should combine quantitative measures with qualitative evidence to reveal causes, patterns and opportunities.**

---

# 143. Final Improvement Principle

> **Experience improvement must connect evidence, priority, change and measurable outcome.**

---

# 144. Final Assurance Principle

> **Experience assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that journeys, touchpoints, accessibility, feedback and service standards are operating as intended.**

---

# 145. Final Integration Principle

> **Experience Management must integrate with Membership, Customer, Service, Product, Process, Communications, Accessibility, Privacy, Security, Data, CRM and Enterprise Assurance governance.**

---

# 146. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage experience through a controlled lifecycle connecting personas, journeys, touchpoints, requirements, accessibility, feedback, satisfaction, engagement, relationships, complaints, analytics, improvement and continuous assurance.**
