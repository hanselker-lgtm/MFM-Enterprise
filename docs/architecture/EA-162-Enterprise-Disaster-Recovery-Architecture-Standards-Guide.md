# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise disaster recovery throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise disaster recovery ensures that infrastructure, platforms, services and applications can recover from major operational disruptions through standardized planning, recovery procedures, recovery objectives and governance while preserving operational continuity and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Disaster Recovery Planning
- Recovery Objectives (RTO/RPO)
- Recovery Sites
- Disaster Classification
- Recovery Procedures
- Recovery Testing
- Business Continuity Integration
- Governance

All enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Disaster Recovery Principles

Enterprise disaster recovery shall follow these principles.

- Recovery by Design
- Defined Recovery Objectives
- Verified Recovery Procedures
- Operational Continuity
- Standardized Recovery Planning
- Governance by Default
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Disaster recovery implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Disaster Recovery Domains

Enterprise disaster recovery shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Recovery Planning
- Recovery Objectives
- Recovery Sites
- Recovery Procedures
- Disaster Classification
- Recovery Testing
- Recovery Verification
- Business Continuity Integration

Additional disaster recovery domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Disaster Recovery Ownership

Each disaster recovery domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the disaster recovery lifecycle.

---

# 7. Disaster Recovery Governance

Enterprise disaster recovery governance shall define

- disaster recovery governance
- recovery approval
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- recovery verification
- governance reporting

Disaster recovery governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Disaster Recovery Responsibilities

Enterprise disaster recovery shall provide controlled management of disaster recovery capabilities.

Disaster recovery responsibilities shall

- separate disaster recovery from business execution
- coordinate disaster recovery ownership
- ensure operational continuity
- validate recovery objectives
- preserve recovery traceability
- support enterprise operational resilience

Disaster recovery implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Disaster Classification

Enterprise disaster recovery shall implement standardized disaster classification.

Disaster classification shall

- classify infrastructure disasters
- classify application disasters
- classify data disasters
- classify cybersecurity incidents
- preserve classification history
- maintain classification traceability

Disaster classification shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Recovery Objectives (RTO/RPO)

Enterprise disaster recovery shall define standardized recovery objectives.

Recovery objectives shall

- define Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- define Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- support business continuity requirements
- preserve objective history
- maintain objective traceability
- support operational planning

Recovery objectives shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Recovery Sites

Enterprise disaster recovery shall implement standardized recovery sites.

Recovery sites shall

- support primary recovery locations
- support secondary recovery locations
- support geographically separated facilities where applicable
- preserve recovery site documentation
- maintain recovery site traceability
- support operational resilience

Recovery sites shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Recovery Procedures

Enterprise disaster recovery shall implement standardized recovery procedures.

Recovery procedures shall

- support controlled recovery
- support prioritized service restoration
- support operational verification
- preserve recovery history
- maintain recovery traceability
- support disaster diagnostics

Recovery procedures shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Disaster Recovery Dependencies

Enterprise disaster recovery shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- backup infrastructure
- recovery platforms
- networking infrastructure
- identity services
- monitoring systems
- enterprise governance

Disaster recovery implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Disaster Recovery Documentation

Each disaster recovery domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- recovery objectives
- ownership information
- disaster classifications
- recovery procedures
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Disaster Recovery Lifecycle

Enterprise disaster recovery shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Planned
- Designed
- Classified
- Implemented
- Verified
- Operational
- Tested
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Retired

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Disaster Recovery Quality Attributes

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- reliability
- recoverability
- availability
- resilience
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability
- operational readiness

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the disaster recovery lifecycle.

---

# 17. Disaster Recovery Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized disaster recovery registry.

The registry shall contain

- recovery identifiers
- ownership assignments
- disaster classifications
- lifecycle status
- recovery objectives
- recovery site configurations
- documentation references
- governance status

The disaster recovery registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise disaster recovery.

---

# 18. Disaster Recovery Reviews

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- recovery quality
- disaster classification completeness
- recovery objective compliance
- recovery procedure effectiveness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Disaster Recovery Metrics

Enterprise disaster recovery shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- recovery success rate
- recovery time achievement
- recovery point achievement
- recovery testing frequency
- disaster recovery readiness
- operational downtime
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous disaster recovery improvement.

---

# 20. Disaster Recovery Verification

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm recovery objectives
- verify disaster classifications
- verify recovery procedures
- verify recovery sites
- verify recovery testing
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve operational readiness

Disaster recovery verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Disaster Recovery Improvement

Enterprise disaster recovery shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve recovery readiness
- improve recovery effectiveness
- improve operational continuity
- improve disaster preparedness
- strengthen governance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall handle disaster recovery-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify recovery execution failures
- classify recovery site failures
- classify recovery objective violations
- classify recovery verification failures
- classify recovery testing failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Disaster recovery exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational continuity or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Disaster recovery implementations may depend upon

- approved backup infrastructure
- approved recovery platforms
- approved networking infrastructure
- approved identity services
- approved monitoring systems
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Disaster recovery implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external disaster recovery services

Disaster recovery capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A disaster recovery implementation is compliant when

- Disaster recovery responsibilities are documented.
- Disaster classifications are implemented.
- Recovery objectives (RTO/RPO) are defined.
- Recovery procedures are documented.
- Recovery sites are implemented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Disaster Recovery Registry is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Recovery verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Undefined Recovery Objectives

Enterprise disaster recovery shall never operate without documented Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).

---

## Untested Recovery Procedures

Recovery procedures shall never remain untested for critical enterprise systems.

---

## Single Recovery Site

Critical enterprise services shall never rely solely on a single recovery location where resilience requirements mandate redundancy.

---

## Missing Recovery Verification

Disaster recovery procedures shall never be considered complete without documented verification.

---

## Undocumented Disaster Recovery Dependencies

Disaster recovery implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Disaster Recovery Outside Governance

Disaster recovery implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise disaster recovery implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- recovery quality
- disaster classification completeness
- recovery objective compliance
- recovery procedure effectiveness
- recovery site readiness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational continuity
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Disaster Recovery Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing disaster recovery throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and applications implement standardized disaster recovery planning, recovery objectives, recovery procedures, recovery verification and governance through controlled lifecycle management and continuous improvement while preserving operational continuity, resilience and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise disaster recovery implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
