# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Metaverse Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise Metaverse capabilities.

The Metaverse Architecture Framework shall provide:

- metaverse principles
- metaverse governance
- metaverse strategy
- metaverse capability architecture
- virtual environment architecture
- 3D experience architecture
- digital avatar architecture
- spatial computing architecture
- extended reality architecture
- virtual collaboration architecture
- metaverse data architecture
- metaverse integration architecture
- metaverse security architecture
- metaverse operations
- metaverse performance management
- metaverse evolution

The framework ensures that immersive digital environments and virtual experiences are designed, governed and operated as integrated Enterprise capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

The Metaverse Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture metaverse domains:

- Enterprise Metaverse Model
- Metaverse Governance
- Metaverse Strategy
- Metaverse Capability Architecture

The framework applies to:

- virtual environments
- immersive collaboration platforms
- extended reality solutions
- digital experience environments
- digital twins connected to virtual worlds
- virtual training environments
- enterprise immersive services

---

# 3. Enterprise Metaverse Architecture Principles

The Metaverse Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Immersive Value Creation

Metaverse capabilities shall create measurable value through immersive digital experiences.

---

## Principle 2 – Digital and Physical Alignment

Virtual environments shall maintain meaningful relationships with physical assets and Enterprise operations.

---

## Principle 3 – Human-Centered Experience

Metaverse solutions shall improve human interaction, collaboration and experience.

---

## Principle 4 – Identity and Trust

Digital identities and virtual representations shall be securely managed.

---

## Principle 5 – Interoperability

Metaverse capabilities shall integrate with Enterprise platforms, services and digital ecosystems.

---

## Principle 6 – Secure Immersive Environments

Metaverse environments shall include security and privacy controls throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Metaverse Evolution

Metaverse capabilities shall continuously evolve according to Enterprise needs and technology development.

---
