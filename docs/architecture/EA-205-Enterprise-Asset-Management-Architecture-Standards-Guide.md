# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Asset Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Asset Management ensures that physical and digital assets are consistently identified, classified, owned, managed, maintained and retired while preserving operational stability, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Asset Identification
- Asset Classification
- Asset Ownership
- Asset Inventory
- Asset Lifecycle Management
- Asset Verification
- Asset Retirement
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Asset Management Principles

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall follow these principles.

- Complete Asset Visibility
- Single Source of Truth
- Lifecycle Governance
- Complete Traceability
- Accurate Ownership
- Operational Stability
- Technology Independence
- Centralized Governance

Asset Management implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

---

# 5. Asset Management Responsibilities

Enterprise Asset Management shall provide

- asset identification
- asset classification
- asset inventory management
- lifecycle management
- ownership management
- verification
- retirement management
- governance reporting
- compliance verification

Additional Asset Management responsibilities shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

# 6. Asset Management Ownership

Asset Management ownership shall define

- business ownership

- operational ownership
- governance responsibility
- asset stewardship

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the Asset Management lifecycle.

# 7. Asset Management Governance

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall define

- governance structure

- architecture review responsibilities
- operational governance
- reporting responsibilities

# 8. Asset Identification

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall implement standardized asset identification.

Asset identification shall

- assign unique asset identifiers
- identify physical assets
- identify digital assets
- identify service assets
- preserve identification traceability
- maintain identification consistency

Asset identification shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 9. Asset Classification

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall implement standardized asset classification.

Asset classification shall

- classify business assets
- classify technical assets
- classify infrastructure assets
- classify information assets
- preserve classification traceability
- maintain classification consistency

Asset classification policies shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Asset Ownership

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall implement standardized asset ownership.

Asset ownership shall

- assign business owners
- assign technical owners
- assign operational custodians
- preserve ownership traceability
- maintain ownership consistency
- support governance accountability

Asset ownership shall remain documented throughout the asset lifecycle.

---

# 11. Asset Inventory

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall implement standardized asset inventory management.

Asset inventory shall

- maintain complete asset records
- maintain asset status
- maintain asset location where applicable
- preserve inventory traceability
- maintain inventory consistency
- support enterprise reporting

Asset inventory shall remain continuously updated.

---

# 12. Asset Lifecycle Management

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall implement standardized asset lifecycle management.

Asset lifecycle management shall

- manage asset acquisition
- manage asset deployment
- manage operational maintenance
- manage asset upgrades
- manage asset retirement
- preserve lifecycle traceability

Asset lifecycle management shall align with Enterprise Change and Release Management standards.

---

# 13. Asset Verification

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall implement standardized asset verification.

Asset verification shall

- verify asset existence
- verify ownership accuracy
- verify inventory accuracy
- verify lifecycle status
- preserve verification traceability
- maintain verification consistency

Asset verification shall be performed regularly.

---

- approved configuration management services

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall handle asset management-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify asset identification failures
- classify asset ownership failures
- classify inventory management failures
- classify lifecycle management failures
- classify verification failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Asset Management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, asset integrity, operational stability, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Asset Management implementations may depend upon

- approved configuration management services
- approved change management services
- approved procurement systems
- approved inventory repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external asset management providers

Asset Management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Asset Management implementation is compliant when

- Assets are uniquely identified.
- Asset classifications are maintained.
- Asset ownership is documented.
- Asset inventories are current.
- Asset lifecycle management is implemented.
- Asset verification is performed.
- Asset retirement procedures are documented.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Unregistered Assets

Enterprise assets shall never exist without registration in the approved asset inventory.

---

## Undefined Ownership

Assets shall never exist without documented business and technical ownership where applicable.

---

## Outdated Asset Inventory

Asset inventories shall never remain inconsistent with the operational environment after approved changes.

---

## Unauthorized Asset Modifications

Asset information shall never be modified outside approved governance and Change Management procedures.

---

## Unverified Asset Retirement

Assets shall never be retired without updating inventories, ownership records and lifecycle documentation.

---

## Asset Management Logic Inside Business Components

Business components shall never implement independent asset management mechanisms outside approved Enterprise Asset Management services.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise Asset Management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- asset identification compliance
- classification compliance
- ownership compliance
- inventory compliance
- lifecycle compliance
- verification compliance
- dependency compliance
- documentation completeness
- operational readiness
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Asset Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Asset Management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that physical and digital assets are consistently identified, classified, owned, managed, maintained and retired while preserving asset integrity, operational stability, governance, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Asset Management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
