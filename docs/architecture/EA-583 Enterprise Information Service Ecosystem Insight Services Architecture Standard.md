# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Insight Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Insight Services shall follow these principles.

## Insight by Design

Enterprise insight shall be deliberately developed from information, knowledge, experience and wisdom.

---

## Evidence-Based Understanding

Enterprise insight shall be supported by reliable evidence and appropriate interpretation.

---

## Contextual Awareness

Enterprise insight shall consider business, operational, technical and strategic context.

---

## Insight Ownership

Insight domains shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Insight Reuse

Enterprise insight shall be preserved and reused to improve future decisions.

---

## Continuous Discovery

Enterprise insight shall continuously evolve through analysis, learning and experience.

---

## Insight Measurement

Insight capabilities shall be evaluated through appropriate indicators and evidence.

---

## Governed Insight Management

Insight activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.
