## Purpose
Establish the authoritative mandatory-measurement layer defining how measurable characteristics of an applicable mandatory state are quantified, compared, interpreted and governed. The layer controls measurement objectives, scope, definitions, methods, sources, quality, integrity, frequency, baselines, thresholds, uncertainty, deviations, escalation and review.

## Core Principle
Measurement shall provide a reliable basis for determining the state of an applicable mandatory condition. A measurement is not merely a number; it is a governed observation with a defined meaning, source, method, context, quality and interpretation.

```text
MANDATORY STATE
      ↓
MEASUREMENT OBJECTIVE
      ↓
MEASUREMENT DEFINITION
      ↓
SOURCE / METHOD / CONTEXT
      ↓
COLLECT / CALCULATE
      ↓
VALIDATE QUALITY / INTEGRITY
      ↓
COMPARE WITH BASELINE / THRESHOLD
      ↓
INTERPRET
      ↓
PASS / WARNING / FAIL / UNDETERMINED
```

DEFINED STATE

## Measurement Structure

| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| State | State being measured |
| Objective | What measurement must establish |
| Metric | Defined measurable variable |
| Unit | Unit or scale |
| Source | Data origin |
| Method | Collection/calculation method |
| Context | Conditions of measurement |
| Frequency | Required measurement interval |
| Baseline | Reference value/state |
| Threshold | Decision boundary |
| Uncertainty | Known measurement uncertainty |
| Result | Observed value or classification |
| Interpretation | Meaning of result |

## Measurement Objective

The measurement objective shall state why the measurement is needed and how it supports verification of the mandatory state.

```text
REQUIRED STATE
↓
MEASUREMENT OBJECTIVE
↓
WHAT MUST BE QUANTIFIED / ASSESSED?
```

## Measurement Scope

Scope shall identify the services, systems, controls, processes, data, environments, populations, samples and time periods included. Exclusions shall be explicit.

## Measurement Definition

Each material measurement shall define what is measured, how it is represented, units or scale, calculation logic where applicable, and interpretation rules.

## Measurement Method

Methods shall be suitable for the characteristic being measured and proportionate to materiality. Automated collection does not remove the need for method validation.

## Measurement Source

Sources shall be identifiable, authoritative enough for the intended use and protected against inappropriate manipulation. Source limitations shall be recorded.

## Measurement Quality

Measurement quality shall address accuracy, precision where relevant, completeness, consistency, timeliness, representativeness and fitness for purpose.

## Measurement Integrity

Measurement data shall be protected against unauthorized alteration, loss, corruption or substitution. Material measurements shall retain sufficient provenance to support reconstruction.

## Measurement Frequency

Frequency shall reflect the rate at which the measured state can materially change and the risk of delayed detection.

```text
RATE OF CHANGE + RISK
        ↓
REQUIRED MEASUREMENT FREQUENCY
        ↓
OBSERVE BEFORE MATERIAL REGRESSION ESCAPES DETECTION
```

## Measurement Baseline

Baselines establish a governed reference for comparison. A baseline shall have a version, effective date, source and rationale. A changed baseline shall not silently rewrite historical comparisons.

## Measurement Thresholds

Thresholds define the meaning of measured values and the response to deviations.

```text
MEASUREMENT
    ↓
WITHIN ACCEPTABLE RANGE?
 ├── YES → NORMAL / PASS
 └── NO
      ↓
WARNING OR FAILURE?
 ├── WARNING → MONITOR / ACT
 └── FAILURE → BLOCK / ESCALATE
```

## Measurement Uncertainty

Where uncertainty can materially affect the determination, it shall be identified and considered. Uncertainty shall not be used as an informal basis for ignoring an adverse result.

## Security Measurement

Security measurements may include control effectiveness, exposure, vulnerability state, access anomalies, availability, integrity indicators and incident measures. Thresholds shall align with mandatory security requirements.

## Resilience Measurement

Resilience measurements may include recovery performance, restoration time, capacity, dependency availability, continuity readiness and degraded-mode performance.

## Compliance Measurement

Compliance measurements shall assess actual conformity where quantification is appropriate. Administrative activity counts shall not substitute for evidence of actual compliant state.

## Data Measurement

Data measurements may include accuracy, completeness, timeliness, consistency, integrity, lineage and availability. Data-quality metrics shall preserve context and source.

## AI and Agent Measurement

AI and agent measurements shall consider applicable performance, policy adherence, authority boundaries, intervention rates, exceptions, unsafe actions, tool use and other governance-relevant indicators.

```text
AI / AGENT
↓
MEASURE RESULT + BEHAVIOUR + GOVERNANCE BOUNDARY
↓
COMPARE WITH REQUIRED LIMITS
↓
PASS / WARNING / FAIL
```

## Measurement Deviation

A measurement deviation occurs when an observed result falls outside an approved range, threshold or expected state.

```text
MEASURED RESULT
      ↓
DEVIATION?
 ├── NO  → RECORD / CONTINUE
 └── YES → CLASSIFY
              ↓
CONTAIN / ESCALATE / REMEDIATE
```

## Measurement Escalation

Material, systemic, persistent or ambiguous measurement deviations shall be escalated according to the applicable governance path. Uncertainty that could conceal material failure shall be treated as a governance concern.

## Measurement Review and Learning

Measurement definitions, sources, methods, frequencies and thresholds shall be reviewed following repeated false alarms, missed regressions, material incidents, architecture changes, changed risks, changed criteria or evidence that a metric no longer represents the intended state.

## Measurement Determination Model
```text
MEASUREMENT
↓
SOURCE / METHOD VALID?
├── NO → INVALID / UNDETERMINED
└── YES
     ↓
QUALITY SUFFICIENT?
├── NO → INVALID / LIMITED / UNDETERMINED
└── YES
     ↓
COMPARE WITH BASELINE / THRESHOLD
     ↓
INTERPRET
     ↓
PASS / WARNING / FAIL
```

## Measurement Change Control
Changes to metric definitions, sources, methods, frequencies, baselines or thresholds shall be governed, approved, versioned and effective-dated. Historical measurements shall remain associated with the versions used when they were produced.

```text
CURRENT MEASUREMENT DEFINITION
        ↓
CHANGE PROPOSAL
        ↓
IMPACT / RISK ASSESSMENT
        ↓
AUTHORITY APPROVAL
        ↓
NEW VERSION
        ↓
EFFECTIVE DATE
```
