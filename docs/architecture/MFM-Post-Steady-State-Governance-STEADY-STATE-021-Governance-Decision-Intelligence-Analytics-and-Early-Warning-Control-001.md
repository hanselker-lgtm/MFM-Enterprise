# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-021 establishes the controlled mechanism for turning governed information into decision-support intelligence, analytics, trend detection and early-warning signals.

The purpose is to improve governance awareness and decision preparation without allowing analytics, models, indicators or predictive outputs to become unauthorized decisions.

The operating sequence is:

```text
GOVERNED INFORMATION
↓
QUALITY / CONTEXT CHECK
↓
ANALYSIS
↓
PATTERN / TREND DETECTION
↓
INTELLIGENCE
↓
EARLY-WARNING SIGNAL
↓
MATERIALITY ASSESSMENT
↓
DECISION PREPARATION
↓
AUTHORIZED DECISION
↓
OUTCOME / VALIDATION
↓
LEARNING
```

---

# 2. Core Principle

Decision intelligence supports governance; it does not replace governance authority.

The following distinctions are mandatory:

```text
DATA
≠
INFORMATION

INFORMATION
≠
INTELLIGENCE

INTELLIGENCE
≠
DECISION

PREDICTION
≠
FACT

SIGNAL
≠
EVENT

CORRELATION
≠
CAUSATION

MODEL OUTPUT
≠
AUTHORIZED ACTION

HIGH CONFIDENCE
≠
CERTAINTY
```

```text

```

---

# 5. Intelligence Boundary

Decision intelligence shall remain advisory unless explicitly incorporated into an authorized governance decision mechanism.

```text
INTELLIGENCE
↓
DECISION PREPARATION
↓
HUMAN / AUTHORIZED GOVERNANCE DECISION
```

Not:

```text
INTELLIGENCE
↓
AUTOMATIC DECISION
```

unless such automation has been explicitly authorized, bounded, validated and governed.

---

# 6. Analytics Classification

Analytics may be classified as:

```text
DESCRIPTIVE
DIAGNOSTIC
PREDICTIVE
PRESCRIPTIVE
```

The classification shall be explicit where it affects interpretation.

---

# 7. Descriptive Analytics

Descriptive analytics answers:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED?
```

Examples:

```text
Historical Performance
Event Counts
Decision Volumes
Exception Counts
Outcome Results
```

---

# 8. Diagnostic Analytics

Diagnostic analytics addresses:

```text
WHY MIGHT IT HAVE HAPPENED?
```

Diagnostic conclusions shall distinguish:

```text
OBSERVED FACT
from
INFERENCE
from
HYPOTHESIS
```

---

# 9. Predictive Analytics

Predictive analytics addresses:

```text
WHAT MAY HAPPEN?
```

Predictions shall identify:

```text
Model / Method
Input Data
Time Horizon
Assumptions
Uncertainty
Limitations
Confidence Where Meaningful
```

A prediction shall never be represented as a guaranteed future state.

---

# 10. Prescriptive Analytics

Prescriptive analytics addresses:

```text
WHAT COULD BE DONE?
```

Prescriptive outputs are recommendations unless an explicit governance mechanism authorizes automated execution.

---

# 11. Early Warning

An early-warning signal identifies a condition that may require attention before a material outcome occurs.

The model is:

```text
INDICATOR
↓
THRESHOLD / PATTERN
↓
SIGNAL
↓
ASSESSMENT
↓
ACTION OR NO ACTION
```

A signal is not itself proof of an event.

---

```text

EARLY WARNING
MATERIAL WARNING
CRITICAL WARNING
FALSE POSITIVE
CLOSED
UNVERIFIED
```


# 65. Final STEADY-STATE-021 Statement

> **STEADY-STATE-021 establishes the controlled decision-intelligence layer for governance.
