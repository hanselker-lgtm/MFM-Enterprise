# 1. Purpose

This document defines the fifty-fifth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-54 – Project & Portfolio Management, Planning, Resource, Milestone, Delivery & Project Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled grant and funding management capability covering funding opportunities, eligibility, applications, assessment, approval, awards, budgets, disbursement, conditions, reporting, compliance, monitoring, evidence, closure and assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must manage grants and funding as controlled end-to-end lifecycles with clear eligibility, accountable assessment, authorized awards, traceable financial use, documented conditions, measurable outcomes, compliance evidence and controlled closure.**

# 131. Final Grant Principle

> **MFM must manage grants as complete funding lifecycles from opportunity identification through controlled closure.**

---

# 132. Final Eligibility Principle

> **Grant eligibility must be assessed before material application effort is committed and must be supported by appropriate evidence.**

---

# 133. Final Application Principle

> **Grant applications must be complete, accurate, authorized and submitted within defined deadlines.**

---

# 134. Final Award Principle

> **Every grant award must have clearly documented funding, purpose, conditions, responsibilities, reporting and payment requirements.**

---

# 135. Final Financial Principle

> **Grant expenditure must be traceable to approved budgets, eligible activities and sufficient financial evidence.**

---

# 136. Final Compliance Principle

> **Grant conditions and obligations must have accountable owners, deadlines, evidence and controlled remediation of exceptions.**

---

# 137. Final Evidence Principle

> **Grant decisions, expenditure, activities, outcomes and compliance must be supported by sufficient and retrievable evidence.**

---

# 138. Final Reporting Principle

> **Grant reporting must be accurate, timely, reconciled and supported by the evidence required by the funder and MFM governance.**

---

# 139. Final Assurance Principle

> **Grant assurance must provide evidence-based confidence that funding is governed, used appropriately, reported accurately and managed according to applicable obligations.**

---

# 140. Final Closure Principle

> **A grant is not operationally complete until financial, reporting, compliance, evidence and record-retention obligations have been addressed.**

---

# 141. Final Integration Principle

> **Grant management must integrate with projects, finance, procurement, membership, services, documents, processes, reporting, security, privacy and enterprise governance.**

---

# 142. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should operate grants and funding through a controlled lifecycle connecting opportunity, eligibility, application, assessment, award, budget, disbursement, monitoring, reporting, compliance, evidence, assurance and closure.**
