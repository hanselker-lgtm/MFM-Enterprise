# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-002 establishes the continuous monitoring and trigger-management mechanism for the steady-state governance environment.

The purpose is to ensure that material changes, risks, dependencies, outcomes, value variances, evidence gaps and governance signals are detected and routed through the established governance system.

The operating principle is:

```text
MONITOR
↓
DETECT
↓
TRIGGER
↓
CLASSIFY
↓
ROUTE
↓
ASSESS
↓
DECIDE / ACT / CONTINUE
```

