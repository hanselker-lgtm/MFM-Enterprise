# 2. Architectural Position

RG-496 established the enterprise strategic option network, future-state investment governance, discontinuity readiness, adaptive resilience portfolios and autonomous strategic option orchestration.

RG-497 moves from **option availability to controlled state transition**. The architecture defines how the enterprise decides that an option should be exercised, how several options converge toward a future state, how transition risk is governed, and how autonomous mechanisms operate during a strategic transition without becoming strategic authorities.

```text
RG-495 MULTI-HORIZON VALUE
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
OPTION EXERCISE

        ↓
DISCONTINUITY RESPONSE
        ↓

        ↓
FUTURE-STATE STABILISATION
```

# 3. Core Architectural Question

**How can an enterprise move from one strategic state to another at the right time and with controlled exposure, while coordinating option exercise, dependencies, resources, resilience, capital, liquidity and human decision authority during periods of elevated uncertainty and change?**

# 4. Governing Principle

**A future state SHALL be entered through an explicitly governed transition, not through uncontrolled accumulation of autonomous decisions. Option exercise SHALL be evidence-based, transition dependencies SHALL be visible, resilience SHALL be protected throughout the transition, and the enterprise SHALL retain the ability to pause, reverse or redirect where authorised.**

```text
CURRENT STATE
      ↓

      ↓

      ↺
```
