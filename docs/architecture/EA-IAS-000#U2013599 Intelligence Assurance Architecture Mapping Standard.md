# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Assurance Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive assurance architecture for ensuring trust, reliability, quality and continuous improvement of Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Assurance Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence assurance principles
- assurance governance
- intelligence quality assurance
- validation architecture
- verification architecture
- reliability architecture
- trust architecture
- transparency architecture
- explainability architecture
- safety assurance
- performance assurance
- assurance operations
- assurance evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities deliver reliable, trustworthy and measurable outcomes.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Assurance Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture assurance domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Assurance Model
- Assurance Governance
- Quality Assurance Architecture
- Validation Architecture
- Verification Architecture
- Reliability Architecture
- Trust Architecture
- Transparency Architecture
- Explainability Architecture
- Safety Assurance
- Performance Assurance
- Assurance Operations
- Assurance Maturity
- Assurance Roadmaps
- Assurance Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- intelligent operating models
- future adaptive enterprises

---

# 3. Intelligence Assurance Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Assurance Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Trustworthy Intelligence

All intelligence capabilities shall provide results that can be trusted and assessed.

---

## Principle 2 – Continuous Validation

Intelligence capabilities shall be continuously validated against defined requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Evidence-Based Assurance

Assurance decisions shall be based on measurable evidence.

---

## Principle 4 – Transparency

Intelligence operation and outcomes shall be understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 5 – Reliability

Intelligence capabilities shall operate consistently and predictably.

---

## Principle 6 – Human Assurance Responsibility

Human oversight shall remain an essential part of intelligence assurance.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

Assurance activities shall continuously improve intelligence capability.

# 9. Intelligence Quality Assurance Architecture

The Intelligence Quality Assurance Architecture defines how the quality, accuracy, consistency and effectiveness of Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are evaluated and maintained.

The architecture ensures that intelligence outputs meet defined quality requirements and provide reliable value to the Enterprise.

- intelligence accuracy

- evidence-based evaluation
