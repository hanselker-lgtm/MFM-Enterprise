# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise capacity management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Capacity management ensures that enterprise infrastructure, platforms and services have sufficient resources to meet current and future business demand while preserving performance, scalability, operational efficiency and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Capacity Planning
- Resource Utilization
- Performance Forecasting
- Scalability Planning
- Capacity Monitoring
- Capacity Verification
- Governance
- Compliance

All enterprise capacity management implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## CM-001

Provide standardized enterprise capacity management.

---

## CM-002

Ensure sufficient enterprise resource capacity.

---

## CM-003

Support predictable scalability.

---

## CM-004

Optimize resource utilization.

---

## CM-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Capacity Management Principles

Enterprise capacity management shall follow these principles.

- Capacity by Design
- Demand-Based Planning
- Performance-Driven Scaling
- Continuous Capacity Monitoring
- Predictive Capacity Forecasting
- Complete Traceability
- Governance by Default
- Continuous Improvement

Capacity management shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Capacity Management Categories

Enterprise capacity management shall be organized into standardized categories.

Categories shall include

- Infrastructure Capacity
- Platform Capacity
- Database Capacity
- Application Capacity
- Storage Capacity
- Network Capacity
- Compute Capacity
- Capacity Forecasting

Additional capacity categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Capacity Management Ownership

Each capacity management domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- capacity responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the capacity management lifecycle.

---

# 7. Capacity Management Governance

Enterprise capacity management governance shall define

- capacity governance
- capacity approval
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- capacity verification
- governance reporting

Capacity management governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Capacity Management Responsibilities

Enterprise capacity management shall provide controlled coordination of enterprise capacity planning and operational scalability.

Capacity management responsibilities shall

- separate capacity management from operational execution
- coordinate capacity ownership
- ensure capacity consistency
- validate capacity objectives
- preserve capacity traceability
- support enterprise operational efficiency

Capacity management implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Capacity Planning

Enterprise capacity management shall implement standardized capacity planning methodologies.

Capacity planning shall

- identify current resource utilization
- forecast future demand
- define capacity thresholds
- identify capacity risks
- preserve planning history
- support long-term scalability

Capacity planning shall remain consistent across the enterprise.

---

# 10. Performance Forecasting

Enterprise capacity management shall maintain standardized performance forecasting.

Performance forecasting shall

- analyze historical utilization
- identify performance trends
- forecast resource consumption
- estimate future capacity requirements
- preserve forecasting history
- maintain forecasting traceability

Performance forecasting shall remain under governance control.

---

# 11. Resource Utilization

Enterprise capacity management shall support standardized resource utilization monitoring.

Resource utilization shall

- monitor infrastructure utilization
- monitor application utilization
- monitor database utilization
- identify resource bottlenecks
- preserve utilization history
- maintain utilization traceability

Resource utilization shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 12. Scalability Management

Enterprise capacity management shall implement standardized scalability management.

Scalability management shall

- define scaling strategies
- support horizontal scalability
- support vertical scalability
- validate scalability objectives
- preserve scaling history
- maintain scalability traceability

Scalability management shall ensure predictable enterprise growth.

---

# 13. Capacity Management Dependencies

Enterprise capacity management shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- high availability
- infrastructure management
- monitoring services
- environment management
- operational readiness
- enterprise governance

Capacity management implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Capacity Management Documentation

Each capacity management domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- capacity objectives
- ownership information
- capacity plans
- forecasting reports
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Capacity Management Lifecycle

Enterprise capacity management shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Planned
- Assessed
- Forecasted
- Approved
- Implemented
- Monitored
- Optimized
- Reviewed
- Verified
- Improved

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Capacity Management Quality Attributes

Enterprise capacity management implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- scalability
- efficiency
- reliability
- predictability
- availability
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the capacity management lifecycle.

---

# 17. Capacity Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized capacity management registry.

The registry shall contain

- capacity identifiers
- ownership assignments
- capacity classifications
- lifecycle status
- capacity forecasts
- utilization history
- documentation references
- governance status

The capacity registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise capacity management.

---

# 18. Capacity Reviews

Enterprise capacity management implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- capacity planning quality
- forecasting accuracy
- scalability effectiveness
- utilization efficiency
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Capacity Metrics

Enterprise capacity management shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- resource utilization percentage
- capacity forecast accuracy
- infrastructure utilization
- storage utilization
- compute utilization
- scalability efficiency
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous capacity improvement.

---

# 20. Capacity Verification

Enterprise capacity management implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm capacity objectives
- verify forecasting accuracy
- verify scalability implementation
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve operational readiness

Capacity verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Capacity Improvement

Enterprise capacity management shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve capacity forecasting
- improve resource utilization
- improve scalability
- reduce operational risk
- strengthen governance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise capacity management implementations shall handle capacity management exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify capacity planning failures
- classify forecasting failures
- classify resource utilization anomalies
- classify scalability failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Capacity management exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational efficiency or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Capacity management implementations may depend upon

- approved high availability systems
- approved infrastructure management systems
- approved monitoring systems
- approved environment management systems
- approved operational readiness systems
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Capacity management implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external capacity management services

Capacity management capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A capacity management implementation is compliant when

- Capacity management responsibilities are documented.
- Capacity planning has been completed.
- Performance forecasting is documented.
- Resource utilization is continuously monitored.
- Scalability management has been verified.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Capacity Registry is maintained.
- Capacity verification has been completed.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Audit logging is enabled where applicable.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Capacity Planning

Enterprise services shall never operate without documented capacity planning.

---

## Inaccurate Forecasting

Capacity forecasts shall never be based upon undocumented assumptions or obsolete data.

---

## Ignoring Resource Bottlenecks

Resource bottlenecks shall never remain unresolved without documented risk acceptance.

---

## Reactive Scaling Only

Enterprise systems shall never rely exclusively on reactive scaling where predictive capacity planning is practical.

---

## Undocumented Capacity Dependencies

Capacity management implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Capacity Changes Outside Governance

Capacity modifications shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise capacity management implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- capacity planning quality
- forecasting accuracy
- scalability effectiveness
- utilization efficiency
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational efficiency
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Capacity Management Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing capacity management throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms and services maintain sufficient capacity through standardized planning, forecasting, scalability management, verification, governance and continuous improvement while preserving operational efficiency and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise capacity management implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
