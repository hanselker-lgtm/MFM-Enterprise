# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, architecture, and governance required for an **Adaptive Enterprise Architecture** capable of continuously responding to internal and external change.

Previous standards enabled the Enterprise Architecture to:

- Observe
- Learn
- Collaborate
- Self-organize
- Evolve
- Understand

EA-IAEA49 introduces the next capability:

**Adapt.**

Adaptation is the ability to recognize change, determine its significance, evaluate response alternatives, and implement the optimal adjustment while preserving enterprise objectives, governance, and operational stability.

The Adaptive Enterprise Architecture transforms intelligence into controlled enterprise action.

---

# 2. Vision

The Enterprise Architecture shall continuously adapt to changing conditions without requiring large-scale redesign.

Adaptation shall occur across:

- Business
- Organization
- Processes
- Information
- Data
- Applications
- Infrastructure
- Security
- Governance
- Artificial Intelligence

The architecture shall maintain strategic alignment while dynamically optimizing enterprise capabilities.

# 3. Enterprise Adaptation Philosophy

Adaptive Enterprise Architecture is founded upon eight principles.

---

## Principle 1

Change is continuous.

The enterprise shall assume permanent change rather than temporary stability.

---

## Principle 2

Adaptation is intentional.

Changes shall support enterprise objectives rather than react blindly to events.

---

## Principle 3

Context determines adaptation.

Every adaptive decision shall consider the current enterprise context.

---

## Principle 4

Governance remains active.

Adaptive behavior shall always comply with enterprise governance.

---

## Principle 5

Small adaptations prevent large disruptions.

Continuous optimization is preferred over infrequent transformation.

## Principle 6

Knowledge improves adaptation.

Every adaptation shall increase enterprise knowledge.

## Principle 7

Human oversight remains essential.

## Principle 8

| 3 | Context-Aware | Enterprise context drives adaptation decisions. |

| 5 | Adaptive | Continuous optimization occurs across architecture domains. |
| 6 | Autonomous | AI performs governed adaptive execution. |

| 8 | Adaptive Enterprise | Enterprise-wide adaptation operates as an integrated, intelligent capability aligned with strategy, governance, and continuous learning. |

Organizations shall periodically evaluate adaptive maturity and define measurable initiatives for advancing toward higher levels while maintaining governance, resilience, and enterprise value.
