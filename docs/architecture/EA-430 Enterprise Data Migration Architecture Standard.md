# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Migration Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Replication Architecture principles are inherited from EA-429.

All Enterprise Data Migration implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Migration Architecture governing secure, controlled and repeatable migration of Enterprise information between systems, applications, databases, cloud environments and technology platforms.

Enterprise Data Migration shall

- ensure data integrity
- minimize business disruption
- support modernization initiatives
- enable platform transitions
- preserve information quality
- reduce migration risk
- improve operational continuity
- strengthen governance
- support automation
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Data Migration shall provide a standardized architectural framework for trusted movement of Enterprise information throughout its lifecycle.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- application migrations
- database migrations
- cloud migrations
- platform migrations
- infrastructure migrations
- storage migrations
- data center migrations
- ERP migrations
- CRM migrations
- enterprise modernization projects

The standard applies regardless of technology platform, database engine, cloud provider or deployment model.

# 3. Enterprise Data Migration Principles

Enterprise Data Migration shall be governed by the following principles.

## Integrity by Design

Migration shall preserve Enterprise information integrity throughout all migration activities.

---

## Business Continuity by Design

Migration shall minimize operational disruption and maintain business continuity.

---

## Secure Migration

Migration shall protect Enterprise information during extraction, transformation, transmission and loading.

---

## Metadata-Driven Migration

Migration processes shall be governed through Enterprise metadata and standardized mappings.

---

## Reusable Migration Services

Migration capabilities shall be reusable across Business and Technology Domains.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Migration shall remain independent of vendors, migration tools and infrastructure platforms.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Migration Architecture establishes the standardized framework for secure, controlled and repeatable migration of Enterprise information between applications, databases, cloud environments and modern digital platforms.

By integrating Enterprise Data Architecture, Data Integration, Data Synchronization, Data Replication, Metadata Management, Security, Observability and Automation, the Enterprise enables trusted migration processes that preserve information integrity, reduce implementation risk and support business continuity throughout modernization initiatives.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Migration initiatives and shall guide the modernization of existing Enterprise migration capabilities.

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Data Migration Architecture shall continuously evolve to support AI-assisted migration planning, autonomous migration orchestration, intelligent data mapping, predictive risk analysis, self-validating migration pipelines, adaptive rollback strategies, policy-driven migration governance and emerging cloud-native migration technologies.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Migration continues to provide secure, interoperable, scalable and business-driven modernization capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
