# 1. Purpose

This document defines the forty-fourth implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-43 – IT Financial Management, Cost Transparency, Budgeting, Chargeback & Technology Economics Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled governance capability for vendors, suppliers, contracts, procurement and third-party services.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must govern third-party relationships throughout their lifecycle so that suppliers are appropriately selected, contracted, onboarded, measured, secured, financially controlled, reviewed, renewed, remediated and exited with clear accountability and manageable operational risk.**

> **Every material supplier relationship should remain sufficiently exit-ready to prevent uncontrolled operational, data or contractual dependency.**

---

# 139. Final Financial Principle

> **Supplier economics must connect contract commitments, procurement, invoices, budgets, forecasts and service consumption.**

# 140. Final Improvement Principle

> **Supplier governance should use performance, risk, incidents and financial evidence to improve the value and resilience of third-party relationships.**

---

# 141. Final Integration Principle

> **Third-party governance must connect procurement, contracts, services, configuration, security, privacy, incidents, changes, finance, risk, continuity and architecture.**
