# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Artificial General Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing future general intelligence capabilities within the Enterprise architecture.

The Artificial General Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- AGI architecture principles
- AGI governance
- AGI strategy
- AGI capability architecture
- general reasoning architecture
- general learning architecture
- transfer learning architecture
- AGI knowledge architecture
- AGI planning architecture
- AGI problem solving architecture
- AGI human collaboration architecture
- AGI safety architecture
- AGI alignment architecture
- AGI operations
- AGI performance management
- AGI evolution

The framework ensures that advanced general intelligence capabilities are designed, governed and operated as responsible Enterprise capabilities.

---

---

# 3. Enterprise Artificial General Intelligence Architecture Principles

The Artificial General Intelligence Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – General Intelligence Value Creation

AGI capabilities shall create measurable Enterprise value through advanced understanding, reasoning and problem solving.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Intelligence

AGI systems shall operate according to ethical, secure and governed principles.

---

## Principle 3 – General Understanding

AGI capabilities shall support broad understanding across multiple Enterprise domains.

---

## Principle 4 – Continuous Learning

AGI capabilities shall improve through experience, knowledge acquisition and controlled learning.

---

## Principle 5 – Human Collaboration

AGI systems shall augment human capability and support responsible human decision-making.

---

## Principle 6 – Safety and Alignment

AGI capabilities shall operate within defined safety, governance and alignment boundaries.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Evolution

AGI capabilities shall continuously develop through learning, feedback and innovation.

---
