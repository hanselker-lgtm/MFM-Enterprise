# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise business continuity throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Business continuity ensures that critical enterprise services and business capabilities remain available or are restored within acceptable timeframes during disruptions while preserving operational stability, resilience and compliance with Enterprise Architecture principles.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Business Continuity Planning
- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Recovery Objectives
- Critical Business Services
- Crisis Management
- Disaster Recovery Coordination
- Governance
- Compliance

All enterprise business continuity implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 4. Business Continuity Principles

Enterprise business continuity shall follow these principles.

- Continuity by Design
- Risk-Based Planning
- Business Impact Driven Prioritization
- Verified Recovery Capability
- Complete Traceability
- Governance by Default
- Operational Resilience
- Continuous Improvement

Business continuity shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Business Continuity Categories

Enterprise business continuity shall be organized into standardized categories.

Categories shall include

- Critical Business Services
- Essential Supporting Services
- Recovery Planning
- Business Impact Analysis
- Crisis Response
- Disaster Recovery Coordination
- Business Recovery
- Continuity Testing

Additional continuity categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Business Continuity Ownership

Each business continuity domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- continuity responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the continuity lifecycle.

---

# 7. Business Continuity Governance

Enterprise business continuity governance shall define

- continuity governance
- recovery governance
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- continuity approval
- governance reporting

Business continuity governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Business Continuity Responsibilities

Enterprise business continuity shall provide controlled coordination of continuity preparation and recovery activities.

Business continuity responsibilities shall

- separate continuity management from operational execution
- coordinate continuity ownership
- ensure continuity consistency
- validate continuity objectives
- preserve continuity traceability
- support enterprise operational resilience

Business continuity implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Business Impact Analysis

Enterprise business continuity shall implement standardized Business Impact Analysis (BIA).

Business Impact Analysis shall

- identify critical business capabilities
- classify business impact
- identify service dependencies
- determine recovery priorities
- preserve assessment history
- support objective recovery planning

Business Impact Analysis shall remain consistent across the enterprise.

---

# 10. Recovery Objectives

Enterprise business continuity shall define standardized recovery objectives.

Recovery objectives shall include

- Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
- Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)
- Minimum Business Continuity Objectives (MBCO)
- Recovery priority
- Recovery verification

Recovery objectives shall remain under governance control.

---

# 11. Continuity Planning

Enterprise business continuity shall support controlled continuity planning.

Continuity planning shall

- define recovery procedures
- identify required resources
- coordinate recovery responsibilities
- support disaster recovery
- preserve planning history
- maintain planning traceability

Continuity planning shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 12. Crisis Management

Enterprise business continuity shall implement standardized crisis management procedures.

Crisis management shall

- define escalation procedures
- coordinate crisis response
- assign decision authority
- support communication planning
- preserve crisis history
- maintain crisis traceability

Crisis management shall ensure predictable incident response.

---

# 13. Business Continuity Dependencies

Enterprise business continuity shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- operational readiness
- release management
- deployment management
- environment management
- infrastructure management
- enterprise governance

Business continuity implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Business Continuity Documentation

Each business continuity domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- continuity objectives
- ownership information
- Business Impact Analysis
- recovery objectives
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Business Continuity Lifecycle

Enterprise business continuity shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Planned
- Assessed
- Approved
- Prepared
- Verified
- Operational
- Exercised
- Monitored
- Reviewed
- Improved

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Business Continuity Quality Attributes

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- resilience
- availability
- reliability
- recoverability
- consistency
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the business continuity lifecycle.

---

# 17. Business Continuity Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized business continuity registry.

The registry shall contain

- continuity identifiers
- ownership assignments
- business criticality classifications
- lifecycle status
- recovery objectives
- Business Impact Analysis references
- documentation references
- governance status

The business continuity registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise business continuity.

---

# 18. Business Continuity Reviews

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- continuity quality
- Business Impact Analysis completeness
- recovery objective compliance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment
- operational resilience

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Business Continuity Metrics

Enterprise business continuity shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- recovery success rate
- recovery time achievement
- Recovery Point Objective compliance
- Business Impact Analysis completion rate
- audit findings
- continuity exercise results
- operational resilience
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous business continuity improvement.

---

# 20. Recovery Verification

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm continuity objectives
- verify recovery procedures
- verify recovery objectives
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve operational resilience

Recovery verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Business Continuity Improvement

Enterprise business continuity shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve recovery capability
- improve Business Impact Analysis
- reduce operational risk
- strengthen governance
- improve operational resilience
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall handle continuity exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify Business Impact Analysis failures
- classify recovery planning failures
- classify continuity testing failures
- classify recovery verification failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Business continuity exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational resilience or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Business continuity implementations may depend upon

- approved operational readiness systems
- approved environment management systems
- approved deployment management systems
- approved infrastructure management systems
- approved monitoring systems
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Business continuity implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external business continuity services

Business continuity capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A business continuity implementation is compliant when

- Business continuity responsibilities are documented.
- Business Impact Analysis has been completed.
- Recovery objectives are approved.
- Continuity plans are documented.
- Crisis management procedures are established.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Business Continuity Registry is maintained.
- Recovery verification has been completed.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Audit logging is enabled where applicable.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Business Impact Analysis

Critical business services shall never be operated without a documented Business Impact Analysis.

---

## Undefined Recovery Objectives

Recovery Time Objective (RTO), Recovery Point Objective (RPO), Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD) and Minimum Business Continuity Objective (MBCO) shall never be omitted for critical services.

---

## Untested Continuity Plans

Business continuity plans shall never be considered valid without documented exercises and verification.

---

## Inadequate Crisis Management

Critical incidents shall never be handled without documented escalation procedures and assigned decision authority.

---

## Undocumented Dependencies

Business continuity implementations shall never rely on undocumented infrastructure, operational or business dependencies.

---

## Missing Recovery Verification

Recovery capability shall never be assumed without documented verification and governance approval.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise business continuity implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- continuity quality
- Business Impact Analysis completeness
- recovery objective compliance
- continuity planning effectiveness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational resilience
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Business Continuity Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing business continuity throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise business capabilities are protected, recoverable and continuously available through standardized planning, recovery objectives, verification, governance and continuous improvement while preserving operational resilience and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise business continuity implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.

End of Document.
