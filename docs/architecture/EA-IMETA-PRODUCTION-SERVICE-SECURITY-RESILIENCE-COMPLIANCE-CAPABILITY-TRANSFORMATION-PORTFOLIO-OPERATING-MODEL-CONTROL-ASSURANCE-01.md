# 1. PURPOSE

Establish the authoritative assurance layer for portfolio operating-model controls, ensuring that control design, operating effectiveness, evidence, risk coverage, findings, remediation and residual risk are independently and systematically evaluated.

# 2. ASSURANCE PRINCIPLE

EA-IMETA SHALL PROVIDE RISK-BASED, EVIDENCE-BASED AND TRACEABLE ASSURANCE OVER PORTFOLIO OPERATING-MODEL CONTROLS WITHOUT REPLACING THE ACCOUNTABILITY OF CONTROL OWNERS.

# 3. ASSURANCE OBJECTIVES

Determine whether controls are appropriately designed, implemented, operating effectively, producing sufficient evidence, addressing material risks and remaining within approved governance boundaries.

# 4. ASSURANCE SCOPE

Covers control design, operation, evidence, risk, security, resilience, data, AI/agents, benefits, finance, decisions, change, exceptions, remediation, audit, compliance, automation and continuous assurance.

# 5. ASSURANCE INDEPENDENCE

Assurance shall be sufficiently independent from control execution to provide credible conclusions. Independence shall be proportional to risk and materiality.

# 6. THREE-LAYER ASSURANCE

First line owns and operates controls; second line provides oversight and challenge; third line provides independent assurance or audit as appropriate.

# 7. ASSURANCE LIFECYCLE

PLAN → SCOPE → ASSESS → TEST → EVIDENCE → FIND → RATE → REPORT → REMEDIATE → RETEST → CONCLUDE → FOLLOW UP.

# 8. DESIGN EFFECTIVENESS

Design assurance determines whether a control is logically capable of addressing its stated risk and objective before relying on operating performance.

# 9. OPERATING EFFECTIVENESS

Operating-effectiveness assurance determines whether the control actually executed as designed, by authorized roles, at the required frequency and with sufficient evidence.

# 10. EVIDENCE PRINCIPLE

Evidence shall be attributable, timely, complete, authentic, relevant, reproducible and traceable to the control execution.

# 11. SAMPLING PRINCIPLE

Sampling shall be risk-based and documented, with population, method, rationale, sample size and limitations recorded.

# 12. FINDING PRINCIPLE

Findings shall distinguish design deficiencies, operating failures, evidence deficiencies, process gaps, systemic issues and isolated exceptions.

# 13. REMEDIATION PRINCIPLE

A remediation shall not be considered complete until implementation is evidenced and effectiveness is retested or otherwise validated.

# 14. RESIDUAL RISK

Assurance conclusions shall consider risk remaining after control operation and remediation.

# 15. AI ASSURANCE PRINCIPLE

AI-generated assurance signals, summaries or classifications require appropriate validation before becoming authoritative assurance evidence.

# 16. AGENT ASSURANCE PRINCIPLE

Agent identity, authority, tools, data access, action scope, stop conditions, oversight and auditability shall be explicitly assured.

# 17. CONTINUOUS ASSURANCE

Where feasible, control evidence and telemetry shall support continuous or near-continuous assurance rather than relying exclusively on periodic reviews.

# 19. ASSURANCE MODEL

```text
CONTROL → TEST → EVIDENCE → ASSESS → FIND → REMEDIATE → RETEST → CONCLUDE
```

# 20. ASSURANCE OPINION MODEL

```text
EFFECTIVE / PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE / INEFFECTIVE / NOT TESTED / NOT APPLICABLE
```

# 21. FINDING LIFECYCLE

```text

```

# 22. EVIDENCE LIFECYCLE

```text
GENERATE → CAPTURE → VALIDATE → STORE → RETAIN → REVIEW → ARCHIVE
```

# 23. REMEDIATION LIFECYCLE

```text
FINDING → ROOT CAUSE → ACTION → IMPLEMENT → EVIDENCE → RETEST → VALIDATE → CLOSE
```

# 24. ASSURANCE ESCALATION

```text

```

# 1. ASSURANCE CONTROL — Assurance Charter

**Assurance Control ID:** ASR-SECRCTPOMA-001

**Domain:** Assurance Governance

**Assurance objective:** Determine whether assurance charter is adequately designed, appropriately owned, operating effectively where applicable, supported by sufficient evidence and producing an acceptable residual-risk position.

**Primary criteria:**
```text
DESIGN
AUTHORITY
EXECUTION
EVIDENCE
TIMELINESS
COVERAGE
EXCEPTIONS
REMEDIATION
RESIDUAL RISK
```

**Testing approach:**
1. Confirm scope and population.
2. Validate control objective and risk.
3. Inspect design and ownership.
4. Test execution using risk-based sampling or continuous evidence where available.
5. Validate evidence authenticity and traceability.
6. Evaluate exceptions and failures.
7. Assess remediation status.
8. Determine conclusion.

**Minimum evidence:**
```text
CONTROL DEFINITION
OWNER
EXECUTION RECORD
APPROVAL
TIMESTAMP
RESULT
EXCEPTION
REMEDIATION
RETEST
```

**Conclusion states:**

# 166. FINDING TAXONOMY

## 167. Design Deficiency

Control design cannot adequately address the intended risk.

## 168. Operating Failure

Control exists but did not operate as required.

## 169. Evidence Deficiency

Execution may have occurred but evidence is insufficient.

## 170. Authority Breach

Activity occurred outside approved decision rights or authority.

## 171. Coverage Gap

Material population, risk or scenario is not covered.

## 172. Timeliness Failure

Control or remediation occurred outside required timeframe.

## 173. Systemic Issue

Issue indicates a recurring or structural weakness.

## 174. Isolated Exception

Limited deviation without evidence of systemic failure.

## 175. Residual Risk Issue

Risk remains above approved tolerance after control operation.
