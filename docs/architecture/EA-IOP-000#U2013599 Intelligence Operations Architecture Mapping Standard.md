# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Operations Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive operational architecture for managing Enterprise Intelligence capabilities.

The Intelligence Operations Architecture Framework shall provide:

- intelligence operations principles
- operations governance
- intelligence service operations
- intelligence platform operations
- intelligence model operations
- intelligence data operations
- intelligence workflow operations
- intelligence agent operations
- incident management
- change management
- capacity management
- operational monitoring
- operations evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities operate efficiently, reliably and continuously.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Operations Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture operational intelligence domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Operations Model
- Operations Governance
- Service Operations Architecture
- Platform Operations Architecture
- Model Operations Architecture
- Data Operations Architecture
- Workflow Operations Architecture
- Agent Operations Architecture
- Incident Operations
- Change Operations
- Capacity Operations
- Monitoring Operations
- Performance Operations
- Operations Maturity
- Operations Roadmaps
- Operations Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- intelligence ecosystems
- intelligent operating environments
- future adaptive enterprises

---

# 3. Intelligence Operations Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Operations Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Operational Excellence

Intelligence capabilities shall be operated efficiently and reliably.

---

## Principle 2 – Continuous Availability

Intelligence services shall remain available according to defined requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Controlled Operations

All intelligence operations shall follow defined operational procedures.

---

## Principle 4 – Proactive Monitoring

Operational conditions shall be continuously monitored.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Improvement

Operations shall continuously improve through measurement and learning.

---

## Principle 6 – Integrated Operations

Intelligence operations shall integrate with Enterprise operating processes.

---

## Principle 7 – Adaptive Operations

Operations shall adapt to changing intelligence requirements.
