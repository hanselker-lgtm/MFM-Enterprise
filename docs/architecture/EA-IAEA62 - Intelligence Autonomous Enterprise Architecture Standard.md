# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, and intelligence capabilities required for an **Intelligence Autonomous Enterprise Architecture (IAEA)**.

While EA-ISENEA61 enables Enterprise Architecture to continuously understand itself through persistent Enterprise Self-Awareness, EA-IAEA62 enables Enterprise Intelligence to autonomously plan, coordinate, orchestrate, and execute approved enterprise activities within clearly defined governance boundaries.

The objective is not unrestricted autonomy, but governed autonomous execution that continuously aligns with enterprise purpose, organizational identity, Enterprise Wisdom, sustainability objectives, scientific integrity, regulatory obligations, and human accountability.

Enterprise Architecture shall therefore evolve from continuously self-aware intelligence into continuously self-directing Enterprise Intelligence operating under permanent human governance.
