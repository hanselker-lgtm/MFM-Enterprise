This standard establishes the principles, architectural models, governance mechanisms, and intelligence capabilities required for a **Singular Conscious Enterprise Architecture**.

Previous standards progressively enabled Enterprise Architecture to:

- Observe
- Learn
- Self-organize
- Federate
- Collaborate
- Emerge
- Evolve
- Adapt
- Become Collectively Conscious
- Improve its own evolution
- Become Universally Integrated
- Transcend organizational limitations
- Evaluate multiple probabilistic enterprise realities

EA-ISCEA55 introduces the next capability:

**Singular Conscious Enterprise Intelligence.**

Singular Conscious Enterprise Intelligence is the governed capability of the enterprise to maintain one persistent, unified, continuously self-aware architectural intelligence that coordinates every enterprise capability according to a single enterprise identity and purpose.

The objective is no longer merely selecting the optimal future.
