# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Service Management Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise services as strategic capabilities that deliver business value.

The Service Management Architecture Framework shall provide:

- service principles
- service governance
- service structures
- service lifecycle management
- service delivery management
- service performance management
- service improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise services support business capabilities, processes, applications, information, technology and infrastructure.

---

# 2. Scope

The Service Management Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture service domains:

- Enterprise Service Model
- Service Governance
- Service Strategy
- Service Domains
- Service Portfolio Management
- Service Design Management
- Service Lifecycle Management
- Service Delivery Management
- Service Operations Management
- Service Level Management
- Service Quality Management
- Service Monitoring
- Service Improvement
- Service Performance
- Service Evolution

The framework applies to:

- business services
- information services
- application services
- technology services
- infrastructure services
- operational services
- external services
- transformation initiatives

---

# 3. Enterprise Service Management Architecture Principles

The Service Management Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Service Value Focus

Services shall be managed according to the value they provide to stakeholders and business capabilities.

---

## Principle 2 – Business Alignment

Services shall support Enterprise objectives, business capabilities and operational requirements.

---

## Principle 3 – Service Ownership

All services shall have defined ownership, accountability and responsibility.

---

## Principle 4 – Lifecycle Management

Services shall be managed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 – Service Quality Management

Services shall be measured according to defined quality, performance and availability requirements.

---

## Principle 6 – Standardized Service Management

Services shall be managed using consistent methods, processes and governance.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

Services shall continuously evolve according to changing business and technology requirements.

---

# 9. Service Domain Architecture

The Service Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise services are structured into logical domains to support governance, management and effective service delivery.

Service domains provide a common structure for organizing Enterprise services, capabilities and responsibilities.

---

# 9.1 Service Domain Principles

Service domains shall ensure:

- clear service ownership
- logical service organization
- consistent service management
- service transparency
- value-based service delivery

---

# 9.2 Service Domain Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Service Domain | Logical grouping of related services |
| Service Owner | Accountability for service domain |
| Service Capabilities | Defines service abilities |
| Service Components | Defines service building blocks |
| Service Standards | Defines service management rules |

---

# 15. Service Level Management

- performance transparency
