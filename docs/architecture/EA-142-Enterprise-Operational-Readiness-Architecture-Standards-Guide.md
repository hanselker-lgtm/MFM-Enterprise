# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise operational readiness throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Operational readiness ensures that enterprise solutions are fully prepared for production operation through standardized assessment, verification, acceptance and governance before go-live while preserving operational stability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Operational Readiness Assessment
- Go-Live Readiness
- Operational Acceptance
- Service Handover
- Operational Verification
- Production Readiness
- Governance
- Compliance

All enterprise operational readiness implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## OR-001

Provide standardized enterprise operational readiness.

---

## OR-002

Ensure consistent production readiness assessments.

---

## OR-003

Support controlled operational acceptance.

---

## OR-004

Maintain operational stability during go-live.

---

## OR-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Operational Readiness Principles

Enterprise operational readiness shall follow these principles.

- Readiness by Design
- Objective Readiness Assessment
- Controlled Go-Live
- Verified Operational Capability
- Complete Traceability
- Governance by Default
- Operational Stability
- Continuous Improvement

Operational readiness shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Operational Readiness Categories

Enterprise operational readiness shall be organized into standardized categories.

Categories shall include

- Development Readiness
- Testing Readiness
- Deployment Readiness
- Operational Readiness
- Production Readiness
- Service Readiness
- Disaster Recovery Readiness
- Business Continuity Readiness

Additional readiness categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Operational Ownership

Each operational readiness domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the operational readiness lifecycle.

---

# 7. Operational Readiness Governance

Enterprise operational readiness governance shall define

- readiness governance
- operational approval
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- go-live approval
- governance reporting

Operational readiness governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Operational Responsibilities

Enterprise operational readiness shall provide controlled coordination of operational preparation activities.

Operational responsibilities shall

- separate readiness management from operational execution
- coordinate operational ownership
- ensure operational consistency
- validate readiness objectives
- preserve operational traceability
- support enterprise operational stability

Operational readiness implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Readiness Assessment

Enterprise operational readiness shall use standardized readiness assessment methodologies.

Readiness assessments shall

- define readiness criteria
- verify operational capabilities
- identify operational risks
- validate operational procedures
- preserve assessment history
- support objective decision making

Readiness assessments shall remain consistent across the enterprise.

---

# 10. Operational Acceptance

Enterprise operational readiness shall implement standardized operational acceptance procedures.

Operational acceptance shall

- verify production readiness
- confirm operational documentation
- validate support capability
- confirm monitoring readiness
- preserve acceptance history
- maintain acceptance traceability

Operational acceptance shall remain under governance control.

---

# 11. Service Handover

Enterprise operational readiness shall support controlled service handover.

Service handover shall

- define operational responsibilities
- transfer operational knowledge
- validate support readiness
- coordinate operational ownership
- preserve handover documentation
- maintain handover traceability

Service handover shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 12. Go-Live Planning

Enterprise operational readiness shall implement standardized go-live planning.

Go-live planning shall

- coordinate deployment activities
- minimize operational disruption
- support release schedules
- define rollback procedures
- document operational checkpoints
- maintain planning traceability

Go-live planning shall ensure predictable production deployment.

---

# 13. Operational Dependencies

Enterprise operational readiness shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- release management
- deployment management
- configuration management
- environment management
- monitoring services
- enterprise governance

Operational readiness implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Operational Documentation

Each operational readiness domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- readiness objectives
- ownership information
- assessment records
- acceptance records
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Operational Readiness Lifecycle

Enterprise operational readiness shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Planned
- Assessed
- Verified
- Approved
- Prepared
- Go-Live Ready
- Operational
- Monitored
- Reviewed
- Retired

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Operational Readiness Quality Attributes

Enterprise operational readiness implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- consistency
- reliability
- predictability
- availability
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability
- recoverability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the operational readiness lifecycle.

---

# 17. Operational Readiness Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized operational readiness registry.

The registry shall contain

- readiness identifiers
- ownership assignments
- readiness categories
- lifecycle status
- assessment history
- verification records
- documentation references
- governance status

The operational readiness registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise operational readiness.

---

# 18. Operational Readiness Reviews

Enterprise operational readiness implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- readiness quality
- assessment effectiveness
- operational acceptance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment
- production readiness

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Operational Readiness Metrics

Enterprise operational readiness shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- readiness success rate
- assessment completion rate
- go-live success rate
- rollback frequency
- audit findings
- operational stability
- production incident rate
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous operational improvement.

---

# 20. Operational Verification

Enterprise operational readiness implementations shall undergo formal verification before production use and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm readiness objectives
- verify operational procedures
- verify governance compliance
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve production readiness

Operational verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Operational Improvement

Enterprise operational readiness shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve readiness consistency
- improve assessment effectiveness
- reduce operational risk
- strengthen governance
- improve production stability
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise operational readiness implementations shall handle operational readiness exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify readiness assessment failures
- classify operational acceptance failures
- classify service handover failures
- classify production readiness failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Operational readiness exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational stability or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Operational readiness implementations may depend upon

- approved release management systems
- approved deployment management systems
- approved environment management systems
- approved configuration management systems
- approved monitoring systems
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Operational readiness implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external operational readiness services

Operational readiness capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An operational readiness implementation is compliant when

- Operational responsibilities are documented.
- Readiness assessments have been completed.
- Operational acceptance has been approved.
- Service handover has been completed.
- Go-live planning has been documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Operational Readiness Registry is maintained.
- Operational verification has been completed.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Audit logging is enabled where applicable.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Incomplete Readiness Assessment

Solutions shall never proceed to production without a completed and documented readiness assessment.

---

## Missing Operational Acceptance

Operational acceptance shall never be bypassed before go-live.

---

## Inadequate Service Handover

Solutions shall never enter production without documented knowledge transfer and operational ownership.

---

## Unplanned Go-Live

Production deployments shall never occur outside approved go-live planning and governance processes.

---

## Undocumented Operational Dependencies

Operational readiness implementations shall never rely on undocumented dependencies or hidden operational relationships.

---

## Missing Operational Verification

Production operation shall never begin without documented verification of readiness and operational approval.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise operational readiness implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- readiness quality
- assessment effectiveness
- operational acceptance
- service handover completeness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- production readiness
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Operational Readiness Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing operational readiness throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise solutions are consistently assessed, verified, accepted and transitioned into production while preserving operational stability, traceability, governance and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise operational readiness implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
