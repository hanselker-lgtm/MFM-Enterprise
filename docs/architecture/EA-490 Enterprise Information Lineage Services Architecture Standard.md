# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Lineage Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed tracking of Enterprise information origin, movement, transformation and usage across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Lineage Services shall

- provide centralized information lineage management
- establish information origin transparency
- track information movement
- document information transformations
- support impact analysis
- improve governance visibility
- strengthen compliance assurance
- support audit requirements
- improve information quality management
- support lifecycle governance
- support master information governance
- support reference information governance
- support privacy and security requirements
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure lineage traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Lineage Services shall provide trusted and reusable lineage capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

By integrating data origin management, source lineage management, transformation lineage management, data flow lineage management, business lineage management, technical lineage management, metadata lineage integration, master information lineage integration, reference data lineage integration, information quality lineage, lifecycle lineage management, governance lineage services, compliance lineage services, audit lineage services, privacy lineage services, security lineage services, impact analysis services, dependency management services, change impact assessment, event integration, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables transparent, traceable and policy-driven information management while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.
