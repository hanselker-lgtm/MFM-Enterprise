# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Autonomy Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive autonomy architecture for enabling Enterprise Intelligence capabilities to perform independent actions, decisions and operations within defined governance, security and responsibility boundaries.

The Intelligence Autonomy Architecture Framework shall provide:

- autonomy principles
- autonomy governance
- autonomous architecture
- autonomous agent architecture
- autonomous operations
- autonomous decision execution
- human-AI collaboration
- autonomy boundaries
- autonomous control framework
- autonomous risk management
- autonomy measurement
- autonomy maturity
- autonomy evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence autonomy is developed and operated in a controlled, responsible and measurable manner.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Autonomy Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture autonomy domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Autonomy Model
- Autonomy Governance
- Autonomous Architecture
- Autonomous Agent Architecture
- Autonomous Operations Architecture
- Autonomous Decision Execution
- Human-AI Collaboration
- Autonomy Boundaries
- Autonomous Control Framework
- Autonomous Risk Management
- Autonomy Metrics
- Autonomy Maturity
- Autonomy Roadmaps
- Autonomy Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- autonomous systems
- adaptive enterprises
- future Intelligence environments

---

# 3. Intelligence Autonomy Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Autonomy Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Controlled Autonomy

Autonomous capabilities shall operate within defined boundaries.

---

## Principle 2 – Human Accountability

Human responsibility shall remain clearly defined.

---

## Principle 3 – Governance by Design

Autonomy shall include governance mechanisms from the beginning.

---

## Principle 4 – Transparent Decisions

Autonomous decisions shall provide appropriate visibility and explanation.

---

## Principle 5 – Safe Autonomous Operation

Autonomous behaviour shall include security and risk controls.

---

## Principle 6 – Adaptive Autonomy

Autonomy shall evolve through learning and experience.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Intelligence

Autonomous capabilities shall operate according to ethical and organisational requirements.
