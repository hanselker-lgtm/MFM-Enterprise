# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise audit throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise audit ensures that governance, compliance, risk management, security and operational controls are independently evaluated through standardized audit processes while preserving transparency, accountability, auditability and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Internal Audits
- External Audits
- Audit Planning
- Audit Execution
- Audit Evidence
- Audit Reporting
- Audit Follow-up
- Governance

All enterprise audit implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 3. Objectives

## AU-001

Provide standardized enterprise audit.

## AU-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Audit Principles

Enterprise audit shall follow these principles.

- Independence
- Objectivity
- Evidence-Based Assessment
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Auditability
- Technology Independence
- Continuous Improvement

Audit implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Audit Domains

Enterprise audit shall be organized into standardized domains.

Domains shall include

- Governance Audits
- Compliance Audits
- Risk Audits
- Security Audits
- Operational Audits
- Financial Audits
- Technical Audits
- Architecture Audits

Additional audit domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Audit Ownership

Each audit domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- audit responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the audit lifecycle.

---

# 7. Audit Governance

Enterprise audit governance shall define

- audit governance
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- audit verification
- governance reporting

Audit governance shall remain technology independent.

---

# End of Part 1

# 8. Audit Responsibilities

Enterprise audit shall provide controlled execution of enterprise audits.

Audit responsibilities shall

- separate audit from business execution
- coordinate audit ownership
- ensure objective evaluation
- validate audit objectives
- preserve audit traceability
- support enterprise governance

Audit implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Audit Planning

Enterprise audit shall implement standardized audit planning.

Audit planning shall

- define audit scope
- define audit objectives
- identify audit criteria
- identify audit resources
- preserve planning history
- maintain planning traceability

Audit planning shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Audit Execution

Enterprise audit shall implement standardized audit execution.

Audit execution shall

- collect objective evidence
- verify implemented controls
- evaluate compliance
- evaluate governance effectiveness
- preserve execution history
- maintain execution traceability

Audit execution shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 11. Audit Evidence

Enterprise audit shall implement standardized management of audit evidence.

Audit evidence shall

- support audit conclusions
- remain objective
- remain verifiable
- preserve evidence history
- maintain evidence traceability
- support audit integrity

Audit evidence shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 12. Audit Findings

Enterprise audit shall implement standardized management of audit findings.

Audit findings shall

- classify observations
- classify non-conformities
- identify improvement opportunities
- preserve finding history
- maintain finding traceability
- support corrective actions

Audit findings shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Audit Dependencies

Enterprise audit shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- governance capabilities
- compliance capabilities
- risk management capabilities
- security capabilities
- reporting platforms
- enterprise infrastructure

Audit implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Audit Documentation

Each audit domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- audit plans
- ownership information
- audit evidence
- audit findings
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Audit Lifecycle

Enterprise audit shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Planned
- Approved
- Scheduled
- Executed
- Verified
- Reported
- Reviewed
- Followed-up
- Closed
- Archived

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Audit Quality Attributes

Enterprise audit implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- independence
- objectivity
- traceability
- transparency
- auditability
- maintainability
- governance compliance
- evidence integrity

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the audit lifecycle.

---

# 17. Audit Register

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized audit register.

The audit register shall contain

- audit identifiers
- ownership assignments
- audit scope
- audit status
- audit findings
- evidence references
- documentation references
- governance status

The audit register shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise audits.

---

# 18. Audit Reviews

Enterprise audit implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- audit planning quality
- audit execution quality
- evidence integrity
- finding completeness
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Audit Metrics

Enterprise audit shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- completed audits
- audit coverage
- findings by severity
- corrective action completion
- overdue audit actions
- audit cycle duration
- audit findings closed
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous audit improvement.

---

# 20. Audit Verification

Enterprise audit implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm audit planning
- verify audit execution
- verify evidence integrity
- verify findings
- verify ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- confirm governance compliance
- approve audit closure

Audit verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Audit Improvement

Enterprise audit shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve audit quality
- improve evidence collection
- improve governance
- improve reporting quality
- improve audit efficiency
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise audit implementations shall handle audit-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify audit planning failures
- classify audit execution failures
- classify audit evidence failures
- classify audit reporting failures
- classify governance violations
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Audit exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, governance, audit integrity or regulatory obligations.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Audit implementations may depend upon

- approved governance capabilities
- approved compliance capabilities
- approved risk management capabilities
- approved security capabilities
- approved reporting platforms
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Audit implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external audit services

Audit capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An audit implementation is compliant when

- Audit responsibilities are documented.
- Audit planning procedures are implemented.
- Audit execution follows approved standards.
- Audit evidence is maintained.
- Audit findings are documented.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Audit Register is maintained.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit verification is documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Audit Planning

Enterprise audits shall never be conducted without documented planning.

---

## Insufficient Audit Evidence

Audit conclusions shall never be based upon unverifiable or incomplete evidence.

---

## Undefined Audit Ownership

Enterprise audits shall never exist without documented ownership and accountability.

---

## Missing Corrective Action Follow-up

Audit findings shall never remain unresolved without documented corrective actions and follow-up.

---

## Undocumented Audit Dependencies

Audit implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Audit Outside Governance

Audit implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise audit implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- audit management quality
- planning completeness
- execution quality
- evidence integrity
- finding quality
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Audit Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise audit throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and business operations implement standardized audit planning, execution, evidence management, reporting, verification and governance through controlled lifecycle management and continuous improvement while preserving transparency, accountability, audit integrity and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise audit implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
