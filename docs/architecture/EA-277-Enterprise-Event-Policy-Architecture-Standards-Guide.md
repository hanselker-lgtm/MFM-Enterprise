# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Event Policy Architecture throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Event Policy Architecture provides standardized mechanisms for defining, managing, enforcing and monitoring enterprise event policies while preserving consistency, traceability, accountability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Event Policies
- Policy Definition
- Policy Lifecycle Management
- Policy Enforcement
- Policy Monitoring
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Event Policy implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Enterprise Event Policy Principles

Enterprise Event Policy implementations shall follow these principles.

- Standardized Policy Definition
- Controlled Policy Lifecycle
- Consistent Policy Enforcement
- Continuous Policy Monitoring
- Technology Independence
- Explicit Policy Ownership
- Centralized Governance
- Complete Policy Traceability

Enterprise Event Policy implementations shall remain independent of presentation, persistence and unrelated business workflow concerns.

Enterprise Event Policy implementations shall handle Event Policy-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Event Policy implementations may depend upon

- approved governance services
- approved compliance services
- approved audit services
- approved reporting services
- approved monitoring services
- approved configuration services
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Enterprise Event Policy implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Reporting implementations
- Query implementations
- Command implementations outside approved interfaces
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Unapproved external policy frameworks

Enterprise Event Policy capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

- Policy definition is implemented.
- Policy lifecycle management is implemented.
- Policy enforcement is implemented.
- Policy monitoring is implemented.
- Policy management is implemented.
- Policy verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Compliance coordination is operational.
- Documentation is complete.

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Undefined Enterprise Policies

Enterprise event capabilities shall never operate without formally approved enterprise policies.

---

## Uncontrolled Policy Changes

Policies shall never be modified outside the approved policy lifecycle and governance process.

---

## Missing Policy Enforcement

Approved enterprise policies shall never exist without appropriate enforcement mechanisms.

---

## Hidden Policy Dependencies

Enterprise implementations shall never introduce undocumented runtime or infrastructure dependencies.

---

## Cross-Capability Policy Access

Policy implementations shall never access internal capability components except through approved enterprise interfaces.

---

## Unapproved Policy Overrides

Policy enforcement shall never be bypassed or overridden without explicit governance approval.

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Event Policy Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Event Policy Architecture throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that policy definition, policy lifecycle management, policy enforcement, policy monitoring and governance are implemented consistently while preserving maintainability, scalability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Event Policy implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
