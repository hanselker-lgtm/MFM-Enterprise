# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Automation Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed automation capabilities for improving ecosystem execution, operational efficiency and digital collaboration across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Automation Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem automation capabilities
- support workflow automation
- support business process automation
- support robotic process automation
- support intelligent automation
- support partner automation
- support supplier automation
- support integration automation
- support AI-driven automation
- support automation governance
- support automation lifecycle management
- support automation monitoring
- support automation optimization
- improve ecosystem efficiency
- improve ecosystem scalability
- reduce manual activities
- improve operational consistency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Automation Services shall provide trusted and reusable automation capabilities enabling efficient ecosystem operations.
