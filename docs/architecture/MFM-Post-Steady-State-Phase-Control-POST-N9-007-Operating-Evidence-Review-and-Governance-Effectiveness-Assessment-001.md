# 1. Purpose

POST-N9-007 reviews the evidence generated by the first controlled post-N9 operating cycle.

The purpose is to determine:

```text
WHAT WAS ACTUALLY OPERATED
WHAT WAS ACTUALLY EVIDENCED
WHAT WAS VALIDATED
WHAT REMAINS UNVERIFIED
WHAT DEVIATIONS OCCURRED
WHAT GOVERNANCE EFFECTIVENESS CAN CURRENTLY BE ASSESSED
WHAT REQUIRES REMEDIATION
```

This work package does not convert model-level evidence into operational proof without supporting evidence.

# 3. Evidence Hierarchy

The review shall distinguish:

```text
DEFINED
↓
VALIDATED
↓
ACTIVATED
↓
OPERATED
↓
EVIDENCED
↓
EFFECTIVE
```

A lower state shall not be silently promoted to a higher state.


# 5. Review Questions

For each governance control:

```text
Was the control defined?
Was it activated?
Was it actually used?
Was the use recorded?
Was evidence captured?
Was the evidence validated?
Was the intended control behavior observed?
Was the control effective?
```

Where evidence is insufficient:

```text
UNVERIFIED
```

shall be used.

# 33. Final POST-N9-007 Statement

> **POST-N9-007 reviews the actual evidence generated by the first post-N9 continuous governance operating cycle.
