## Purpose
Establish the authoritative re-closure architecture after successful re-acceptance, ensuring closure criteria, final evidence, conditions, residual risk, baseline update, authorization, historical integrity, archive and post-closure monitoring are complete.

## Core Principle
Re-acceptance establishes authorization to proceed toward closure. Re-closure is a distinct governed lifecycle event that confirms closure conditions are satisfied and activates the next monitoring state.

```text
RE-ACCEPTANCE
 ↓
CLOSURE CRITERIA
 ↓
FINAL EVIDENCE
 ↓
CONDITIONS VERIFIED
 ↓
RESIDUAL RISK CONFIRMED
 ↓
BASELINE UPDATE
 ↓
KNOWLEDGE CAPTURE
 ↓
CLOSURE AUTHORITY
 ↓
RE-CLOSURE
 ↓
ARCHIVE
 ↓
POST-CLOSURE MONITORING
```

## Re-Closure Quality Test
```text
VALID RE-ACCEPTANCE
+
CLOSURE CRITERIA SATISFIED
+
FINAL EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT
+
OPEN CONDITIONS RESOLVED / GOVERNED
+
RESIDUAL RISK ACCEPTABLE
+
BASELINE UPDATED
+
AUTHORIZED CLOSURE
=
GOVERNED RE-CLOSURE
```

## Re-Closure Status Model
```text
NOT READY
CLOSURE PACKAGE PREPARATION
EVIDENCE REVIEW
CONDITION REVIEW
RISK REVIEW
BASELINE REVIEW
AUTHORITY REVIEW
READY FOR RE-CLOSURE
AUTHORIZED
RECLOSED
ARCHIVED
MONITORING ACTIVE
REOPENED
```

## Re-Closure Invariants

```text
RE-ACCEPTANCE ≠ RE-CLOSURE
```

```text
NO VALID RE-ACCEPTANCE → NO RE-CLOSURE
```

```text
NO CLOSURE CRITERIA → NO GOVERNED CLOSURE
```

```text
NO FINAL EVIDENCE → NO CLOSURE
```

```text
MATERIAL OPEN CONDITION → NO UNCONDITIONAL CLOSURE
```

```text
RESIDUAL RISK SHALL BE CURRENT AND EXPLICIT
```

```text
BASELINE UPDATE SHALL BE GOVERNED AND TRACEABLE
```

```text
ORIGINAL HISTORY SHALL NOT BE OVERWRITTEN
```

```text
RE-CLOSURE SHALL CREATE A NEW IMMUTABLE GOVERNANCE EVENT
```

```text
ARCHIVE ≠ MONITORING
```

```text
POST-CLOSURE MONITORING SHALL BE ACTIVATED AS PART OF CLOSURE
```

```text
AI RECOMMENDATION ≠ CLOSURE AUTHORITY
```

```text
AGENT ACTION ≠ CLOSURE AUTHORITY
```

VALID REGRESSION MAY REOPEN THE CLOSED LIFECYCLE

## Condition Closure
Every material condition shall have an explicit disposition. Conditions may be verified closed, transferred into an approved ongoing control, conditionally retained with authority, or escalated. Silent carry-over is prohibited.

## Residual Risk Closure
Re-closure shall confirm that the current residual risk is within approved appetite or is covered by an explicit authorized exception. Historical risk records remain immutable.

## Historical Integrity
Original finding, remediation, validation, acceptance, reopening, re-validation and re-acceptance records shall remain immutable. Re-closure creates a new linked lifecycle event.

## Post-Closure Monitoring Activation
Re-closure shall activate the applicable post-closure monitoring state, including metrics, thresholds, ownership, review frequency and escalation paths.

   ↓
