# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Event Delivery Architecture throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Event Delivery Architecture provides standardized mechanisms for delivering enterprise events reliably, consistently and securely while preserving architectural integrity, scalability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Event Delivery
- Delivery Mechanisms
- Delivery Policies
- Delivery Guarantees
- Delivery Reliability
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Event Delivery implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

# 4. Enterprise Event Delivery Principles

Enterprise Event Delivery implementations shall follow these principles.

- Explicit Event Delivery
- Reliable Delivery Mechanisms
- Deterministic Delivery Policies
- Technology Independence
- Traceable Event Delivery
- Centralized Governance
- Explicit Delivery Ownership
- Consistent Delivery Behavior

Enterprise Event Delivery implementations shall remain independent of presentation, persistence and unrelated business workflow concerns.

Enterprise Event Delivery implementations shall provide

- delivery reliability

- compliance verification

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Event Delivery implementations shall handle Event Delivery-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify delivery mechanism failures
- classify delivery policy violations
- classify delivery guarantee failures
- classify delivery reliability failures
- classify infrastructure failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Enterprise Event Delivery exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, business integrity, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Event Delivery implementations may depend upon

- approved messaging infrastructure
- approved delivery services
- approved monitoring services
- approved logging services
- approved configuration services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Event Delivery implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Reporting implementations
- Query implementations
- Command implementations outside approved interfaces
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Unapproved external event delivery frameworks

Enterprise Event Delivery capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Event Delivery implementation is compliant when

- Delivery mechanisms are implemented.
- Delivery policies are implemented.
- Delivery guarantees are implemented.
- Delivery reliability is implemented.
- Delivery management is implemented.
- Delivery verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Unauthorized Event Delivery

Events shall never be delivered without approved governance, routing and authorization.

---

## Broken Delivery Guarantees

Delivery guarantees shall never be weakened or bypassed outside approved enterprise policies.

---

## Missing Delivery Validation

Events shall never be delivered without successful validation of delivery configuration and destination readiness.

---

## Hidden Delivery Dependencies

Enterprise implementations shall never introduce undocumented runtime or infrastructure dependencies.

---

## Cross-Capability Delivery Bypass

Capabilities shall never bypass approved enterprise delivery mechanisms to communicate directly with other capabilities.

---

## Uncontrolled Delivery Policies

Delivery policies shall never be modified or bypassed without governance approval.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise Event Delivery implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- Event Delivery architecture compliance

- delivery policy compliance
- dependency compliance
- documentation completeness
- operational readiness
- governance compliance
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Event Delivery Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Event Delivery Architecture throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that delivery mechanisms, delivery policies, delivery guarantees, delivery reliability and governance are implemented consistently while preserving maintainability, scalability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Event Delivery implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
