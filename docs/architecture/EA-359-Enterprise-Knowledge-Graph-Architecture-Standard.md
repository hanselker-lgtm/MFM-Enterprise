# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Knowledge Graph Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Infrastructure Layer principles are inherited from EA-320.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-358.

All Enterprise Knowledge Graph implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Knowledge Graph Architecture governing semantic knowledge, business relationships and connected Enterprise information across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Knowledge Graph Architecture shall

- connect Enterprise information across domains
- establish semantic interoperability
- enable enterprise-wide knowledge discovery
- strengthen business understanding
- improve information reuse
- support intelligent search
- enable AI-ready knowledge representation
- remain technology independent

Enterprise knowledge shall be represented consistently through governed semantic relationships.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to all Enterprise semantic knowledge capabilities.

The scope includes

- knowledge graphs
- ontologies
- taxonomies
- business vocabularies
- semantic relationships
- linked enterprise data
- graph databases
- knowledge APIs
- semantic search
- AI knowledge integration
- enterprise knowledge governance

The standard applies regardless of graph technology, ontology language or cloud provider.

# 17. Enterprise Knowledge Graph Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Duplicate Business Definitions

Enterprise business concepts shall never have multiple conflicting definitions.

Business terminology shall remain centrally governed.

---

## Isolated Knowledge Silos

Knowledge Graph implementations shall never create isolated semantic repositories outside Enterprise Governance.

Enterprise knowledge shall remain interconnected.

---

## Unmanaged Ontologies

Ontologies shall never be modified without governance approval.

Ontology evolution shall remain controlled.

---

## Broken Semantic Relationships

Semantic relationships shall never be established without documented business meaning.

Every relationship shall remain traceable and governed.

---

## Vendor Lock-In

Enterprise Knowledge Graph Architecture shall remain independent of proprietary graph technologies, ontology platforms and semantic tooling.

Architectural portability shall always be preserved.

---

## Missing Semantic Governance

Enterprise semantic assets shall never exist without ownership, lifecycle management and governance.

Knowledge shall remain continuously governed.
