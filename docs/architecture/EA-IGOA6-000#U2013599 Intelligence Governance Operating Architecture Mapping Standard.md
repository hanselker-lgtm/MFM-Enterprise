# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework is to establish an operational governance architecture that ensures Intelligence capabilities are developed, operated, controlled and evolved through defined decision structures, responsibilities, policies, standards, compliance and continuous improvement.

The Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework shall provide:

- governance operating principles
- governance frameworks
- decision governance models
- policy governance models
- standard governance models
- responsibility models
- compliance governance models
- risk governance models
- ethics governance models
- lifecycle governance models
- change governance models
- performance governance models
- audit governance models
- monitoring governance models
- automation governance models
- evolution governance models
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for controlled, responsible and continuously improving Intelligence capabilities.

# 3. Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Governance Operating Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

## Principle 1 – Governance by Design

Governance capability shall be integrated into Intelligence architecture from the beginning.

## Principle 2 – Clear Decision Authority

Decision rights shall be clearly defined and controlled.

---

## Principle 3 – Accountability

Responsibility for Intelligence activities and outcomes shall be clearly assigned.

---

## Principle 4 – Policy Alignment

Intelligence capability shall operate according to defined policies and standards.

---

## Principle 5 – Compliance Management

Intelligence capability shall comply with applicable requirements.

## Principle 6 – Continuous Governance Improvement

Governance capability shall continuously improve through measurement and feedback.

---

## Principle 7 – Evolution Through Governance
