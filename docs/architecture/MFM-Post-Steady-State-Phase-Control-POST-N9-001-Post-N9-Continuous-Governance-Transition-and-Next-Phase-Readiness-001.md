---

# 1. Purpose

POST-N9-001 establishes the controlled transition following formal closure of N9.

The purpose is to ensure that N9 closure does not create a governance gap and that applicable N9 closure conditions are transferred into the appropriate continuing governance mechanisms.

This document does not authorize N10 or any other new execution phase.

---

# 2. Transition Principle

The transition is:

```text
N9
CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS
        ↓
POST-N9 TRANSITION
        ↓
CONTINUOUS GOVERNANCE
        ↓
MATERIAL CHANGE / NEW REQUIREMENT
        ↓
ASSESSMENT
        ↓
FUTURE PHASE DEFINITION
        ↓
SEPARATE AUTHORIZATION
```

---

# 3. N9 Closure Protection

N9 remains:

```text
CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS
```

Closure shall not be interpreted as:

```text
Permanent completion of enterprise evolution
Permanent closure of architecture governance
Permanent closure of risk governance
Permanent closure of outcome monitoring
Permanent closure of value monitoring
Permanent closure of strategic monitoring
```

The governance mechanisms established through N9 continue where applicable.

---

# 4. N8 Protection

N8 remains:

```text
CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS
```

POST-N9 shall not:

```text
Reopen N8
Rewrite N8 closure
Remove N8 conditions
Change historical N8 decisions
Upgrade historical N8 evidence
```

Any N8 reopening requires a separate controlled decision.

---

# 8. Material Change Trigger

A future phase or work package may become necessary when a material change affects:

```text
Strategy
Outcome
Capability
Architecture
Portfolio
Investment
Risk
Dependency
Operating Model
Technology
Regulation
Stakeholder Requirements
Governance
Value
```

A trigger does not automatically create a new phase.

---

# 9. Future Phase Trigger Assessment

A proposed future phase should assess:

```text
Why is a new phase required?
What has changed?
What cannot be handled through existing governance?
What is the proposed scope?
What are the objectives?
What are the deliverables?
What are the dependencies?
What are the risks?
What authority is required?
```

---

# 10. Existing Governance vs New Phase

A future requirement should first be classified:

```text
CAN BE HANDLED BY EXISTING GOVERNANCE
OR
REQUIRES NEW CONTROLLED WORK PACKAGE
OR
REQUIRES NEW PHASE
```

This prevents unnecessary creation of new phases.

---

# 11. New Phase Boundary

A new phase shall not be created merely because:

```textN9 is closed.
```

A new phase requires a material reason, defined scope and explicit authorization.

---

# 12. No Automatic N10

The following rule is mandatory:

```text
N9 CLOSED
≠
N10 AUTHORIZED
```

If N10 becomes appropriate, the sequence shall be:

```text
N10 Concept
    ↓
N10 Scope
    ↓
N10 Readiness
    ↓
N10 Authorization Assessment
    ↓
N10 Formal Authorization
    ↓
N10 Execution
```

> **POST-N9-001 establishes the controlled transition from the formally closed N9 workstream into post-N9 continuous governance.
