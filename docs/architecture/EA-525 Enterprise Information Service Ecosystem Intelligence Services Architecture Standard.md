This standard defines the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Intelligence Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Information Service Intelligence Management principles are inherited from EA-502.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Management principles are inherited from EA-512 and EA-521.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Collaboration principles are inherited from EA-524.

Enterprise Information Governance principles are inherited from EA-486.

All Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Intelligence Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Intelligence Services Architecture supporting secure, standardized and continuously governed intelligence capabilities for understanding, analysing and optimizing digital ecosystems, relationships, interactions and value networks across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Intelligence Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem intelligence capabilities
- support ecosystem analytics
- support ecosystem insights
- support partner intelligence
- support supplier intelligence
- support relationship intelligence
- support ecosystem performance intelligence
- support ecosystem opportunity identification
- support predictive ecosystem analysis
- support AI-supported ecosystem decisions
- support ecosystem dashboards
- support ecosystem intelligence governance
- support data-driven ecosystem optimization
- improve ecosystem transparency
- improve ecosystem decision-making
- improve ecosystem value realization
- support continuous ecosystem evolution

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Intelligence Services shall provide trusted and reusable intelligence capabilities enabling informed ecosystem decisions.
