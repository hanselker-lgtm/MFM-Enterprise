# 1. Purpose

This document defines the ninety-seventh implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-96 – Records Management, Information Governance, Document Lifecycle, Retention, Archiving, Legal Hold & Information Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise risk, compliance, internal control, audit and regulatory assurance capability covering enterprise risk management, risk identification, risk assessment, risk treatment, risk acceptance, control framework, internal controls, control ownership, control testing, compliance obligations, regulatory requirements, policy governance, policy lifecycle, audit management, audit findings, corrective actions, regulatory reporting, compliance monitoring, risk and control self-assessment, assurance mapping and enterprise assurance.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must identify and manage material risks, obligations and control requirements through accountable ownership, evidence-based assessment, effective controls, independent challenge where appropriate, tracked remediation and continuous assurance.**

# 131. Definition of Ready

A risk, compliance or assurance work item is Ready when:

- The objective, risk, obligation, policy or control need is identified.
- Ownership is established.
- Applicable requirements are understood.
- Scope and materiality are known.
- Evidence requirements are identified.
- Relevant dependencies and escalation paths are known.
- Required approvals and independence considerations are identified.

---

# 133. Final Risk Principle

> **Material risks must be visible, owned, assessed, treated and monitored according to their potential effect on organizational objectives.**

---

# 134. Final Control Principle

> **Controls must have clear objectives, accountable owners, sufficient evidence and appropriate testing of design and operation.**

---

# 135. Final Compliance Principle

> **Applicable obligations must be identified, assessed, mapped to controls and monitored throughout their lifecycle.**

---

# 136. Final Policy Principle

> **Policies must be controlled documents with accountable ownership, appropriate approval, effective communication, defined review and governed exceptions.**

---

# 137. Final Audit Principle

> **Audit and independent assurance activities must provide sufficient evidence and appropriate challenge while preserving required independence.**

---

# 138. Final Remediation Principle

> **Findings remain open until corrective action is evidenced, effectiveness is verified and closure is appropriately authorized.**

---

# 139. Final Regulatory Principle

> **Regulatory reporting must be accurate, complete, timely, appropriately approved and supported by traceable evidence.**

---

# 140. Final Assurance Principle

> **Enterprise assurance must connect objectives, risks, obligations, controls, evidence, findings and remediation into a coherent view of organizational confidence.**

---

# 141. Final Integration Principle

> **Risk, Compliance and Assurance must integrate with Security, Privacy, Finance, Architecture, Service Management, Procurement, Projects, Data, Records, Business Continuity and Enterprise Governance.**

---

# 142. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage risk and assurance through a controlled lifecycle connecting objectives, risks, obligations, policies, controls, evidence, assessments, audit, remediation, regulatory reporting and continuous assurance.**
