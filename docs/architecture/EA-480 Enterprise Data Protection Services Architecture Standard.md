# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Data Protection Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Encryption Services principles are inherited from EA-479.

Enterprise Certificate Management Services principles are inherited from EA-478.

Enterprise Key Management Services principles are inherited from EA-477.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Data Protection Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Data Protection Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and continuously governed protection of Enterprise data across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Data Protection Services shall

- provide centralized data protection management
- standardize protection controls
- strengthen data confidentiality
- protect sensitive information
- reduce data exposure risks
- support regulatory compliance
- support Zero Trust principles
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure data protection traceability
- improve operational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Data Protection Services shall provide trusted and reusable data protection capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

This standard applies to

# 3. Enterprise Data Protection Services Principles

Enterprise Data Protection Services shall follow these principles.

## Data Protection by Design

Data protection capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, applications, APIs, services, workloads and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Enterprise data shall be protected according to approved security requirements, classification levels and regulatory obligations.

---

## Data Classification Driven Protection

Protection mechanisms shall be determined by data classification, sensitivity, business value, regulatory requirements and risk exposure.

---

## Privacy and Confidentiality Protection

Enterprise data protection shall ensure appropriate protection of confidential, sensitive and regulated information.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Data Protection Services shall remain independent of vendors, protection platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 33. Conclusion

Enterprise Data Protection Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed protection of Enterprise data across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating data classification, protection policy management, Data Loss Prevention, sensitive data protection, privacy protection, data masking, data tokenization, data access protection, data usage monitoring, data sharing protection, encryption integration, key management integration, certificate integration, secrets management integration, identity integration, authentication integration, authorization integration, risk, audit, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables secure, scalable and policy-driven data protection while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Data Protection Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise data protection capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 34. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Data Protection Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted data protection, autonomous protection policy optimization, predictive data exposure analytics, semantic-aware data protection intelligence, adaptive privacy controls, confidential computing protection models, advanced privacy-enhancing technologies and next-generation Enterprise data protection infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Data Protection Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven data protection capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
