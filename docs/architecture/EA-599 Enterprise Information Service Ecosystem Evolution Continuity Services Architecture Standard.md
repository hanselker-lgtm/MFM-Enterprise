# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Evolution Sustainability Continuity Services Architecture supporting the long-term continuity, sustainability and adaptive evolution of the future Enterprise state following successful convergence of transformed Enterprise capabilities.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Evolution Sustainability Continuity Services shall

- provide standardized evolution sustainability continuity services
- support future-state continuity governance
- support continuous Enterprise evolution
- support sustainable capability continuity
- support sustainable service continuity
- support sustainable technology continuity
- support sustainable information continuity
- support sustainable knowledge continuity
- support strategic continuity
- support innovation continuity
- support transformation continuity
- support maturity continuity
- support continuous improvement continuity
- support adaptive Enterprise continuity

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Evolution Sustainability Continuity Services shall provide trusted and reusable continuity capabilities enabling Enterprise stakeholders to maintain strategic relevance and continuous evolution capability.
