# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Artificial Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise Artificial Intelligence capabilities.

The AI Architecture Framework shall provide:

- AI principles
- AI governance
- AI strategy
- AI capability architecture
- AI service architecture
- AI data architecture
- AI model architecture
- machine learning architecture
- generative AI architecture
- AI security architecture
- responsible AI architecture
- AI operations and MLOps
- AI performance management
- AI evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise AI capabilities are designed, implemented and operated responsibly to support business objectives, intelligent automation and digital transformation.

---

# 2. Scope

The AI Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture AI domains:

- Enterprise AI Model
- AI Governance
- AI Strategy
- AI Capability Architecture
- AI Service Architecture
- AI Data Architecture
- AI Model Architecture
- Machine Learning Architecture
- Generative AI Architecture
- AI Security Architecture
- Responsible AI Architecture
- AI Operations (MLOps)
- AI Performance Management
- AI Maturity
- AI Roadmaps
- AI Evolution

The framework applies to:

- artificial intelligence services
- machine learning solutions
- generative AI solutions
- intelligent automation
- AI-enabled applications
- AI platforms
- AI models
- AI data pipelines

---

# 3. Enterprise AI Architecture Principles

The AI Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Business Value Alignment

AI capabilities shall support Enterprise strategy, business capabilities and measurable outcomes.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible AI

AI solutions shall be designed and operated responsibly with consideration for transparency, fairness and accountability.

---

## Principle 3 – Data Quality

AI capabilities shall depend on trusted, governed and high-quality data.

---

## Principle 4 – Security by Design

AI solutions shall include security controls throughout the complete lifecycle.

---

## Principle 5 – Human Oversight

AI decisions shall support appropriate human involvement and accountability.

---

## Principle 6 – Explainability and Transparency

AI capabilities shall provide appropriate understanding of their purpose, behaviour and limitations.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous AI Evolution

AI capabilities shall continuously evolve according to business requirements and technology development.

---
