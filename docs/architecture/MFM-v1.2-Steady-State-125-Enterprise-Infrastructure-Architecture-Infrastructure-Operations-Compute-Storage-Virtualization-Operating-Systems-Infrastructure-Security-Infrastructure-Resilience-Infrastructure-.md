# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the one-hundred-and-twenty-fifth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-124 – Enterprise Cybersecurity Architecture, Security Governance, Security Operations, Threat Detection, Vulnerability Management, Security Incident Response & Cybersecurity Assurance.

The purpose is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for infrastructure strategy, infrastructure governance, infrastructure architecture, compute, physical and virtual servers, operating systems, storage, backup infrastructure, infrastructure networking, infrastructure monitoring, infrastructure capacity, infrastructure performance, infrastructure patching, infrastructure configuration, infrastructure security, infrastructure incidents, infrastructure changes, infrastructure lifecycle, infrastructure suppliers, infrastructure compliance, infrastructure exceptions, remediation, infrastructure assurance, metrics, dashboards, maturity and continual enterprise infrastructure capability improvement.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must govern infrastructure as a controlled enterprise foundation, ensuring that compute, storage, operating systems, virtualization and supporting infrastructure are appropriately architected, secured, monitored, maintained, resilient, recoverable and lifecycle-controlled in alignment with business and service requirements.**

# 125. Final Infrastructure Governance Principle

> **MFM must govern infrastructure as a controlled enterprise foundation, ensuring that compute, storage, operating systems, virtualization and supporting infrastructure are appropriately architected, secured, monitored, maintained, resilient, recoverable and lifecycle-controlled in alignment with business and service requirements.**

# 126. Final Architecture Principle

> **Infrastructure architecture must provide standardized, scalable, secure, observable, maintainable and recoverable foundations for enterprise applications, data, integration and services.**

# 127. Final Compute Principle

> **Compute capacity must be appropriately sized, monitored, secured and lifecycle-controlled according to workload and business requirements.**

# 128. Final Storage Principle

> **Storage must provide appropriate capacity, performance, security, resilience, backup and recovery aligned with data and service criticality.**

# 129. Final Operating System Principle

> **Operating systems must remain supported, hardened, patched, monitored and lifecycle-controlled, with unsupported platforms removed, isolated or formally risk-accepted.**

# 130. Final Virtualization Principle

> **Virtualization platforms must be securely governed, appropriately isolated, resilient, monitored and maintained throughout their lifecycle.**

# 131. Final Configuration Principle

> **Infrastructure configuration must be baselined, controlled, monitored for drift and recoverable through reliable configuration records.**

# 132. Final Backup Principle

> **Critical infrastructure backups must be protected from unauthorized access, deletion, corruption and ransomware and must be validated through appropriate restore testing.**

# 133. Final Resilience Principle

> **Critical infrastructure must address single points of failure and provide tested recovery capabilities aligned with service RTO and RPO requirements.**

# 134. Final Security Principle

> **Infrastructure security must integrate hardening, patching, access control, segmentation, encryption, logging, monitoring and vulnerability management.**

# 135. Final Lifecycle Principle

> **Infrastructure must be actively managed from procurement and deployment through support, maintenance, modernization, replacement and secure retirement.**

# 136. Final Assurance Principle

> **Material infrastructure controls and services must be supported by reliable evidence and periodically assessed through risk-based assurance.**

# 137. Final Improvement Principle

> **Infrastructure incidents, capacity constraints, vulnerabilities, configuration drift, recovery findings, lifecycle risks and assurance results must continuously improve MFM's infrastructure capability.**

# 138. Final Enterprise Infrastructure Integration Principle

> **Infrastructure Architecture and Operations must integrate with Enterprise Architecture, Applications, Data, Integration, Identity, Network, Cloud, Cybersecurity, IT Service Management, Configuration Management, Asset Management, Finance, Suppliers, Risk, Compliance, Legal and Business Continuity.**
