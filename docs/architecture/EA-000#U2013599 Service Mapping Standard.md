# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Service Reference Model is to establish a consolidated reference model describing Enterprise Services across the complete MFM Enterprise Architecture Framework from EA-000 through EA-599.

The Service Reference Model shall provide:

- service identification
- service classification
- service ownership
- service relationships
- service capability mapping
- service lifecycle management
- architecture traceability
- service portfolio visibility

The model shall establish a common Enterprise language for defining, managing and evolving services.

---

# 2. Scope

The Service Reference Model covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture service domains:

- Architecture Services
- Business Services
- Information Services
- Data Services
- Application Services
- Technology Services
- Security Services
- Governance Services
- Ecosystem Services
- Evolution Services

The model applies to:

- strategic services
- operational services
- enabling services
- supporting services
- transformation services
- evolution services

---

# 3. Enterprise Service Architecture Principles

The Service Reference Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Service Orientation

Enterprise capabilities shall be delivered through defined services.

---

## Capability Alignment

Every service shall support one or more Enterprise capabilities.

---

## Reusability

Services shall be designed as reusable Enterprise assets.

---

## Standardization

Services shall follow common Enterprise standards.

---

## Lifecycle Management

Services shall be managed throughout their complete lifecycle.

---

## Continuous Evolution

Services shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
