The purpose of this phase is to establish the enterprise risk-management, business-risk, control-assurance and resilience-governance baseline for MFM.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must identify, assess, treat, monitor and report material risks while maintaining clear ownership of controls, residual risk and resilience measures across financial, operational, security, privacy, data, technology, third-party and business-continuity domains.**

# 135. Final Risk Principle

> **Every material risk must have an accountable owner, an explicit assessment, a defined treatment or acceptance decision and a review mechanism.**

---

# 136. Final Control Principle

> **Controls must be mapped to risks, owned, evidenced, tested and remediated when ineffective.**

---

# 137. Final Residual-Risk Principle

> **Risk is not eliminated merely because controls exist; residual exposure must be understood and explicitly governed.**

---

# 138. Final Appetite Principle

> **Risk acceptance must remain within approved appetite and tolerance or be explicitly escalated and authorized.**

---

# 139. Final Treatment Principle

> **Risk treatment should prioritize material exposure and demonstrate the expected reduction in risk.**

---

# 140. Final Resilience Principle

> **Critical business processes must have identified dependencies, recovery expectations and tested resilience measures.**

---

# 141. Final Interdependency Principle

> **Risks that share dependencies or can cascade across domains must be assessed as connected exposures rather than isolated entries.**

---

# 142. Final Assurance Principle

> **Risk and control effectiveness should be challenged through evidence-based assurance that is sufficiently independent where required.**

---

# 143. Final Monitoring Principle

> **Key risk indicators should provide early warning before material risk becomes an uncontrolled incident.**

---

# 144. Final Learning Principle

> **Material incidents, control failures and resilience exercises must feed lessons back into risk treatment and governance.**

---

# 145. Final Lifecycle Principle

> **Risk records, assessments and acceptance decisions must remain historically traceable as risk conditions change.**

---

# 146. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should operate an integrated risk and resilience governance model in which risks, controls, incidents, changes, projects, vendors and continuity requirements are connected, measurable and actively managed.**
