# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Utilization Services Architecture supporting effective, efficient and optimized use of Enterprise capabilities, services, information and technology across the MFM Enterprise Platform ecosystem.

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Utilization Services shall

- provide standardized ecosystem utilization services
- support utilization governance
- support utilization strategy
- support utilization objectives
- support utilization planning
- support business utilization
- support operational utilization
- support service utilization
- support capability utilization
- support technology utilization
- support application utilization
- support infrastructure utilization
- support data utilization
- support information utilization
- support knowledge utilization
- support user utilization
- support utilization measurement
- support utilization optimization
- support utilization improvement
- support utilization maturity development
- support continuous utilization capability development

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Utilization Services shall provide trusted and reusable utilization capabilities enabling Enterprise stakeholders to maximize value from Enterprise capabilities and services.
