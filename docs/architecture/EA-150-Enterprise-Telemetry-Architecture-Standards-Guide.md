# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing enterprise telemetry throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Telemetry ensures that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and applications continuously produce operational data that supports monitoring, diagnostics, analytics, troubleshooting and Enterprise Architecture governance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Telemetry Collection
- Event Instrumentation
- Metrics Streaming
- Telemetry Pipelines
- Telemetry Storage
- Telemetry Analytics
- Governance
- Compliance

All enterprise telemetry implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

Enable data-driven operations.

Support diagnostics and analytics.

## TM-005

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Telemetry Principles

Enterprise telemetry shall follow these principles.

- Telemetry by Design
- Continuous Data Collection
- Standardized Instrumentation
- Centralized Telemetry
- Secure Telemetry Transport
- Complete Traceability
- Governance by Default
- Continuous Improvement

Telemetry shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Telemetry Categories

Enterprise telemetry shall be organized into standardized categories.

Categories shall include

- Infrastructure Telemetry
- Platform Telemetry
- Application Telemetry
- Database Telemetry
- Network Telemetry
- API Telemetry
- User Experience Telemetry
- Operational Analytics

Additional telemetry categories shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Telemetry Ownership

Each telemetry domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- telemetry responsibility
- governance responsibility
- compliance responsibility

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the telemetry lifecycle.

---

# 7. Telemetry Governance

Enterprise telemetry governance shall define

- telemetry governance
- telemetry approval
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- telemetry verification
- governance reporting

Telemetry governance shall remain technology independent.

# 8. Telemetry Responsibilities

Enterprise telemetry shall provide controlled coordination of enterprise operational data collection and analysis.

Telemetry responsibilities shall

- separate telemetry from operational execution
- coordinate telemetry ownership
- ensure telemetry consistency
- validate telemetry objectives
- preserve telemetry traceability
- support enterprise operational excellence

Telemetry implementations shall never contain enterprise business rules.

---

# 9. Event Instrumentation

Enterprise telemetry shall implement standardized event instrumentation.

Event instrumentation shall

- define telemetry events
- instrument infrastructure events
- instrument platform events
- instrument application events
- preserve event history
- maintain event traceability

Event instrumentation shall remain centrally governed.

---

# 10. Metrics Streaming

Enterprise telemetry shall implement standardized metrics streaming.

Metrics streaming shall

- collect real-time metrics
- transport metrics securely
- support scalable ingestion
- preserve streaming history
- ensure delivery reliability
- maintain metrics traceability

Metrics streaming shall remain continuously operational.

---

# 11. Telemetry Pipelines

Enterprise telemetry shall implement standardized telemetry pipelines.

Telemetry pipelines shall

- collect telemetry
- validate telemetry
- enrich telemetry
- route telemetry
- preserve pipeline history
- maintain processing traceability

Telemetry pipelines shall support reliable enterprise analytics.

---

# 12. Telemetry Storage

Enterprise telemetry shall implement standardized telemetry storage.

Telemetry storage shall

- preserve telemetry integrity
- support efficient retrieval
- support long-term retention
- protect sensitive telemetry
- preserve storage history
- maintain storage traceability

Telemetry storage shall remain aligned with enterprise governance.

---

# 13. Telemetry Dependencies

Enterprise telemetry shall document all dependencies.

Dependencies shall include

- monitoring systems
- logging systems
- analytics platforms
- infrastructure management
- storage management
- enterprise governance

Telemetry implementations shall never introduce undocumented dependencies.

---

# 14. Telemetry Documentation

Each telemetry domain shall maintain complete documentation.

Documentation shall include

- telemetry objectives
- ownership information
- instrumentation standards
- pipeline architecture
- dependency analysis
- governance records

Documentation shall remain synchronized with Enterprise Architecture.

# 15. Telemetry Lifecycle

Enterprise telemetry shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

Lifecycle stages shall include

- Planned
- Designed
- Instrumented
- Verified
- Operational
- Collected
- Reviewed
- Optimized
- Approved
- Improved

Lifecycle transitions shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 16. Telemetry Quality Attributes

Enterprise telemetry implementations shall satisfy defined quality attributes.

Quality attributes shall include

- completeness
- accuracy
- reliability
- scalability
- availability
- traceability
- auditability
- maintainability

Quality attributes shall be evaluated throughout the telemetry lifecycle.

---

# 17. Telemetry Registry

The enterprise shall maintain a centralized telemetry registry.

The registry shall contain

- telemetry identifiers
- ownership assignments
- telemetry classifications
- lifecycle status
- instrumentation definitions
- pipeline configurations
- documentation references
- governance status

The telemetry registry shall be considered the authoritative source for enterprise telemetry.

---

# 18. Telemetry Reviews

Enterprise telemetry implementations shall undergo formal architecture reviews where applicable.

Architecture reviews shall verify

- telemetry quality
- instrumentation completeness
- pipeline reliability
- storage compliance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- enterprise alignment

Review outcomes shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 19. Telemetry Metrics

Enterprise telemetry shall be measured using standardized metrics.

Metrics shall include

- telemetry coverage
- event collection success rate
- pipeline throughput
- telemetry latency
- storage availability
- analytics readiness
- audit findings
- architecture compliance

Metrics shall support continuous telemetry improvement.

---

# 20. Telemetry Verification

Enterprise telemetry implementations shall undergo formal verification before approval and periodically thereafter.

Verification shall

- confirm telemetry objectives
- verify instrumentation implementation
- verify pipeline operation
- verify storage implementation
- confirm ownership
- verify documentation completeness
- approve operational readiness

Telemetry verification shall remain documented and auditable.

---

# 21. Continuous Telemetry Improvement

Enterprise telemetry shall continuously improve.

Continuous improvement shall

- improve telemetry quality
- improve instrumentation coverage
- improve analytics capabilities
- improve operational diagnostics
- strengthen governance
- support future enterprise capabilities

Continuous improvement shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture Principles.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise telemetry implementations shall handle telemetry exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify telemetry collection failures
- classify instrumentation failures
- classify pipeline failures
- classify storage failures
- classify analytics processing failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Telemetry exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, operational visibility or governance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Telemetry implementations may depend upon

- approved monitoring systems
- approved logging systems
- approved analytics platforms
- approved infrastructure management systems
- approved storage management systems
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Telemetry implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations
- Business Services
- Unapproved external telemetry services

Telemetry capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A telemetry implementation is compliant when

- Telemetry responsibilities are documented.
- Event instrumentation standards are implemented.
- Metrics streaming is operational.
- Telemetry pipelines are configured.
- Telemetry storage is secured.
- Dependencies are documented.
- Telemetry Registry is maintained.
- Telemetry verification has been completed.
- Architecture Review has been completed where applicable.
- Audit logging is enabled where applicable.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Missing Instrumentation

Enterprise services shall never operate without required telemetry instrumentation.

---

## Incomplete Telemetry Collection

Telemetry pipelines shall never omit required operational events or metrics.

---

## Unsecured Telemetry Transport

Telemetry data shall never be transmitted without appropriate protection where confidentiality or integrity is required.

---

## Fragmented Telemetry Pipelines

Enterprise telemetry shall never rely upon undocumented or inconsistent telemetry processing pipelines.

---

## Undocumented Telemetry Dependencies

Telemetry implementations shall never rely upon undocumented infrastructure or service dependencies.

---

## Telemetry Outside Governance

Telemetry implementations shall never bypass enterprise governance, approval processes or audit requirements.

---

# 26. Governance

Enterprise telemetry implementations shall undergo Enterprise Architecture Review where required.

Architecture Review shall verify

- telemetry quality
- instrumentation completeness
- pipeline reliability
- storage compliance
- dependency compliance
- governance compliance
- documentation completeness
- auditability
- operational visibility
- compliance with enterprise standards

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Telemetry Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing telemetry throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise infrastructure, platforms, services and applications generate reliable, secure and traceable telemetry through standardized instrumentation, collection, transport, storage, verification, governance and continuous improvement while preserving operational excellence and Enterprise Architecture compliance.

All enterprise telemetry implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.

End of Document.
