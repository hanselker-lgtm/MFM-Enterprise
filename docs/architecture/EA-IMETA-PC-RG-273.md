## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory procedural verification layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-273, determining whether the substantive validation of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-272 verification was correctly performed, evidenced, authorized, decided, recorded and implemented.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-272 determines whether the RG-271 verification is substantively effective. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-273 verifies that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-272's validation of that verification was correctly performed and implemented.

```text
RG-270 → VALIDATE
RG-271 → VERIFY
RG-272 → VALIDATE
RG-273 → VERIFY
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-272 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-273 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
WAS EA-IMETA-PC-RG-272 CORRECTLY PERFORMED?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-272 validation SHALL NOT automatically prove that EA-IMETA-PC-RG-272 was correctly verified.
