# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Distributed Transactions & Saga throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Distributed Transactions & Saga provides standardized mechanisms for coordinating distributed business processes, ensuring consistency across multiple capabilities, supporting compensation patterns and preserving reliability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Distributed Transactions
- Saga Orchestration
- Saga Choreography
- Compensation Patterns
- Transaction Consistency
- Failure Recovery
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Distributed Transactions & Saga implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Distributed Two-Phase Commit Across Enterprise Services

Enterprise implementations shall never rely on distributed two-phase commit across independently deployable services where approved Saga patterns are required.

---

## Missing Compensation Logic

Distributed business processes shall never execute irreversible business actions without approved compensation mechanisms where compensation is required.

---

## Hidden Transaction Dependencies

Distributed transaction implementations shall never introduce undocumented orchestration, messaging or coordination dependencies.

---

## Inconsistent Transaction State

Distributed transactions shall never leave business processes in an inconsistent state without approved recovery or compensation strategies.

---

## Untracked Saga Execution

Saga execution shall never occur without traceability, monitoring and audit logging.

---

## Business Logic Inside Transaction Infrastructure

Enterprise Distributed Transactions & Saga implementations shall never contain business logic that belongs within the appropriate Domain or Capability implementation.

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Distributed Transactions & Saga Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing distributed transactions and Saga implementations throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that distributed business processes, compensation patterns and transaction consistency are implemented consistently while preserving scalability, resilience, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Distributed Transactions & Saga implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
