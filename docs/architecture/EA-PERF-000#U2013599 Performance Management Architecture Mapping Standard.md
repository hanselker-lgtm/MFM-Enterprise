# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Performance Management Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise performance as a strategic capability.

The Performance Management Architecture Framework shall provide:

- performance principles
- performance governance
- performance structures
- measurement architecture
- KPI management
- analytics management
- reporting management
- performance improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise performance is measured, understood and improved in alignment with strategic objectives and business value.

---

# 2. Scope

The Performance Management Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture performance domains:

- Enterprise Performance Model
- Performance Governance
- Performance Strategy
- Performance Domains
- Measurement Architecture
- KPI Management
- Performance Analytics
- Reporting Architecture
- Dashboard Management
- Performance Improvement
- Performance Risk Alignment
- Performance Maturity
- Performance Evolution

The framework applies to:

- strategic performance
- business performance
- capability performance
- process performance
- service performance
- application performance
- technology performance
- infrastructure performance
- transformation performance

---

# 3. Enterprise Performance Management Architecture Principles

The Performance Management Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategy Alignment

Performance measurements shall directly support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Value-Based Measurement

Performance shall be measured according to delivered business value and outcomes.

---

## Principle 3 – Data-Driven Decisions

Performance decisions shall be based on reliable measurements and information.

---

## Principle 4 – Clear Performance Ownership

All performance areas shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Principle 5 – Balanced Measurement

Performance shall consider effectiveness, efficiency, quality, risk and value.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Monitoring

Performance shall be continuously monitored to identify trends and opportunities.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

Performance management shall drive ongoing improvement and evolution.

---

# 9. Performance Domain Architecture

The Performance Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise performance is structured into logical measurement domains to support governance, decision-making and continuous improvement.

Performance domains provide a common structure for organizing objectives, indicators, measurements and responsibilities.

---

# 9.1 Performance Domain Principles

Performance domains shall ensure:

- clear performance ownership
- strategic measurement alignment
- consistent evaluation
- measurable outcomes
- continuous improvement

---

# 9.2 Performance Domain Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Performance Domain | Logical grouping of performance areas |
| Performance Owner | Accountability for performance domain |
| Objectives | Defines expected outcomes |
| Measurements | Defines evaluation criteria |
| Improvements | Defines optimization activities |

---

```text

        |

        |

        |

        |
