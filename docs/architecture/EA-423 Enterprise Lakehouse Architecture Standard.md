# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Lakehouse Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Infrastructure Architecture principles are inherited from EA-320.

Enterprise Integration Architecture principles are inherited from EA-340 through EA-345.

Enterprise Data Architecture principles are inherited from EA-350 through EA-359.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

Enterprise Data Fabric Architecture principles are inherited from EA-421.

Enterprise Data Mesh Architecture principles are inherited from EA-422.

All Enterprise Lakehouse implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Lakehouse Architecture governing unified storage, processing, governance and analytics of Enterprise data across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Lakehouse Architecture shall

- unify Data Lake and Data Warehouse capabilities
- support structured and unstructured data
- enable real-time analytics
- support batch analytics
- improve data scalability
- strengthen governance
- support artificial intelligence
- simplify Enterprise analytics
- improve operational efficiency
- remain technology independent

Enterprise Lakehouse shall function as the primary analytical data platform supporting all Business and Technology Domains.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- analytical data platforms
- operational analytics
- business intelligence
- artificial intelligence
- machine learning
- streaming analytics
- historical analytics
- data engineering
- metadata management
- enterprise reporting

The standard applies regardless of infrastructure platform, cloud provider or deployment model.

---

# 3. Enterprise Lakehouse Principles

Enterprise Lakehouse shall be governed by the following principles.

## Unified Data Platform

The Enterprise shall maintain a unified analytical platform supporting both operational and analytical workloads.

---

## Open Architecture

Enterprise Lakehouse shall utilize open architectural principles supporting interoperability and portability.

---

## Data Once

Enterprise data shall be stored once and reused multiple times wherever practical.

---

## Metadata Driven

Metadata shall govern storage, discovery, governance and lifecycle management.

---

## Scalable Analytics

The architecture shall scale horizontally to support increasing analytical workloads.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Lakehouse Architecture shall remain independent of specific vendors, storage technologies and processing engines.
