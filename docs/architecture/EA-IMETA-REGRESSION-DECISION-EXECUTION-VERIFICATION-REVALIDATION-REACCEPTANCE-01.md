`EA-IMETA-REGRESSION-DECISION-EXECUTION-VERIFICATION-REVALIDATION-REACCEPTANCE-01`

### Governing Architecture: EA-IMETA-MASTER-01

## Purpose
Establish the authoritative re-acceptance architecture after successful re-validation, ensuring that the evidence package, current residual risk, open conditions, authority, challenge and decision criteria support an explicit and traceable re-acceptance decision before re-closure.

## Core Principle
Re-validation establishes readiness for re-acceptance. Re-acceptance is a distinct governance decision and shall not be inferred from a positive validation result.

```text
RE-VALIDATION PASSED
 ↓
EVIDENCE PACKAGE
 ↓
CURRENT RESIDUAL RISK
 ↓
OPEN CONDITIONS
 ↓
AUTHORITY REVIEW
 ↓
CHALLENGE
 ↓
RE-ACCEPTANCE DECISION
 ↓
RE-CLOSURE
```

## Re-Acceptance Quality Test
```text
VALID RE-VALIDATION
+
COMPLETE EVIDENCE PACKAGE
+
CURRENT RESIDUAL RISK
+
CONDITIONS GOVERNED
+
AUTHORIZED DECISION MAKER
+
INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE
+
TRACEABLE RATIONALE
=
GOVERNED RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

## Re-Acceptance Status Model
```text
NOT READY
PACKAGE IN PREPARATION
EVIDENCE REVIEW
RISK REVIEW
CONDITIONS REVIEW
AUTHORITY REVIEW
UNDER CHALLENGE
READY FOR DECISION
ACCEPTED
CONDITIONALLY ACCEPTED
DEFERRED
REJECTED
ESCALATED
READY FOR RE-CLOSURE
```

## Re-Acceptance Invariants

```text
RE-VALIDATION ≠ RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
NO VALID RE-VALIDATION → NO POSITIVE RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
NO CURRENT EVIDENCE → NO POSITIVE RE-ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
CURRENT RESIDUAL RISK SHALL BE EXPLICIT
```

```text
OPEN MATERIAL CONDITIONS SHALL BLOCK UNCONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE
```

```text
CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE SHALL HAVE OWNER, DUE DATE AND REVIEW CRITERIA
```

```text
DEFERRED ACCEPTANCE SHALL HAVE EXPLICIT RATIONALE AND RISK TREATMENT
```

```text
AUTHORITY SHALL BE APPROPRIATE TO MATERIALITY AND RISK
```

```text
CHALLENGE SHALL BE TRACEABLE
```

```text
RE-ACCEPTANCE SHALL CREATE A NEW GOVERNANCE EVENT
```

```text
ORIGINAL ACCEPTANCE HISTORY SHALL NOT BE OVERWRITTEN
```

```text
AI RECOMMENDATION ≠ RE-ACCEPTANCE AUTHORITY
```

```text
AGENT EXECUTION ≠ RE-ACCEPTANCE AUTHORITY
```

```text
POSITIVE RE-ACCEPTANCE → RE-CLOSURE READINESS
```

## Re-Acceptance Decision Matrix
| Dimension | States |
|---|---|
| Re-Validation | Passed / Partial / Failed |
| Evidence | Missing / Partial / Sufficient / Invalid |
| Residual Risk | Within Appetite / Elevated / Above Appetite / Critical |
| Conditions | None / Open / Verified / Overdue |
| Authority | Confirmed / Delegated / Insufficient / Escalated |
| Challenge | Complete / Open / Material Objection |
| Decision | Accept / Conditional Accept / Defer / Reject / Escalate |

## Re-Acceptance Record Model
| Record | Minimum information |
|---|---|
| Re-Acceptance Record | ID, re-validation, evidence, risk, conditions, authority, decision, rationale, timestamp |
| Evidence Package | source, version, integrity, completeness, sufficiency, traceability |
| Residual Risk Record | current risk, appetite, treatment, owner, acceptance rationale |
| Condition Record | condition, owner, due date, expiry, monitoring, disposition |
| Challenge Record | challenger, issue, evidence, response, resolution |
| Authority Record | decision maker, role, authority level, delegation, validity |
| Decision Record | outcome, rationale, constraints, approval, effective date |

## Conditional Re-Acceptance
Conditional re-acceptance may be used only when the remaining conditions are explicitly defined, risk-assessed, owned, time-bounded where appropriate, monitored and subject to clear closure criteria.

```text
CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE
 ↓
CONDITION
 ↓
OWNER
 ↓
DUE DATE / REVIEW POINT
 ↓
MONITOR
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
CLOSE / EXTEND / REJECT
```

## Deferred Re-Acceptance
Deferral is not acceptance. A deferred decision shall state why the decision cannot yet be made, who owns the next action, the decision deadline, interim controls and the risk of delay.

## Residual Risk
Re-acceptance shall consider current residual risk rather than relying on the risk position recorded before remediation. Material residual risk above appetite requires escalation, additional treatment or explicit authorized exception.

## Re-Closure Boundary
Positive re-acceptance establishes readiness for re-closure. Re-closure remains a separate authorized lifecycle event.
