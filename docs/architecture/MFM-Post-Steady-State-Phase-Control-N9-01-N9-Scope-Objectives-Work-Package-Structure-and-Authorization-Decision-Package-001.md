# 3. N9 Primary Objective

The primary objective of N9 is to establish a controlled mechanism for continuous enterprise evolution after the completion of the N8 strategy, architecture, portfolio, investment and realization governance layers.

The core model is:

```text

Enterprise Impact

      ↓

      ↓
Portfolio Impact
      ↓
Investment Impact
      ↓
Operational Impact
      ↓

      ↓
Evidence
      ↓

```

```text

AUTH-N9-10
```
