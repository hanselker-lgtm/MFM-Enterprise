# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise AI Architecture.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Event Architecture principles are inherited from EA-112.

Infrastructure Layer principles are inherited from EA-320.

Enterprise Search principles are inherited from EA-332.

Enterprise Knowledge Graph principles are inherited from EA-333.

All Enterprise AI implementations shall conform to this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to define the Enterprise principles governing Artificial Intelligence throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise AI shall

- provide trustworthy AI services
- support Enterprise decision making
- improve productivity
- enhance knowledge discovery
- augment human capabilities
- preserve governance
- remain technology independent

Enterprise AI shall complement human expertise rather than replace Enterprise accountability.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every Artificial Intelligence capability implemented within the Enterprise Platform.

It governs

- AI services
- Large Language Models
- AI agents
- model management
- prompt management
- AI governance
- AI security
- AI lifecycle
- monitoring

The standard applies independently of AI vendors, foundation models and deployment technologies.

---

# 3. Enterprise AI Definition

Enterprise AI is the controlled application of Artificial Intelligence technologies to support Enterprise business capabilities.

Enterprise AI may include

- Large Language Models
- Generative AI
- AI assistants
- AI agents
- reasoning engines
- machine learning services
- intelligent automation
- decision support

Enterprise AI shall operate under Enterprise governance, security and compliance requirements.

---

# 4. Enterprise AI Objectives

Enterprise AI shall

- improve operational efficiency
- support knowledge workers
- improve information accessibility
- support intelligent automation
- improve decision support
- preserve explainability
- preserve human accountability

Enterprise AI shall remain an Enterprise-wide Infrastructure capability serving all authorized business domains.

---

# 5. Enterprise AI Responsibilities

The Enterprise AI Architecture is responsible for

- AI service governance
- model governance
- prompt governance
- AI integration
- AI monitoring
- AI security
- lifecycle management
- compliance

Enterprise AI shall never

- replace business ownership
- bypass Enterprise security
- make uncontrolled autonomous business decisions
- expose confidential information
- operate outside approved governance

Business accountability shall always remain with authorized human decision makers.

# 15. Explainability

Enterprise AI shall support explainable decision support wherever technically feasible.

Explainability shall include

- source attribution
- reasoning visibility
- confidence indicators
- model identification
- prompt traceability
- data provenance
- decision context
- recommendation rationale

Enterprise users shall be able to distinguish

- authoritative Enterprise information
- inferred information
- AI-generated recommendations
- probabilistic predictions

AI-generated output shall never be represented as authoritative Enterprise knowledge without verification.

# 21. Enterprise AI Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Uncontrolled Autonomous Decisions

AI shall never perform business-critical decisions without defined human accountability.

---

## Vendor Lock-in

Enterprise AI implementations shall not become dependent upon proprietary AI vendors.

Provider replacement shall remain feasible through Enterprise abstractions.

---

## Prompt Sprawl

Business-critical prompts shall never exist as unmanaged application code.

Prompt assets shall be centrally governed.

---

## Hidden AI Usage

Enterprise users shall always know when they are interacting with AI-generated content.

AI assistance shall remain transparent.

---

## Unverified AI Output

AI-generated responses shall never become authoritative Enterprise records without validation.

Enterprise information shall always remain the system of record.

---

## Missing Governance

AI services shall never be deployed without

- ownership
- approval
- monitoring
- security controls
- lifecycle management
- compliance verification

Enterprise AI governance is mandatory.
