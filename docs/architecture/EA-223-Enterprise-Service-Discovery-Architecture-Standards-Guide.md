# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Service Discovery throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Service Discovery ensures that enterprise services are registered, discovered, resolved and monitored consistently while preserving scalability, availability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Service Registration
- Service Catalog
- Service Resolution
- Health Checking
- Service Availability
- Service Monitoring
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Service Discovery implementations shall comply with this guide.

---

## SD-001

## SD-003

Maintain compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

# 4. Service Discovery Principles

Enterprise Service Discovery implementations shall follow these principles.

- Centralized Service Registry
- Dynamic Service Discovery
- High Availability
- Health-Based Resolution
- Traceability by Design
- Technology Independence
- Security by Default
- Centralized Governance

Enterprise Service Discovery implementations shall remain independent of business logic.

Enterprise Service Discovery shall provide

- service reporting

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Service Discovery implementations shall handle service discovery-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify service registration failures
- classify service resolution failures
- classify health check failures
- classify service monitoring failures
- classify service catalog failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Service Discovery exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, service integrity, traceability, governance or compliance.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Service Discovery implementations may depend upon

- approved service registry services
- approved API services
- approved messaging services
- approved monitoring services
- approved security services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Service Discovery implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external service registry providers

Enterprise Service Discovery capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Service Discovery implementation is compliant when

- Service registration is documented.
- Service catalog is maintained.
- Service resolution is implemented.
- Health checking is continuously active.
- Service monitoring is continuously active.
- Service verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Unregistered Services

Enterprise services shall never be deployed without registration in the approved service registry.

---

## Duplicate Service Registrations

A service shall never have multiple conflicting registrations within the enterprise service catalog.

---

## Stale Service Information

Service registry entries shall never contain outdated endpoints, metadata or health information.

---

## Unverified Service Resolution

Service consumers shall never bypass approved service discovery mechanisms or use undocumented endpoints.

---

## Unmonitored Service Availability

Enterprise services shall never operate without continuous health monitoring and availability verification.

---

## Business Logic Inside Service Discovery Infrastructure

Service discovery infrastructure shall never contain business logic that belongs within the appropriate Domain or Capability implementation.

---
