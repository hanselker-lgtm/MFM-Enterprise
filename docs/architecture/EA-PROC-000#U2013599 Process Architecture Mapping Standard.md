# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Process Architecture Framework is to establish a structured approach for managing Enterprise processes as strategic operational capabilities.

The Process Architecture Framework shall provide:

- process principles
- process governance
- process strategy
- process structures
- process domains
- process classification
- process mapping
- process assessment
- process optimization
- process improvement

The framework ensures that Enterprise processes are identified, understood and continuously improved in alignment with strategic objectives, capability requirements and business value.

---

# 2. Scope

The Process Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture process domains:

- Enterprise Process Model
- Process Governance
- Process Strategy
- Process Domains
- Process Classification
- Process Mapping
- Process Assessment
- Process Maturity
- Process Optimization
- Process Performance
- Process Roadmaps
- Process Evolution

The framework applies to:

- business processes
- operational processes
- management processes
- support processes
- service delivery processes
- transformation processes

---

# 3. Enterprise Process Architecture Principles

The Process Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Process-Centric Management

Enterprise activities shall be managed through clearly defined processes.

---

## Principle 2 – Strategic Process Alignment

Processes shall support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 3 – Process Ownership

Every process shall have defined ownership and accountability.

---

## Principle 4 – Process Transparency

Processes shall be documented, understood and measurable.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Process Improvement

Processes shall continuously evolve based on performance, requirements and opportunities.

---

## Principle 6 – Process Integration

Processes shall connect capabilities, services, applications and technology.

---

## Principle 7 – Value-Oriented Processes

Processes shall create measurable value for stakeholders.

---

# 9. Process Domain Architecture

The Process Domain Architecture Model defines how Enterprise processes are structured into logical domains to support governance, operation, optimization and continuous improvement.

Process domains provide a common structure for organizing Enterprise processes and ensuring alignment between business capabilities, services and operational execution.

---

# 9.1 Process Domain Principles

Process domains shall ensure:

- clear process organization
- defined process ownership
- operational transparency
- consistent process management
- continuous process improvement

---

# 9.2 Process Domain Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Process Domain | Logical grouping of processes |
| Process Owner | Accountability for process area |
| Process Purpose | Defines process objectives |
| Process Relationships | Defines dependencies |
| Process Measures | Evaluates process effectiveness |

---

# 9.3 Process Domain Lifecycle

```text

        |

Define Process Scope

        |

Assign Process Ownership

        |

Establish Process Structure

        |

Assess Process Performance

        |

Improve Process Domain

# 21. Process Governance Operating Model
