# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the thirty-fifth document in the MFM v1.2 Steady-State series.

It follows MFM v1.2-Steady-State-34 – Enterprise Financial Management, Accounting, Budgeting, Treasury, Internal Financial Controls & Financial Governance.

The purpose of this document is to establish the permanent enterprise operating model for procurement governance, sourcing, supplier selection, supplier onboarding, supplier performance, contract management, third-party risk, vendor security, commercial governance, supplier continuity, supplier assurance, supplier findings and supplier lifecycle management.

The central objective is:

> **MFM third-party relationships must be selected, contracted, governed, monitored and retired through controlled, transparent and risk-based processes that protect business value, security, continuity, compliance and organizational interests.**

# 125. Definition of Ready

A procurement, supplier or contract work item is Ready when:

- The requirement, sourcing activity, supplier, contract, renewal, issue, risk, finding, exception or exit activity is clearly identified.
- Ownership, scope, value, criticality, risk, security, privacy, financial, legal and continuity requirements are known.
- Evaluation, approval, implementation, monitoring, evidence and acceptance requirements are defined.
