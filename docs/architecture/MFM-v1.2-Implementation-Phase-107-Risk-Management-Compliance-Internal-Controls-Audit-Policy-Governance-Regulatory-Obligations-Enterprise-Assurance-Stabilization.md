# 1. Purpose

This document defines the one-hundred-and-seventh implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-106 – Privacy, Information Rights, Records Management, Data Protection, Retention, Legal Hold & Information Governance Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a controlled enterprise assurance capability covering enterprise risk management, risk taxonomy, risk ownership, risk assessment, risk treatment, control framework, internal controls, control testing, compliance obligations, regulatory register, policy governance, policy lifecycle, audit management, audit findings, corrective actions, compliance monitoring, evidence management, enterprise assurance, risk and compliance reporting and governance quality gates.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must identify, assess, treat, monitor and assure enterprise risks and obligations through clearly owned risks, effective controls, governed policies, traceable compliance requirements, independent assurance and evidence-based corrective action.**

> **Ensure that material risks are understood, owned, treated and monitored at a level appropriate to their potential impact on MFM objectives and obligations.**

# 136. Definition of Ready

An enterprise assurance work item is Ready when:

- Objective, risk, obligation or assurance subject is identified.
- Ownership and authority are established.
- Applicable criteria and requirements are understood.
- Relevant controls, evidence and dependencies are identified.
- Scope, assessment method and expected outcome are defined.
- Reporting and remediation requirements are understood.

---

# 137. Definition of Done

An enterprise assurance work item is Done when:

```text
Requirement Defined
        ↓
Risk / Control / Obligation Identified
        ↓
Assessment Completed
        ↓
Evidence Captured
        ↓
Finding / Outcome Recorded
        ↓
Remediation Completed or Accepted
        ↓
Verification Completed
        ↓
Assurance Status Updated
```

# 138. Final Risk Principle

> **Every material risk must have an accountable owner, an assessed exposure, an appropriate treatment and a defined monitoring approach.**

---

# 139. Final Control Principle

> **Controls must be designed against identifiable risks, operated consistently and supported by reliable evidence.**

---

# 140. Final Compliance Principle

> **Applicable obligations must be identified, assigned, translated into controls and monitored through evidence-based compliance processes.**

---

# 141. Final Policy Principle

> **Policies must be authoritative, current, approved, communicated and connected to operational requirements and controls.**

---

# 142. Final Audit Principle

> **Audit activity must be risk-informed, appropriately independent, evidence-based and focused on actionable improvement.**

---

# 143. Final Remediation Principle

> **Findings must remain visible until corrective action has been implemented and effectiveness verified or an appropriately authorized risk decision has been made.**

---

# 144. Final Assurance Principle

> **Enterprise assurance must provide an integrated, evidence-based view of whether MFM risks, obligations and controls are adequately governed.**

---

# 145. Final Integration Principle

> **Enterprise Assurance must integrate with Security, Privacy, Data, Service, Process, Financial, Supplier, Project, Grant, Document, Configuration, Architecture and Resilience governance.**

---

# 146. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should manage risk, compliance and assurance through a controlled lifecycle connecting risk identification, control design, obligation management, policy governance, audit, evidence, findings, remediation and executive reporting.**
