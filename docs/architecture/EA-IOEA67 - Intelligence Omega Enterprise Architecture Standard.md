# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the principles, governance mechanisms, architectural models, intelligence capabilities, and orchestration framework required for an **Intelligence Omega Enterprise Architecture (IOEA)**.

Where **EA-ITEA66** enables Enterprise Architecture to transcend its current organizational limitations, **EA-IOEA67** enables Enterprise Intelligence to orchestrate every previous intelligence capability into one continuously coherent, governed, explainable, sustainable, self-expanding, and permanently aligned enterprise intelligence ecosystem.

Omega Enterprise Intelligence represents the convergence of all previous architectural evolution stages into a unified enterprise cognition framework.

Rather than discovering isolated future possibilities, the architecture continuously coordinates all enterprise intelligence simultaneously.

Its purpose is to establish an enterprise capable of maintaining complete architectural coherence while continuously expanding organizational intelligence.

Omega Enterprise Intelligence shall always remain:

- Purpose-governed
- Human-supervised
- Explainable
- Scientifically credible
- Ethically accountable
- Sustainable
- Trustworthy
- Fully traceable
- Continuously learning
- Permanently aligned with Enterprise Identity

Enterprise Intelligence shall never become independent from Enterprise Purpose.
