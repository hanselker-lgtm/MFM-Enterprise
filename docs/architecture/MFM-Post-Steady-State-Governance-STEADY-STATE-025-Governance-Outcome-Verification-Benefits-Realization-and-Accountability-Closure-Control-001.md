# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-025 establishes the controlled mechanism for verifying whether authorized commitments and delivered changes have produced their intended outcomes, benefits and value.

The purpose is to prevent governance closure from occurring merely because work was delivered.

The operating sequence is:

```text
AUTHORIZED DECISION
↓
COMMITMENT
↓
EXECUTION
↓
DELIVERY
↓
ACCEPTANCE
↓
OUTCOME VERIFICATION
↓
BENEFIT REALIZATION
↓
VALUE ASSESSMENT
↓
ACCOUNTABILITY CLOSURE
↓
LEARNING
```

---

# 2. Core Principle

The following distinctions are mandatory:

```text
DELIVERED
≠
ADOPTED

ADOPTED
≠
EFFECTIVE

EFFECTIVE
≠
BENEFIT REALIZED

BENEFIT REALIZED
≠
VALUE REALIZED

ACTIVITY COMPLETE
≠
GOVERNANCE OUTCOME COMPLETE

EXPECTED OUTCOME
≠
ACTUAL OUTCOME

CLAIMED BENEFIT
≠
VERIFIED BENEFIT
```

# 74. Final STEADY-STATE-025 Statement

> **STEADY-STATE-025 establishes the controlled mechanism for verifying actual outcomes, benefits and value before material accountability is closed. It ensures that delivery is not mistaken for effectiveness, claimed benefits are not mistaken for verified benefits, and favorable outcomes are not automatically attributed to the implemented initiative. It establishes outcome, benefit and value registers, verification controls, attribution boundaries, sustainability controls and controlled closure/reopening mechanisms. N8 and N9 remain CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS, while N10 remains NOT DEFINED and NOT AUTHORIZED.**
