# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Analytics Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how data and knowledge are analysed, interpreted and transformed into intelligent insights supporting decisions, actions and adaptive operations.

The Intelligence Analytics Architecture Framework shall provide:

- analytics architecture principles
- analytical modelling frameworks
- analytics capability models
- insight generation structures
- predictive and prescriptive analysis mechanisms
- analytics governance model
- evolution framework
- maturity assessment
- operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through analytical intelligence ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Analytics Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Analytics Model
- Analytics Intelligence Architecture
- Analytical Knowledge Architecture
- Analytics Capability Model
- Analytics Architecture Integration
- Analytics Governance Model
- Analytics Evolution Framework
- Analytics Metrics
- Analytics Maturity Model
- Analytics Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Analytics Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Analytics Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Analytics Value Alignment

Analytics shall support enterprise objectives and Intelligence capability development.

---

## Principle 2 – Analytical Accuracy

Analytics shall provide reliable, validated and meaningful results.

---

## Principle 3 – Insight Generation

Analytics shall transform data and knowledge into actionable intelligence.

---

## Principle 4 – Analytical Integration

Analytics shall connect data, knowledge, models and decision capabilities.

---

## Principle 5 – Predictive Capability

Analytics shall support forecasting, prediction and future understanding.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Analytics Evolution

Analytics capability shall improve through learning, optimisation and innovation.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Analytics Governance

Analytics shall maintain transparency, accountability and ethical alignment.
