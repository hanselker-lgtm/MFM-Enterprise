# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Access Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Access Services shall follow these principles.

## Access by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include access considerations from initial design through operational lifecycle.

---

## Controlled Access

Access shall only be granted through approved governance processes.

---

## Need-Based Access

Access shall be provided according to business need, operational requirements and approved responsibilities.

---

## Access Ownership

Every access capability shall have defined ownership, responsibility and accountability.

---

## Access Lifecycle Management

Access shall be created, approved, reviewed, modified and removed through controlled lifecycle processes.

---

## Governed Access Management

Access activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.

---

## Continuous Access Improvement

Access capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
