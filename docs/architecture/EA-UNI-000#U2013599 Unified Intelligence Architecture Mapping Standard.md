# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Unified Intelligence Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive integration architecture that unifies all intelligence capabilities across the Enterprise Architecture landscape.

The Unified Intelligence Architecture Framework shall provide:

- unified intelligence architecture principles
- unified intelligence governance
- unified intelligence strategy
- unified capability architecture
- intelligence architecture integration framework
- unified knowledge architecture
- unified reasoning architecture
- unified decision architecture
- unified human-intelligence collaboration
- unified safety architecture
- unified alignment architecture
- unified operations
- unified performance management
- unified evolution

The framework ensures that all intelligence capabilities operate as a coordinated Enterprise intelligence ecosystem.

---

# 2. Scope

The Unified Intelligence Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture intelligence domains:

- Enterprise Unified Intelligence Model
- Unified Intelligence Governance
- Unified Intelligence Strategy
- Unified Capability Architecture
- Intelligence Architecture Integration Framework
- Unified Knowledge Architecture
- Unified Reasoning Architecture
- Unified Decision Architecture
- Unified Human–Intelligence Collaboration
- Unified Safety Architecture
- Unified Alignment Architecture
- Unified Operations
- Unified Performance
- Unified Maturity
- Unified Roadmaps
- Unified Evolution

The framework applies to:

- Enterprise intelligence ecosystems
- integrated intelligence platforms
- strategic decision environments
- knowledge-driven organisations
- future intelligent operating models

---

# 3. Enterprise Unified Intelligence Architecture Principles

The Unified Intelligence Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Intelligence Integration

All intelligence capabilities shall operate as integrated components of one Enterprise Intelligence architecture.

---

## Principle 2 – Unified Governance

All intelligence capabilities shall be governed through common principles, standards and controls.

---

## Principle 3 – Shared Intelligence Foundation

Knowledge, reasoning and intelligence resources shall operate on a common foundation.

---

## Principle 4 – Intelligence Collaboration

Different intelligence capabilities shall cooperate to create greater Enterprise value.

---

## Principle 5 – Continuous Evolution

The intelligence ecosystem shall continuously improve through learning and adaptation.

---

## Principle 6 – Human-Centred Intelligence

Intelligence capability shall enhance human decision-making, creativity and innovation.

---

## Principle 7 – Responsible Intelligence Management

All intelligence capabilities shall operate safely, transparently and responsibly.

---
