# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Governance Architecture Framework is to establish an advanced architecture layer describing how Intelligence capabilities are directed, controlled, governed and evolved through responsible decision structures, policies, standards and accountability mechanisms.

The Intelligence Governance Architecture Framework shall provide:

- governance architecture principles
- intelligence governance frameworks
- policy management models
- responsibility structures
- decision governance mechanisms
- compliance frameworks
- ethics governance structures
- governance maturity assessment
- governance operating model

The framework provides the foundation for developing advanced Intelligence capabilities through controlled, responsible and accountable ecosystems.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Governance Architecture Framework covers:

- Enterprise Intelligence Governance Model
- Intelligence Policy Architecture
- Intelligence Decision Governance Architecture
- Intelligence Responsibility Architecture
- Intelligence Compliance Architecture
- Intelligence Regulatory Architecture
- Intelligence Ethics Governance Architecture
- Governance Architecture Integration
- Governance Evolution Framework
- Governance Metrics
- Governance Maturity Model
- Governance Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- all MFM Intelligence Architecture Frameworks
- all enterprise architecture domains
- all Intelligence capability areas
- all future architecture development

---

# 3. Intelligence Governance Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Governance Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Accountability

Intelligence capabilities shall have clear ownership and responsibility.

---

## Principle 2 – Transparency

Governance decisions shall be visible, understandable and traceable.

---

## Principle 3 – Responsibility

Intelligence development and operation shall follow defined responsibilities.

---

## Principle 4 – Compliance

Intelligence capabilities shall operate within applicable rules, standards and requirements.

---

## Principle 5 – Ethical Governance

Intelligence capabilities shall support responsible and ethical use.

---

## Principle 6 – Continuous Governance Evolution

Governance capability shall continuously improve.

---

## Principle 7 – Controlled Decision Making

Important Intelligence decisions shall be governed through defined structures.

# 15. Intelligence Ethics Governance Architecture

The Intelligence Ethics Governance Architecture defines how ethical principles, responsible behaviour and societal considerations are incorporated into the governance of Intelligence capabilities.

The architecture ensures that Intelligence capabilities are developed and operated with:

- responsible use
- ethical decision-making
- human accountability
- transparency
- social responsibility
- continuous ethical improvement

Ethics governance shall ensure:
