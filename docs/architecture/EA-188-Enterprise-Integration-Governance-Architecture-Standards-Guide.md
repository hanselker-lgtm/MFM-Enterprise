# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Integration Governance throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Integration Governance ensures that all integrations are planned, approved, implemented, operated and continuously improved according to Enterprise Architecture principles while preserving interoperability, consistency, traceability, security and compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Integration Governance
- Integration Ownership
- Integration Lifecycle Management
- Integration Policies
- Architecture Review
- Compliance Management
- Integration Risk Management
- Operational Governance
- Audit and Reporting
- Continuous Improvement

All Enterprise Integration Governance implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 4. Integration Governance Principles

Enterprise Integration Governance shall follow these principles.

- Governance by Design
- Standardized Decision Making
- Enterprise-wide Consistency
- Controlled Lifecycle
- Traceability
- Accountability
- Continuous Improvement
- Technology Independence

Governance implementations shall remain independent of business logic implementations.

---

# 5. Governance Domains

Enterprise Integration Governance shall standardize the following domains.

Domains shall include

- Integration Ownership
- Lifecycle Governance
- Policy Management
- Compliance Management
- Risk Management
- Operational Governance
- Audit Management
- Reporting

Additional governance domains shall require Enterprise Architecture approval.

---

# 6. Governance Ownership

Each governance domain shall have documented ownership.

Ownership shall define

- business ownership
- technical ownership
- architectural ownership
- operational responsibility
- governance responsibility
- stewardship

Ownership shall remain documented throughout the governance lifecycle.

# 7. Governance Structure

Enterprise Integration Governance shall define

- governance structure
- approval authority
- standards enforcement
- architecture review responsibilities
- governance verification
- governance reporting

Governance shall remain technology independent.

Enterprise Integration Governance shall implement standardized ownership for all enterprise integrations.

- assign technical ownership
- assign architectural ownership
- define operational responsibilities
- preserve ownership history
- maintain ownership traceability

Integration ownership shall remain centrally governed.

Enterprise Integration Governance shall implement standardized lifecycle management.

Lifecycle management shall

- define planning activities
- define approval activities
- define implementation activities
- define operational activities
- define retirement activities
- preserve lifecycle history

Lifecycle management shall remain centrally governed.

# 10. Integration Policies

Enterprise Integration Governance shall implement standardized integration policies.

Integration policies shall

- define mandatory standards
- define approval criteria
- define documentation requirements
- define security requirements
- define operational requirements
- preserve policy traceability

Policies shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture.

# 11. Architecture Review

Enterprise Integration Governance shall implement standardized architecture reviews.

Architecture reviews shall

- evaluate architectural compliance
- evaluate interoperability
- evaluate scalability
- evaluate operational readiness
- preserve review history
- maintain review traceability

Enterprise Integration Governance shall implement standardized compliance management.

- monitor governance compliance
- verify policy compliance
- verify architecture compliance
- preserve compliance history
- support corrective actions
- maintain audit readiness

Compliance management shall remain continuously monitored.

Enterprise Integration Governance shall implement standardized risk management.

- identify integration risks

- classify architectural risks
- define mitigation strategies
- preserve risk history
- maintain risk traceability

Risk management shall remain centrally governed.

# 22. Error Handling

Enterprise Integration Governance implementations shall handle governance-related exceptions consistently.

Implementations shall

- classify governance failures
- classify compliance failures
- classify audit failures
- classify policy violations
- classify operational governance failures
- preserve complete auditability
- notify governance authorities

Governance exceptions shall never compromise enterprise architecture, interoperability, governance integrity, compliance, resilience or traceability.

---

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Integration Governance implementations may depend upon

- approved Enterprise Architecture standards
- approved governance capabilities
- approved monitoring platforms
- approved compliance services
- approved enterprise repositories
- approved enterprise infrastructure

Enterprise Integration Governance implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external governance services

Governance capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Integration Governance implementation is compliant when

- Governance responsibilities are documented.
- Ownership is assigned and maintained.
- Lifecycle management is implemented.
- Integration policies are documented.
- Architecture reviews are completed.
- Compliance management is operational.
- Risk management is documented.
- Operational governance is implemented.
- Audit requirements are fulfilled.
- Governance reporting is operational.
- Continuous improvement activities are documented.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Undefined Ownership

Enterprise integrations shall never exist without documented ownership.

---

## Missing Governance Reviews

Enterprise integrations shall never bypass mandatory governance reviews.

---

## Policy Violations

Integration implementations shall never ignore approved governance policies.

---

## Unmanaged Lifecycle

Enterprise integrations shall never be deployed or retired outside the approved lifecycle process.

---

## Incomplete Audit Trail

Governance activities shall never be performed without preserving complete auditability.

---

## Governance Outside Enterprise Architecture

Enterprise Integration Governance shall never operate independently of Enterprise Architecture governance.

---

Architecture Review shall verify

- traceability
- compliance with enterprise standards

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Integration Governance Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Integration Governance throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise integrations remain governed, compliant, traceable and continuously improved while preserving interoperability, accountability and alignment with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Integration Governance implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
