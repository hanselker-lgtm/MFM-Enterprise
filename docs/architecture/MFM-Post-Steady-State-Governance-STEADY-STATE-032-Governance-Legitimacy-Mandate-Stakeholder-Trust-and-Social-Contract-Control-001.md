# 1. Purpose

STEADY-STATE-032 establishes the controlled mechanism for maintaining and assessing governance legitimacy, mandate acceptance, stakeholder trust and the relationship between formal authority and sustained stakeholder acceptance.

The purpose is to ensure that governance is not assessed only by whether it has formal power, but also by whether its mandate, processes, decisions and conduct remain sufficiently understandable, accountable, proportionate and accepted by the stakeholders to whom the governance system materially applies.

The operating sequence is:

```text
MANDATE
↓
AUTHORITY
↓
PROCESS
↓
DECISION
↓
OUTCOME
↓
STAKEHOLDER EXPERIENCE
↓
TRUST / ACCEPTANCE
↓
LEGITIMACY ASSESSMENT
↓
LEARNING
↓
ADAPTATION
```

# 4. Legitimacy

For this control, legitimacy concerns whether the exercise of governance authority is regarded as sufficiently justified, appropriate and acceptable within the applicable governing context.

Legitimacy may have multiple dimensions:

```text
FORMAL
PROCEDURAL
SUBSTANTIVE
RELATIONAL
PERCEPTUAL
```

---

# 5. Formal Legitimacy

Formal legitimacy may arise from:

```text
Constitutional Basis
Law / Regulation
Articles / Charter
Formal Mandate
Authorized Delegation
Governance Instrument
```

The applicable source must be identified.

---

# 6. Procedural Legitimacy

Procedural legitimacy concerns whether governance processes are:

```text
Consistent
Understandable
Traceable
Fair
Predictable
Authorized
Reviewable
```

---

# 7. Substantive Legitimacy

Substantive legitimacy concerns whether material decisions and outcomes remain reasonably aligned with:

```text
Purpose
Mandate
Foundational Principles
Stakeholder Obligations
Proportionality
Expected Outcomes
```

---

# 8. Relational Legitimacy

Relational legitimacy concerns whether affected stakeholders experience governance as:

```text
Credible
Respectful
Consistent
Accountable
Responsive
Understandable
```

---

# 9. Perceptual Legitimacy

Perceived legitimacy is a stakeholder perception and may differ from formal legitimacy.

It should therefore be measured or assessed without automatically treating perception as proof of formal validity.

```text

=
```

```text
=
```

> **STEADY-STATE-032 establishes the controlled mechanism for assessing governance legitimacy, mandate acceptance, stakeholder trust and the relationship between formal authority and sustained stakeholder acceptance.
