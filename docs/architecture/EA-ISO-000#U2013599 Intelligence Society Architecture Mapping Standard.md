# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Society Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive architecture for understanding and managing the relationship between Enterprise Intelligence capabilities and society.

The Intelligence Society Architecture Framework shall provide:

- societal intelligence principles
- human–Intelligence relationship models
- digital society Intelligence architecture
- intelligent organisation society models
- social impact assessment
- human capability evolution
- workforce transformation
- education evolution
- economic impact analysis
- cultural impact analysis
- societal trust development
- society measurement
- society maturity
- society evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence develops in alignment with human capability, societal needs and responsible innovation.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Society Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture society domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Society Model
- Human–Intelligence Relationship Architecture
- Digital Society Intelligence Architecture
- Intelligent Organisation Society Model
- Social Impact Architecture
- Human Capability Evolution
- Workforce Intelligence Architecture
- Education Intelligence Architecture
- Economic Intelligence Architecture
- Cultural Intelligence Architecture
- Societal Trust Architecture
- Society Metrics
- Society Maturity
- Society Roadmaps
- Society Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- digital societies
- human-machine ecosystems
- future workforce environments
- emerging Intelligence societies

# 3. Intelligence Society Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Society Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Human-Centred Society

Intelligence development shall enhance human capability and quality of life.

---

## Principle 2 – Inclusive Intelligence

Intelligence capabilities shall support broad participation and accessibility.

---

## Principle 3 – Responsible Societal Impact

Intelligence development shall consider social consequences.

---

## Principle 4 – Human Capability Enhancement

Intelligence shall augment human knowledge, skills and creativity.

---

## Principle 5 – Sustainable Society Development

Intelligence evolution shall support long-term societal wellbeing.

---

## Principle 6 – Societal Trust

Society shall maintain confidence in intelligent technologies.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Societal Evolution

Society shall continuously adapt through learning and innovation.
