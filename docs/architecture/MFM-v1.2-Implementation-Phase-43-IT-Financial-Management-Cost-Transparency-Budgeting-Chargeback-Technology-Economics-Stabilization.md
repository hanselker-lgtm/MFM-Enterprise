# 1. Purpose

This document defines the forty-third implementation phase following MFM v1.2-Implementation-Phase-42 – Service Level Management, SLA Governance, Service Performance & Operational Assurance Stabilization.

The purpose of this phase is to establish a transparent, controlled and decision-oriented financial management capability for technology, services, applications, infrastructure, vendors and digital investments.

The central objective is:

> **MFM must make technology and service economics visible, attributable and governable so that management can understand cost, value, consumption, investment, financial risk and optimization opportunities across the enterprise.**

# 104. Final Transparency Principle

> **Management must be able to understand what technology and services cost and where those costs originate.**

---

# 105. Final Attribution Principle

> **Material technology costs should be attributable to meaningful services, applications, capabilities, projects or business functions wherever practical.**

---

# 106. Final Budget Principle

> **Budgets are controlled plans, not guarantees; actuals, commitments and forecasts must remain visible throughout the financial cycle.**

---

# 107. Final Forecast Principle

> **Forecasts must reflect current evidence and known future commitments rather than simply repeat historical assumptions.**

---

# 108. Final TCO Principle

> **Technology economics must consider the full lifecycle cost rather than acquisition price alone.**

---

# 109. Final Cloud Principle

> **Cloud consumption must be treated as an actively governed economic resource rather than an invisible operating expense.**

---

# 110. Final Optimization Principle

> **Cost optimization must preserve or improve business value, service quality, security, resilience and compliance wherever possible.**

---

# 111. Final Investment Principle

> **Technology investment must be evaluated through cost, benefit, risk, strategic alignment and measurable outcomes.**

---

# 112. Final Chargeback Principle

> **Chargeback and showback models must be transparent, reproducible and based on meaningful consumption or allocation drivers.**

---

# 113. Final Assurance Principle

> **Financial decisions must be supported by reconciled, attributable and auditable evidence.**

---

# 114. Final Integration Principle

> **Technology financial management must connect services, applications, capabilities, vendors, contracts, projects, architecture, risk, performance and enterprise finance into one decision-support model.**

---

# 115. Final Implementation Principle

> **MFM should make technology economics visible and actionable so management can balance cost, value, risk, service quality and long-term investment.**
