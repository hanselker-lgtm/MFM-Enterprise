## Purpose

Establish the authoritative mandatory substantive validation layer for EA-IMETA-PC-RG-284, determining whether the procedural verification of EA-IMETA-PC-RG-283 is itself substantively true, effective, complete and supportable in the actual current operating state.

## Core Principle

EA-IMETA-PC-RG-283 verifies that RG-282 validation was correctly performed and implemented. EA-IMETA-PC-RG-284 validates whether the EA-IMETA-PC-RG-283 verification remains substantively effective and provides reliable assurance.

```text
RG-282 → VALIDATE
RG-283 → VERIFY
RG-284 → VALIDATE
```

```text
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-283 PROCEDURAL VERIFICATION
        ↓
EA-IMETA-PC-RG-284 SUBSTANTIVE VALIDATION
        ↓
IS THE EA-IMETA-PC-RG-283 VERIFICATION ACTUALLY EFFECTIVE AND TRUE?
```

A positive EA-IMETA-PC-RG-283 verification SHALL NOT automatically establish substantive current effectiveness.
