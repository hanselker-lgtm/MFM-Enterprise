# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory enterprise standards governing performance, capacity planning and scalability throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The guide ensures that enterprise systems remain responsive, scalable and operational under varying workloads through standardized performance engineering and capacity management.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Performance Architecture
- Capacity Planning
- Scalability
- Performance Metrics
- Load Management
- Resource Optimization
- Performance Testing
- Capacity Monitoring
- Performance Governance
- Compliance

All enterprise performance and scalability capabilities shall comply with this guide.

---

## PERF-001

## PERF-003

## PERF-004

Prevent performance degradation.

## PERF-005

Maintain enterprise responsiveness during changing workloads.

Enterprise capacity shall be planned proactively.

- estimate resource requirements

- define scaling thresholds

- support budget planning

- service availability

Enterprise performance shall be verified through systematic testing.

- Persistence models

# 24. Compliance Checklist

A performance implementation is compliant when

- Capacity planning is documented.
- Scalability strategy is defined.
- Performance metrics are continuously monitored.
- Performance testing is performed regularly.
- Resource optimization activities are documented.
- Capacity monitoring is operational.
- Performance registry is maintained.
- Governance requirements are enforced.
- Audit logging is enabled.
- Continuous performance improvement is demonstrated.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Reactive Capacity Planning

Enterprise capacity shall never be expanded only after critical resource exhaustion has occurred.

---

## Missing Performance Baselines

Enterprise systems shall never operate without documented and approved performance baselines.

---

## Uncontrolled Resource Consumption

Applications shall never consume enterprise resources without defined limits or monitoring.

---

## Untested Scalability

Enterprise systems shall never be assumed scalable without documented scalability testing.

---

## Ignored Performance Degradation

Performance degradation shall never remain unresolved without investigation, remediation and documented corrective actions.

---

## Capacity Monitoring Gaps

Critical enterprise services shall never operate without continuous capacity monitoring and alerting.

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Performance, Capacity & Scalability Architecture Guide defines the mandatory standards governing enterprise performance, capacity planning and scalability throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise systems remain responsive, scalable and operational under changing workloads through standardized performance engineering, proactive capacity planning, continuous monitoring and systematic optimization.

All performance, capacity and scalability implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
