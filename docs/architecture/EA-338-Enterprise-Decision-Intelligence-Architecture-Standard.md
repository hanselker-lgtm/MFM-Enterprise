# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to define the Enterprise principles governing Decision Intelligence throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

The Enterprise Decision Intelligence Architecture shall

- provide standardized decision support
- integrate business rules and analytics
- combine Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- support explainable decisions
- improve operational consistency
- establish Enterprise governance
- remain technology independent

The Enterprise Decision Intelligence Architecture shall ensure that Enterprise decisions are transparent, traceable and based upon authoritative information.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every Enterprise Decision Intelligence implementation throughout the Enterprise Platform.

It governs

- decision models
- business rules
- decision execution
- AI-assisted decisions
- Machine Learning integration
- Knowledge Graph integration
- semantic reasoning
- governance
- lifecycle management

The standard applies independently of decision engines, AI frameworks, rule engines and infrastructure technologies.

---

# 3. Enterprise Decision Intelligence Definition

Enterprise Decision Intelligence is the coordinated application of business rules, analytics, semantic knowledge, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to support, automate and optimize Enterprise decision making.

Decision Intelligence encompasses

- business rules
- predictive analytics
- prescriptive analytics
- semantic reasoning
- AI-assisted decision support
- automated recommendations
- decision orchestration
- explainable decision outcomes

Decision Intelligence shall operate under Enterprise governance and preserve human oversight where required.

---

# 4. Enterprise Decision Intelligence Objectives

The Enterprise Decision Intelligence Architecture shall

- improve decision quality
- improve operational consistency
- support explainability
- support regulatory compliance
- enable reusable decision services
- preserve Enterprise governance
- support continuous optimization

Decision Intelligence capabilities shall be available as reusable Enterprise services.

---

# 5. Enterprise Decision Intelligence Responsibilities

The Enterprise Decision Intelligence Architecture is responsible for

- decision modeling
- rule orchestration
- AI integration
- Machine Learning integration
- semantic reasoning
- decision execution
- monitoring
- governance
- lifecycle management

The Enterprise Decision Intelligence Architecture shall never

- replace Enterprise governance
- bypass business ownership
- circumvent regulatory requirements
- expose implementation-specific decision technologies directly to business applications

Business accountability for decisions shall remain with the responsible business domain, even when decision support is automated.

# 19. Enterprise Decision Intelligence Anti-Patterns

The following architectural anti-patterns are prohibited.

## Unexplained Decisions

Business-critical decisions shall never be produced without an appropriate explanation of how the decision was reached.

Explainability shall remain mandatory.

---

## AI-Only Decision Making

Artificial Intelligence shall never replace approved business governance where human accountability is required.

Human oversight shall remain available for governed decision processes.

---

## Hard-Coded Business Policies

Business rules shall never be embedded directly within application code where centralized Enterprise Rule services exist.

Business policies shall remain externally governed.

---

## Missing Decision Traceability

Decision inputs, applied rules, AI recommendations, supporting evidence and final outcomes shall always remain traceable.

Decision provenance shall remain permanently available.

---

## Unvalidated Decision Models

Decision Models shall never be promoted to production without validation, approval and governance.

Controlled review shall precede deployment.

---

## Ignoring Decision Performance

Decision services shall never operate without continuous monitoring of

- decision quality
- execution performance
- confidence levels
- rule effectiveness
- AI effectiveness
- Machine Learning effectiveness

Observed degradation shall initiate review and corrective action according to Enterprise governance.
