# Architecture Compliance

This standard defines the Enterprise Audit Services Architecture for the MFM Enterprise Platform.

General Enterprise Architecture principles are inherited from EA-020.

Enterprise Architecture positioning is inherited from EA-111.

Enterprise Governance Services principles are inherited from EA-467.

Enterprise Risk Services principles are inherited from EA-468.

Enterprise Security Architecture principles are inherited from EA-360 through EA-369.

All Enterprise Audit Services implementations shall comply with this standard.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish the Enterprise Audit Services Architecture supporting centralized, secure, standardized and traceable audit management across the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Audit Services shall

- provide centralized audit management
- standardize audit processes
- improve governance
- strengthen accountability
- support compliance verification
- enable enterprise automation
- improve interoperability
- ensure evidence traceability
- support organizational transparency
- support continuous improvement

Enterprise Audit Services shall provide trusted and reusable audit capabilities throughout the Enterprise.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to

- enterprise audit services
- audit planning
- audit execution
- audit evidence
- audit findings
- corrective actions
- audit reporting
- audit lifecycle management
- enterprise users

The standard applies regardless of deployment model, business domain or infrastructure platform.

---

# 3. Enterprise Audit Services Principles

Enterprise Audit Services shall follow these principles.

## Audit by Design

Audit capabilities shall be integrated into Enterprise Architecture, business processes, technology platforms and operational activities from their inception.

---

## Secure by Default

Every audit activity shall be authenticated, authorized, audited and continuously monitored.

---

## Evidence Integrity

Audit evidence shall remain complete, accurate, immutable, traceable and protected throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Metadata Driven Audit Management

Audit plans, evidence, findings and recommendations shall be classified and governed using Enterprise metadata to ensure traceability, discoverability and consistent reporting.

---

## Technology Independence

Enterprise Audit Services shall remain independent of vendors, audit platforms, cloud providers and implementation technologies.

# 32. Conclusion

Enterprise Audit Services Architecture establishes the standardized architectural framework for centralized, secure and governed Enterprise audit management across all Business and Technology Domains.

By integrating audit planning, audit execution, evidence management, findings management, corrective actions, reporting, governance, risk, compliance, metadata, event integration, security, observability and automation into a unified Enterprise capability, the MFM Enterprise Platform enables transparent, repeatable and scalable audit operations while maintaining compliance, resilience and architectural consistency.

This standard shall be applied to all new Enterprise Audit Services initiatives and shall guide modernization of existing Enterprise audit capabilities across the Enterprise.

---

# 33. Future Evolution

The Enterprise Audit Services Architecture shall evolve to support AI-assisted audit planning, autonomous evidence analysis, predictive audit analytics, semantic-aware audit knowledge, adaptive audit scheduling, intelligent corrective action recommendations, continuous assurance capabilities and quantum-safe Enterprise audit infrastructures.

Future enhancements shall remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture governance, ensuring that Enterprise Audit Services continue to provide secure, scalable, interoperable and business-driven audit capabilities across the MFM Enterprise Platform.
