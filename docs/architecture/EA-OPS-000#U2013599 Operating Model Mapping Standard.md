# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Operating Model Framework is to establish the operational foundation required to execute, manage and continuously improve the MFM Enterprise Architecture environment.

The Operating Model Framework shall provide:

- operating principles
- operating structures
- roles and responsibilities
- operational processes
- service operating models
- capability execution models
- performance management
- continuous improvement

The framework defines how Enterprise Architecture becomes an operational capability.

---

# 2. Scope

The Operating Model Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture operating domains:

- Enterprise Operating Model
- Architecture Operating Model
- Governance Operating Model
- Capability Operating Model
- Service Operating Model
- Process Operating Model
- Application Operating Model
- Technology Operating Model
- Infrastructure Operating Model
- Information Operating Model
- Data Operating Model
- Knowledge Operating Model
- Decision Operating Model
- Value Operating Model
- Evolution Operating Model

The framework applies to:

- strategic operations
- business operations
- digital operations
- technology operations
- governance operations
- transformation operations

---

# 3. Enterprise Operating Model Principles

The MFM Operating Model shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Strategy Driven Operations

All operational activities shall support Enterprise strategy and objectives.

---

## Principle 2 – Capability Based Operation

The Enterprise shall operate through defined capabilities rather than isolated functions.

---

## Principle 3 – Service Orientation

Operational delivery shall be organized around services and value creation.

---

## Principle 4 – Process Excellence

Processes shall be defined, measured and continuously improved.

---

## Principle 5 – Clear Ownership

Every operational capability, service and process shall have defined ownership.

---

## Principle 6 – Integrated Governance

Operations shall follow approved governance structures and decision models.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Improvement

Operations shall continuously evolve through measurement, learning and improvement.

---

# 9. Capability Operating Model

The Capability Operating Model defines how Enterprise capabilities are planned, operated, measured and continuously improved.

Capabilities represent the fundamental abilities required for Enterprise execution and value creation.

---

# 9.1 Capability Operating Principles

The Capability Operating Model shall ensure:

- capability ownership
- capability maturity management
- capability performance measurement
- capability improvement
- capability alignment with strategy

# 15. Information Operating Model

# 21. Evolution Operating Model

The Evolution Operating Model defines how Enterprise capabilities, services, processes, technologies and operating structures continuously improve and adapt.

Evolution ensures that the MFM Enterprise Architecture remains relevant, sustainable and future-ready.

---

# 21.1 Evolution Operating Principles

Enterprise Evolution shall:

- align with Enterprise strategy
- be governed through defined processes
- create measurable improvements
- incorporate learning and experience
- support continuous adaptation

---

# 21.2 Evolution Management Structure

| Evolution Function | Responsibility |

| Evolution Strategy | Define future direction |
| Evolution Governance | Control transformation activities |
| Evolution Planning | Prioritize improvements |
| Evolution Execution | Implement changes |
| Evolution Measurement | Evaluate results |
| Evolution Optimization | Improve evolution capability |
