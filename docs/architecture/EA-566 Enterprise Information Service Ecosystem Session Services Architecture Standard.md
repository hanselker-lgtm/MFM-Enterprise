# 3. Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Session Services Principles

Enterprise Information Service Ecosystem Session Services shall follow these principles.

## Session by Design

Enterprise ecosystem capabilities shall include session considerations from initial design through operational lifecycle.

---

## Controlled Session Establishment

Sessions shall only be established through approved authentication, authorization and access mechanisms.

---

## Session Context Preservation

Sessions shall maintain appropriate context required for secure and reliable Enterprise interactions.

---

## Session Ownership

Every session capability shall have defined ownership, responsibility and accountability.

---

## Session Lifecycle Management

Sessions shall be created, maintained, monitored, terminated and reviewed through controlled lifecycle processes.

---

## Governed Session Management

Session activities shall operate through approved Enterprise governance structures.

---

## Continuous Session Improvement

Session capabilities shall continuously improve according to Enterprise requirements.
