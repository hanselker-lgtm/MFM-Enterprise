## Purpose
Establish the authoritative outcome layer beneath accountability, defining expected outcomes, criteria, baselines, measurement, actual results, variance, acceptance, ownership, evidence, risk, escalation and review for post-closure regression governance.

## Core Principle
An outcome is the governed result that a responsibility exists to achieve or protect. Completion of an activity does not prove that the intended outcome has been achieved.

```text
ACCOUNTABILITY
      ↓
OUTCOME
      ↓
EXPECTED OUTCOME
      ↓
OUTCOME CRITERIA
      ↓
BASELINE
      ↓
MEASUREMENT
      ↓
ACTUAL OUTCOME
      ↓
VARIANCE
      ↓
CORRECTION / ACCEPTANCE
      ↓
ASSURANCE
      ↓
REVIEW
```

```text
DEFINED PURPOSE

+

+

+
ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
+
ACCEPTANCE RULE

ESCALATION PATH

VALID GOVERNED OUTCOME
```

## Outcome Structure
| Element | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Why the outcome matters |
| Expected Outcome | Intended result |
| Criteria | Conditions defining success |
| Baseline | Approved reference state |
| Measurement | Method and frequency |
| Actual Outcome | Observed result |
| Variance | Difference from expected/baseline |
| Acceptance | Governed determination of acceptability |
| Owner | Accountable role |
| Evidence | Proof supporting the result |
| Risk | Risk associated with outcome failure |
| Escalation | Conditions requiring intervention |
| Review | Reassessment requirements |

## Expected Outcome
Expected outcomes shall be stated in terms of observable, measurable or otherwise verifiable results. They shall avoid ambiguous activity-only statements.

```text
WE WILL PERFORM X
        ≠
THE SYSTEM / SERVICE WILL ACHIEVE Y```

## Outcome Criteria
Criteria shall define what constitutes achievement, partial achievement, unacceptable variance and failure.

```text
EXPECTED OUTCOME
      ↓
CRITERIA
 ├── ACHIEVED
 ├── PARTIALLY ACHIEVED
 ├── DEVIATING
 └── FAILED```

## Outcome Baseline
The baseline is the approved reference against which current outcome performance is assessed. Baseline changes shall be governed, versioned and traceable.

```text
APPROVED BASELINE
      ↓
CURRENT MEASUREMENT
      ↓
COMPARISON
      ↓
VARIANCE```

## Outcome Measurement
Measurement shall use appropriate methods, data quality controls, frequency and evidence. Automated measurement shall remain subject to validation where material.

## Actual Outcome
Actual outcome represents the observed state supported by evidence. It shall not be inferred solely from intended actions or declarations of completion.

## Outcome Variance
```text
ACTUAL OUTCOME      ↓
COMPARE TO EXPECTATION / BASELINE      ↓
VARIANCE
      ↓
WITHIN TOLERANCE? ├── YES → CONTINUE / ACCEPT
 └── NO  → ASSESS / CORRECT / ESCALATE```

Variance shall be evaluated for materiality, trend, duration, systemic impact and recurrence.

## Outcome Acceptance
Outcome acceptance is a governed determination that the result satisfies defined criteria or that an explicitly authorized conditional acceptance exists.

```text
MEASURED OUTCOME
      ↓
CRITERIA SATISFIED?
 ├── YES → ACCEPT
 └── NO  → CORRECT / CONDITIONALLY ACCEPT / REJECT / ESCALATE```

## Outcome Ownership
Every material outcome shall have one identifiable accountable owner. Supporting roles may contribute, but ownership shall remain explicit.

## Outcome Control
Controls shall be linked to the outcomes they protect. A control may be operationally effective while an outcome still fails, requiring separate outcome assessment.

```text
CONTROL EFFECTIVE
      ≠
OUTCOME GUARANTEED```

## Outcome Evidence
Evidence shall support the measurement, baseline, interpretation, acceptance and any variance disposition. Evidence shall be attributable, relevant and preserved.

## Outcome Risk
Outcome risk shall consider impact if the expected result is not achieved, including security, resilience, compliance, financial, operational and customer consequences.

## Security and Resilience Outcome
Security and resilience outcomes shall explicitly address the desired protection, recovery, continuity or service behavior where applicable.

## Data Outcome
Data outcomes may include integrity, accuracy, completeness, timeliness, lineage, availability, authorized access and correct use.

## AI and Agent Outcome
AI and agent outcomes shall evaluate not only task completion but also correctness, safety, policy compliance, bounded autonomy, explainability where required and absence of unacceptable side effects.

```text
AI / AGENT TASK
      ↓
TASK COMPLETED?
      ↓
OUTCOME CORRECT?
      ↓
WITHIN POLICY / RISK LIMITS?
      ↓
ACCEPTABLE RESULT?```

## Compliance Outcome
Compliance outcomes shall define the required state of conformity and the evidence necessary to demonstrate it.

## Outcome Escalation
```text
OUTCOME DEVIATION
      ↓
MATERIAL?
 ├── NO  → CORRECT / MONITOR
 └── YES → ESCALATE
              ↓
        DECISION AUTHORITY```

Repeated or systemic variance shall trigger escalation even where individual deviations appear small.

## Outcome Continuity
Critical outcomes shall have continuity arrangements covering measurement, ownership, evidence and acceptance during organizational or operational disruption.

## Outcome Failure
```text
OUTCOME FAILED
      ↓
CONTAIN IMPACT
      ↓
ASSESS ROOT / CONTRIBUTING CAUSES
      ↓
REMEDIATE
      ↓
RE-MEASURE
      ↓
ACCEPT / REJECT / REOPEN```

## Outcome Review
Outcome definitions and criteria shall be reviewed after material regression, baseline change, architecture change, risk change, repeated variance or significant lifecycle change.

```text
OUTCOME ACTIVE
      ↓
REVIEW TRIGGER
      ↓
STILL RELEVANT / MEASURABLE?
 ├── YES → CONTINUE
 └── NO  → MODIFY / RETIRE / REBASELINE```

## Governance Learning
Repeated outcome failures shall trigger review of responsibility, controls, architecture, baselines, measurement quality, acceptance criteria and governance assumptions.

## Relationship to Existing Architecture
This document specializes the outcome layer beneath accountability and responsibility for post-closure regression governance. It does not replace the governance, authority, mandate, role or lifecycle control documents.
