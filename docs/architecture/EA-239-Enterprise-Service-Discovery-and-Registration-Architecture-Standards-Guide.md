# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the mandatory standards governing Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration provides standardized mechanisms for service registration, service discovery, dynamic endpoint resolution and operational governance while preserving scalability, reliability, traceability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Scope

This guide applies to

- Service Registration
- Service Discovery
- Dynamic Endpoint Resolution
- Service Registry
- Health Checking
- High Availability
- Governance
- Compliance

All Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration implementations shall comply with this guide.

# 23. Dependency Rules

Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration implementations may depend upon

- approved service registry infrastructure
- approved monitoring services
- approved logging services
- approved configuration services
- approved reporting services
- approved enterprise infrastructure
- approved governance services

Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration implementations shall never depend upon

- Presentation implementations
- Workflow implementations containing business logic
- Domain implementations
- Repository implementations across capability boundaries
- Business Services
- Unapproved external service registry providers

Enterprise Service Discovery capabilities shall remain centralized wherever practical.

---

# 24. Compliance Checklist

An Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration implementation is compliant when

- Service registration is implemented.
- Service discovery is implemented.
- Dynamic endpoint resolution is implemented.
- Health checking is implemented.
- Service validation is performed.
- Service verification is performed.
- Governance requirements are fulfilled.
- Audit requirements are satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.

---

# 25. Common Anti-Patterns

The following practices are prohibited.

## Hard-Coded Service Endpoints

Enterprise implementations shall never depend upon fixed service addresses where approved service discovery mechanisms are required.

---

## Unregistered Services

Services shall never participate in enterprise communication without approved registration.

---

## Missing Health Checks

Critical enterprise services shall never be deployed without approved health monitoring.

---

## Stale Service Registrations

Inactive or failed services shall never remain registered after governance-defined thresholds have been exceeded.

---

## Hidden Service Registry Dependencies

Service discovery implementations shall never introduce undocumented registry infrastructure or discovery mechanisms.

---

## Business Logic Inside Service Discovery Infrastructure

Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration implementations shall never contain business logic that belongs within the appropriate Domain or Capability implementation.

---

# Final Statement

The Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration Architecture Standards Guide defines the mandatory standards governing service registration, discovery and endpoint resolution throughout the MFM Enterprise Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise services are discoverable, highly available and consistently governed while preserving scalability, traceability, operational reliability and compliance with Enterprise Architecture.

All Enterprise Service Discovery & Registration implementations developed for the MFM Enterprise Platform shall comply with this guide.
