# 1. Purpose

N8-C establishes controlled traceability between:

```text
Strategy
    ↓
Outcome
    ↓
Capability
    ↓
Architecture
    ↓
Roadmap
    ↓
Initiative
    ↓
Investment
    ↓
Prioritization
    ↓
Approval
    ↓
Execution
```

The purpose is to make portfolio and investment decisions traceable to the strategic, capability and architecture baselines established in N8-A and N8-B.

N8-C does not constitute financial approval or funding authority.

# 45. Final N8-C Statement

> **N8-C establishes the Investment, Prioritization & Portfolio Traceability layer of the authorized N8 workstream. It creates controlled relationships from strategic need through capability, architecture, roadmap, initiative, investment, prioritization, approval, funding and execution while preserving the distinction between each decision state. N8-C does not constitute financial approval or funding authority and does not establish that an investment has been implemented or that value has been realized. The outputs of N8-C provide the controlled input to N8-D Value, Outcome & Realization Governance.**
