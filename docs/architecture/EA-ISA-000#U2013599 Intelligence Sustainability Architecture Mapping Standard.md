# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the MFM Enterprise Architecture Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Framework is to establish a comprehensive sustainability architecture for ensuring that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are developed, operated and evolved in a responsible, efficient and sustainable manner.

The Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Framework shall provide:

- sustainability principles
- sustainability governance
- sustainable intelligence architecture
- green AI architecture
- energy efficient intelligence
- sustainable data architecture
- sustainable infrastructure
- responsible intelligence
- ethical sustainability
- social impact architecture
- sustainability measurement
- sustainability maturity
- sustainability evolution

The framework ensures that Enterprise Intelligence creates long-term value while reducing environmental impact and supporting responsible development.

---

# 2. Scope

The Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Framework covers all MFM Enterprise Architecture sustainability domains:

- Enterprise Intelligence Sustainability Model
- Sustainability Governance
- Sustainable Intelligence Architecture
- Green AI Architecture
- Energy Efficient Intelligence
- Sustainable Data Architecture
- Sustainable Infrastructure
- Responsible Intelligence
- Ethical Sustainability
- Social Impact Architecture
- Sustainability Metrics
- Sustainability Maturity
- Sustainability Roadmaps
- Sustainability Evolution

The framework applies to:

- intelligent organisations
- AI-enabled enterprises
- sustainable digital environments
- responsible Intelligence systems
- future Enterprise ecosystems

---

# 3. Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Principles

The Intelligence Sustainability Architecture Framework shall follow these principles.

---

## Principle 1 – Sustainable Intelligence Development

Enterprise Intelligence shall be developed with long-term sustainability in mind.

---

## Principle 2 – Responsible Resource Usage

Intelligence capabilities shall optimise the use of energy, infrastructure and data resources.

---

## Principle 3 – Environmental Responsibility

Enterprise Intelligence shall minimise environmental impact.

---

## Principle 4 – Responsible Intelligence

Intelligence systems shall operate according to ethical and organisational values.

---

## Principle 5 – Sustainable Data Management

Data shall be managed efficiently throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Principle 6 – Social Value Creation

Enterprise Intelligence shall contribute positively to people and society.

---

## Principle 7 – Continuous Sustainability Improvement

Sustainability capability shall continuously evolve through measurement and learning.

---

# 9. Intelligence Sustainability Architecture

The Intelligence Sustainability Architecture defines the structural organisation of Enterprise Intelligence sustainability capabilities, including sustainable design, resource efficiency, environmental responsibility, responsible Intelligence and long-term value creation.

The architecture ensures that Enterprise Intelligence capabilities are developed and operated with consideration for environmental, economic and social sustainability.

- sustainable Intelligence development
- efficient resource utilisation
- reduced environmental impact
- responsible technology usage

- measurable sustainability outcomes
- continuous sustainability improvement
